# Amsterdam-Oost: blind recall, 2026-11-23

An independent test. It does not use deepcities.org, `pnpm dc`, earlier research files or scripts/data.

Scope: stadsdeel Oost. The official GGW-gebieden (api.data.amsterdam.nl/v1/gebieden/ggwgebieden) are four, not five:
GM14 Oud-Oost, GM15 Indische Buurt/Oostelijk Havengebied, GM16 Watergraafsmeer, GM17 IJburg/Zeeburgereiland. That gives 4 x 20 = 80 items.

How the list was built: local "verborgen parels" lists (Lex and the City's 28 Oost gems, iamsterdam's hidden-gem pages), walking blogs (frankwandelt, wandelvrouw, davides, Wandelnetwerk NH), Ons Amsterdam, Oost-Online's ommetje, Atlas Obscura, Spotted by Locals, NH Nieuws, Amsterdam Local Gems and the Amsterdamian. Each list is ranked by how many sources named a place and how strongly they recommended it. One URL is given per item, usually the strongest source.

Boundary checks (PDOK address lookup): De Gooyer/Funenkade, the Muiderpoort gate (Alexanderplein) and the Oranje-Nassau Kazerne are in Centrum and are left out. The Linnaeushof and Amstelstation fall in Watergraafsmeer. Polderweg, Oostpoort and Oranje-Vrijstaatkade are in the Dapperbuurt (Oud-Oost). Valentijnkade is in the Indische Buurt. The Marineterrein and Scheepvaartmuseum are in Centrum.

A-list items left out as instructed: Artis, Wereldmuseum (Tropenmuseum), Dappermarkt, NEMO, Brouwerij 't IJ, Muziekgebouw.

## GM14 Oud-Oost (Oosterparkbuurt, Dapperbuurt, Transvaalbuurt, Weesperzijde)

1. **Brouwerij Poesiat & Kater, old pet gravestones**: a brewery in a former 1927 animal shelter and ammonia building of the Oostpoort gasworks; the shelter's pet gravestones still line the terrace (a gorilla is said to be buried there). Polderweg, Oostpoort. https://www.frankwandelt.nl/noord-holland/amsterdam-in-10-000-stappen-oost-watergraafsmeer
2. **De Titaantjes**: statue of Bavinck, Hoyer and Koekebakker from Nescio's novella, a quiet literary tribute. Oosterpark. https://www.iamsterdam.com/ontdek/buurten/oost/dingen-om-te-doen-in-de-oosterparkbuurt
3. **De Schreeuw**: contested, much-discussed monument to the murdered Theo van Gogh. Oosterpark, near the Linnaeusstraat side. https://www.iamsterdam.com/ontdek/buurten/oost/dingen-om-te-doen-in-de-oosterparkbuurt
4. **Nationaal Monument Slavernijverleden**: the national slavery memorial (2002), where Keti Koti is held every 1 July. Western part of the Oosterpark. https://amsterdamian.com/see/photos/a-day-in-oud-oost-neighbourhood-amsterdam-tips-by-a-local/
5. **"Domweg gelukkig" poem wall**: J.C. Bloem's "Domweg gelukkig, in de Dapperstraat" painted big on a facade in eight languages, right in the street it names. Dapperstraat. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/
6. **Mike's Badhuistheater**: a 1921 Amsterdam School public bathhouse, saved from demolition in 1985 and now a theatre. Boerhaaveplein 28. https://www.iamsterdam.com/ontdek/buurten/oost/dingen-om-te-doen-in-de-oosterparkbuurt
7. **Bolgewas**: Paul Koning's 1969 bust of a cheerful little figure peeking up out of the grass, easy to miss. Oosterpark lawn. https://www.frankwandelt.nl/noord-holland/amsterdam-in-10-000-stappen-oost-watergraafsmeer
8. **Muziekkoepel Oosterpark**: cast-iron 1908 bandstand by park designer Leonard Springer, the start point of local walks. Oosterpark, north side. https://www.frankwandelt.nl/noord-holland/amsterdam-in-10-000-stappen-oost-watergraafsmeer
9. **Border post in the Oosterpark (grenspaal)**: a hidden boundary marker "with a story", a remnant of the old municipal border. Oosterpark. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/
10. **Transvaalplein, Berlage's first block**: Berlage's square (from 1911) and the first big housing project of the then empty Transvaalbuurt, built by the socialist AWV. Transvaalplein. https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=299577
11. **Joods Monument Transvaalplein**: wall memorial to the Jewish residents deported from the Transvaalbuurt, a district the Nazis made into a "concentration neighbourhood". Transvaalplein. https://www.tracesofwar.com/sights/24371/Joods-Monument-Transvaalplein-Amsterdam.htm
12. **Transvaalbuurt street art**: a cluster of very large murals, including the colourful walls that brighten the grey Platanenweg. Transvaalbuurt, Platanenweg and side streets. https://www.spottedbylocals.com/amsterdam/transvaalbuurt-street-art/
13. **Aldo van Eyck playground with elephants**: one of the roughly 20 surviving Van Eyck playgrounds (of 730), with two elephant figures and a climbing arch. Transvaalbuurt. https://www.frankwandelt.nl/noord-holland/amsterdam-in-10-000-stappen-oost-watergraafsmeer
14. **Former Volkskrant building (Volkshotel, Canvas)**: the old newspaper building on the former "newspaper street", now a hotel with a 7th-floor club, rooftop hot tubs and the underground Doka bar. Wibautstraat 150. https://www.iamsterdam.com/ontdek/buurten/oost/dingen-om-te-doen-in-de-oosterparkbuurt
15. **Framer Framed in the old gasworks**: a decolonial contemporary art space in a building of the former Gasfabriek Oost. Oranje-Vrijstaatkade, Oostpoort. https://www.amsterdamlocalgems.com/neighborhoods/oost/
16. **Mark Bischof's kinetic studio**: a basement workshop where an artist builds kinetic installations from wood and metal, visible below street level. Tweede Oosterparkstraat 218. https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/mark-bischof-studio
17. **Muiderpoort station deportation monument**: memorial to the more than 11,000 Jews deported from this station via Westerbork. Muiderpoortstation, Oosterspoorplein (on the border with the Indische Buurt). https://oost-online.nl/ommetje-indische-buurt/
18. **Villa Mattern**: a bungalow where writer Renate Rubinstein spent her last years, an odd survivor next to the railway. Polderweg. https://oost-online.nl/ommetje-indische-buurt/
19. **Karel Appel's childhood home marker**: a small street artwork marking where the CoBrA painter grew up, above his father's barber shop. Dapperbuurt, near the Dappermarkt. https://voicemap.me/tour/amsterdam/vibrant-amsterdam-east-a-story-of-urban-revival-beyond-the-canals
20. **Generator hostel lecture hall**: a former university lab with an intact old auditorium, now a hostel bar. Mauritskade, Oosterpark edge. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/stadswandeling-amsterdam-prive/wandeling-amsterdam-oost-via-de-molen/

Runners-up: Brandweerkazerne Victor (second-oldest fire station, near Muiderpoortstation); Hartog's bakery with its own mill (Wibautstraat 77); Kastanjeplein's monumental chestnuts; the Oosterpark spreeksteen; the Plein Theater.

## GM15 Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied (incl. Flevopark, Zeeburgerdijk, Cruquius, Java, KNSM, Borneo-Sporenburg)

1. **Museum Perron Oost**: billed as the world's smallest museum, a six-square-metre platform hut by Joep van Lieshout, viewable 24/7, with an audio walk. Cruquiusweg 11. https://www.iamsterdam.com/zien-en-doen/natuur-en-actief/routes/wandelroute-door-het-oostelijk-havengebied
2. **Java-eiland letter bridges and canals**: nine footbridges designed from the Azart alphabet over small canals (Lamongracht and others) that mimic the canal belt in miniature. Java-eiland. https://www.iamsterdam.com/zien-en-doen/natuur-en-actief/routes/wandelroute-door-het-oostelijk-havengebied
3. **Distilleerderij 't Nieuwe Diep**: a genever distillery since 2010 in a white former pumping station, with an orchard and terrace on the water. Flevopark. https://www.iamsterdam.com/ontdek/buurten/oost/verborgen-parels
4. **Joodse begraafplaats Zeeburg**: an old Jewish cemetery (from 1714) on the edge of the Flevopark, a striking, little-known history site. Flevopark, west entrance. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/
5. **Het Badhuis, Javaplein**: the last municipal bathhouse built in Amsterdam (opened 1942, under occupation), later a Hindu temple and thrift shop, now a café with the original tiles. Javaplein. https://ommetjemettom.substack.com/p/de-verhalen-achter-de-indische-buurt
6. **Loods 6 and the harbour crane**: the 1950s KNSM passenger terminal (until 1979) with the Kompaszaal, and an original harbour crane across the water. KNSM-laan 143. https://www.iamsterdam.com/zien-en-doen/natuur-en-actief/routes/wandelroute-door-het-oostelijk-havengebied
7. **Het Angstige Konijn**: a huge, slightly unsettling rabbit sculpture in the middle of the Flevopark. Flevopark. https://www.wandelvrouw.nl/de-watergraafsmeerroute/
8. **Herberg Zeeburg**: the oldest building in the Indische Buurt, an old inn on the Zeeburgerdijk (a dike older than Amsterdam), renovated in 2023. Zeeburgerdijk. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/
9. **Indië 1**: a "Hansel and Gretel" monumental mini-village of early Eigen Haard workers' houses. Indische Buurt, near Borneostraat. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/stadswandeling-amsterdam-prive/wandeling-amsterdam-oost-via-de-molen/
10. **Pythonbrug**: the wavy red snake bridge between Sporenburg and Borneo, winner of an international footbridge award. Spoorwegbassin. https://www.iamsterdam.com/uit/agenda/attracties-en-bezienswaardigheden/tours/themawandeling-het-oostelijk-havengebied
11. **Scheepstimmermanstraat**: a street of narrow private houses, each by a different architect, a showcase of self-commissioned design. Borneo-eiland. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/
12. **NedPho-Koepel**: the domed former Gerardus Majellakerk (1925), saved and now the home of the Nederlands Philharmonisch Orkest. Ambonplein. https://oost-online.nl/ommetje-indische-buurt/
13. **Lloyd Hotel**: built in 1921 as a hotel for emigrants (many Eastern European Jews heading to South America), later a prison and youth detention centre, now a hotel. Oostelijke Handelskade 34. https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna7261072
14. **The Whale**: a zinc-clad apartment block whose tilted shape rises above Sporenburg. Sporenburg. https://www.iamsterdam.com/uit/agenda/attracties-en-bezienswaardigheden/tours/themawandeling-het-oostelijk-havengebied
15. **Berlage blocks**: three large worker-housing blocks by Berlage, with courtyards; residents rearranged the tiny layouts over a century. Molukkenstraat and Langkatstraat. https://ommetjemettom.substack.com/p/de-verhalen-achter-de-indische-buurt
16. **Timorplein ambachtsschool (Studio/K)**: a 1912 trade school, now a student-run venue, hostel and Cybersoek. Timorplein. https://oost-online.nl/ommetje-indische-buurt/
17. **Johan Cruijff tram shelter**: a big Cruijff street-art portrait by StreetArt Frankey on the tram 14 shelter. Javaplein. https://oost-online.nl/ommetje-indische-buurt/
18. **Barcelonaplein and Piraeus**: KNSM's square and Hans Kollhoff's massive Piraeus block, which swallowed an old harbour building. KNSM-eiland. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/
19. **De Nieuwe KHL**: the former coffee house of the Koninklijke Hollandsche Lloyd shipping line, now a stage and café. Oostelijke Handelskade 44. https://www.iamsterdam.com/ontdek/buurten/oostelijk-havengebied/verborgen-parels
20. **Cruquius warehouses and the old Veemarkt coffee house**: surviving cattle-market and harbour buildings around Brouwerij Crux. Cruquiuseiland, Veemarkt. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/

Runners-up: De Smaragd (2016 Zuiderkerk Prize, Balistraat); Borneohof with turret (Javaplein); the Elthetokerk (Javastraat); the "Samen zijn wij Oost" tile artwork under the railway viaduct; Hotel Jakarta's subtropical garden; Borneo Architectuurcentrum; "Voor de bijen" beehive tables (KNSM); the villa on the water by the Flevopark; Ceramplein (4 May ceremony); De Meevaart.

## GM16 Watergraafsmeer (incl. Betondorp, Frankendael, Middenmeer, Science Park)

1. **Museum Tot Zover**: the national funeral museum in a former foreman's house, said to be the only museum on a listed cemetery. Kruislaan 124. https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/museum-tot-zover
2. **De Nieuwe Ooster**: Amsterdam's largest cemetery, by Leonard Springer, which doubles as an arboretum; you can see Surinamese funeral processions with music. Kruislaan 126. https://www.wandelvrouw.nl/de-watergraafsmeerroute/
3. **Johan Cruijff's childhood home**: the corner house where Cruijff grew up, near the old Ajax grounds. Akkerstraat 32, Betondorp. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/
4. **Betondorp, De Brink**: an eight-sided village square by Dick Greiner, with a workers' reading room linked by a pergola, at the heart of the 1920s concrete experiment. De Brink. https://www.davides.nl/wandeling-door-het-groene-betondorp-in-amsterdam/
5. **Former Stadskwekerij (De Kas) and its chimneys**: the 1927 city nursery greenhouse, 5 metres below sea level, where the boiler house hid resistance weapons; now restaurant De Kas. Kamerlingh Onneslaan, Park Frankendael. https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/langs-de-gulden-middenweg
6. **Linnaeushof**: Kropholler's almost medieval Catholic court with its Hofkerk, where Nescio lived until 1956. Linnaeushof, Middenmeer. https://www.vrijeacademie.nl/ons-aanbod/wandeling-in-de-watergraafsmeer
7. **Huize Frankendael**: the only surviving country house of about 80 in the polder, with a romantic garden; its park once had a hermitage with a wooden hermit. Middenweg 72. https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/langs-de-gulden-middenweg
8. **Tuindorp Frankendael (Jeruzalem)**: the first postwar district made a national monument, low white houses by Van Eesteren, Mart Stam and Mien Ruys, with a treehouse book swap. East of Park Frankendael. https://www.davides.nl/wandeling-door-het-groene-betondorp-in-amsterdam/
9. **Rechthuis Watergraafsmeer**: the 1777 polder courthouse, with dungeons in the cellars and the polder's swan arms. Middenweg 2, by the Oetewalerbrug. https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/langs-de-gulden-middenweg
10. **Het Huis op de Dijk (old smithy)**: a former blacksmith's workshop on the old ring dike. Ringdijk. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/
11. **Esplanade de Meer and the Ajax centre spot**: the former Ajax stadium site (until 1996), now housing, keeping the old centre spot and four element carvings. Park de Meer. https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/langs-de-gulden-middenweg
12. **Van het Reve hofje and Eetcafé De Avonden**: the court where Gerard and Karel van het Reve grew up, and a café with a passage from "De Avonden" on its facade. Ploegstraat 89, Betondorp. https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/langs-de-gulden-middenweg
13. **Ringdijk 5, the cholera house**: a small house built in 1832 as an emergency hospital during a cholera epidemic. Ringdijk 5. https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/langs-de-gulden-middenweg
14. **Hildo Krop school sculptures**: the city sculptor's work on the facades of two Betondorp schools. Zuivelplein and Huismanshof. https://www.davides.nl/wandeling-door-het-groene-betondorp-in-amsterdam/
15. **Amstelstation hall**: a national monument station with large murals in its hall. Julianaplein. https://www.davides.nl/wandeling-door-het-groene-betondorp-in-amsterdam/
16. **Oetewalerbrug**: the former border bridge between Amsterdam and the Watergraafsmeer (until 1921), where Napoleon received the city keys in 1811. Linnaeusstraat at the ringvaart. https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/langs-de-gulden-middenweg
17. **Christiaan Huygensplein, the first Ajax stadium**: site of Ajax's wooden stadium (1911 to 1934); the nearby streets are named after foreign stadiums where Ajax won (Wembleylaan). Middenmeer. https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/langs-de-gulden-middenweg
18. **Ringdijk and the Ringdijktuinen**: the old ring dike with small allotment gardens and houses on the water along the Ooster Ringvaart. Ringdijk. https://heerenmakelaars.nl/en/welke-verborgen-pareltjes-vind-je-in-de-watergraafsmeer/
19. **Science Park windowless "mega data tower"**: the blank data centre tower among the institutes, a surprising landmark of the internet age. Science Park. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/fietstour-met-gids-amsterdam/rondleiding-op-ijburg/
20. **Telefoonkantoor by Albert Boeken**: an Amsterdam School telephone exchange with Hildo Krop sculptures. Middenmeer. https://www.vrijeacademie.nl/ons-aanbod/wandeling-in-de-watergraafsmeer

Runners-up: Voltaplein's atom statue; the former Gooische Stoomtram depot (Middenweg, 1881); the steel flood sluices in the Kruislaan viaduct; the Arie Keppler plaque (Landbouwstraat); the Lamarckhof; the Jaap Edenbaan; Park de Meer's grass snakes and kingfishers.

## GM17 IJburg, Zeeburgereiland

1. **Diemerpark, former toxic dump**: a former landfill full of waste oil, pesticides and hospital waste, now a park twice the size of the Vondelpark with grass snakes, foxes and a hidden beach. Diemerpark. https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/288299/natuurlijk-noord-holland-van-gifbelt-naar-natuur-in-het-diemerpark
2. **Steigereiland floating homes**: a colourful neighbourhood of floating villas, built outside the usual design rules. Steigereiland-Noord (Waterbuurt). https://www.frankwandelt.nl/noord-holland/vinex-wandeling-amsterdamse-havens-ijburg
3. **Tom Claassen's floating hippo**: a life-size rubber hippo floating in a small lock, soft enough to act as a fender. Near the Groenetunnel, IJburg. https://www.frankwandelt.nl/noord-holland/vinex-wandeling-amsterdamse-havens-ijburg
4. **"Space To Take Place", the longest bench**: a red bench about 100 metres long, facing the IJmeer and Durgerdam. Near Bert Haanstrakade. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/fietstour-met-gids-amsterdam/rondleiding-op-ijburg/
5. **The Zeeburgereiland silos**: three former sewage-works silos being converted, with the Rialto cinema and a roof park 25 metres up. Sportheldenbuurt, Zeeburgereiland. https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/353514/silos-zeeburgereiland-krijgen-vorm-over-een-jaar-gym-bios-en-terrassen-met-uitzicht
6. **Sluishuis**: BIG and Barcode's stepped block hanging over the water, with a public roof route and pier. Entrance to Steigereiland. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/fietstour-met-gids-amsterdam/rondleiding-op-ijburg/
7. **Urban Sport Zone skatepark**: the largest skatepark in the Netherlands (3,100 m2), decorated with tiles, under the Faas Wilkesstraat. Zeeburgereiland. https://www.amsterdamnow.com/en/lifestyle/grootste-skatepark-van-nederland-op-zeeburgereiland/
8. **Strand IJburg**: Amsterdam's "real beach", with surfing on the IJmeer. Pampuslaan 505. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/
9. **Self-built houses and "het gele huis"**: a street of all-different self-build plots, including VMX's yellow house, the most photographed house on IJburg. Steigereiland-Zuid, Jan Olphert Vaillantlaan. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/fietstour-met-gids-amsterdam/rondleiding-op-ijburg/
10. **Nesciobrug**: once the longest cyclist and foot bridge in the Netherlands and the steepest in Amsterdam, over the Amsterdam-Rijnkanaal to the Diemerpark. IJburg to Diemerpark. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/fietstour-met-gids-amsterdam/rondleiding-op-ijburg/
11. **Baggerbeest**: a cultural hub built on dredged IJ sludge, with a bar and studios. Sluisbuurt, Zeeburgereiland. https://www.iamsterdam.com/zien-en-doen/natuur-en-actief/routes/wandelroute-door-ijburg
12. **Diemerzeedijk**: the sea dike where Nescio walked and Rembrandt is said to have drawn, with views of the IJburg skyline. Diemerzeedijk. https://www.frankwandelt.nl/noord-holland/vinex-wandeling-amsterdamse-havens-ijburg
13. **Houses on stilts by the Kadegebouw**: pile dwellings reached by walkways. Steigereiland. https://www.frankwandelt.nl/noord-holland/vinex-wandeling-amsterdamse-havens-ijburg
14. **Bert Haanstrakade**: a waterfront "city boulevard" of colourful self-built houses, none alike. Haveneiland. https://www.iamsterdam.com/zien-en-doen/natuur-en-actief/routes/wandelroute-door-ijburg
15. **Vondice, a one-room hotel in a sludge pumping station**: a former sludge pumping station turned into a single-room hotel. Zeeburgereiland. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/fietstour-met-gids-amsterdam/rondleiding-op-ijburg/
16. **De Witte Kaap**: a snow-white landmark block inspired by Museum 't Schip, at the edge of Haveneiland. Haveneiland-Oost/Centrumeiland. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/fietstour-met-gids-amsterdam/rondleiding-op-ijburg/
17. **Centrumeiland self-build island**: a whole island of self-built houses, wood to concrete. Centrumeiland. https://amsterdamlokaal.nl/ijburg/
18. **Enneüs Heermabrug ("BH-brug")**: the gateway bridge to IJburg, its arches nicknamed the bra bridge by locals. Zeeburgereiland to Steigereiland. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/fietstour-met-gids-amsterdam/rondleiding-op-ijburg/
19. **Blijburg (gone) / Lolaland**: the legendary temporary beach club and festival site, displaced as the islands were built; a temporary use holds the spot now. Centrumeiland edge. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/fietstour-met-gids-amsterdam/rondleiding-op-ijburg/
20. **Amsterdam 750 flag**: a giant flag with the city arms raised for the 750th anniversary, by the skatepark. Zeeburgereiland. https://www.lexandthecity.nl/28-verborgen-pareltjes-van-amsterdam-oost/

Runners-up: Theater Vrijburcht (40 seats, IJburg's only professional music theatre); Factor IJ art library (4,000+ works); Camping Zeeburg (kingfishers, petting farm); Haven IJburg with the Pampus ferry and the "Jonas" building; Blok 14 floating over a canal; Kleine Rieteiland's "gold coast"; Four Elements eco hotel.

## Notes on the sources

- Reddit (r/Amsterdam) searches returned no usable threads. The Parool and AT5 turned up no list-style pages through search, so NH Nieuws stands in for local news.
- Spotted by Locals and hmdb are behind Cloudflare and were not read directly. Those items rest on search snippets.
- Some items are inferred from a single source, not confirmed in person: GM14 #9 (border post), #19 (Appel marker), #20 (Generator hall); GM15 #20; GM16 #10, #19 and #20; GM17 #15 and #20. Check these before adding them.
- Lex and the City lists an "Outersdorp/Oetewaler molen" windmill in the Flevopark. No other source confirms it, so it is left out.
