## On-site checks the brief asked for

- **218 horse-tram depot: survives.** The main corner building at Linnaeusstraat 30 (corner Derde Oosterparkstraat) still stands; nl.wikipedia says most of the complex was demolished in 1986 and the main building is now a yoga studio. Confirmed by OSM (Equal, yoga, node 1578734049), Commons photos of August 2018 with the Equal sign, and current Urban Sports Club / ClassPass listings. Not a listed monument. Date conflict: 1884 (nl.wikipedia Remise Linnaeusstraat), 1893 (list of works in nl.wikipedia Abraham Salm), 1886 (BAG construction year). Attribute set to "1880s"; the body names the conflict.
- **221 brewery stables: survive, converted.** Stedenbouw (25 August 2025): Salm 1912, converted summer 2024 to April 2025 by up architecture (Anne Boonstra) into offices for Rewire, cast-iron columns with tethering rings, troughs and the hayloft roof kept. nl.wikipedia Mauritskade 55 GH: not a monument because of the conversion. Visible only from outside, behind the newer hall at Mauritskade 55; I could not confirm how much shows from the street, so the Look for line is cautious.
- **235 Spreeksteen: still there, use unclear.** Commons photos of May 2018 and February 2020; iamsterdam still announces the Sunday slot; no 2025 or 2026 report of anyone speaking found. The text says so plainly.
- 209 and 224 belong to other slices.

## Corrections to the scout list

- **202:** Leendert Pop was a diamond worker ("diamantbewerker en eigenwerkmaker", Joods Monument / stadsdeel press release; NH Nieuws), not a kettle mender. Ages on the back plaque run from 32 (Sientje Roodveldt) to 88, not 53 to 88. The Joods Monument notes one resident missing from the plinth (Hendrina Hamburger-van Rijn, died 11 January 1941); mentioned factually. The artist is credited as Louise Schiffmacher-van Teylingen (4en5mei: "Louise Schiffmacher van Teylingen"; nl.wikipedia: Louise van Teylingen).
- **214:** Weesperzijde 99 was the fire brigade's head office 1987–2009 and is now flats (nl.wikipedia Brandweer Amsterdam-Amstelland), not an "old fire station" in 1957; a Commons caption calls it the former Utermöhlen factory, not used. The stone was photographed in place in October 2019.
- **222:** Arcam says 1,200 people; nl.wikipedia Torontobrug says the 1965 plan was for 900. Used 1,200 (built figure). Added the Heineken kidnapping (9 November 1983) from nl.wikipedia Brug 325. Pin is nl.wikipedia's point for bridge 325 (approximate, on the tunnel), confirmed in borough Oost.
- **223:** nothing about the shelter is visible or signposted at Wibautstraat itself (Arcam only describes Weesperplein's doors). The merge says so and points to the visible sliding door and air lock at the Rhijnspoorplein entrance of Weesperplein (Centrum, one stop on). Capacity of up to 3,000 at Wibautstraat is from en.wikipedia only.
- **226: the school has been renamed.** By 2020 it is Montessori Lyceum Oostpoort (AHH interior project 2020–2021; nl.wikipedia Montessori Scholengemeenschap Amsterdam: "voorheen Montessori College Oost"), with havo as well as vmbo. Title kept on the building's architectural name, "Hertzberger's Montessori College Oost"; body gives the current name. The live AHH project pages are gone (404); I used Wayback copies of 2024. Ranked Okay, not the scout's Nice: everything that makes it remarkable (the hall, the tribune stairs, the Rooskens tapestry, the plaque) is inside a working school and cannot be visited. Raise to Nice if the user values the architecture itself.
- **244: not the 1838 type.** The two OSM nodes (10967570465 by the Nesciobrug, mapped 2023; 14226913441 by Fort Diemerdam, mapped September 2026) are on today's border with Diemen, with the inscription "Kom der Gemeente Amsterdam". No photo found, so material and shape are unknown; nhpalen.blogspot.com shows a cast-iron post on the Diemen border. The merge says they are not part of the Oosterpark pair and does not describe their look. Both are 3 to 6 km from the Oosterpark, so a Look for line there is a stretch; they could become a small place of their own if someone photographs them.
