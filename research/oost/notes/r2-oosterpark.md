## Corrections to the scout list

- **101 is not a banpaal.** The two columns in the Oosterpark are 1838 *limiet-* or *kompalen* (one says "Kom der Gemeente Amsterdam" with an arrow). The top of the 1624 Spaarndammerdijk banpaal that the Kunstwacht text describes did stand beside them at the Beatrixoord, but it was moved to the Spaarnwouderdijk (Spaarnwoude, near the pumping station) in 2006 (nl.wikipedia Banpaal Spaarnwouderdijk; nhpalen.blogspot.com; SPQA blog 2012). The city's public art text (id 1211) is outdated. Ranked Okay, not Nice: it is a curiosity, and the Rembrandt banpaal is no longer here. The banpaal itself is outside Oost (Westpoort area) and could be a place for the West map.
- **102: Albert is not buried at the stone.** Geheugen van Oost (2024, from Delpher) quotes Water's 1923 interview: the dog was buried in the Burgerziekenhuis back garden; melandlangeveld.com says he was buried "somewhere in the park". Body follows the contemporary interview. Hat vs cap: sources differ (hoed / pet); text says "hats".
- **106:** Kunstwacht says 188 on board and 10 survivors; nl.wikipedia says 187 on board (178 passengers, 9 crew), 176 dead, 11 survivors. Used Wikipedia. The commemoration still takes place: oost-online.nl, 9 June 2026 (flowers and drawings at the monument at 19.00 on 7 June 2026, alongside a Real Sranang event at Sportpark Middenmeer). The 2014 NOS report of the end of the yearly commemoration is outdated.
- **123:** the Kunstwacht pin (52.362, 4.924922) is about 350 m from Dapperstraat 7; used the BAG address point. Also: the Appel family shop was at no. 17, not 7 (nl.wikipedia, citing NH Nieuws); told in the place.
- **104:** year 1992 from nl.wikipedia (Kunstwacht undated).

## Gone / left out

- **108 Geloof, Hoop en Liefde (Nicholas Pope, 1997): destroyed on 25 November 2022.** oost-online.nl (24 Nov 2022): the figures were moved in 2017 from the roof of the former KIT training centre by the Muiderkerk tower to the KIT's inner garden, blew over in a 2020 storm, were declared a total loss and destroyed with the artist's consent. Nothing remains at either spot, so it fails the high bar for a gone place. Suggestion: one sentence in round 1 "Read the facade of the Colonial Institute" (not edited, not my merge target), as Pope's trio answered the three female allegories over the KIT entrance.
