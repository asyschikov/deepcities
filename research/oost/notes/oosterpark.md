# Oosterpark slice: notes

33 places: 26 built from the 45 monument records (44 numbers; 200707 has two records), 7 scouted non-monument places (3 Muiderpoort deportation memorial, 8 Slavery Monument and Keti Koti, 5 De Schreeuw and the Van Gogh murder, 14 Noch Einmal, 16 Flowers, 21 Het begon met een dubbeltje, 37 Dappermarkt). Scouted items 4 (43 kinderen, Here Comes The Sun), 12 (KIT facade) and 15 (U Bevindt Zich Hier) are folded into monument places.

Ranks: 1 Amazing (National Slavery Monument and Keti Koti), 20 Nice, 12 Okay. No gone places (the demolished Bonifatiuskerk, Muiderkerk nave and three retort halls are told inside other places).

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- **Wild & Hardebeck**: the city register says the Jewish owners were deported and the manager Van de Bos ran the firm until closure in the 1970s; amsterdamopdekaart says the firm went bankrupt in 1927 and restarted as N.V. Hardebeck's Gordijnenfabriek, ending about 1978. Both told; the "behoudens goedkeuring" anecdote is attributed to the register as tradition (volgens overlevering). The register also spells the architect "C. Tenentij jr."; amsterdamopdekaart "Carel Tenenti (di Tenenti)". Used Tenenti.
- **Noch Einmal and Haarlem**: stadscuratorium says it was meant as a gift to Haarlem and was refused after resistance from Haarlemmers; nl.wikipedia only says Ymere could not place it after the Amersfoort show. Text says the Haarlem plan "fell through" without blaming anyone.
- **Het begon met een dubbeltje pin**: OSM gives 52.361741, 4.917055 (by the houses, used); buitenkunstamsterdam gives 52.360853, 4.912176, which is 400 m west at the Rhijnspoorplein and looks wrong.
- **Muiderpoort deportation memorial pin is estimated** (52.36045, 4.93135) on the Oosterspoorplein in front of the station; no source gives exact coordinates. Needs an on-site check.
- **Dappermarkt pin** is on the Dapperstraat at no. 74, roughly midway; the market runs the whole street.
- **Oosterpark 87-88**: city register says built 1882 (drawings), the slice year field says 1902; used 1882.
- **Witsenhuis**: RCE says Witsen lived there from 1890, amsterdamopdekaart says 1891–93 and 1904–1923; used the latter. Built 1884 per RCE, still under construction in 1886 per amsterdamopdekaart; built given as 1884–1886.
- **Otter-Knoll architect**: city data say Jan de Looy, amsterdamopdekaart "Van Looy (1852-1911)". Same person presumably; used Jan de Looy.
- **Muziekkoepel**: city register dates it to 1916 (possibly replacing an earlier dome); amsterdamopdekaart says it was approved in 1907 and built "a few years later". Both mentioned.
- **Raad van Arbeid**: could not confirm whether the HvA "Singelgrachtgebouw" (OIII, 2008) is this building or a new building next to it, so the current user is not named. Commons has photos of figures on the building (one titled as by Hildo Krop); body only says "stone figures", artist not named.
- **Station Muiderpoort architects**: nl.wikipedia adds Johannes Leupen of the city; registers name only Schelling. Used Schelling.
- **U Bevindt Zich Hier**: date 2019 from the scout; commissioned 2008 as the closing work of "Erfenis van de 20ste Eeuw". Exact completion year not confirmed from a primary source.
- **Slavery museum**: nl.wikipedia still says opening 2030; the delay to 2033 and the search for a temporary location come from news of 22 October 2026 (no Dutch source with that wording in nl.sources).
- **Theo van Gogh**: no marker exists at the murder spot; a citizens' petition on petities.nl asks for a commemorative stone. A yearly informal commemoration is held on 2 November on the Linnaeusstraat. Not given its own pin.
- **43 kinderen** is in the school courtyard; access is limited to school hours and permission.
- **Karel Appel's Flowers**: the scout claimed a stolen flower was recast in Italy; not confirmed, left out.
- **Barmat house**: no nl.wikipedia article; Dutch text spliced from RCE and the amsterdamopdekaart blog.

## Walking route ideas

- **Oosterpark loop (about 2.5 km)**: Rhijnspoorplein (Raad van Arbeid, ATO flats) > Amstel brewery office > Talmud Tora school > Boerhaaveplein (bathhouse, curtain factory) > Blasiusstraat 124 > midwives' school > 's-Gravesandeplein and Oosterparkkerk > Slavery Monument > into the park (music dome, De Schreeuw) > south side (Barmat, Valkenburg, Witsenhuis, 87-88) > Eikenplein > back along the north side (KIT facade, Metis, labs, open-air school, Dubbeltjespanden, St. Elisabethgesticht, Mauritskade 23-24).
- **Jewish Oost and the war (about 2 km)**: Mauritskade 24 (Joodse Kweekschool) > Talmud Tora school > Wild & Hardebeck > Blasiusstraat 124 > Metis (43 kinderen) > Muiderpoort memorial. Pairs with the Transvaalbuurt memorials in the indische slice.
- **Colonial and Surinamese Oost**: KIT facade > Slavery Monument > Dappermarkt > Suriname Museum on the Zeeburgerdijk.
- **Muiderpoort to the gasworks**: Dappermarkt > Pontanusschool > Muiderpoortstation (Campendonk window, Hünd mosaic, Flowers, memorial) > Oostergasfabriek (Sportfondsenbad, U Bevindt Zich Hier) > Noch Einmal on the Polderweg.
