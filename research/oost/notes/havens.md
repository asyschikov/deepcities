## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Cattle market closing year: city register texts say market and abattoir closed in 1980; nl.wikipedia says 1974; the scout says abattoir to about 1985. Text says "around 1980 (some sources say 1974)".
- Register record fields for Cruquiusweg 3 and 9 give the client as "Christelijke School Vereeniging Aeneas Mackay", which is clearly a data error (they are Publieke Werken buildings for the entrepot). Not used. Function and dates from amsterdamopdekaart.nl (MIP based): wachtlokaal 1901, volkskoffiehuis 1902. nl.wikipedia (Brug 352) names Hendrik Leguyt as designer of the timber-framed buildings; the register says only Publieke Werken. Whether the two buildings are still in hospitality use in November 2026 was not checked tenant by tenant (Google's AI overview claimed Gare de l'Est is closed; unconfirmed), so the text only says they have served food and drink since they were built.
- "Afrekenlokalen" in the waiting room: what was settled there (duties, wages) is not stated in the source; the text does not guess.
- Loods 6 date: register c. 1925 (architect "onbekend"); nl.wikipedia says rebuilt c. 1920 by J.G. and A.D. van Gendt; nl.wikipedia (Kraan 2868) says 1922. Built field "c. 1920–1925". The harbour-worker sculptures at the entrance: register says origin unknown; nl.wikipedia says Hildo Krop, from the KHL gate, here since 1998. Text says "said to be".
- KNSM canteen date: register record field 1954, register text 1959–1960. Used 1959–1960.
- KNSM staff houses address: record says Oostelijke Handelskade 44-66, the text 46-66 (44 is the Lloyd coffee house). Used 46-66.
- Lloyd Hotel dates: register 1917–1920, nl.wikipedia designed 1918 and completed 1 June 1921. Complex built field 1915–1923 covers office (1915–1917) to disinfection building (1920–1923).
- Pakhuis Afrika: a Commons caption (Fred Romero) credits it to Van Lookeren Campagne 1883–85, which is the Azië data; the register (1915, A.J. Joling) is followed.
- Vettewinkel: the 2012 register text says the building was still in use by Sigma Coatings; the IAA Architecten project page (search snippet only, site did not load) says the factory was there 1934 to 2008 and Cruquius was redeveloped from 2013. Current use of the listed building not confirmed; the text says only that it was kept as a landmark.
- Hydraulic power station: amsterdamopdekaart (2017) says "nu Club Panama". Current operator not checked, so the text says it "has since served as a nightclub and event venue".
- Kraan 2868: still bookable as a short-stay apartment (Numa/YAYS listings with 2026 reviews), but an undated Funda business listing offers Surinamekade 34 for sale. Not mentioned in the text.
- Listed year for the cattle market place: the 14 records were designated 2012–2014; field set to 2014. Entrepot place: 2013–2014; field set to 2013.
- Graft pin: the five works are not in OSM; the pin is the single coordinate nl.wikipedia gives for the group (52.369064, 4.953236, near the Archiefstraat), not a verified individual sculpture. Exact positions of Graft 1-5 still need a look (Graft 3 stands in the verge of the Cruquiusweg in front of the hotel "the niu", per the Commons photo).
- Drijvende Tuinen pin: nl.wikipedia's coordinate on the Zeeburgerkade side of the Entrepothaven (52.369531, 4.940856). Two Bing-traced garden polygons in OSM at 4.923-4.924 turned out to be in the Oostenburgervaart (Centrum), probably other rafts; not used. The Panamatuin at the Panamakade is not pinned separately.
- Dutch text: all Dutch is spliced from the registers, nl.wikipedia, amsterdamopdekaart.nl, Stadscuratorium and museumperronoost.nl. Short linking sentences written in Dutch where sources gave only facts (for example the borough line in Het Licht van Jan, the "Let op" lists for artworks).
