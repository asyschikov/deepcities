- The brief calls the Spaklerweg group "waterworks and water tower". The records are the **Zuidergasfabriek** (gasworks) complex 526947; the water tower is the gasworks' tower. Written as gasworks.
- The brief mentions the Omval; no Omval monument is in the slice file. The Blooker cocoa factory at the Omval is mentioned only in the Weesperzijde 4-15 place.
- Weesperzijde ensembles split by status and stretch: 4-15 all municipal (2017), 16-34 all national (2003). De IJsbreker (23), Weesperzijde 39-40 (one of the oldest buildings in Oost) and the villa at no. 70 pulled out as their own places.
- The two farms are 400 m apart; merged as "the last farms" because the city register frames Altoos Zorg exactly that way. Could be split if the coordinator prefers.
- Blasiusstraat 13-19 (1954 garage) and Eerste Boerhaavestraat 1-9 put in the Swammerdambuurt ensemble (both in the same few blocks).

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- **Architect attributions on the Weesperzijde** differ between sources. Slice/city data give 526989 (16-19) to J. van Olst, amsterdamopdekaart to W.D. Hendriks & L.H. Nijhuis (1879); left unnamed. For 526999 (33-34) the RCE text gives Henkenhaf & Ebert for no. 33 (for Krasnapolsky) and says no. 34 was added 1886-87; the city register lists Ed. Cuypers, amsterdamopdekaart gives no. 34 to Cuypers. Text names Henkenhaf & Ebert for no. 33 only. Slice data also give 526998 (29-31) to Henkenhaf & Ebert; RCE text names no architect; not stated.
- Ruyschstraat 11-13 (200541): city register says architect G. ter Braake; amsterdamopdekaart says designer Yme Bijvoets with Ter Braake as client. No architect named in the place.
- Weesperzijde 70: city register says 1885, amsterdamopdekaart 1883; built given as 1883–1885.
- Klarenbeek: "1604" is widely quoted (Historisch Amstelland, city data year field), but RCE says the present look is 19th-century. Body says so; built given as 18th–19th century for the merged place.
- Kohnstammhuis: RCE says the tax service left at the end of the 1990s, nl.wikipedia says 1994 and HvA moved in 1999. Body says "in the 1990s".
- Wibautstraat newspaper street: the scout said the Trouw building was "said to be a rijksmonument since 2009". Not found in the national register and not in this slice; treated as not listed. A 2013/2014 zoning document says the Parool tower and Trouw building were to be kept and renovated, with a student hotel; I did not confirm the exact current occupants, so the text stays general.
- Bajeskwartier: AM's project page claims six towers are kept; nl.wikipedia and all other sources say five of six were demolished and one is kept. Used five of six. Completion of the whole district planned for about 2028. Het Nieuwe Bajesdorp is finished (2025 Nieuwbouwprijs finalist, did not win; the scout's "no 2025 confirmation" is resolved).
- De Buurtoptimist: sources only say "on a bench at the waterside of the Weesperzijde" and "Weesperzijde 12". **Pin is estimated** on the quay opposite no. 12 (52.35822, 4.906); needs an on-site check. Unveiling date not found (2023).
- IJsbreker music centre: amsterdamopdekaart says it grew from 1979, nl.wikipedia says the Wolffs rented from 1979 and the first concerts were in 1980. Both used consistently.
- Category choices: De IJsbreker as hidden-history (not food-drink; the place is about the building, the icebreaker and the music centre). Farms as green (care farm with garden and terrace).

## Walking route ideas

1. **Weesperzijde riverfront** (about 1.5 km): Former tax office, Wibautstraat 2 → Weesperzijde 4-15 → De Buurtoptimist → Weesperzijde 16-34 → De IJsbreker → Swammerdambuurt and its four-cornered square → Weesperzijde 39-40 → Former LTS Patrimonium → Wibautstraat, the newspaper street → Villa Weesperzijde 70 → Former Veerkamp lingerie factory. Theme: how the city reached the Amstel in the 1880s, and the post-war Wibautstraat behind it.
2. **Along the Amstel to the farms** (cycle, about 4 km): Villa Weesperzijde 70 → Zuidergasfabriek water tower and gasworks houses → Bajeskwartier and Bajesdorp → Klarenbeek and Altoos Zorg. Theme: industry, prison and polder on the edge of the city; end with lunch at the care farm.
