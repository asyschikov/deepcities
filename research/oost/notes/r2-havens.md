## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Keesje cross: Kunstwacht says two resistance men put it up (placement 1954); keesjebrijdeherdenking.nl says neighbours (buurtbewoners) did, poem by baker J. Schagen ("Rijmelaar"). Text follows the committee. Where he was shot: Kunstwacht says the Sporenburg yard, nl.wikipedia and the committee say the Rietlanden near the present Piet Hein tunnel entrance; text follows the latter.
- Stenen gedicht date: oost-online and Straatpoëzie 2000, Commons caption 2001. Used 2000. Kunstwacht address "Panamalaan 11", Straatpoëzie "Panamalaan 52"; address given as the corner of Panamalaan and Borneolaan.
- Fortuna: Kunstwacht 1950 (gift), nl.wikipedia mounted 1951 on a tower with a zodiac clock. nl.wikipedia puts the statue in Amsterdam-Centrum; the city's stadsdelen layer puts the pin in Oost.
- Scheepstimmermanstraat height limit: Arcam 9.2 m, MVRDV 9.5 m envelope. Used Arcam. Years: Grokipedia snippet 1996-2000 (not used as a source), MVRDV 1996-1999, Arcam 1999; built field 1996–2000.
- The Whale: e-architect 1995-2000, Arcam 2000.
- Katsenarie: Kunstwacht/Art@Site give the daycare name Oehoeboeroe; current operator not checked.
- Barcelona: Kunstwacht pin of the gate used; nl.wikipedia says the gate is eight storeys high.
- Wokkel: Kunstwacht credits W.F. (Wim) Snieder; OSM and the Commons caption say Jan Willem Snieder (his son, who continued the firm). Followed Kunstwacht and nl.wikipedia (Wim).

## Route idea

Docklands art and housing walk: Katsenarie, Museum Perron Oost (Gyroscoop, Veemarkt paving), Stenen gedicht, Keesje Brijde's cross, The Whale, Python Bridge, Scheepstimmermanstraat, then back over to Java-eiland canals and bridges, Barcelona, and Vrouwe Fortuna on the way out.
