## Conflicts

- Frankendael date: sources give 1659/1660 (hofstede), 1695 (Balde's purchase), about 1700, 1719 and 1733 (Wikipedia). Body says "about 1700" and notes the spread. The huizefrankendael.nl page dates Bacchus and Ceres to 1714 by Van Logteren; the register says about 1700 and gives 1714 to the fountain. Followed the register.
- Emmakerk listing: register date 1997-12-08, Wikipedia says April 1998. Used 1997.
- Rechthuis and Napoleon 1811: Wikipedia says the entry took place opposite the building; Arcam suggests the keys were handed over here. Body says only that he entered the city here and the dome is in Van Bree's painting.
- Dreibholz: city register says built 1914; tender 1915. Used 1914-1915.
- Anna Hoeve: rebuilt 1900 (register) vs 1901 elsewhere. Used 1900.
- Banketbakkersvakschool: current user Orion College Amstel (since 2019) from amsterdamopdekaart; not checked on site.
- Christus Koningkerk: hotel opening year (2024) from Vrienden van Watergraafsmeer; theatre opened November 2025 (Oost-online).
