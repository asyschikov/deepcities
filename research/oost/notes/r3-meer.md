## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- HEDW numbers: 4en5mei and nl.wikipedia say 169 HEDW and 67 WV victims (162 HEDW named, 7 not named); plus 33 names in 2024 (21 HEDW, 12 WV). Text says "more than 260" on the summary (236 + 33 = 269) and 162 named HEDW victims in the body.
- Monument move: 4en5mei says renewed in 2020 and moved ten metres; nl.wikipedia says 2019. Text: 2019–2020.
- Jewish share: 4en5mei 45% (WV) and 95% (HEDW); the "three quarters of the members murdered" is a club historian quoted by NH Nieuws.
- BPC: De Sportwereld calls it "probably a mixed club of Jewish and Christian players"; 4en5mei calls it "het joodse Be quick Pollux Combinatie". Text says Jewish and Christian, with the 1941 withdrawal.
- BPC stone pin: 4en5mei 52.343902, 4.952989; OSM 52.345736, 4.956111; address point Voorlandpad 15 52.347842, 4.954085. Not used as a pin (place is pinned on WV-HEDW).
- T.O.G. stone pin: 4en5mei 52.352110, 4.941807 differs from the Radioweg 69 address point (52.349232, 4.948274). Not pinned.
- Anna's Tuin: size 1 ha (own site) vs 1.5 ha (UvA 2017 release, as quoted in search results; the UvA URL now redirects to the home page). The student founding story (three biology students, city commission, foundation with a lecturer, opened 20 May 2017) comes from that release via search snippets; not read in full.
- Anna: the garden's sign says Anna van den Broeke, a farmer, inherited the farm in 1850; "Anna Hoeve" (round 1) says she inherited it from her father the dike-reeve around 1850. Text says "inherited the farm next door around 1850".
- Carlton crash: the aircraft was a Halifax (DG423), not a Stirling; cause given as flak (TracesOfWar), a night fighter in other sources; text says "shot down". Nine dead on the ground from BAAA (16 in total).
- Het Houten Stadion: the scout said players changed in what is now Grand Café Frankendael (nl.wikipedia says so); NH Nieuws only says "a café across the Middenweg". Text does not name the café.
- Jaap Eden: nl.wikipedia says the new hall build starts 2026; one municipal-based report spoke of finishing by autumn 2028. Text follows the club's March 2026 post (phased, ready for 2028–2029).
- HAUT: scout's "BREEAM award 2018" confirmed (8 March 2018, before construction). The text calls it a hybrid on Arup's own description; no critic is quoted.
