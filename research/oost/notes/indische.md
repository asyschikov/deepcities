## Status checks and decisions

- Kraaipanbuurt (10 records) and the umbrella record of the Tugelaweg blocks: still "In procedure Gemeentelijk Monument". No heritage_status or monument_number for the Kraaipanbuurt place; update when listed.
- 2009971 (Steve Bikoplein 18-20) looks like a duplicate or sub-record of 200997 (18-24, same register text). Both are in the ensemble; check whether the city meant two records.
- Noodschool, Kraaipanstraat 54: current use not confirmed. Kraaipanstraat 54-A was let as a 57 m² flat "with large windows and high ceilings" (Pararius/Funda), which may mean the school has been converted, but that is not proven. The Kraaipan Oase (Kraaipanstraat 58) and the Dynamo "Kraaipan" building are probably other buildings. The text names no use. No photo.
- Herberg Zeeburg: now flats (Open Monumentendag archive). Not open to the public.
- Jewish cemetery Zeeburg: closed for the winter until 1 April 2027; text gives the seasonal rule. Tour dates 2027 are in the sources line.
- NedPhO-Koepel: concerts listed for 2026, still in use.
- Distillery 't Nieuwe Diep (Flevopark pumping station), Badhuis (Javaplein 21) and BOOST (Danie Theronstraat 2) checked as operating in 2026.
- Anxiety Rabbit: permanent permit since April 2026 (oost-online 21 April 2026).
- Indische Buurt renaming: the "Archipelbuurt" idea was raised in 2023; no council decision found.
- Hoofdrioleringsgemaal: the register text (2009) mentions a multimedia school in the house; amsterdamopdekaart (2022) just says office. The text says office.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Pretoriusplein renamed Steve Bikoplein: nl.wikipedia says 1 February 1978 (signs changed 21 August 1978), amsterdamopdekaart says 1982 (probably confusing it with the 1982 sign action). The text uses 1978.
- Anxiety Rabbit: the scout list says 2017; oost-online, Mondriaan Fonds and Parool say 2018. The text uses 2018.
- Zeeburgerdorp: 1926 (nl.wikipedia) vs 1925 in some sources. The text uses 1926.
- Gerardus Majellakerk: nl.wikipedia says 1925, the national register 1923–1926 (school buildings 1926, stone MCMXXVI). The text uses 1923–1926.
- Gemaal Flevopark: amsterdamopdekaart says 1881 (decision 1880), the city register says 1885 as begin year for both 11-12 and 13. The text uses 1880–1881 for the pumping station and about 1885 for the cottages.
- Herberg Zeeburg: inn here from 1675, present form 18th century (the national register only says 18th century; 1766 is in a secondary source, left out). End of the inn "around 1915" and start of the quarantine office 1914/1916 differ between sources; the text says around 1915.
- Steve Bikoplein 13-21: architect given as Andries Johannes or Abram Johannes Donker; the text says just Donker.
- Gerardus Majella torendrama: from nl.wikipedia only.
- 101 Namen: exact position of the work on the viaduct not confirmed; pin is the OSM artwork node under the viaduct (52.363246, 4.930989). Check on site.
- Zeeburgerdorp: nl.wikipedia says the village was at the east end of the Zeeburgerpad, then later says the west end. The plaque is on the John Rädeckerbrug; the pin is the bridge centre (plaque position on the bridge not exact).
- Sparrenweg plaque: the address 39 comes from earlier research; oost-online does not give a house number.

## Route ideas

- Indische Buurt walk (about 3 km): 101 Namen under the viaduct, the sewage pumping station, the HIJSM block, Indië I and the Zeeburgerdijk row, Javaplein with the Berlage blocks and the bathhouse, Van der Pek's garden blocks, the Gerardus Majella enclave and the Batjanstraat school, then into the Flevopark: Hekkepoort, Jewish cemetery (Sundays in season), pumping station and cottages, Anxiety Rabbit.
- Zeeburgerdijk by bike (about 2.5 km): Flevopark, Zeeburgerdorp plaque on the John Rädeckerbrug, Gemaal Zeeburg, Herberg Zeeburg at the tram 7 terminus.
- Transvaalbuurt walk (about 2.5 km): Berlage's gateway blocks on the Linnaeusstraat, Steve Bikoplein, Smitstraat, the Tugelaweg blocks and Krugerhof, Transvaalplein memorial, Krugerplein monument, Albert Wittenbergplein and the Couzijn relief, Kraaipanbuurt and Afrikanerplein, the emergency school, the Buurthuis, out through the Wayang tunnel.
