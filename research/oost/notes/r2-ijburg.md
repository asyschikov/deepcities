## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Silos, which tower: NH Nieuws says the cinema is the "meest westelijke" tower; de Brug (4 July 2026) says "meest oostelijke". OSM puts the Rialto Silo node (Faas Wilkesstraat 102) on the north-western tank of the row. The text follows NH and OSM.
- Silos, opening: de Brug gave test screenings from 17 September and an opening weekend 23–25 October; that did not happen as planned. Regular screenings from 5 November per the operator's site. The text uses 5 November.
- Silos, age: silos.amsterdam says built 1980; nl.wikipedia says the works were laid out in the 1970s. Attribute "c. 1980". NH says sewage was treated "tot begin 2000"; nl.wikipedia and silos.amsterdam say replaced/out of use 2006. Used 2006.
- Silos, the scout's "chocolate swimming pool": confirmed by NH Nieuws (an entry in the contest, not built).
- Skatepark area: 3,100 m² (municipality via Amsterdam Now and HBO Kennisbank), 3,885 m² (SkateOn), 4,000 m² (Parool). The text says "some 3,100 to 3,900 square metres".
- Skatepark "initiative of an Amsterdam skater" (scout): not confirmed. Amsterdam Now says the park was "conceived by the skate scene and local residents"; the text uses that.
- An outcast sanctuary maker: nl.wikipedia says the duo Roskam and Coenen split and Roskam finished it; SkateOn still credits both. Attribute names Roskam with Coenen for the design.
- Heermabrug length: 230 m (nl/en Wikipedia, Arcam) vs 250 m (Grimshaw). Used about 230 m. The scout's "smiling bridge" nickname was not found anywhere; the sourced nicknames are "beha-brug", "Cup IJ" (and "Pam An"), used instead. The canal-house gables and crab skeleton come from en.wikipedia only, so the text says "are said to have inspired".
- Waterbuurt West size: Arcam 75 water homes (55 floating, 3 pile, 17 dike); Rohmer's own page 158 waterside homes in all (it counts the Kadegebouw too). The text uses 75. Date: Arcam 2010; scout 2011 (Arcam lists a 2011 nomination). Used 2010. Density: Rohmer "some 100 homes per hectare, comparable to the Jordaan", Archello 60 per hectare; the text only says "compared to the Jordaan".
- Vrijburcht: gebouwdin says 2006/2007, Kunstwacht "in 2007 onthuld", official opening 21 April 2007; the residents count their 20 years from 2006. Attribute 2006–2007.
- Nautilus date: inscription and artist's site 2008; oost-online (2023) says the block opened in 2016, which looks wrong. Used 2008.
- Nesciobrug length: 780 m (nl.wikipedia, ONH, VvWGM) vs "almost 800 m" (en.wikipedia). Used 780 m.

## Route idea

"New land, new water" (about 8 km by bike, can join round 1's "From toxic dump to floating poems"): RI-Oost silos and Rialto Silo, Skatepark Zeeburgereiland, Enneüs Heermabrug, Vrijburcht and its fish wall, Waterbuurt West, Nautilus, then back west via the Diemerpark to the Nesciobrug.
