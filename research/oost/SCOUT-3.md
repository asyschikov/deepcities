# Oost scout, round 3 (items 201–244)

Scouted 2026-11-23. Sweeps 3 to 7 of the West standard (war and memory, hidden history, behind the gate, modern and contested, curiosities and green). Nothing is written yet: the user picks.

I have 44 candidates, numbered 201 to 244. None of them is in SCOUT.md, SCOUT-2.md or the 166 places in `research/oost/places/*.yaml`; I checked each against the YAML and against a dump of the live map (853 places, 166 in Oost). Every location was point-checked with `research/oost/scripts/areas.py` and is in borough Oost. Nothing in Zuid or Centrum. 20 items are proposed as **merges** into an existing place (a paragraph or a Look for line), 24 as possible new places.

Each item: name; location (gebied); hook; why it fits; lead source; caveats; suggested rank; possible merge.

**Sources gone through this round:**
- **4en5mei.nl monument database:** every monument within about 6 km of Oost (210 Amsterdam records, sorted by distance, detail pages downloaded and point-checked). 33 lie in Oost; 13 were already on the map, the rest are below or under "Checked and dropped".
- **OpenStreetMap:** `historic=*`, `memorial=*`, `military=*`, `building=bunker`, `tourism=museum|attraction|viewpoint`, `landuse=allotments` in the Oost bounding box, filtered to the borough (83 features).
- **nl.wikipedia:** every article in Categorie:Amsterdam-Oost and its subcategories (821 articles; 328 non-street, non-bridge, non-sculpture articles read at intro level with coordinates, plus the memorial-type sculptures).
- **Arcam:** Kees van Leeuwen's cycle route along the Cold War public shelters (PDF).
- **Museum Perron Oost:** the 2014 exhibition booklet "KNSM vaarders, krakers en woonpioniers" (squatters in the docklands).
- **iamsterdam:** "verborgen parels" pages for Oost and the Oostelijk Havengebied.
- **Geheugen van Oost:** its site search did not return results to a script or a browser, so it was used only through web search hits (see COVERAGE.md).
- **Web search, Dutch and English:** NH Nieuws, NOS, tracesofwar, planning documents (ruimtelijkeplannen.nl), UvA, IISG, the Jaap Eden rebuild news.

## Sweep 3: War and memory

201. **Zeeman op Uitkijk (Sailor on the lookout)**; Javakade, at the head of the Java-eiland (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); a 9 m granite seaman in oilskins and sou'wester, hand over his eyes, looking toward the North Sea Canal for the 611 men of the Stoomvaart Maatschappij Nederland (SMN) who did not come back from the war; unveiled 4 May 1950 on the island, moved to the De Ruyterkade when the line left, returned "to its historic place" in April 2003; a big, little-known war memorial on the spot where the Indies line had its base 1910–1968; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1506/amsterdam-zeeman-op-uitkijk ; same artist (Pieter Starreveld) and same company as the Vrouwe Fortuna already on the map; the 4en5mei text mentions a nickname but is cut off, check it; Nice; possible merge with "Vrouwe Fortuna (Starreveld)" or with 229 (Hotel Jakarta), but it stands well on its own.

202. **Leendert Pop and Pension Verdooner**; Weesperzijde, by no. 141 (Oud-Oost, Weesperzijde); a smiling bronze old man with hat and walking stick (Louise Schiffmacher, unveiled 28 March 2024) for an 83-year-old retired kettle mender, one of 39 residents of a Jewish care home at Weesperzijde 141 who were murdered; all 39 names with ages (53 to 88) are on the back; the Schiffmachers found the history of their own house and pushed for the statue; strong, recent, personal; https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/334074/standbeeld-leendert-pop-weesperzijde-amsterdam (and 4en5mei 4592) ; sensitive, write factually; Nice; no merge (wiki article "Verdoonermonument" is the same thing).

203. **Football clubs' war memorials of the Watergraafsmeer**; Radioweg 86 (WV-HEDW), Radioweg 69 (T.O.G.) and Voorlandpad 15 (BPC) (Watergraafsmeer, Middenmeer); at the gates of ordinary amateur clubs: a wall with the names of 169 HEDW and 67 Wilhelmina Vooruit members murdered, mostly Jewish (33 names found and added on 4 May 2024), a stone for four T.O.G. members (two resistance men and the Jewish Kroonenberg brothers), and the stone of BPC (Be Quick Pollux Combinatie), a Jewish club; football as a way into Jewish Amsterdam and its loss; still the scene of yearly 4 May gatherings; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/2950/amsterdam-monument-van-voetbalclub-wv-hedw (also 2952, 2953; NH Nieuws https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/335565/nieuwe-namen-van-vermoorde-clubgenoten-op-oorlogsmonument-amsterdamse-voetbalclub) ; one place with the WV-HEDW wall as pin; sports grounds, visible from the gate or open on match days; Nice.

204. **Internment camp Levantkade (gone)**; KNSM-eiland, harbour sheds along the Levantkade and Surinamekade (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); June 1945 to July 1946 a camp for NSB members and collaborators, up to 6,000 at once, on straw behind barbed wire, with uprisings, a fire and dysentery; among the guards the later football referee Leo Horn, among the internees ex-mayor Voûte; in 1944 the Germans had already used the same sheds for men rounded up for forced labour; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interneringskamp_Levantkade ; nothing on site, so under the high bar only as a paragraph; Okay; merge into "Loods 6 and the Kompaszaal" (the KNSM shed that survives).

205. **KNSM war memorial in the Kompaszaal**; Loods 6, KNSM-laan 143 (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); Starreveld's white stone relief of a lying man (1947) with plates naming 247 KNSM seamen lost in the war, 49 ships sunk; moved from the Scheepvaarthuis to a seamen's home in Egmond, back to Amsterdam in 1992, into the Kompaszaal in 2005; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1510/amsterdam-knsm-monument ; indoors, check that the Kompaszaal is open to the public; Okay; merge into "Loods 6 and the Kompaszaal".

206. **Memorial stone in the Van der Pek courtyard**; inner garden between the Eerste Atjehstraat and the Balistraat, Molukkenstraat 68-70 (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); "Zij gaven hun jonge leven voor onze vrijheid": a stone placed by neighbours in June 1945 for three young resistance men from the block shot in March 1945 (Friederich and the brothers Engelen); residents still gather there on 4 May; also a "behind the gate" item; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/4921/amsterdam-gedenksteen-in-de-binnentuin-van-het-van-der-pekblok (Geheugen van Oost story of 11 October 2024 cited there) ; inside a residents' courtyard, say how and when it can be seen; Okay; merge into "Van der Pek's garden blocks, Balistraat".

207. **"Het Gezin" (Nic Jonk, 1955) for the Arbeiderspers staff**; inside the IISG, Cruquiusweg 31 (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); a bronze family for the socialist publisher's staff killed in the war, made for the Hekelveld, sent to Het Vrije Volk in Rotterdam (1970–1991), re-unveiled in the archive of the labour movement in 1993; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1447/amsterdam-monument-aan-de-cruquiusweg ; indoors; Okay; merge into 236 (IISG) if that is picked.

Frank and J. de Vries, "victims of the terror 1940–1945", on a house on the old abattoir grounds; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1441/amsterdam-monument-aan-de-j-m-van-der-meylaan ; small; check whether they were abattoir workers before saying so; Okay; merge into "Former cattle market and abattoir, Cruquiusweg".

209. **Railway staff memorial, Panamakade**; Panamakade (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); a stone "for fallen staff of the Nederlandse Spoorwegen", by the old Rietlanden rail yard; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1442/amsterdam-monument-aan-de-panamakade ; the only source is a 1995 book; the 4en5mei pin may be approximate and the stone may have moved when the area was rebuilt, so check on site; Okay; merge into "Keesje Brijde's cross" (same rail yard).

210. **Ceramplein: "Hun leven, onze vrijheid" and the freedom tree**; Ceramplein, end of the Eerste Ceramstraat (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); the Indische Buurt celebrated the liberation for years around a freedom tree on this square; a tile was laid in 1957 and a small stone reads "Their life, our freedom. 1940–1945"; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_leven_en_vrijheid ; whether the tile is the original of 1957 is unclear; small; Okay.

211. **Plaques for the 153 deported pupils, Laing's Nekstraat**; IKC Frankendael school, Laing's Nekstraat 44 (Oud-Oost, Transvaalbuurt); two plaques for 153 pupils of the President Kruger and Oranje Vrijstaat schools deported in 1942; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1478/amsterdam-plaquettes-aan-de-laings-nekstraat ; indoors in a primary school, not visible; Okay; merge into "Jewish memorials of the Transvaalbuurt" (one sentence).

212. **Tile tableau for six parishioners killed in the Indies, 1945–1950**; devotion chapel of the Catholic church at Linnaeushof 93 (Watergraafsmeer, Middenmeer); 80 young men "grateful to Mary for their safe return from the Indies" commissioned 56 painted milk-glass tiles (Alex Asperslagh, 1951) with six crosses in a tropical landscape, rifle and helmet at their feet; the chapel burned in 2008 and a new tableau was blessed in 2009; a rare memorial to the colonial war, which needs careful wording; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3454/amsterdam-monument-in-de-linnaeuskerk ; inside a church, only during services or open days; the 4en5mei address (Linnaeushof 93) points at the Hofkerk, check that it is the same church; Okay; merge into "Linnaeushof and the Hofkerk".

213. **Memorial trees of the Watergraafsmeer**; Pythagorasstraat 77 and the south side of the Robert Kochplantsoen (Watergraafsmeer); a prunus planted on 3 May 1995 by neighbours for "all Jewish families of the street deported in '42 and '43", and a 1955 liberation tree ringed by bricks reading "1945 Vrijheid Blijheid 1955"; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/4527/amsterdam-herdenkingsboom (and 1453) ; both slight; Okay; better as Look for lines in "Tuindorp Frankendael (Jeruzalem)" (Robert Kochplantsoen) and a nearby Middenmeer place.

214. **"Weesperzijdekwartier 1945 Bevrijding" gable stone**; Weesperzijde 99 (Oud-Oost, Weesperzijde); a 1957 liberation stone on the old fire station; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1473/amsterdam-monument-aan-de-weesperzijde ; small; Okay; Look for line in 202 or a nearby Weesperzijde place.

215. **War graves and the Angel of Death at De Nieuwe Ooster**; Kruislaan (Watergraafsmeer); the Commonwealth field includes the crew of the bomber that crashed into the Carlton Hotel in 1943; a wall and a grave stone for the Dutch soldiers of May 1940; and Fred Carasso's bronze Engel des doods (1955), hidden for decades on the back wall of the aula and rehung when the crematorium was built in 2014; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/2123/amsterdam-graven-van-de-geallieerden (also 1450, 1452; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engel_des_doods_(Fred_Carasso)) ; Okay; merge into "De Nieuwe Ooster" (it already names the Commonwealth field and the Buchenwald monument).

## Sweep 4: Hidden history

216. **Het Houten Stadion, Ajax 1907–1934 (gone)**; around the Christiaan Huygensplein (Watergraafsmeer, Middenmeer); Ajax's wooden ground in the polder, with no stands until 1911 and players changing in the café that is now Grand Café Frankendael on the Middenweg; outgrown by the 1930s successes, which led to De Meer; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Het_Houten_Stadion ; nothing left on site except the café, so under the high bar only as a paragraph; Okay; merge into "Stadion De Meer (gone)".

217. **Outersdorp and the Oetewalermolen (gone)**; Flevopark (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); the park lies on a vanished hamlet on the old Sint Anthonisdijk, and from about 1631 the Oetewaler mill drained the polder where Flevopark 13 now stands; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outersdorp ; nothing visible; Okay; merge into "Flevopark pumping station and the polder cottages" (the mill's successor role makes the link).

218. **Horse-tram depot, Linnaeusstraat**; Linnaeusstraat near the Muiderpoort (Oud-Oost, Oosterparkbuurt); built 1884 by Abraham Salm for the Amsterdamsche Omnibus Maatschappij's Dam-Linnaeusstraat horse tram; when line 9 went electric, 250 tram horses were auctioned here on 4 January 1904; later a store for old trams and a garage; West has its own "Horse-tram depot, Overtoom" on the map; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remise_Linnaeusstraat ; **status unverified**: I could not confirm what stands there now or whether it is listed; Okay.

219. **The Theo van Goghpark on IJburg**; Haveneiland-Oost (IJburg, Zeeburgereiland); a park named after the murdered filmmaker, laid out partly sunken, with 13 mature plane trees moved from the Europaplein for the North-South metro line; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_van_Goghpark ; slight on its own; Okay; merge as one sentence into "De Schreeuw and the murder of Theo van Gogh".

220. **The 1997 referendum against IJburg**; IJburg (IJburg, Zeeburgereiland); on 19 March 1997 most voters (about 133,000) said no to building in the IJmeer, but the turnout missed the 155,000 threshold and the islands were built anyway; the whole district is the result of a vote it lost; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IJburg (also the TU Delft study of the case) ; no single spot; Okay; merge into "Enneüs Heermabrug" (the gateway bridge) or "Diemerpark and De Ontmoeting".

221. **Amstel brewery stables, Mauritskade 55**; Mauritskade by the Oosterpark (Oud-Oost, Oosterparkbuurt); Abraham Salm designed a stable, workshop and coachman's house here for the brewery's horses and drays ("Stalgebouw van de Brouwerij de Amstel" over the door on a 1915 photo); https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauritskade_55_GH ; the article is unclear about how much of the stable survives; Okay; merge into "Amstel brewery office" as a Look for line if it survives.

## Sweep 5: Behind the gate

222. **Cold War shelter in the Torontobrug cycle tunnel**; Weesperzijde at the corner of the Mauritsstraat, east end of the Torontobrug (Oud-Oost, Weesperzijde); behind the tiles of a 33 m cycle tunnel hide five atomic blast doors to a public shelter for 1,200 people, built with the bridge in 1968–1970, with air filters and its own generator; a garage door on the Mauritsstraat was to be bricked up in wartime, and three air outlets show in the wall; https://arcam.nl/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Fietsroute-langs-de-Openbare-Schuilplaatsen-van-Amsterdam-1.2.pdf ; seen from outside only; the bridge's west end is in Zuid/Centrum, so pin the tunnel on the Weesperzijde side (pin approximate, about 52.3592, 4.9055); Nice.

223. **Wibautstraat metro station as an atomic shelter**; Wibautstraat metro station (Oud-Oost); the East Line stations Wibautstraat, Weesperplein and Nieuwmarkt (1977–1979) were built to close with sliding doors and air locks as public shelters; https://arcam.nl/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Fietsroute-langs-de-Openbare-Schuilplaatsen-van-Amsterdam-1.2.pdf ; the PDF only describes the doors at Weesperplein (Centrum), so check what is visible at Wibautstraat; Okay; merge into "Wibautstraat, the newspaper street" (which already covers the station's art).

224. **Military guardhouse on the Zuider IJdijk**; Zuider IJdijk 24, Zeeburgereiland (IJburg, Zeeburgereiland); built by the War Department in 1907–1909 at the island's only land access, with a troop shelter beside it; the last trace of the military camp of the Stelling van Amsterdam (UNESCO defence line) on Zeeburg, which closed in the 1960s; the city's heritage office advised keeping it (2018) and a 2021 plan proposed restoring the three military buildings on the site; https://www.ruimtelijkeplannen.nl/documents/NL.IMRO.0363.M1801BPGST-VG01/b_NL.IMRO.0363.M1801BPGST-VG01_6.pdf (also https://www.tracesofwar.com/sights/156835/Stelling-van-Amsterdam---Guards-House-Zeeburg-Camp.htm) ; **current state unverified** (restored, in use, or still waiting); private ground, seen from the dike; Nice if restored, otherwise Okay.

## Sweep 6: Modern and contested

225. **Pakhuis Wilhelmina, the squat that became policy**; Veemkade 576 (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); an 1892 warehouse squatted by artists on 25 June 1988, during the European Championship, threatened with demolition in 1994, bought by its own foundation and kept as about 95 cheap studios; the model for the city's broedplaatsen (incubator) policy; nearby the docklands' other artists' squat, the Vrieshuis Amerika ("ten degrees colder inside than out", with a cinema, a restaurant called The Fridge and roller disco), and the 1980 Levantkade squats; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakhuis_Wilhelmina (also the Perron Oost booklet https://www.museumperronoost.nl/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/KNSM-VAARDERS-KRAKERS-EN-WOONPIONIERS_-HET-OOSTELIJK-HAVENGEBIED-IN-TRANSITIE.-Tentoonstelling-Museum-Perron-Oost-september-2014-PRONKZUCHT.pdf) ; open to the public during its biennale open studios; the fate of the Vrieshuis Amerika is unverified, so keep it to one line or leave it out; Nice.

226. **Montessori College Oost (Herman Hertzberger)**; Polderweg 3 (Oud-Oost, Dapperbuurt); a 1993–2000 school for about 1,200 pupils built around one huge hall of stairs and balconies where the whole school meets, a key late work of the Dutch structuralist; inside hangs an Auschwitz plaque with a text by Huub van der Lubbe (2006); https://www.ahh.nl/index.php/en/projects2/9-onderwijs/55-montessori-college-oost-amsterdam (also 4en5mei 2858) ; a working school, outside only; Nice.

227. **Sluishuis (BIG and Barcode, 2022)**; Steigereiland-Noord, at the entrance to IJburg (IJburg, Zeeburgereiland); a block that lifts one corner out of the IJmeer so boats can sail into its courtyard, with a public stepped roof walk; opened 13 July 2022; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sluishuis_(Amsterdam) ; the roof route is public; Nice.

228. **Muziekgebouw aan 't IJ and the Bimhuis**; Piet Heinkade 1–3 (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); the black box of the jazz club founded in 1974 sticks out of the glass concert hall (3XN, 2005) over the water, and the window behind the Bimhuis stage frames the IJ and the trains; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bimhuis ; well known to music lovers, maybe too close to the A-list for some; venue, visit during concerts; Okay.

229. **Hotel Jakarta and the head of the Java-eiland**; Javakade 766 (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); SeARCH's timber hotel (2018) with a subtropical garden under glass, on the spot where SMN's ships left for the Indies and where the city chose to build the National Slavery Museum (opening not before the early 2030s); https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Jakarta ; a hotel; the museum part is already a line in "National Slavery Monument and Keti Koti"; Okay; possible merge with 201 (or 201 merges here).

230. **De Warren, the first housing co-op**; Centrumeiland, IJburg (IJburg, Zeeburgereiland); Amsterdam's first self-built housing cooperative, which won a city pilot tender in April 2018 and finished its timber building in early 2023, with shared rooms and rents kept out of the market; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wooncoöperatie_De_Warren ; residents' building, outside only; Okay.

231. **KNSM-eiland's east end: Emerald Empire and Skydome**; Venetiëhof and KNSM-laan (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); Jo Coenen's round block ringed by water villas (1996–97) and Wiel Arets' 63 m tower (1996), the end of Coenen's own 1989 superblock plan; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerald_Empire ; Barcelona and Piraeus are already on the map; Okay; could merge into "Barcelona and its gate (Barcelonaplein)" as Look for lines.

232. **Entrepotbrug and the Entrepot-West water tower**; Entrepothaven and C. van Eesterenlaan (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); Atelier PRO's 1992 housing slab built over the water of the Entrepothaven, and the same firm's water-tower-shaped tower block; the early, experimental phase of the docklands; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepotbrug ; Okay.

233. **HAUT, the timber tower**; Amstelkwartier, by Park Somerlust (Watergraafsmeer, Omval/Overamstel); a 73 m housing tower with a timber structure, one of the tallest wooden buildings in the world when finished, BREEAM award 2018; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/HAUT ; Okay.

234. **Mellow Yellow, the first coffeeshop (gone)**; Weesperzijde 53 (Oud-Oost, Weesperzijde); in 1972 Wernard Bruining and friends squatted a bakery here and sold hashish openly, the start of the Dutch coffeeshop; the shop later moved to the Vijzelgracht and closed in 2017 under the school-distance rule; lasting impact, so it passes the gone-things bar; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mellow_Yellow_(coffeeshop) ; nothing on site that I found; sources differ on 1972 or 1973; **flagged as food and drink** (a café of sorts), proposed for its impact on Dutch drug policy, not as a venue; Okay.

235. **De Spreeksteen, the speakers' corner**; Oosterpark, north entrance behind the Wereldmuseum (Oud-Oost, Oosterparkbuurt); a Dutch Speakers' Corner set up on 5 May 2005, half a year after Theo van Gogh was murdered a few hundred metres away; anyone may climb it on Sunday afternoons; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spreeksteen ; I found no 2025–2026 report of it actually being used, only the standing invitation on iamsterdam; Okay; merge into "De Schreeuw and the murder of Theo van Gogh" or "Oosterpark and its music dome".

## Sweep 7: Curiosities, green corners and odd museums

236. **IISG: Marx's manuscripts in a cocoa warehouse**; Cruquiusweg 31 (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); the International Institute of Social History keeps the papers of Marx and Engels, including the last surviving handwritten page of the Communist Manifesto (UNESCO Memory of the World), bought from the SPD archive in 1938; the Nazis came looking for it in 1940; since 1989 in a former cocoa warehouse; https://iisg.amsterdam/en/about/history/detailed-history-iish (also https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/363610/nazis-stonden-ooit-voor-de-deur-van-deze-amsterdamse-schatkamer-dit-zochten-ze) ; a research archive with a reading room, not a museum; nothing to see outside; Nice, a case for Amazing.

237. **Jaap Edenbaan, the oldest outdoor artificial ice track**; Radioweg 64 (Watergraafsmeer, Middenmeer); opened 9 December 1961, the world's third 400 m artificial track and now the oldest outdoor one still in use; it freed the Dutch from waiting for natural ice and started their speed-skating run; nearly 300,000 skaters in the first season; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaap_Edenbaan ; the indoor hall is being rebuilt from spring 2026 (done 2027–2028), the outdoor track (renovated 2023) stays open in winter; Nice.

238. **UvA Computermuseum**; Science Park 904 (Watergraafsmeer, Middenmeer); a university collection of early computers and calculating machines, founded 1991; https://www.uva.nl/over-de-uva/organisatie/faculteiten/faculteit-der-natuurwetenschappen-wiskunde-en-informatica/locatie-en-contact/faciliteiten/uva-computermuseum.html ; by appointment only; Okay; merge into "CWI and the Science Park internet hub".

239. **Borneo Architectuur Centrum**; in the Pacman building (Koen van Velsen, 1998), Borneo-eiland (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); a small volunteer-run centre since 2013 on how the docklands became housing, with changing exhibitions; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borneo_Architectuur_Centrum ; its site is up (2026), check opening days; Okay.

240. **Leeuwen van Zeeburg**; both banks of the Amsterdam-Rijnkanaal mouth by the Zeeburgerdijk (Indische Buurt, Oostelijk Havengebied); four identical lions (Marieke van Diemen, 2015) that bring back Bart van Hove's 19th-century lions of the old Merwedekanaal lock; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeuwen_van_Zeeburg ; this is public art that sweep 2 left out, so maybe it was dropped on purpose; check which lions stand on the Oost bank; Okay.

241. **Anna's Tuin & Ruigte**; Science Park, next to the UvA science faculty (Watergraafsmeer, Middenmeer); about 1.5 ha of student-started permaculture garden, food forest, dye garden and deliberately wild "ruigte" on a scrap of old polder, always open; https://placemakingamsterdam.nl/partners/annas-tuin-en-ruigte ; Okay.

242. **Allotment parks of the Watergraafsmeer**; Nieuwe Levenskracht (between the railway and the A10, behind Sportpark Middenmeer), Klein Dantzig (by Park Frankendael), De Hof van Eden and Amstelhof (Watergraafsmeer); garden villages that open their paths to walkers in season; https://dezwijger.nl/programma/tuinpark-nieuwe-levenskracht ; I found no founding dates or stories yet, so this needs research before it is worth a place; Okay; possibly one place for Nieuwe Levenskracht only.

243. **School gardens (Vink and Kalff)**; by Park Frankendael and in Betondorp (Watergraafsmeer); Amsterdam's municipal school gardens, where generations of city children grew their own vegetables; OSM `landuse=allotments` names "Vink Schooltuin" and "De Kalff Schooltuin"; lead: OSM relation 21262370 and way 1299598457 ; history and visiting not yet checked; Okay.

244. **"Kom der Gemeente Amsterdam" stones beyond the Oosterpark**; one in the Watergraafsmeer (about 52.355719, 4.969361) and one on the IJburg side (about 52.343853, 5.011947), per OSM; the same 1838-type limit stones as the two in the Oosterpark, marking the old city limit; https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/10967570465 ; positions and inscriptions from OSM only; Okay; merge as Look for lines into "Limietpalen in the Oosterpark".
