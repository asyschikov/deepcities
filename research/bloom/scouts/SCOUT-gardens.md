# Scout: gardens (flowers on the ground), Greater Amsterdam

Slice: botanical gardens, rose gardens, heemtuinen and wildflower spots, tulip spots and events,
allotment parks open to walkers, community and museum gardens, façade gardens and hollyhock
streets, rooftop gardens, flower markets. Scouted 2026-11-25 (read dates are this day unless noted).

Line format: `name | address or exact spot | what flowers | when | access | hook | still there? | sources`

Notes on sources:
- amsterdam.nl and amsterdamsebos.nl blocked automated reads (bot check); nothing below rests on them.
- amstelpark.info is the long-standing Amstelpark info site but now carries pharmacy spam links;
  its garden facts read as older park texts. Treated as lower confidence, flagged per line.
- tuinenvannederland.nl is a garden directory (looks partly auto-generated). Used only as a lead
  list; where a line rests on it alone, it says so.
- Bloom months marked "(general)" are standard bloom periods for the plant, not stated by the
  source for this spot. Everything else is from the cited page.
