# SCOUT: trees (flowering trees), Amsterdam in Bloom

Scout: bloom trees scout, 2026-11-25. Area: Amsterdam, Amstelveen's Amsterdamse Bos, Diemen, Duivendrecht.
Line format: name | address or exact spot | what flowers (species if known) | when (months, approx) | access | why it's special (hook) | still there? (latest evidence, year) | source links

Notes on method: every line rests on a page I read (or, where marked "snippet", only the search-result text of an official or press page). Parool's 2025 list "Bloesemgekte: op deze 11 plekken" is paywalled (also in the Wayback Machine); only the items visible in snippets are used. Plain street clusters already in register-spots.json are not repeated unless a local source names them as a loved spot.
