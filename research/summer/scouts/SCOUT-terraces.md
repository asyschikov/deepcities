# Scout: terraces (Magic of Summer)

Slice: summer terraces, beach bars, park and garden cafés, rooftops open to anyone, evening-sun
terraces, and ice cream with real standing. Researched 2026-11-25 in BrowserOS (DuckDuckGo, then
the sources themselves). Het Parool is paywalled: Parool links below are cited for their
headline and standfirst (which are public), not for body text. Wheelchair access was not checked
for any venue; treat it as "check" throughout. No swimming advice is given here: where a
terrace sits on water, the swim status is noted and left to the `swim` slice.

## Shortlist (best first)

1. **Hannekes Boom** (Dijksgracht 4, by the Oosterdok, Centrum) (N): the city's archetypal summer waterside café, a self-built wooden shack under big trees with a pontoon on the Oosterdok, where you sit with a beer by the water a few minutes' walk from Centraal and stay until the light goes. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open year-round, at its best May to September; it has stood on a five-yearly renewed lease since the early 2000s. Evidence: Man-Man; Girls Who; Entree; AmsterdamNow; ELLE NL; YLBB water; Het Parool on its lease, https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/als-de-brug-gesloten-blijft-wil-hannekes-boom-langer-blijven~b7afcd1e/. Caveats: packed on any warm evening; tenure is temporary in principle (lease renewed every five years until a zoning plan is fixed).

2. **Waterkant** (Marnixstraat 246, under the Marnix parking garage, on the Singelgracht) (N): a Surinamese canal-side terrace in an unlikely spot under a parking garage, where you queue at the bar for a Parbo and roti snacks and share tables with strangers in the long west-facing evening sun. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open, since 2014. Evidence: YLBB water ("blijft een favoriet"); YLBB 55; Man-Man; Girls Who; Entree; AmsterdamNow (no. 1 for long sun); Het Parool, https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/brian-fernandes-van-de-waterkant-trots-op-wat-het-is-geworden~b7136ebd/ and the photo lead of its 2025 list https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/24-x-leuke-terrassen-aan-het-water-in-het-centrum-van-amsterdam~b2a064f8/. Caveats: very busy, loud on weekend nights.

3. **Pllek** (TT. Neveritaweg 59, NDSM, Noord) (E: "Pllek", onzcl74s6kps21bou4msg3mt): the NDSM's container café with a sand beach on the IJ, where you take the free ferry over, lie on the sand and watch the ships and the sunset over the water. Kind: Beach. Status: open year-round, beach in summer; since 2012. Evidence: YLBB water; YLBB 55; YLBB beaches; Man-Man; Girls Who; Vogue NL; Entree; AmsterdamNow. Caveats: the beach is on the IJ shipping lane: not a swim spot (leave to the swim slice to state); crowded on hot weekends.

4. **Proeflokaal 't Blauwe Theehuis** (Vondelpark, Zuid) (E: "Blauwe Theehuis", sbbk8696zu95zuu2qvw7cd2o): the round 1937 tea house in the middle of the Vondelpark, now Brouwerij 't IJ's park taproom, whose big terrace fills with picnickers' friends, cyclists and dog walkers from the first sunny day. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open year-round, terrace is the summer draw. Evidence: Man-Man; Girls Who; Vogue NL; YLBB 55; AmsterdamNow (list and https://www.amsterdamnow.com/uitgaan/blauwe-theehuis-vondelpark-brouwerij-t-ij/). Caveats: very busy on warm weekends.

5. **De Ysbreeker** (Weesperzijde 23, Oost) (E: "De IJsbreker, Weesperzijde 23", qrztsoso1e5ox1zik2gbxv0g): a classic big café terrace on the Amstel, with afternoon and evening sun over the river and the rowing boats going by. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open. Evidence: YLBB water ("waar de zon altijd lijkt te schijnen"); YLBB 55; Entree; AmsterdamNow; indebuurt; Het Parool terras series, https://www.parool.nl/ps/bij-de-ysbreeker-zul-je-nooit-van-je-stoel-vallen-van-het-eten-en-toch-komen-we-terug-7~bcc1df40/. Caveats: none notable; the Weesperzijde swim strip just south belongs to the swim slice.

6. **Strandzuid** (Europaplein 22, between the Beatrixpark and the RAI, Zuid) (N): the oldest of the city beaches, a sand beach and terrace on the Beatrixpark pond, where office workers and families come for a long summer evening by the water. Kind: Beach. Status: seasonal (April to September as a beach; a winter venue the rest of the year); since about 2005 (20th anniversary in 2025). Evidence: YLBB beaches; De Buik beaches; Man-Man; Entree; AmsterdamNow; RAI on its 20 years, https://www.rai.nl/en/news/strandzuid-celebrates-20-year-anniversary,-c-,-two-decades-as-a-summer-hotspot-at-the-rai. Caveats: event-heavy (football screenings, company parties); the pond is not a bathing water.

7. **Kaap Amsterdam** (IJdijk 10, Oostpunt of the Zeeburgereiland, Oost) (N): a free-spirited sand beach at the raw eastern tip of the Zeeburgereiland, with swings, a vegetarian kitchen, live music and the feeling of being out of town. Kind: Beach. Status: seasonal, since about 2020; may stay at least to the end of 2028 while the Oostpunt becomes a dune park with official swimming from summer 2027. Evidence: YLBB beaches; De Buik beaches; Entree; Het Parool, https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/bij-strand-kaap-op-zeeburgereiland-is-het-een-en-al-strand-maar-waar-is-het-water~b6fca7f8/; De Brug, https://debrugkrant.nl/eindelijk-zwemmen-op-zeeburgereiland-wat-komt-er-op-de-oostpunt/. Caveats: no swimming here now (fenced off, not allowed); remote by bike, near the ring road noise.

8. **Hangar** (Aambeeldstraat 36, Nieuwendammerham, Noord) (N; could be clustered with E: "Hotel de Goudfazant", feyuw76f647qvlyui3qmgfat, at no. 10 on the same quay): an old aircraft hangar with a 300 m² terrace and sand beach on the IJ looking at the skyline, Noord's big afternoon-to-sunset hangout. Kind: Beach. Status: open (Wednesday to Sunday), summer is the point. Evidence: YLBB water; Girls Who; De Buik beaches; Barts water; Entree; De Buik, https://www.debuik.nl/amsterdam/restaurant/hangar. Caveats: the quay is on the IJ; De Buik mentions people taking a dip off it (with Barracuda's showers), but this is not an official bathing spot: leave to the swim slice. Suggest one place "Aambeeldstraat quay: Hangar, Barracuda and Goudfazant" rather than three.

9. **De Vergulden Eenhoorn** (Ringdijk 58, Watergraafsmeer, Oost) (E: "De Vergulden Eenhoorn", txc1sxgprmhaoxxlnoovld7g): a farmhouse of about 1702 whose garden turns into a summer picnic garden, a green, rural-feeling terrace under the old dike. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open; the picnic garden is a summer thing. Evidence: YLBB 55; Vogue NL; AmsterdamNow ("grote stadstuin"); ELLE NL; Oost-Online, https://oost-online.nl/de-vergulden-eenhoorn-combineert-historie-met-bourgondisch-buitenleven/. Caveats: none notable.

10. **Monte Pelmo** (Tweede Anjeliersdwarsstraat 17, Jordaan) (N): the Jordaan's ice cream maker since 1957 (at this address for decades), where on summer days you see the queue before you see the shop. Kind: Hangout and sunset (ice cream; no better kind on the list). Status: seasonal (spring to autumn). Evidence: Mokum Magazine IJstest 2026, no. 2 ("op zonnige dagen standaard een rij voor de deur"), https://www.mokummagazine.nl/2026/07/12/ijstest-2026-dit-is-de-beste-ijssalon-van-amsterdam/; Het Parool 2009, up to a thousand customers on a summer day and a neighbourhood petition when the district tried to curb it, https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/jordaanoproer-om-een-ijssalon~b3b30b1d/; YLBB, https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/ijsjes-in-amsterdam/. Caveats: take-away only, queue.

11. **De Mark at Hotel de Durgerdam** (Durgerdammerdijk 73, Durgerdam, Noord) (E: "De Durgerdam, inn since 1664", p8x4039zjc0eix6r26ismx41): lunch on a terrace right on the IJmeer dike in the old fishing village, the classic end point of a summer bike ride east along the water. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open. Evidence: Het Parool terras series ("pure idylle", pal aan het IJmeer), https://www.parool.nl/ps/de-locatie-van-restaurant-de-mark-in-durgerdam-is-pure-idylle-nu-nog-even-de-gerechten-perfectioneren-7-5~b55e4c9f/; YLBB water; Barts water. Caveats: small terrace on a narrow dike road with cars and cyclists.

12. **Ouderkerk aan de Amstel's Amstel terraces** (Amstelzijde and Kerkstraat, Ouderkerk; outside Amsterdam, municipality Ouder-Amstel, the Amstelzijde bank partly Amstelveen) (N): the bike ride up the Amstel past the Riekermolen ends at a row of terraces on the river (Paardenburg, Heisa, Ron Gastrobar Indonesia, SAAM, Bakker Out by the bridge), where boats moor alongside. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open, busiest in summer. Evidence: YLBB, "9 X hotspots in Ouderkerk aan de Amstel" ("een aantal van de mooiste terrassen ... aan de Amstel"), https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/ouderkerk-aan-de-amstel/; Barts water (SAAM). Caveats: one place for a stretch of venues; pick a pin at the bridge by the church. Aan de Plas at the Ouderkerkerplas beach belongs to the swim slice.

13. **Groot Melkhuis** (Vondelpark, west part, Zuid) (N): the Vondelpark's self-service pavilion with a big lakeside terrace next to the park's large playground, where parents sit in the sun while kids play; summer weekends add a bouncy castle. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open year-round, summer terrace. Evidence: Barts Boekje, https://www.bartsboekje.com/groot-melkhuis-amsterdam/; Vondelpark Info, https://vondelpark.info/ondernemer/groot-melkhuis/; CityMom, https://citymom.nl/lunch/groot-melkhuis/. Caveats: family-heavy; wasps.

14. **Café de Jaren** (Nieuwe Doelenstraat 20, Centrum) (E: "Café De Jaren, in the old securities bank", rw55vpm7zt1enizxwkc8ytl5): the big grand café whose waterside terrace and first-floor balcony look out over the Amstel, a summer classic for sun and boat-watching in the centre. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open, since 1990. Evidence: Time Out ("be prepared to fight for a spot on the Amstel-side terrace in summer"), https://www.timeout.com/amsterdam/bars-and-pubs/cafe-de-jaren; AmsterdamNow, https://www.amsterdamnow.com/restaurants/cafe-de-jaren-zonneterras-amstel-leestafel/. Caveats: touristy in high season.

15. **Helling 7** (TT. Melissaweg 57, NDSM, Noord) (N): a restaurant built from old shipyard materials on a former slipway, with a terrace looking across the IJ to the city. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open, since 2022. Evidence: Time Out (no. 2); Vogue NL; YLBB 55; Barts water; Entree. Caveats: more restaurant than hangout; reservation culture.

16. **Lagerwal** (TT. Melissaweg 57, next to Helling 7, NDSM, Noord) (N): a modern beer garden with one of the biggest terraces in town, on the water among the boats, sun until late evening. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: seasonal (Fridays to Sundays, until September), since summer 2023. Evidence: Entree, https://www.entreemagazine.nl/openingen-design/lagerwal-moderne-biertuin-in-amsterdam-noord; YLBB, https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/lagerwal-amsterdam-noord/; YLBB water. Caveats: could share a pin with Helling 7 (same owners, same address).

17. **De Ceuvel** (Korte Papaverweg 4, Buiksloterham, Noord) (E: "De Ceuvel", ihp6bionlwz4429w1gxf33uo): the salvage-built café among houseboats on polluted ground, with a laid-back waterside terrace in the sun where you can moor a boat. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open. Evidence: YLBB water; Girls Who; AmsterdamNow. Caveats: none notable.

18. **Boothuis** (Christoffel Plantijngracht 4, Sloterplas, Nieuw-West) (N): a family restaurant with a big terrace on the Sloterplas, the summer base for a day on the lake's shore in Nieuw-West. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open, since summer 2024. Evidence: De Buik beaches ("onze hangplek"); YLBB 55; Barts Boekje, "de ultieme zomerstek", https://www.bartsboekje.com/boothuis-de-ultieme-zomerstek/; Het Parool, https://www.parool.nl/ps/van-pasta-vongole-tot-kalfskroketten-boothuis-aan-de-sloterplas-wil-niet-trendy-zijn~b537cfc8/. Caveats: Sloterplas water quality varies (blue-green algae warnings recur): swim slice to state.

19. **BUITEN strandcafé** (on the Sloterplas, Nieuw-West) (N): the beach café on the Sloterplas (ex Hotel Buiten) with sand, terrace and lake views from lunch to sunset. Kind: Beach. Status: seasonal opening from late April (winter break). Evidence: YLBB 55; YLBB beaches; YLBB, https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/buiten-strandcafe-en-restaurant-amsterdam/. Caveats: renamed recently, confirm exact address; same algae note as Boothuis. Coordinator may want one Sloterplas pick, not two.

20. **De VerbroederIJ** (Johan van Hasseltweg 21, by the Oostveer ferry, Noord) (N): a neighbourhood beach café on a strip of IJ shore, lots of sand, cheap and kind, run as a social enterprise with free activities; great with kids. Kind: Beach. Status: open, active in 2026; began on a temporary city permit. Evidence: YLBB beaches; De Buik beaches; NU.nl on the start, https://www.nu.nl/amsterdam/5216465/tijdelijk-stadsstrand-bij-oostveer-in-noord.html. Caveats: temporary site; IJ shore, not a swim spot.

21. **Tolhuistuin's summer garden** (IJpromenade 2, by the Buiksloterweg ferry, Noord) (E: "Tolhuistuin and the Shell canteen", jy7exiurclv6nusx4f9bu3zk): step off the ferry into an old park garden where in summer the garden terrace opens with free live music, markets and Friday-afternoon drinks under the plane tree. Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal ("Zomer in de Tuin", weekends and from July Fridays). Evidence: Entree; AmsterdamNow; Tolhuistuin, https://tolhuistuin.nl/zomer-in-de-tuin; AT5 on its free summer programme, https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/239159/gratis-activiteiten-in-tolhuistuin-lekker-met-je-handen-bezig-weg-van-die-schermen. Caveats: overlaps with the water-openair slice; coordinate.

22. **Dignita Hoftuin** (in the Hoftuin, behind the Hermitage, Centrum) (N): a glasshouse café with a big terrace in a hidden inner garden, a quiet green summer spot right in the centre, home to an annual Hoftuin summer festival. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: YLBB 55; Entree; ELLE NL; bySam, https://www.bysam.nl/dignita-hoftuin-amsterdam-terras/. Caveats: the Hoftuin already has "Telefoon van de Wind" (gmz5qw46xd04e3tjr2ototjk) on the map.

23. **Merkelbach in Park Frankendael** (Middenweg 72, Watergraafsmeer, Oost) (N, or E via "Frankendael", vg0z6rjngmso1otuczhltuth): lunch or dinner on the terrace in the formal garden of the last country house of Amsterdam, with the sun setting into the green. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open; terrace in summer. Evidence: Time Out (no. 5); ELLE NL; De Buik, https://www.debuik.nl/amsterdam/restaurant/merkelbach. Caveats: restaurant prices and reservations; could simply be a summer text on Frankendael.

24. **Gent aan de Schinkel** (Theophile de Bockstraat 1, Oud-Zuid/Schinkelbuurt) (N): a Belgian beer café whose tables stand right on the Schinkel, a local's sun-trap for an after-work beer just past the Vondelpark. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: open. Evidence: YLBB 55; AmsterdamNow (list and https://www.amsterdamnow.com/restaurants/gent-schinkel-cafe-terras-belgisch-bier/); indebuurt. Caveats: none notable.

25. **The roof of NEMO** (Oosterdok 2, Centrum) (E: "NEMO Science Museum", cd6lwb2flcsk50v3nn5z2u7u): the free public sloping roof square with one of the best views of the old city; in summer it stays open on Thursday evenings with live music. Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open daily, summer evening programme (late June to late August). Evidence: YLBB 55; Barts roofs; NEMO, https://www.nemosciencemuseum.nl/zomer-op-het-dak. Caveats: overlaps with the green-hangout slice (sunset spots); coordinate.

26. **Van der Linde** (Nieuwendijk 183, Centrum) (N): a pastry shop serving one flavour of soft vanilla cream ice since 1937, with long summer queues on the Nieuwendijk. Kind: Hangout and sunset (ice cream). Status: open, summer is queue season. Evidence: YLBB ("de rijen zijn in de zomer altijd lang"), https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/ijsjes-in-amsterdam/; Mokum Magazine IJstest 2026 (no. 8); Nieuwendijk traders, https://nieuwendijk.nl/horeca/banketbakkerij-van-der-linde/. Caveats: shopping-street setting, take-away.

27. **Noorderlicht Café** (TT. Neveritaweg 33, NDSM, Noord) (E: "Noorderlicht", rv8ru6n0a5t6ld6h3qluqw40): the NDSM pioneer café's greenhouse and lawn terrace on the IJ, a sunny, outdoorsy hangout after the ferry. Kind: Waterside terrace. Status: check (bankrupt December 2025, restarted 2026 per our own record). Evidence: YLBB water; AmsterdamNow ("NDSM-terras met buitengevoel"). Caveats: confirm the 2026 restart is still running in 2027.

28. **Boerderij Meerzicht** (Koenenkade 56, Amsterdamse Bos; outside Amsterdam, municipality Amstelveen) (N): the 19th-century pancake farm in the Amsterdamse Bos, with a big sunny terrace, animals and a playground, the traditional summer family stop on a day in the Bos. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open most of the year. Evidence: YLBB, https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/boerderij-meerzicht-amsterdamse-bos/ ("in de zomer kan het er krioelen van de kinderen"); ONH, https://onh.nl/verhaal/boerderij-meerzicht-pannenkoeken-in-het-bos. Caveats: wasps in summer (YLBB); overlaps with the green-hangout slice's Amsterdamse Bos.

29. **Canvas, 7th floor of the Volkshotel** (Wibautstraat 150, Oost) (N): a rooftop bar on the old Volkskrant building anyone can walk into, with a terrace over Oost for summer sunsets. Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open. Evidence: YLBB 55; Barts roofs. Caveats: queues and door policy on weekend nights; the newspaper-street place (jphe2n6mbe6vwjkkd7h4kqio) may already mention the building.

30. **REM Eiland** (Haparandadam 45, Houthavens, West) (N): the 1960s offshore broadcasting platform now standing in the Houthavens, with a restaurant and roof terrace high over the IJ for a sunset drink. Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open. Evidence: Barts water; Barts roofs. Caveats: restaurant-led.

## Borderline

- **Brouwerij 't IJ, Proeflokaal De Molen** (E: "Brouwerij 't IJ, the brewery in the Funen bathhouse", j5tatb0fv50i1mhn9h7ms5gn): an obvious summer terrace (long tables under the windmill), but I found only one independent rating (Man-Man, https://www.man-man.nl/brouwerijen-in-amsterdam-bier-met-terras/) plus the brewery's own page; worth a second look, probably fine.
- **Cantina, Het Rijk van de Keizer** (E: v01v3rp7xtgylwywjhwau024): seasonal canal-side terrace (opens May), rated by Tuinen van West (https://tuinenvanwest.nl/lid/cantina-het-rijk-van-de-keizer/) and its own "8 leukste terrassen" post; no independent newspaper or blog list found.
- **Strand van Oost** (roof of Orchid House, Linnaeusstraat, Oost): 800 m² sand rooftop beach over the Oosterpark (Het Parool, https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/amsterdam-oost-heeft-nu-een-groot-stadsstrand-op-een-dakterras-op-1-hoog-waar-een-biertje-nog-2-80-euro-kost~b1a6e781/; Oost-Online; Barts roofs), but it opened only in spring 2026: one season, wait for a second.
- **Ode aan de Amstel** (Amstelboulevard, Amstelkwartier, Oost): floating terrace on the Amstel with the last sun (Het Parool terras series, AmsterdamNow), but Parool scored it low and it overlaps with the swim slice's Amstelkwartier beach; could join that place.
- **IJshoeve Sloten** (Sloterweg 1335, Sloten): Mokum Magazine's best ice cream four times and a summer bike destination with queues, but only open since 2017, short of the "decades" bar.
- **Café 't Sluisje** (E: ca6alyq7j6oq4q9vod63wrhz, Nieuwendam): its waterside neighbourhood tables are loved (AT5 on the 2025 table row, https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/238043/cafe-t-sluisje-moet-stamtafels-weghalen-na-klacht-staan-er-al-30-jaar; YLBB water), but it is a brown café first, not a summer place.
- **Café-Restaurant Amsterdam** (E: oawjflgv424sm9gj69lgw897): only Vogue NL lists its terrace; nothing summer-specific found.
- **WestWeelde, Westergas**: big covered terrace by the Gashouder (Entree, AmsterdamNow), but it is an event venue; Westergas is better served by the green slice.
- **Distilleerderij 't Nieuwe Diep** (covered by E: "Flevopark pumping station and the polder cottages", cltp1mv5m1ayhhjg5grdcmj1): orchard terrace by the pump hall, summer pop-up dinners; only Cityguys ("ons favoriete terras van de stad") rates it. A good summer text for the existing place if the green slice picks Flevopark.
- **Brasserie / Café Vrijburcht** (Steigereiland, IJburg; building is E: dccz2puust6ktiq2zrh42sw7): sunny water terrace with a jetty (YLBB water, zonopjebakkes), evidence thin.
- **Polly Goudvisch** (Buiksloterweg ferry, Noord): two big sunny terraces on the IJ (YLBB 55, indebuurt), but new (around 2024).
- **Paviljoen Twiske** (Het Twiske, outside Amsterdam, Oostzaan): waterside terrace in the recreation area (De Orkaan, Recreatie Noord-Holland); leave to the green slice's Twiske entry.
- **Café Binnen Buiten** (Ruysdaelkade, De Pijp): well-known canal terrace, but only Man-Man in this round.
- **tHuis aan de Amstel** (Amstelkwartier): De Buik and AmsterdamNow; belongs with the swim slice's Amstelkwartier beach.

## Checked and dropped

- **Vooges aan 't IJ** (Westerdoksdijk 40): on many 2025/2026 lists, but went bankrupt on 2 November 2026 (AD, https://www.ad.nl/amsterdam/restaurant-vooges-aan-t-ij-failliet-alweer-gaat-een-horecazaak-op-deze-pechlocatie-ten-onder~ad911380/).
- **'t Smalle** (E: pkv9ewmu19g7rc6hqry403so): its famous jetty terrace was removed in 2022 (Het Parool; our own record says so).
- **Roest**: the Oostenburg city beach and club is long gone; the name now belongs to a restaurant.
- **Mediamatic**: still at the Dijksgracht, but no source rates it as a summer place.
- **Hollandsche Manege**: no summer evidence; not a terrace place.
- **Pompstation, De Tulp, Lion Noir, The Butcher Social Club, GAPP, Floor 17, LuminAir, Soho House, Bar Bonnie and the like**: single-list restaurant or hotel terraces, not places that make an Amsterdam summer; several are hotel rooftops with a door policy.

## Summary
