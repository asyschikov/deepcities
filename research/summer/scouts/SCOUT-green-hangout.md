# Scout: green-hangout (parks and shade, picnic lawns, hangout and sunset spots)

Scouted 2026-11-25 through BrowserOS (Google, then the pages themselves). Het Parool's Dutch "PS" and
"uit" lists are behind the paywall; its free English edition (parool.nl/international) carries
the same guides and is cited instead. The municipality's own "Zomerrecreatie in Oost" pages
(amsterdam.nl) are a strong official source: they name the lawns that fill up on hot days.
Swimming at these spots belongs to the `swim` slice; this list cites swim facts only to warn.

## Shortlist (best first)

1. **Grote Speelweide and the Grote Vijver, Amsterdamse Bos** (Amsterdamse Bos, south end of the Bosbaan; outside Amsterdam (municipality Amstelveen), owned and run by Amsterdam) (N: the Bos itself is not on the map): the Bos's big summer field: a huge lawn on the Grote Vijver with an official bathing zone in the lake, canoe and pedal-boat hire on the lawn, and marked barbecue fields, so whole families spend the day there. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open; swim season and boat hire seasonal (May to September). Evidence: https://www.amsterdamsebos.nl/kinderen/kinderbaden/ (official: the Grote Vijver bathing zone is one of the Bos's two official swim spots) ; https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/getipt/bbq-in-amsterdam-hier-mag-het-in-het-openbaar~423171/ (barbecue fields at the Grote Vijver, the speelbos and the Groot Kinderbad) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/picknicken-in-amsterdam/ (picnic benches, swimming lakes, six public barbecues). Caveats: very busy on hot weekends; check zwemwater.nl for blue-green algae before swimming; barbecue only on stands and 50 m from the forest edge, banned in drought.

2. **Westerpark: the big lawn behind the Westergasterrein** (Westerpark, between the Gashouder and the Haarlemmerweg side) (E: "Westerpark", w4lbzyt68totp1szvnlsss9h): the classic West summer evening: blankets and barbecues on the big field behind the old gasworks, the only big inner-city park where you may grill almost anywhere, with fixed public barbecues. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/getipt/bbq-in-amsterdam-hier-mag-het-in-het-openbaar~423171/ ("bij het grasveld achter het Westergasterrein wordt vaak gebarbecued") ; https://www.dewestkrant.nl/de-ultieme-bbq-lijst-van-amsterdam-west/ (De Westkrant 2026: "de beste optie", barbecue everywhere except the sports fields, fixed grills) ; https://vrijetijdamsterdam.nl/maatschappij/picknicken-in-amsterdam-dit-zijn-leuke-picknickplekken/ . Caveats: crowded on hot days; festival weekends fence off parts of the park.

3. **Park Somerlust on the Amstel bend** (Korte Ouderkerkerdijk, Amstelkwartier, Oost) (N): a riverside lawn at the wide Amstel bend by De Omval, where Oost lies in the sun all afternoon and watches the sun go down behind the skyline. Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/international/17-spots-to-see-the-best-sunsets-in-amsterdam~bd31fedb/ (Parool 2026, readers' sunset list, opening pick) ; https://www.amsterdam.nl/stadsdelen/oost/zomerrecreatie-oost/park-somerlust/ (official: one of Oost's three summer hotspots, with extra toilets and wardens) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/barbecueen-in-amsterdam/ and https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/picknicken-in-amsterdam/ ("Amstelkwartier: een van mijn favorieten ... ga vroeg"). Caveats: NOT an official swim spot (water not tested, boats on the Amstel), though people do swim; barbecue only on the grass between the cycle path and the water east of rowing club RIC; neighbours complain of noise.

4. **Oosterpark: the lawns by the ponds and the paddling pool** (Oosterpark, Oost) (E: "Oosterpark and its music dome", iytb6uj68x6hg73hr1k2gwpg): Oost's living room on a hot day: barbecue allowed everywhere in the park, a busy paddling pool with a big sunbathing lawn around it, and picnics by the water and the fountain. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open; paddling pool seasonal (summer). Evidence: https://www.amsterdam.nl/leefomgeving/parken-recreatiegebieden/oosterpark/ (official: barbecue allowed everywhere) ; https://oost-online.nl/zomers-warm-lekker-zwemmen-buiten-of-binnen-dat-kan-in-oost/ (Oost-Online 2026: "drukbezochte pierenbadje" with a big lawn) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/barbecueen-in-amsterdam/ ("een van de fijnste parken ... zit aan het water of plof neer in het gras"). Caveats: crowded; a social-media report mentions an alcohol ban in the park (check the current APV area before writing it).

5. **Vondelpark: the lawns by the ponds and the Openluchttheater** (Vondelpark, Zuid) (E: "Vondelpark", qd0m975el7mdfp3aysy59gps): the city's front lawn on a summer evening: people-watching from the fields by the big ponds and around the open-air theatre, quieter grass further west. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/208183/vondelpark-afgesloten-te-druk-in-het-park (AT5: the park closed because it was too full on a warm day) ; https://vrijetijdamsterdam.nl/maatschappij/picknicken-in-amsterdam-dit-zijn-leuke-picknickplekken/ ("zoek een rustig veldje bij de vijver of bij het Openluchttheater") ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/picknicken-in-amsterdam/ ("druk op de grote weides ... verder fietsen"). Caveats: no barbecue allowed (amsterdam.nl); very crowded; the terraces (Blauwe Theehuis, Groot Melkhuis, Vondeltuin) belong to the terraces slice.

6. **Flevopark: the big field and the Nieuwe Diep shore** (Flevopark, Oost) (N: the park itself is not on the map; its pumping station and art are): the locals' "don't tell anyone" park: a big lawn looking over the Nieuwe Diep, shade under old trees, barbecues on the big field and a genever at 't Nieuwe Diep distillery on the water. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/picknicken-in-amsterdam/ ("heerlijk aan het water picknicken en onder de bomen wat schaduw") ; https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/getipt/bbq-in-amsterdam-hier-mag-het-in-het-openbaar~423171/ (barbecue on the big field) ; https://www.parool.nl/international/what-to-do-in-amsterdam-top-100-activities~b69533b1/ (Parool top 100: 't Nieuwe Diep, "a magical spot overlooking the water"). Caveats: sources disagree on where the barbecue field is (by the tennis courts vs by the Jewish cemetery): check the BBQ map; Flevoparkbad is for the swim slice.

7. **Sloterplas: the north boulevard and the east-bank benches** (Sloterpark, Nieuw-West) (E: "Sloterplas: the lake dug for sand", yzhlf1o6uh09s9zgn6obz6b4): Nieuw-West's summer shore: picnic, swim, barbecue on the marked spots in the Sloterpark, then the sunset over the lake from the north boulevard or the raised three-bench spot on the east bank. Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/international/17-spots-to-see-the-best-sunsets-in-amsterdam~bd31fedb/ (north boulevard and the east-bank benches) ; https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/145060/prachtige_zonsondergang_bij_sloterplas (AT5) ; https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/genieten-5-keer-plekken-om-te-picknicken-in-amsterdam~322896/ and https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/getipt/bbq-in-amsterdam-hier-mag-het-in-het-openbaar~423171/ . Caveats: barbecue reportedly not allowed around the Sloterstrand itself (check the BBQ map); swimming facts for the swim slice.

8. **Bogortuin, Java-eiland** (Java-eiland by the Verbindingsdam, on the IJhaven, Oost) (N): a long lawn between the houses and the harbour water, sun until late, a neighbourhood beach-without-sand that Oost calls its new favourite summer spot. Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.amsterdam.nl/stadsdelen/oost/zomerrecreatie-oost/bogortuin/ (official: one of Oost's three summer hotspots, temporary toilets, wardens 15:00 to 22:30 on summer days) ; https://oost-online.nl/zomers-warm-lekker-zwemmen-buiten-of-binnen-dat-kan-in-oost/ (Oost-Online 2026: "wordt steeds populairder") ; https://onderzoek.amsterdam.nl/artikel/zomerrecreatie-bogortuin-en-park-somerlust-zorgt-voor-overlast (city research on the summer crowds). Caveats: the IJhaven is a working inland-shipping harbour and the city says swimming there is in principle NOT allowed (Oost-Online and Kidsproof call it an unofficial swim spot: we must not recommend it); barbecue ban on part of the lawn; noise complaints.

9. **Erasmuspark: the central field** (Erasmuspark, Bos en Lommer, West) (E: "Erasmuspark", esioyoe9ueen62my3l2cdxo1): a fenced neighbourhood park with canals all round, where West barbecues from 1 April to 1 October and the summer Buurtcamping pitches its tents. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open (gates close at night). Evidence: https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/getipt/bbq-in-amsterdam-hier-mag-het-in-het-openbaar~423171/ ; https://www.dewestkrant.nl/de-ultieme-bbq-lijst-van-amsterdam-west/ (De Westkrant: grilling only in the "yellow" area, not on the central sports field) ; https://www.parool.nl/international/summer-vacation-2026-in-amsterdam-the-best-tips-and-outings-for-children-and-parents~b134e642/ (Buurtcamping 2026 in the Erasmuspark). Caveats: sources differ on exactly which field allows barbecue (YLBB says the big field in the middle, De Westkrant says not the sports field): use the city BBQ map.

10. **Rembrandtpark: the barbecue lawns** (Rembrandtpark, Nieuw-West/West) (E: "Rembrandtpark (western half)", r03v323omw6y207in0nj4au6; also "'t Landje", p5e3zkomtpk8ca3nr8w75530): the big lawns and ponds where, on the longest days, locals bring portable barbecues and beers. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.timeout.com/amsterdam/things-to-do/best-parks-in-amsterdam ("locals flock to the park with their portable BBQs") ; https://vrijetijdamsterdam.nl/maatschappij/picknicken-in-amsterdam-dit-zijn-leuke-picknickplekken/ ; https://www.dewestkrant.nl/de-ultieme-bbq-lijst-van-amsterdam-west/ . Caveats: barbecue only in marked parts.

11. **Het Twiske: Kure-Jan strand, the Doesstrand and the Speelsloot** (Het Twiske; outside Amsterdam (municipalities Oostzaan and Landsmeer)) (N; only the drum houses and a sculpture nearby are on the map): the bike-ride escape north of Noord: lake beaches, a shallow play ditch for kids and long grass, "midden in de natuur". Kind: Park and shade. Status: open; kiosk seasonal. Evidence: https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/picknicken-in-amsterdam/ ("ontsnap hier aan de drukte van de stad") ; https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/langste-dag-in-amsterdam-zo-haal-je-alles-uit-deze-dag~318968/ (longest-day tip) ; https://www.twiske-waterland.nl/activiteiten/zwemmen (official recreation board: beaches, Speelsloot). Caveats: official bathing spots (swim slice should check zwemwater.nl, algae recurs in Dutch lakes); one beach is dog-friendly, one area is a naturist beach.

12. **De Oeverlanden: the three little beaches on the Nieuwe Meer** (Oude Haagseweg side of the Nieuwe Meer, Nieuw-West) (E: "De Oeverlanden", avse5r5gizki80pu54k5gnfp): wild, unmown shore with three small sand beaches and lawns where the motto is "live and let live". Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/op-de-drie-strandjes-van-de-nieuwe-meer-is-het-motto-leven-en-laten-leven~b7b6c8e2/ (Parool 2023) ; https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/e29073/de-oeverlanden (AT5: "vooral in de warme zomermaanden") ; https://www.amsterdam.nl/leefomgeving/parken-recreatiegebieden/oeverlanden/ (official: beaches and lawns on the Nieuwe Meer). Caveats: whether these beaches are official bathing water is for the swim slice to check (Amsterdecks is the official one on the Nieuwe Meer).

13. **Gaasperpark: the lawns and the south-shore beach at sunset** (Gaasperpark, Zuidoost) (E: "Gaasperpark", g4igs6lw3gmky63u4cq5wz41): Zuidoost's lake park, newly redone, with wide lawns, shade under trees, barbecue spots and a small south-shore beach where friends raise a glass as the sun drops between the trees. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open (renovation finishing late 2026; a new beach opened June 2026). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/international/17-spots-to-see-the-best-sunsets-in-amsterdam~bd31fedb/ ; https://vrijetijdamsterdam.nl/maatschappij/picknicken-in-amsterdam-dit-zijn-leuke-picknickplekken/ ; https://www.groengebied-amstelland.nl/nieuws/opening-nieuw-strandje-aan-gaasperplas/2540 (official park manager) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/barbecueen-in-amsterdam/ . Caveats: parts were closed for works until 2026; beach and swim facts for the swim slice.

14. **Tuinen van West: the Polderheuvel and the pick-your-own gardens** (Tuinen van West, Osdorp/Geuzenveld edge, Nieuw-West) (E: "Tuinen van West", onulzrpzjz21yiyu12o4b4dt; "Fruittuin van West, a pick-your-own orchard in the polder", wr0dgwob4899414usffcisli): pick your own fruit and vegetables for a picnic, then barbecue on the Polderheuvel or kayak in the polder: a countryside summer day inside the city. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open; picking seasonal (summer). Evidence: https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/genieten-5-keer-plekken-om-te-picknicken-in-amsterdam~322896/ ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/barbecueen-in-amsterdam/ ("barbecueën mag op de Polderheuvel") ; https://www.amsterdam.nl/leefomgeving/parken-recreatiegebieden/tuinen-van-west/ (official). Caveats: little shade on the polder.

15. **Diemerpark and the Diemerzeedijk** (Diemerpark, between Zeeburgereiland and IJburg, Oost) (E: "Diemerpark and De Ontmoeting", kapgrw5hh9ge5asngh71p0pr): a wild park that "feels like a nature reserve", with a picnic on the dyke above the IJmeer and a small sand beach on the Haveneiland side. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://vrijetijdamsterdam.nl/maatschappij/picknicken-in-amsterdam-dit-zijn-leuke-picknickplekken/ ; https://oost-online.nl/zomers-warm-lekker-zwemmen-buiten-of-binnen-dat-kan-in-oost/ (swim only at the beach in the north-east corner) ; https://www.amsterdam.nl/leefomgeving/parken-recreatiegebieden/diemerpark/ (official: "een strandje waar u kunt zwemmen"). Caveats: Kidsproof warns not to dig on the beach (the park is a capped former dump); little shade.

16. **Noorderpark: the field by the restaurant and the rose garden** (Noorderpark, Noord) (N: park not on the map; E sub-places "Noorderparkkamer...", ffuzb80s0rfixsbqlol68t4d, and "Noorderparkbad", g4u6mhmp7639tu3359rn2lpo): Noord's neighbourhood park: picnic tables, grass by the water and the summer pool next door. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://vrijetijdamsterdam.nl/maatschappij/picknicken-in-amsterdam-dit-zijn-leuke-picknickplekken/ ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/barbecueen-in-amsterdam/ ("tussen al het groen en aan het water") ; https://noorderpark.nl/agenda/culturele-picknick/ (park's own site: picnic on the field next to the restaurant). Caveats: barbecue only in parts.

17. **Oeverpark, the lawn next to Eye** (IJpromenade, Overhoeks, Noord) (N; E nearby: "Iepenarboretum (elm arboretum), Oeverpark", nfa75togmm7bbycca6kaue36): a big soft lawn on the IJ, one ferry hop from Centraal, with the old-city skyline across the water and sun until late. Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open (the city plans a larger IJ-Oeverpark here). Evidence: https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/picknicken-in-amsterdam/ ("een groot grasveld aan het water ... uitzicht op de skyline") ; https://thesecret.app/secrets/1244/oeverpark ("quickly becoming one of the most popular picnic spots"). Caveats: never swim here (IJ shipping lanes, banned); few trees.

18. **The roof of NEMO** (Oosterdok 2, Centrum) (E: "NEMO Science Museum", cd6lwb2flcsk50v3nn5z2u7u): a free stepped roof square over the harbour where you may bring your own picnic; on summer Thursdays it stays open into the evening. Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open; summer late openings (late June to end August). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/international/what-to-do-in-amsterdam-top-100-activities~b69533b1/ (Parool top 100, no. 37: "you are allowed to bring your own picnic") ; https://www.nemosciencemuseum.nl/zomer-op-het-dak (own site: open till 21:00 on summer Thursdays, free) ; https://www.kidsproof.nl/amsterdam/uitje/nemo-science-museum-dakplein-eten-drinken-lunch-nemo-uitje/ . Caveats: overlaps the terraces slice (roof bar); steps, but a lift inside the museum.

19. **Park Frankendael** (Middenweg, Watergraafsmeer, Oost) (E: "Frankendael", vg0z6rjngmso1otuczhltuth): the quieter alternative to the Oosterpark: barbecue allowed throughout, lawns by the old country house and its gardens. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/barbecueen-in-amsterdam/ ("Te druk in het Oosterpark? Ga naar het Park Frankendael") ; https://vrijetijdamsterdam.nl/maatschappij/picknicken-in-amsterdam-dit-zijn-leuke-picknickplekken/ ; https://www.timeout.com/amsterdam/things-to-do/best-parks-in-amsterdam . Caveats: the summer angle is "picnic and barbecue", not much more.

20. **Sarphatipark** (De Pijp, Zuid) (E: "Sarphatipark and the Sarphati monument", pjz6wbejfbc7mb01wbtrucmo): De Pijp's bike-free picnic lawn behind the Albert Cuyp market. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.timeout.com/amsterdam/things-to-do/best-parks-in-amsterdam ("great for people watching or enjoying a picnic on the grass") ; https://www.amsterdam-nu.nl/parken-en-tuinen/17130-wat-te-doen-in-het-sarphatipark-tips-voor-een-bezoekje.html ; https://www.reddit.com/r/Amsterdam/comments/1sngf4b/beste_park_in_amsterdam_poll/ (lead only). Caveats: small and full on sunny days.

21. **Amstelpark** (Europaboulevard, Zuid) (E: "Amstelpark", zsu0hyso8k4q0b13sp14xnwt): the calm, groomed park for a summer picnic, with the rose garden, the mini train and the petting farm for kids. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/picknicken-in-amsterdam/ ; https://vrijetijdamsterdam.nl/maatschappij/picknicken-in-amsterdam-dit-zijn-leuke-picknickplekken/ ; https://www.timeout.com/amsterdam/things-to-do/best-parks-in-amsterdam . Caveats: the summer angle is mild (calm, not lively).

22. **Speeleilanden and the kinderbaden, Amsterdamse Bos** (islands in the Grote Vijver; Klein and Groot Kinderbad; outside Amsterdam (municipality Amstelveen)) (N): the kids' summer: play islands reached by rope bridges and a pull ferry, and two paddling pools filled from May to September when it is over 20 degrees. Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal (May to September). Evidence: https://www.amsterdamsebos.nl/kinderen/kinderbaden/ (official) ; https://www.kidsproof.nl/Amsterdam/eropuit/Amsterdamse-Bos-Gemeente-Amsterdam/zwem-in-het-amsterdamse-bos ; https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/getipt/bbq-in-amsterdam-hier-mag-het-in-het-openbaar~423171/ . Caveats: islands being renewed 2025 and 2026, fewer play structures in 2026; overlaps water-openair (splash) slice; could merge with item 1.

23. **Weesperzijde quay by Café Hesp** (Weesperzijde, loswal near Hesp, Oost) (N; Hesp not on the map): sitting on the Amstel quay with your legs over the water on a hot evening, one of Oost's three official summer hotspots. Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.amsterdam.nl/stadsdelen/oost/zomerrecreatie-oost/weesperzijde/ (official) ; https://oost-online.nl/zomers-warm-lekker-zwemmen-buiten-of-binnen-dat-kan-in-oost/ ; https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/genieten-5-keer-plekken-om-te-picknicken-in-amsterdam~322896/ ("Aan de Amstel ... altijd gezellig druk"). Caveats: the city says swimming here is in principle NOT allowed (working-boat mooring), Oost-Online says people swim: we must not recommend it; barbecue ban.

24. **Het Stenen Hoofd** (Westerdoksdijk, end of the pier) (E: "Stenen Hoofd", qb7k7ve5iibqgyyv1o0lgrci): the summer pier: evenings on the stones looking over the IJ, the neighbourhood's free end-of-summer festival (Nazomerfeest). Kind: Hangout and sunset. Status: open; events seasonal. Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/een-avondwandeling-langs-het-stenen-hoofd~bbfc3e20/ (Parool, paywalled) ; https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/uittips/weekendtips-deze-dingen-doe-je-in-amsterdam-van-4-tot-en-met-6-september~af75746c/ ("lekker nazomeren op het Stenen Hoofd") ; https://www.stenenhoofd.nl/events/lijst/ . Caveats: swimming banned with fines since 2023 (say so plainly).

25. **Jeugdland's natural pool** (Valentijnkade 131, edge of the Flevopark, Oost) (N): a wild nature playground where kids build huts and, in summer, swim in a supervised natural pool. Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal (summer holidays, afternoons). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/international/summer-vacation-2026-in-amsterdam-the-best-tips-and-outings-for-children-and-parents~b134e642/ ; https://oost-online.nl/zomers-warm-lekker-zwemmen-buiten-of-binnen-dat-kan-in-oost/ . Caveats: kids 6 to 12 with a swim diploma only; may suit water-openair better.

26. **Wertheimpark** (Plantage Parklaan, Centrum) (E: "Wertheimpark, the park that lost its name", t2x3qk1pd08zzz0jizj17ttc): a small, old park on the Nieuwe Herengracht where you can picnic, and even grill until 21:00, right on the water. Kind: Park and shade. Status: open. Evidence: https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/doen/genieten-5-keer-plekken-om-te-picknicken-in-amsterdam~322896/ ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/barbecueen-in-amsterdam/ . Caveats: small; weakest of the list.

## Borderline

- **Siloterras, end of the Silodam** (E: "Silodam (MVRDV)", f7vjybzqezxdn3wnm9jumub9): residents' mini-terrace with two picnic benches facing the IJ sunset; only Parool's readers' list (NL 2025 and EN 2026, same source), so one source.
- **Jan Schaeferbrug** (N): wide, open sunsets from the bridge to the Java-eiland; Parool readers' list only.
- **Distelwegkade, Noord** (N): sun setting into the western harbour; Parool readers' list only.
- **Ruwelswal, Zuidoost** (N): meadows, cows, a windmill and benches at the city edge at sunset; Parool only.
- **Ringdijk sunset walk** (E: "Ringdijk and the Watergraafsmeer polder", cfpl1jwwcnurv2cxrr0z0ptb): benches over the Ringvaart walking into the setting sun; Parool only. Could be a summer text on the existing place.
- **Dageraadsbrug and De Kop van Oost** (N): sunset along the Nieuwe Vaart axis around 21 June; Parool only; the terrace is for the terraces slice.
- **Entrepotdok** (E: "Entrepotdok, the bonded warehouses behind a wall", elt3qfrn5jv7jt44s2d9amcj): benches and sunsets (Parool) and swim jetties (Oost-Online), but busy with boats: never recommend swimming; a summer text on the existing place is the most it earns.
- **Sluishuis public jetties, IJburg** (E: "Sluishuis", dueh7jpz2cx3g7v0nk7e6m6z): seats on the floating jetties for summer sunsets (Vera Wandelt blog; De Brugkrant review mentions the sunset on the façade); no newspaper rates it as a summer spot.
- **Vecht banks in Weesp and the Roskambrug** (N): picnic on the Vecht by Bastion De Nieuwe Achtkant (Vrije Tijd Amsterdam) and the sunset behind mill 't Haantje (Parool); two different spots, one source each.
- **Vogeleiland "mijmerboom", Amsterdamse Bos** (N): a forester's favourite beech with benches over the water (indebuurt); not a summer rating.
- **Vliegenbos** (E: "W.H. Vliegenbos", ac6mfnqh892uoglflsnng7ul): shady elm wood, picnic and play (Kidsproof, city page); no source rates it as a summer spot, but it is the shade answer for Noord.
- **De Ceuvel** (E, ihp6bionlwz4429w1gxf33uo): jetties on the water in Noord; it is a café terrace, so for the terraces slice.
- **Beatrixpark** (E, vxk4tfmr0lq9hw05ppszm99o): children's paddling pool (Time Out); one source.
- **Heemtuin Sloterpark and Het Ruige Riet** (E, e19ubdiixvglexkf54mxc4w3): "mini Veluwe" quiet (Parool top 100 reader tip); not summer-specific.

## Checked and dropped

## Anything odd

- The municipality runs "Zomerrecreatie in Oost" with wardens, extra toilets and annual surveys for exactly three spots: Bogortuin, Park Somerlust and the Weesperzijde. That is an official map of where Oost goes on a hot day, worth quoting.
- Two of those three (Bogortuin, Weesperzijde) are places where local media call swimming normal while the city says it is in principle not allowed. Texts must not tell people to swim there.
- Barbecue rules disagree between sources (Flevopark's field, Erasmuspark's field, Sloterstrand): writers should check maps.amsterdam.nl/bbq at drafting time and keep the wording general.
- The Amsterdamse Bos, Flevopark, Noorderpark and Sloterpark have no park-level place on the map (only sub-places). The coordinator may want one place per specific summer spot (as above) rather than the whole park.
- Het Parool's Dutch "relaxplekken" series (per district, 2023) is paywalled; titles confirm it exists but its picks could not be read.
