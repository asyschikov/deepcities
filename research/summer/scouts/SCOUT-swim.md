# Scout: swim (Magic of Summer)

Slice: swimming spots, beaches and outdoor pools. Shortlist only, nothing drafted.
Researched 25 Nov 2026 via BrowserOS (Google, Parool, AT5, NH Nieuws, AD, YLBB, Vogue NL, De Westkrant,
Oost-Online, 1Amstelveen) plus the official layers.

## Official baseline (read this first)

- amsterdam.nl says the city has **9 official open-water swim spots**; the municipality's own swim map
  layer (maps.amsterdam.nl/zwemwater, open data `KAARTLAAG=ZWEMWATER`) lists as "Zwemplek":
  Strand IJburg, Diemerpark, Marineterrein, Nieuwe Meer (noordoever, westzijde/Oeverlanden, zuidoever =
  the Amsterdamse Bos steiger), Amsterdamse Bos Grote Speelweide, Gaasperplas (strand zuidoever,
  speelvijver noord), De Hoge Dijk (strand, speelsloot), and just outside: Het Twiske (Does, Vennegat),
  Ouderkerkerplas (zand, groen). Outdoor pools: Brediusbad, Noorderparkbad, Flevoparkbad, De Mirandabad,
  Sloterparkbad, Sportplaza Mercator, Victoriabad (Weesp).
  https://www.amsterdam.nl/sport/zwemmen-in-amsterdam/zwemmen-open-water/
- zwemwater.nl (the provinces' official register) per spot, 4-year water-quality class at season end 2026:
  Strand IJburg uitstekend; Diemerpark uitstekend; Nieuwe Meer West (Oeverlanden) uitstekend; Nieuwe Meer
  Noord (sampled via West) uitstekend; Nieuwe Meer Steiger (Bos) uitstekend; De Hoge Dijk strand
  uitstekend, speelsloot goed; Gaasperplas Strand Zuidoever goed; Gaasperplas Noord **slecht**;
  Amsterdamse Bos Grote Speelweide **slecht**; Marineterrein **slecht** (new since 2025, see item);
  Ouderkerkerplas zand and groen aanvaardbaar (groen has a permanent negative advice per Parool);
  Twiske: Leers uitstekend, Baaiegat/Kure-Jan/Speelsloot goed, Vennegat/Schoorl/Poort aanvaardbaar,
  Doesstrand class slecht with a **negative swim advice**. Season everywhere: 1 May to 30 Sept.
- **Sloterplas is NOT an official spot** since 2023 (E. coli from birds, blue-green algae); swimming is
  at own risk. https://www.amsterdam.nl/leefomgeving/parken-recreatiegebieden/sloterpark/waterkwaliteit-sloterplas/
- After heavy rain the water board (AGV) issues a 72-hour negative advice for all Amsterdam outside
  water (e.g. 29 Jun to 1 Jul 2026, AD/indebuurt). Good line for the map intro.
- Chemicals: Natuur & Milieu / NIOO report (AT5, 3 Sep 2026; Parool 6 Sep 2026) found too many
  chemicals (PFOS, flame retardants, mercury) at Gaasperplas, Ouderkerkerplas (10 substances), IJmeer
  at IJburg and Diemerpark (8), Nieuwe Meer (7), Twiske. Researchers stress one swim does not make you
  ill. Mention neutrally, don't scare.
  https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/239754/onderzoek-zwemwater-gaasperplas-nieuwe-meer-en-ouderkerkerplas-zwaar-vervuild-met-chemicalien

## Shortlist (best first)

1. **Strand IJburg** (Pampuslaan, Strandeiland, IJburg) (N): Amsterdam's only real sand beach on the
   IJmeer: towel in the sand, shallow clean water rated "uitstekend", surf and SUP lessons, volleyball,
   and the tram back to town. Kind: Beach. Status: seasonal (lifeguards and toilets 1 May to 30 Sept);
   **closed Oct 2026 to 1 May 2027, then a smaller beach until after 2031** (a lock and bridge go where
   sand is now). Evidence: https://www.amsterdam.nl/leefomgeving/parken-recreatiegebieden/strand-ijburg/ ;
   https://www.amsterdam.nl/projecten/strandeiland/deelproject/strand-ijburg-verkleining/ ;
   Parool list 22 May 2026 (https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/waar-kun-je-buiten-zwemmen-in-amsterdam-dit-zijn-de-beste-zwemplekken-van-de-stad~bdefe879/) ;
   Vogue NL 22 May 2026 ; zwemwater.nl "IJburg" (Pampuslaan). Caveats: shrinking from 2027, text must
   say so; beach wheelchair on loan from the beach concierge; dogs banned 1 Apr to 1 Oct. The old
   Blijburg pavilion is gone (closed 2018), its successor building is Strandlokaal + De Japanner.

2. **Binnenhaven, Marineterrein** (Kattenburgerstraat 5, Centrum) (E: "Binnenhaven, the Marineterrein's
   swimming harbour and water lab", prjxiu18of2b2u2epr860f47): the old navy harbour, fenced off from
   boats by a floating boardwalk, where the centre swims till late with the city skyline beyond and
   picnics on the shady lawn. Official since 1 May 2025, first in the Centrum. Kind: Swim spot.
   Status: seasonal (official season May to Sept). Evidence: Parool 20 Feb 2025 (https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/binnenhaven-marineterrein-eerste-officiele-zwemplek-in-amsterdamse-binnenstad-historische-beslissing~b4581ebe/) ;
   NH Nieuws 20 Feb 2025 (https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/345985/wildzwemplek-marineterrein-wordt-eerste-officiele-zwemplek-in-de-binnenstad) ;
   NH Nieuws 18 Jun 2026 (water good) ; YLBB 21 Jun 2026 ; Vogue NL 2026. Caveats: very crowded on
   hot days; zwemwater.nl shows the 4-year class as "slecht" (probably thin data since 2025, check before
   writing); after heavy rain a 72-hour negative advice is posted (Marineterrein Instagram, 2026).

3. **Nieuwe Meer, the Oeverlanden beaches** (Anton Schieperspad, Nieuw-West) (E: "De Oeverlanden",
   avse5r5gizki80pu54k5gnfp): three small sand beaches in a wild strip on a deep, clear lake, with the
   Zuidas skyline across the water; Parool's motto for them: "leven en laten leven". Kind: Swim spot.
   Status: seasonal (official May to Sept). Evidence: zwemwater.nl "Nieuwe Meer West" (uitstekend, three
   beaches) ; Parool 29 Jul 2023 (https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/op-de-drie-strandjes-van-de-nieuwe-meer-is-het-motto-leven-en-laten-leven~b7b6c8e2/) ;
   Parool list 2026 ; YLBB 2026. Caveats: lake is ~30 m deep, steep in places; chemicals report (7
   substances). The Nieuwe Meer Noord beach is official too, sampled together with West; can be named in
   the same text.

4. **Zwemsteiger Nieuwe Meer (Amsterdecks), Amsterdamse Bos** (south-east shore of the Nieuwe Meer, by
   the A4; Amsterdam, managed by the Bos) (N): a long public swim deck in the woods, ladders into
   "surprisingly clear" water, live water temperature online; the Bos's quiet alternative to the Grote
   Vijver. Kind: Swim spot. Status: seasonal (official May to Sept). Evidence: zwemwater.nl "Nieuwe Meer
   Steiger" (uitstekend) ; https://www.amsterdamsebos.nl/kinderen/kinderbaden/ ; Vogue NL 2026 ;
   1Amstelveen 11 Aug 2025 (https://1amstelveen.nl/veilig-zwemmen-in-natuurwater-kans-op-klachten-groot-in-amsterdamse-bos/) ;
   Parool list 2026. Caveats: a walk from the nearest entrance; no lifeguard.

5. **Gaasperplas beaches** (Strand Zuidoever, Valburgdreef, and the new accessible beach by the Bastion,
   Gaasperpark, Zuidoost) (E: "Gaasperpark", g4igs6lw3gmky63u4cq5wz41): broad sand beach and lawn on
   the sand-pit lake of the Bijlmer, barbecues allowed, metro to the door; since summer 2026 a third
   beach with a Mobi-Mat so wheelchair users can roll into the water on their own. Kind: Beach / Swim
   spot. Status: seasonal (official May to Sept). Evidence: zwemwater.nl "Strand Zuidoever" (goed) ;
   municipality press release (https://www.amsterdam.nl/bestuur-organisatie/college/wethouder/lian-heinhuis/persberichten-nieuws-lian-heinhuis/rolstoeltoegankelijk-zwemstrand/) ;
   Parool list 2026 ; YLBB swim and city-beach lists 2026. Caveats: **Speelvijver Noord has class
   "slecht", no swimming there** (Parool); new beach is not yet official (two seasons of sampling
   started 2026); chemicals report (10 substances). Best as a child place or a summer text on Gaasperpark.

6. **Diemerpark beach** (Jan Beijerpad, north-east side of the park, facing Haveneiland) (E: "Diemerpark
   and De Ontmoeting", kapgrw5hh9ge5asngh71p0pr): a small sand beach on the quiet water between the park
   and IJburg, rated "uitstekend", where you can swim in a wild park without the IJburg crowds.
   Kind: Swim spot. Status: seasonal (official May to Sept). Evidence: zwemwater.nl "Diemerpark" ;
   Parool list 2026 ; AT5 18 Jul 2024 (https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/227715/het-wordt-tropisch-warm-waar-kan-je-zwemmen-in-amsterdam).
   Caveats: swimming only at the beach, not elsewhere in the park (Parool); chemicals report.

7. **Flevoparkbad** (Insulindeweg 1002, Oost) (N): the 1967 open-air bath by Enrico Hartsuyker on the
   edge of the Flevopark, a 50 m pool, a shallow pool and a big lawn; saved from closure in 2014 by
   swimmers who formed a club and open the gate at dawn and in the evening. Kind: Swim spot (outdoor
   pool). Status: seasonal (late April to early Sept). Evidence: Oost-Online 3 Aug 2024 (https://oost-online.nl/met-trots-kijken-oprichters-terug-op-10-jaar-vereniging-flevoparkbad/) ;
   YLBB pools 29 Aug 2026 (https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/buitenzwembaden-amsterdam-en-omgeving/) ;
   Parool 21 Apr 2026 (outdoor pools open 28 April) ; https://www.flevoparkbad.com/achtergronden/historie/ .
   Caveats: paid entry; the "best outdoor pool" claims on AmsterdamNow are unverified, don't use.

8. **De Mirandabad, the outdoor pool** (De Mirandalaan 9, Rivierenbuurt) (E: "De Mirandabad, the
   Mediterranean of 1979", p3mdn6b7ibk2rdoeih7asilw): the 50 m outdoor pool with lawn and play ship,
   reopened in July 2024 after a ten-month rebuild; Parool's PS calls it the bath where you see "all
   classes, nationalities and colours". Kind: Swim spot (outdoor pool). Status: seasonal (late April to
   Sept). Evidence: AT5 2024 ; Parool PS 28 Apr 2025 (https://www.parool.nl/ps/amsterdamse-buitenbaden-zijn-weer-open-in-dit-bad-zie-je-alle-standen-nationaliteiten-en-kleuren~b626cee5/) ;
   YLBB pools 2026. Caveats: paid; the existing text is about the indoor tropical pool, summer text
   should be about the outdoor 50 m.

9. **Brediusbad** (Spaarndammerdijk 306, Spaarndammerbuurt) (E: "Brediusbad and the Bredius
   sign-sculpture", rhx9rdb7j9oia0wpum8n6m5a): a heated 1957 open-air pool hidden behind two hotel
   towers, small, green and neighbourly. Kind: Swim spot (outdoor pool). Status: seasonal (late April to
   Sept). Evidence: YLBB pools 2026 ; Parool 21 Apr 2026 ; Parool 4 Aug 2020 (https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/afkoelen-het-kan-in-deze-amsterdamse-buitenbaden~be420243/) ;
   municipal swim layer. Caveats: paid.

10. **Noorderparkbad, outdoor pools** (Sneeuwbalweg 5, Noord) (E: "Noorderparkbad",
    g4u6mhmp7639tu3359rn2lpo): the 2015 pool in the Noorderpark, outdoor pools open in summer next to
    the park's lawns. Kind: Swim spot (outdoor pool). Status: seasonal outdoor part (late April to
    Sept), indoor all year. Evidence: YLBB pools 2026 ; AT5 2024 ; Parool 21 Apr 2026. Caveats: paid.

11. **Sportplaza Mercator, outdoor 50 m** (Jan van Galenstraat 315, West) (E: "Sportplaza Mercator",
    x9jffzoxm1lvuu3ud06mgq3n): a 50 m outdoor pool framed by the complex's planted green walls, with a
    kids' pool and lawn; up to 1,500 swimmers on a warm day (Parool 2020). Kind: Swim spot (outdoor
    pool). Status: seasonal (May to end Aug). Evidence: Parool 4 Aug 2020 ; YLBB pools 2026 ; AT5 2024.
    Caveats: paid, run by Sportfondsen.

12. **Ouderkerkerplas, Zandstrand** (Middenweg, south of Ouderkerk aan de Amstel; outside Amsterdam,
    municipality Ouder-Amstel) (N): a 200 m sand beach on a big recreation lake, a bike ride along the
    Amstel, lunch on an Ouderkerk terrace after. Kind: Beach / Swim spot. Status: seasonal (official May
    to Sept). Evidence: zwemwater.nl "Ouderkerkerplas Zandstrand" (aanvaardbaar) ; Parool list 2026 ;
    YLBB 2026 ; 1Amstelveen 2025. Caveats: **Groenstrand (the lawn side) has a permanent negative swim
    advice**; blue-green algae can occur; chemicals report (10 substances); fatal swimming accident 2024
    (deep water off the line).

13. **Het Twiske beaches** (Noorderlaaik / Zuiderlaaik; outside Amsterdam, municipalities Oostzaan and
    Landsmeer, Den Ilp) (N): Noord's summer escape by bike from the ferry: seven official beaches on
    the Stootersplas and other lakes, a kids' paddling ditch, rowing boats, a naturist beach (Baaiegat).
    Kind: Swim spot. Status: seasonal (official May to Sept). Evidence: zwemwater.nl (Leers uitstekend,
    Baaiegat, Kure-Jan, Speelsloot goed) ; Parool list 2026 ; Vogue NL 2026 ; YLBB 2026. Caveats:
    **Doesstrand has a negative swim advice (4-year class slecht)**; paid parking; festivals on summer
    weekends; chemicals report. Best as one place for the recreation area, naming Leers and Kure-Jan.

14. **De Hoge Dijk, beach and play ditch** (Abcouderstraatweg, between the AMC and Abcoude; zwemwater
    gives an Abcoude address, the city lists it as an Amsterdam spot, check the border) (N): a quiet
    recreation area with a shallow fenced swim area (max 1.4 m) and a long shallow "speelsloot" with
    sand on both sides for toddlers. Kind: Beach / Play and splash. Status: seasonal (official May to
    Sept). Evidence: zwemwater.nl (strand uitstekend, speelsloot goed) ; Parool list 2026 ;
    zuidoost.nl 12 Jun 2023 (https://zuidoost.nl/2023/06/12/goed-vertoeven-aan-de-hoge-dijk/) .
    Caveats: AT5 2024 still listed a negative advice for the strand; current class is uitstekend.

15. **Sloterstrand, Sloterplas** (Oostoever, Sloterpark, Nieuw-West) (E: "Sloterplas: the lake dug for
    sand", yzhlf1o6uh09s9zgn6obz6b4): the sandy city beach on the big lake of Nieuw-West, sun, SUP and
    canoe, sunset drinks at Buiten and Het Boothuis. A **beach, not a recommended swim**. Kind: Beach.
    Status: open (beach); swimming unofficial since 2023. Evidence: amsterdam.nl water-quality page ;
    Parool 3 May 2023 (https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/sloterstrand-geen-officiele-zwemplek-meer-volgepoepte-zwemluiers-en-blauwalg-doen-stadsstrand-de-das-om~be215532/) ;
    De Westkrant 27 Jan 2023 (https://www.dewestkrant.nl/sloterstrand-verliest-status-officiele-zwemplek/) ;
    YLBB city beaches 12 Aug 2026. Caveats: E. coli from birds and blue-green algae; the city tests three
    times a week and posts signs at the beach; dogs banned on the beach.

16. **Grote Vijver swimming area, Amsterdamse Bos** (by the Grote Speelweide, Amstelveen side of the
    Bos) (N): the classic family day in the Bos: lawn under tall trees, a swim area 1 to 1.5 m deep,
    canoes and pedal boats for hire on the west side, adventure islands with a hand ferry. Kind: Swim
    spot / Play and splash. Status: seasonal (official May to Sept). Evidence: Parool list 2026 ; Vogue
    NL 2026 ; amsterdamsebos.nl 31 Jul 2026 ; zwemwater.nl. Caveats: **4-year class "slecht" and a
    negative advice from 7 Aug 2025 (1Amstelveen); check zwemwater.nl before writing it up as a swim
    spot**; may fit better as a Park/Play place (overlap with water-openair: canoe hire, kinderbaden).
    Play islands partly renewed 2025/2026.

17. **Het Eikenhout swim deck, Houthavens** (Revaleiland 500, West) (E: "Waterplein, Revaleiland (Het
    Eikenhout)", b7b1k9r5nsngy3ao6tori847): broad timber steps into the Houthaven behind hotel The July,
    evening sun till late; West's newer swim spot. Kind: Hangout and sunset (swim tolerated). Status:
    open (no season). Evidence: YLBB 2026 ; Vogue NL 22 May 2026. Caveats: **unofficial, untested water,
    own risk; YLBB warns of currents and boats**. Keep the text about the deck and the sun, not a dive tip.

18. **Erasmuspark swim jetty, Admiralengracht** (Erasmuspark, Bos en Lommer) (E: "Erasmuspark",
    esioyoe9ueen62my3l2cdxo1): a small jetty and lawn where West's neighbours cool off in the canal;
    a 2023 test found the water "good". Kind: Swim spot (unofficial). Status: open. Evidence: Parool list
    2026 ; Vogue NL 2026 ; De Westkrant 21 Jun 2023 (https://www.dewestkrant.nl/waterkwaliteit-wildzwemplek-bij-erasmuspark-goed/) .
    Caveats: unofficial, not monitored by the province; it is a canal, small boats pass. Borderline on
    the user's "canals with boat traffic" rule; I'd use it only as a summer note on the existing park.

19. **Strandzuid** (Europaplein 22, by the RAI, Zuid) (N): a sand beach bar on the water by the RAI, 21
    seasons by 2026, beds in the sand, Friday drinks, big screens for football summers. No swimming.
    Kind: Beach. Status: seasonal (terrace season, roughly April to Sept/Oct). Evidence: YLBB city
    beaches 2026 (https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/stadsstranden-in-amsterdam/) ; Strandzuid's own
    Instagram ("21 seizoenen", 2026) and site (https://strand-zuid.nl/). Caveats: overlaps with the
    terraces slice; evidence is one blog plus own channels, weak on independent press.

20. **Kaap** (IJdijk 10, tip of Zeeburgereiland) (N): a rough city beach at the far end of the island,
    feet in the sand, wide views over the water, dancing on summer weekends. No swimming. Kind: Beach.
    Status: seasonal. Evidence: YLBB city beaches 2026 ; halloijburg.nl (https://halloijburg.nl/locatie/1215/kaap-amsterdam) ;
    https://kaapamsterdam.nl/ . Caveats: overlaps with terraces; party venue more than a beach; YLBB
    says explicitly you can't swim there.

## Borderline

- **Park Somerlust, Amstel** (Amstelkwartier, Oost): beloved lawn on the Amstel with steps into the
  water (Parool 2026, YLBB 2026, AT5). The city says it is not an official spot, water untested, boat
  traffic on the Amstel. Strong as a green hangout; hand to green-hangout, no swim recommendation.
- **De Ceuvel** (Korte Papaverweg 4, Noord) (E: "De Ceuvel", ihp6bionlwz4429w1gxf33uo): Noord's
  favourite jetty dive (Parool, YLBB, Vogue, CityMom 2026), but residents warned of polluted sludge in
  the Johan van Hasseltkanaal (Parool 19 Aug 2020) and Parool still advises not to swallow water. Better
  as a terrace/hangout place than a swim tip.
- **Durgerdam, "derde bocht"** (Durgerdammerdijk, IJmeer): Parool 2026 and Tuinpark Tuinwijck mention
  jumping off the dike into the IJmeer. Unofficial, no monitoring, one strong source only.
- **Oosterbad** (Aalsmeer, outside Amsterdam): natural-water bath since 1928, official on zwemwater.nl,
  Parool 2026 and 1Amstelveen 2025. But it is Aalsmeer, a long ride; "for Amstelveners" (Parool).
- **Sloterparkbad, outdoor pool** (Slotermeerplein, Nieuw-West): 25 m outdoor pool on the Sloterplas
  (AT5 2024, municipal layer), but the city plans to **roof it over** (Uitvoeringsagenda zwemmen
  2025-2028), which would end it as a summer place. Hold until the decision.
- **Victoriabad** (Basisweg 1, Weesp, now Amsterdam): has an outdoor pool on the city's map layer, but
  no local press rating it as a summer spot found.
- **Amstelbad** (Ouderkerk aan de Amstel, outside): heated outdoor pool, only YLBB found.
- **Amsterdam City Swim** (6 Sep 2026, Keizersgracht, Amstel, Nieuwe Herengracht): the only legal way
  to swim the canals, a one-day event for ALS, not a place. Maybe a line in the intro or the Amstel text.
- **Gibraltarbadje and the other city paddling pools** (E: "Keesmanblokken and Gibraltarbadje",
  mzfvepsc894gk6vm26je3bcu; municipal layer lists 15 peuterbadjes incl. Vondelpark, Westerpark,
  Oosterpark, Beatrixpark, two in the Bos): belong to the water-openair slice (Play and splash).

## Checked and dropped

- **Weesperzijde, "Costa del Weesper"** (by Café Hesp): top of Parool's 2026 list and in YLBB/Vogue,
  but the municipality says swimming there is "in principe niet toegestaan" (a commercial mooring
  wharf), water untested. Not a swim spot; maybe a hangout for green/terraces.
- **Bogortuin** (Java-eiland): very popular lawn, but the IJhaven is an active inland port and the city
  says swimming is "in principe niet toegestaan". Hangout only.
- **Borneokade swim jetty**: a court ruled the recreational use doesn't fit the zoning plan; the city
  fenced it on 30 May 2026, then shortened it in a settlement (NOS 1 Jun 2026, AT5 5 Jun 2026, Parool
  4 Jun 2026). Contested, enforced: drop.
- **Entrepotdok**: jetties by Artis, but Parool 2026 warns of heavy boat traffic. Canal with boats: drop.
- **Levantkade / KNSM jetties, Entrepothaven (Cruquius)**: Parool-only, unofficial, near shipping; drop.
- **Stenen Hoofd** (E: qb7k7ve5iibqgyyv1o0lgrci): swimming banned with a 150 euro fine since 2023
  (AT5). The beach and events belong to green-hangout/terraces, never as a swim spot.
- **Pllek** (E: onzcl74s6kps21bou4msg3mt): YLBB mentions "zwemmen in het IJ" there; that is the busy
  IJ, unsafe. Terrace/beach slice only, no swim mention.
- **Duivendrechtse brug** (Amstel): Parool says "also for diving", but boats pass; diving off bridges
  is exactly what we shouldn't recommend.
- **Spaarnwoude naturist pond** (Genieweg, Velsen): the only official Spaarnwoude bathing water is a
  small naturist pond with blue-green algae warnings in 2025 and June 2026 (NH Nieuws). No "Spaarnwoude
  beach" found on the official list.
- **Olympisch Stadion jetties** (YLBB): single source, unofficial canal: drop.
- **Gaasperplas Speelvijver Noord, Ouderkerkerplas Groenstrand, Twiske Doesstrand**: negative advice;
  mention only as warnings inside their parent places.
- **Blijburg**: the beach pavilion closed (AD 2018); its Instagram says it closed with a "Grande Finale".
- **Strand van Oost** (rooftop beach on Orchid House, Linnaeusstraat): new, one blog only; terraces
  slice can judge.

## Odd things worth knowing

- The official count is moving: the city says 9 spots; AGV said 10 with the Marineterrein (2025); the
  Gaasperplas accessible beach is in its two-season trial. Re-check zwemwater.nl in May 2027 before
  publishing swim texts.
- Several official spots carry 4-year class "slecht" (Grote Speelweide, Gaasperplas Noord,
  Marineterrein). For the Marineterrein that looks like a data artefact of a new site (NH Nieuws called
  it good in June 2026); for the Grote Speelweide it matches the 2025 negative advice.
- Strand IJburg is the map's best swim place and it is about to shrink for five years. Worth saying
  plainly rather than leaving it off.
- Many of the city's most-loved swim spots (Weesperzijde, Bogortuin, Borneokade, Somerlust) are
  officially "not allowed" or untested; the municipality runs summer enforcement there. The honest
  framing is lawn and hangout, which the green slice can own.
- Browser note: my BrowserOS tab was reported as a "user tab" on close (session id changed after
  naming); it was the tab I opened for this research and it is now closed.
