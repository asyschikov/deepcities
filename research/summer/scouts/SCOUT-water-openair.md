# Scout: water-openair (on the water, open-air culture, splash spots)

Scouted 2026-11-25 with BrowserOS (DuckDuckGo, Parool incl. PS and the Parool bucket list, YLBB, local and kids' blogs, venues' sites). Existing places checked with `pnpm -s dc places sights --all --q`. Event series are tied to the place they happen in; where the place is already on Deep Amsterdam it is marked (E).

## Shortlist (best first)

1. **Vondelpark Openluchttheater** (Vondelpark; exact spot to pin on site) (N): free open-air stage in the Vondelpark, almost every summer weekend: concerts, comedy, dance, Saturday family shows; you bring a blanket and stay for the evening. Locals' institution for over fifty summers, kept free with a collection tin. Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal (May to mid-September). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/toegang-tot-het-vondelpark-openluchttheater-is-al-50-jaar-gratis-en-blijft-dat-ook-anders-is-de-lol-er-meteen-af~bad06046/ ; https://www.parool.nl/redactie/parool-bucketlist/index.html (bucket list entry) ; https://www.openluchttheater.nl/editie/2026/ . Caveats: very full on sunny weekends; funding has wobbled (city subsidy saved it, Parool); step-free from the park paths.

2. **Pluk de Nacht on the Stenen Hoofd** (Stenen Hoofd, Westerdoksdijk) (E: "Stenen Hoofd", qb7k7ve5iibqgyyv1o0lgrci): open-air film festival on the old stone pier every August since 2003, beach chairs facing the IJ, odd and arthouse films; the films can be followed free from the terrace, and there is a free kids' afternoon (Pluk Mini). Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal (about ten days in late August; 2026: 19 to 29 August). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/films-kijken-onder-de-sterren-8x-openluchtbioscopen-in-amsterdam~b9ebc65b/ ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/amsterdam-openlucht-bioscopen/ ; https://www.stenenhoofd.nl/event/filmfestival-pluk-de-nacht/ . Caveats: swimming off the Stenen Hoofd is banned (fines since 2023), say so in the text; rain cancellations.

3. **Waterspeelplaats Gaasperplas** (Gaasperpark, near metro Gaasperplas) (E: "Gaasperpark", g4igs6lw3gmky63u4cq5wz41): the best water playground in town for small kids: a play pond, a stream full of boulders, a complex of little sluices with pumped water, a cable ride over the water and a mini beach. Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal (water pumped April to October per Parool). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (ranked no. 1) ; https://www.parool.nl/redactie/parool-bucketlist/index.html ; https://www.groengebied-amstelland.nl/activiteiten/zwemmen/waterspeelplaats-gaasperplas/615 (official). Caveats: not a swim spot itself (the Gaasperplas beach is the swim slice's); busy in school holidays.

4. **Amsterdamse Bostheater** (De Duizendmeterweg 7, Amsterdamse Bos; outside Amsterdam (Amstelveen)) (N): open-air theatre in the woods: a big summer play every year (a tradition of almost forty years), concerts, family shows and film nights (Cinetree's Forest Film Festival). Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal (May to September). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/films-kijken-onder-de-sterren-8x-openluchtbioscopen-in-amsterdam~b9ebc65b/ (Cinetree) ; https://amsterdamlokaal.nl/concerten-amsterdamse-bostheater/ ; https://bostheater.nl/ons-programma/theatervoorstellingen/ . Caveats: ticketed; mosquitoes and cold evenings; reached by a footpath from the road.

5. **Speeleilanden and kinderbaden, Amsterdamse Bos** (Grote Vijver and Grote Speelweide; outside Amsterdam (Amstelveen)) (N): the Bos's summer water corner for families: play islands in the Grote Vijver reached by wobbly rope bridges and a hand-pulled ferry, plus the big and small paddling pools by the Speelweide. Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal (summer). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (no. 10, "two pools in the Bos") ; https://go-kids.nl/amsterdam/10x-lekker-buiten-zwemmen-in-en-om-Amsterdam ; https://www.kidsproof.nl/amsterdam/uitje/Amsterdamse-Bos-Gemeente-Amsterdam-Speeleilanden-in-het-bos-zomer-buitenspelen/ ; https://www.visitamstelveen.nl/nl/locaties/4001734818/groot-kinderbad . Caveats: Grote Vijver swim water quality is the swim slice's call (check zwemwater.nl); muddy.

6. **Kanoverhuur Amsterdamse Bos** (Grote Speelweide, on the Grote Vijver; outside Amsterdam (Amstelveen)) (N): the classic summer canoe trip: hire a canoe, pedal boat or electric boat and follow the marked water routes through the woods, then sit on its terrace. Kind: On the water. Status: seasonal. Evidence: https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/kajakken-kanoen-in-amsterdam/ ; https://ideeenvoordates.nl/amsterdam/kano-amsterdamse-bos/ ; https://www.kanoverhuur-adam.nl/ . Caveats: no newspaper write-up found, two blogs plus the operator; could merge with item 5 as one "Grote Vijver" place.

7. **Prinsengrachtconcert** (Prinsengracht in front of the Pulitzer hotel) (N): one August evening a year since 1981, the country's biggest free classical open-air concert, musicians on a pontoon in the canal, thousands on the quays, bridges and in boats. Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal (one Saturday in late August; 2026 held on 22 August). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/redactie/parool-bucketlist/index.html ; https://prinsengrachtconcert.nl/stichting-prinsengrachtconcert/ ; https://www.npoklassiek.nl/concerten/bbaaced4-4259-4be6-af09-82c6da68a7d9/prinsengrachtconcert-2026 . Caveats: a single night; boat places need registration; very crowded quays.

8. **De Parade in the Martin Luther Kingpark** (Martin Luther Kingpark, on the Amstel) (N; the park's statue is E: "Martin Luther King, the statue that came without a permit", odzemquiwlg0dgkj52qfi9si): the travelling theatre festival's tents, short shows, food and terraces fill the park for two and a half weeks each summer; the KinderParade is for kids. Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal (late July to early August; 2026: 24 July to 9 August; 2027 announced). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/een-patatje-rendang-een-witbiertje-en-een-voorstelling-dit-moet-je-doen-en-zien-op-theaterfestival-parade~b9b6e831/ ; https://www.parool.nl/ps/lof-en-afkeer-zijn-op-de-parade-weer-gegarandeerd-maar-het-circus-met-vrijgevochten-artiesten-in-het-martin-luther-kingpark-is-meer-dan-een-yuppenfeest~bca029b8/ ; https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/de-parade-2026-amsterdam~ae590d73/ . Caveats: entry plus show tickets; since when in this park not yet confirmed.

9. **Free IJ ferries, above all the NDSM ferry** (Centraal Station back, to NDSM-werf and Buiksloterweg) (N): the cheapest summer outing in town: a free boat across the IJ with the breeze in your hair; the long NDSM crossing is a mini cruise, best on a light evening. Kind: On the water. Status: open (all year; a summer evening thing). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/redactie/parool-bucketlist/index.html ("Met de pont over het IJ ... een gratis minivakantie") ; https://www.reiswijs.nu/post/amsterdam-met-de-pont-over-het-ij ; https://hello-amsterdam.com/nl/fietsroutes/noord-free-ferry . Caveats: rush-hour bike crowds; a transport line rather than a spot (pin it at the NDSM landing).

10. **Pierenbad Oosterpark** (Oosterpark, opposite the Tropenmuseum) (N, or fold into E "Oosterpark and its music dome", iytb6uj68x6hg73hr1k2gwpg): Amsterdam's favourite paddling pool, an Aldo van Eyck design, renovated, with a white dome to slide off, fountains, a big lawn and toilets. Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal (filled on warm days, May to September). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (no. 2) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/overzicht-pierenbadjes-amsterdam/ ; https://www.mytravelboektje.com/buiten-zwemmen-met-kids-in-amsterdam . Caveats: around a thousand users a day on hot days (Parool on the cleaning contractor); small children only.

11. **Oosterpark and its music dome** (Oosterpark) (E: "Oosterpark and its music dome", iytb6uj68x6hg73hr1k2gwpg): the park of free summer music: Amsterdam Roots Open Air (world music, late June), the Keti Koti commemoration on 1 July, picnic chamber concerts in the dome, and the Concertgebouworkest's free Opening Night in September. Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal events. Evidence: https://oost-online.nl/t-is-zomer-muziek-in-de-open-lucht/ ; https://www.amsterdamroots.nl/ ; https://www.concertgebouworkest.nl/nl/verhalen/gratis-openluchtconcert-in-het-oosterpark/ ; https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/238907/vandaag-is-het-keti-koti-dit-is-er-te-doen-in-de-stad . Caveats: Keti Koti is a commemoration first, word it with care; green-hangout slice may also claim Oosterpark.

12. **The roof of NEMO** (Oosterdok 2) (E: "NEMO Science Museum", cd6lwb2flcsk50v3nn5z2u7u): the free sloping roof square with a water cascade kids play in, a terrace and one of the best views over the city; in summer Thursday evenings with live music, and a few rooftop film nights. Kind: Play and splash. Status: open (roof free; summer programme late June to August). Evidence: https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/overzicht-pierenbadjes-amsterdam/ (water play on the roof) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/amsterdam-openlucht-bioscopen/ (rooftop cinema) ; https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/7-gratis-uitkijkpunten-voor-het-mooiste-uitzicht-over-de-stad~b5063807/ (lead picture) ; https://www.nemosciencemuseum.nl/zomer-op-het-dak . Caveats: stairs from the east side, or the lift inside the museum; windy.

13. **Westerpark spartelvijver** (Westerpark) (E: "Westerpark", w4lbzyt68totp1szvnlsss9h): a long, chlorine-free paddling pond cleaned by filters, its water then used on the park's plants; on hot days the shrieks carry across the park. Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal (warm days, May to September). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (no. 3) ; https://www.dewestkrant.nl/lekker-spartelen-in-peuterbadjes-in-de-buurt/ ; https://www.kidsproof.nl/amsterdam/uitje/kidsproof-amsterdam-spartelvijver-westerpark-zwemmen-kinderen/ . Caveats: no dogs, no glass; it stayed shut in 2020 (natural water, covid rules), so check yearly.

14. **Pllek's Films with a View** (Pllek beach, NDSM) (E: "Pllek", onzcl74s6kps21bou4msg3mt): every Tuesday all summer, classics on the city beach with headphones, deck chairs and the IJ behind the screen. Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal (Tuesdays, early summer to September). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/films-kijken-onder-de-sterren-8x-openluchtbioscopen-in-amsterdam~b9ebc65b/ (no. 1, "vaste prik") ; https://pllek.nl/en/films-with-a-view/ ; https://www.cinema.nl/artikelen/overzicht-alle-filmvertoningen-in-de-openlucht-2026 . Caveats: overlaps with the terraces slice (Pllek as beach bar): one place, two hooks; no swimming in the IJ here.

15. **Movies @ H'ART** (courtyard garden of H'ART Museum, Amstel 51) (E: "H'ART Museum in the Amstelhof", rdwnvux0lssmnnlu9hihuuzy): in August the Amstelhof's big inner garden becomes the city's largest open-air cinema, nineteen evenings of classics after sunset. Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal (August; 2026 was the eighth edition). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/films-kijken-onder-de-sterren-8x-openluchtbioscopen-in-amsterdam~b9ebc65b/ ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/amsterdam-openlucht-bioscopen/ ("misschien wel de meest romantische") ; https://www.hartmuseum.nl/activiteiten/movies-hart-museum/ . Caveats: ticketed; the garden is not open as a hangout outside the screenings.

16. **Vondelpark pierenbad** (Vondelpark, end of the Kattenlaan) (N): probably the best-known paddling pool in town, ringed by a lawn of lounging parents, a sandpit next to it and the Blauwe Theehuis and Groot Melkhuis close by. Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal (warm days, May to September). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (no. 6) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/overzicht-pierenbadjes-amsterdam/ ; https://www.mytravelboektje.com/buiten-zwemmen-met-kids-in-amsterdam . Caveats: very busy; exact spot to pin on site.

17. **Haarlemmerplein fountain** (Haarlemmerplein) (E: "Haarlemmerplein, gate and dune water", c2dej0dnbopnqgyrgp2caway): a playful ground fountain that turned a dull square into the Jordaan's hot-day magnet: kids lie down in the jets, parents watch from the café terraces. Kind: Play and splash. Status: open (fountain runs in the warm months). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (no. 4) ; https://www.parool.nl/zomer-in-amsterdam/zo-trotseren-amsterdammers-de-hitte-in-de-fontein-van-het-haarlemmerplein-is-het-heerlijk-afkoelen~b89570e2/ ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/overzicht-pierenbadjes-amsterdam/ . Caveats: none big.

18. **The Amstel by canoe, SUP or sloep, to Ouderkerk** (start points Weesperzijde 1094 and Jachthaven Berlage, Amsteldijk 132A; Ouderkerk aan de Amstel is outside Amsterdam (Ouder-Amstel)) (N): paddle or putter up the Amstel out of the city, past windmills and polder, to the terraces of Ouderkerk; the hot-day escape from the centre by water. Kind: On the water. Status: seasonal (rental spring to autumn). Evidence: https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/suppen-in-amsterdam/ ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/kajakken-kanoen-in-amsterdam/ ; https://skippertje.com/blog/sloeproute-amstel-ouderkerk-aan-de-amstel/ . Caveats: the YLBB pieces are one outlet; busy river with rowing clubs and tour boats, not for wobbly beginners; better as a route than a pin.

19. **Waterland by whisper boat** (Holysloot (Amsterdam), Broek in Waterland and Overleek (outside Amsterdam: Waterland / Landsmeer)) (N): hire a silent electric boat or canoe and glide through peat-meadow ditches and wooden villages just north of the city, picnic hamper on board. Kind: On the water. Status: seasonal (rental roughly April to October). Evidence: https://www.gezinopreis.nl/fluisterboot-waterland/ ; https://kidseropuit.nl/theetuin-overleek-fluisterboot-waterland/ ; https://www.laagholland.com/nl/blogs/mee-met-renee-fluistervaren-waterland ; https://holyboot.nl/ . Caveats: family blogs and the regional tourism site, no newspaper; municipality of Overleek to confirm; pick one start point (Holysloot keeps it inside Amsterdam, next to the existing "Pontje Holysloot", z3uwtm32mgwtriuh8jjsnhte).

20. **Pampus ferry from IJburg** (departs IJburg; Forteiland Pampus is outside Amsterdam (Gooise Meren)) (N): the summer boat from IJburg across the IJmeer to the fort island, a 45-minute crossing to a 19th-century fort in the water. Kind: On the water. Status: seasonal (weekends and school holidays, April to end October in 2026). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/redactie/parool-bucketlist/index.html ("Bootje huren en het IJ op") ; https://veerponten.nl/veren/amsterdam-ijburg-pampus-muiden/ ; https://navigoamsterdam.nl/veerdienst-amsterdam . Caveats: the Parool mention is one line; operator-run since 2010 (pontjes.nl), service details change yearly.

21. **Artisplein mist creek** (Artisplein, Plantage Kerklaan) (E: "Artisplein, a corner of the zoo you can sit in for free", hbiljbj4a04sg7rihtumeb3f): a misting creek with the flamingos in view where toddlers cool off while parents sit on the café terrace. Kind: Play and splash. Status: open. Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (no. 8) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/overzicht-pierenbadjes-amsterdam/ . Caveats: small; a summer line on the existing text rather than a draw on its own.

22. **Beatrixpark pierenbad and its little mermaid** (Beatrixpark, by the Boerenweteringpad) (E: "Beatrixpark", vxk4tfmr0lq9hw05ppszm99o): the neighbourhood paddling pool with a copper mermaid and a part-roofed edge for shade. Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal. Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (no. 11) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/overzicht-pierenbadjes-amsterdam/ . Caveats: mainly local.

23. **Bellamyplein pierenbad** (Bellamyplein, Oud-West, by De Hallen) (N): a renovated paddling pool in the middle of a city square, then on to the Foodhallen; Parool calls it "a Parisian city park". Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal. Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (no. 9) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/overzicht-pierenbadjes-amsterdam/ . Caveats: small, neighbourhood scale.

24. **Noorderpark pierenbad** (Noorderpark, Adelaarsweg side) (N): Noord's paddling pool, with the nature playground and the Noorderparkkamer café nearby. Kind: Play and splash. Status: seasonal. Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/ps/dit-zijn-de-11-beste-pierenbadjes-in-amsterdam~b7a8a686/ (no. 5) ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/overzicht-pierenbadjes-amsterdam/ . Caveats: modest; could be one line in a Noorderpark summer text.

25. **West Beach Film Festival at the Sloterplas** (Sloterstrand / Bastion, President Allendelaan 3) (E: "Sloterplas: the lake dug for sand", yzhlf1o6uh09s9zgn6obz6b4): free films from beach chairs and hammocks on the Sloterplas shore, five days, running for over twenty years. Kind: Open-air culture. Status: seasonal (mid-September). Evidence: https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/films-kijken-onder-de-sterren-8x-openluchtbioscopen-in-amsterdam~b9ebc65b/ ; https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/amsterdam-openlucht-bioscopen/ ; https://www.rodi.nl/amsterdam-nieuw-west/307489/west-beach-film-festival-gratis-aan-de-sloterplas . Caveats: September, the tail of summer; the swim slice will have the Sloterplas, so this is one more line in its summer text.

## Borderline

- Cityscape Cinema (A'DAM Lookout roof, Hotel Arena garden): in Parool and YLBB, but a ticketed commercial pop-up whose venues move.
- Bijlmerbios Openluchtbios (Zuidoost): free and loved, but its square changes every year (2026: Annie Romeinplein and Bijlmerplein), and it is September.
- Cinema at the Zuidas (George Gershwinplein): a single free evening in early September.
- Grachtenfestival (August, 100+ venues, floating stages): strong summer event but city-wide, no one place.
- Rijksmuseum garden fountain (jets every twelve seconds): one Parool PS mention (2019) only; could be a line on the existing "Rijksmuseum garden" (sc0avxwg8c6l1o0p7g4v98q1).
- Jeugdland natural pool (Valentijnkade 131): Parool kids' summer tip; belongs to the swim slice.
- Canal boating in a hired electric sloep (Sloepdelen, Mokumboot, Boaty, Ecoboats): what locals do with friends on a summer evening, but businesses with no fixed place and busy canals; maybe one general tip.
- SUP at the Nieuwe Meer (Suppen Amsterdam in Park de Oeverlanden, Sup & Meer on the Boeierspad): rental bases; a line for existing "De Oeverlanden" (avse5r5gizki80pu54k5gnfp).
- Twiske Haven boat hire (Oostzaan): rowing boats, canoes, whisper boats; overlap with the swim slice's Twiske, and NH Nieuws has reported swim bans there for blue-green algae.
- Pontje Holysloot (E, z3uwtm32mgwtriuh8jjsnhte): charming ring-the-bell ferry but no source rates it as a summer outing.
- Kano Amsterdam Noord (Noorder IJplas), Kanovereniging Sloterplas, Kano op IJburg, WSZ Gaasperplas windsurfing: clubs, mostly members only.
- Pure Markt (Frankendael, Amstelpark): monthly Sunday market all year, not a summer place.
- Rollende Keukens (Westergas, Ascension week in May): spring, one week.
- Keti Koti Festival on the Museumplein (1 July): one day; mention under Oosterpark instead.

## Checked and dropped

- Over het IJ Festival (NDSM): stopped after 32 years when its subsidy ended (Volkskrant, NDSM).
- Kwaku (Nelson Mandelapark): stops after the 2026 edition in its current form (Parool, July 2026); the existing place already says so.
- Open Air Film Festival on the Mercatorplein: one-off for WorldPride 2026.
- FC Buitenbios (FC Hyena terrace), Goethe-Institut garden films, 't Blauwe Filmhuis: a few nights each.
- Midnight Market (NDSM): a winter market.
- Rialto: no open-air programme in the 2026 Parool (8x) or YLBB (11x) lists.
- Westergas open-air films: none in 2026 lists; Westergas only had Rollende Keukens and a Grachtenfestival piano in the pond.
- Canal swims: only as official events (Amsterdam City Swim), left to the swim slice.

## Anything odd

- Many "places" in this slice are events with a fixed home. I tied each to its venue; the coordinator may prefer summer lines on existing places (Stenen Hoofd, Sloterplas, Oosterpark, H'ART, Pllek) rather than new pins.
- The Bostheater, Kanoverhuur, Speeleilanden and kinderbaden all sit on the Amstelveen side of the Bos (postcode 1182); they could be one cluster with the green-hangout slice's Amsterdamse Bos entries.
- The 2019 Parool PS pierenbad ranking is old, but the 2026 Parool list (paywalled) and the municipality's 15 peuterbadjes confirm the pools are still filled each summer; pierenbadjes open only on warm days (above 20 degrees).
- The Pampus ferry and Waterland whisper boats have the weakest newspaper backing; keep them only if the map wants a couple of day trips.
- Parool's 2026 open-air cinema list does not include the Vondelpark Openluchttheater or the Bostheater's own film nights apart from Cinetree, so cinema evidence there leans on the theatres' own programmes.
