# Food, drink and shops, Centrum: merged shortlist

Test (user): worth visiting even if you don't eat, drink or buy anything. Details, sources and
status per item: scouts/SCOUT-FW.md (Jordaan, west canals), SCOUT-FC.md (old centre),
SCOUT-FS.md (south canals, east), SCOUT-FI.md (eateries inside existing places).
Duplicates between scouts merged (Hoppe, Pilsener Club, De Dokter, Stubbe's, D'Vijff Vlieghen).
