# SCOUT-FW: food, drink and shops, Centrum west

Scouted 2026-11-25 for the food, drink and shops round (`../SCOUT-BRIEF.md`). Slice FW: the
Jordaan, the Haarlemmerbuurt with the Western Islands, the western Grachtengordel (Singel to
Prinsengracht, north of the Leidsegracht), the Negen Straatjes, and the Spui and the west side of
the Kalverstraat (Begijnensteeg, Rozenboomsteeg, Voetboogstraat, Raamsteeg, south Spuistraat).
Scouting only: nothing written to the map, no commits.

**28 candidates (FW-01 to FW-28)**: bars and cafés 13, proeflokalen 1, eateries 4, food shops 4,
shops 2, markets 4. Rank proposals: Nice 12, Okay 14, near miss 2 (FW-18, FW-22, kept numbered so
the user can overrule), no Amazing (see "For the user"). Plus a near-miss table (16) and notes on
places already in the pool.

Pins: PDOK address points (door of the house number), 6 decimals; market and stall pins are
marked "approximate". Pool checked read only with `pnpm -s dc places sights --all --q` and a full
`--all` dump (2,455 places), and against `SCOUT-FI.md` sections 1 and 3.

## Bars and cafés

**FW-01. Café Hoppe, the Spui's brown café of 1670.** Spui 18-20 (pin 52.368775, 4.888615,
no. 18). Hook: a narrow standing bar with jenever casks behind it in a house that has served
drink, by its own account, since 1670, and where the Spui's Provo-era crowd met. Why: one of
the city's oldest and best-known brown cafés; interior old and unchanged in character, no music
policy; the Resistentieorkest's founders (Hub Mathijsen, Theo Kley, Max Reneman) met here in the
Provo years (nl.wikipedia, Resistentieorkest); the café also claims Hans van Mierlo laid the
groundwork for D'66 here (own site, claim only). Status: open (iamsterdam-listing Oct 2025 and own
site; no closure news), checked 2026-11-25. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spui_(Amsterdam)
("het uit 1670 daterende café Hoppe"); second: https://cafehoppe.com/about/ ; EN
https://www.diffordsguide.com/bars/p9yxr7/cafe-hoppe . Pool: new. Related: place
`x8gqs47530f2hdx8zylghgpu` (Hoppe's wine house, Egelantiersgracht) tells the Hoppe firm; no place
names the café. Caveats: 1670 is the café's own date, repeated by Wikipedia; the D'66 line is the
café's claim; Difford's says interior and exterior are listed, not checked in the register. Rank
**Nice**.

**FW-02. 't Smalle, Pieter Hoppe's distillery on the Egelantiersgracht.** Egelantiersgracht 12
(pin 52.376731, 4.884133). Hook: Pieter Hoppe started his liqueur distillery here around 1780;
the house is now the brown café whose jetty terrace was "maybe the most photographed terrace in
the city" until the city made it go. Why: distillery and tasting-room origin (allowed type), brass
and old fittings kept, and a fight locals and the city had about it: the jetty lay there without
a permit from 1987, the city refused it in November 2021, the court case followed in 2022, and
the D66 Centrum group notes neighbours' complaints and a new owner. Status: café open (own site
t-smalle.nl, 2026); whether the jetty is gone or was saved: check (AT5 and NH Nieuws 2022 say it
had to go; the owner was trying to keep it). Lead: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/217326/terrassteiger-van-jordanees-cafe-t-smalle-moet-na-jaren-gedogen-weg ;
second: https://pont.media/jurisprudentie/eclinlrbams20227418/ (ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2022:7418);
Parool headline "Amsterdams café 't Smalle raakt terrassteiger kwijt: 'Eeuwig zonde, dit is ons
uithangbord'"; EN https://www.timeout.com/amsterdam/bars-and-pubs/t-smalle . Pool: new (not
named in the Hoppe's wine house place two doors on: link them). Caveats: 1780 vs 1786 in tourist
sources; the 1780 date needs a non-tourist source (Hoppe firm history, Schiedam heritage page says
Pieter Hoppe bought an Amsterdam wine business and then set up a liqueur distillery, no year for
this house). Rank **Nice**.

**FW-03. Café Chris, the Westerkerk builders' pub (legend) and film set.** Bloemstraat 42 (pin
52.374266, 4.881577). Hook: tradition says the builders of the Westerkerk were paid out here in
1624; what is certain is that the tiny café was the set of the 1934 Jordaan film De Jantjes and
has carried the name Chris since "Ome Chris en Tante Annie" took it over in 1957. Why: claimed
oldest café of the Jordaan; a film-history tie; local voice: "Bep van café Chris" stood behind the
counter for thirty years (WJGR 00000188, 2021 obituary by Kees Roorda); Mokum Magazine notes the
men's toilet whose flush is outside the door. Status: open, Nel Mulder runs it (Mokum Magazine
23 Oct 2024). Lead: WJGR 00000042 (2013, "in Café Chris, ooit het decor van De Jantjes");
second: https://www.mokummagazine.nl/2024/10/23/cafe-chris-zon-naam-is-een-begrip-daar-moet-je-niet-aankomen/ ;
Parool teaser 30 Jun 2023 ("volgens de overlevering ... schaftlokaal voor de arbeiders die de
Westerkerk bouwden"); nl.wikipedia Amsterdam lists it among the oldest cafés. Pool: new. Caveats:
1624 and the pay-out story are tradition only (tell as legend); the De Jantjes set needs a second
source (film archive or Eye). Rank **Nice**.

**FW-04. Café Nol, the Jordaan's sing-along living room.** Westerstraat 109 (pin 52.378274,
4.883484). Hook: red velvet, chandeliers and Dutch hits; the founder wanted the café to look
exactly like her own front room. Why: "het beroemdste café van de Jordaan" (Parool, 50th
anniversary teaser), founded 1966 by Nol and Miep Uilenbroek, run today by their granddaughter
Anne-Marie Grijzenhout and Lisa Grijzenhout; the café once ran a street festival on the
Westerstraat that stopped in 1986 when police and fire brigade could no longer guarantee safety
(Parool teaser). The 1934 house is a municipal monument whose register text names the café as a
base of Jordaan music. Status: open; licence renewed 2026 (gazette gmb-2026-227454), Koningsdag
permits 2025 and 2026. Lead: https://www.mokummagazine.nl/2024/08/21/cafe-nol-door-de-hele-jordaan-bekend/ ;
second: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/gmb-2026-227454.html and Parool teasers
("Anne-Marie Grijzenhout van Café Nol"; "Café Nol viert 50e verjaardag in Paradiso"). Pool:
mentioned in `ui99513tg73p0s7b963pjnhq` (Westerstraat's 1930s rebuild) and cluster
`f0lvb8rajf5liwoz7e2038r2` (SCOUT-FI 3.2). Caveats: 1966 (Mokum) vs a 50th birthday whose year the
teaser does not show: check. A mapstr listing says "opened 2024": probably a refit; check the
interior is the old one. Rank **Nice** (pair with De Twee Zwaantjes, `bbq1ip4pzsis5dx8vh8b2uze`).

**FW-05. Café Hegeraad, the Noordermarkt's round table.** Noordermarkt 34 (pin 52.379390,
4.886101). Hook: a brown café with slijterij on the corner of the Boomstraat for over a century,
whose back room gave its name to the "Hegeraadconferentie", the round-table talks where the
Jordaan museum was planned. Why: authentic interior (the cover photo of the 2025 book on 38
authentic brown cafés); neighbourhood role: WJGR 00000088 (2005) on the Hegeraad conferences;
WJGR 00000085 on "Ome Jan van café Hegeraad", who grumbled at street theatre but lent money;
the StadsdorpBuurt7 neighbours' coffee every Thursday (Zeven 134, 2022; 142, 2024). Status:
open (Zeven 142, 2024; own site). Lead: Stadsarchief beeldbank 010122054294 ("Noordermarkt 34:
café en slijterij Hegeraad"); second: WJGR 00000088 (2005), Zeven 142 (2024); Parool headline
4 Oct 2025 (photo caption "Café Hegeraad op de Noordermarkt"). Pool: new; the square is told in
`ug76c3fail8cl23ygpumw6xc` and `o6t15j96yfw3mt3t6b8hjcds`. Caveats: founding year not found in a
checkable source ("more than 100 years", amsterdamsights). Rank **Nice**.

**FW-06. Café Kalkhoven at the foot of the Westertoren.** Prinsengracht 283 (pin 52.373896,
4.883417), corner Westermarkt. Hook: casks still behind the bar from which drink was once
poured, and the corner where Princess Beatrix turned up unannounced for a coffee on 9 August
1973. Why: old brown café ("officieel 1670", Mokum Magazine), no music; local voice: WJGR
00000128 calls it a café "zonder muziek en dus bij uitstek geschikt om een goed gesprek te
voeren"; WJGR 00000039 tells the Beatrix visit. Status: open (Zeven 138, 2023; own site). Lead:
WJGR 00000039; second: https://www.mokummagazine.nl/2024/09/18/cafe-kalkhoven-andre-van-duin-drinkt-er-graag-een-glas/ ;
EN https://cityinsider.nl/explore-collection/cafe-kalkhoven . Pool: new (next to the meat hall
place `oy6h22rulfpcieac38r0rag7`). Caveats: 1670 is the café's own date. Rank **Okay**.

**FW-07. Café Pieper on the Leidsegracht corner.** Prinsengracht 424 (pin 52.366207, 4.882315).
Hook: a sagging brown café with leaded windows that says it has poured since 1665, long the
lawyers' local while the court sat next door. Why: one of the old brown cafés of the western
canal belt; same house as the herring-buss stone place. Status (coordinator's question): open;
the café's own site says it "recent een kleine make-over" got: a new bar, a new drinks buffet and
taps in the wall, "met behoud van de oude stijl". So the bar counter is new. It sells the Van Wees
liqueurs as "onze Jordaansche distilleerderij 'de Ooievaar'": that wording suggests a link with
the Van Wees family business, but neither the Pieper nor the De Ooievaar site states shared
ownership (De Ooievaar names only its own tasting room on the Herengracht). Checked 2026-11-25.
Lead: https://cafepieper.nl (make-over text; photos uploaded 2023-04, menus 2025-06); second:
iamsterdam listing ("verzakt, maar prachtig in oude staat"; tourism portal, lead only). Pool:
same house as `g7blhkpokmrafj22p1wp7uqz` (In de Buis); the café is not named there. Caveats:
interior partly renewed; ownership open (KvK extract would settle it); 1665 is the café's claim.
Rank **Okay** (fold into In de Buis as a Visiting line unless the old interior is confirmed).

**FW-08. De Pilsener Club, "De Engelse Reet".** Begijnensteeg 4 (pin 52.369542, 4.890852).
Hook: a café with no bar counter: drinks are poured in a back room and carried to your table, as
they have been since 1899. Why: opened 1893 on the Spui as Zum Pilsner Club, here since 1899,
renamed in May 1940 to shed the German name; nickname from the dead-end alley ("reet") by the
English church of the Begijnhof; voted Amsterdam's "meest monumentale bruine kroeg" in a 2023
Parool poll; sand lay on the floor until recently. Status: open (listings updated June 2026; no
closure news). Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Pilsner_Club_(Amsterdam) (links an Ons
Amsterdam article); second: https://www.beeradvocate.com/mag/17547/a-look-inside-the-pilsener-club-one-of-amsterdams-storied-brown-cafes/ (EN).
Pool: new (SCOUT-BC listed it under borderline). Caveats: the Ons Amsterdam article (robots:
browser only) would be the authoritative second source for the 1940 rename. Rank **Nice**.

**FW-09. Café De Dokter, the smallest bar in town.** Rozenboomsteeg 4 (pin 52.369254,
4.890826). Hook: a one-room café of 1798, opened by a surgeon, still in his family, so small
that a handful of regulars fill it. Why: claimed smallest and among the oldest cafés; old
interior. Status: open (own Instagram hours Wed to Sat, 2026; no closure or sale notice; the
2026 licence in the gazette for the steeg is for no. 10A, not the café). Lead:
https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/cafe-de-dokter (EN); second: nl.wikipedia Amsterdam (lists
De Dokter among the oldest cafés) and https://www.diffordsguide.com/pubs-and-bars/1300/amsterdam/cafe-de-dokter .
Pool: new (SCOUT-CC borderline). Caveats: the 1798/family facts rest on tourist and bar-guide
sources: needs a local or archival second source before it goes in. Rank **Okay**.

**FW-10. Café Gollem, where Amsterdam's special-beer cafés began.** Raamsteeg 4 (pin 52.370252,
4.888715). Hook: in 1974 this pocket café between the Spuistraat and the Singel started pouring
Belgian beers its owner fetched in his car boot; it is counted as the city's first special-beer
café. Why: origin of a whole Amsterdam café type; in a former liqueur distillery (claim). Status:
open (own site and Instagram, "speciaalbier sinds 1974", 2026). Lead: SENB beer-culture database
https://www.nederlandsebiercultuur.nl/databank/entiteit?item-id=2700 ; second:
https://www.diffordsguide.com/pubs-and-bars/1295/amsterdam/cafe-gollem (EN; founder Gerard
Nooter). Pool: new. Caveats: "first" needs a stronger source (PINT or Parool); distillery claim
unsourced. Rank **Okay**.

**FW-11. Café Brandon, the corner that slept for eighteen years.** Keizersgracht 157 (pin
52.375401, 4.886439). Hook: after the 1940 bomb wiped out the family's first café on the
Blauwburgwal, the Brandons kept this corner; it stood shut for eighteen years behind a sign "Niet
te koop en niet te huur" until 2001, and now opens its doors every other Thursday for the
neighbourhood's over-seventies. Why: local fabric and a war thread; landlady Connie once refused
Queen Beatrix because she came before opening time; Drs. P drank his coffee here daily (Zeven
143, 2024). Status: open, Rogier Wolffenbuttel; seniors' coffee still running (Zeven 150, March
2026; Zeven 152, 2026). Lead: Zeven 143 (June 2024) https://oudestadt.nl/wp-content/uploads/Zeven143.pdf ;
second: Zeven 150 (2026); SCOUT-DW's Zeven 143 p3 and 151 p5. Pool: new; the 1940 bombing is in
DW-17's place (Blauwburgwal). Caveats: the 1630 builders' canteen story is unsourced: leave out;
the interior is a 2001 re-creation with stuffed animals, so it passes on story, not interior.
Rank **Okay**.

**FW-12. Café Lowietje, the Noord-Jordaan's singers' café.** Derde Goudsbloemdwarsstraat 2,
corner Goudsbloemstraat (pin 52.380336, 4.882330). Hook: the little corner café where the
Toppers turned up after an Arena show, Dré Hazes shot his video clip "Leef!", and Baantjer was
filmed. Why: a living base of Jordaan levenslied culture; Tino Martin sang here before he was
known (WJGR 00000012, 2015). Status: open Fri to Sun (own site cafelowietje.nl, 2026); new
owners Rick Lelieveld and Mark Bruyns took over in 2015. Lead: WJGR 00000013 (2015, "Buurtcafé
Lowietje"); second: own site; Parool not found. Pool: new. Caveats: needs a second independent
source (Parool, AT5); story is recent (no old history). Rank **Okay** (or a line in a "singing
cafés of the Jordaan" cluster with Nol and De Twee Zwaantjes).

**FW-13. Café 't Monumentje, the laundry that became a brown café.** Westerstraat 120 (pin
52.378515, 4.883100). Hook: a wash house and before that a tobacconist, a brown café since 1973,
small and crammed with pictures. Why: local fabric; a 1974 national-archive photo series
"Bruine cafés in Amsterdam" already shot it. Status: open (Mokum Magazine Aug 2024; own site).
Lead: https://www.mokummagazine.nl/2024/08/13/cafe-t-monumentje-iedereen-is-er-welkom/ ; second:
Nationaal Archief / ANP photo 1974 (via picryl). Pool: new. Caveats: thin story. Rank **Okay**;
I lean near miss.

## Proeflokalen

**FW-14. A. van Wees distillery De Ooievaar, the last craft distiller in Amsterdam.**
Driehoekstraat 10 (pin 52.382650, 4.883244). Hook: on a three-way crossing at the far north-west
tip of the Jordaan, "de enige overgebleven ambachtelijke distilleerderij van Amsterdam" still
makes some 18 genevers and 60 liqueurs, with a family member as the stoker. Why: the producer
behind the Herengracht tasting room already on the map; firm lines back to De Ooievaar in The
Hague (1782) and A. van Wees (1883, here since 1922); shop and tours (by booking, max 18) let you
see the stills. Local voice: the Driehoekstraat "volgens velen een van de schilderachtigste
plekjes van Amsterdam" (WJGR 00000010, 2015). Status: shop and webshop live, tours bookable
(de-ooievaar.nl, 2026); family-run (ABN AMRO interview Nov 2025, per search). Lead: WJGR
00000010 (2015 p5) and SCOUT-DW (WJGR 2022, 00000199 p4: a hundred years in the family); second:
https://de-ooievaar.nl/nl/ and https://www.diffordsguide.com/producers/749/a-van-wees-distilleerderij-de-ooievaar (EN).
Pool: new; the tasting room is `ii2mr8hcrgobefbv3gqt3d6f` (Herengracht 319): link, or bring the
distillery into that place as a second pin. Caveats: a working distillery, so "visiting" means
the shop and booked tours; 1782/1883/1922 dates to keep apart. Rank **Nice**.

## Eateries

**FW-15. De Gouden Reael, the inn of Jan Mens's novels.** Zandhoek 14 (pin 52.388107, 4.889983).
Hook: the corner house of 1648 on the Zandhoek has been a café or inn since 1824 and gave its name
to Jan Mens's 1940 novel cycle about herberg De Gouden Reael, and through it to the whole
neighbourhood. Why: history and literature in a Stadsherstel house (restored 1978); the
neighbourhood paper and wijk carry the name "Gouden Reael". Status: open as De Gouden Reael (by
Caron), French bistro of the Caron family since late 2019 (uitagenda and Yelp 2026). Lead: Parool
PS "Familie Caron blaast nieuw leven in De Gouden Reael" (teaser names Jan Mens, 1940, Zandhoek
14); second: https://stadsherstel.nl/monumenten/in-den-gouden-reael/ and
https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2021/03/25/dit-eten-zit-donders-goed-in-elkaar-wij-werden-gelukkig-a4037065 .
Pool: the house and its stone are told in `tjc2u1sr8nq7x2pl7x69ol3a` and
`jdfyx4qn1j0wqb374nvf0h99` (Zandhoek); the inn and the novels are not. Better as a bring-forward
(Visiting line plus the Jan Mens sentence) than a separate pin. Caveats: "since 1824" from a
freelancer platform: needs the Stadsherstel or Mens source. Rank **Nice** (as an addition to the
Zandhoek place).

**FW-16. D'Vijff Vlieghen, a restaurant through five 17th-century houses.** Spuistraat 294-302

houses, hung with Rembrandt etchings and old Dutch objects. Why: interior to see. Status: open
(listings 2025). Lead: https://www.timeout.com/amsterdam/restaurants-and-cafes/dvijff-vlieghen

## Bakeries, fish and food shops

**FW-19. Stubbe's Haring at the Haarlemmersluis.** Singel at the Haarlemmersluis (pin about
52.378570, 4.892380, approximate: the stall on the lock bridge). Hook: herring was salted, packed
and traded round this lock for centuries; the Stubbe family stall has stood here since 1976 (the
family says it has sold herring since 1903). Why: the classic Amsterdam herring stall at the spot
the herring packers worked (pool: Haringpakkersbrug place nearby); also the subject of a
2024-25 fight over permits, as the city ended permanent pitches for herring and flower stalls.
Status: open (2026 listings). Lead: Parool "Vijftien nieuwe plekken en geen vaste vergunning
meer voor haring- en bloemenkar" (teaser: "Vanaf 1976 staat Stubbe's Haring er"); second:
Telegraaf "Amsterdam jaagt haringkar de stad uit"; Zeven 117 (2017) on lorries stopping at the
herring stall on the bridge. Pool: new; near `esde0b83ua681iohkzcvkglh` (Haringpakkersbrug) and
`wfib983r2q1fwii6pg8j7taj` (De Kruiwagen). Caveats: 1903 vs 1976; pin to be set on the stall.
Rank **Okay**.

**FW-20. Wegewijs, the Rozengracht grocer of 1905.** Rozengracht 32 (pin 52.373781, 4.881582).
Hook: fifteen years after the canal was filled, Hermanus Wegewijs opened a butter and cheese shop
here; still a traditional counter shop, still "voor de gewone Amsterdammer". Why: a 120-year-old
neighbourhood grocer; owner Piet Buis for three decades refuses to court TikTok queues. Status:
open (Parool PS 2025 teaser; own site). Lead: Parool PS "Hoe kruidenier Wegewijs al bijna 120
jaar de 'gewone Amsterdammer' bedient" (teaser: opened 1905); second: https://wegewijs.nl/over-ons/ .
Pool: new. Caveats: interior age not checked (photo needed). Rank **Okay**.

**FW-21. Fromagerie Abraham Kef, where French cheese came to Amsterdam.** Marnixstraat 192
(pin 52.371617, 4.875836). Hook: in 1953 a cheese man from France opened a shop on the
Marnixstraat for French raw-milk cheese, a novelty in a city of Gouda. Why: pioneer shop at the
same address for 70 years; the Jordaan buys its cheese board here (WJGR 00000090 column). Status:
open, run by Marike van der Werff with more branches (MT Sprout; own site 2026). Lead:
https://www.abrahamkef.nl/pages/over-ons ; second: https://mtsprout.nl/groei/marketing/kok-wordt-kaasimporteur .
Pool: new. Caveats: "first French cheese shop in NL" not stated by a source: say "pioneer".
Rank **Okay**.

**FW-22. Het Oud-Hollandsch Snoepwinkeltje.** Tweede Egelantiersdwarsstraat 2 (pin 52.376901,
4.881899). Hook: glass jars of old Dutch sweets, a shop "zoals in grootmoeders tijd". Status:
open. Lead: own site; WJGR many mentions (mostly ads). Caveats: a nostalgia shop, not an old
one (founding year not found). Rank: **near miss**.

**FW-24. Antiekcentrum Amsterdam (De Looier), the antiques market in a car garage.**
Elandsgracht 109 (pin 52.368799, 4.878805). Hook: in 1978 a Peugeot garage on the Elandsgracht
became De Looier, now the country's largest indoor antiques centre, some 70 stands and a
labyrinth of cabinets. Why: a Jordaan institution and an antiques market you can wander without
buying; named after the tanners of this quarter. Status: open (own site, 2026; renamed
Antiekcentrum Amsterdam, new owners Emrys and Arnout ter Haak since 2010). Lead:
https://www.antiekcentrumamsterdam.nl/antiekcentrum/ ; second:
https://vintagezoeker.nl/winkel/antiekcentrum-amsterdam/ ; WJGR "Antiekfestijn op de
Elandsgracht" (2015). Pool: new; "Looier" appears in several place texts (Looiersgracht places)
as the street name only. Caveats: second source is a directory; look for a Parool piece. Rank
**Okay**.

## Markets

**FW-25. Noordermarkt: the Monday market and the Saturday boerenmarkt.** Noordermarkt (pin
about 52.379600, 4.886700, approximate, the square by the church). Hook: the city's first organic
farmers' market has filled the square every Saturday since 1987, and on Monday mornings the
square and the Westerstraat turn into a flea and fabric market. Why: market as local fabric;
WJGR residents' voice ("Shoppen op de boerenmarkt is voor mij het beste begin van het weekend",
WJGR 00000076, 2007; a resident who finds the Noordermarkt "te duur" and shops on the Lindengracht,
WJGR 00000071). Status: running (2026). Lead: WJGR 00000076 (2007); second: nl.wikipedia
Westerstraat-markt; our own place text (boerenmarkt since 1987). Pool: mentioned in
`o6t15j96yfw3mt3t6b8hjcds` and `ug76c3fail8cl23ygpumw6xc` (market named, not pitched). Proposal:
bring forward as the market line of those places, or one market place joined with FW-26. Caveats:
"first organic market" not sourced. Rank **Nice**.

**FW-26. Westerstraat market, the "lapjesmarkt".** Westerstraat (pin about 52.378200,
4.884300, approximate, mid-street). Hook: every Monday some 150 stalls of fabrics and remnants
fill the filled-in Anjeliersgracht, hence the nickname "lapjesmarkt". Why: a market with a name
and a trade of its own. Status: running (markten.nl 2026). Lead:
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerstraat-markt ; second: WJGR 00000071 ("De lapjesmarkt op
maandag is ook leuk, maar er is altijd een file"). Pool: the Westerstraat cluster
`f0lvb8rajf5liwoz7e2038r2` does not name the market. Caveats: the "before 1825" date is from a
Wikipedia talk page: do not use. Rank **Okay** (merge with FW-25 as "the Monday market").

**FW-27. Lindengracht Saturday market.** Lindengracht (pin about 52.380100, 4.884400,
approximate). Hook: since the canal was filled in 1895 a 900-metre general market has run its
full length on Saturdays, the Jordaan's everyday market (some 220 stalls, a street organ). Why:
neighbourhood market where the Jordaan shops (WJGR 00000071, 00000177), not a tourist market.
Status: running (2026). Lead: https://www.amsterdamhv.nl/wiki/lindengracht.html (since the
filling); second: Geheugen van West, "Markt op de Lindengracht in 1961"
(https://geheugenvanwest.amsterdam/page/12780/markt-op-de-lindengracht-in-1961). Pool: mentioned
by street in `hfikzkck3l694xia27eu00i5` and `zi7ywq6hebic9l5s3feaw3uu` (eel riot); the market is
not told. Caveats: 1894 (iamsterdam, not usable) vs 1895 filling: check Stadsarchief. Rank
**Nice** as a line in the Lindengracht place, **Okay** as its own pin.

**FW-28. Book market on the Spui.** Spui (pin about 52.368660, 4.889600, approximate, the
northern half by the Lawrence Weiner pavement work). Hook: every Friday a guild of antiquarian
booksellers fills the Spui; the association vets who may sell, and on Sundays (March to
December) an art market takes over. Why: a fixture of the book square (Athenaeum, the ABC) for
over thirty years; in 2025-26 threatened by the Kalverstraat works (NH Nieuws). Status: running;
possible move: check. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spui_(Amsterdam) ; second:
https://www.deboekenmarktophetspui.nl/over-ons/ and https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/363348/boekenmarkt-op-het-spui-in-het-gedrang-door-opknapbeurt-amsterdamse-winkelstraat .
Pool: the Spui place `l1awc3rqmfpgktsp5zqwnl18` and Athenaeum `w6ij4myfouees8i69phdn789` do not
name the market. Caveats: founding year not found ("al dertig jaar een begrip"). Rank **Nice**.
