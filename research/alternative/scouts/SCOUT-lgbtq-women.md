# Scout: lgbtq-women (Alternative Amsterdam)

Slice: key LGBTQ+ places city-wide, plus women's movement and feminist places that belong to the
alternative story. Centrum's LGBTQ+ history set was added today and is listed as (E) in one block
(items 12 to 26). Effort went to the rest of the city and to women's and feminist places.

Ids checked with `env -u DC_URL -u DC_TOKEN pnpm -s dc places sights --all --q <word>`. Research
was done in BrowserOS (Google until it showed a captcha, then DuckDuckGo). Het Parool's Plus
articles were paywalled in the browser, so some Parool facts come from headlines and search
snippets only. Those are marked.

## Shortlist (ordered by strength)

1. **De Trut, the potten- en flikkerdisco in the Tetterode squat** (Bilderdijkstraat 165E, West; since 8 Dec 1985) (E: "Tetterode type foundry", ki3ocy9mc2qou3zeusvqwdx0): the squatters' disco Flux turned into a Sunday night "for dykes and fags" in 1985. It is still run by volunteers, still non-commercial, and gives its takings to LGBTQIA+ causes, 40 years on in the cellar of a squatted monument. Status: open (now calls itself a queer club). Evidence: [nl.wikipedia De Trut](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Trut); [Parool, 20 Dec 2025, "Disco De Trut bestaat 40 jaar"](https://www.parool.nl/ps/disco-de-trut-bestaat-40-jaar-en-dit-zijn-de-queer-vrijwilligers-en-gasten-die-dat-mogelijk-maken-na-al-die-jaren-is-het-nog-steeds-een-natte-vieze-feestgrot~bb437227/); [Parool, Jun 2009, "Homoclub De Trut blijft gewoon"](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/homoclub-de-trut-blijft-gewoon~bc1bee5e/). Caveats: the existing place is the whole foundry, so the theme text should lead with De Trut. The Tetterode squat also belongs to the squats slice.

2. **Vrouwenhuis, the women's house** (Nieuwe Herengracht 95, Centrum; 1973 to 2014) (N): on 6 June 1973 the Vrouwen Krakerscollectief squatted a former drawing school as the country's first women's house, and other cities copied it. Dolle Mina, the feminist publisher De Bonte Was, the film collective Cinemien, the Vrouwenkrant and the lesbian group Paarse September all had a home here. Men were not let in until 1991 (they got their own toilet in 1998). It was renamed Akantes in 2003 and went bankrupt in 2014. Status: changed (the city owns the building and has offered it for sale; current use not verified). Evidence: [Atria, Archief Vrouwenhuis Amsterdam](https://collectie.atria.nl/archives/item/440606-archief-vrouwenhuis-amsterdam); [Atria, "Collectief verheugen" (2024)](https://atria.nl/nl/kennis/artikelen/herstory-collectief-verheugen); [nl.wikipedia Vrouwenhuis (Amsterdam)](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrouwenhuis_(Amsterdam)) (cites Trouw); [NIW, on the sale of no. 95](https://niw.nl/joods-erfgoed-te-koop-en-niemand-weet-het-555/). Caveats: the building is a national monument and has a Jewish past (it was the Centraal Diamant Handelsbond), which may overlap with the Jewish Heritage map.

3. **Dolle Mina burns a corset at the Wilhelmina Drucker monument** (Churchilllaan, Zuid; 23 Jan 1970) (E: "Wilhelmina Drucker monument, the free woman on the Churchilllaan", kw64ov5tb9c5ss0i2911pfne): in January 1970 a new action group burned a corset at the statue of the feminist "Iron Mina" and named itself after her: Dolle Mina. The statue is still there. Status: open (public statue). Evidence: [Stadsarchief Amsterdam canon, venster 42 "1970 Dolle Mina"](https://www.amsterdam.nl/stadsarchief/canon/venster/42/); [Archief Amsterdam beeldbank, photo of 23 Jan 1970](https://archief.amsterdam/beeldbank/detail/4bd00f39-a352-8862-1a8d-9cd0f600a108); [Canon van Nederland, Dolle Mina](https://www.canonvannederland.nl/nl/page/444168/dolle-mina); [Parool, 15 Aug 2026, on the monument](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/alles-aan-het-wilhelmina-drucker-monument-op-de-churchilllaan-schreeuwt-verzet~b8755332/). Caveats: dollemina.org names the storming of Nijenrode business school as the first action day. The corset burning is the Amsterdam founding image.

4. **Frans Wong's abortion clinic** (Oosterpark 59, Oost; 1970/71 to after 2012) (N): while abortion was still illegal, the doctor Frans Wong opened one of the first Dutch abortion clinics in a doctor's house on the Oosterpark. Women came from all over Europe, and his family received threats. In 2012 the national provider CASA took it over. It is a house again now. Status: changed (a residence). Evidence: [Parool, 4 Apr 2026, "De abortuskliniek van ... Frans Wong aan het Oosterpark was de eerste van Nederland"](https://www.parool.nl/geschiedenis-van-amsterdam/de-abortuskliniek-van-arts-en-cowboy-distingue-frans-wong-aan-het-oosterpark-was-de-eerste-van-nederland~b65ad594/); [Parool, "In dit woonhuis aan het Oosterpark zat ooit een van de eerste abortusklinieken"](https://www.parool.nl/geschiedenis-van-amsterdam/in-dit-woonhuis-aan-het-oosterpark-zat-ooit-een-van-de-eerste-abortusklinieken-van-nederland~beb6de32/); [Historisch Nieuwsblad, 2000](https://www.historischnieuwsblad.nl/frans-wong-oprichter-van-de-eerste-abortuskliniek/); [nl.wikipedia Frans Wong](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frans_Wong). Caveats: sources give 1970 or 1971 as the opening year, and "the first" or "one of the first". The clinic was run by a man, so the text should say how it fits the women's story (Dolle Mina's "baas in eigen buik").

5. **Xantippe, the first women's bookshop** (Westerstraat 193 from 1976, Prinsengracht 290 from 1980 to 1 June 2026; Centrum) (N): six friends from the women's movement founded it in 1976 as Amsterdam's first women's bookshop. It moved to the Prinsengracht in 1980 and later became Xantippe Unlimited, a general literary shop. It closed in June 2026 after 46 years on the canal. Status: gone (closed 2026). Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Xantippe (boekhandel)](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xantippe_(boekhandel)); [Parool, 11 Apr 2026](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/historische-boekwinkel-xantippe-verdwijnt-uit-centrum-we-zijn-een-etalage-geworden~b0ef6519/); [Boekblad, 7 Apr 2026](https://boekblad.nl/Nieuws/Item/xantippe-unlimited-amsterdam-stopt-per-1-juni). Caveats: closed and nothing marks the shop, so this is weak under the "gone" rule. It could be told as a line in the Vrouwenhuis text instead.

6. **Club RAUM** (Basisweg, Sloterdijk, Westpoort; since 19 Apr 2024) (N): a queer-run techno club in a former bookbindery by Sloterdijk station, grown out of the SPIELRAUM parties. It waited months for permits from Nieuw-West, and it shows where queer nightlife went once the centre got too expensive. Status: open (five-year permit). Evidence: [AT5, "Queerclub Raum krijgt toch vergunningen"](https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/225836/queerclub-raum-krijgt-toch-vergunningen-en-opent-half-april); [Parool, 27 Mar 2024](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/club-raum-kan-open-na-maandenlange-strijd-zijn-vergunningen-voor-queerclub-eindelijk-binnen~bd04a5f3/); [Parool, 4 Oct 2023](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/nieuwe-queerclub-club-raum-mag-dit-weekend-nog-niet-open-we-zijn-er-kapot-van~b1d76d6e/). Caveats: young, and the permit is temporary. Coordinate with music-clubs.

7. **Bar Buka** (Albert Cuypstraat 124, De Pijp, Zuid; since 1 Mar 2019) (N): opened after Vivelavie closed, when Saarein was the city's only women's café left. It is a bar first for women who love women (Buka is Indonesian for "open"), and one of the few lesbian bars outside the old centre. Status: open. Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Bar Buka](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Buka); [Parool, 11 Apr 2024, "Waar zijn de vrouwencafés in Amsterdam?"](https://www.parool.nl/ps/waar-zijn-de-vrouwencafes-in-amsterdam-ze-zijn-er-nog-en-blijven-nodig-maar-de-tendens-is-die-van-tijdelijke-feestconcepten-ook-binnen-de-queer-scene~be261edf/) (snippet only); [homohoreca.amsterdam, elders](https://www.homohoreca.amsterdam/elders.php). Caveats: young and commercial. It is included as the successor to Vivelavie and Saarein.

8. **Krukrecht: women occupy Café Mulder** (Weteringschans 163, Centrum/Zuid border; 13 Apr 1973) (N): Café Mulder would not let a woman sit alone at the bar, because she might be taken for a hostess. A handful of women occupied the bar under a banner demanding "krukrecht" (the right to a bar stool), and the café gave in. It still pours, a café since 1920. Status: open. Evidence: [Vrouwen nu voor later, "Krukrecht 1973"](https://www.vrouwennuvoorlater.nl/krukrecht1973/) (credits a Nationaal Archief photo); [Amsterdams Verleden post with the 1973 photo by H. Peters](https://www.instagram.com/p/DIYj9SUIdby/); [Café Mulder, own site](https://www.cafemulder.eu). Caveats: weak. These are a volunteer archive and a history account, so the Anefo photo record should be found at the Nationaal Archief before drafting.

9. **IHLIA LGBTI Heritage at the OBA** (Oosterdokskade 143, 3rd floor, Centrum; IHLIA since 1999, roots in Homodok and the Lesbisch Archief) (E: "OBA Oosterdok, the library with a free view", i49qa98lxns1x90vlsxrpmxf): Europe's largest LGBTI archive and library. It has a free exhibition floor in the public library, and its exhibitions recover places like Zuidoost's queer history. Status: open. Evidence: [nl.wikipedia IHLIA LGBTI Heritage](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHLIA_LGBTI_Heritage); [ihlia.nl](https://ihlia.nl/). Caveats: an institution, not a scene. Add it as an angle on the existing OBA place.

10. **Vrankrijk's WTF Queer Wednesday** (Spuistraat 216, Centrum) (E: "Vrankrijk", k8xtefn8yygqj0f1b8lfg1dl): the squatted political café has run a weekly queer night with Drag Queers Amsterdam for years, with cheap vegan food. Status: open. Evidence: [vrankrijk.org, queer events](https://vrankrijk.org/events/categories/queer/); [radar.squat.net listing, Oct 2026](https://radar.squat.net/nn/node/599400). Caveats: the place belongs to the squats slice. This is a one-line queer angle for its text.

11. **QueerHub Nieuw-West in Garage Notweg** (Notweg 38, Osdorp, Nieuw-West; since 2 Oct 2025) (E: "Garage Notweg", vwm2d6xtbt8cuqa510ljipn4): the first queer living room in Nieuw-West, set up by Pink Nieuw-West in a district with a bad name for queer safety. People "can walk in without it being noticed". Status: open. Evidence: [Parool, "In Nieuw-West is nu de eerste huiskamer voor queers geopend"](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/in-nieuw-west-is-nu-de-eerste-huiskamer-voor-queers-geopend-mensen-kunnen-hier-naar-binnen-zonder-dat-het-opvalt~b61d5f55/); [Rodi, opening 2 Oct](https://www.rodi.nl/amsterdam-nieuw-west/nieuws/466353/queerhub-feestelijk-geopend); [pinknieuwwest.org/queerhub](https://www.pinknieuwwest.org/queerhub/). Caveats: very new and small. It is an angle on an existing place, not a standalone sight.

### Centrum, not yet on any map (N, if the theme map wants living queer venues)

27. **Lellebel** (Utrechtsestraat 4; 1990s, reopened 2021) (N): the country's only drag show bar, founded by the drag artist Hans Wijtenburg as a multicultural transgender bar. It was saved during the pandemic and is now "a trans-alt-indie-grunge-rock queer bar". Status: open. Evidence: [VICE NL](https://www.vice.com/nl/article/dragshowbar-de-lellebel-overge/); [transarchief.nl, history of Café Lellebel](https://transarchief.nl/); [homohoreca.amsterdam, reopening](https://www.homohoreca.amsterdam/nieuws/dragshowbar-de-lellebel-heropend.php). Caveats: founding year not pinned (sources point to the 1990s). The only trans-led place with a history found in this slice.
28. **Kerkstraat, the leather and fetish street** (Kerkstraat 23 to 60) (N): from the 1960s a gay street, between DOK and De Schakel. Homolulu (Kerkstraat 23, 1975 to 1997) was where lesbians could dance, and the Thermos night sauna was at no. 60. Club Church (Kerkstraat 52, since 2008) is a fetish and cruise club still open. Status: open. Evidence: [NRC, 6 Jun 2018, "Tien jaar diensten in de fetisjkerk"](https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2018/06/06/tien-jaar-diensten-in-de-fetisjkerk-a1605671); [IHLIA With Pride, Güner Kuban (Homolulu)](https://withpride.ihlia.nl/story/guner-kuban/); [homohoreca.amsterdam, Kerkstraat](https://www.homohoreca.amsterdam/kerkstraat.php). Caveats: best as one street place like the Reguliersdwarsstraat. It is mostly nightlife for men.
29. **Warmoesstraat leather bars: Argos and the Cockring** (Warmoesstraat 95 and 96) (N): the Argos (from the 1950s) was the first leather bar in Amsterdam and probably the oldest in Europe. It closed in 2015, and the Cockring disco (1987) closed in 2022. Dirty Dicks and the Eagle keep the street's leather character. Status: changed. Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Argos (bar)](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argos_(bar)); [NH Nieuws, "Homoclub Cockring dichtgespijkerd"](https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/38514/homoclub-cockring-dichtgespijkerd); [Gay News on the RoB takeover](https://www.gaynews.nl/artikel/3175/Horeca-News---Ook-bar-Cockring-gered-van-definitieve-sluiting/). Caveats: the Argos was first in the Warmoesstraat hotel and later in the Heintje Hoekssteeg, so check addresses before drafting.
30. **Tabu, the first women-only café** (Leidsekruisstraat 19; 1969 to 1979) (N): you rang the bell to be let in. Status: gone. Evidence: [homohoreca.amsterdam, history](https://www.homohoreca.amsterdam/elders.php); historypin tag "tabu". Caveats: weak (hobby site only). Find IHLIA or Parool support before drafting, or fold it into Saarein.
31. **Hartjesdag on the Zeedijk** (Zeedijk, mid-August; revived 1997) (N): an old Amsterdam street festival where men dressed as women and women as men, revived by the Zeedijk's businesses. Today it is a queer-friendly drag parade past 't Mandje. Status: open (annual, with gaps in 2013 and 2014). Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Hartjesdag](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartjesdag); [Gay News, 25 years](https://www.gaynews.nl/artikel/7577/Van-Hertendag-tot-Hartendag-tot-Hartjesdag--jubileum-25-jaar-Hartjesdagen-Zeedijk/); [hartjesdagen.nl history](https://www.hartjesdagen.nl/geschiedenis/). Caveats: an event, not a place. It could be a line in 't Mandje's text.

### Rest of the city

32. **The Vondelpark rosarium, the park where sex was allowed** (Vondelpark, rosarium area, Zuid) (N): long a cruising ground that the COC called the most dangerous in Amsterdam. In 2008 the city's draft park policy let men have sex there at night as long as nobody was bothered. NRC, Trouw and the foreign press covered it. Status: open (park). Evidence: [NRC, 20 Mar 2008](https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2008/03/20/welke-homo-gaat-nu-nog-naar-het-park-voor-de-seks-11506764-a915876); [Trouw, "Vrij in Amsterdamse Vondelpark cruisen"](https://www.trouw.nl/nieuws/vrij-in-amsterdamse-vondelpark-cruisen~b6245915/); [Parool, "Meer toezicht bij homo-ontmoetingsplaats Vondelpark"](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/meer-toezicht-bij-homo-ontmoetingsplaats-vondelpark~b4a18e64/). Caveats: tone needs care. Whether the policy is still in force was not verified.
33. **Frissenstein, "Flikkerstein"** (Frissenstein, F-buurt, Bijlmermeer, Zuidoost; built 1967 to 1970) (N): many gay couples moved into the new Bijlmer to escape being watched in the old city, and one honeycomb flat was soon nicknamed "Flikkerstein". Two wings survive, renovated. Status: changed (partly demolished, renovated). Evidence: [IHLIA / Imagine IC, "Queering Zuidoost"](https://ihlia.nl/events/queering-zuidoost/); [nl.wikipedia Frissenstein](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frissenstein_(Amsterdam)) (for the building only). Caveats: weak. The nickname rests on one exhibition text. It is the only Zuidoost lead found, and worth one more search in Imagine IC's archive.

## Borderline

- **Gay Games 1998 opening, Johan Cruijff ArenA** (E: t70e9cjjigw6f1phx30kryw3): the opening and closing in the new ArenA were watched by about a million Dutch viewers ([nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay_Games_1998), [IHLIA](https://ihlia.nl/verhalen/sport/gay-games-amsterdam/)). It was a mainstream event, so at most a line in the stadium's text.
- **Sauna Fenomeen** (Eerste Schinkelstraat 14, in E: "Schinkelhaven horse tram depot (OCCII and De Binnenpret)", pmt4b6bhzta5j6zk5aa44rwt): a squatters' sauna from 1984, legalised in 1989 and threatened with closure in 2008 and 2011 ([AT5 2011](https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/57288/einde-sauna-fenomeen-nu-echt-nakend)). It was mixed rather than LGBTQ, so it belongs to the squats slice. Listings say it is closed now.
- **Orka La Rose, the lesbian café in the squatted Handelsblad** (E: "The newspaper street, from Handelsblad to De Standaard", mz3uyg46zbqkj9udjl2k2xcy): lesbians squatted part of the newspaper building and opened a women's café on the corner. Only one volunteer archive says so ([Vrouwen nu voor later](https://www.vrouwennuvoorlater.nl/wonen/)). A possible line for the existing place if confirmed.
- **Prostitutie Informatie Centrum** (Enge Kerksteeg 3, Centrum; since Aug 1994, N): founded by the former sex worker Mariska Majoor, who also founded the union PROUD in 2015 ([nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prostitutie_Informatie_Centrum), [NH Nieuws](https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/342312/achter-deze-ramen-helpt-het-pic-al-30-jaar-sekswerkers-en-hun-klanten)). It is a sex workers' rights place, which fits Alternative Amsterdam better than this slice. It may belong with red-light content already on the map.
- **Atria** (Vijzelstraat 20, Centrum, N): the women's history institute, descended from the IAV that Rosa Manus, Johanna Naber and Willemijn Posthumus-van der Goot founded in 1935. It is an archive, not an alternative scene. Better as a source than a sight.
- **Aletta Jacobs' house** (E: dd88s0dry0fmfo8adxmr00q6): first-wave feminism, already on the map, but establishment rather than alternative.
- **Pink Point** (Westermarkt, N): an LGBTQ+ information kiosk born at the Gay Games in 1998, now mostly selling souvenirs. A line in the Homomonument's text at most.
- **Café Dapper** (Dapperstraat 26, Oost, since 2025), **Pamela** (Jacob van Lennepstraat 86, West, since 2020), **TILLATEC** (Dr. Jan van Breemenstraat, West, temporary, 2024): new queer-minded venues outside the centre. They show the move out of the centre but are too young and lack press weight. Revisit in a few years.
- **Two memorials on the Museumplein (Ravensbrück)** (E: omzscowut0ag6ez0oz2xle8c): the 1975 monument for the women of Ravensbrück. It is a women's memorial, not an alternative place.

## Report

- Counts: shortlist 20 (E) (the 15 Centrum items 12 to 26, plus De Trut/Tetterode, the Drucker monument, OBA/IHLIA, Vrankrijk and Garage Notweg) and 13 (N). Borderline adds 5 (E) and 6 (N).
- Top ten: De Trut (E), Vrouwenhuis (N), Dolle Mina at the Drucker monument (E), Oosterpark 59 abortion clinic (N), Club RAUM (N), Bar Buka (N), Saarein (E), 't Mandje (E), Homomonument (E), Lellebel (N).
- Odd things:
  - Outside the centre the LGBTQ+ heritage is thin. Queer venues have only moved out since about 2020, because of rents, so most non-Centrum candidates are young (RAUM, Buka, QueerHub, Dapper).
  - Trans history barely shows up in authoritative sources. Lellebel is the one solid place.
  - Women's places are strongest as 1970s movement sites (Vrouwenhuis, the Drucker corset, Oosterpark 59, krukrecht at Café Mulder). Several are in Centrum but not on the map yet.
  - Google showed a captcha midway (the browser is shared), so the rest of the searches ran through DuckDuckGo.
  - The Parool Plus walk "Van De Trut tot Bar Lellebel" (27 Jul 2021) was paywalled and could not be read. Reading it is worth doing before the picks.
