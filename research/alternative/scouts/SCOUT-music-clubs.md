# Scout: music-clubs (venues, clubs, record shops, free-festival homes)

Slice of the Alternative Amsterdam theme map. Shortlist only, nothing drafted.
Research 2026-11-25, mostly through BrowserOS (Google until it threw a captcha, then Bing; Het
Parool is behind a hard paywall even signed in, so Parool items are cited by headline and
date, with the facts taken from the open lead or from a second source). Existence checked with
`dc places sights --all --q` for every candidate.

Key: (E: "live title", id) is already on a map and would be list-added; (N) is new.
"weak" marks a fact resting on one hobby or listings site.

## Shortlist, by strength

1. **Paradiso** (Weteringschans 6-8; 1968 to now) (E: "Paradiso, the free congregation's meeting hall", tu9bo2m469n39meqc88978xo): the mother of Amsterdam's alternative venues, opened on 30 March 1968 as the "Cosmisch Ontspanningscentrum" after hippies took over the meeting hall; revived by punk in the late 1970s (first Dutch Rock Against Racism festival, 22 Nov 1978; Dead Kennedys, Sinterklaas 1982; the Kraakkrant was printed in its cellars). Now a big mainstream pop temple, the "legend turned normie" of the brief. Status: open. Evidence: [Paradiso history](https://www.paradiso.nl/info/over-ons/paradiso-geschiedenis); [RCE post-65 heritage: Paradiso](https://www.cultureelerfgoed.nl/onderwerpen/p/post-65-erfgoed/verhalen-en-tijdlijnen/beschrijvingen-post-65-objecten/paradiso); [NPO Radio 1, Deze Dag Toen, 30 Mar 2018](https://www.nporadio1.nl/nieuws/binnenland/346235c5-045b-464e-a7f7-f8a1ffe4a1b3/deze-dag-toen-kosmisch-ontspanningscentrum-paradiso-geopend); [Ons Amsterdam, Punk in Amsterdam (deel 2), 2013](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/punk-in-amsterdam-deel-2). Caveats: existing text leads with the church; the theme-map text should lead with 1968 and punk.

2. **Melkweg** (Lijnbaansgracht 234A; 1970 to now) (E: "Melkweg, the milk factory that became a 'cosmic centre'", cc3fb71jiphd5owntcb99vhz): the second hippie "cosmic centre", a summer experiment in a disused milk factory in 1970 that never left; with Paradiso and Fantasio one of the three places the Provo/hippie generation took over (Hans Plomp in Vice). Now a large mainstream venue and cinema. Status: open. Evidence: [Melkweg history](https://www.melkweg.nl/nl/over-ons/geschiedenis/); [Het Parool, Stadsgezichten: De Melkweg, 8 Jun 2012](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/stadsgezichten-de-melkweg~b590b654/); [Vice NL, De grondleggers van het Amsterdamse nachtleven..., 2017](https://www.vice.com/nl/article/de-grondleggers-van-het-amsterdamse-nachtleven-verblijven-al-decennialang-in-ruigoord/). Caveats: none.

3. **RoXY** (Singel 465-467; Aug 1987 to 21 Jun 1999) (N): the club where house broke through in the Netherlands (Eddy de Clercq and Joost van Bellen, Acid Heaven 1988), a haven for drag, queer and art crowds (Hard gay Wednesdays from 1992, Pussy Lounge from 1995, Love Balls for the Aidsfonds); burned down during the wake of co-founder Peter Giele when fireworks were let off inside. Now a Patagonia shop (2024) in the rebuilt shell. Status: gone (now a shop). Evidence: [Stadsarchief, RoXY. Een paradijselijk toevluchtsoord](https://www.amsterdam.nl/stadsarchief/stukken/uitgaan/roxy/); [Het Parool, Laatste avond van roemruchte RoXY te horen..., 21 Jun 2024](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/laatste-avond-van-roemruchte-roxy-te-horen-dankzij-gevonden-geluidsopname-het-is-pijnlijk-maar-we-moeten-het-niet-vergeten~bd85c1a2/); [Het Parool review of the Stadsarchief RoXY show, 13 Oct 2023](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/recensie-in-het-stadsarchief-komt-de-roxy-tot-leven-de-discotheek-voor-iedereen-die-afweek-van-de-norm~b3ff79c2/); [VPRO Vrije Geluiden, Acid Heaven](https://www.vpro.nl/vrije-geluiden/artikelen/acid-heaven); [FashionNetwork, Patagonia in the former RoXY, Apr 2024](https://ww.fashionnetwork.com/news/In-amsterdam-patagonia-revisits-the-former-roxy-club-a-temple-to-techno,1624863.html). Caveats: Ons Amsterdam (2008) places RoXY "in de Kalverstraat", every other source says Singel 465-467; use the Singel. Pairs with (E) "The Giele Skull and the Paradiso field, Zorgvlied", r54hqrxogjroogns9dv1h5ir (Giele's grave).

4. **iT** (Amstelstraat 24; 1980s to 2008) (E: "iT, Manfred Langer's club (Amstelstraat 24)", bw0u002t00e6kkkwmo3hrng3): with RoXY the house palace of the 1990s, drag shows and theme nights, a place "where everyone could be themselves". Status: gone. Evidence: [Stadsarchief/Geschiedenislokaal, Nachtclubs in Amsterdam van de jaren 80 tot nu](https://geschiedenislokaalamsterdam.nl/bronnen/nachtclubs-in-amsterdam-van-de-jaren-80-tot-nu); [Stadsarchief RoXY page (RoXY "samen met de iT" the house palace)](https://www.amsterdam.nl/stadsarchief/stukken/uitgaan/roxy/). Caveats: LGBTQ slice also claims it.

5. **OCCII** (Amstelveenseweg 134; venue since 1992, squatted 1980s) (E: "Schinkelhaven horse tram depot (OCCII and De Binnenpret)", pmt4b6bhzta5j6zk5aa44rwt): the city's longest-running squat-born underground stage ("keeping Amsterdam weird since 1992"), named by Duivenvoorden among the squatters' big concert halls; punk, noise, experimental. Status: open. Evidence: [AT5, Poppodium OCCII bestaat 30 jaar, 1 Oct 2022](https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/217065/poppodium-occii-bestaat-30-jaar-iedereen-die-hier-komt-vertrekt-flabbergasted); [OCCII](https://occii.org/); [Duivenvoorden, Een voet tussen de deur (full text)](https://archive.org/stream/een-voet-tussen-de-deur-geschiedenis-kraakbeweging/Een-voet-tussen-de-deur-Geschiedenis-van-Eric-Duivenvoorden_djvu.txt). Caveats: squat side belongs to the squats slice; one place on the map.

6. **The first Bimhuis, Oudeschans 73-77** (1 Oct 1974 to Feb 2005) (N; the present Bimhuis is E: "Muziekgebouw aan 't IJ and the Bimhuis", bl7qb8jvn5x9g1oa2xh1bwh5): musicians of the improvised-music union BIM stripped an empty furniture showroom to bare concrete and built a makeshift stage; it became one of Europe's key jazz and improv clubs until noise rules forced the move to the IJ. Status: gone (site redeveloped). Evidence: [Bimhuis history](https://www.bimhuis.nl/over-ons/geschiedenis); [Ons Amsterdam, Vaarwel Oudeschans!, 2005](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/vaarwel-oudeschans); [de Volkskrant, Het oude Bimhuis is nu al vergeten, 21 Feb 2005](https://www.volkskrant.nl/cultuur-media/het-oude-bimhuis-is-nu-al-vergeten~ba8a95f1/); [VVAB, Oudeschans 73-75-77](https://www.amsterdamsebinnenstad.nl/binnenstad/223/oudeschans.php). Caveats: check what stands at 73-77 now (VVAB article is about the replacement building); could also be handled as a "roots" note on the existing Bimhuis entry instead of a separate pin.

7. **Club Mazzo** (Rozengracht 114; 5 Jun 1980 to 2004) (E: "Club Mazzo, behind an upholsterer's granite front (gone)", bey97viwn1x2hh7a7xdd6d4v): the new-wave, video and slide "audiovisual" club; with RoXY one of the first to play house (the Soho Connection parties of 1988). Status: gone (front remains). Evidence: [de Volkskrant, Amsterdamse club Mazzo moet weg van Rozengracht, 20 Aug 2004](https://www.volkskrant.nl/cultuur-media/amsterdamse-club-mazzo-moet-weg-van-rozengracht~b18e2d34/); [Ons Amsterdam, Hier gebeurde het... Surinamekade, 3 september 1988](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/hier-gebeurde-het-surinamekade-3-september-1988); [Mazzo Digital, Over Mazzo](https://www.mazzodigital.eu/over/). Caveats: none.

8. **Akhnaton** (Nieuwezijds Kolk 25; at the Kolk since 1971) (N): the Catholic students' society of 1953 that turned into the doorway for "new" music from far away: salsa, merengue, reggae, punk and, crucially, Dutch hip-hop (Osdorp Posse); still an independent venue. Status: open. Evidence: [Akhnaton, Historie](https://akhnaton.nl/geschiedenis-van-akhnaton/); [De Groene Amsterdammer, 1 Oct 1997](https://www.groene.nl/artikel/leven-in-gelul-aangejaagd-door-bewoners-die-in-gestalttherapie-en-sensitivity-training-de-beste-garanties-zagen-voor-geluk-eindigde-menige-vergadering-in-een-huilpartij); [en.wikipedia, Akhnaton (music venue)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhnaton_(music_venue)). Caveats: Akhnaton's own site says the Oranje Vrijstaat (Kabouters, Feb 1970) was proclaimed "in Akhnaton"; that predates the 1971 move to the Kolk, so do not tie it to this building.

9. **Fantasio, later Meditatiecentrum De Kosmos** (Prins Hendrikkade 142; Fantasio 29 Mar 1968 to 1970, De Kosmos to its bankruptcy in 1992) (N): the third of the 1968 "cosmic" youth centres (with Paradiso and Melkweg), with music, a tea room and happenings; it became the country's main New Age centre. Status: gone (building in other use). Evidence: [Ons Amsterdam, Oosterse wijzen in De Kosmos](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/oosterse-wijzen-in-de-kosmos); [De Appel archive, Fantasio en Kosmos, 2015](https://www.deappel.nl/nl/archive/events/820-fantasio-en-kosmos); [Leiden University collection guide, De Kosmos](https://collectionguides.universiteitleiden.nl/agents/corporate_entities/248); [Vice NL 2017 (Hans Plomp)](https://www.vice.com/nl/article/de-grondleggers-van-het-amsterdamse-nachtleven-verblijven-al-decennialang-in-ruigoord/). Caveats: current use of no. 142 not checked; more a hippie legend than a music club, could sit in the squats slice.

10. **"De Emma", Pakhuis Koningin Emma** (Van Diemenstraat 20-200; squatted mid-1980s) (E: "Pakhuis Koningin Emma", g0cd9cgj5xkql0rtu9dba7k5): squatted warehouse with a punk stage, rehearsal rooms and restaurant; Sonic Youth played there; named with the Van Hall-hal, Vrankrijk and OCCII as the squat era's big concert halls. Status: changed (business centre). Evidence: [De Groene Amsterdammer, Woedend lawaai, 1 Mar 2012 (Terrie of The Ex)](https://www.groene.nl/artikel/woedend-lawaai); [Ons Amsterdam, Punk in Amsterdam (deel 2)](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/punk-in-amsterdam-deel-2); [Duivenvoorden full text](https://archive.org/stream/een-voet-tussen-de-deur-geschiedenis-kraakbeweging/Een-voet-tussen-de-deur-Geschiedenis-van-Eric-Duivenvoorden_djvu.txt); [Diana Ozon (weak)](http://www.diana-ozon.nl/?e=117). Caveats: exact years of the squat not found; existing text says nothing of it.

11. **Concerto** (Utrechtsestraat 52-60; since 1955) (N): the city's institution record shop, a "second living room" for vinyl collectors for seventy years, with in-store gigs. Status: open. Evidence: [Het Parool, Graaien en grijsdraaien: het verhaal van Concerto, 26 Nov 2021](https://www.parool.nl/ps/graaien-en-grijsdraaien-het-verhaal-van-concerto-meer-dan-een-platenzaak~b04f638d/); [Het Parool, Concerto viert verjaardag: al 65 jaar..., 10 May 2020](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/concerto-viert-verjaardag-al-65-jaar-de-vaste-stek-voor-amsterdamse-muziekfreaks~b480faf1/); [Het Parool, Gijs Molenaar: de man die Concerto groot maakte, 2009](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/gijs-molenaar-de-man-die-concerto-groot-maakte~b29cb679/). Caveats: more "beloved" than "underground"; Ewoud Kieft wrote its history.

12. **Boudisque** (Haringpakkerssteeg 10-18; Dec 1968 to 2008) (N): record shop founded by Ruud Jacobs that sold Dylan and Stones bootlegs on its own Hobo label, imported punk and indie, ran labels (Torso, Megadisc) and backed pirate stations; "one of the country's most famous record shops". Status: gone. Evidence: [Het Parool, Boudisque haalt de veertig niet, 5 Feb 2008](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/boudisque-haalt-de-veertig-niet~b9c0a9b4/); [de Volkskrant, Rouwen om Boudisque, 2 Feb 2008](https://www.volkskrant.nl/nieuws-achtergrond/rouwen-om-boudisque~b200b2b1/); [Stadsarchief beeldbank, Boudisque at Haringpakkerssteeg 18](https://archief.amsterdam/beeldbank/OSIM00008005362); [nl.wikipedia, Boudisque](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boudisque). Caveats: what the shop is now not checked; "something related stands there" test is weak, the impact test is strong.

13. **Trouw** (Wibautstraat 127; 2009 to 4 Jan 2015) (E via "Wibautstraat, the newspaper street", jphe2n6mbe6vwjkkd7h4kqio): club in the old Trouw newspaper print hall, "the only Amsterdam club of world level" (Parool), a 24-hour pioneer whose closing was mourned city-wide; made by the Club 11 team. Status: gone (building redeveloped). Evidence: [Het Parool, Nog 2 weken Trouw..., 22 Dec 2014](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/nog-2-weken-trouw-de-enige-amsterdamse-club-op-wereldniveau~b283c180/); [Het Parool, Het zit wel goed met de erfenis van Trouw, 2 Jan 2016](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/het-zit-wel-goed-met-de-erfenis-van-trouw~ba8d0c75/); [Het Parool, Club 11 vindt nieuwe plek in Wibautstraat, 24 Dec 2008](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/club-11-vindt-nieuwe-plek-in-wibautstraat~b1bae374/). Caveats: list-add the Wibautstraat entry, or a separate pin at no. 127.

14. **De School** (Dr. Jan van Breemenstraat 1; 2016 to Jan 2024) (N): 24-hour club, restaurant and gym in a former technical school in West, called "a nightclub of world level" when it closed after a marathon weekend; Tilla Tec took over the building. Status: changed (now Tilla Tec, itself reported closing Nov 2026, weak). Evidence: [Het Parool, Nachtclub De School sluit in januari 2024, 13 Jul 2023](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/nachtclub-de-school-sluit-in-januari-2024-definitief-de-deuren~bde0a69f/); [Het Parool, De Amsterdamse dancescene verliest met De School..., 12 Jan 2024](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/de-amsterdamse-dancescene-verliest-met-de-school-een-nachtclub-van-wereldniveau-die-vrijheid-zo-hoort-het-te-zijn-in-een-club~b8c2092f/); [Het Parool, Nieuwe nachtclub Tilla Tec komt in oude pand van De School, 11 Mar 2024](https://www.parool.nl/nederland/nieuwe-nachtclub-tilla-tec-komt-in-oude-pand-van-de-school-wij-gaan-het-gebouw-heel-anders-gebruiken~b7c3a4d2/). Caveats: 2022 criticism over door and safety (Parool 30 Jul 2022) belongs in the text for balance.

15. **Radion** (Louwesweg 1; since 2014) (N; the building's art is E: "Graphic Surgery lines on the ACTA building", j12gzze1zr3g1lyevm0u3xu8): club and broedplaats in the former ACTA dentistry faculty, one of the first Amsterdam clubs with a 24-hour licence (2015). Status: open. Evidence: [Het Parool, Radion krijgt 24-uursvergunning..., 11 Aug 2015](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/radion-krijgt-24-uursvergunning-en-trapt-af-met-36-uur-feest~b7cbdb92/); [NU.nl, Club Radion heeft 24-uursvergunning officieel binnen, 11 Aug 2015](https://www.nu.nl/amsterdam/4104517/club-radion-heeft-24-uursvergunning-officieel-binnen.html); [De Westkrant, 24 uur feesten in Radion, 2015](https://www.dewestkrant.nl/24-uur-feesten-in-radion/). Caveats: could merge with the ACTA art entry.

16. **Club 11** (Oosterdokskade 3-5, 11th floor of the Post CS building; 2005 to 28 Jun 2008) (N): temporary rooftop club in the empty post office tower (while the Stedelijk sat downstairs), the start of the "temporary club in an empty building" model that led to Trouw and De School. Status: gone. Evidence: [NRC, Nog elf dagen en dan stopt club 11, 19 Jun 2008](https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2008/06/19/nog-elf-dagen-en-dan-stopt-club-11-11558339-a1310811); [3voor12, Het boek 11 sluit met een boek, 21 May 2008](https://3voor12.vpro.nl/artikelen/het-boek-11-sluit-met-een-boek); [Het Parool, Kaalslag dreigt voor undergroundclubs, 6 Aug 2022](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/kaalslag-dreigt-voor-undergroundclubs-we-moeten-redden-wat-er-te-redden-valt~b91bde17/). Caveats: RA says the building was "torn down in 2008"; the Post CS building was in fact kept and redeveloped. Verify before drafting.

17. **Rush Hour** (Spuistraat, since 1997) (N): record shop, distributor and label that began in a hairdresser's basement and became a world-known house and techno brand (Antal Heitlager). Status: open. Evidence: [Het Parool, Platenzaak Rush Hour verhuist op de Spuistraat, 9 Feb 2016](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/platenzaak-rush-hour-verhuist-op-de-spuistraat~b4dbe9ef/); [Het Parool, Verdraaid lekker vinyl: 14 Amsterdamse platenzaken, 3 Sep 2024](https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/verdraaid-lekker-vinyl-14-amsterdamse-platenzaken-om-in-te-grasduinen~b3b2c41c7/); [Rush Hour, About](https://www.rushhour.nl/about-rush-hour-music). Caveats: house number after the 2016 move not confirmed (98 is the commonly given one).

18. **Cruise Inn** (Zuiderzeeweg 29, Zeeburgereiland, from Dec 1982; moved Nov 2026 to the former Pantar office on the same island) (N): rock-'n-roll clubhouse started by the Bopcats, called Europe's oldest surviving rock-'n-roll club; a subculture's home for 44 years. Status: open (new building since 21 Nov 2026). Evidence: [Het Parool, Europa's oudste rock-'n-rollclub naar nieuwe locatie Zeeburgereiland, 18 Nov 2026](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/europa-s-oudste-rock-n-rollclub-naar-nieuwe-locatie-zeeburgereiland-na-zaterdag-ruikt-het-hier-weer-gewoon-als-de-cruise-inn~bdd970f14/); [de Brug, Rock-'n-roll 3.0 op Zeeburgereiland, 7 Oct 2026](https://debrugkrant.nl/rock-n-roll-3-0-op-zeeburgereiland-cruise-inn-bouwt-voor-de-derde-keer-een-eigen-thuis/); [Het Parool, Hoe goed kent u de stad?, 7 Oct 2023](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/hoe-goed-kent-u-de-stad~b4e45a04/). Caveats: new street address still to find; "third home" so there was an earlier one.

19. **Garage Noord** (Gedempt Hamerkanaal 40; since Aug 2017) (N): bar and club in a former car repair shop that put local, queer-friendly and non-Western club music on the map; lease extended to about 2026 as the Hamerkwartier is built up. Status: open (threatened). Evidence: [RA, New club, Garage Noord, opens in Amsterdam, 22 Aug 2017](https://ra.co/news/39803); [Het Parool, Nachtclubs Garage Noord en Skatecafé blijven 3 jaar langer open, 5 Apr 2023](https://www.parool.nl/nederland/nachtclubs-garage-noord-en-skatecafe-blijven-3-jaar-langer-open-er-komen-leuke-dingen-aan~b50da95c/); [3voor12, Garage Noord opent nieuwe club in het centrum, 26 Oct 2022](https://3voor12.vpro.nl/artikelen/garage-noord-opent-nieuwe-club-in-het-centrum-van-amsterdam). Caveats: young (2017); include as today's underground, not as a legend.

20. **Skatecafé (Karin & Yvonne)** (Gedempt Hamerkanaal 42; 2016 to 2026) (E: "Skatecafé Karin & Yvonne", wto2stp9s00l2u87fpgh3ckl): restaurant with a skate ramp by day, club by night, now partying its farewell as the Hamerkwartier is redeveloped. Status: closing 2026. Evidence: [Het Parool, Skatecafé, Oedipus en de rest van het Hamerkwartier gaan feestend hun afscheid tegemoet, 28 May 2026](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/skatecafe-oedipus-en-de-rest-van-het-hamerkwartier-gaan-feestend-hun-afscheid-tegemoet-deze-geschiedenis-kun-je-niet-terugbouwen~ba415ea9/); [Het Parool, 5 Apr 2023 (lease extension)](https://www.parool.nl/nederland/nachtclubs-garage-noord-en-skatecafe-blijven-3-jaar-langer-open-er-komen-leuke-dingen-aan~b50da95c/); [RA event, Mar 2026](https://ra.co/events/2352455). Caveats: exact last date not found; check before drafting.

21. **Bitterzoet** (Spuistraat 2; since 2003) (N): small club and stage that became the home of Amsterdam hip-hop and a breeding ground for new acts, in the former home of the Werkteater collective (from 1970). Status: open. Evidence: [AT5, Bitterzoet bestaat 10 jaar, 25 Sep 2013](https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/111356/bitterzoet-bestaat-10-jaar); [Het Parool, Bitterzoet viert 10-jarig bestaan, 20 Sep 2013](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/bitterzoet-viert-10-jarig-bestaan~b4bef275/); [3voor12, Bitterzoet verkocht, 28 May 2008](https://3voor12.vpro.nl/artikelen/bitterzoet-verkocht); [TheaterEncyclopedie, Bitterzoet Theater](https://theaterencyclopedie.nl/wiki/Bitterzoet_Theater,_Amsterdam). Caveats: none.

22. **Doka** (Wibautstraat 150, cellar of the Volkshotel; 2013 to now) (E via "Wibautstraat, the newspaper street", jphe2n6mbe6vwjkkd7h4kqio): began as the temporary Club Doka in the Volkskrant's old darkroom cellar (2013-2014), now an intimate sound bar where subcultures meet. Status: open. Evidence: [Het Parool, Wibautstraat wil geen 24 uursclubs, 24 Dec 2013](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/wibautstraat-wil-geen-24-uursclubs-ik-word-meerdere-keren-per-nacht-wakker~b3662086/); [Het Parool, ... het einde van Club Doka, 30 May 2014](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/10x-weekendtips-ambachtslieden-in-beeld-en-het-einde-van-club-doka~bbb8b05e/); [Volkshotel, Doka](https://www.volkshotel.nl/nl/doka). Caveats: same E entry as Trouw; maybe one combined "Wibautstraat clubs" text.

23. **De Marktkantine** (Jan van Galenstraat 6; 2014 to 20 Jun 2022) (N; next to E "Centrale Markthal", tz8nsaab1ju8t1a9oo56hans): club in the former canteen of the central food market, closed for redevelopment despite a demonstration and petition. Status: gone. Evidence: [Het Parool, Nachtclub De Marktkantine sluit definitief in juni..., 21 Apr 2022](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/nachtclub-de-marktkantine-sluit-definitief-in-juni-ondanks-demonstratie-en-petitie~bfdaf527/); [Het Parool, demonstratie, 8 Apr 2022](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/demonstratie-tegen-vroegtijdige-sluiting-nachtclub-de-marktkantine-zonde-als-ze-gaan-slopen-en-dan-jaren-niets-gaan-doen~be83a87a/); [AD, 21 Apr 2022](https://www.ad.nl/amsterdam/nachtclub-de-marktkantine-sluit-definitief-in-juni-ondanks-demonstratie-en-petitie~afdaf527/). Caveats: whether the canteen is demolished yet not checked; mainstream-leaning programme, middling fit.

24. **Rijkshemelvaart** (Oude Haagseweg 58; squatted free space) (E: "Emergency waterworks of the Defence Line (Rijkshemelvaartdienst)", vyk5qo1oo3ae6j3ck6au9ipa): the squatted waterworks by the Nieuwe Meer, with its yearly Ascension Day party, DIY gigs (Sleazefest) and part of the Vrije Ruimte network. ## Borderline

- **Van Hall-hal** (Van Hallstraat, now the Meander flats): legal punk hall of the 1980s (The Ex, Gramschap LP launch 1986), cited by Ons Amsterdam, De Groene and Duivenvoorden, but nothing related stands there now.
- **The 1988 KNSM warehouse party** (Surinamekade): Ons Amsterdam calls it the definitive breakthrough of house in Amsterdam (2,000 people, Soho Connection, Radio 100); which loods is unclear, and the area is rebuilt.
- **Maloe Melo** (Lijnbaansgracht 163): long-running blues bar, "oldest rock and blues bar" only per its own site; no news source found. Might suit the dive-bars slice.
- **Café Alto** (Korte Leidsedwarsstraat 115): nightly live jazz "since 1953" per its own site, Hans Dulfer's weekly gig; evidence weak.
- **Chet Baker's window** (E, b3vh32b0r3wje35hepwe0zs1): a jazz death site, not a venue; a link from Casablanca rather than its own pin.
- **The Giele Skull, Zorgvlied** (E, r54hqrxogjroogns9dv1h5ir): RoXY co-founder's grave; attach to RoXY.
- **Odeon/DOK** (E, izb9mb5kui93v4tgn3s4ixri): gay dance hall; LGBTQ slice.
- **Norderney, Radio Veronica's ship** (E, lmi8gnotgg9s09ahk7gtru04): pirate radio legend, but Veronica became mainstream; only if the map wants pirate radio.
