# Scout: squats-freezones (Alternative Amsterdam)

Slice: the squatters' movement and its places, Provo/hippie/Kabouter legends, the first coffeeshops, and today's free zones and breeding grounds. Shortlist only, nothing drafted, nothing written to the live site.

Method: `dc places sights --all` (2,373 places) and `dc export sights --all` searched by title, summary and body for squat/kraak/occupied/Provo/hippie/free space/broedplaats and by address; then BrowserOS (own tab, closed) for Het Parool, AT5, Trouw, NH Nieuws, Ons Amsterdam, Rijksmuseum, Arcam, Volkskrant/VPRO, Nationaal Archief, venue history pages, squat.net/radar and the Amsterdam Alternative network list. Google rate-limited the tab early, so searches ran through DuckDuckGo; articles were read in the signed-in browser.

Headline: Deep Amsterdam already holds most of this slice. The live map has a deep bench of squat stories (about 60 places mention squatters), so the theme map is mostly a list-add job. The real gaps are a handful of famous places: Wyers, the Kalenderpanden, Gliphoeve, Mellow Yellow, the first Bulldog, and the living social centres Joe's Garage, Molli, Plantage Dok and Zaal 100. Two found matches are hidden under building names: Kostgewonnen is "Van der Palmschool and Allebéschool", and the KNSM squat "Edelweiss" is "Former KNSM canteen".

## Shortlist (ordered by strength)

1. **Ruigoord** (Ruigoord 76; squatted 1973, still active) (E: "Ruigoord and the Sint-Gertrudiskerk", jbyuj41uiemtajl9n2l8ir0h): the oldest free space in the Netherlands, a village emptied for a port that never came, squatted by artists, home of the Landjuweel festival. Status: open; a 25-year lease with the Port and the city was signed in the village church on 17 December 2025. Evidence: [ruigoord.nl](https://ruigoord.nl/); [Rodi: Vrijhaven Ruigoord gered voor minstens 25 jaar](https://www.rodi.nl/amsterdam-nieuw-west/nieuws/477494/vrijhaven-ruigoord-is-gered-voor-minstens-25-jaar); [nl.wikipedia Ruigoord](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruigoord). Caveats: the place text predates the lease; add it. The separate "Eendenkooi Ruigoord" (tmut7ls26la7h9s6st9o85gh) is not part of this story.

2. **ADM** (Hornweg 6, Westpoort; 1997-2019) (E: "ADM squat (gone)", q56jkyz83pqv6sj2xb83mo2e): for 21 years the country's biggest free state on an old shipyard, birthplace of Robodock. Status: gone (evicted 2019; city bought the land back in 2025). Evidence: [EenVandaag on the ADM](https://eenvandaag.avrotros.nl/artikelen/adm-van-scheepswerf-tot-de-grootste-en-oudste-vrijplaats-van-nederland-116149); [NOS: gemeente koopt haventerrein terug](https://nos.nl/artikel/2583909-gemeente-amsterdam-koopt-haventerrein-terug-voor-165-miljoen-euro); [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woon-_en_werkgemeenschap_ADM). Caveats: qualifies as gone because its community lives on at item 3.

3. **Het Groene Veld (ADM Noord)** (Buikslotermeerdijk 95; since 2019) (E: "Het Groene Veld (ADM Noord)", wmnu6em0co0gjbcmr8x5drj3): where the ADM people went after the eviction, on old sludge fields; café, concert hall and festival. Status: open (Friday eetcafé and punk nights listed for Oct 2026). Evidence: [NH Nieuws: opnieuw dreigt ontruiming](https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/315743/opnieuw-dreigt-ontruiming-voor-voormalig-adm-krakers-maar-we-hebben-nog-hoop/); [hetgroeneveld.amsterdam](https://hetgroeneveld.amsterdam/en/event/eetcafe/2026-10-30/); [Amsterdam Alternative](https://amsterdamalternative.nl/network/1006/adm-noord-het-groene-veld). Caveats: lease keeps shortening; recheck outlook.

4. **OT301** (Overtoom 301; squatted 1999, bought 2006) (E: "OT301 (former Film Academy)", itvuljfzfp8o53797ssc5e75): the old Film Academy, squatted by the EHBK collective, now a collectively owned centre with cinema, club and the vegan kitchen De Peper. Status: open. Evidence: [ot301.nl](https://www.ot301.nl/); [Trouw 2000, the Film Academy as "the next bolwerk"](https://www.trouw.nl/nieuws/waren-de-kalenderpanden-het-laatste-krakersbolwerk~b6f7ec10/); [Amsterdam Alternative: 25 jaar OT301](https://amsterdamalternative.nl/articles/17043/25-jaar-ot301). Caveats: AA dates EHBK from 1998, the place says 1999; the squat itself was 1999.

5. **Vrankrijk** (Spuistraat 216; squatted 1982, bought 1992) (E: "Vrankrijk", k8xtefn8yygqj0f1b8lfg1dl): the political café of the movement, punk, queer and benefit nights in an old printing works. Status: open (Friday Punk Nights through 2026). Evidence: [vrankrijk.org](https://www.vrankrijk.org/); [RA event listing 2026](https://ra.co/events/2445672); [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrankrijk). Caveats: also inside the cluster "Spuistraat south, seamstresses and squatters" (c8p509fdg084u55be2rak7dt), which could be list-added as the street's parent.

6. **De Groote Keijser** (Keizersgracht 242-252; squatted 1978, bought by the city 1980) (E: "De Groote Keijser, the squat that armed itself", sa22rywq1wqxg9v6n0i8kqib): the barricaded canal squat with its own pirate radio (De Vrije Keyser), whose purchase split the movement. Status: changed (housing). Evidence: [IISG Staatsarchief dossier](http://www.iisg.nl/staatsarchief/archieven/kraken-amsterdam/grootekeijser.php); [nl.wikipedia Krakersrellen](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krakersrellen); [nl.wikipedia Groote Keijser](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groote_Keijser).

7. **The squatters' Nieuwmarkt** (Nieuwmarkt and Sint Antoniesbreestraat; 1970-1975) (E: "The squatters' Nieuwmarkt: the house that stopped a motorway and the riot underground", u57btdb6059ay01csuuxp75x; children E: "Huis De Pinto, the house that stopped a motorway", iod6ugp3yos61fyy3gy0sdjf; "Nieuwmarkt metro station, the riot remembered underground", mg4nb7359sveh5atnr6hnp5c): the fight against the motorway and the metro, the 1975 riots, and the station that tells the story in its art. Status: changed (rebuilt neighbourhood; station open). Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Nieuwmarktrellen](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nieuwmarktrellen); [AT5 on the station art](https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/221229); [NH Nieuws](https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/76163). Caveats: list the cluster or the two children, not all three.

8. **Vondelstraat 72** (Vondelstraat/Eerste Constantijn Huygensstraat; Feb-Mar 1980) (E: "Vondelstraat 72 (Vondelstraat riots)", h5pye48czhh54i3umrxs8fa9): the barricaded "free state" that ended with army tanks. Status: changed (housing). Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Vondelstraatrellen](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vondelstraatrellen); [isgeschiedenis: Geen woning, geen kroning](https://isgeschiedenis.nl/reportage/geen-woning-geen-kroning-de-kroningsrellen-van-1980); [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vondelstraat_Riots).

9. **Lucky Luijk** (Jan Luijkenstraat 3; Oct 1982) (E: "Lucky Luijk (Jan Luijkenstraat 3)", e8yv8jpiop9tmx3lghuwdrgv): the eviction that burned a tram and brought the first emergency decree since the war. Status: changed (four flats). Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Lucky Luyk](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucky_Luyk); [monument register 505807](https://monumentenregister.cultureelerfgoed.nl/monumenten/505807); [Trouw: Oude tijden leken te herleven](https://www.trouw.nl/nieuws/oude-tijden-leken-te-herleven~bae1bf64/). 10. **Tabakspanden** (Spuistraat 219-221 and Wijdesteeg 3-7; March 1983 to 25 March 2015) (E: "The Tabakspanden, Amsterdam's longest-running squat", w4qmushssaqhv0cwnrgdwi8l): a slum landlord's empire held for 32 years. Status: changed (evicted 2015, redeveloped). Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Tabakspanden](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabakspanden); [Trouw: Ontruiming laatste kraaklocatie begonnen](https://www.trouw.nl/nieuws/ontruiming-laatste-kraaklocatie-begonnen~bab4a707/); [Amsterdam op de kaart](https://amsterdamopdekaart.nl/1850-1940/Spuistraat/219-221). Caveats: the Trouw link is about a 2010 eviction day, not this one; find an AT5/Parool report of the 25 March 2015 eviction battle when drafting.

11. **Kraakliedenbuurt and Hans Kok** (Schaepmanstraat 59; 1980s, 1985) (E: "Kraakliedenbuurt and Hans Kok (Schaepmanstraat)", jtfibja538zai0hy5mlprl5n): 800 squatters in the Staatsliedenbuurt, and the squatter who died in a police cell. Status: changed. Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Hans Kok](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Kok_(kraker)); [Ons Amsterdam: Een fijne biotoop](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/een-fijne-biotoop); [Amsterdam Alternative](https://amsterdamalternative.nl/articles/3256/it-was-once-the-kraakliedenbuurt).

12. **Het Lieverdje and the Spui** (Spui; Provo happenings 1964-1967) (E: "Het Lieverdje and the words on the Spui", clrl0tm9f4ge90aibrn9ooc3; or the cluster "Spui, where Provo was born, and its corners", l1awc3rqmfpgktsp5zqwnl18): Grootveld's Saturday-night magic happenings round the cigarette-funded statue, the birthplace of Provo and the White Bicycle Plan. Status: open (public square). Evidence: [nl.wikipedia Het Lieverdje](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Het_Lieverdje); [AT5](https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/226839); [De Groene: Het Magisch Centrum van de asfaltjungle](https://www.groene.nl/artikel/het-magisch-centrum-van-de-asfaltjungle). Caveats: pick one of the two places.

13. **Wyers** (Nieuwezijds Voorburgwal 5, head of the street; squatted 1981, evicted 14 Feb 1984) (N): the textile wholesaler's block that became the city's biggest squat complex ("Wyers wijkt niet"), cleared with riots for a Holiday Inn. Status: gone (now a Crowne Plaza hotel). Evidence: [Rijksmuseum: Ontruiming van het kraakpand Wijers (photos, 1984)](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/nl/collectie/object/Ontruiming-van-het-kraakpand-Wijers--42bbb3526f17e03d1b01f55edfcbf1c0); [Ons Amsterdam: De twee werelden van Wyers](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/de-twee-werelden-van-wyers); [NOS Journaal 14 Feb 1984 (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iC32XO6jz8Y). Caveats: gone place; it qualifies by impact (the eviction was the first one a mayor announced in advance, a turning point), but nothing on site tells the story. Could be drafted as a "(gone)" place like the Grote Wetering. Spelling varies: Wyers / Wijers.

14. **Kroningsoproer: the Slag om de Blauwbrug** (Blauwbrug and Waterlooplein; 30 April 1980) (E: "Blauwbrug", wiuoj22wga9vggny6injz4ws): "Geen woning, geen kroning": the coronation-day riots, whose fiercest battle was fought at this bridge. Status: open (bridge). Evidence: [Het Parool: Geen woning, geen kroning (readers' memories, 2010)](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/geen-woning-geen-kroning~b1516ff5/); [Nationaal Archief: Kroningsrellen 1980](https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/beleven/verhalenarchief/kroningsrellen-1980); [isgeschiedenis](https://isgeschiedenis.nl/reportage/geen-woning-geen-kroning-de-kroningsrellen-van-1980). Caveats: the live Blauwbrug text is about its 1883 design and has no 1980 story; a theme-map override (or a body addition) is needed. Alternative: a separate N place "Kroningsoproer" pinned on the Waterlooplein.

15. **Kalenderpanden** (Entrepotdok 87-98; squatted Nov 1996 to 31 Oct 2000) (N; possible child of E "Entrepotdok, the bonded warehouses behind a wall", elt3qfrn5jv7jt44s2d9amcj): twelve warehouses named after the months, the last great squat bastion of the 1990s, with a public hall for concerts and theatre; its eviction came with raised bridges and barricades. Status: changed (luxury flats). Evidence: [Trouw 1 Nov 2000: Waren de Kalenderpanden het laatste krakersbolwerk?](https://www.trouw.nl/nieuws/waren-de-kalenderpanden-het-laatste-krakersbolwerk~b6f7ec10/); [Digibron: Kalenderpanden in Amsterdam ontruimd](https://www.digibron.nl/viewer/collectie/Digibron); [squatters' own archive site](https://entrepotdok.squat.net/history.html); [Vrienden van de Amsterdamse Binnenstad: Op de bres LIII](https://www.amsterdamsebinnenstad.nl/). Caveats: the buildings stand; their month names are worth checking on the gables. The Trouw piece also names Vrieshuis Amerika and the Westerdoksdijk studios (see Borderline) and the 2000 start of the city's broedplaatsen policy.

16. **Grote Wetering squat** (Weteringschans/Vijzelgracht corner; evicted Dec 1980) (E: "Grote Wetering squat, evicted with Sinterklaas on the roof (gone)", gsaa5e9ntbjc4p90jq3utsc2): two thousand police, containers on the roof, and Sinterklaas stepping out of one. Status: gone (1983 block). Evidence: [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grote_Wetering_(Amsterdam)); [Delpher scans already on the place](https://resolver.kb.nl/resolve?urn=ddd:010621572:mpeg21:a0162). Caveats: also inside "Weteringcircuit..." (f25e8j91i9jbfbqc5mh7jl9h).

17. **Vondelbunker** (Vondelpark 8a; beat cellar 1968, free space today) (E: "Vondelbunker: fallout shelter, beat cellar, free space", zkgf7wnd6hdhxrwxi1b1l53y): a Cold War shelter under a bridge, volunteer-run, no entrance fee. Status: open. Evidence: [vondelbunker.nl](https://vondelbunker.nl); [indebuurt: schuilen en swingen](https://indebuurt.nl/amsterdam/toen-in/schuilen-en-swingen-in-de-beatkelder-dit-gebeurde-er-met-de-bunker-onder-de-vondelbrug~410287/); [Amsterdam Alternative venues](https://amsterdamalternative.nl/network/venues). Caveats: the music slice may also claim it; one entry.

18. **Mellow Yellow, the first coffeeshop** (Weesperzijde 53, later Vijzelgracht; 1972/73-2017) (N): hippies squatted a bakery, turned it into a tea house in 1973 where hash was sold over the bar: the first coffeeshop. Fire in 1978 moved it to the Vijzelgracht; the school-distance rule closed it on 1 January 2017. Status: gone. Evidence: [Het Parool: Oudste coffeeshop slachtoffer van afstandscriterium](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/oudste-coffeeshop-van-amsterdam-slachtoffer-van-afstandscriterium~bd6bce18/); [Andere Tijden: Oudste coffeeshop Mellow Yellow sluit deuren](https://anderetijden.nl/artikel/6658/Oudste-coffeeshop-Mellow-Yellow-sluit-deuren); [Ons Amsterdam: De eerste coffeeshop ter wereld](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/de-eerste-coffeeshop-ter-wereld). Caveats: gone at both addresses; qualifies on impact. Pin at Weesperzijde 53 (the squat, which is the story); what stands there now is unchecked. nl.wikipedia says founded 1972, the shop itself 1973.

19. **The Bulldog The First** (Oudezijds Voorburgwal 90; since 1975) (N): Henk de Vries's first coffeeshop in a basement under his family's sex cinema on the Wallen, the seed of the Bulldog chain and its hundreds of police raids. Status: open. Evidence: [Het Parool, Geschiedenis van Amsterdam: the Leidseplein Bulldog and the Wallen shop before it](https://www.parool.nl/geschiedenis-van-amsterdam/in-het-pand-op-het-leidseplein-waar-de-politie-vroeger-kantoor-hield-opende-henk-the-bulldog-de-vries-zijn-coffeeshop-en-dat-was-geen-toeval~b48cf4e5/); [thebulldog.com: The First](https://thebulldog.com/locations/coffeeshops/the-bulldog-the-first/); [nl.wikipedia Mellow Yellow (Bulldog in 1975)](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mellow_Yellow_(coffeeshop)). Caveats: commercial, touristy today; "first" claims compete (Rusland also says 1975). Pair with item 20 or pick one.

20. **Politiebureau No. 14, now The Bulldog** (Leidseplein 13-17; since 1985) (E: "Politiebureau No. 14, now The Bulldog", n62kgli3jrtrz1thoeji9jec): the coffeeshop king moving into the old police station, on April Fools' Day 1985. Status: open. Evidence: [Het Parool history piece above](https://www.parool.nl/geschiedenis-van-amsterdam/in-het-pand-op-het-leidseplein-waar-de-politie-vroeger-kantoor-hield-opende-henk-the-bulldog-de-vries-zijn-coffeeshop-en-dat-was-geen-toeval~b48cf4e5/); [data.amsterdam monument](https://data.amsterdam.nl/monumenten/1c55b452-8428-41a8-bca0-0def6e8e6e3f). Caveats: legend more than underground.

21. **Damslapers and the Oranje Vrijstaat at the National Monument** (Dam; summers 1967-1970; 5 Feb 1970) (E: "National Monument on the Dam", w2ym89og3or6sdq4eu5thz9t): hundreds of hippies slept on the monument's steps until the sleeping ban of 24 August 1970 and the marines who cleared it; on the same Dam the Kabouters proclaimed their Orange Free State. Status: open. Evidence: [Historiek: Mariniers vegen de Dam schoon (1970)](https://historiek.net/damslapers-mariniers-1970-verjoegen-hippies-dam/139857/); [EenVandaag: De zomer van 1970, de Damslapers](https://eenvandaag.avrotros.nl/artikelen/de-zomer-van-1970-de-damslapers-38416); [Ons Amsterdam: Kleine mensjes met grote idealen (Oranje Vrijstaat)](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/kleine-mensjes-met-grote-idealen). Caveats: A-list place; the live body has one line on the hippies, the Kabouter proclamation is not in it.

22. **Vondelpark, the 1971 sleeping park** (Vondelpark; summers 1971-1972) (E: "Vondelpark", qd0m975el7mdfp3aysy59gps): after the Dam ban the city made the park an official place to sleep, with a luggage depot and washrooms; up to 1,500 to 2,000 young travellers a night, world news. Status: open. Evidence: [Ons Amsterdam: Het Vondelpark, zomer 1971](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/het-vondelpark-zomer-1971); [ONH: Het Vondelpark, zomer 1971](https://onh.nl/verhaal/het-vondelpark-zomer-1971); [Geschiedenislokaal Amsterdam: Slapen in het Vondelpark](https://geschiedenislokaalamsterdam.nl/bronnen/slapen-in-het-vondelpark). Caveats: the live Vondelpark text says nothing of this; needs a theme-map override. The Blauwe Theehuis (sbbk8696zu95zuu2qvw7cd2o) has no documented hippie link I could find, so leave it out.

23. **Sleep-in, St. Elisabethgesticht (Hotel Arena)** ('s-Gravesandestraat 51; sleep-in from 1982) (E: "St. Elisabethgesticht, now Hotel Arena", kf8swumgnokewmp0ugngtfdr): the city's budget sleep-in for young travellers in an old Catholic home. Status: changed (Hotel Arena). Evidence: [Hotel Arena: geschiedenis](https://hotelarena.nl/sint-elisabeth-geschiedenis/); [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sint_Elisabeth_Gesticht). 24. **Gliphoeve** (Bijlmer-Oost, now Geldershoofd and Gravestein; summer 1974) (N; possible child of E "Bijlmermeer", et0awp08hhog3xeyhfwv3crm): Surinamese activists under Just Maatrijk squatted nearly a hundred empty flats against the housing corporations' quotas: one of the first acts of Black activism in the Netherlands, and they won leases. Status: changed (renovated, renamed Geldershoofd and Gravestein). Evidence: [Volkskrant: Hoe een kraakactie de Bijlmer voorgoed veranderde](https://www.volkskrant.nl/kijkverder/2017/gliphoeve/); [VPRO: Gliphoeve, een vergeten strijd](https://www.vpro.nl/gliphoeve/artikelen/gliphoeve-een-vergeten-strijd); [Arcam: Gliphoeve](https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/gliphoeve/). Caveats: strongest Zuidoost item in this slice. The Bijlmermeer body already mentions it in one sentence.

25. **NDSM Loods and Kunststad** (NDSM-plein; since 1999/2000) (E: "NDSM Loods (Scheepsbouwloods)", seladrj25mmhtixn8g69n02c): in 1999 skaters, theatre makers and ex-squatters of the harbour bastions won the shipyard tender as Kinetisch Noord and built an "art city" of studios inside the hall; the foundation now owns it on a 50-year lease. Status: open. Evidence: [NUL20: Make your city, NDSM-werf](https://www.nul20.nl/boeken/make-your-city-ndsm-werf-amsterdam); [NDSM Loods: broedplaats history](https://www.ndsmloods.nl/broedplaats/); [Eva de Klerk: NDSM project](https://www.evadeklerk.com/projecten/ndsmshipyard). Caveats: the live text mentions the art city but not Kinetisch Noord or its squat roots.

26. **Joe's Garage** (Pretoriusstraat 43; social centre since 2005) (N): a squatted, volunteer-run political social centre in the Transvaalbuurt: voku, give-away shop, the Oost squatting advice hour. It began in 2001 at Pretoriusstraat 28 and moved to no. 43, squatted in September 2005. Status: open. Evidence: [squat.net: 18 jaar Pretoriusstraat 43 en Joe's Garage](https://nl.squat.net/2023/09/13/amsterdam-18-jaar-pretoriusstraat-43-en-joes-garage-in-oost/); [Oost-online: Een monument in de Transvaalbuurt](https://oost-online.nl/een-monument-in-de-transvaalbuurt/); [joesgarage.nl](https://joesgarage.nl/). Caveats: no Parool/AT5 article found; the sources are a local paper and the movement's own. Dates differ (radar says open since June 2005, squat.net says no. 43 was squatted 25 Sept 2005): verify.

27. **Molli (Molli Chaoot)** (Van Ostadestraat 55; since 1979) (N): the squat café of De Pijp, active since 1979, still the Monday home of the squatting advice hour, with a vegan voku and free market. Evidence: [molli.squat.net](https://molli.squat.net/); [radar.squat.net listing Nov 2026](https://radar.squat.net/en/event/amsterdam/molli-chaoot/2026-11-24/vegan-voku-and-free-market?language=en); [offbeat.amsterdam](https://offbeat.amsterdam/event/vegan-voku-and-free-market-40). 28. **Plantage Dok** (Plantage Doklaan 8-12; squatted 1998, bought 2000) (N): a former church, printing works and craft school squatted by artists from the evicted Graansilo, bought with city help; studios, the Dokzaal and a gallery café. Status: open. Evidence: [Het Parool: Wandelen langs de kraakgeschiedenis van Amsterdam (2022)](https://www.parool.nl/ps/wandelen-langs-de-kraakgeschiedenis-van-amsterdam~bffb5ab1/); [Amsterdam op de kaart](https://amsterdamopdekaart.nl/1850-1940/Plantage_Doklaan/8-12); [plantagedok.nl history](https://plantagedok.nl/history/); [Nieuwe Instituut](https://nieuweinstituut.nl/projects/ontwerp-van-het-sociale/plantage-dok). 29. **Zaal 100** (De Wittenstraat 100; squatted 1984) (N): a squatted public school in the Kraakliedenbuurt, still a members' association and "stage for unheard sound": jazz, odd theatre, no private parties. A 2026 film (De Spelende Mens) tells its story. Status: open. Evidence: [zaal100.nl: over](https://zaal100.nl/over-zaal-100/); [OCCII: Human at Play screening](https://occii.org/events/cinemoccii-presents-human-at-play/); [Geheugen van West: documentary on the Staatsliedenbuurt and Zaal 100](https://geheugenvanwest.amsterdam/page/41020/documentairefilm-staatsliedenbuurt-zaal-100).
