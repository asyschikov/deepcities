# Scout: dive bars and cult cafés (Alternative Amsterdam)

Slice `dive-bars`. Scouted 2026-11-25. Bars that are "an institution with a scene": punks, rockers,
bluesmen, hip-hop, squatters, writers, journalists, artists, students. Shortlist only, nothing
drafted. Live DB checked with `dc places sights --all` (2360 places).

Source notes: Het Parool articles are mostly behind the paywall in the agent browser, so many
Parool facts below come from the search-result lead text of the article (date and opening
lines visible); they are cited with the article link. AT5, Volkskrant snippets, Babel, Vice,
Ons Amsterdam and venue pages were read in full where marked.

## Shortlist (by strength)

1. **Café Soundgarden** (Marnixstraat 164-166, on the Lijnbaansgracht side; since 1993) (N): the user's named pick and the city's best-known rock dive: wooden floors, pinball, table football, Sid Vicious and Iggy Pop on the walls, a canalside terrace, and a house cat (Dexter) that has its own Parool write-up. Anthony Bourdain drank here for "The Layover". Owner Maarten Luijendijk told Parool in 2023 the café turned thirty that year; Parool calls it a café with "a raw, alternative edge" that "bravely resists" like Asterix's village. Status: open. Evidence: [Parool, Maarten & Mayke, 25 Apr 2023 ("Dit jaar bestaat mijn café, Sound Garden, dertig jaar")](https://www.parool.nl/ps/maarten-mayke-dat-rauwe-randje-zie-je-nauwelijks-nog-in-amsterdamse-kroegen~b018b73d/); [Parool, bierterrassen, 31 Aug 2018 (Asterix quote)](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/de-fijnste-bierterrassen-in-de-binnenstad~b4ac2c63/); [Parool, 24 x terrassen aan het water, 2025 ("lekker rauwe, alternatieve rand")](https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/24-x-leuke-terrassen-aan-het-water-in-het-centrum-van-amsterdam~b2a064f8/); [De Westkrant, 1 Aug 2026 (heavy metal, Free Palestine murals)](https://www.dewestkrant.nl/vinyl-rocks-biedt-beats-bier-en-brazilian-bites/); [cafesoundgarden.nl](https://www.cafesoundgarden.nl/). Caveats: spelled both "Soundgarden" and "Sound Garden"; the 1993 date rests on the owner's own words in Parool; Bourdain link from travel blogs only (fine as colour, not as a headline fact).

2. **Maloe Melo** (Lijnbaansgracht 163; music café since c. 1980, Jur Scherpenzeel's since 1984) (N): the oldest blues and rock café in Amsterdam, a tiny back-room stage where De Dijk and The Fatal Flowers started and where touring acts (Dire Straits, Jeff Healey, Patti Smith, Chet Baker, Herman Brood) came after their big-hall shows. Cheapest beer in the centre, musicians' living room, nearly lost its live licence over noise in 1988 and was saved by a regulars' crowdfund in 2021. Status: open; Babel (May 2026) reports the 83-year-old owner is expected to hand over soon. Evidence: [AT5, 1 May 2021](https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/208754/stamgasten-helpen-bluesbar-maloe-melo-er-zijn-mensen-die-wel-duizend-euro-doneerden); [Parool, 28 Jan 2023 (took over the café, existing since 1980, in 1984)](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/kroegbaas-van-maloe-melo-viert-80ste-verjaardag-met-festival-in-paradiso-twintigers-zijn-gek-op-die-oude-shit-van-ons~b245e3da/); [Babel Magazine, 25 May 2026 (uses Parool and AD archive)](https://babelmagazine.nl/2026/05/25/de-op-onder-en-overgang-van-maloe-melo/). Caveats: the famous-guest list differs per source; use only "Dire Straits" etc. as "according to AT5/Babel". Watch for an ownership change.

3. **Café De Duivel** (Reguliersdwarsstraat 87; since 1992) (N): Daniël Eeuwens's "small dark hole" that became the hip-hop embassy of the Netherlands: only hip-hop, soul and funk on the speakers, the meeting place of Dutch rap since the 1990s. Survived a 2011 shooting and a mayoral closure. Status: open. Evidence: [Parool, 28 Jun 2020 "Hoe De Duivel uitgroeide tot hét hiphopcafé van de stad: dit moet een monument worden"](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/hoe-de-duivel-uitgroeide-tot-het-hiphopcafe-van-de-stad-dit-moet-een-monument-worden~b77f171d/); [Parool, Vijftig jaar hiphop, 10 Aug 2023](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/vijftig-jaar-hiphop-het-is-een-state-of-mind-en-als-je-het-leeft-deel-je-het~bb21da36/); [Parool, 23 Dec 2011 (closed after shooting)](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/cafe-de-duivel-gesloten-om-wapenincident~b08a5eb6/). Caveats: it is in the Reguliersdwarsstraat, the gay street (E: "Reguliersdwarsstraat, the gay street", euwnpg1ejb39ujh9tilpeqt1); keep them as separate places. Lead with the music, mention the shooting at most in passing.

4. **Molli (Molli Chaoot)** (Van Ostadestraat 55, De Pijp; since 1979) (N): the oldest (ex-)squatted bar in the city, a legalised squat café run by a volunteer collective: vegan Tuesday dinners, squatting-advice hour, acoustic bands, chess, benefit nights, feminist and queer nights. Somewhere between a living room, a pub and a political centre. Status: open (per Amsterdam Alternative listing; confirm). Evidence: [Amsterdam Alternative network listing](https://amsterdamalternative.nl/network/20465/molli-chaoot); [GoFundMe from the collective (pandemic, closed since March, refused aid as an "ideological organisation")](https://www.gofundme.com/f/molli). Caveats: no news or archive source found yet (Parool/AT5 search empty); 1979 is the collective's own claim. Overlaps the `squats-freezones` slice: decide which slice owns it. Needs one independent source (try Staatsarchief / Pijpkrant) before drafting.

5. **Korsakoff** (Lijnbaansgracht 161; 1988 to 2013) (N): the legendary alternative night bar of the Leidseplein back side, where goths, metalheads, punks and ravers shared "keiharde gitaarmuziek als trance" until 7 a.m. "Known to all of alternative Amsterdam" (AT5). Killed by the 2008 smoking ban and the crisis. Status: gone (Israeli restaurant Mesiba since 2022). Evidence: [AT5, 18 Aug 2013 "Korsakoff sluit na 25 jaar de deuren"](https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/109062/korsakoff-sluit-na-25-jaar-de-deuren); [Parool, 24 Dec 2012](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/korsakoff-redt-het-niet-meer~b16a9748/); [Volkskrant, 3 Feb 2001 (closed for fire safety)](https://www.volkskrant.nl/voorpagina/amsterdam-sluit-cafe-korsakoff-om-brandgevaar~b75873a3/); [Amsterdam op de kaart (building history: Boddy's Music Inn 1973, Stip, Korsakoff, Mesiba)](https://amsterdamopdekaart.nl/1850-1940/Lijnbaansgracht/161-165); [Archief Amsterdam beeldbank photo ("vermoedelijk een kraakcafé")](https://archief.amsterdam/beeldbank/detail/caa263be-886a-5b81-62ca-6b53dde838a7). Caveats: gone, so it fits only as a legend; nothing marks the site. A separate "Korsakoff Amsterdam" metal bar site claims 2004 to 2014 at the same address: conflicting, needs sorting before use. Strong pair with Maloe Melo next door (one entry "Lijnbaansgracht music cafés"?).

6. **Cruise Inn** (now Zuiderzeeweg 6B, Zeeburgereiland; since Dec 1982) (N): Europe's oldest rock 'n' roll club, a self-built wooden clubhouse of the rockabilly gang The Bopcats, first on the Zeeburgerdijk in an old church-disco shed, from 1999 at Zuiderzeeweg 29 (they demolished their own building with a party), and since 21 Nov 2026 in a new home. Jukebox, quiffs, dance lessons, band nights. Status: open (just moved, "Cruise Inn 3.0"). Evidence: [Parool, 18 Nov 2026 "Europa's oudste rock-'n-rollclub naar nieuwe locatie Zeeburgereiland"](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/europa-s-oudste-rock-n-rollclub-naar-nieuwe-locatie-zeeburgereiland-na-zaterdag-ruikt-het-hier-weer-gewoon-als-de-cruise-inn~bdd970f14/); [Parool, 18 Nov 2023 "kloppend hart van de rock-'n-roll"](https://www.parool.nl/ps/cruise-inn-kloppend-hart-van-de-rock-n-roll-moet-plaatsmaken-voor-nieuwbouw-wij-zijn-de-laatste-club-in-europa-die-trouw-is-aan-een-tijdperk~b7649504/); [cruise-inn.nl history](https://www.cruise-inn.nl/98ee9-about/). Caveats: "Europe's oldest" is Parool's headline phrasing; new address only a week old, verify coordinates.

7. **Café Eijlders** (Korte Leidsedwarsstraat 47; since Dec 1940) (N): opened in the first winter of the occupation and became the refuge of artists who refused to join the Nazi Kultuurkamer (Aafjes, Hoornik, Koos Schuur) and of banned student clubs; after 1945 the stamcafé of the Vijftigers (Campert, Lucebert, Vinkenoog, Bernlef, Ramses Shaffy). Still shows art and hosts poetry. Status: open. Evidence: [Ons Amsterdam, "Café Eijlders is al 80 jaar een artistieke huiskamer"](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/cafe-eijlders-is-al-80-jaar-een-artistieke-huiskamer); [Volkskrant, 18 Oct 2011 "Epicentrum Leidseplein" (stamcafé of the Vijftigers)](https://www.volkskrant.nl/nieuws-achtergrond/epicentrum-leidseplein~b461e51c/); [Parool, 15 Mar 2023](https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/5-cafes-met-kunst-aan-de-muur-in-amsterdam~b98368a4f/); [cafeeijlders.com history](https://www.cafeeijlders.com/geschiedenis-cafe-eijlders). Caveats: Kultuurkamer framing comes from the café's own history; Ons Amsterdam confirms the artists' founding.

8. **Café De Zwart** (Spuistraat 334; since 1921) (N): the newspaper-and-writers' café of the old Nieuwezijds press quarter, with its "moppertafel"; in the 1980s and 1990s the leading writers' café (A.F.Th. van der Heijden and others). Status: open. Evidence: [Ons Amsterdam, "Het schrijverscafé: Café de Zwart bestaat een eeuw"](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/het-schrijverscafe-cafe-de-zwart-bestaat-een-eeuw); [NH Nieuws, 22 Nov 2022](https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/311322/cafe-de-zwart-viert-honderdjarig-bestaan-het-is-thuiskomen); [Parool, 21 Nov 2022](https://www.parool.nl/ps/100-jaar-nederlandse-schrijvers-aan-de-moppertafel-cafes-als-de-zwart-zijn-belangrijk-voor-de-samenleving~bc977dfad/). Caveats: Parool itself says it is "long no longer a writers' café". Could pair with Scheltema (no. 10) as one "press quarter cafés" entry.

9. **Café Hesp** (Weesperzijde 130-131; since 1890, here since 1897) (N): a brown café on the Amstel that became the "branch office" of journalists when Parool, Volkskrant and Trouw sat in the Wibautstraat; women were "a rare sight". Family-run by the Hesp brothers until 1991; a 125-year book (De Republiek). Status: open. Evidence: [Parool, 16 Sep 2015 "Vrouwen waren een zeldzaam verschijnsel in journalistencafé Hesp"](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/vrouwen-waren-een-zeldzaam-verschijnsel-in-journalistencafe-hesp~b66ad3f0/); [Ons Amsterdam, "Café Hesp alias De Posthoorn is er al bijna 125 jaar"](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/cafe-hesp-alias-de-posthoorn-is-er-al-bijna-125-jaar); [Oost-Online, 23 Oct 2020](https://oost-online.nl/boeiend-boek-over-125-jaar-cafe-hesp-aan-de-amstel/). Caveats: a journalists' bar more than an "alternative" one; good as the only Oost entry with a press scene.

10. **Café Scheltema** (Nieuwezijds Voorburgwal 242; since 28 Apr 1908) (N): "of old the journalists' café of the capital", when the Nieuwezijds was Fleet Street (Telegraaf, Handelsblad, Volkskrant); saved from bankruptcy in 2021 by Geert Mak, Sonja Barend and other regulars. Status: open. Evidence: [Parool, 13 Jan 2021](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/bekende-amsterdammers-in-de-bres-voor-bijna-failliet-cafe-scheltema~b181155c/); [Parool, Geschiedenis van Amsterdam, 5 Dec 2025](https://www.parool.nl/geschiedenis-van-amsterdam/dit-cafe-was-ooit-het-stamlokaal-van-journalisten-en-werd-in-coronatijd-door-bekende-amsterdammers-voor-de-ondergang-behoed~b6b0e7d4/); [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheltema_(caf%C3%A9)). Caveats: same press-quarter story as De Zwart; pick one or merge.

11. **The Minds** (Spuistraat 245; since 1984 per the bar) (N): self-styled "the only real punk bar left in Amsterdam", a gritty dive among the Spuistraat squats, cheap beer, punk records, small underground gigs (e.g. Konijnedag fest). Status: open. Evidence: [Lonely Planet listing ("hard-core but friendly punk bar", the city's last)](https://www-lonelyplanet-com-57e2.imagizer.com/the-netherlands/amsterdam/medieval-centre-and-red-light-district/nightlife/cafe-the-minds/a/poi-dri/1097240/1323036); [official Facebook ("Located in Amsterdam since 1984")](https://www.facebook.com/mindsamsterdam/); [theminds.nl (certificate expired at time of check)](https://theminds.nl/). Caveats: weak: no news or archive source found; 1984 only from the bar itself. Scene-wise it belongs here, but needs one Parool/AT5/Vice hit before drafting.

12. **Rock Club The Cave** (Prinsengracht 472, basement; since c. 1995) (N): the basement home of rock and metal in the centre, bar every day and bands at weekends; a 2020 "Save The Cave" campaign by musicians. Status: open. Evidence: [Parool, 2 Oct 2020 "Save The Cave" (25 years)](https://www.parool.nl/ps/save-the-cave-rockclub-the-cave-mag-niet-verdwijnen~b55e16b0/); [thecaveamsterdam.nl](https://www.thecaveamsterdam.nl/); [Rock Bar Legends guide, Sep 2026](https://rockbarlegends.com/guides/amsterdam-rock-bars). Caveats: start year derived from "25 years in 2020".

13. **Ruk & Pluk** (Linnaeusstraat 48, Oost; under this name since 1988) (N): a gloriously odd night café with hundreds of LED lights, a wall for the dead and an ape and an inflatable penis on the ceiling, run for decades by two barwomen in their seventies: "the most striking café in Amsterdam" (Vice). Status: open (check current staff). Evidence: [Vice NL, 31 May 2018](https://www.vice.com/nl/article/we-keuvelden-met-de-barvrouwen-van-ruk-and-pluk-het-markantste-cafe-van-amsterdam/); [Volkshotel blog (staff tip)](https://volkshotel.nl/fr/blog/hotspots/omas-tip-ruk-pluk). Caveats: one real press source; a cult dive with no music scene, so it is "dive" rather than "alternative". Before 1988 it was café Centraal.

14. **Café Reynders** (Leidseplein 6; café since 1902 under this family) (N): in the 1950s and 1960s the bohemian café of the Leidseplein, "where all the artists and journalists came", trefpunt of several generations of bohemians (book: "Reynders, legendarisch kunstenaarscafé"). Status: open (now mostly tourists, per Parool). Evidence: [Parool, 28 Sep 2017](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/cafe-reynders-daar-kwamen-alle-kunstenaars-en-journalisten~b0a19080/); [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reynders_(caf%C3%A9)); [Uitgeverij De Republiek book](https://www.uitgeverijderepubliek.nl/reynders-legendarisch-cafe-op-het-leidseplein/). Caveats: legend of the past, normie today; pairs naturally with Eijlders round the corner.

15. **Café De Pels** (Huidenstraat 25; since 1970) (N): "no café is named in literature as often", a brown café for journalists, artists, "intellectual types and welders"; one of the late-1960s "P-cafés" (Pels, Pieper, etc.). Status: open. Evidence: [Parool, 28 Jul 2020 "50 jaar De Pels"](https://www.parool.nl/ps/50-jaar-de-pels-er-wordt-geruzied-gedronken-gehuild-en-gesekst~b9a27fa7/); [Parool, 26 May 2023 (Dirk Wiarda's living room)](https://www.parool.nl/kunst-media/cafe-de-pels-herdenkt-stamgast-en-illustrator-dirk-wiarda-dit-was-zijn-huiskamer~b49a9c16/); [Amsterdamse schrijverscafés exhibition](https://amsterdamseschrijverscafes.wordpress.com/2020/10/26/cafe-de-pels/). Caveats: the "P-cafés" story needs one more source before use.

16. **Café Weber (and Lux)** (Marnixstraat 397 and 403; Weber "over thirty years") (N): the Leidseplein back-street night cafés where actors, musicians and the city's bar staff go after their own shifts; DJs, three floors of kitsch. Status: open. Evidence: [cafeweber.nl](https://cafeweber.nl/) ("al ruim dertig jaar een begrip"); [Parool, 27 Sep 2024 "de leukste nachtcafés" (Lux listed)](https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/we-zullen-doorgaan-de-leukste-nachtcafes-van-amsterdam~b5efed31/); [ADE venue page (Weber and Lux)](https://www.amsterdam-dance-event.nl/en/venues/weber-lux/2128733/). Caveats: weak on history: no founding year or news story found; reputation is real but mostly word of mouth. Borderline for "scene".

17. **Jazz Café Alto** (Korte Leidsedwarsstraat 115; live jazz "every day since 1953" per the café) (N): a narrow pijpenla with live jazz and blues every night until late, long Hans Dulfer's regular stage. Status: open. Evidence: [Parool, 22 Aug 2025 "9 x jazz in Amsterdam" ("al sinds 1953")](https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/van-pianobars-tot-relaxte-sessies-op-zondagmiddag-9-x-jazz-in-amsterdam~b7dead4e/); [jazz-cafe-alto.nl](https://www.jazz-cafe-alto.nl/home/). Caveats: the 1953 date is the café's own; Dulfer link from memory and listings, verify. Could go to `music-clubs` instead.

18. **Café Gollem** (Raamsteeg 4; since 1974, Parool says 1972) (N): the first specialty-beer café in Amsterdam, started by a Tolkien fan; it brought Belgian beer culture to the city long before craft beer. Status: open (now a six-café group). Evidence: [Parool, 18 Sep 2015 "10 cafés met een grote bierkaart"](https://www.parool.nl/nieuws/10-cafe-s-met-een-grote-bierkaart~bca045d5/); [cafegollem.nl history](https://cafegollem.nl/gollem-raamsteeg/); [Difford's Guide](https://www.diffordsguide.com/bars/E70yg9/gollems-proeflokaal). Caveats: a pioneer, not a scene bar; 1972 vs 1974 conflict. Borderline-strong.

19. **Café Schuim** (Spuistraat 189; since the late 1980s) (N): the artists' café of the squatter-era Spuistraat, with painter Peter Klashorst's work on the walls and a long counterculture clientele. Status: open. Evidence: [Parool, 22 Jul 2023 (Klashorst's paintings hang in café Schuim)](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/kunstenaar-peter-klashorst-66-ik-hoop-nog-steeds-dat-ik-ooit-word-ontdekt-en-daarna-rijk-en-beroemd-word~ba677423/); [Ons Amsterdam, De vaste route van Melle Daamen (site history behind the Handelsblad building)](https://onsamsterdam.nl/artikelen/de-vaste-route-van-melle-daamen). Caveats: weak: no founding date or scene article found; listings' "countercultural" claims are unsourced.

20. **Pacific Parc** (Polonceaukade 23, Westergas; from c. 2000s) (N): the bar that was "ground zero for punk and rock 'n' roll nights" in the Westergas, where today's young punk bands (The Covids) met; the crew now runs "Pacific Ondergronds" elsewhere. Status: changed (now "Pacific", restaurant and club under new owners). Evidence: [Bandcamp Daily scene report, 18 Apr 2024](https://daily.bandcamp.com/scene-report/punk-amsterdam-scene-report-2); [Rock Bar Legends, history section](https://rockbarlegends.com/guides/amsterdam-rock-bars). Caveats: weak on dates; no Dutch news source found; "gone/changed" legend of a recent scene, fails the "impact lasted" test unless more turns up.

### Existing places that belong on this map (E)

21. **Café 't Mandje** (Zeedijk 63; 1927) (E: "Bet van Beeren and Café 't Mandje", lfy792mylty8hyilrd3cvcn2): the original "everyone welcome" dive of the Zeedijk; also on the LGBTQ slice. Status: open.

22. **De Engelbewaarder** (Kloveniersburgwal 59; 1971) (E: "De Engelbewaarder, the first literary café", wn0kueflexn7v9p12vr4nv0v): first literary café of the Netherlands, then Sunday jazz. Status: open.

23. **Cotton Club** (Nieuwmarkt 5) (E: "Cotton Club, the Nieuwmarkt's Surinamese jazz café", ztxusocyqxritry12i9dtpgw): first haunt of the Surinamese community and Black GIs, jazz. Status: open.

24. **Kriterion** (Roetersstraat 170; 1945) (E: "Kriterion, the cinema the resistance students built", acidtq3bomknl9gh40gu7bo8): its bar is the student-run late café; occupied by students in 1982. Evidence for the café angle: [AT5, 7 Nov 2020](https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/205454/van-verzetsgroep-naar-studentenbioscoop-kriterion-bestaat-75-jaar); [Amsterdam Alternative network](https://amsterdamalternative.nl/network/amsterdam/recommendations). Status: open.

25. **Café Theo Ruiter** (Rozengracht 160) (E: "Café Theo Ruiter, the resistance man's literary bar", x1zb9tk0p5mafwrm6shd75zr): writers' and actors' bar until 1980; later the hipster dive Struik (closed 22 Mar 2020, [Parool](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/cafe-struik-sluit-maar-eerst-nog-een-keer-100-uur-struik~b137934c/)). Status: changed (check current tenant).

26. **Skatecafé Karin & Yvonne** (Gedempt Hamerkanaal 42; 2016) (E: "Skatecafé Karin & Yvonne", wto2stp9s00l2u87fpgh3ckl): skate ramp in a bar-restaurant, late club; on Amsterdam Alternative's list. Status: open. Young, but a real subculture home.

## Borderline

- **Café Hoppe** (Spui 18-20; 1670): famous brown café where Van Mierlo laid D66's groundwork and Carmiggelt drank; establishment, not alternative. Not on the map (only "Hoppe's wine house on the Egelantiersgracht" is).
- **Café Welling** (J.W. Brouwersstraat 32): musicians' and writers' café behind the Concertgebouw; on the writers'-café exhibition, but no scene story found.
- **Sociëteit De Kring** (Kleine-Gartmanplantsoen 7-9; 1922): the artists' society, 100 years in 2022, archive at Stadsarchief; private members' club (ballot), so not visitable as a bar upstairs ([en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Kring)).
- **Café Schiller** (Rembrandtplein 24a): painter-hotelier's café, artists and journalists; already covered by E "Schiller, the painter-hotelier's hotel" (cunjo2jebngw6z8xk6lyuehc). List-add only if the map wants writers' cafés.
- **Café Américain** (E: "Hotel American and Café Américain", k7wgh26mzqbpjs6x8les8hiy): writers' café, grand rather than alternative.
- **Café De Koe** (Marnixstraat 381; 1993): eetcafé that "draws musicians and artists" (own site); no press story on a scene found.
- **Checkpoint Charlie** (Nassaukade 48; 2015): DIY rock night café by ex-Melkweg and Soundgarden staff ([Westkrant 2015](https://www.dewestkrant.nl/checkpoint-charlie-open-op-koningsdag/)); too young.
- **The Waterhole** (Korte Leidsedwarsstraat 49): daily live rock covers bar, "over 25 years"; touristy, no scene coverage.
- **Bourbon Street** (Leidsekruisstraat 6-8; 1990): blues club; listings only.
- **Jazz café Casablanca** (Zeedijk 24-26; c. 1945/46): post-war jazz café (Kid Dynamite, Teddy Cotton), now partly karaoke; listing sources only, dates conflict. Better on `music-clubs`.
- **Café Excalibur** (Oudezijds Achterburgwal 48): heavy metal bar in De Wallen; no history found.
- **Café Nol** (Westerstraat 109): Jordaan kitsch sing-along night café (family-run "since 1966" per Fodor's); Jordaan levenslied scene, not alternative.
- **Café Belgique** (Gravenstraat 2): tiny beer bar with DJ nights; a "nice bar", no scene.
- **Schaakcafé Het Hok** (Lange Leidsedwarsstraat 134): chess café for 50 years, closed April 2025, now a Dutch restaurant ([Parool 14 Apr 2025](https://www.parool.nl/amsterdam/roemrucht-schaakcafe-het-hok-vlak-bij-leidseplein-na-50-jaar-gesloten-ik-beschouw-het-als-een-mislukking~bc42c82b/)); a cult, but gone with no trace.
- **Bar Bukowski / Joost** (Oost): 2000s neighbourhood dives of the Struik owners ([AD 2017](https://www.ad.nl/amsterdam/deze-4-bars-vieren-dit-weekend-allemaal-een-jubileum~a658068e/)); not institutions yet.

## Checked and dropped
