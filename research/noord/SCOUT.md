**A. Hidden history and curiosities**

1. Asterdorp gatehouse (Atelier Volten); Asterdwarsweg 10; the only surviving piece of a walled 1927 "woonschool" (housing school) where the city put "problem families" under the eye of female supervisors; then it housed refugees from bombed Rotterdam and, from 1942, became a Jewish ghetto (307 residents, 227 murdered; Germany recognised it as a ghetto in 2015); sculptor André Volten worked in the gatehouse from 1952 to 2002; strong layered story with a building you can still see; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asterdorp and https://joodsmonument.nl/en/page/721645/asterdorp; the inside opens only during Atelier Volten's weekend exhibitions (the 2026 show ended 1 November), and I found no memorial at the site; **Amazing**.
2. Volewijck gallows field (site); the tip of the old Volewijck spit by the former Tolhuis, roughly the Shell/Overhoeks bank opposite Centraal; until 1795, executed bodies hung here as a warning to ships entering the harbour; Rembrandt drew Elsje Christiaens hanging here in 1664; grim founding story of Noord; https://www.geocaching.com/geocache/GC79PX6 and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsje_Christiaens; nothing marks the spot and the location is approximate, so pin it as a site; **Nice**.
