## Merges (titles unchanged)

- 302 into "Schellingwouderkerk and bandstand" (waterland-south.yaml): new paragraph on the May 1573
  blockade of sunk ships ballasted with gravestones and stone from the church (De Frémery 1925, via
  Ons Amsterdam), the Spanish fleet passing in September 1573, clearing 1578–1580 and the 2022 sonar
  search. It fits the existing "burned in 1572" line (the village was burned in 1572, the church
  stones went into the IJ in 1573). The outcome of the search after 2022 is unclear (one December 2024
  headline claims a find, other reports say nothing found): left out.
- 303 into "Dorpskerk Durgerdam" (Hemony bell spared: Jan Kalf's four-language notice of 20 April
  1940 and the letter M) and "De Kapel, former town hall of Durgerdam" (bell by Gerard Koster, end
  1689, paid from a 1687 lottery; taken 13 January 1943, found in Groningen, returned). Single source
  (Ons Amsterdam 2016, village historian Dick Reedijk). The article's 1499 date for the Kapel is left out.
- 304 into "Durgerdam" (not the west-end ensemble: nos. 30–31 fall outside 37–63) as one Look for line:
  Ons Genoegen, three-door house of 1919 by Cor Harder, fisherman from Kuinre. Left out: the reason
  given for the move (Kuinre "ending up in the polder" happened in 1942, after the 1919 house) and the
  boat at the jetty (bought back around 2020, nothing current).
- 312 into "Tuinpark Tuinwijck, the allotments that kept moving" (r3-dijk.yaml): one paragraph on De
  Molen, Rust en Vreugd, Kweeklust and Wijkergouw's Arbeid Adelt, plus the route's 12 km and blue-topped
  posts. Correction to the scout: Kweeklust was founded in Nieuwendam (Noord), not in Oost.
- Pointer to 313 in "Holysloot and the 1916 flood (former Reformed church)" (waterland-north.yaml),
  one clause in each language.

## Site checks asked for

- 313 season and fares: settled from the ferry's own site (read November 2026): 1 April to 1 October,
  weekends and holidays 9–17, daily in July and August; 2026 fares 1 euro on foot, 2 with a bike,
  children and Stadspas free. 2026 season ran 4 April to 27 September. Rodi's 2025 figure (1.50,
  15 April) is older.
- 313 Queen Beatrix: the sources do agree. Holysloot.nl (14 June 1991: Bart John did not ask her a
  quarter) and Rodi 2025 ("schold ... het kwartje kwijt", i.e. waived it) both say she rode free; the
  scout misread Rodi. Kept, without the Máxima anecdote (single source).
- 314 pull ferries: no dated evidence after 2023 that both work (Komoot tours routed over them in
  September 2024 and July 2026 do not prove it). `check: Still there?` set in both languages.

## Uncertain or left out

- 301: start of digging 1826 (Wikipedia, Ons Amsterdam) against 1825 (HollandRoute): 1826 used. The
  "150 m wide canal" is left out (Wikipedia's note says 150 m was the whole strip with quays and side
  ditches). The 1990s nature banks are left out (no source found).
- 308: Heemschut asked for municipal monument status in 2020; no decision found, so no status claim.
  The year the bridge house became a hotel suite differs (SWEETS says 2017, Wikipedia 2020): left out.
- 314: the Oranjesluizen are given as "around 1870" (MIP; Wikipedia ties them to the 1872 IJ dam).
