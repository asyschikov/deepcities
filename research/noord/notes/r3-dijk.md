## Merges

- 205 into dijk.yaml:
  - "Nieuwendammerdijk east, from the Sluisje": a Clara sentence, plus a sentence on 283–285 (notaries' house of about 1750, back wing in brick 1877, Vereniging Hendrick de Keyser since 1959). One link (HCAN) added.
  - The scout's "first shown to the Dutch public at Nieuwendam in August 1741" is **not** supported. HCAN says Mout first let her graze among cows in the Buikslotermeer just north of Nieuwendam. Wikipedia says she landed in Rotterdam on 22 July 1741 and was shown at once. The text follows HCAN.
  - De Halve Maan went into "Nieuwendammerdijk west, with the Meerpad and Molenpad" in a different form from the scout's. Wikipedia (Nieuwendam) has De Halve Maan as the yacht yard Gijs de Vries Lentsch started around 1905 on the sawmill site at Molenpad 16. The stone kwakel on the Molenpad marks its old access bridge.
  - No source found for a house "De Halve Maan" at Nieuwendammerdijk 202–204, so that claim is left out. I added a sentence and a Look for line on the kwakel.
- 211 into nieuwendam.yaml, "Purmerplein and the Purmerweg gatehouses": one sentence on Eduard Cuypers' 1915 plan for Nieuwendams Belang. It covers 179 houses, mostly for HSM boatmen, of which only 56 were built after the 1916 flood. The houses were replaced from 1979.
- 234 and 253 into dijk.yaml, "W.H. Vliegenbos":
  - 234: a paragraph on the Hamerkwartier and Ketjen. The Raad van State annulled the Exclusiva plan on 13 March 2024 and the Draka plan on 5 June 2024. The deal of 19 February 2026 sets towers at least 200 m away, adds a waterside buffer park and keeps the Storkhal. Building starts in 2027 and the first homes come from 2030.
  - The scout's 840 m lethal zone, the 2025 evictions and the 900-band rehearsal complex are left out: no source opened for them.
  - 253: Beeldenbos details added (3 ha, free, Dansende figuur, native herbs, pavilion workshops) and the Bregman gateway.
  - I set `check: Still there?` on the Vliegenbos, because only the beeldenbosnoord.nl announcement of the unveiling on 22 March 2026 was found. One link added (NH Nieuws, now 6).
- 247 into r2-east.yaml, "Flyways (Harmen Liemburg), metro station Noord": a paragraph on Benthem Crouwel's station (132 m island platform on a viaduct, five voids, 16 m high). It also notes that in 2019 the line's seven Benthem Crouwel stations jointly won the BNA Beste Gebouw and the Gouden A.A.P., so these are not prizes for Noord alone. Two links added.
- 248 into r2-east.yaml, "Zilveren Manen, Waterlandplein": a paragraph and two Look for lines. It covers the 1968 metro plan terminus that never came (its strip is still empty), the 2000–2013 rebuild with six lighthouse-named towers, and the October 2012 renaming. Two links added.
  - The scout's "six towers by Van Heeswijk" is wrong. Van Heeswijk renovated existing flats, and the new towers and plinth are by others (Architects Cie, atelier PRO per Architizer). No architect is named in the text.
- Pointer from "Tajiri's Ontmoetingsplaats and Van Kruiningen's mosaics, Het Breed" to Plan Van Gool: one sentence in both languages.

## Left out and conflicts

- 202 Anemoi exists after all. There is a dated photo (Commons, 31 July 2025) and nl.wikipedia's Nieuwendammerdijk article says it was replaced in 2024/2025. Rademacher de Vries lists "Air Quality Station Anemoi".
  - The design story (Cultuurfonds money, Tower of the Winds) rests on nl.wikipedia only.
  - The scout's "glows from purple to blue" is not in the text. The photos show a dial with that colour scale, not a light, and the text describes the dial.
- 242:
  - The construction dates disagree: architectuurgids says 1962–1968, nl.wikipedia (Buikslotermeer) 1966–1970, and the TU Delft paper building from 1966 with completion in 1972. The text uses 1966–1972.
  - "After objections" is softened to "a round of public comments": the gazette only mentions zienswijzen and a reply note of November 2022, and their content was not found.
- 243:
  - "Built in the 1980s" rests on Amsterdam Alternative only.
  - The 2.4 million to 600,000 euro price cut is a quote from the adviser Eva de Klerk in that article.
  - What "5711" stands for is unclear, so it is not explained.
- 244: the 2026 redevelopment figures (from 1 October 2027, about 70% of the land lost) come from the community's own Amsterdam Alternative article, and the text attributes them to the community.
- 257: the scout's native-plant garden, bird island and owl boxes were not found on the club's site and are left out. The 1909/1910 opening date comes from a 1979 local newspaper article on the club's site ("nog geen jaar later" after 31 March 1909), so the text says "within a year" and "about 1910".
- 258: opening on 20 March 2026 (Amsterdam Now). Exact opening hours were not in the scraped page, so the text gives only the days.

## Route idea

"Three ways to keep a place", Noord-Oost by bike, about 7 km:

Tuinpark Buitenzorg → W.H. Vliegenbos (Beeldenbos) → Air-quality station Anemoi → Plan Van Gool → Tajiri's Ontmoetingsplaats → De Bowling → KiMu → Flyways, metro station Noord → Het Groene Veld (on a concert night).

Tuinwijck fits a separate eastern loop with Schellingwoude and Ransdorp.
