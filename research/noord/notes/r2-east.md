## Conflicts and decisions

- 132: the scout's "De Turfdrager" is the OSM name. Every published source calls it "De Veenarbeider" (AT5, the Broek dorpsraad paper, Oud Broek newsletter, the artist's site), so that is the title. It was unveiled on 19 April 2011 by Prince Willem-Alexander when the Volgermeer reopened. It would also make a good Look for line in "Volgermeerpolder", but that place was not on my merge list.
- 131 bandstands: the city monument register says unemployed shipbuilders built them as work relief, around 1910–1925; Kunstwacht says the village bands built them around 1922 under carpenter Cornelis Schreuder, with Amsterdam's timber. Both accounts are now in the texts, attributed. No check flag set, since the date was already given as a range.
- 116 the scout calls the plate corten steel; no source confirms the material, so it is not mentioned. Wikipedia calls the lake a provincial monument; not repeated because no provincial register entry was checked.
- 126 Tajiri: Kunstwacht calls him "originally Japanese"; Stadscuratorium says he was a child of Japanese immigrants (he was born in the US). The text follows Stadscuratorium.
- 137 Breur's statelessness (for serving in Spain) is sourced from 4en5meiamsterdam.nl and nl.wikipedia (Aat Breur-Hibma). This also gives the Spanjemonument slice a source for the loss of citizenship.
