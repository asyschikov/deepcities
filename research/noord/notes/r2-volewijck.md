## Decisions and conflicts

- **Friendly Fire pin**: on the Van der Pekplein (War Pigeon cover, the first one unveiled), not the Mosveld as the scout suggested, because HCAN says the Mosveld and Ranonkelkade covers were moved to new spots in May/June 2021 (the Mosveld one had been damaged by street sweepers) and their new positions are not mapped. Exact positions of all 12 covers are not in OSM; `check: Location`.
- **Friendly Fire count**: 11 covers in 2018 plus a twelfth in 2021 (corrections to the names; Fazantenweg/Meeuwenlaan, where a direct hit on Dr Van Haga's practice killed 29). HCAN pages say both "more than thirty" and 29 dead there; 29 used (the more specific figure).
- **Friendly Fire evidence of current state**: Commons photos of covers in 2020, 2023 and 2024; Het Parool "Blikvangers" piece of 6 July 2026 (paywalled, headline and lead read).
- **De Phoenix moves**: the sources only seem to disagree. 1951 on a 6 m needle in a plantsoen lost for the IJ-tunnel roads; stored; on the Mosveld from 1968 (Commons 2011 caption); to the Kamperfoelieweg in 2016 (4en5mei; Commons photo 2018 there). Kunstwacht's "moved in 1968 to the present spot" was written while it stood on the Mosveld. Kunstwacht also gives 179 dead for 17 July (not used).
- **Naald van Goedkoop artist**: Kunstwacht names no artist; nl.wikipedia gives Carel Kneulman with details (his first commission, his name on the medallion, his resignation from the yard in 1941). Used without a check flag. Kunstwacht's "placed here in 1987 to mark the bond with Tuindorp Oostzaan" conflicts with nl.wikipedia's account of later moves (2011, 2017, once to Tuindorp Oostzaan); the bond claim was left out.
- **Nauta windows**: the wall-art register dates them 1939 (jubilee and memorial window) and 1946 (two more memorial windows); 4en5mei has the memorial windows unveiled on 4 July 1947; nl.wikipedia says 1939 and 1947. Written as "one of 1939" and "memorial windows unveiled on 4 July 1947". They are in the rear wing (Clink hostel, Badhuisweg 3), not in A Lab's own wing; said that they can't be seen from the street. Floors left out (sources disagree).
- **Spanjemonument numbers**: nl.wikipedia gives about 700 Dutch volunteers (Internationale Brigades article) and about 1,400 (Spanjemonument article). Written as "hundreds". Citizenship loss is sourced in both articles, as the scout asked.
- **Lancaster squadron**: 4en5mei says 96 Squadron with code OF-C; OF is the code of 97 Squadron. Squadron left out.
- **Noorderklankpark date**: OSM start_date 2010; Van Dorp's own video is titled "Noorderklankpark mei 2011". Written "around 2010".
- **Stienstra**: Kunstwacht says the building held businesses from 1995 (the occasion for the commission), while the existing place says Stadsherstel bought it in 2002. Both can be true; kept both.

## Route idea

"Bombs on Noord, July 1943": Friendly Fire covers (Van der Pekplein, Meidoornplein, Hagedoornplein with Sint Rita complex (BUNK) and the Spanjemonument, Kraaienplein with the Gerben Wagenaar plaque), Fazantenweg twelfth cover and 1946 stone, Noorderpark station (Lancaster), Mosveld's stone floodlights and the Mosveld cover, De Phoenix (Kamperfoelieweg), ending at the Fokker bombing memorial and the Draka wall at De Nieuwe Noorder.
