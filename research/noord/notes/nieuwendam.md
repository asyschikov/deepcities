## Pins

- Purmerplein: register point of Purmerplein 15-19 (central north block).
- Kitchen houses north: Beemsterstraat 41-55 register point; south: Avenhornstraat 1-15.
- Old people's houses: Monnikendammerplantsoen 21-28 (middle of the horseshoe).
- Boterenbrood: the U-shaped elderly court at Ilpendammerstraat 37-71. The register text names the court on Ilpendammerstraat but gives no numbers; I located it from the BAG address pattern (odd numbers 37-71 curving in a U at the eastern tip, own postcode 1023AT) and a Commons photo titled "2024 Ilpendammerstraat 37-71". Pin is at no. 51 (bottom of the U). Worth a quick check on aerial imagery.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- School, Wognumerplantsoen 2-4: the national register says a Christian primary school with drawings by Publieke Werken; DBNL (Monumenten in Nederland) says it was "possibly" by C.B. Posthumus Meyjes; Rodi (Aug 2026) calls it the former "Openbare Wognumschool" (public school) and mentions Lloyd Wright influence. I followed the register (Christian school, Publieke Werken) and mentioned the Wright touch as local historians' view. A search summary also attributed a different school/teacher's house to Cornelis Schuitemaker; not used.
- Current use of the school: Vrije School Kairos, in the building since 7 July 2017 (school guide 2017-2018) and confirmed by Rodi in Aug 2026.
- Tuindorp build dates: register 1923-1927 (design to completion), city text and Rodi 1926-1927 for construction, Wikipedia 1924-1934 for the whole village incl. extensions. Used 1923–1927 for the complex 508390 places (as in the register).
- Wikipedia calls the Monnikendammerplantsoen houses "bankwoningen"; no second source explains the term, so it is not used. The register does describe a brick bench in one gatehouse, which is mentioned.
- Church: opening date 18 February 1849 from Rodi (175 years on 18 Feb 2024). Register attributes the design to G. Bronke and L.J. Immink (city data); not discussed further in text. The 1916 flood level stone is from nl.wikipedia only (Rodi article does not mention it).
- Brede Kerkepad 6: some event listings give the church's address as "Brede Kerkepad 6-8"; any link between house 6 and the church is not established and not claimed.
- Status: all eight items are standing. Church still in use (2024 anniversary service; tower restored 2022-2023). No demolition or closure news found.

## Walking route idea

"Garden village and dike church" (about 1.5 km): Nieuwendammerkerk -> Wooden houses, Brede Kerkepad -> (up the Nieuwendammerstraat) Boterenbrood's village, Ilpendammerstraat -> Purmerplein and the Purmerweg gatehouses -> Kitchen houses north of the Purmerweg -> Kitchen houses south of the Purmerweg -> Old people's houses, Monnikendammerplantsoen -> Former school, Wognumerplantsoen.
