## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- **Stories height**: Dezeen gives 47 m (used); the scout had 45 or 47 m. Storeys: 13 (Baunetz; Commons description: three concrete plus ten timber storeys). Body states only the 47 m.
- **Patch22 "tallest timber apartment building in NL"**: stated as billed at completion (2016 source); later towers are taller, so the text does not claim it is still the tallest.
- **Top-Up's PTT story**: now confirmed by the architect's own BNA sheet ("transformatie van ptt kabelopslag") and the Council on Open Building (concrete cable reels). The transatlantic-cable testing detail from the scout snippet was left out (single secondary source).
- **Tolhuiskade beacon pin**: no source gives its address. Sources say it stands "on the bend of the Tolhuiskanaal" at the corner of Cityplot C. Pinned at the scout's point by the bend (Conny Stuartstraat 22). Leen Jongewaardkade 43–65 turned out to be another building (The Doors, Space Encounters), so the beacon is likely among the Leen Jongewaardkade numbers nearer the bend (about 75–109); `check: Location` set.
- **Cityplot years**: the scout's 2016–2025 range is not confirmed; no year given for the district as a whole.
- **Republica awards**: the Architectenweb Awards 2024 "residential building of the year" claim rests only on a property listing; left out.
- **Republica public access**: the press text says the squares and alleys are car-free and connect via two bridges to the neighbourhood's walking routes; body says they were "designed to be walked through", without promising access at all hours.
- **Self-build plots**: the zoning amendments (2013, 2017) are the backbone: crisis, markets autumn 2011, July 2012 and 8 June 2013, Verwo concrete-plant site (kavel 3), about 60 plots there by 2017, height cut 30 to 15 m. Which plot holds which architect's house (NEXT, Fem) is unconfirmed, so no individual house is named.
- **De Jakoba**: nominated for the Gouden A.A.P. 2023; the winner was not checked, so only the nomination is stated. Address: the BAG puts the building's flats on the Spadinalaan (numbers around 688–778); pin is the OSM building centroid.
- **Hyperion's hidden artwork** (Lara Almarcegui, hidden behind Bold with a 10 cm gap): from nl.wikipedia only.
