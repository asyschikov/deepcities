## Status checks

- Atelier Volten: open Saturday and Sunday 13:00–17:00 during exhibitions until 2 November 2026, again from 15 March 2027; groups by appointment in winter.
- Begoniastraat 6: the HCAN pop-up ran May 2019 to February 2021; no regular opening now. Text says to look from the street.
- Sint Ritakerk: hotel BUNK. Convent and school are homes.
- Disteldorp: Lieven de Key is weighing renovation or demolition and rebuilding in the same style; decision expected 2028 (NH Nieuws, 5 September 2025). Said plainly.
- Floradorp bonfire: banned for 2025 and again for 2026.
- Parakeet statue: unveiled 28 August 2026 (AT5). The 2023 resident vote comes from the SCOUT.md lead only; not independently verified.
- Café Modern: open in 2026 (Entree Magazine, own site).
- Gone: only the gallows field (title "(gone)", `status: Gone`). It passes the high bar: lasting impact, the Rembrandt drawings, the 2016 bridge name, and the pit thought to lie under the A'DAM Toren.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Tolhuis listing year: register 1997 (used), nl.wikipedia 1998.
- Asterdorp: 131 vs 132 homes, and 1926 vs 1927 for completion; slice record year_from 1925 (design). Used 132 and 1927 opening, built 1926–1927.
- Sint Rosaschool (498729): nl.wikipedia says the girls' school was demolished around 1991, but RCE still lists 498729 (Hagedoornplein 12-30 / Wingerdweg 4), noting roof loss to fire in 1992. I kept the register version; the demolished buildings are probably the cloister's original neighbouring schools.
- Sint Rita as a hotel: about 2018 per RCE, 2022 per nl.wikipedia (BUNK opening). The nl text says "sinds ongeveer 2018 een horecabestemming".
- Shell canteen dates: register 1963–1965 / 1974 vs 1971–1974 elsewhere. Used 1963–1974.
- Mosveld shopping centre architect: SeARCH (nl.wikipedia) vs NAMA (a search hit). Left the architect out.
- Mosveld towers: presented at the opening as a nod to industry; the shopping centre and Jurryt van de Vooren say they refer to the floodlights. Both given.
- Ponthuys: café-restaurant today; probably Polly Goudvisch (Buiksloterweg 3-5) but not confirmed, so no name in the text.
- Villa Abspoel: the register gives Tolhuisweg 5; the Tolhuistuin site uses the name "Villa Abspoel". The register text attributes design to Abspoel in 1937, build 1938.
- Wingerdschool: no register reasons text published (record only); text built from nl.wikipedia and Stadsherstel.

## Walking route idea

Ferry Buiksloterweg, Ponthuys, Players and Supporters, 't Tolhuis, Tolhuistuin and canteen, Villa Abspoel, A Lab, gallows field (A'DAM Toren), parakeet statue, police post and lock-keeper's house at the Willemssluis, then north along the Buiksloterweg to the Sint Rita complex, Café Modern and Begoniastraat 6 in the Van der Pekbuurt, Wingerdweg (school, GG&GD post, Johanna Margaretha, Florapark shed), Mosveld, Disteldorp, Asterdorp gatehouse, and Floradorp. The Fokker memorial at De Nieuwe Noorder is a 15-minute detour north. About 5 km.
