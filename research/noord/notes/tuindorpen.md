## Status checks and decisions

- Vogeldorp (needs watching): in late August 2025 owner Lieven de Key said it is studying two scenarios, thorough renovation or demolition and rebuilding in the same style (NH Nieuws, 5 September 2025). No decision found. The text says so plainly; update when a decision comes.
- Bosplaat: not yet a monument. The city record says "In procedure Gemeentelijk Monument"; the COK advised listing in 2025. heritage_status and monument_number are left out; add them once the listing is final.
- Salvatorkerk: last mass 24 June 2012; I found no reliable current use, so the text does not name one.
- Sacramentskerk: no mass since 2008; the Historisch Archief Tuindorp Oostzaan says it is used for gatherings and is for sale. Not confirmed elsewhere.
- Museum Amsterdam Noord: opening hours (Thursday to Sunday afternoons) come from a third-party listing; the text tells readers to check the website.
- Skatecafé: the user called it "quite unique" and wants it added, but it is item 40 of the ndsm slice, not this one, so it is not in this file.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Bethlehemkerk listing date: nl.wikipedia says 28 January 1988, the city data says 1997. `listed` is left out.
- Doctor's house and villas, Zonnehuis/Zonneplein: national and municipal listings with different years, so `listed` is left out.
- 6575/6576/6608/6609/6731: listing year not found, `listed` left out. 6607 uses 1970 from nl.wikipedia.
- Noorder IJplas: nl.wikipedia says fresh water, the NH Nieuws guide says slightly brackish next to the canal. The text gives both in that way.
- IJplein: the scout said residents disliked the layout; I found no solid source, so it is left out.
- Museumwoning photo (File:Hatomuseum.jpg) is a probable but unconfirmed match; Salvatorkerk photo (File:Amsterdam-Noord - Savelsbos.JPG) may not show the church. Check both before import.

## Route ideas

- Vogelbuurt walk (about 3 km): Willem I-sluis, Sixhaven, IJplein, Villa De Volewijck, the Spreeuwenpark blocks, the three hidden schools, Zwanenplein and the Bethlehemkerk, the Fazantenweg and Kievitstraat blocks, Vogeldorp, Museum Amsterdam Noord, the Vijfde Technische School.
- Tuindorp Oostzaan walk (about 2 km): Castorplein, 't Zonnehuis and the Zonneplein, Mercuriusplein, the bathhouse and Museumwoning on the Meteorenweg, the old people's houses, the Orionplantsoen canteen, the Kometensingel row (doctor's house, kindergarten, fire engine house, reading room), Bethelkerk, Keienmonument, Sacramentskerk; then on to the Zuideinde at Oostzanerwerf.
- Cycling link: Buiksloterkerk and d'Admiraal on the canal, then the Noorderparkbad, the Buikslotermeer churches and the Bosplaat patio houses.
