## Status checks

- Sint-Augustinuskerk: closed for worship since 1 September 2014 and empty. It was squatted from February to April 2019. A plan for 15 apartments got its subsidy in 2024, but in November 2025 it was still stuck on neighbours' objections and a dispute between the owners, and the building is decaying (Het Parool, 5 November 2025, cited on nl.wikipedia). SpitsVastgoed advertises rooms in it for events. Said plainly in the text.
- Meerpadkerk: still in use; Doopsgezind Amsterdam lists services into 2027.
- Café 't Sluisje: open in 2026 (own site and listings). Its 120th anniversary was in 2024.
- Café Noord-Holland 1907: no longer a café. It is offices plus a meeting room for hire, so I used `hidden-history`, not `food-drink`.
- Nothing in the slice is gone.

## Ketjen silos (scouted 24): needs a decision

I did not make this its own place. The two painted silos (by Deeffeed, Sket185 and Rasa for Graffitifun, mid-2025, a fox, a goose and greenery) stand inside the fenced Ketjen chemical plant. The only photo I found shows them among a battery of raised silos deep in the works, and I could not confirm that they can be seen from any public spot. Every source is promotional (Graffitifun, its sister site, a reworded press piece in Amsterdams Dagblad), and the "largest silo street art in Amsterdam" claim comes only from Graffitifun. BAG footprints (PDOK) show several round structures on the site around 52.3867, 4.9333, but I could not tell which ones are the painted pair, and raised silos may not appear in BAG at all. I put the silos into the Vliegenbos place as one factual sentence. If the user wants them as a separate street-art place, someone needs to check on site where they can be seen from (the south edge of the Vliegenbos or the Nieuwendammerkade) and pin that spot.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Café 't Sluisje's date: the café's cooperative says the house dates from 1565. The national register says "XVIII of ouder" (18th century or older). Both are given in the text, and the register is the one that goes in the attribute.
- Age of the Nieuwendammersluis: the cooperative says it has worked "since 1516" and calls it the smallest and oldest lock in the city. nl.wikipedia (Nieuwendam) dates a lock east of the overtoom to 1628. I left both claims out.
- Nieuwendam town hall: nl.wikipedia says "gevestigd aan de Nieuwendammerdijk 323, sinds 1918 321". It is unclear whether that is a renumbering or a move, so I left the town hall out of the east ensemble. No. 323 is described only as a merchant's house with grain storage in the attic, which is in the same article.
- Sint-Augustinuskerk: the city register gives the start year as 1884. The national register and every other source give 1888–1889, and I used that. The register says the Van de Velde paintings are 18th century, Reliwiki says 17th century; Adriaen van de Velde lived 1636–1672, so the text says 17th century.
- Vliegenbos dates: the infobox says 1912–1918 and the article says planting was finished in 1917. I used 1912–1918.
- The 1984 killing in the Vliegenbos: confirmed by Ons Amsterdam ("Protest tegen 'Potenrammen'": Elbert van de Biezen, 54, 25 August 1984, eight teenage boys) as well as by nl.wikipedia, which cites Hekma. Written in a factual tone.
- Doctor's house: amsterdamopdekaart says the client was "probably" Dr Honig, and the text keeps that hedge ("most likely").
- The `listed` years for the 1970 rijksmonuments come from the register's inscription date (3 November 1970, checked on 6597, 6695 and 6709). I set `listed` only on the single-monument places.

## Walking route ideas

1. "Two dike villages", about 2.5 km from west to east along the old Waterland sea dike: Café Noord-Holland 1907 → Buiksloterdijk, the old dike village of Buiksloot → (cross the Nieuwe Leeuwarderweg) → Nieuwendammerdijk west, with the Meerpad and Molenpad → Meerpadkerk → Sint-Augustinuskerk, Nieuwendam → Café 't Sluisje → Nieuwendammerdijk east, from the Sluisje → Doctor's house, Nieuwendammerdijk 335.
2. "Nieuwendam loop", short: W.H. Vliegenbos (in over the Zosenerdambrug) → Nieuwendammerdijk west → Meerpadkerk → Sint-Augustinuskerk → Café 't Sluisje → Doctor's house, and back through the Beeldenbos.
