## Scouted items

- 11 B-80: written as `(gone)`, status Gone. The scrapping-year conflict is settled: towed away on 16 December 2019 to Vlaardingen, dismantled during 2020 (Struijk, NH Nieuws). The English uses Wikipedia's build place (Molotovsk); Amports says "uit Letland" and a Flickr caption says Riga, so the Dutch text leaves the yard out.
- 13 Schoonschip, 14 Kraanspoor, 21 Kobra, 22 Giants, 23 Iets/Niets, 26 container arch, 32 De Ceuvel, 34 Pllek, 35 Goudfazant, 38 Noorderlicht, 40 Skatecafé: each its own place.
- Kraanspoor: I left out the scout's "Hooykaas cycled past in 1997" (ULI) because I could not confirm it in a second source. nl.wikipedia only says she noticed it early in the clean-up.

## Status checks (November 2026)

- Skatecafé: amsterdamlokaal.nl (May 2026) claimed it was closing. That site looks unreliable (it also says Oedipus is ending its taproom there, though Oedipus moved in 2024). Het Parool (28 May 2026) says Hamerkwartier tenants are leaving, but Skatecafé has agreements until March 2028. Its ticket shop lists events into March 2027, including a 10-year party on 19 December 2026. The text says open, with agreements to March 2028.
- Noorderlicht: filed for bankruptcy 16 December 2025. Parool (2 March 2026) reported a restart "onder een nieuwe naam" in spring 2026, but the live website (noorderlichtndsm.nl, NDSM-plein 102) still calls it Noorderlicht and lists events for November 2026. The text says it still trades as Noorderlicht. I could not confirm whether the planned new building has been finished, so the text does not say. Founding year: 2004 (own site, ndsm.nl); headliner.nl says 2006.
- Pllek: the permit ran to 2025. I found no later ruling, but it is plainly operating in 2026 (Midsummer 2026, ADE 2026). The text says only that.
- De Ceuvel: must leave in early 2028 (AT5 / NH Nieuws, 5 June 2026). It may bid in the tender.
- Goudfazant: also in the Hamerkwartier redevelopment area. I found nothing on its lease, so the text says nothing.
- Kobra mural: defaced in 2017 (AT5; an NH 2018 piece dates it to 2017; the scout's "within two days" refers to a different Anne Frank mural on the Wibautstraat in 2018). The mural is visible in a 2022 Commons photo and is in the STRAAT collection, so it is written as repaired.
- Timmerwerkplaats: Paramount Benelux left in early 2026 (nl.wikipedia). I did not find the new user.

## Sources and Dutch text

- Dutch for the monuments comes from the RCE register texts, the city register text (Afbramerij) and nl.wikipedia (NDSM-werf, Crane Hotel Faralda, Kraanspoor, De Ceuvel).
- The other Dutch texts are built from facts and short quotes in Dutch news sources and project sites (AT5, NH Nieuws, Struijk, Amsterdam op de kaart, Hooyschuur, Van Milt, ndsm.nl, ndsmloods.nl, 3X3 Unites, gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl, hethamerkwartier.nl, 3voor12, VICE/De Buik snippets). No full copying of non-free text.
- Several facts come only from search-result snippets of paywalled or blocked pages (Parool, VICE, De Buik, AD). These are marked "search snippet" in `sources`.
- grachtenbeans.nl (Skatecafé history) reads like a generated directory page. I used it only for facts that 3voor12, VICE, De Buik or Nine Yards confirm (founders, mothers, ramp). The December 2016 opening matches the 10-year party in December 2026.
- Papaverschool: the city register gives 1923–1924 and client "Christelijke School Vereeniging Aeneas Mackay"; Amsterdam op de kaart gives 1924–1925 and two schools. The built field uses 1923–1925.
