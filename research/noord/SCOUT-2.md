# Noord scout, round A (items 101–147)

Scouted 2026-11-23. Sweeps 2 (public art and walls) and 3 (war and memory) of the West standard, done for every gebied of Noord: Oud-Noord, Noord-West and Noord-Oost (with the Waterland villages). Nothing is written yet: the user picks.

I have 47 candidates, numbered 101 to 147. None of them is in SCOUT.md (round 1) or among the 127 places in `research/noord/places/*.yaml`; I checked each against the YAML and against a dump of the live map (872 places, 127 in Noord, the same 127 titles as the YAML). Every location was point-checked with `research/noord/scripts/areas.py` and is in borough Noord. Nothing in Zuid or Centrum. 16 items are proposed as **merges** into an existing place (a paragraph or a "Look for" line), 31 as possible new places.

Each item: name; location (gebied, buurt); hook; why it fits; lead source; caveats; suggested rank; possible merge.

**Sources gone through this round:**
- **City public-art database (Kunstwacht / buitenkunstamsterdam.nl):** the full list through the site's own API (1,749 works citywide), point-checked to the borough: **88 works in Noord** (30 in Noord-Oost, 23 in Noord-West, 33 in Oud-Noord, 2 without coordinates). All 88 read; the ones not below are under "Checked and dropped". Links are written as `buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/<id>` with the API id; the site is a JavaScript app, so if a link opens the wrong work, search the site by title.
- **City wall-art register (maps.amsterdam.nl, "Wandkunst"):** 226 works citywide, **16 in Noord**, all read.
- **Street Art Cities:** the full marker feed for the Amsterdam zone, point-checked: **130 markers in Noord** (86 active, 43 removed, 1 in progress), 115 of them on the NDSM wharf, mostly STRAAT's collection. Selective, as asked.
- **OpenStreetMap:** `tourism=artwork`, `historic=*`, `memorial=*`, `military=*`, `building=bunker` in a Noord bounding box, filtered to the borough: **140 features**.
- **nl.wikipedia:** "Lijst van beelden in Amsterdam-Noord" (about 165 rows, every row read) and articles on single works and memorials.
- **4en5mei.nl monument database:** every record within reach of Noord (three distance-sorted searches from Oud-Noord, Waterland and Noord-West, 137 records, detail pages downloaded and point-checked; the searches reached Monnickendam, Broek in Waterland and Landsmeer, so nothing in Noord can be missing). **16 records lie in Noord**, all detail pages read.
- **Friendly Fire Amsterdam** (project site), Kunstwacht route texts "Kunstroute Molenwijk" and "Kunstroute Oosten van Noord" (inside the Kunstwacht records).

## Sweep 2: Public art and walls

### Oud-Noord

101. **Hildo Krop's Saint George with a snake, and the post-war gable stones of Tuindorp Nieuwendam**; former school at Schermerstraat 3-19 (gate pier at no. 15), plus Schermerstraat 47, Andijkstraat 1-2, Hoornsestraat 58-59 and Nibbixwoudstraat 9-10 (Oud-Noord, Tuindorp Nieuwendam); on the gate pier of a socialist Amsterdam School school (1925) Krop replaced Saint George's dragon with a snake, so the saint slays capitalism, not the infidel; next to it a fluting faun with frogs and a small snake's head on the facade; a few streets on, the 1949 rebuilding blocks carry gable stones by Wim van Hoorn (one, a reclining woman with a sash reading VREDE, is also a 4 May memorial) and Ben Guntenaar; across the Purmerweg stand Fioen Blaisse's "Danseres met tamboerijn" (2000) and Johan Polet's "Scheepstimmerman" (1960) on the Enkhuizerplein; a political joke in stone and a small open-air gallery that is not on the map; https://www.buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/378 (also 403, 353, 382; wall-art register entries for Guntenaar and Van Hoorn; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1477/amsterdam-monument-aan-de-schermerstraat ); the school building itself is not listed; Polet's later Kultuurkamer membership is mentioned by Kunstwacht, write it factually if used; Nice; pin on the gate pier (52.392000, 4.943311); possible merge as a "Look for" in "Kitchen houses north of the Purmerweg", but it reads better on its own.

102. **Lex Horn's tile mosaic, former GGD post, Purmerweg 46**; Purmerweg 46 / Brede Kerkepad 5 (Oud-Noord, Tuindorp Nieuwendam); a 1968 facade mosaic of rectangular tiles by Lex Horn, one of the leading monumental artists of post-war Amsterdam, rated "Top" (the register's highest grade) by the city; one of his few works left in place (his sgraffito in the Marcanti is gone); visible from the street; wall-art register (maps.amsterdam.nl/wandkunst, Purmerweg 46) and nl.wikipedia list ("Compositie", 1968); the register says "and possibly the stairwell": status of the building and use unverified; Nice; possible merge with "Wooden houses, Brede Kerkepad" as a "Look for", since it stands at the end of that path.

103. **De 7 Poorten (Broersen and Lukács), Noorderpark metro station**; on the approach to the platform of station Noorderpark (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); seven Amsterdam School brick gates that the train runs through, "overgrown" by computer-game polygons, set in sedum: growth, bloom and decay in a few seconds of the ride; https://www.buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/1166 ; seen from the train and the platform; the Lancaster memorial (136) is at the same station; Nice; possible merge with 136 as one "Noorderpark station" place.

104. **Noorderklankpark (Hans van Dorp, 2010)**; Noorderpark by the Eva Schalkbrug (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); corten-steel instruments you can play with bats: two "tube drums" laid out like the black and white keys of a piano and a xylophone, which the artist admits is slightly out of tune in the high notes; playable art in a park; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noorderklankpark ; now in the shadow of the bridge ramp; condition unverified; Okay.

105. **"Genie Mythology" (Jan Stel aka Simbl, 1990)**; wall at IJplein 3 (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); the artist calls it one of his first big walls, sprayed in 1990 with the old Sparvar cans; if it is still there, it is one of the oldest surviving graffiti walls in Amsterdam, older than most of NDSM's street art; https://streetartcities.com/cities/amsterdam/markers/6fc6c870 (marker listed as active); **status unverified**: needs an on-site check of its state and whether it has been painted over or restored; Nice if confirmed.

106. **Parakeets of 1923, Zwanenplein**; corner of the Zwanenplein and the Koekoekstraat (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); a sandstone relief by Driekus Jansen van Galen of two parakeets on a nest of chicks, on a Patrimonium block in the Vogelbuurt, a century before Noord put up a 7 m ceramic parakeet as an ode to the "strange birds"; plus an older gable stone on the same square (1914–1918); a small, funny pairing; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lijst_van_beelden_in_Amsterdam-Noord ; Okay; merge into "Zwanenplein (Patrimonium housing)" (a "Look for" line, and a cross-reference from "Statue for the Rose-ringed Parakeet").

### Noord-West

117. **Drijfbeeld (Atelier Van Lieshout, 1999)**; in the water along Lange Vonder, Twiske-West (Noord-West, Oostzanerwerf); a little green-plank hut with a polyester tadpole tail that turns slowly on a chain: Van Lieshout's monument to the self-built houseboats that were banned when the new estate went up; in hard frost it can serve as a koek-en-zopie (ice stall), with a sink and a toilet; on a Dutch stamp in 2006; https://www.buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/408 ; status unverified; Nice.

118. **Molenwijk, the "little Bijlmer", and its art route**; Molenwijk, between Spinnenkop, Tjasker, Rosmolen and Molenaarsweg (Noord-West, Oostzanerwerf); a 1960s high-rise estate laid out like turning mill sails, a test run for the Bijlmermeer, with the borough's best art story cluster: Henk Veen's 68 painted "art traffic signs" ("Beestenboel" where rubbish piles up, an Ajax dog that keeps getting stolen) that grew into a neighbourhood SMS alert group; Herman Damen's "Windbuilroute" of deflating balloons (1974; three of six left, one an empty pond, one burned); Marijke Ouendag's "Familie Woezel" (1972), a bored housewife's protest against monotonous playgrounds; one of about 90 surviving Aldo van Eyck climbing frames; Karski and Beyond's 50-years mural in the Rosmolen garage (2019); Marin Kasimir's "Silhouetten" (1995), which he disowned after it was painted orange; https://www.buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/2712 (also 369, 371, 2713, 343, 370, 368) ; residents' estate; the Van Eyck frame and the Woezels need pinning; Nice; one place pinned on the Woezels or the Veen sign at the shopping centre.

121. **"Verzonken Stad" (Bořek Šípek, 1991)**; Meteorenweg 266, in front of the borough works building (Noord-West, Oostzanerwerf); three playful towers of an invisible sunken city, first set on the roof, now on the ground; by the Czech designer whose baroque glass went to Clinton and Jagger and who led the renewal of Prague Castle under Havel; https://www.buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/372 ; at a works depot; Okay.

### Noord-Oost (with the Waterland villages)

127. **Poort van Nieuwendam (Marcel Smink, 1999)**; Zuiderzeeweg at the A10 exit (Noord-Oost, Waterland side of Nieuwendam); a 6 m twisted concrete gate made from 100 m of the abandoned 1930s Zuiderzeeweg, whose paving curls up into it so cars can't go on: Noord's only "city gate"; saved when a roundabout was planned and re-placed even closer to the road; BMX riders use it as a ramp for flips; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poort_van_Nieuwendam (Kunstwacht 352) ; Nice.

128. **"Flyways" (Harmen Liemburg, 2018)**; platform floor of metro station Noord (Noord-Oost, Elzenhagen); a 130 m bird encyclopedia cut into the floor tiles: geese from Scandinavia at the north end, resident birds in the middle, Africa-bound migrants at the south end, a godwit chick in a ditch and a goose's head seen with a raptor's eyes; https://www.buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/1165 ; Nice.

## Sweep 3: War and memory

### Oud-Noord

133. **Hollandia-Kattenburg monument**; IJplein, at no. 227, where the factory gate was (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); on 11 November 1942 the 367 Jewish workers of the Hollandia-Kattenburg raincoat factory were taken from the works with their families, 826 people; the three bronze plaques were put up in the factory hall after the war (one given by the staff of the sister firm Kattenburgs Ltd in Manchester), saved when the factory was demolished in the 1970s, and set on a black column on the site in 1985; name plates with 359 dead added in 1995; a whole workforce deported in one day, on a spot now surrounded by OMA's housing; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1479/amsterdam-hollandia-kattenburgmonument (Kunstwacht 333) ; Kunstwacht says 367 deported and 8 returned, 4en5mei counts 359 names: give both carefully; Nice; possible merge into "IJplein" but it deserves its own place.

134. **Spanjemonument (Eddy Roos, 1986)**; Plein Spanje '36-'39 (Oud-Noord, Volewijck); two bronze women, Dutch and Spanish, turn on a steel spiral for the Dutch volunteers who fought Franco in the Spanish Civil War; the paving is in the colours of the Spanish flag and the plaque reads "No pasarán"; one of very few memorials for them in the country; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/401/amsterdam-spanjemonument (Kunstwacht 335) ; the volunteers' loss of Dutch citizenship is widely told but needs a source before it goes in; Nice.

135. **Friendly Fire (Hester Oerlemans, 2018)**; 11 manhole covers on squares of the Van der Pekbuurt and around: Hagedoornplein, Kraaienplein, Hagedoornweg, Meidoornplein, Van der Pekplein, Heimansweg, Ranonkelkade, Lupineplein, Distelplein, Mosveld and Mosplein (Oud-Noord, Volewijck and IJplein/Vogelbuurt); cast covers (a B-17, a Mitchell, a war pigeon, a pilot, falling bombs, "BANG!", a 1943 map) mark where the Allied bombs of July 1943 fell on the houses instead of the Fokker works; made because the hardest-hit streets had no memorial at all; the rebuilt houses are the ones without the yellow brick bands of the originals; a 45-minute walk; https://friendly-fire-amsterdam.nl/locaties/ ; the existing "Fokker bombing memorial, 1943" mentions one cover at the Sint Rita church; Amazing (as a walk); pin on the Mosveld cover ("FRIENDLY FIRE") or merge as a long "Look for" list into "Fokker bombing memorial, 1943" (but that place is pinned at the cemetery, 2 km away).

136. **Lancaster R5512, Noorderpark station**; plaque at metro station Noorderpark (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); on 20 December 1942 a British Lancaster on its way to Duisburg was shot down into the Volewijkspark; three crew were buried the next day, but four lay in the ground under the park, missing to their families, for 20 years, until the IJ-tunnel works found them in 1962; plaque with a drawing after Marvano, unveiled with relatives on 20 December 2022; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/4505/amsterdam-gedenkteken-lancaster ; exact spot of the plaque in the station to check; Nice; possible merge with 103.

137. **Bezinningsmonument Nieuwendam (Marius van Beek, 1983)**; Purmerweg by the Volendammerweg (Oud-Noord, Tuindorp Nieuwendam); a seated, brooding woman in front of four granite slabs, one carrying, in his handwriting, resistance man Krijn Breur's farewell letter to his family before his execution in 1943 ("Tell your children about us and our struggle... Live. Live, all of you, and grow up."); the sculptor was himself in the armed resistance; adopted by the IJdoornschool, 4 May gatherings; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1482/amsterdam-bezinningsmonument-nieuwendam (Kunstwacht 332) ; Nice.

138. **De Phoenix (Henk Dannenburg, 1951)**; plantsoen at the Kamperfoelieweg and Azaleastraat (Oud-Noord, Volewijck); Noord's monument to its civilians killed in the war, unveiled by mayor d'Ailly; it first stood on a 6 m limestone needle with a verse by Da Costa, lost when the square made way for the IJ-tunnel roads; stored, put on the Mosveld, moved here in 2016; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1480/amsterdam-de-phoenix (Kunstwacht 357) ; the sources disagree on when it moved (1968 to the Mosplein in Kunstwacht and Wikipedia, 2016 from the Mosveld in 4en5mei); Okay; merge as a "Look for" in "Fokker bombing memorial, 1943".

139. **Gerben Wagenaar bridge plaque**; Gerben Wagenaarbrug at the Kraaienplein (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); for the Noord-raised meter reader who helped organise the February Strike of 1941, led the communist resistance as "blonde Freek" and the Raad van Verzet, then led the CPN in parliament until the party expelled him in 1958; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3096/amsterdam-plaquette-op-de-gerben-wagenaarbrug ; small; Okay; a Friendly Fire cover is on the same square (135).

140. **"Bombed 17 July 1943, rebuilt 1946", Fazantenweg 14-16**; (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); a 1946 gable stone on a rebuilt house; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3095/amsterdam-gevelsteen-aan-de-fazantenweg ; Okay; merge into "Fokker bombing memorial, 1943" (next to the Kievitstraat stone already listed there).

### Noord-West

142. **Gedenkteken Atatürk and the Turkish workers' hostel**; the short street Atatürk between Klaprozenweg and Strekkerweg (Noord-West, Tuindorp Oostzaan); from 1965 the hostel "Atatürk" housed Turkish "guest workers" for the building firms and the NDSM yard, eight men to a unit, four to a room; after protests over Dutch food, hotplates; the canteen was a mosque on Fridays; they founded the club Ataspor and learned to cycle, and for a decade it was the heart of Turkish Amsterdam; when it closed in 1978 a concrete plate with Atatürk's "Peace at home, peace in the world" in Turkish and Dutch was unveiled; the street is named after the camp; https://www.buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/338 ; Nice.

143. **Monument to the 1960 flood (Robbert Ritmeester, 1990)**; Zonneplein (Noord-West, Tuindorp Oostzaan); in 1960 a quay broke and Tuindorp Oostzaan ran full, forcing 15,000 residents out; the monument is a brick "quay" with a bronze plaque of the Zonneplein's roofs as if still under water, a telescope-like cylinder and frozen bronze drops; OSM calls it "Zonnewijzer"; https://www.buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/342 ; the 1960 flood itself needs a second source (date, cause); Nice; merge into "'t Zonnehuis and the Zonneplein" or its own place (the flood is not on the map at all).

144. **Tuindorp Oostzaan war memorial (Jan Havermans, 1950)**; Meteorensingel (Noord-West, Tuindorp Oostzaan); a bronze bird with spread wings on a concrete column with a falling man in relief, paid for by a collection among residents, unveiled 4 May 1950; by the maker of the resistance memorial on the Apollolaan; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1481/amsterdam-oorlogsgedenkteken-tuindorp-oostzaan ; Okay; merge into "'t Zonnehuis and the Zonneplein" or a nearby Tuindorp place.

145. **Zuideinde 443: shelter for 30 Jewish refugees**; Zuideinde 443 (Noord-West, Oostzanerwerf); a 2005 plaque: between 1933 and 1939 about 75 Jews fleeing Germany, Poland and Eastern Europe found shelter in this stretch and in Oostzaan, 30 of them in this house; 46 survived; Fajga Rozenszajn hid here with her son Leo, who was murdered in Auschwitz in 1944; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/2673/amsterdam-plaquette-aan-woonhuis-zuideinde ; private house, plaque seen from the dike road; Nice; merge into "Zuideinde, Oostzanerwerf".

### Noord-Oost (with the Waterland villages)

146. **Draka memorial**; De Nieuwe Noorder, Buikslotermeerdijk 83 (Noord-Oost, Elzenhagen); a brick wall of 1945 with the names of 17 staff of the Hollandsche Draad- en Kabelfabriek (Draka), Jewish workers deported and resistance men shot, with copper-wire suns, a plane and a ship; it stood at the foot of the factory's C building on the Hamerstraat until the works closed in 2016 and moved to the cemetery; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/4189/amsterdam-draka-monument ; Okay; merge into "Fokker bombing memorial, 1943" (same cemetery; that place could become "Memorials at De Nieuwe Noorder").

147. **Zunderdorp's two villagers**; plaque by the main door of the church, Kerkplein, Zunderdorp (Noord-Oost, Waterland); for D.C. de Graaf of the Pinto resistance group, who sheltered Engelandvaarders (people trying to reach England) and died in Neuengamme in 1942, and C. Hoeve, who died in Mauthausen in 1945; placed 2003; https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3100/zunderdorp-plaquette-op-de-gevel-van-de-protestantse-kerk ; Okay; merge into "Zunderdorp village church".
