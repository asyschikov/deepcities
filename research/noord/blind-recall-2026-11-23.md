# Amsterdam-Noord: blind recall, 2026-11-23

Independent list, built without looking at deepcities.org, the `pnpm dc` CLI, scripts/data or earlier research files.
Sources: iAmsterdam neighbourhood pages ("verborgen parels", Landelijk Noord, Nieuwendammerham, groen, then and now), Rodi "Wandelen in/door Noord" walk series (John Jansen van Galen), Rodi columns, NH Nieuws, ANWB "Amsterdam 750 jaar Noord" walk, Your Little Black Book walk, amsterdamforvisitors, Spotted by Locals, Dutch Review, Laag Holland, Komoot route notes, Atlas Obscura search, TU Delft circular design atlas, Stadsherstel.
Reddit returned nothing usable through search; Atlas Obscura has only STRAAT and the A'DAM swing for Noord; Parool and AT5 pages were not reachable by search, so they are not cited.

Ranking: by how many independent sources named the place and how strongly. Items marked (gone) or (contested) are flagged inline. A-list excluded: A'DAM Toren/Lookout, Eye, NDSM ferry, This is Holland.

## Notes

- Gebied boundaries are my best reading: Buiksloterkerk and the Nieuwendammerdijk/Buiksloterdijk ribbon placed in Noord-Oost; Floradorp, Noorderpark and Vliegenbos in Oud-Noord; Krijtmolen d'Admiraal in Noord-West (Banne). Check against the official wijk map.
- Thin coverage: Twiske-West and Oostzanerwerf (sources lump them with Molenwijk/Tuindorp Oostzaan), Waterlandpleinbuurt (no recommended sight found beyond the square's towers).
- Sources lean heavily on Rodi's walk series and iAmsterdam; English travel blogs repeat NDSM, Tolhuistuin and De Ceuvel and say almost nothing about Noord-West or the villages beyond Durgerdam and Ransdorp.
