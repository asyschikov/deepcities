## Sweep 4: Hidden history

### Oud-Noord

201. **Johan van Hasseltkanaal, the canal that never connected**; dead end of the western arm by the Ridderspoorweg bridge, about 52.393928, 4.906835 (Oud-Noord, Noordelijke IJ-oevers-West); dug from 1908 to cut across Noord so that the IJ could one day get a fixed bridge to the city, but the state never paid for the link, so both halves stayed dead-end harbours; the strip kept free for it became De Volewijckers' ground on the Mosveld, then the raised Johan van Hasseltweg of 1968 ("De Bult") and the 1966 hospital site (now a hotel); the 2009 Ridderspoorweg bridge has a middle section that lifts out to let houseboats through; a story that explains the odd shape of half of Noord; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johan_van_Hasseltkanaal ; pin is a canal centroid, set it on the dead end; the lift-out bridge detail is from Wikipedia only; **Nice**; no merge (Schoonschip lies in the canal but does not tell this).

202. **Air-quality station "Anemoi", Nieuwendammerdijk**; grass field by the old harbour, about Nieuwendammerdijk 421, 52.389472, 4.944100 (Oud-Noord, Nieuwendammerdijk/Buiksloterdijk); a GGD measuring station (running since 1998) rehoused in an octagon after the Tower of the Winds in Athens, meant to glow from purple (very bad air) to blue (excellent); a real oddity on a dike of wooden houses; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luchtmeetstation_Nieuwendammerdijk ; only Wikipedia describes the design (Rademacher de Vries, a Cultuurfonds competition) and I could not confirm it was built; the station itself exists (GGD, NL49003); **Okay**, `check: Still there?`; no merge.

203. **Kromwijk ship's bow, NDSM**; Ms. van Riemsdijkweg 75, 52.398364, 4.891376 (Oud-Noord, Noordelijke IJ-oevers-West); the front section of the motor freighter "Kromwijk" set on land, listed by Kunstwacht as "Kromwijk Monument" (1992) with no text, and a canvas for graffiti; https://streetartcities.com/markers/d472618a-1b60-4873-bc97-dddda925f867 ; one weak source (logged March 2024), the ship's history unknown; **Okay** only if a site check finds it; possible merge: a Look for line in "NDSM slipways and Kraan 13 (Faralda Crane Hotel)".

204. **The "kindertjesboom" on the Volewijck**; Amsterdam children were told babies were picked from a tree on the Volewijck, next to the gallows; earliest written trace in Hooft's Warenar (1617); https://hcan.nl/geschiedenis/1617-de-kindertjesboom-op-de-volewijck/ ; **Okay**; merge into "Volewijck gallows field (gone)" (one sentence).

205. **Clara the rhinoceros at Nieuwendam, and two dike houses**; Clara, the rhino the VOC captain Douwe Mout toured around Europe, was first shown to the Dutch public at Nieuwendam in August 1741; Nieuwendammerdijk 283–285 is a wooden notary's house of about 1750, owned by the Vereniging Hendrick de Keyser since 1959 (52.391307, 4.937486); De Halve Maan, Nieuwendammerdijk 202–204 (about 1910), was built for the yacht builder De Vries Lentsch; https://hcan.nl/verhalen/een-neushoorn-in-de-wilmkebreek-het-moet-niet-gekker-worden/ and https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nieuwendammerdijk_285 ; where in Nieuwendam Clara stood is unknown; **Okay**; merge into "Nieuwendammerdijk east, from the Sluisje" (Clara, 283–285) and "Nieuwendammerdijk west, with the Meerpad and Molenpad" (De Halve Maan; check which stretch nos. 202–204 are on). See also 216.

206. **Noordergasfabriek and the Ponthaven**; the Aambeeldstraat stands on Noord's own gasworks (1913–1924, dismantled); the Ponthaven dug next door in 1930 is still the GVB ferry harbour, where retired ferries leave for heritage trips; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noordergasfabriek ; **Okay**; merge into "Hotel de Goudfazant" (one or two sentences).

208. **Tramstation Amsterdam-Noord, the "Gare du Nord"**; the Waterland tram's terminus on the Valkenweg; the first, wooden station by Sanders and Berlage burned in 1895; demolished in 1956 for the IJ-tunnel, its site is now the IJplein ferry pier; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tramstation_Amsterdam-Noord ; nothing left; **Okay**; merge into "Willem I-sluis" or "Sixhaven and the ghost metro station" (one sentence); pairs with 220.

### Noord-West

214. **Paard van Noord**; Bongerdkade by Zijkanaal I, 52.407954, 4.906766 (Noord-West, Tuindorp Oostzaan, De Bongerd); a 42 m riveted grab crane (1956–1959, P.M. 215. **Gemaal Kadoelen and the Concertgemaal**; Landsmeerderdijk 213, 52.409028, 4.908918 (Noord-West, Kadoelen); a pumping station of about 1875 that drained all of Waterland into Zijkanaal I with a paddle wheel, first by steam, later electric; Stadsherstel restored it in 2014 as a small classical concert hall; next door the 1987 replacement has a roof shaped like the screw pumps inside (concrete prize 1987); https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemaal_Kadoelen ; 1875 and the paddle wheel are from Wikipedia only; concerts confirmed by iamsterdam; **Nice**; no merge.

216. **Wilmkebreek and Clara the rhino**; Wilmkebreekpad / Kadoelenweg, 52.416869, 4.905674 (Noord-West, Kadoelen); a scour lake from an early-16th-century dike breach, drained in 1633, still lower than everything around and unbuilt; it flooded again in 1916 and is part of the provincial monument Waterlandse Zeedijk; HCAN says Douwe Mout bought this polder as grazing for Clara after she had first grazed in the Buikslotermeer; https://hcan.nl/verhalen/een-neushoorn-in-de-wilmkebreek-het-moet-niet-gekker-worden/ ; the purchase is HCAN's claim only (the Rhino Resource Center confirms Clara at Nieuwendam in 1741 and Durgerdam in 1745, not the polder); `check: Facts`; **Nice**; possible merge into "Kadoelen stolp farms" (its text already names the deep Wilmkebreek polder).

## Sweep 5: Behind the gate

### Noord-Oost (with the Waterland villages)

223. **Adcock station Durgerdam, a German direction-finding hut**; meadow east of the Durgerdammergouw, about 100 m from the cycle path to Holysloot, 52.390151, 5.003470 (Noord-Oost, Waterland); a brick Luftwaffe hut that stood in the middle of a square of four aerial masts, probably linked to the seaplane base Fliegerhorst Schellingwoude and out of use by September 1944; on 20 August 1944 the resistance's Albrecht group reported "five radio masts, without antenna wires", a sentry and a guess, "Beacon Schiphol Airport?"; passers-by take it for a farm shed; this is the OSM bunker (way 57863724, `historic:period=1942 - 1956`, surveyed 2017); https://www.tracesofwar.com/sights/157734/Adcock-station-Durgerdam.htm ; on private meadow behind fences, seen only from the Durgerdammergouw or the Holysloot cycle path; no Dutch source beyond TracesOfWar; the "1956" end date is unexplained; `check: Still there?`; **Nice**; no merge.

## Sweep 7: Curiosities, green corners and odd museums

### Oud-Noord

250. **Radio Veronica's ship Norderney**; NDSM-pier 1, 52.401378, 4.890219 (Oud-Noord, Noordelijke IJ-oevers-West); built in 1949 as a German fishing ship, from 1964 to 31 August 1974 the home of the pirate station Radio Veronica off Scheveningen (a storm drove it onto the beach in April 1973); after the ban on sea radio it drifted through life as a disco ship and has lain at the NDSM since 17 November 2013, a club and restaurant since 2018; a 50-years-on reunion broadcast was made on board; a national pop-culture relic most visitors walk past; https://offshoreradio.info/?p=3602 and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Norderney ; seen from the quay any time, inside during events; listed events into autumn 2026; round A lead; **Amazing**; no merge.

251. **Iepenarboretum (elm arboretum), Oeverpark**; Oeverpark, Overhoeks, about 52.386400, 4.899400 (Oud-Noord, Noordelijke IJ-oevers-West); Amsterdam's (probably the country's) first elm arboretum, planted in 2010: about 30 elm types on a sloping lawn, including forgotten cultivars and transplanted monumental elms, the city's answer to Dutch elm disease in "the elm capital" (75,000 elms), right on the skyline seen from Centraal; https://boomzorg.nl/upload/artikelen/bz210iepenaboretum.pdf and https://boomzorg.nl/upload/artikelen/bz211iepenarboretum.pdf ; no current species list, name tags may be gone; **Nice**; no merge.

252. **Tuinpark Buitenzorg**; Zamenhofstraat 41, 52.387718, 4.929422 (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); Amsterdam's oldest allotment park still in its original place: in 1914, in the First World War food shortage, neighbours led by Jan Hoeksel began growing vegetables on a strip left over when the Vliegenbos was planted, and the club was founded on 19 July 1917; poet Simon Vinkenoog took a garden here in 1991 and drew artists in; the Ketjen silos loom over it; https://www.tuinparkbuitenzorg.nl/geschiedenis ; open to all 1 April to 1 October, daily about 9:00 to 19:00; **Nice**; no merge (cross-reference "W.H. Vliegenbos").

253. **Beeldenbos Noord**; Nieuwendammerkade 20, 52.389960, 4.938581 (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); a free sculpture wood of about 3 ha on the Vliegenbos edge, begun with Jan Sierhuis's "Dansende figuur" (2019); a new gateway to the Groot Nederlands Stadsbos was due in March 2026; https://www.beeldenbosnoord.nl/ ; the "W.H. Vliegenbos" place already calls it a sculpture wood (round A dropped its works as part of that place); **Okay**; merge into "W.H. Vliegenbos" (update only, e.g. the 2026 gateway).

254. **De Roze Tanker and De Gele Pomp**; Nieuwe Leeuwarderweg 15 and 16, 52.394146, 4.922978 and 52.393550, 4.924030 (Oud-Noord, IJplein/Vogelbuurt); two abandoned petrol stations at the Noorderpark entrance, rebuilt by Sophie Valla into culture pavilions under the old forecourt roofs, beside the sunken motorway; exhibitions, yoga, a flamingo mural, and Sunday services of a Ghanaian church at the Tanker; named in independent "things to do" lists; https://architizer.com/projects/de-roze-tanker and https://popupcity.net/insights/derelict-gas-station-transforms-into-a-dreamy-public-space/ ; build and conversion dates not found; **Okay**; no merge (near "Noorderklankpark").

255. **Two old trams by Kraan 13**; 52.398999, 4.894618 and 52.399154, 4.894671 (Oud-Noord, Noordelijke IJ-oevers-West); two retired trams parked on the wharf; one reportedly burned in an arson attack and was replaced by a 1918 tram from a tram-collectors' charity; https://www.amsterdamforvisitors.com/?p=3008 (weak) and OSM `historic=locomotive`; why they are there is unknown; `check: Still there?`; **Okay**; merge into "NDSM slipways and Kraan 13 (Faralda Crane Hotel)" (Look for line).

### Noord-West

256. **NoordOogst, with the No Chateau vineyard and Mot in Mokum**; Meteorenweg 272, 52.419316, 4.874183 (Noord-West, Oostzanerwerf); a pesticide-free city farm on the former Melkweg sports park, with a 750-vine city vineyard that Amsterdammers make wine from together, Noord's biggest nature playground and the butterfly garden Mot in Mokum, where you can sleep among the butterflies in summer; in 2026 half its land was set to go to sports fields, against a petition of 4,552 signatures; https://www.iamsterdam.com/ontdek/buurten/ndsm/verborgen-parels and https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/356840/moestuinclub-in-amsterdam-noord-moet-wijken-voor-sportvelden ; future uncertain; Mot in Mokum's address (Meteorenweg 28 per iamsterdam) needs checking; **Okay**.

### Noord-Oost (with the Waterland villages)

257. **Tuinpark Tuinwijck, the allotments that wandered**; about 52.384677, 4.973410 (Noord-Oost, Waterland); Amsterdam's first allotment complex, opened in 1910 at the Polderweg by the gasworks, pushed out by the city several times until it settled in Noord in 1974 as the first allotment park laid out as a public park; native-plant garden, bird island, owl boxes; https://www.tuinparktuinwijck.nl/de-bibliotheek/geschiedenis ; pairs with 252 ("one that stayed, one that wandered"); **Okay**.

258. **KiMu, the children's art museum**; Termini 487, 52.401376, 4.933839 (Noord-Oost, Buikslotermeer); a children's art museum opened by mayor Halsema in 2026, where children run their own making process, with a "light studio"; open Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday; https://www.kimu.nl/ ; narrow audience; opening date (March 2026) not fully confirmed; **Okay**.

Also a one-sentence merge without its own number: **Pierre van Soest's Albatros relief** was dismantled with the youth centre in 2001, stored near the NDSM for almost 20 years and shipped to the Van Klingeren Pavilion in The Hague in May 2019 (https://stroom.nl/en/art-in-the-city/pierre-van-soest-monumentaal-kunstwerk-gered); possible line in "Gijs Frieling's light cabinets, De Albatros" (round A lead closed).
