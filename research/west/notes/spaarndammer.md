## Dutch texts

Almost every `nl` text is spliced from Dutch source wording: the city register `text`, the national register omschrijving, or nl.wikipedia. Each source is listed in `nl.sources` and `links`. There is one exception. The **B-17 memorial** text is based on the 4en5mei.nl page, which is not open-licensed, so its Dutch is my own wording of the facts, with one short quote. The **Hembrugstraat block** has no register text, so its Dutch draws on the nl.wikipedia list of municipal monuments and the Rutgers article.

## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Gashouder**: the register reads "ANNO 1902", while the slice and the city give 1904. I used 1902.
- **Westergasfabriek listed**: the slice gives 1990. The register gives inschrijving 1991 for the 337… numbers and 1999 for the 512… numbers. I used 1990 from the slice.
- **De Klerk blocks**: the register gives 1913 and 1914. The literature gives 1914 and 1918, with the second block designed 1915–16. I gave the range 1913–1918.
- **Spaarndammercarré**: the city text says 1916–1917, the fields say 1917–1921. I used 1916–1921.
- **Zaanhof**: the register counts 82 + 38 + 14 dwellings. nl.wikipedia gives 82 in total.
- **Hembrugstraat block (200028)**: the city register gives 1920. The nl.wikipedia list and Wikidata give 1925 (Eigen Haard). I wrote 1920–1925 and named both in the text.
- **Volkskoffiehuis block (223023)**: the city register names J.H. Lesmeister. nl.wikipedia's Spaarndammerstraat article attributes the block to Walenkamp. I followed the register.
- **Het Veem 412 (Brummer & Co)**: the city text says built 1897, the city field says 1899.
- **Deli warehouses 526745 (Deli/Langkat/Serdang)**: three sources disagree. The register says 1903–04, P.J. Hamer. nl.wikipedia says 1898–1900. The city says W. Hamer. I used the combined range 1895–1914 and named W. Hamer as the architect.
- **Stenen Silo**: the register gives 120 bins and listing in 1992 (inschrijving 13 May 1992). nl.wikipedia gives 60 shafts and listing in 1996. I used 1992 and left the bin count out.
- **Maria Magdalenakerk**: one nl.wikipedia article says demolished in 1968 and the plantsoen named in 1972; the other says demolished from March 1967 and named in 1973. I used 1967 and 20 Dec 1972, because the plantsoen article gives the exact date.
- **B-17 memorial address**: 4en5mei gives Westzaanstraat 61 (52.38824, 4.88167). OSM gives no. 71. The point I used (52.387980, 4.881274) comes from the OSM memorial node. Check on site.
- **Wester sugar refinery memorial**: nl.wikipedia places it on the north side of the Van Noordtkade. My point is at the Suikerplein/Van Noordtkade junction, which is approximate.
- **Begraafplaats Sint Barbara**: the dike there is the separately protected 200523, kept as its own place.
