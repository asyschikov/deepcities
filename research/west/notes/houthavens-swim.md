# Houthavens swim spot: Waterplein, Revaleiland (Het Eikenhout)

One place, `green`, rank Nice. Not a monument.

## Identification

- Official name: **Waterplein** (Revaleiland), as used by the city (via De Westkrant, AT5) and the builder Knipscheer. BAG street name of the deck: **Revalsteiger** (openbare ruimte 0363300003767122; its line runs along the inner edge of the deck).
- Popular name: **Het Eikenhout** (Google Maps place, Grachtenfestival venue name, OSM way 977440742 `natural=beach`, `sport=swimming`, `surface=wood`, YouTube "Zwemsteiger Eikenhout"). Not an official BAG name; origin unclear (Grachtenfestival says it refers to the area's past). Also described as "the swim jetty by hotel The July / Boat & Co" (Revaleiland 500, right next to it).
- Pin 52.394742, 4.882455: middle of the "lazy" steps on the north-east edge of the deck, where people go into the water. Checked against the OSM polygon (edge midpoint 52.394738, 4.882471) and the PDOK aerial photo (steps visible). The jetty (OSM way 976052092, part of the same deck) runs east to about 52.39467, 4.88341. Zen Mast is about 55 m north-east (OSM node 11307556050 at 52.395097, 4.883083).

## Status (checked Nov 2026)

- **Not official.** Not in the province's Besluit aanwijzen zwemlocaties 2026 (Amsterdam sites: Marineterrein, Nieuwe Meer x3, IJburg, Diemerpark, Sloterplas etc.). Not on zwemwater.nl. No lifeguards, no testing, no bathing season.
- **Not banned.** De Westkrant (3 Jun 2023): a city warning sign (poor water quality possible, underwater obstacles, boats), but no ban; the ban with a 150 euro fine is at Het Stenen Hoofd. weswim.amsterdam (an unofficial site) claims swimming is "officially prohibited" here; no official or press source supports that, so it is left out.
- **Water quality**: unmeasured. City (2020): IJ-connected water does not meet the EU Bathing Water Directive. District (2023): quality unknown but expected good due to flow through the IJ. No 2025 or 2026 data found.
- **Official status attempt**: refused in 2023 (crowding, conflicts with the square's design); earlier soil pollution. 3 Jul 2026: district committee unanimously adopted a motion (D66, PRO, Volt) asking the board to discuss official status with the province. No outcome yet; recheck before the 2027 season (the province's draft list for 2027 should appear around January 2027).
