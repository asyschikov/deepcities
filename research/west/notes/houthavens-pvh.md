# Houthavens: PVH Campus (Danzigerkade)

One ensemble place: **PVH Campus, Danzigerkade** (`places/houthavens-pvh.yaml`).

## Why an ensemble

The PVH Europe office is not a standalone building but the first of three MVSA buildings
planned and marketed as one "PVH Campus" (architect, developer Heren 2, contractor HerenBouw
and façade builders Aluverre/Aldowa the same for all three). They stand in a row at the tip of
the Danzigerkade, share one concept (three readings of water: ship, waterfall, waves; white,
grey, black), are linked by glass footbridges and share the underground Houthavens garage
(346 places). Pinned on the head building, Porcellis (Danzigerkade 165).

Other modern buildings on the Danzigerkade (Theater Amsterdam at no. 5, 2014; the hotel at
175-189; offices at 15/16 and 207-215) were left out: different architects, no shared story.
Theater Amsterdam could be its own place later if wanted.

## Facts and sources

- Porcellis, Danzigerkade 165: MVSA (project architect Wouter Thijssen), 12 storeys, 50 m,
  completed 2016 (MVSA press release Nov 2016, e-architect, Arcam "realisatie 2016"; BAG
  bouwjaar 2018). Arcam: leans like a bow, canopy about 7 m, twice the height of the rest of
  the quay. MVSA: top five storeys showrooms.
- The other two: finished 2019 (gevelbouw.info, 1 Oct 2019, "Nieuw zijn de gebouwen DK 85 en
  DK 125"; BAG bouwjaar 2019 and 2018). MVSA factsheet says "Completion April 2018", probably
  the first phase or a planning date.
- PVH Europe still uses Danzigerkade 165 as its registered address (2026).

## Conflicts and doubts

- **Third building's number**: gevelbouw.info says DK 85, Kooij & Dekker (structural engineer)
  says Danzigerkade 95, MVSA's page says both "95" and "195". BAG has the campus buildings as
  Danzigerkade 107 (office, 2019, curved footprint, likely the "waterfall" DK 125) and 83
  (12,307 m², "overige gebruiksfunctie", 2018, likely the black "waves" building). The text
  only names Danzigerkade 165 and 125 in English, and 125 and 85 in Dutch (as gevelbouw does);
  the address field avoids numbers for the two newer ones.
- **Height of the black building**: MVSA says ten storeys; not cross-checked.
- **Name "Porcellis"**: used by Arcam, gevelbouw, hoog500, Aldowa. Probably after the marine
  painter Jan Porcellis, but no source says so, so it's not in the text.
- **Category**: the map has no architecture category. Used `curiosities` (as for Air cells);
  the coordinator may prefer another key.
- **Stadsdeel**: the Danzigerkade tip is formally in Westpoort (BAG/OSM: Minervahaven,
  Westpoort), though Wikipedia counts the Houthavens as West. Written into `research/west` as
  asked; it could belong to the Westpoort map data instead.
- **rank**: `rank: Nice` is set as requested, but `research/west/map.yaml` has no `rank` field,
  so `research/west/scripts/validate.py` reports "unknown attribute rank". The Nieuw-West
  validator (which has `rank`) passes with 0 errors. Drop the field or update the West
  map.yaml snapshot.
