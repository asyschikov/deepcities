## Conflicts and notes

- Aan Tafel: the artist's site gives 2024; Kunstwacht 5691 gives 2025. Used 2024. The scout's link to the Vrouwen van West route was not confirmed, so it is left out.
- Jan Wolkerstuin figures: Kunstwacht says they are replicas of Wolkers's mooring-post sculptures. A Commons caption says "by a German manufacturer, dated 2007". The text only says replicas. The scout's 3,000 m2, design contest, hand pump etc. were not verified, so they are left out.
- Zonnehoek: the dike through it is an old railway dike (Westkrant), not the sea dike.
- Gedichtenroute: nothing dated after the 2006 restoration.
- 402: the artist's name is spelled Rignalda and Ringnalda in Kunstwacht. I used Ringnalda (Commons, OSM). No year.
- 418: the "one of few churches in a back garden" claim is a local historian's guess, presented as such.
- 439: the scout's "30,500 buildings at 1:1300" is not in any source read. Used "more than 30,000".
- Van Bossestraat: which house numbers were destroyed (32/34) is disputed in reader comments, so left out.
- Telefooncentrale (not mine): gebouwdin credits Alfred Boeken, the register credits Public Works. The oud-west slice kept Public Works.
- Several merged entries now run 250 to 330 words (Westerpark, Domela Nieuwenhuisplantsoen, Centrale Markthal, and both full entries, which were already long live).
- De Tuinen van Westerpark is a combined place and runs a little over 250 words.

## Fact check
