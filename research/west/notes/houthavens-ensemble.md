# Houthavens: the islands as one ensemble place

One place: **The Houthavens islands** / **De eilanden van de Houthaven**
(`places/houthavens-ensemble.yaml`). Category `housing`, rank `Nice`, detail **city** (the
draft format has no `detail` key; set it on import). Not yet in
`scripts/data/amsterdam-c-list-ranks.json` (keyed by live id; add after import).

## What makes it an "experiment" (sourced)

- Plan: Stedenbouwkundig Plan adopted 2006. PPHP (the Soeters office) says it was developed with
  the city "op basis van het ontwerp van Ton Schaap"; MooiNL Atlas: city (Ton Schaap, Jan
  Brouwer, Paco Bunnik and others) with Sjoerd Soeters, who was also architectural supervisor.
  architectuur.nl (2007) and architectenweb (2013) credit the plan to "Soeters Van Eldonk".
  Text says "city planner Ton Schaap and architect Sjoerd Soeters", which fits all of them.
- Rules: Beeldkwaliteitplan April 2007 (listed on the city's publications page; the PDF itself
  was not reachable, so its contents come via architectuur.nl): "warm en informeel", materials
  that age well (brick, timber), each part its own character.
- Island themes (city project pages): Wiborgeiland 'Stoere Jongens' (canal warehouses),
  Narva-eiland 'Vrijbuiters', Memeleiland 'Deftige dames', Libau-eiland 'Timmerlieden',
  Karlskrona-eiland 'Rode Rondingen'. Themes for Revaleiland and Stettineiland not found in an
  authoritative source, so not named.
- MooiNL: styles from robust warehouses to Dudok and Amsterdam School; many architects; a broad
  consortium of developers. City: islands mostly max 4 or 5 storeys; about 2,700 homes, 20 %
  social rent; climate neutral (solar for public space, LED, reused paving bricks, FSC timber,
  district heating).
- Blok 0: nine self-build plots, taller as sound barrier to the port (architectenweb 2013);
  Arcam says six building groups. PUUUR Blok, first project (start Dec 2013), Zuiderkerkprijs
  2015 (Arcam). The Future: smallest plot, 17 flats, energy-positive (city).
- Spaarndammertunnel opened 5 Feb 2018, Houthavenpark on its deck (Wikipedia, city).
- Names: islands, 15 bridges and 8 canals after Baltic ports (Wikipedia, city). Canals filled
  from July 2023; last one 30 July 2026, dam cut through with an angle grinder (nl.wikipedia,
  citing Parool).
- Gevlebrug: 1930 lift bridge by Piet Kramer (with Cornelis Biemond), at the Oostertoegang near
  Centraal Station until 2001 (city says 2000), stored, re-erected 2019 to 2020 without lifting
  gear; model for the Helsingforsbrug and Windaubrug (nl.wikipedia Gevlebrug, city).

## Claims dropped

- "Blocks built over the tunnel": no source; the tunnel lies under the Houthavenpark along the
  Spaarndammerdijk, not under housing. Not used.
- "Kavelpaspoort": no Houthaven document found by that name; the rulebook is the 2007
  beeldkwaliteitplan. Not used.
- From the video only (not used): social housing cut from 30 % to 20 % after the financial
  crisis; canals "not needed"; island 0/Pontsteiger as the "right way to do modernism"; Libau as
  a "Baltic coast" recreation; Memel as neo-Renaissance; Reval/Stettin as Amsterdam School;
  "one of the most expensive neighbourhoods"; inner walkways under Blok 0; the quiet streets.
- architectuur.nl's "eiland 7 in Amsterdamse School style" (2007): island numbering of 2007 not
  matched to today's names with certainty. Left out.
- Tunnel length: city says 800 m (with ramps?), Wikipedia 470 m covered. No length in text.
- Built range "2010–2026": city expects completion in 2026, but the Entreegebouw has no date
  and Memeleiland water villa 3 is due February 2027.

## The video

Hoog, "The Weirdest Neighborhood in Amsterdam" (31 May 2024, 16:37). Angle: the Houthavens as a
"microcosm of Amsterdam", each island a modern remake of a historical Amsterdam style, "ordered
chaos" and choice within constraints as an answer to post-war modernist mass housing. Linked as
requested; no facts taken from it.

## Rank

Nice. A district-wide walk with a clear idea behind it and a few real sights (Gevlebrug, PUUUR
Blok, the style shifts); but most blocks are ordinary new housing, and Borneo-Sporenburg is the
better-known experiment of this kind. Could go to Amazing if the user wants it as the West entry
point for contemporary housing.
