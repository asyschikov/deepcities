# De Piramides (Jan van Galenstraat): notes

## What they are

The two "pyramids" about 100 m east of the Jan van Galenbrug are **De Piramides**, a housing
complex by Sjoerd Soeters (Soeters Van Eldonk Ponec architecten), designed from 2000 and completed
in 2006. Two stepped, triangular brick towers pushed into each other: one building up to the
11th floor, two separate peaks above, 17 storeys and 55 m, 82 flats, a raised public plaza with a
half-sunken car park behind. Address Jan van Galenstraat 1-29 and Marcantilaan 496-500, on the
north tip of the Marcanti island (Kop van Jut), De Baarsjes, opposite the Centrale Markthallen.
Client AM Wonen with Woonmaatschappij Zomers Buiten; contractor Van Wijnen.

Identified via BAG (pand 0363100012110293, bouwjaar 2006, woonfunctie on Jan van Galenstraat 1-29),
the city panorama of 2025-06-18 (stepped brick tower on the south side of the street) and
Wikidata Q2281305. The towers form one building and one BAG pand, so **one place**.

Not to be confused with the stepped **Marcanti College** next door (Jan van Galenstraat 31, BAG
2001, same plan and architect): mentioned in a Look for line, not a place of its own.

## Conflicts and claims dropped

- **Height**: architectuur.org and nl.wikipedia say 55 m; Arcam says "vijftig meter". Used 55 m.
- **Dates**: architect and architectuur.org 2000 to 2006 (design to completion), Arcam and BAG
  2006; nl.wikipedia infobox 2003 to 2007, Wikidata lists 2000, 2006 and 2007. Used 2000–2006.
- **Van Rhijn's housing**: architect says late 1970s, nl.wikipedia 1980s. Wrote "around 1980".
- Dropped: crèche and gallery on the ground floor (nl.wikipedia only, undated, current use not
  confirmed); the parking deal with the former stadsdeel Westerpark for Frederik Hendrikbuurt
  residents (wiki only); bus 18 stop; aroundus.com's "counter architectural uniformity" line
  (aggregator, not traceable).
