# Blind recall: stadsdeel West, off-the-A-list sights (2026-11-23)

Independent test. Compiled only from outside sources (iamsterdam neighbourhood pages and walking routes, Wikivoyage, Atlas Obscura, NH Nieuws, Parool-era press via NOS/Stedelijk, Amsterdam Alternative, a Rochdale neighbourhood walk, Guardian, VPRO, TracesOfWar, Stadscuratorium, Art@Site, architecture guides, travel blogs). No deepcities.org, no `pnpm dc`, no existing research files were consulted.

**Gebieden.** `api.data.amsterdam.nl/v1/gebieden/ggwgebieden` lists three GGW-gebieden in stadsdeel West: **GE03 Westerpark**, **GE04 Bos en Lommer**, **GE05 Oud-West, De Baarsjes**.

**Placement method.** Each address was geocoded with the PDOK Locatieserver, then the point was tested against `gebieden/buurten?geometrie[contains]=lon,lat` and `gebieden/ggwgebieden?geometrie[contains]=…`. The buurt code is given per item. Items marked "approx" were placed by a hand-picked point inside the named neighbourhood because the source gives no address. Removed by geocoding: Molen De 1200 Roe (Haarlemmerweg 701, GF07 Geuzenveld/Slotermeer), Jeroen Henneman's "Het Wiel" on the tax office by the A10 (FA08 Sloterdijk Stationskwartier), the "All Aboard" roundabout ship (Sloterdijk), Rembrandtpark and its "Rembo" statue (FM05, Slotervaart), and OCCII (Zuid edge). Surprises: Bilderdijkpark lands in Oud-West (EQ01), not Westerpark. Molen De Bloem lands in Bos en Lommer (EE03). The "Bois & Lombre" horse at Westerpark West lands in Westerpark (EA03), not Bos en Lommer.

**Ranking.** Within each gebied, items are ordered by how often and how strongly the sources recommended them: several independent mentions first, single mentions in a local paper or walk last. One URL is given per item (the strongest one).

### Notes and caveats

- The Haring mural point is the postcode centroid of the Foodcenter stretch of the Jan van Galenstraat. It falls in EG04 Marcanti, next to EG03 Markthallen. Both are in Westerpark, so the gebied is certain but the buurt is approximate.
- The Kolenkitbuurt items (Cascoland, "(Zoals) Ze is", the A10 underpass) have no street address in their sources. They were placed with a point inside ED01 Kolenkitbuurt-Noord. The whole Kolenkitbuurt is in GE04, so the gebied is safe.
- Atlas Obscura and Spotted by Locals refused automated fetches (HTTP 403). Atlas Obscura was used only where search results confirmed the entry. No usable r/Amsterdam threads came up in search.
- Westergasfabriek, Museum Het Schip, Foodhallen/De Hallen and Vondelpark were left out as A-list. Sub-parts of Westergas (Ketelhuis, Gashouder, Troost) were left out too.
