## Decisions

- **Ferry vs bridge**: the Hempont stands alone. The Hembrug is gone (status Gone) while the ferry runs, so merging them would make the status wrong for one of them. Each has its own pin: Hembrug on the old bridge axis mid-canal (nl.wikipedia/Olympedia coordinate 52.419722, 4.827778, roughly where the middle pier stood); Hempont on the Amsterdam ferry stop (OSM node 4848393940); Kissing Couple on its OSM node (5257348407), about 70 m east of the landing.
- **Hembrug rank**: the scout said Amazing; I gave Nice because nothing of the bridge survives on this bank (the brief's rule for gone things). The three stories (1928 Olympic start, 1944 swimmers, 1983–85 demolition) are strong, so the coordinator may want to raise it.
- **Hemweg power station** (#11) is written as the coal plant, status Gone. Vattenfall says the demolition of Hemweg 8 and its chimney was finished in September 2026. The gas-fired Hemweg 9 (2012) still runs on the site, which the body says. Pin: nl.wikipedia coordinate of the Centrale Hemweg (52.405161, 4.845128).
- **Coal mountains** pinned at the port's own Hemhaven-route viewpoint on the Hemweg (52.409553, 4.826477), outside the fence. Title uses the current operator name HES Bulk Terminal Amsterdam (OBA merged into HES; HES's site lists "steam coal and met coal" among products, so coal is still there in 2026).
- **Bank swallow wall** pinned at the port's uMap point "Oeverzwaluwen" (52.418604, 4.822199), next to the OSM "Uitzichtpunt Hempont" nature board. Not visible itself (shrubs).

## Corrections to SCOUT.md

- #5: "the swing bridge with the widest openings in the world" was not found in any source; sources say "the largest swing bridge in Europe" (Commons 1914 photo caption, Mei tot Mei). Used that. The 1944 action is now known to have failed in effect: floating labels gave it away and the Germans re-filled the pier (Mei tot Mei, also nl.wikipedia). Text says so plainly.
- #15: the ferries already went electric in 2021–2022 (GVB ponten 100–104). No source found for "plans to make it a foot-and-bike-only ferry"; left out. In 2026 cars and lorries still use it.
- #11: the scout's "row of eight stacks" belongs to the 1950s plant, demolished 1980, not to unit 8. Chimney completion: Vattenfall's own pages say demolition started 11 June 2025 and was expected to take about three months; BouwBelang (March 2026) says it took six weeks; De Orkaan shows it half-standing in August 2025 and gone by November 2025. Text says "a metre a day from 11 June 2025" and avoids an end date for the chimney.
- #11/#29: the Hemweg peregrine nest box was removed on 24 November 2020 (Vattenfall), so only the AEB box remains for item 29 (not my slice; flag for the port slice).
- #23: the Berm Bunnies are still in place (social posts April and November 2026); the vandalised rabbit was back on 18 March 2021.
- #27: build year conflict: Port of Amsterdam says 1996; De Gierzwaluw (Vogelwerkgroep Amsterdam, August 2002) says "sinds 2001" and that no martins bred in it in 2001. Both given in the text. Breeding numbers (10–20 pairs) are from the port only, undated; Het Parool (2019) mentions an "old" martin wall eroded and raided by a stoat, which may be a different wall (Afrikahaven). No 2025–2026 confirmation that the Hemweg wall is still occupied.

## Other conflicts and uncertainties

- Hembrug piers: nl.wikipedia says "spring 1985"; Mei tot Mei gives 24 January 1985 for the middle pier. Text says "in 1985".
- Hembrug 1944 date: ZaanWiki snippet says 21 September; nl.wikipedia and Mei tot Mei say 26/27 September. Used 26/27.
- Olympedia marks the 1928 start coordinate as "most likely here"; which bank the start was on is unknown, so the text says "at the Hembrug".
- Kissing Couple: placed 30 November 2017 (OSM start_date, Hempontplein article), unveiled 14 December 2017 (Kissing Couple article). Weight: "around 6,700 kg" for the pair.
- The turning-ring relic of the Hembrug is on the Hembrugterrein in Zaandam (outside Amsterdam), mentioned only in "Look for".

## Route idea

**Hemhaven cycle loop (about 15 km from Sloterdijk, follows the port's signposted route)**: Berm Bunnies, Hemweg coal plant and the "cloud factory" (gone), Coal and bulk mountains (HES Bulk Terminal), Bank swallow wall, Hemweg, Kissing Couple XXXL, Hempont, Hembrug (gone); optionally cross on the ferry to the Hembrugterrein.
