## Sweep 4: Hidden history

### De Aker/Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten

201. **The Biber midget submarine "Fenix", Sloterweg 795**; garage yard, 52.341756, 4.817077 (Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten). A red-and-white German one-man Biber submarine (built in Lübeck, 1944, under 9 m long) has stood on a garage yard on the Sloterweg since shortly after the war. Garage owner Jan van der Veldt raised it while clearing the Waalhaven in Rotterdam, reportedly with the pilot still inside, painted it and named it after his farm Fenix, which had burned down twice (demolished in the 1970s). In 1978, after the kidnapping of property developer Maup Caransa, the clairvoyant Peter Hurkos rode with Caransa in a taxi on live TV and declared at the Sloterweg that he had been held in the submarine; the taxi driver later said Hurkos had rehearsed the route. Fits: a war object turned roadside sign with a tabloid sequel, unknown outside Sloten; one of very few surviving Bibers. Lead: https://slotenoudosdorp.nl/onderzeeboot-sloterweg-jan-van-der-veldt-deed-een-macabere-vondst/ (from Ons Amsterdam, May 2020, which says it still stands there "to this day"). Status: present (2020; an excursion report of Oct 2017 also mentions it). Caveats: private yard, seen from the road; house number 795 or 785 (PDOK puts 795 at the OSM node); the "pilot inside" story is hearsay; round A dropped the OSM "submarine v.d. Veldt" node as a garden ornament before the story was found. `check: Still there?` (newest evidence 2020). Rank: **Nice** (Amazing candidate). New place.

202. **Tolhuis Sloten and the toll board in Café Kerkzicht**; Sloterweg 1191-1193 and 1201, 52.341377, 4.800308 (Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten). From 1816 the Sloterweg was a toll road: the village borrowed to pave it and plant trees, and kept gates at the Sloterkade and here, opposite the Sint-Pancratiuskerk. Early KLM passengers paid toll twice on the way to Schiphol. Amsterdam abolished the toll in 1923, two years after the annexation; the tariff board still hangs in Kerkzicht next door, and since 1 July 2023 a camera-enforced "knip" 75 m east fines drivers about €110 a pass, which locals call the return of the toll. Fits: a small house with a long story, and a board you can see over a beer. Leads: https://slotenoudosdorp.nl/hoe-in-sloten-de-tol-kwam-verdween-en-weer-terugkwam/ (29 Feb 2024); https://slotenoudosdorp.nl/de-slotense-tol-in-het-parool/ (Dec 2022). Status: house lived in; board reported in Kerkzicht in 2024. Caveats: Kerkzicht's claim to be "the oldest café in Amsterdam" is unproven, leave it out. Rank: **Nice**. New place, or merge into "Sint-Pancratiuskerk (Sloten)" (opposite) as a paragraph.

203. **The annexation stone, the time capsule and the GEB doors**; Dorpsplein by the police post, 52.341654, 4.797175. On 16 September 2022 mayor Halsema unveiled a stone for the 1921 annexation, quoting De Standaard of 31 December 1920 ("the countryside does not get prettier or better when it is hung with big-city clothes"); she called herself chair of the village council of a suburb of Sloten. Behind two restored GEB (municipal energy company) doors in the wall are a working old company telephone and a time capsule filled by villagers in 2022, to stay shut for 50 to 100 years. Alderman and MP Willem de Buisonjé is still locally "the traitor of Sloten" (lecture, July 2025). Leads: slotenoudosdorp.nl posts on the stone, the time capsule, the telephone and the "verrader van Sloten" (2022 to 2025). Caveats: the telephone is dated "1932 or 1949". Merge into **"Dorpsplein Sloten and the police post"**.

204. **Sloten's coat of arms on the Akergemaal**; Akersluis, 52.343137, 4.793590. The town hall of the gemeente Sloten stood in Sloterdijk, not in Sloten, and was demolished in 1957 for the new station's level crossing. Its arms stone was saved and walled into the 1950-1952 Akergemaal, the pumping station beside the mill: a star for Sloterdijk, padlocks for Sloten, a wedge for the Vrije Geer, an ox for Osdorp. Leads: nl.wikipedia "Raadhuis van Sloten"; slotenoudosdorp.nl "Geschiedenis van Sloten". Status: gemaal in use; no recent photo of the stone. Merge into **"Molen van Sloten"** (Look for line).

205. **Wees- en Armenhuis and Speeltuin Sloten**; Akerpolderstraat 9-47, 52.341859, 4.796747 (Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten); playground 52.342147, 4.795798. The village orphanage and poorhouse of 1906, with corner towers, became an old people's home after the annexation, was squatted from January 1973 to late 1976, then youth housing, sold off from 2011 and renovated in 2021; the "Boerentuintje" opposite was its vegetable garden. Next door, Speeltuin Sloten was founded in 1921 by local shopkeepers to keep children off the Schiphol road and is said to be Amsterdam's oldest playground association; the Germans requisitioned it (and paid rent). Leads: https://slotenoudosdorp.nl/wees-en-armenhuis-1906-met-zijn-rijke-historie-kan-er-weer-jaren-tegen/ (Jul 2021); slotenoudosdorp.nl "Geschiedenis Dorpshuis Sloten" (Jul 2024). Status: present (2021, 2024). Caveats: private flats, outside only; "oldest playground association" is a local claim. Rank: **Okay**. New place.

206. **The bell JERON**; Sloterkerk. The 1516 bell by the Moer brothers is called JERON and is the second-oldest church bell in Amsterdam, after the Zuiderkerk's of 1511. Lead: https://slotenoudosdorp.nl/de-klok-van-de-sloterkerk-is-510-jaar-oud/ (4 Feb 2026). Merge into **"Sloterkerk"** (it already has the 1516 bell: add the name and the ranking).

207. **The hidden bundled lime of Nieuw en Meer**; Oude Haagseweg 125, middle terrain. A Dutch lime grown from fused stems, planted around 1900 beside the former ammunition depot; tree experts think its odd shape comes from war damage. In August 2026 the city's tree committee advised making it a protected monument tree; the stadsdeel board has not yet decided. Leads: slotenoudosdorp.nl (31 Dec 2025 and 24 Aug 2026). Merge into **"Nieuw en Meer"** (Look for line).

208. **The 1692 house and the butcher's lettering, Sloterweg**; Osdorperweg 1 / Sloterweg 1238 and Sloterweg 1233. The timber-framed corner house of 1692 was a coffee house and taproom and still has its beer cellar; a 1983 gevelsteen by Ruud van Boom shows a tree and the arms of Sloten. At no. 1233 the Jugendstil "Vleeschhouwerij" lettering of the former butcher Koning is still readable. Lead: slotenoudosdorp.nl "Toen en nu in Sloten en Oud Osdorp" (26 Jul 2024). Merge into **"Sloterweg village houses"** (Look for lines).

### Osdorp

209. **Storks on the Lutkemeerschool**; Osdorperweg, beside the schoolmaster's house. Storks nest on the chimneys of the 1929 village school (now artists' studios); the Oud Osdorp chicks are ringed every year (nine in 2024). Lead: slotenoudosdorp.nl (21 Jun 2024). Merge into **"Schoolmeesterswoning, Osdorperweg 635"**.

210. **The vanished Catholic church of Oud Osdorp**; Osdorperweg. In late March 2025 city archaeologists uncovered the foundations of the Sint-Pancratiuskerk (1633 to 1901), with sacristy and parsonage, for one week, then covered them again for new housing. It explains why the Sloten church carries the same name. Lead: https://slotenoudosdorp.nl/fundamenten-kerkcomplex-in-oud-osdorp-weeklang-zichtbaar/ (7 Apr 2025). Caveat: nothing visible now. Merge into **"Oud-Osdorp cemetery and the Deckers tomb"** (one sentence).

211. **The RAF flat on the Baden Powellweg**; Baden Powellweg 217, 52.348414, 4.795647 (Osdorp-Midden). In 1977 members of the Rote Armee Fraktion used a flat in the Aeckerstijn block; on 10 November police tried to arrest Christof Wackernagel and Gert Schneider at a phone box on the Pieter Calandlaan and it ended in a shoot-out. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Baden Powellweg (Amsterdam)". Caveats: one wiki source, an ordinary flat with no marker; check against Delpher before use. Merge into **"Blauwe Boog (Lixenberg)"** (one sentence; the sculpture stands opposite).

### Geuzenveld-Slotermeer

212. **Cockel Bockel, the underwater house in the Sloterplas**; off the Oostoever, divers enter at the stone quay, 52.370090, 4.830158. Since 1965 or 1966 a small underwater house has lain on the bed of the lake off the east shore, said to be the oldest underwater house in the Netherlands; it is a fixture of the lake's dive site. Leads: nl.wikipedia "Sloterplas"; https://www.duikersgids.nl/en/sloterplas-oostoever (dive logs to August 2026). Caveats: only divers see it. Merge into **"Sloterplas: the lake dug for sand"** (Look for line).

### Osdorp

213. **Payens's climbing wall at Sportpark Ookmeer**; Herman Bonpad, beside the Poort van Constant, 52.367516, 4.797903 (geocoded; pin the object). A 12 m climbing wall that is also an artwork: two concrete triangles in relief by artist and climber Boudewijn Payens, put up in 1988 at the Jaap Edenbaan as one of the first climbing walls in the Netherlands, taken apart in 2008 and its 20 slabs (60 tonnes) rebuilt here. Lead: https://www.architectuurgids.nl/project/item/prj_id/4819 (photo 2017). Status: unverified since 2017. Merge into **"Poort van Constant"** (Look for line, after a check).

### De Aker/Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten

214. **Amsterdam South Connection, the heat tower**; Johan Huizingalaan 801-803, 52.340535, 4.834261 (Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten). Vattenfall's plant that links the city's two district-heating networks: a 3,600 m³ hot-water buffer and four boilers in one half-transparent glass skin with rounded corners, meant as a landmark for travellers from Schiphol; the control room on top had to stay just under Schiphol's radar limits. Willem Schutter, National Steel Prize 2024. Leads: https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/3901 ; https://nationalestaalprijs.nl/sites/default/files/webredacteur/NSP24_B_Winnaar_Hulpwarmtecentrale.pdf . Status: built 2022, prize 2024. Caveats: closed plant, outside only. Rank: **Okay**. New place.

### Slotervaart

215. **The Cold War shelter under the Louis Bouwmeesterschool**; Louis Bouwmeesterstraat 14, 52.355701, 4.820768 (Slotervaart-Zuid). A 1960 H-plan primary school holds one of Amsterdam's 40 public Cold War shelters, for 150 people: its role was "mainly symbolic", a place for passers-by caught in the street. It was one of five Amsterdam shelters opened on Open Monumentendag 2024 (with the Vondelbunker and Weesperplein). The school was renovated in 2018 with its H-plan kept. The same school has adopted the "Bevrijde vogel, Sierplein" memorial. Leads: https://forten.nl/?p=92959 (4 Sep 2024); https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/3307 . Status: school in use; shelter open only on open days (last 2024). Caveats: working school, say so; no photo of the shelter found. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

216. **Eindgemaal Postjesweg and the buried booster**; Postjesweg 529, deck at 52.363861, 4.834632 (Overtoomse Veld). Everything the western neighbourhoods flush collects in a concrete chamber under a walk-on deck (BureauVanEig, 2022) that doubles as the entrance to the new Spoorpark; a kilometre east the 2005 booster station is hidden in the bank of the Rembrandtpark on purpose. Leads: https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/3898 ; /projectdetail/33 . Caveats: the booster pin is approximate; thin unless the deck is attractive on site. Rank: **Okay**. New place (or drop).

### Sloterdijk Nieuw-West

217. **RWZI West, Hertzberger's sewage works**; Australiëhavenweg 15, office block 52.398950, 4.793312. The city's western sewage plant (2005) replaced the Zeeburgereiland works when the flow of the sewer system was reversed. Unusually, an architect was hired: Herman Hertzberger lined the process up at right angles to the road so a passing driver can follow it stage by stage, with a glass office tower fused to three huge digestion tanks and a curtain of treated water in front. Leads: https://www.architectuurgids.nl/project/item/prj_id/2102 ; https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/11 . Status: in operation. Caveats: closed site, seen from the road and the Geuzenbos edge; the water curtain needs a site check. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

218. **Biotope 2.0 (Leonard van Munster, 2021)**; Heining 148, 52.394890, 4.764019. A glass bell set over bare ground with no floor, so plants keep growing inside and the work changes with the seasons, on the edge between the port and the Geuzenbos; by the artist whose "Under Heaven 02" was destroyed in 2025. Lead: https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/3695 . Caveats: one source, not known if public. Rank: **Okay**. New place only after `check: Still there?` (or a Look for in 245).

## Sweep 6: Modern and contested

### Geuzenveld-Slotermeer

219. **Bakemabuurt**; Jan Zijvertszstraat / Bouwen Ewoutszstraat, 52.378375, 4.805601 (Geuzenveld). Jaap Bakema's late-1950s neighbourhood, which Docomomo calls "a three-dimensional Mondrian" of stacked volumes. The 2000s renewal plan meant to demolish most of it; in 2014 Stadgenoot changed course and decided building by building: two portiekflats stripped to the shell and reopened with double-width glass entrances, the gallery flats replaced by new blocks that copy Bakema's long low slabs and big underpasses. Nearly 600 homes, finished December 2021. Bakema is not on the map yet. Leads: https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/1136 ; /projectdetail/3303 ; https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/296271/renovatie-bakemabuurt-in-geuzenveld-eindelijk-klaar ; Docomomo Journal. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

220. **Parkrand (MVRDV) and Hutten's outdoor living rooms**; Dr. H. Colijnstraat 250-710, 52.380810, 4.807376 (Geuzenveld). Built in 2006 to kick-start the renewal of Geuzenveld: one huge block with giant holes punched through it so the streets behind keep their view of the Eendrachtspark, black outside and white glazed brick inside; Richard Hutten furnished the three raised patios as oversized rooms with soft furniture and chandeliers. Leads: https://www.architectuurgids.nl/project/item/prj_id/2086 ; https://mvrdv.com/projects/146/parkrand . Caveats: patio access and the Hutten furniture unverified after about 2009 (`check: Still there?`); 100 m from "De sleutel ligt onder de deurmat", link it. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

221. **Moskee Al-Ihsane, a mosque in Dudok's colours**; Mendes da Costahof 32, 52.376659, 4.798809 (Geuzenveld). When the mosque was rebuilt in Dudok's neighbourhood (Gerard Rijnsdorp, 2017), the city set terms: it had to look related to Dudok, be recognisable as a mosque and have no dome and no minaret; so it is built in Dudok's pale yellow brick with square-paned windows. Leads: https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/1580 ; OAPEN book on religious buildings (library.oapen.org/bitstream/20.500.12657/24710/1/9789048533589.pdf). Status: active (2026). Caveats: the conditions come from the architect's text; find a news source; 400 m from "Dudok's farmhouse homes". Rank: **Nice**. New place (or a paragraph in "Dudok's farmhouse homes").

222. **Louis Couperusbuurt and Nieuwenhuysenbuurt renewal (round 1 item 38, re-checked)**; A.M. de Jongstraat 2, 52.376073, 4.825766 (Slotermeer-Zuidoost). 700 social-rent homes in a green working-class quarter laid out in Van Eesteren's spirit are to become about 1,200, mostly by demolition; Stadgenoot says 82% of residents agreed, the residents' committee chair has fought it since 2008; the job takes ten years. Earlier, residents here were fined for altering their facades to protect the street's unity. In the Nieuwenhuysenbuurt nearby (de Alliantie, 282 homes to 400) demolition began on 10 September 2025; the Savornin Lohmanstraat (26 homes) faces demolition from Q3 2026 after a petition for a right of return. Leads: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/358752/deze-geliefde-amsterdamse-buurt-verandert-voorgoed (12 May 2026); https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/337395/welstand-couperusbuurt-gevels-dwangsom ; https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/343489/petitie-van-bewoners-tegen-nieuwbouw-en-sloopplannen-stadgenoot-we-willen-terugkeergarantie . **Outlook: Demolition planned, from about late 2027.** Caveats: sensitive, keep factual; **check whether the live "Adriaan van Oordthof" (described as "never renovated, still as built, at the heart of the Couperusbuurt") and "De Hoeksteen and ULO school (Zanstra)" are in the plan: if so they need an Outlook too.** Rank: **Okay**. New place.

Noorderhof: see 237 (merge).

### Osdorp

223. **WoZoCo, the flats that hang in the air**; Ookmeerweg / Reimerswaalstraat, 52.363857, 4.794237 (Osdorp-Midden; move the pin onto the cantilevers). MVRDV's breakthrough (1994 to 1997): the brief asked for 100 flats for over-55s, but sun and height rules allowed only 87 in the slab, so the other 13 hang off the north side in boxes cantilevered up to 11 m, with coloured glass balconies on the south. Mies van der Rohe Award finalist; "as frivolous as it is frightening" (architectuurgids). One of the most published Dutch buildings of the 1990s, which most Amsterdammers have never seen. Leads: https://www.architectuurgids.nl/project/item/prj_id/658 ; https://mvrdv.com/projects/170/wozoco . Status: in use as senior housing (2024). Caveats: residents' privacy, view from the street. Rank: **Nice** (Amazing candidate). New place.

224. **De Meervaart and the theatre in the Sloterplas**; Meer en Vaart 300, 52.359051, 4.807352 (Osdorp-Oost). Nieuw-West's theatre (1977, rebuilt 1999) is to be replaced by a new Meervaart on the south-west shore of the Sloterplas, partly in the protected park and the water; the council chose the site in March 2024. An opposition party's poll found 87% against, the Friends of the Sloterplas objected, residents called the consultation rigged, and the director was threatened. Design competition from 2026, opening planned 2032. Leads: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/352787/nieuw-theater-bij-sloterplas-krijgt-meer-en-meer-vorm-maar-protest-blijft-aanhouden (Sep 2025); https://www.rodi.nl/amsterdam-nieuw-west/nieuws/503391/uitslag-enquete-87-procent-tegen-nieuwbouw-meervaart ; https://meervaart.nl/en/nieuwbouw . **Outlook: Major change ahead, 2032** (fate of the current building not found). Rank: **Nice**. New place.

225. **Wildemanbuurt, waiting for the wrecking ball**; around Station Wildeman, Nieuwe Laan 34 (pin to geocode; Wildeman 1 is 52.364252, 4.797580, Osdorp-Oost). Mould-ridden 1960s flats where residents themselves campaigned for demolition (77% in favour, 2023 poll); Stadgenoot plans 10 to 15 years of works, council decision in 2026, first rehousing in 2027. Meanwhile a former school became Station Wildeman (2024), a neighbourhood hub. Leads: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/302200/huizen-vol-schimmel-in-amsterdamse-wildemanbuurt-onze-kinderen-zijn-ziek ; https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/4053 . **Outlook: Demolition planned, rehousing from 2027.** Rank: **Okay**. New place (or one "renewal" text with 222).

### De Aker/Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten

226. **B.Amsterdam and The David**; Johan Huizingalaan 763a, 52.342406, 4.829889, and David Ricardostraat 2. IBM's typewriter factory stood empty for eleven years before becoming B.Amsterdam (2015), one of Europe's biggest start-up hubs, with IBM back as a tenant; William McDonough's cradle-to-cradle office for IBM became The David (2020), 365 flats, the first office-to-housing conversion in the business park. Leads: https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/1355 ; /projectdetail/3608 . Merge into **"IBM headquarters, Johan Huizingalaan"**.

### Slotervaart

227. **Blauwe Moskee (round 1 item 40, re-checked)**; Henri Dunantstraat, 52.348523, 4.831472 (Slotervaart-Zuid). A 2008 mosque and cultural centre in blue glazed brick with no classic mosque forms and services in Dutch, funded via Europe Trust Nederland by Kuwait; critics tied founders to the Muslim Brotherhood. In November 2019 it was the first mosque in Amsterdam to amplify the Friday call to prayer, and days later the cable was cut; it later refused a city request to sign an LGBTI support statement. Leads: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blauwe_Moskee_(Amsterdam) ; https://nos.nl/artikel/2309517 ; https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/256299/versterkte-gebedsoproep-blauwe-moskee-gesaboteerd-kabel-doorgesneden . Status: active (newest news September 2023). Caveats: sensitive, keep factual; architect not found; Mecanoo's Park Haagseweg housing around it as a Look for. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

228. **What is left of De Drie Hoven**; Louis Bouwmeesterstraat 25-311, 52.355582, 4.822369 (Slotervaart-Zuid), 100 m from 215. Herman Hertzberger's 1971 to 1975 home for the elderly was built as a small city, with paths crossing the entrances so residents and neighbours would meet by chance; it was once called the largest old people's home in Europe. New ideas about care doomed it: in 2013 demolition was granted for all but the wing along the street, and Mecanoo's "De Bouwmeester" (2015) took the rest. Leads: https://www.architectuurgids.nl/project/item/prj_id/404 ; https://nieuweinstituut.nl/en/projects/ouderenhuisvesting/de-drie-hoven-1970-h-hertzberger-en-c-van-empelen ; stcrt-2013-27143. Caveats: current use of the surviving wing not found (VPRO 2019: residents leaving); `check: Facts`. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

229. **Jacob Geelbuurt: Braat's reliefs come back?**; Jacob Geelstraat / Hermanus van der Tuukhof, approx. 52.3627, 4.8303 (Slotervaart-Noord). ### Sloterdijk Nieuw-West

233. **Station Sloterdijk and Teleport**; Orlyplein 105, 52.389007, 4.837545. A three-level crossing station (H.C.H. Reijnders, 1983 to 1986): the Haarlem line at ground level, a hall above, the Schiphol line on the top deck under a glass arch, all under a 50 x 65 m space-frame "table". Tracks 1 and 2 don't exist (they belonged to the old station), and the Hemboog platforms are a few hundred metres off with their own entrance. It was built for Teleport, the 1980s "telematics" office park named after airports (Orly, Changi, Barajas), which never filled up and is now turning into housing (Haven-Stad). Look for: Ven, the former KPN head office with three wings at 120 degrees, now hotel and casino. Leads: https://www.architectuurgids.nl/project/item/prj_id/441 ; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Station_Amsterdam_Sloterdijk ; https://thecityateyelevel.com/stories/the-metamorphosis-of-sloterdijk/ . Status: in use (70,000 passengers a weekday, 2025). Note: the station point-checks to borough Nieuw-West. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

234. **VERTICAL and the offices turning into homes**; Barajasweg 70, 52.386909, 4.835067. 168 homes "for human and non-human residents" (NL Architects and four other firms, 2023): planters in the facade, green roofs, nest boxes; around it Wasa Student Village (2017, modular, meant to be taken apart after ten years), the Barajas and Naritaweg office conversions and Lovely Rita (2024). ## Sweep 7: Curiosities, green corners and odd museums

### Geuzenveld-Slotermeer

235. **Tuinen van West**; picnic hill on the Pieter Moeskopspad, 52.379655, 4.779121 (Geuzenveld). Four polders that Van Eesteren's 1935 plan left open as a green wedge, then pencilled in for housing and business parks for decades, until a 2007 decision ended "nearly a century of debate" and made them city-farming and recreation land; the A5 was then cut through the north-west side. The result is an odd patchwork: allotments, a pick-your-own organic orchard (Fruittuin van West, since 2014), a pig farm fed on food waste, and bird hides from a Rietveld Academy contest. Look for: Dimitra Chrysovergi's concrete-tube hide "In between the gap" (52.379459, 4.765537) over a wetland dug to replace one the A5 destroyed; Natasha Oduber's bamboo hide "Amfibion" (52.356571, 4.758753, Osdorp); the ash avenue on the Nico Broekhuysenweg, "maybe the finest in the country" (NH Nieuws, Dec 2025). Leads: https://tuinenvanwest.nl/tuinen-van-west/ ; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuinen_van_West ; https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/354970 . Status: active (events Nov and Dec 2026). Caveats: an area, the pin is a visitor point; Amfibion unverified; link "Lutkemeerpolder" and "De 1800 Roeden", don't repeat them. This also gives round A's dropped "270 degrees" hide a home as a Look for. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

236. **A. Nouriplein**; Michel de Klerkhof, 52.376412, 4.797112 (Geuzenveld). The football court where Abdelhak "Appie" Nouri played as a boy, before his collapse on the pitch as an Ajax player in July 2017 left him severely disabled. Since 2005 it had been the Nigel de Jongplein, after another Ajax player from the neighbourhood; De Jong proposed handing the name to Nouri. Renamed on 12 June 2022, with his number 34 on the centre spot. Leads: https://www.hartvannederland.nl/sport/artikelen/amsterdam-doopt-voetbalplein-om-tot-abdelhak-nouriplein ; https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/305028/familie-vertelt-nouri-alles-over-eigen-pleintje-dit-doet-heel-veel-met-hem . Status: in iamsterdam's 2026 tips. Caveats: about a living person: factual and kind, nothing medical beyond what the family has made public. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

237. **Noorderhof, the anti-garden-city**; around the M.J. Granpré Molièreplein. Rob Krier and Christoph Kohl (1995 to 1999) built 230 homes in closed streets, squares and gatehouses, the opposite of Van Eesteren's open rows; architects called it "fussy and outdated", buyers queued. Noorderhof Zuid (2015 to 2018) added 124 more, and "Carte Blanche Noorderhof" (2016) let buyers pick one of three styles and spend "architecture points" on French balconies or flower boxes. Leads: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noorderhof ; https://www.architectuurgids.nl/project/item/prj_id/1773 ; https://gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl/projectdetail/1424 . Merge into **"Grote man, kleine man (Stephan Balkenhol)"** (which already introduces the Noorderhof; add the Zuid extension and the architecture points). A new place would repeat that text and the Lourdeskerk's.

238. **Laan van Verdienste**; Sloterpark. Each year residents nominate volunteers, and three winners each plant a cherry tree with a name plaque in the "avenue of merit". Leads: Rodi (19 Jun 2024; 2025 nominees). Caveats: exact spot not found. Merge into **"Sloterplas: the lake dug for sand"** (Look for line), or drop.

### Osdorp

239. **Raesberg, the hill with a ghost village**; Slibveldenweg, summit 52.368169, 4.761366 (Lutkemeer/Ookmeer). A man-made hill beside the A5, raised around 2016 to 2017 from the Westrandweg's sand depot, with low walls of mixed brick laid on its slope as the foundations of a village that isn't there: Raasdorp, a free manor between the Lutkemeer and the Spieringmeer that a storm flood nearly destroyed in 1632 and that vanished when the lakes were drained. Underneath are the city's old sewage-sludge fields, where Amsterdam made fertiliser the market gardeners would not buy. Look for: the Wijsentkade (52.370553, 4.759939), a dike older than the polders that ringed the Lutkemeer (date from one secondary source; OSM marks the bank itself as no access). Leads: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Raesberg (14 photos, April 2022, incl. the city's info board); https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raasdorp ; OSM way 46859105 (Wijsentkade). Status: 2022 photos; no press article found. Caveats: designer unknown. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

240. **Van Eyck-style paddling pool, Piet Wiedijkpark**; park centre 52.354650, 4.817959 (Osdorp-Oost; pin the pool). A pierenbad of 1972 to 1973 on Aldo van Eyck's model, without his play frames; iamsterdam's southern Nieuw-West route still sends families there. Leads: nl.wikipedia "Piet Wiedijkpark"; iamsterdam "Wandelroute door zuidelijk Nieuw-West". Caveats: Van Eyck's own hand not confirmed. Merge into **"Van Eyck playground, Witte Klok"** (Look for line), or drop.

### De Aker/Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten

241. **Tuinpark VAT, the allotments the garden cities pushed out**; entrance Lies Bakhuyzenlaan 17, 52.340533, 4.797630. Amsterdammers began growing potatoes on a meadow by the Sloterstraatweg in the hungry year 1916 and founded the Vereniging Amateur Tuinders in 1919; the garden cities ended their lease in 1952, and after years of haggling 116 members moved to eight hectares in Sloten (1955); the park opened on 28 June 1958, when they gave the city Hans IJdo's "De wiedster". Open to the public, with arched bridges and a native-plant garden. Lead: https://vat-sloten.nl/historie . Caveats: history from the club's own page; gate hours not found. Merge into **"Dorpsplein Sloten and the police post"** (it already points to the Wiedster and the fire pump inside the park).

### Slotervaart

242. **Rembrandtpark (western half)**; park centre 52.362590, 4.846829, or Kinderboerderij De Uylenburg, Staalmeesterslaan 420, 52.367361, 4.845186 (Overtoomse Veld). Van Eesteren planned it in the 1930s as a buffer between the old city and the garden cities; it was named in 1958 and then left unbuilt, so through the 1960s it was a wasteland of pools, mosquitoes, dumped rubbish and motocross, until it was finished as a landscape park in 1973. A road planned through it was struck out after years of protest. De Uylenburg (1973, named after Saskia van Uylenburgh) is called the city's oldest children's farm (disputed; say "one of the oldest"). Look for: the nodding dogs and Rembo (already a place), Arno Coenen's mosaic tunnels; the Duizendste brug (the city's thousandth bridge) is just over the line in West. Leads: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rembrandtpark ; https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/7819/weg-rembrandtpark-adam-geschrapt ; TenderNed "Realisatie Renovatie Rembrandtpark" (22 Apr 2026). **Outlook: Major change ahead (renovation tendered 2026, dates unknown).** Caveats: the park's eastern part is borough West; pin in the western half. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

243. **De KasKantine**; by the Westlandgracht, 52.343714, 4.836878 (Westlandgracht). An off-grid, self-built greenhouse canteen and garden run by volunteers (pay-what-you-want vegan meals from rescued food), which has moved four times, from the Sugar Factory at Halfweg via the Haarlemmerweg, and lives on land that is "temporarily available". Leads: https://www.kaskantine.nl/ ; https://amsterdamalternative.nl/articles/5514/setting-up-camp-kaskantine-3-0 . Status: workshops announced for 2026. **Outlook: Under threat (temporary land, end date unknown).** Caveats: open by event only; a free-zone story more than a food one. Rank: **Okay**. New place.

244. **Origin, the light windows of the Derkinderen viaduct (Vendel & De Wolf, 2022)**; Derkinderenstraat under the Cornelis Lelylaan, approx. 52.357900, 4.840600 (Westlandgracht; pin to be set on site). Fourteen trapezoid frames between the viaduct's pillars hold hand-engraved panels of shapes like microbes, pollen and floor plans, lit from the edges in changing colours; the frames echo the viaduct's own perspective, "light at the end of the tunnel". Found in the blind-recall check (iamsterdam's southern Nieuw-West walk lists "Kunstwerk Origin"); not in round A's art lists. Lead: https://www.vendelendewolf.nl/origin.shtml (installed March 2022). Caveats: Paul Vendel also made "The Swarm" (dropped in round 1); pin approximate (`check: Location`); best after dark. Rank: **Okay**. New place.

### Sloterdijk Nieuw-West

245. **Geuzenbos and its lookout tower**; tower 52.397402, 4.760136. Sixty hectares of wood and wet scrub on what was IJ water until 1875, buried under four metres of harbour sand in the 1960s and then sludge from the Halfweg sugar factory, which is why the ground is lumpy. The first part was planted in 1991 as a production forest, on the initiative of councillor Roel van Duijn (of Provo and the Kabouters); the unplanted gaps turned out richer. In 2002 the province earmarked it for relocated polluting industry; since 2007 konik horses and Highland cattle graze it, with a wildlife bridge over the Wethouder van Essenweg (2012) and a 15 m lookout tower. Leads: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geuzenbos ; https://www.komoot.com/highlight/3073826 . Caveats: the Van Duijn claim rests on nl.wikipedia; `check: Still there?` for the tower (no 2025-2026 evidence); link "Banpaal Spaarnwouderdijk" at its edge. Rank: **Nice**. New place.

246. **Orlyplein roof garden**; Orlyplein, 52.389351, 4.838347. Sloterdijk's old rooftop bus station, above two car parks and the railway, became a 1,566 m² garden in 2012 to 2014, its plants fed with stored rainwater through an energy-free capillary system; Atlas Obscura's only Nieuw-West entry. Leads: https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/orlyplein-roof-garden ; https://landezine-award.com/orlysquare-amsterdam/ . Caveats: nothing dated after 2017; the station district is being rebuilt; `check: Still there?`. Rank: **Okay**. Merge into 233 (preferred) or new place.

247. **Train Lodge**; Changiweg 121, 52.388734, 4.841460. A hostel in Swiss sleeping cars from the Zürich to Rome night train, parked on a siding beside Sloterdijk station since about 2016 (132 beds). Leads: https://trainlodge.com/ ; OSM node 9142767672. Status: trading 2026. Caveats: a commercial hostel; outside only for non-guests. Rank: **Okay**. New place (or a Look for in 233).

248. **Kapkar/GTO-B10 (Frank Havermans, 2019)**; Basisweg, 52.391953, 4.838926. A bright red, angular shelter you can step into, made by folding pieces of the floor plan of a demolished 1970s Sloterdijk office into "a vehicle that landed" on a leftover patch between slip roads and viaducts. Leads: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapkar/GTO-B10 ; designboom (Jul 2019); Commons photo 2024. Status: standing in 2024. Rank: **Okay**. New place.
