# Nieuw-West scout, round A (items 101–154)

Scouted 2026-11-23. Sweeps 2 (public art and walls) and 3 (war and memory) of the West standard, done for every gebied of Nieuw-West: Geuzenveld-Slotermeer, Osdorp, De Aker/Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten, Slotervaart and Sloterdijk Nieuw-West. Nothing is written yet: the user picks.

I have 54 candidates, numbered 101 to 154: **34 possible new places, 16 merges into existing places, 3 that can go either way** (a new place, or a merge into a round 1 item that is not drafted yet). One is suggested as Amazing (112, *Chile vencerá*). Sweep 2 has 43 items, sweep 3 has 11.

Each item: name; location (address, lat,lng, gebied/buurt); hook; why it fits; lead source; caveats; suggested rank; new place or merge.

**Sources gone through this round:**
- **City public-art database (Kunstwacht / buitenkunstamsterdam.nl):** the full list through the site's own API (1,749 works citywide), point-checked to the borough: **331 works with coordinates in Nieuw-West** (Slotervaart 112, Geuzenveld-Slotermeer 87, Osdorp 67, De Aker/Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten 44, Sloterdijk Nieuw-West 21) plus 76 without coordinates whose address is in the borough (mostly SAMA murals and Westgaarde items). All read at title and text level; the 15 "Kunstroute Osdorp" texts are inside these records. Links are written as `buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/<id>` with the API id; the site is a JavaScript app, so if a link opens the wrong work, search by title.
- **City wall-art register (maps.amsterdam.nl, "Wandkunst"):** **66 works in Nieuw-West** (Geuzenveld-Slotermeer 31, Slotervaart 23, Osdorp 11, De Aker 1), all read. Most are 1950s and 1960s entrance mosaics, tile tableaux and brick reliefs on the garden-city housing.
- **Street Art Cities:** the marker feed for the Amsterdam zone, point-checked: **28 markers in Nieuw-West** (23 active, 4 removed, 1 other), almost all SAMA walls in Slotermeer and Geuzenveld. Used selectively: SAMA is already a place.
- **OpenStreetMap:** `tourism=artwork`, `historic=*`, `memorial=*`, `military=*`, `building=bunker` in a bounding box over the borough, point-checked: **157 features**.
- **nl.wikipedia:** "Lijst van beelden in Amsterdam Nieuw-West" (about 300 rows, every row read) and the single-work articles behind it (more than 40 read for status and hooks).
- **Stadscuratorium:** the whole online collection through its WordPress API (147 entries, 21 mention Nieuw-West, all read); its advice on Rembo; its report "Stad als canvas" (24 Nov 2025).
- **4en5mei.nl monument database:** six distance-sorted searches from Sloten, Slotervaart-Zuid, Osdorp, Overtoomse Veld, Slotermeer and Sloterdijk (121 unique records, every detail page downloaded and point-checked; 106 fall outside the borough on every side, in West, Zuid, Centrum, Westpoort and the neighbouring towns, so nothing in Nieuw-West can be missing). **14 records lie in Nieuw-West**, all read.
- **Art routes:** "Kunstroute Osdorp" (Kunstwacht texts); the SAMA route (already a place); the borough has no other current art route online.
- **Local history:** slotenoudosdorp.nl (Sloten and Oud Osdorp), Ons Amsterdam (Siegerpark), Rodi Nieuw-West (4 May 2026 ceremonies, Open Monumentendag 2026), NH Nieuws, Het Parool (by search). Joods Monument was not gone through address by address: almost all of Nieuw-West was built after 1950, and the few pre-war addresses (Sloten village, the Osdorperweg ribbon) are a round B task.
- **Memory beyond WWII:** searches for migrant, colonial, Cold War, disaster and recent-war memorials (results in sweep 3 and under "Checked and dropped").

## Sweep 2: Public art and walls

### Geuzenveld-Slotermeer

101. **De Bouwvakarbeider (Jan Havermans, 1962)**; Burgemeester De Vlugtlaan at the Burgemeester Eliasstraat, 52.380789, 4.827622 (Slotermeer-Noordoost). A builder sits resting and looks with approval at what he has built: the city's thank-you to the men who put up the Westelijke Tuinsteden, unveiled on 26 January 1962, Havermans's 70th birthday, two years before his death (the plaster was shown at the Stedelijk in 1959). Fits: the garden city's only monument to its own builders. Look for, about 600 m east on the north railing of the Hannie Schaftbrug (bridge 601): the bronze plaque recording Queen Juliana opening Tuinstad Slotermeer on 7 October 1952 through a gate of six boys and six girls. Lead: nl.wikipedia "De bouwvakarbeider"; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/853. Status: present (Commons photos 4 Sep 2022); the Juliana plaque unverified after May 2016 (not 27 Oct 1950 as Kunstwacht has it). Rank: Okay. New place.

102. **Entrance mosaics of Slotermeer (Forrer, Both, "Phoenix")**; Speelmanstraat 2-82 (pin 52.379466, 4.830716) and Freek Oxstraat, Slotermeer-Noordoost; Burgemeester Cramergracht 1-98, 52.373356, 4.832469 (Slotermeer-Zuidoost). In 1953 to 1956 the city gave the new blocks art at the front door: Raymond Both's seven mosaics of birds, sun, moon and a hand in the Speelmanstraat entrances, Theo Forrer's "woman, child, farmer and musician" in the Freek Oxstraat, and on the north end wall of Piet Zanstra's gallery flat (one of the first two modern gallery flats in the city) Both's "Phoenix" in red, blue, black and white stoneware: the bird rising from the soil dredged out of the Sloterplas. Fits: a small, findable everyday-art walk in the oldest part of the garden city. Lead: wall-art register; nl.wikipedia "Phoenix (Both)"; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/888. Status: present (Commons 7 Oct 2018 and Aug 2018); the Speelmanstraat was renovated lightly and renumbered, keeping the mosaics. Caveat: the Burma Railway story about Forrer was not confirmed and is left out. Rank: Nice. New place (one place, pinned in the Speelmanstraat, with Phoenix and 103 as Look for lines).

103. **Lex Horn's tile tableaux "Vogels, dieren, vissen"**; Anthony Moddermanstraat 206, 214, 222, 52.384117, 4.815248 (Slotermeer-West). Three tile pictures of birds, animals and fish by the city's busiest monumental artist of the 1950s. Lead: wall-art register; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/839. Status: unverified (no dated photo found). Rank: Okay. Merge into 102 as a Look for line.

104. **Grote man, kleine man (Stephan Balkenhol, 1999)**; M.J. Granpré Molièreplein, 52.374567, 4.820089 (Slotermeer-Zuidoost). An ordinary man in a white shirt on a stool where a hero would stand on a plinth, with a small man climbing his leg: Balkenhol's anti-monument, placed in Rob Krier's neo-traditional Noorderhof, the one enclave of pitched roofs and squares built into the modernist garden city. Fits: a German star sculptor in a corner nobody visits, with a built argument around it. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Grote man, kleine man"; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/900. Status: present (Commons 27 Oct 2018, nothing newer). Rank: Nice. New place (the Noorderhof itself is a sweep 6 lead).

105. **Trimmers (Martie van der Loo)**; bridge 678 over the President Allendelaan, Sloterpark, 52.368575, 4.813186 (Slotermeer-Zuidoost). 25 steel joggers in five poses and two dogs run along the outside of the bridge railings. The 1983 originals (for 25 years of the Westelijke Tuinsteden) were vandalised, taken away for repair and destroyed by mistake; Van der Loo made them again, with her own dog Chet (after Chet Baker) and Pacha. The dog is the piece thieves love most and has been replaced most often. Fits: a joke you see from below, with a story. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Trimmers"; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/913. Status: unverified (newest photos 1 Jun 2018; railings replaced late 2016). Rank: Nice. New place (or a Look for in "Sloterplas: the lake dug for sand").

106. **De sleutel ligt onder de deurmat (Martijn Sandberg, 2012)**; stairs of the De Zilverling tower, Dr. H. Colijnstraat, 52.380024, 4.806809 (Geuzenveld). The sentence "the key is under the doormat" is spelled in concrete pixel blocks under the nosings of the stone stair, and can only be read standing square in front of it: a nod to the post-war habit of trusting the neighbours. Fits: hidden text you have to find the right spot for. Lead: nl.wikipedia; Het Parool 5 Aug 2022; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/928. Status: present (Commons 6 Oct 2022). Rank: Nice. New place.

107. **270 degrees (Xuanhong Huang, 2016)**; Nico Broekhuysenweg at the Ma Braunpad, 52.375778, 4.790017 (Geuzenveld). Three periscope-like concrete bird hides looking out over the Osdorper Binnenpolder, one of the last medieval field patterns around the city; sister hides elsewhere in the Tuinen van West are by Chrysovergi and Oduber. Fits: art you can sit inside, on the green edge. Lead: Kunstwacht; Commons (with info board). Status: present (Commons 14 Apr 2022). Rank: Okay. New place (or a Look for in a Tuinen van West green place in round B).

### Osdorp

112. **Chile vencerá (Brigada Ramona Parra, 1981)**; behind Osdorpplein 815 ("The Hub", with the Nieuw-West stadsloket), 52.358720, 4.807186 (Osdorp-Oost). In 1981 Chilean refugees led by Jorge "Kata" Núñez, a leader of the communist mural brigade Ramona Parra, painted a 20 x 3.4 m anti-Pinochet mural in a few days. It disappeared behind later building, turned up again in October 2018 when the site was cleared for The Hub, and was restored in 2022 by Núñez himself with Juan Heinsohn. It is now in a non-public corridor on the wall of the neighbouring building; the developer made a viewing window so the public can see it. Fits: a buried political mural that came back, 1.5 km from the Allende monument; the borough's strongest art story this round. Lead: Stadscuratorium report "Stad als canvas" (24 Nov 2025); MRP Development news page on the restoration; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/1187. Status: present (Nov 2025). Caveats: how to see it (window position, opening hours of the stadsloket) needs a site check; the "seen through a bike-cellar door" version is unconfirmed. Rank: Amazing. New place (link it from "Allende monument and President Allendelaan").

113. **Patrimonium brick reliefs, Blomwijckerpad (1960)**; Blomwijckerpad / Witte Klok 1 (Lex Horn, 52.360558, 4.801294), Cromme Camp 1 (Dick Zwier) and Groenpad 1 (Berend Hendriks), Osdorp-Oost. Three coloured brick reliefs on the gable ends of a Protestant housing association's blocks, each a Bible text: Horn's "do your work in the field first, then build your house" (Proverbs 24:27, later called "Ladders"), Zwier's lazy man (Proverbs 24:33-34) and Hendriks's Psalm 128 with Patrimonium's name. Horn's and Hendriks's works were mixed up for years. Fits: preaching walls around the Van Eyck playground. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Zonder titel (Lex Horn)"; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/659. Status: present (Commons 22 Jul 2020). Rank: Nice. Merge into "Van Eyck playground, Witte Klok" (same block), or a new place.

### De Aker/Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten

121. **Siegerpark and its Stedelijk sculptures**; Sloterweg 773 (also a bridge from the Joseph Schumpeterstraat), 52.340700, 4.819150 (Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten). Wilhelm Sieger, director of the Amsterdam quinine factory and a keen beekeeper, laid out a bee park here in 1936; in 1950 his family sold it cheaply to the city on condition it stay a park for ever, and the A4 then took half of it. In 1998 six works came out of the Stedelijk Museum's storage, partly to clear room for the museum's extension: Hildo Krop's "Twee kinderen", Jan Bronner's "Diakenhuismannetje", Etienne-Martin's "Demeure 4 (Lanleff)" (which used to stand in front of the Stedelijk's Sandberg wing and was too heavy for the wet soil, so it stands at the firm Sloterweg entrance), André Volten's "Constructie DIN 20", Bryan Hunt's "First Falls II", and Shamai Haber's "Compositie voor een tuin", which Sandberg had traded away in 1960 and which came back as a gift. Director Rudi Fuchs made the park keeper promise to care for them "like a good father". Fits: a museum's sculpture garden that nobody knows, in a park with a condition in its deed. Lead: Ons Amsterdam "Siegerpark? Nooit van gehoord!" (9 Jun 2004); nl.wikipedia "Siegerpark", "Compositie voor een tuin"; Kunstwacht ids 721 to 726. Status: present (Commons 12 Sep 2022 for Haber, Krop, Etienne-Martin; Hunt last photographed 2011, Volten undated). Rank: Nice (close to Amazing). New place; "Villa Maupertuus and Riecker State" keeps its mention.

122. **The apes under the Oude Haagsebrug ("The circus has left town... We just stuck around")**; Ringvaart bridge abutment, Oude Haagseweg, 52.331781, 4.806747 (Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten). In 2008 an anonymous duo painted ten black-and-white apes, illegally, under the bridge, and locals renamed it the Apenbrug. When the bridge was demolished in January 2024 residents asked for the apes back, so the duo (Pipsqueak Was Here) were commissioned: a signed version with five apes, one per panel, visible since August 2024, with Jan van der Ploeg's De Stijl colour planes on the piers. Fits: graffiti that a neighbourhood won back. Lead: nl.wikipedia "The circus has left town...We just stuck around"; slotenoudosdorp.nl "Viaductschilderingen onder de A4 bij Sloten". Status: present (photos Sep 2025). Caveats: the 2011 street-art prize is single-sourced; the "father with dementia" story was not found anywhere and is left out. Rank: Nice. New place.

### Slotervaart

129. **Jan Wolkers's woman and child, and the Sanquin front garden**; Plesmanlaan 125, 52.350869, 4.823114 (Slotervaart-Zuid). In front of the 1959 wing of the former Central Laboratory of the Blood Transfusion Service a copper stooping woman holds a child; according to Kunstwacht the model was Wolkers's wife Annemarie Nauta, who later became the model for Olga in *Turks Fruit*. Beside it: Piet Donk's 1959 emblem relief of the laboratory and Yvonne Kracht's "Spiegelkubus". Fits: a famous writer's early sculpture with a link to his most famous book, at a blood bank. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Zonder titel (Jan Wolkers)"; Kunstwacht ids 756, 758, 747. Status: present (Commons 4 Nov 2018). Caveats: "started the Sanquin collection" not confirmed; Bert de Laaf's "Maan over Ameland" is in an inner court, not visible. Rank: Nice. New place.

130. **Karel Appel's glass wall, former Slotervaartziekenhuis**; Louwesweg 6, 52.347744, 4.824811 (Slotervaart-Zuid). A 1976 glass work by Appel in the central hall of the hospital that went bankrupt in 2018. All 195 artworks then passed to the city; the Appel wall was one of five too hard to move, and 24 works lent to the hospital had gone missing (police report). The building reopened as care centre Het Slotervaart (2023, MVSA roof 2024). Look for: Margot Zanstra's "Fluxuous II" in the pond (photo 2015). Fits: a CoBrA master in a hospital hall, and a bankruptcy art story. Lead: NH Nieuws (Feb 2020); buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/838. Status: unverified since 2020 (Commons in situ 4 Nov 2018). Rank: Nice if still there and the hall is public; Okay otherwise. New place after a site check.

131. **Queen Wilhelmina (Nico Onkenhout, 1968)**; Koningin Wilhelminaplein 13 (World Fashion Centre), 52.355219, 4.841014 (Westlandgracht). The garment-trade centre ordered a Wilhelmina, partly for what she had meant to its Jewish members; Onkenhout made her "a bit of a general" with a hat modelled on a soldier's helmet. Alderman Polak thought the city had enough Wilhelminas (one on horseback was coming for the Rokin) and refused it a place outdoors, so the 30,000 kg dolomite queen went inside. In 1993 the building wanted rid of her and nearly sent her to the Overloon war museum; she ended up outside after all. A Jan Campert verse is on the back. Fits: an unwanted queen. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Koningin Wilhelmina (Onkenhout)". Status: present (Commons 25 May 2019). Rank: Nice. New place (or merge into "Former Stibbe coat factory (Koningin Wilhelminaplein)", next door, as the garment district's story).

132. **GVB Garage West: Jan Bons's "Vogelvlucht" and Lex Horn's strike mural**; Jan Tooropstraat 647, 52.371136, 4.837391 (Overtoomse Veld). Outside, Bons's relief was drawn upright in 1955 and mounted lying down in 1958 (the specification drawings show it); the building is still the city bus garage, now charging electric buses. Inside, in the staff hall, Lex Horn's 1959 mural "Ter herdenking van de Februaristaking 1941" (17 x 5 m) honours the tram and bus men who struck first in February 1941; 4en5mei lists it as a war memorial (pin there is 550 m off, at today's no. 107). Het Parool reported that redevelopment plans threaten the mural (date not found). Fits: a strike memorial hidden in a working garage, behind a sideways relief. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Vogelvlucht (Jan Bons)", "Garage West"; 4en5mei "Februaristaking (1)". Status: relief present (Commons Aug 2021); mural unverified and indoors only. Rank: Nice. New place (sweeps 2 and 3).

133. **Metamorfosen (Harry van Kruiningen, 1960), former Cartesius Lyceum**; Piet Mondriaanstraat 140, 52.366036, 4.837106 (Overtoomse Veld). Two glass mosaics of about 10 m² each on the side walls of the entrance canopy, after Ovid; restored in 2020 at the urging of Heemschut's monumental-art group. A 1962 press photo of Queen Juliana's school visit shows one. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Metamorfosen (Van Kruiningen)"; Kunstwacht ids 803, 805. Status: present (restored 2020). Caveat: Hildo Krop's oak "Descartes" moved with the school to the Frederik Hendrikplantsoen (borough West) in 2011. Rank: Okay. New place.

134. **Studio Job's "Iconic Facades" (2009)**; Jan Tooropstraat / Jan Evertsenstraat, 52.369507, 4.836645 (Overtoomse Veld). More than 50 concrete relief panels, about 800 m², on five housing blocks: Studio Job's emblem-like ornaments cast into the facades of a social-housing renewal. ### Sloterdijk Nieuw-West

139. **Bornhout 8: Choreografie in staal, a Cold War shelter and the city's archaeology depot**; Bornhout 8, 52.394040, 4.791100. Margot Zanstra, a dancer with the Nationale Ballet before she turned to sculpture in 1966, made a 16 m red-and-white steel "choreography" for passing drivers: the planes seem to change position as you drive by. It was moved here, to the city's impounded-bicycle depot, for the Westrandweg (about 2009 to 2012). The same site has an atomic shelter built in 1984 for about 100 staff of the city works department, never finished, and the city's archaeological depot (more than 5,000 crates); both opened on Open Monumentendag, 12 September 2026. Fits: a landmark, a Cold War relic and a hidden store in one industrial yard. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Choreografie in staal"; Rodi Nieuw-West (Open Monumentendag 2026); buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/1028. Status: sculpture present (Commons 2 Sep 2018; register 2026). Caveat: shelter and depot open only on special days. Rank: Nice. New place (sweeps 2, 3 and 5).

140. **Banpaal Spaarnwouderdijk (1624)**; by the pumping station along the Westrandweg, 52.392016, 4.769207. The stump of the 1624 boundary post that marked how far Amsterdam's banishment reached on the road to Haarlem; Rembrandt drew it, already headless, in his etching "Landscape with an Obelisk". Taken away in 1867, it toured the city (the Stedelijk's garden, Beatrixoord in the Oosterpark) until a nature group got it back in 1999, about 150 m from its old spot, which is now under a postal sorting centre. Fits: the twin of the Sloten banpaal, with a Rembrandt link, at the far edge of the city. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Banpaal Spaarnwouderdijk"; Stadscuratorium "Banpaal Spaarndammerdijk (gesprek met Stephan Keppel)". Status: present (Commons 7 Jul 2020). Rank: Nice. New place (a sweep 4 find; link with "Banpaal of Sloten").

141. **Lokroep (Giny Vos, 2006)**; Stadskantoor Westpoortbeheer, Theemsweg 26, 52.389735, 4.811166. 6,500 LEDs on the facade spell a new word every 20 seconds, playing on the place and the work inside (WILD WEST, ZOUTWERK, MISTROOST); the building is the city's green-maintenance and road-salt base. Lead: ginyvos.nl; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/2783. Status: unverified after 2010; only after dark. Rank: Okay. New place after an evening check.

142. **Voetbal (1975)**; edge of Sportpark Spieringhorn by the Haarlemmerweg, 52.385481, 4.827869. A giant concrete football, unveiled by CPN alderman and resistance veteran Harry Verheij. Maker and reason unknown. Lead: Wikidata Q118138878; Commons (12 Apr 2015). Status: unverified since 2015. Rank: Okay. New place only if the story is found.

143. **Brettensuite (Ad Nouwens) and Deur naar het verleden (Pieter Boekschooten)**; Lange Bretten and Seineweg, 52.385210, 4.828132. A 20-part work along the Bretten and a "door to the past". Lead: Kunstwacht ids 1025 and the Brettensuite record. Status: unverified. Rank: Okay. Merge into "Lange Bretten" as Look for lines.

## Sweep 3: War and memory

### Geuzenveld-Slotermeer

144. **O, Kora, dochter van Zeus (Johan Limpers, 1940, cast 1969)**; Arondeusstraat at the Koen Limpergstraat, 52.383561, 4.825878 (Slotermeer-Noordoost). Limpers won the 1940 Prix de Rome with this archaic Greek girl; three years later he joined the attack on the Amsterdam population register (27 March 1943) and was shot at Overveen on 1 July 1943. The bronze cast was placed in 1969 on a plain plinth, not the original one with reliefs of growing and dying plants, on a street named after his fellow attacker Willem Arondeus, opposite the Johan Limpersstraat. Fits: a prize-winning work as its maker's own memorial, among streets named after his comrades. Lead: 4en5mei "Korè"; nl.wikipedia "Johan Limpers"; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/871. Status: unverified (4en5mei notes weathering; no recent photo). Caveat: the 4en5mei inscription says "1944"; the execution was in 1943. Rank: Nice. New place.

145. **De Antifascist (Leo Braat, 1936, placed 1966)**; Willem Kraanstraat, 52.380731, 4.824359 (Slotermeer-Noordoost). A bronze bust that Braat made in 1936, probably of a fighter in the Spanish Civil War ("to the unknown soldier" at the shoulder), cast and set up in 1966 as the memorial for Willem Kraan, one of the organisers of the February Strike, executed in 1942. Fits: a Spanish Civil War sculpture reused for a Dutch communist hero. Lead: 4en5mei "De Antifascist"; buitenkunstamsterdam.nl/kunstwerken/bekijk/863. Status: unverified. Rank: Nice. New place (or one place with 144).

146. **Jan Postma relief and the Reina Prinsen Geerligs plaque**; Jan Postmahof 1 (52.380214, 4.831856) and Reina Prinsen Geerligsstraat (52.379113, 4.833385), Slotermeer-Noordoost. Frits Sieger's stone of armed men in a boat for the CPN resistance leader Postma (shot 1944); and C.B.M. Otten's bronze plaque (unveiled 27 April 1970, her parents present) carrying a poem by Reina Prinsen Geerligs, the Semarang-born CS-6 courier shot in Sachsenhausen in 1943, whose parents founded a literary prize in her name; the Mondriaan Lyceum adopted it. Lead: 4en5mei "monument voor Jan Postma", "monument voor Reina Prinsen Geerligs". Status: both unverified. Rank: Okay. Merge into "Resistance fighters' faces (Verzetsstrijdersbuurt)" as Look for lines.

147. **Monument aan de Haarlemmerweg (Saskia Bongers, 2019)**; Haarlemmerweg between the Burgemeester Eliasstraat and Fockstraat footbridges, 52.384526, 4.832213 (Slotermeer-Noordoost). On 15 December 1944 P.J. Elias and H.J. and M. Verkuyl were shot here in reprisal for sabotage on the railway to Haarlem. A white wooden cross stood before the farm Vredelust from 1947, and another from 1964 when the farm went; the N200 road works moved it in 2017, and on 15 December 2019 Bongers's Corten cross with three uprights and three sweetgum trees was unveiled with an eyewitness, Corrie van den Boogaard-Vlug, present. Still one of the borough's six 4 May ceremony sites (2026). Fits: a living execution site on a main road. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Monument aan de Haarlemmerweg"; 4en5mei; Rodi (4 May 2026). Status: present (2023 and 2026). Caveat: Kunstwacht's pin (52.385096, 4.825765) is out of date. Rank: Nice. New place.

148. **Eendracht (Aart Lamberts, 1980)**; Aalbersestraat at the Ruys de Beerenbrouckstraat, 52.382461, 4.798095 (Geuzenveld). Three bronze figures grown together as one block of "unbending" resistance; Lamberts made one figure first, found it too weak, and liked that the sports park behind is also called Eendracht. Unveiled on 5 May 1980. Lead: 4en5mei "Eendracht". Status: unverified. Rank: Okay. Either: a Look for in 110 / RISE, or new.

### Osdorp

149. **Dwaze Moedersplein: "Verzetsvrouw" (Auke Hettema, 1990) and Nico Blom's stone (1986)**; Dwaze Moedersplein, 52.353675, 4.807179 (Osdorp-Oost). The square was named on 1 November 1985 for the Madres de Plaza de Mayo, who walked in silence in Buenos Aires asking what happened to their disappeared children, and the streets around it are named after the virtues they stand for. Liesbeth den Uyl and Mies Bouhuys campaigned for the mothers in the Netherlands. Fits: Latin American memory in Osdorp, together with 112 and the Allende monument. Lead: BAG naming record 0363300000005830; nl.wikipedia; Kunstwacht ids 617, 616. Status: square present; the two works unverified since 2015. Rank: Nice. New place.

150. **Vredesteken Osdorp (Heppe de Moor, 1986)**; Hoekenesgracht, 52.358454, 4.798755 (Osdorp-Midden). A pink stone form in a pond, "a mirror of society without war", made because the new borough wanted a place for 4 May; Kunstwacht files a stone "Om nooit te vergeten" with it. Ceremonies moved away after wreaths were vandalised in 2006; the Indies remembrance of 2026 still took wreaths there by boat. Lead: 4en5mei "Vredesteken Osdorp"; Rodi. Status: unverified. Caveat: sources disagree on whether the 4 May ceremony at the Sloterplas by the Meervaart is at this work or another. Rank: Okay. New place or drop.

151. **Kars and Bierman stone: the American pilot's tile (update to round 1 item 32)**; Osdorperweg at the Lutkemeerweg, 52.368907, 4.780558. The 1945 obelisk for Jan Kars and Piet Bierman now also has a tile, in Dutch and English, for 2nd Lt Dover Chalmond Fleming Jr., USAAF, whose P-47 Thunderbolt was shot down near Amsterdam on 8 October 1943. Still a 4 May site (Osdorperweg 632, 2026). Lead: 4en5mei "Monument aan de Osdorperweg". Rank: as round 1 (Nice). Add to item 32 when it is drafted.

### De Aker/Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten

152. **Verzetsheldenbuurt memorial and murals, Joop Woortmanplein**; 52.352127, 4.783678 (De Aker). Nico Blom's group (2000) of three polyester market gardeners in clogs leaning on a wall (OSM "Drie Boertjes"), at the heart of a 1990s neighbourhood whose streets are named after resistance people, among them Irawan Soejono of the Indonesian resistance group in Leiden; plus Metin Bagirgan and Irina Ermolaeva's paintings on the square, which link Woortman's illegal posters to street art. One of the six 4 May sites (2026). Lead: 4en5mei "verzetsmonument"; Kunstwacht ids 649, 1033. Status: present (4 May 2026 ceremony). Rank: Okay. New place.

153. **Plaque in the Sloterkerk**; Osdorperweg, Sloten. For two members of the Sloten Christian youth club: sailor Arie Oudshoorn, lost in the Battle of the Java Sea on 27 February 1942, and Jan Kars (see 151). Lead: 4en5mei "plaquette in de Sloterkerk". Status: unverified; inside the church. Rank: Okay. Merge into "Sloterkerk" as a Look for line.

### Slotervaart

154. **Ru Paré plaque and Hugo Kaagman's "Mini Wereld"**; Chris Lebeaustraat 4, 52.362515, 4.838171 (Overtoomse Veld). A black granite plaque on the former Ru Paré school: the Hague painter Henrica "Ru" Paré (1896–1972) found hiding places for 52 Jewish children, all of whom survived, and carried food and false papers in a paint box with a false bottom; Yad Vashem honoured her in 1968. The school closed in 2014 and is now the Ru Paré Community, with her paintings inside and a yearly Ru Paré lecture since 2021. Hugo Kaagman's "Mini Wereld" is on the same building (status unverified). Look for: Hildo Krop's Albert Plesman plaque, Marius Bauerstraat 2. Lead: nl.wikipedia "Ru Paré"; Kunstwacht id 794. Status: present (Commons 17 Nov 2023). Rank: Nice. New place.
