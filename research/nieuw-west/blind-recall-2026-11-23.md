# Nieuw-West blind recall (2026-11-23)

Independent sweep of stadsdeel Nieuw-West for off-the-A-list sights, built only from outside sources (iamsterdam neighbourhood pages and walking routes, Van Eesteren Museum walks and stories, Rodi/Westkrant, NH Nieuws/AT5, NOS, DutchNews, In Your Pocket, travel and design blogs). No Deep Cities data, CLI or research files were consulted.

Area split follows api.data.amsterdam.nl `ggwgebieden` (GF06 to GF10). Each place was assigned by geocoding its address (PDOK locatieserver / OSM) against the `buurten` API. Notable boundary results:
- Sloterpark north and west (Sloterparkbad, Heemtuin, Groot Landschap) and the Noordoever with the Van Eesteren Museum fall in **Geuzenveld-Slotermeer**; the Oostoever falls in **Slotervaart**.
- Rembrandtpark is in **Slotervaart** (Nieuw-West), not West.
- Molen De 1200 Roe (Haarlemmerweg 701) is in Geuzenveld-Slotermeer (Jan de Louterbuurt).
- Tuincentrum Osdorp (Osdorperweg 247) and B. Amsterdam (Johan Huizingalaan 763) fall in De Aker/Sloten/Nieuw-Sloten; Piet Wiedijkpark falls in Osdorp.
- The old village of Sloterdijk and its church lie in stadsdeel West (EA03), so they are excluded. Gerbrandypark is in Slotermeer, not Slotervaart as one blog says. Lokaal van de Stad (Westlandgracht) is in Zuid.

Ranking within each area is by how often and how strongly the sources recommend a place: several independent recommendations first, then single strong mentions (news features, monument nominations, guided walk stops). Atlas Obscura has nothing for Nieuw-West, Spotted by Locals' only nearby entry is in Zuid, and no useful Reddit threads came up. So the iamsterdam neighbourhood pages, the Van Eesteren Museum and Rodi carry more weight here than they would elsewhere.
