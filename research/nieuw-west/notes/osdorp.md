## Status checks (latest news)

- Sint-Lucaskerk: last Mass 26 May 2019; sold to SGI Nederland in 2020, opened as Ikeda Centrum (2022). Title carries the new name.
- Pauluskerk: main church of parish De Vier Evangelisten since 2020, still in use.
- Notweg 32: converted by Stadgenoot into 30 youth flats plus community rooms (2022–2023).
- Notweg 38: business centre / broedplaats since 2009, monument 2022.
- Kampschooltje: Terragon Nature Lab (current website lists Osdorperweg 665).
- Brug 721 was named Mance Postbrug on 3 December 2025 (nl.wikipedia). A fatal shooting on this bridge on 1–2 January 2026 is on nl.wikipedia; deliberately left out of the text.
- Oud-Osdorp cemetery: the parish of Halfweg (owner) wanted to move the graves and sell the land in the 2010s; descendants refused; a 2021 Parool piece (paywalled, not read) reports residents and relatives opposing villas next to the cemetery. Outcome unknown, so not mentioned in the text.
- Osdorperweg 658-660: no designation decision found as of November 2026; the text says "still in procedure at the time of writing". Worth re-checking before import.
- Farms: current use not verified beyond the register (Bij Goedvinding text hedged to "when listed in 2009"). Rust en Vrede still agricultural per amsterdamopdekaart (Jan 2026).

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Teacher's house date: city record says 1860, amsterdamopdekaart "circa 1860", the register text says unknown but probably from around the 1879 map (14 years after the 1865 drainage). Used "c. 1865–1879". nl.wikipedia (Lutkemeerweg) wrongly says the house was designed by Publieke Werken with the 1929 school.
- Deckers tomb: Aleida Schaap's birth year is 1842 in the register, 1847 on amsterdamopdekaart, and the Parool says she died at 36. Ages left out. The Parool also muddles who was buried in 1969 (register lists Isabella Droog-Deckers, d. 1969); Jeroen Buve described only as "a descendant".
- Poort van Constant: OSM artwork node used for the pin (52.368379, 4.800243), 15 m from the register point.
- Salomonson square: built 1960 (nl.wikipedia list) vs 1962 (register; Nieuwe Instituut says designed 1962). Used "c. 1962".
- Lucaskerk: register year 1964; consecrated May 1965. Used 1963–1965.
- Notweg 32 architect: register says Publieke Werken / B.J. Odink; amsterdamopdekaart adds A.N. & N. Schippers (The Hague). Both given.
- Kampschooltje: register says built 1954; a photo of 26 July 1955 calls it "shortly after construction". Used 1954.
- Hint for the 1100 Roe "pumping station" and for 658-660 "farm" were both wrong (see table).

## Walking / cycling routes

1. **Oud Osdorp by bike** (the polder edge, about 5 km): De 1100 Roe → Poort van Constant → Oud-Osdorp cemetery and the Deckers tomb → Rust en Vrede farm → Schoolmeesterswoning → Dekeukeleire double house → Kampschooltje → Bij Goedvinding farm → Tijd is Geld and De Melkweg. Theme: the village that lost its name to the new town; links well with the green slice's Lutkemeerpolder item.
2. **Tuinstad Osdorp on foot** (AUP modernism, about 4 km): Garage Notweg → Former Heimansschool → Sint-Lucaskerk → Pauluskerk → Salomonson shopping square → Slebos and Sterenberg bridges, with a tram 17 ride out to the Hangbrugmaisonettes at the Dijkgraafplein.
