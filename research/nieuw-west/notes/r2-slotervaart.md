## Site checks (results)

- **130 Appel wall**: still there. Het Slotervaart's own art page (read Nov 2026) says it hangs on the ground floor in the Douwe Egberts café; the café is the building's public living room. The pond work *Fluxuous II* and Makkink's salamander (the "hagedis" AT5 mentions as hard to move) are on the same page. Nice.
- **132 Horn mural**: no news after Het Parool, 2 Feb 2020 ("bedreigd", in the "voormalige kantine"). The garage is in use (NH Nieuws, 9 Sep 2026). Flagged.
- **141 Lokroep**: running; Giny Vos posted a video in November 2026.
- **142 Voetbal**: left out (see below).

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- **Wolkers pin**: Kunstwacht 756 pins the statue 230 m south of the building (52.348763, 4.824886); Wikidata and the Plesmanlaan 125 address point agree on 52.350869, 4.823114, and a 2018 Commons photo shows the statue directly below Donk's relief. Used Wikidata.
- **Wolkers model**: Kunstwacht says "Olga uit Turks Fruit stond model"; nl.wikipedia, citing Kunstwacht, names Annemarie Nauta, his wife at the time. Text follows nl.wikipedia.
- **"Started the Sanquin collection"** (scout caveat): confirmed by VBCN (Sanquin's collection page): the 1959 purchases of Wolkers's "Moeder met kind" and Corneille's "Le chant des îles".
- **Vogelvlucht date**: nl.wikipedia (Jan Bons) says 1959; the Vogelvlucht article and Kunstwacht say installed and unveiled December 1958. Used 1958.
- **Horn mural year**: 1959 from Het Parool's photo caption (2020); 4en5mei gives no year.
- **Lokroep year**: 2006 (artist's site, Parool and NRC 2006 reviews, artist's 2026 post) vs 2009 (Kunstwacht text). Used 2006.
- **Choreografie in staal**: "circa 1983" (nl.wikipedia infobox); the move for the Westrandweg is undated in the sources, so the text gives no year.
- **Ru Paré**: the scout's "all of whom survived" and "false bottom" in the paint box are not in the sources found; text says she found addresses for 52 children and turned her paint box into a case of supplies. School closing: 2014 (nl.wikipedia); community opened 2016 (Architectenweb). The stone's unveiling date was not found.
- **Rembo**: the hand was knocked off before the Stadscuratorium advice of 21 Sep 2021; the artist posted "REMBO being repaired" (Instagram reel, spring 2023), and a comment there says the right hand was off again. Whether the repair was in bronze is not documented: flagged `check: Facts`.
- **Metamorfosen**: the Ovid reference (XIV 550, the ships of Aeneas turned into sea nymphs) was checked against the Latin text; "5535" is unexplained in all sources, and the text just reports it.
