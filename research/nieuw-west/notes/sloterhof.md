Where I didn't follow the hints:
- M43: the hint offered splitting off "water garden + restaurant". The water garden runs the full 800 m and is the setting for the flats, and the restaurant alone is thin (it was offered for let as a horeca space on funda in 2026, so it may be empty). Both went into the main Sloterhof place. The tower got its own place: separate address, its own nl.wikipedia article, the Goeting sgraffito and the leaking-facade story, and it sits 800 m west of the main pin.
- M45: Brug 705 and Brug 675 are about 650 m apart and unrelated (a road viaduct and a park footbridge), so I split them.
- Not left out: none.

## Ranks

Nice: Sloterhof, Lelylaan viaduct, Former Caltex garage, Aviodome. Okay: Sloterhof tower, Brug 675, Slotervaart canal and its bridges, Gemaal Halfweg.
Needs a decision: Sloterhof could be **Amazing** (one of the best-preserved post-war ensembles in the city, prefab blocks on stilts over the water). I kept it at Nice to stay within the 1-in-20 limit for Amazing.

## Status checks (Nov 2026)

- Nothing is gone.
- Aviodome: running as Circa Amsterdam, with events booked for 2026 (circa.nl, event sites).
- Garage Johan Huizingalaan 91: the building stands. Directories still list KAV Autoverhuur at the address, but I couldn't confirm that from a primary source, so the text says "for years the garage was used by KAV".
- Sloterhof: westersite.nl (28 May 2025) reported that a renovation "kan eind 2025/begin 2026 van start", following a D66 advice in the district committee. I found no details on its scope or whether it started, so it's left out of the text.
- Restaurant (Johan Huizingalaan 177): currently advertised for let as a horeca space (fundainbusiness, KRK). Commons photos show it as Perla di Roma (2011), and the western twin as Grillroom/Steakhouse Roos. Current tenant unknown, so not mentioned.

## Source conflicts

- Brug 705 piers: the city register says six rows of three piers, each made of two trapezoid volumes. nl.wikipedia says 24 V-shaped piers in six rows of four. The text gives no count.
- Brug 705 date: the register says 1958–1960; nl.wikipedia says ready in 1960 and opened 14 July 1962 (with the whole Lelylaan). Both are used.
- Aviodome architect: the register names E.A. Riphagen, "after Buckminster Fuller's concept". nl.wikipedia lists Fuller as architect. I followed the register. The register says "first in Europe, for a long time largest in NL and Europe"; Wikipedia says "largest in the world". I followed the register.
- Sloterhof size: Arcam says 658 rental homes, amsterdamopdekaart says 900. No number in the text.
- Sloterhof dates: the register says 1955–1958 (water garden) and 1958–1960 (buildings); nl.wikipedia says delivered 1958–59. I used 1958–1960.
- Sloterhof listing: nl.wikipedia says municipal monument in 2008, then rijksmonument in 2016. The slice data has only the rijks status, so heritage_status is "National monument".
- Slotervaart bridges: the shared register text dates all of them to 1960 and credits only "Publieke Werken". nl.wikipedia gives 701 = 1956 (Slebos), 709 = 1960 (Slebos), 711 = 1961/62 (Slebos), 305 = 1962/63 (Slebos), and 787 = 1986/87, a wooden footbridge by Sterenberg. 787 doesn't fit a 1960 ensemble, so `built` is omitted and the architect is given as Publieke Werken (Slebos, Sterenberg).
- Sloterhof tower relief: the Commons file description says "unknown artist". nl.wikipedia and Wikidata (citing the city's "Zie de muren spreken", 2018) name Jan Goeting. I used Goeting.
- The erfgoedregister.amsterdam.nl page for the Goeting sgraffito failed with a TLS certificate error, so I didn't use it.

## Pins

All pins are register points. Sloterhof is pinned on the eastern maisonette block over the water (532232 point). The canal is pinned at the Bloeddonorbrug (701). Brug 705 uses the register point (Johan Huizingalaan 174, the west abutment). It's about 50 m west of the viaduct's centre line, which may be worth nudging after a look at aerial imagery.

## Walking route ideas

1. **Slotervaart prefab and concrete** (about 3 km): Former Caltex garage, Johan Huizingalaan → Sloterhof tower → Sloterhof (along the siergracht, east) → Lelylaan viaduct (Brug 705) → south along the Huizingalaan → Slotervaart canal and its bridges (Bloeddonorbrug, then west along the water to the Piet Römerbrug). Add Satéprikkers and Erotica from the art slice.
2. **Sloterplas edge**: Sloterhof tower → Brug 675, Sloterpark → the Sloterplas (Wegwerphuisje, from the art slice).
3. **Port-edge bike ride** (Sloterdijk/Westpoort): Aviodome (Circa Amsterdam) → west along the North Sea Canal → Gemaal Halfweg (1977) → Museum Stoomgemaal Halfweg (outside Amsterdam). The ride covers 1852 and 1977 pumping side by side.
