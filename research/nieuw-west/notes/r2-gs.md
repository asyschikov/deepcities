- 109 into Vrijheidscarillon (geuzenveld.yaml): Look for lines for Wilfried Put's Hoogovenarbeider (1970, photos 2021) and Joop van den Broek's Phoenix glass window in the former NVV building (Plein '40-'45 1).

## Conflicts and corrections

- Limpers: executed 10 June 1944 at Overveen (inscription, nl.wikipedia). The scout's "1 July 1943" is Arondeus's date; the 4en5mei page also repeats 27 March 1943 (the register attack) as a death date.
- Jan Postma: executed July 1944; 4en5mei says Vught 24 July, Kunstwacht Scheveningen 31 July. Text says only "July 1944".
- Trimmers: original 1983 (Parool 30 Aug 1983 via nl.wikipedia); steel remake year differs (nl.wikipedia 2006, Kunstwacht 2008, "third renewal"). Text avoids a remake year.
- Juliana plaque on the Hannie Schaftbrug: plaque reads 7 October 1952; Kunstwacht 893 says 27 October 1950 (wrong).
- Phoenix on the Cramergracht flat: Kunstwacht 888 credits Piet Zanstra; nl.wikipedia (Buitenbeeld in beeld, RCE 2007) gives Raymond Both. Text gives Both and mentions the dispute.
- Theo Forrer: a Burma railway claim from the scout could not be confirmed; left out.
- Haarlemmerweg monument: Kunstwacht pin is the pre-2017 spot; pinned on OSM node 10863690358.
