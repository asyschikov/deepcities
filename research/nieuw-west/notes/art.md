## Corrections to SCOUT.md

- **#10 Animaris**: it is no longer in the Albardagracht. It stood there 2004–2014 (Geuzenveld, near Kruisherenpad), was restored and since 18 May 2014 stands in a cage at the entrance of Business Park Amsterdam Osdorp, Ookmeerweg at Etnastraat (Buitenkunst, nl.wikipedia, Heijmerink Wagemakers). Also, it was not "dragged into place by local youths": the invited youths did not show up, so guests pulled it (nl.wikipedia, citing NRC 20 Sept 2004). Latest dated evidence it is still there: Commons photos of September 2018 and the current Buitenkunst listing; no removal news found. A field check would be nice but nothing suggests it's gone.
- **#4**: the claim "first public artwork in Amsterdam by a Muslim woman" was left out (unclear which work it refers to).

Buitenkunst coordinates differ by 100+ m for Erotica (52.368791, 4.839276) and Wegwerphuisje (52.357371, 4.813533); OSM and Wikipedia agree with each other, so those were used. ## Conflicts and uncertainties

- Vlindermolen year: 1998 (Buitenkunst, nl.wikipedia article, NH Nieuws 2026) vs 1993 in the nl.wikipedia list of Nieuw-West sculptures. Used 1998.
- Kubus-spiegel-palen: pole length given as 23 m (Buitenbeeld in beeld) or 21 m (another blog); written as "more than 20 metres". nl.wikipedia says the artist would rather the "satéprikkers" nickname were different, while the Parool 2024 headline says he is fine with it; left his opinion out.
- Erotica: Wikipedia gives 1971–1973 (commission to unveiling); Stadscuratorium says 1973. Used 1971–1973 for `built`.
- Animaris size: 4.5 m high (Buitenkunst) vs 4.7 m (nl.wikipedia); weight 2.8 t vs "about 3,000 kg". Both versions use Buitenkunst's 4.5 m and 2.8 t.
- Groot Landschap: commission year 1954 comes only from Perlmuter's recollection in Geheugen van West (2006).
- "5713" (the poem's title): nl.wikipedia ties it to a public-transport zone number for the area; not explained in the text.

## Walking/cycling route ideas

1. **Makkink's bricks and the Sloterplas (cycle, ~6 km)**: Groot Landschap, Wegwerphuisje, Satéprikkers (Kubus-spiegel-palen), then south to Vlindermolen ("de haas"). Theme: odd sculptures the garden city lives with.
2. **Climb, sit, look (Osdorp, cycle)**: Schaap met vijf poten, Bolhuis's Schaapjes on the Osdorpplein (not a place yet), then west to Animaris Rhinoceros Transport. Theme: art you can touch.
3. **Slotervaart short walk**: Erotica, If you know, you know... (5713), Satéprikkers. Theme: 1970s commissions vs 2020s participatory art.
