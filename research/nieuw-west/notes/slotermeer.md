- M24: the hint allowed either. The Bot row is a different architect, client (Patrimonium vs AWV), type and listing (2023 vs 2015), about 150 m away, with its own story (shop-by-shop history, CPN councillor architect). Kept separate; the Verfdoos text mentions the street link.
- M25: the hint says "pumping station"; it is a windmill (achtkante bovenkruier poldermolen, c. 1632) per the national register. Written as a mill, category `industrial` (as De Otter in West).

- M30: Zanstra's church (200898) and his ULO school (200941) are one planned complex, merged. Herman Poortstraat 17 (200921) is Ingwersen's, 600 m east, unrelated: own place.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Brug 612 designer: register says only "Publieke Werken, Dienst der"; nl.wikipedia credits Dick Slebos. Not settled, so the text says Public Works only.
- De Olijftak: register says the Mennonites left in 1960; nl.wikipedia and Amsterdam op de kaart say 1969 (the first minister lived in the parsonage 1956–1969). Text says "only briefly" without a year. Couzijn's bird: register calls it 'Gods Vogel', unveiled 3 May 1958, removed by the mosque users; nl.wikipedia calls it 'Vliegende vogel' (1955), Amsterdam op de kaart 'Vliegend', moved probably c. 1993. Text names neither title nor who removed it; only that it now hangs at the Singelkerk (Herengracht entrance).
- De Verfdoos colours: register (c. 2014) says the 2009 renovation kept earlier altered panels and used a new scheme derived from Ongenae's Prinses Irenebuurt designs; nl.wikipedia says original colours returned; Amsterdam op de kaart says new panels in other colours. Text follows the register: not every panel is Ongenae's.
- Schip van Slebos date: register 1966; nl.wikipedia 1961; Amsterdam op de kaart: base c. 1961, platform and kiosk 1966. Used "c. 1961–1966". Register record has no text (listed May 2025), so Dutch is from nl.wikipedia (Oostoever).
- De Hoeksteen (200898) register record has no text; Dutch from nl.wikipedia, school part from register 200941.
- Lourdeskerk: register gives two sets of building dates (one copied from De Olijftak). Used 1955–1957 and consecration 19 December 1957 (nl.wikipedia). "No regular Masses since 2022" from nl.wikipedia; Amsterdam op de kaart says only incidental use since 2020. Pin is the church (Wikidata point), not the register point, which sits on the presbytery at Noordzijde 42.
- Gerhardhuis: nl.wikipedia says first of its kind in the Netherlands; Amsterdam op de kaart says first in Amsterdam. English uses Amsterdam, Dutch keeps the nl.wikipedia sentence. Architects: register W. van Tijen, M. Boom; Amsterdam op de kaart adds J. Posno (not used).
- Herman Poortstraat 17: current status from the city property register (feitelijk gebruik "Leegstandbeheer", read 22 Nov 2026). Earlier users (Mundus College, Spring High) from a Commons description and Geheugen van West.
- Louis Couperusstraat 131 (ULO): current user unclear (search hints at Al Wafa primary school, not confirmed), left out.
- Shop row: Rochdale renovation planned 2024–2025 (Amsterdam op de kaart, updated July 2026); completion not confirmed, so the text says "is renovating".

## Walking route idea

"Slotermeer's 1950s showcase" (about 3.5 km), from the mill to the lake:
1. De 1200 Roe
2. Shop row, Lodewijk van Deysselstraat (J. Bot)
3. De Verfdoos
4. A.H. Gerhardhuis
5. Onze-Lieve-Vrouw-van-Lourdeskerk
6. Brug 612
7. De Hoeksteen and ULO school (Zanstra)
8. Adriaan van Oordthof
9. De Olijftak (El Hijra mosque)
10. Former Van der Zweep ULO
11. Brug 604
12. Schip van Slebos

Can be joined with the Vrijheidscarillon on Plein '40-'45 (between 4 and 5), also a Slebos design.
