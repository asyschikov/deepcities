Splitting would give two near-identical texts. If the map wants two pins, the second would be the Fockstraat group at its register point 52.378492, 4.831406 (Okay rank).

Pin: register point of record 2 (52.380595, 4.829881), on the Hans Katanhof block in the middle of the restored northern strip.

## Status (not settled)

- The city API (checked 22 Nov 2026) still gives both records status "In procedure Gemeentelijk Monument", no monument number, no designation date, no reasons text. No news of a decision found. So no `heritage_status`, `monument_number` or `listed`; the body says the procedure is running. Re-check before import.
- Already protected as part of the gemeentelijk beschermd stadsgezicht Slotermeer (Van Eesteren Buitenmuseum). Year conflict: nl.wikipedia Airey-woningen says 2007, nl.wikipedia Burgemeester De Vlugtlaan says 2010. Year left out.
- A Geheugen van West article (26 June 2026) is headed "Nu Rijksmonumenten", but that refers to Jeruzalem and Sloterhof, not these blocks (its text is copied from nl.wikipedia). A Dec 2025 rodi.nl item "Geen monumentenstatus" is about the Airey flats on the Johan Jongkindstraat (Slotervaart), not these.

## Leads checked

- **Arie Keppler Prize 2020: not this project.** It went to Rochdale's renovation of Jeruzalem (Watergraafsmeer), category Buurtreparatie (rochdale.nl news, 27 Nov 2020). Left out.
- "Slanted" blocks: confirmed by Architectenweb (2015): blocks "deels haaks en deels schuin op de Burgemeester de Vlugtlaan". TU Delft lists the northern strip as upper and lower flats in three layers.
- Renovation dates: Eigen Haard / Hooyschuur 138 homes + 3 business units, start May 2016, all delivered in 2018; then the other four Eigen Haard blocks. Rochdale 112 homes, start March 2020, delivered 2021. De Alliantie's two blocks (De Nijs page, "112 Airey-woningen", no date; Strackee 2016 says in preparation with a trial flat): completion date unknown, so not given.
- Van Eesteren Museum's old Eesterengesprek page on the Airey homes is a 404.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Number of homes: nl.wikipedia and Architectenweb give 221 Airey homes + 5 business units on the De Vlugtlaan strip, of which 141 to be renovated; Hooyschuur/Gebouwd in Amsterdam say 138 + 3 in the first phase; TU Delft's inventory says "418 boven- en benedenwoningen, 61, 6 bedrijfsruimten" (unclear, probably the whole area). Used 221 + 5 for the original scheme.
- Dates: register 1953; Anefo photo of the skeleton dated 11 Aug 1952; Strackee captions show building in 1952 (De Tourton Bruynsstraat) and 1953 (Speelmanstraat), finished blocks in 1954. Used 1952–1953; Berghoef's design is dated 1951 (Architectenweb).
- De Nijs says De Alliantie's project was 112 homes, the same figure as Rochdale's; may be copied. Not used.
- Panel size: TU Delft gives 4 cm thick, 40 x 60 cm (working size 37.5 x 62.5 cm). Text says "about 60 by 40 cm".

## Walking route idea
