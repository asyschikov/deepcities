## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Shelter date**: city heritage office says the shelter was built in 1956; stelling-amsterdam.nl says handed over 31 Aug 1960; BAG gives the school 1960; forten.nl calls it an H-school of 1960. Body says handed over 1960; `built: 1956–1960`. Forten.nl calls it a "former" primary school, but the school is plainly still running; "former" ignored.
- **Shelter count**: "40 public shelters" (forten.nl) and "at least 104 bunkers and shelters" (city) both used, as different counts.
- **WoZoCo**: the Mies van der Rohe Award is on MVRDV's award list (1999) but its nature (nomination or shortlist) is unclear; left out. "One of the most published buildings" (scout) not sourced; replaced by ArchDaily's AD Classics. Which footpath gives the best view of the cantilevers was not settled; left general. The 9-storey count comes from a search snippet of architectuur.org (OSM height 28.8 m fits).
- **Meervaart**: scout said "council chose the site in March 2024"; sources show the college chose it in February 2024 (Rodi) and the council widened the area in 2024 (amsterdam.nl snippet); written that way. Fate of the current building: only "freeing the site to strengthen the shopping centre" (meervaart.nl); demolition not stated, so not claimed. The director is not named (institution only); the party is described as "a new local party" in the text, named in sources.
- **Wildemanbuurt**: scout said 1960s flats and a council decision in 2026; BAG says 1959 and the council adopted the plan on 2 Apr 2025. 77% (Stadgenoot) vs 76.9% (Stadszaken) is the same figure. Station Wildeman's building year (BAG 1971 vs Parool snippet "1950s mavo school") is not stated. The scout's "named after a farm" was not checked and is left out. Parool's honourable mention (Beste Gebouw van het Jaar 2026) is from a search snippet.
- **Storks**: the year the first chimney nest was built (about 2017–2018 from undated articles) is not stated.
