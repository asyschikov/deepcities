Deviations from the hints:
- **M1 split in three.** 25 records in one place would exceed the 200-character `monument_number` limit and hide the two real stories. Split into the Dorpsplein (square, police post, Osdorperweg houses), the Sloterweg street houses, and Sloterweg 1210-1212 (L.J. de Wolf, listed 2025, own nl.wikipedia page and the best-documented interior).
- **M4 split.** The banpaal (rijksmonument, a strong story: Amsterdam's ban limit, Ziesenis, 1794) is its own place, as SCOUT item 36 suggests. The two wooden gates (200338, 200118) are too slight and went into the Sloterweg houses.
- **M5 split in three.** The hint's "Sloterweg ribbon" spans 1.6 km: the four west-end houses (1252, 1289, 1301, 1309-1311) sit at the village's edge, the Margarethahoeve is 600 m east, and the farm workers' cottages 833/837 are 1.3 km east beside the M6 villas. One pin cannot serve them all, so each got its own place.

## Status (checked November 2026)

Nothing gone.
- Police post: a shop and information point since 2018 (Stadsherstel), open on weekends in season.
- Pancratiuskerk: no regular services since 2024 (parish De Vier Evangelisten), special services still held. No sale or conversion news found.
- Akermolen: Theehuis de Akermolen (Stichting De Vrije Geer), open in 2026.
- Nieuw en Meer: active (events in October/November 2026).
- Rijkshemelvaartdienst: still active at Oude Haagseweg 58.
- IBM: the 1976 building is to be stripped and renovated by Office Winhov for RED Company (Think District / Nieuwe Meer Oost, investment decision January 2026); IBM is the first tenant of a new office next door. Not checked whether IBM has already moved out, so the text says the change is to come. The "IBM" roof letters come from the 2008 register text and may have been taken down.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- Police post date: register says "4e kwart 19e eeuw"; nl.wikipedia, Stadsherstel and Sloten-Oud Osdorp all say 1866. Used 1866.
- Pancratiuskerk: the slice says 1890; the national register says 1899–1901 (first stone 12 May 1900). Used the register. The register calls the building priest J.H. Haverman, nl.wikipedia J.A. Haverman; used the register's.
- Sloterkerk: the slice says listed 1996, the national register gives 21 January 1997. Used 1997.
- Akermolen: register 1876 (gable stone), Stadsherstel 1874, an older Westerpost piece 1874. Gave "1874–1876 (on this site)".
- Sloterweg 745: Ons Amsterdam ("Siegerpark? Nooit van gehoord!") says Dr W. Sieger built a villa *Rieckerstaete* at no. 745; the register and Amsterdam op de kaart say the doctor L.A. Faber commissioned it in 1935–1936 and that the name dates from at least 1963. Followed the register and left Sieger out.
- Rijkshemelvaartdienst: the register says the squat started in the mid-1990s; the group's own site says "since 1989". Left the date out.
- Rembrandt and Sloten: nl.wikipedia says Rembrandt drew and etched the 17th-century Sloterkerk; the SCOUT's Oslo drawing attribution is uncertain, so it is not used. The Rembrandt obelisk etching is of the Spaarndammerdijk post, not Sloten's; not used for the banpaal.
- Margarethahoeve: the register spells the name "Magaretha Hoeve" in one place; used Margarethahoeve (city Erfgoed van de Week, 2023).

## Walking and cycling routes

1. **Sloten village walk** (about 1 km): Sint-Pancratiuskerk, Shop and house Sloterweg 1210-1212, Banpaal of Sloten, Dorpsplein Sloten and the police post, Sloterkerk, Boerderij Welgelegen and the Osdorperweg cottages, Sloterweg village houses, Aker-Stein and the west end of the Sloterweg. Then on to the Molen van Sloten (green slice).
2. **Sloterweg farm-ribbon ride** (east to west, about 3 km): IBM headquarters, Villa Maupertuus and Riecker State (with the Siegerpark), Farm workers' cottages 833-837, Margarethahoeve, Sint-Pancratiuskerk, Dorpsplein Sloten.
3. **Defence Line by the Nieuwe Meer** (bike, with De Oeverlanden from the green slice): Emergency waterworks (Rijkshemelvaartdienst), Nieuw en Meer, back along the Ringvaart dike to the Akermolen.
