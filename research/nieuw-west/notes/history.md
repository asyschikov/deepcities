## Status checks (Nov 2026)

- SAMA: active; Rodi 21 Apr 2026 on the Digital Street Art Depot. Office Slauerhoffstraat 9H (official site and Rodi say 9; SCOUT's "7H" and "Immanuel Kanthof 1" are wrong or outdated).
- Graphic Surgery: painted 2016 (artists' own Instagram post, Feb 2022: "Our 45 meter high ACTA wall painting ... from 2016"). No 2026 photo seen, but the Stadscuratorium story is current. RADION opened its "allerlaatste jaar" (very last year) on 5 Sep 2026 (3voor12); the lease may end in about 18 months. The "slated for demolition since 2016" claim in SCOUT was not confirmed and is left out. If RADION leaves and the building is demolished, this place will need a Gone update.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- **Sloterplas "hand-dug"**: only the first part (a piece of the north-west harbour, 1942) was dug by hand, "wellicht" as work relief (Rodi, from vriendenvandesloterplas). The bulk was dredged 1948–1956 with a bucket-wheel suction dredger. The title and text say so. Depth: Rodi says sand dug "tot circa dertig meter"; nl.wikipedia says dug to 45 m, rubble brought it back to 38 m, infobox 37 m. The text gives "around 30 m" with a note that sources differ. Pin is on the north-west harbour (OSM marina "De Duikelaar"), where the digging began, not a lake centroid.
- **Graphic Surgery pin**: on the high-rise (BAG 0363100012114496, 42.9 m) of Louwesweg 1. The painted part is the dark end tower of that slab; I could not tell from the photos whether it is the north or south end. A field check could move the pin about 40 m.
- **Witte Klok heritage**: SCOUT says "Witte Klok is a monument". Not confirmed: amsterdamopdekaart leaves status blank and nl.wikipedia only cites the Monumentenkaart without saying it is listed. No heritage attributes set. "Only one still in original state" is amsterdamopdekaart's "vermoedelijk"; nl.wikipedia says one of fifteen intact (2016). Both are hedged in the text. The renewal that kept the original equipment comes from an amsterdam.nl snippet whose page has since been removed.
- **Sierplein**: the sculptor is "Siep van den Berg" (nl.wikipedia, OSM, Commons) but "Siep van de Berg" on 4en5meiamsterdam.nl. Kunstwacht/4en5mei.nl give 1994 as the placement year (when the square was laid out and named); the sculpture was unveiled in May 1980 (De Waarheid 7 May 1980 via Wikipedia). The airmen's surname is spelled Barett and Barrett on the same page; I used Barrett. The plane crashed in the Riekerpolder, not on the square itself. I left out Wikipedia's 2003 incident (flowers trampled at the remembrance) to keep the text focused; add it back if wanted.
- **Allende**: the 2024 Parool article is paywalled; only its headline and caption were used. The fact that the artist also took his own life (nl.wikipedia) is left out on purpose.
- **Verzetsstrijdersbuurt**: Rodi (Oct 2026) says "meer dan tachtig portretten" in the neighbourhood. The project's data file lists 91 panels under "Slotermeer", but that includes non-resistance series (philosophers such as Plato and Socrates, the painters' streets). The pin is the Hannie Schaft panel at the coordinates in the project's own data (52.379147, 4.836392). The neighbourhood's 1950s naming date is not sourced beyond Plein '40-'45 (1955), so the text says "in the 1950s" only. The short identifications of Schaft, Arondeus and Belinfante are standard history and are not in the cited sources; check them if a source link is required for every fact.
- **SAMA collection size**: the site says "over 300"; SCOUT said 150–300. The text uses "about 300". Bastardilla's *Memories* (2019) was not put in "look for" because its 2026 state is unconfirmed (it is on a renovated Eigen Haard block in Geuzenveld); its Commons photo is used as the second image.

## Walking route ideas

1. **Garden-city idealism (Slotermeer, about 3 km)**: Van Eesteren Museum, then Sloterplas (north-west harbour), then SAMA (Dichtersbuurt walls), then the resistance fighters' faces around Plein '40-'45. The plan, the lake that made it possible, and how its residents later reclaimed the walls and street names.
2. **Remembrance in Nieuw-West (by bike)**: Resistance fighters' faces, then Allende monument (Sloterpark), then Bevrijde vogel at the Sierplein. Three ways of remembering: names, a foreign cause, and a neighbourhood's own memorial updated for a diverse district.
3. **Play and paint (Osdorp to Slotervaart, by bike)**: Van Eyck playground at Witte Klok, then the Van Eyck frame at the Van Eesteren Museum, then the Graphic Surgery lines at Louwesweg.
