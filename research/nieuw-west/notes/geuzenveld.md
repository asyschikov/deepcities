## Deviations from the hints

- **M19 "Airey houses (prefab concrete)" is wrong.** Osdorperweg 658-660 is a pair of linked family houses of 1978–1980 by F.J.E. Dekeukeleire (Amsterdam op de kaart architect page; city register year 1980). Status is "In procedure Gemeentelijk Monument"; a November 2025 piece on love80sarchitecture.nl objects to the listing. No heritage_status set (not an option for "in procedure"). Decision needed: keep as an Okay footnote, or drop until the designation is final. I could not find whether the designation has been decided since 2025.
- **M22 split in two.** The Timmers semi-detached houses (G. Timmers, 1955, Vening Meineszlaan) and the Sape Kuiperplantsoen flats (Th.J. Lammers, 1953, by Plein '40-'45) have different architects, clients and dates and are about 800 m apart: they just share a group.
- **M21 split into two places**, as the hint allowed: the three H-schools (pinned at the Slotermeerschool, the most intact) and the two near-identical nursery schools (pinned at Al Wafa). The other buildings are named in the body and address. If the map wants one pin per building, De Kans (52.372548, 4.812433), Rendorpschool (52.375742, 4.829911) and De Meerkoet (52.379718, 4.817554) could be split out as Okay places.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- 1800 Roeden build date: national register says munitions complex 1897, laboratory 1901 (used); nl.wikipedia says 1870–1910 with the powder magazine begun in 1870. Listing: city register 29 Dec 1998, national register inscription 23 Apr 1999. SCOUT said "distance painted on a shed roof": it is the text "1800 Roeden" laid in the roof tiles (nl.wikipedia).
- Westereindflat dates: register 1955–1957 (used); nl.wikipedia and Amsterdam op de kaart (Delpher) say construction began Nov 1956 and first residents moved in May 1958. Rijksmonument since 2009 (slice) vs 2010 (nl.wikipedia). Flat count/size: register 83 one-room + 44 two-room; areas 32 and 48–50 m².
- Rietveld/Bloemsma studio houses: register 1955; nl.wikipedia (Bloemsma) says designed 1953/54.
- Carillon inauguration: 4 May 1961 (nl.wikipedia, register context) vs 5 May 1961 (Amsterdam op de kaart). Used 4 May. Wikipedia list gives 1957 for the carillon; ignored.
- De Meerkoet address: register says Jan de Louterstraat 2, its own text and Amsterdam op de kaart say 21 (used 21).
- Dudok homes: register gives 1958; Wikipedia list gives 1953–1957 (project dates). Used 1958.
- Catharina church: nl.wikipedia says built 1954–1956; register 1956–1965 covers the schools. Used 1954–1965.
- Catharina schools current users: Commons filename says VierTaal College (ex A.G. Bell); not stated in the text beyond "still schools".
- Slotermeerschool register text says built 1953; Wikipedia list says 1954. Used 1953–1955 for the merged place.

## Walking routes

1. **Slotermeer north-east, post-war ideals** (about 2.5 km): Vrijheidscarillon, Sape Kuiperplantsoen flats, Shops-and-flats block (De Vlugtlaan), Van Eyck and Rietveld old people's homes, Sint Catharinakerk and the Catharina schools, Slotermeerschool, Timmers houses, Nursery school Het Waterhoentje (Al Wafa), Westereindflat.
2. **Geuzenveld edge, Dudok to the polder** (bike, about 5 km): Dudok's farmhouse homes, Van Tijen studio houses (and Dekeukeleire's own villa on the Willem Mulierhof), Dekeukeleire houses on the Osdorperweg, then north along the polder to De 1800 Roeden.
