The story alone (playful dart, children threw paper planes at the unveiling) is thin for a place.

## Source conflicts and uncertainties

- **Chile vencerá date**: 1981 (Kunstwacht, MRP, Stadscuratorium 2025, the artist's confirmation) vs 1983 (nl.wikipedia, a Commons caption). Used 1981.
- **How the public sees Chile vencerá**: MRP and Rodi (2022) promise visibility "through transparent doors" from the public space; Kunstwacht (after 2022) says it is hidden in a bike cellar and only visible through a door. A lightbox photo and lighting are documented. Flagged `check: Location`.
- **Vredesteken vs Monument voor de Vrede**: 4en5mei calls the pink form in the pond "Vredesteken Osdorp" (plaque in the wall says so). Kunstwacht 622 calls the pond work "Monument voor de Vrede" and Kunstwacht 932 says a separate "Vredesteken" (with the stone "Om nooit te vergeten / zij die vielen voor onze vrijheid") lay opposite it and moved with the 4 May ceremony to the Sloterplas shore in 2008. Title uses both names; the body says "the memorial stone that lay opposite the monument went along". The scout's caveat is settled: the 4 May ceremony by the Sloterplas is not at this work. The Indies remembrance (Aug 2026) does use it.
- **Dwaze Moedersplein naming**: 1984 (Ensie, "Rb. Stadsdeel Osdorp 24-10-1984") vs 1 Nov 1985 (scout, BAG record). Used 1984. Stone unveiling: Anefo photo dated 12 March 1986, tile says 13-3-1986; text says "March 1986".
- **Dwaze moeder statue location**: Kunstwacht pins it at 52.353675, 4.807179, 270 m south of the square, on the same spot OSM has Jannes Limperg's "Vliegende vogels". Commons puts it on the square. Pinned on the square (Kunstwacht pin of Blom's stone); covered by the check note.
- **Kars and Bierman**: Bierman's birth year 1914 (monument, 4en5mei) vs 1912 (4en5meiamsterdam story); used 1914. Place of death Oegstgeest vs Leiden military hospital: text says only "died of his wounds that day". Kars killed by a Canadian grenade (4en5meiamsterdam); 4en5mei says only "a grenade". Fleming's unit as given by 4en5mei looks muddled (63rd squadron "62nd fighter group"), so left out. The 2018 tile amendment and the corrected crash site are reported separately by 4en5mei; the text keeps them as two facts.
- **Lixenberg's arrival**: 1957 (nl.wikipedia) vs 1959 (Kunstwacht); written as "the late 1950s".
- **Heipaal**: the 100,000th-pile story rests on one answer by local historian Erik Swierstra on Geheugen van West (2011), which agrees with the inscription. No plaque.

## Route idea

**Latin America in Nieuw-West** (short walk, about 2 km): Dwaze Moedersplein, Chile vencerá mural (Brigada Ramona Parra), then north past the Sloterplas to Allende monument and President Allendelaan.

**Kunstroute Osdorp (part)**: Blauwe Boog (Lixenberg), Monument voor de Vrede (Vredesteken Osdorp), Totempaal (Rietbroek), Heipaal 100.000, Schaap met vijf poten, then east to the lambs and the paper dart at the Osdorpplein (Chile vencerá mural).
