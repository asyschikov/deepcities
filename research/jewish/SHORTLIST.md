## Stolpersteine

More than 4,500 in Amsterdam, no open dataset with coordinates. Recommended: no pin per
stone; one document, plus emblematic places (twelve stones at one door, Nieuwe Prinsengracht
18; the Artis threshold and the 2025 thresholds; the Weesp cantor's house); stones mentioned
in the text of places that have them.
