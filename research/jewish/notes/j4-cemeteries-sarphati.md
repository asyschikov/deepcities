## Claims dropped

- Beth Haim: finest tombs covered in winter; caretaker's house dated 1883 and "opposite the
  Protestant church"; old gate on the water (replaced by the RCE's landing stage); Ashkenazi burials
  here until 1642 (only Wikipedia, joodsamsterdam and the Muiderberg history society).
- Diemen: about twenty wooden grave markers of 1915-1927 in field A (register only; a fine
  Look-for if a second authoritative source turns up); the 1931 metaheerhuis as compensation for
  the railway (architectureplus, netpha); a Holocaust monument at the cemetery (scout claim; the
  Diemen Holocaust monument, Jasper Krabbe 2025, is in the town at the Van Gemertplantsoen); that
  the viaduct is still in use / still crosses (joodsamsterdam only; text says it split the grounds).
- Muiderberg: purchase for 410 guilders with a 300-guilder Portuguese loan; transport by trekschuit
  along De Goog, the "Jodenvaart"; the separate kohanim gate; priestly hands on kohanim graves;
  memorial stones for murdered relatives; stolpersteine (end 2025) for the wartime caretaker Henri
  Birnbaum and his mother-in-law; P.C. Hooft co-signing the deed; Bogtman's glass mosaic; French
  troops using stones in 1672. Mostly joodsamsterdam and/or Historische Kring Muiderberg only. The
  Birnbaum stones could come back with Joods Monument + the Gooise Meren stolperstein foundation.
- Bread factory: "the first bread factory in the Netherlands" (Ons Amsterdam names a steam bakery
  of 1829 that lasted under a year); weekly output (9,000 loaves in nl.wikipedia vs 90,000 in Ons
  Amsterdam); architect Jan Leliman; fire 1879; sale to Carels' 1955; demolition 1966; "broke the
  bakers' price cartel"; 14 cents / six cents under (Ons only; kept as "about thirty per cent",
  where Ons, amsterdamopdekaart and Wikipedia agree).
- Herengracht 598: that he died in the house (Hagoort says "house of mourning"; not stated by a
  second source); the marriage being childless (Wikipedia/joodsamsterdam only); father-in-law a
  former parnas (Hagoort only); "no plaque" (not verified); 30 carriages and three steamboats at
  the funeral (Hagoort only).

## Sarphati material for the biography document

From Hagoort's essay (dutchjewry, adapted from her 2005 Beth Haim study; her full biography is
*Samuel Sarphati: van Portugese armenarts tot Amsterdamse ondernemer*, Bas Lubberhuizen 2013,
416 pp.) unless noted; second sources still needed where marked (1).

- Born 31 Jan 1813; parents Emanuel Sarphati (tobacco) and Reyna Musafia; grew up in the
  Jodenbreestraat and Nieuwe Herengracht (Hagoort) vs Nieuwe Keizersgracht 49 (joodsamsterdam,
  nl.wikipedia): unresolved. Apprenticed to the pharmacist Coronel, Joden Houtgracht 7
  (joodsamsterdam (1)). The name means "Frenchman" in Hebrew (Hagoort, after Jaap Meijer (1)).
- Leiden doctorate June 1839; physician of the Portuguese poor and of the hospital Mesjiv Nefesj
  from 1 Feb 1840 at 250 guilders; clashes with the parnassim in 1842; resigned in 1846 after the
  scarlet-fever epidemic and gave his unpaid salary to the hospital.
- 1842 Netherlands Society for the Advancement of Pharmacy; trade school; 1844 protest that won
  Jewish burials on Sundays; Jewish kindergartens against conversion (1).
- 1847: Society for Agriculture and Land Reclamation and the permit to collect ashes, refuse and
  night soil and sell it as manure (Hagoort; joodsamsterdam; SOURCES.md adds the city takeover,
  decided 1875, concession ended Oct 1877). No physical trace found to pin.
- 1849 co-founder of the Maatschappij tot Nut der Israëlieten (counterpart of the Nut, which then
  excluded Jews) (1).
- From 1851 member of the Provincial States; from 1854 on the Portuguese community's council,
  gabay, then chairman; mohel; first inventory of the synagogue's ceremonial silver (1).
- Beth Haim: around 1850-1856 he forced through the repair and widening of the Amstel bank that
  kept the graves from flooding; in 1855 banned begging on the cemetery (posters in the
  Stadsarchief) (1). Good for the Beth Haim story in the doc.
- Opposed the anatomy lab and police post opposite the Snoge (opened 1868, demolished 1968) (1).
- 1852 Vereeniging voor Volksvlijt; Paleis voor Volksvlijt opened 16 Aug 1864; knighted that day
  (Orde van de Nederlandse Leeuw; Eikenkroon 1860, nl.wikipedia/joodsamsterdam).
- Banks and companies: Nederlandsche Crediet- en Depositobank, a national mortgage bank, the
  "Nederlandsche Bouwmaatschappij" (Netherlands Building Society) and the Amstel Hotel company
  (Hagoort). "Amsterdamsche Bouwfonds" under his name: not confirmed anywhere; leave the word out.
  Amsterdamopdekaart says that after his death the Nederlandsche Bouw Maatschappij tried to carry
  out his plan for the Pijp without success (1).
- Wife Abigaël Mendes de Leon died May 1864; he died 23 June 1866 after a bowel illness, two days
  after the Palace's first exhibition opened; funeral 25 June from Herengracht 598, thousands in the
  streets; buried at Beth Haim beside his wife, his stone in Hebrew with 1 Sam. 3:4, hers in
  Portuguese. Estate in debt; A.C. Wertheim settled it (1).
- Het Parool readers' "greatest Amsterdammer of all time" (Jan 2025: nl.wikipedia, roods.io (1)).
