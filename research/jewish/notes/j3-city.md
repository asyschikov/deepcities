They could go into j6q04 later if a second source turns up.
- **R-14 and R-15b, Beethovenstraat:** one place. It is pinned at the raid plate opposite Corellistraat 27, and the 2025 threshold at the tram stop (about 52.348779, 4.877200) is a Look-for line.
- **R-15a, Victorieplein threshold:** drafted as its own place.
- **R-12, Concertgebouw:** drafted as its own place. I suggest making it a child of pool place `qlfbyd9polzh2z1innj0hzkm` "Concertgebouw", which already mentions the memorial.
- **R-16, Zuideinde 443:** drafted. Pool place `pbvwv8giret73zc4vza53q4y` "Zuideinde, Oostzanerwerf" already tells this story, pinned about 300 m away at no. 428-430. I suggest cutting the refugee text there down to a Look-for line that points here.

## Uncertainties and conflicts

- **Waalstraat corner:** the sources say "Waalstraat 1, corner of the Havenkade". No. 1 no longer exists; the odd side now starts at no. 3. The pin is on the odd-side corner building, Amstelkade 65 (BAG 1924). The scout's pin was on the even corner. Marked `check: Location`. The building's current use is unknown.
- **Concertgebouw name:** ANP and 4en5mei say "Samuel Gompertz". The panel itself (Commons photo) and Joods Monument say **Simon Gomperts**, double bassist. The draft follows the panel.
- **Beethovenstraat plate dates:** 4en5mei transcribes them as "1943-1946". The Commons photo shows "1943-1945". The draft does not quote the dates.
- **Beethovenstraat threshold wording:** tracesofwar gives "1942 en 1944", while the Artis threshold says 1942 and 1943. Marked `check: Facts`; the years are not quoted.
- **Victorieplein threshold:** its wording comes from one transcription (tracesofwar). Marked `check: Facts`. Laying date: tracesofwar gives 27-01-2025, and NOS/IamExpat confirm January 2025, so the draft says "January 2025".
- **Herman Elteschool building date:** Wikipedia says 1901, BAG says 1905. The date is not used.
- **Living people not named:**
  - The poet of the Beethovenstraat plate, who signs it.
  - The former pupils who initiated the Elteschool plaque.
  - The residents of Zuideinde 443.
- **Era choice for Zuideinde 443:** set to 1796 to 1940, because the plaque is about refugees arriving 1933 to 1939. 1940 to 1945 would also fit.
- **Theme choice for the Concertgebouw:** set to Culture and sport. Persecution and war would also fit.
