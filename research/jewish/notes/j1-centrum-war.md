## C-06 Hollandsche Schouwburg
- Sources: JCK (location page, deportation page, remembrance page); nl.wikipedia; joodsamsterdam; 4en5mei 1516; Rijksmonument 532241.
- Pin: the front facade, 52.366505, 4.911130. The OSM memorial node is at 52.366439, 4.911154.
- Numbers conflict, so I wrote "tens of thousands". The sources give 46,000 (joodsamsterdam), more than 50,000, and 60,000 to 80,000 (4en5mei).
- The last performance is given as 12 July (nl.wiki) or 19 July 1942 (joodsamsterdam citing Van Vree). I wrote "July 1942".
- Dropped, single source: Aus der Fünten rejecting the Portuguese Synagogue because of its windows (JCK); the Heijermans premieres, including Op hoop van zegen in 1900 (nl.wiki); the quote from wethouder Van 't Hull at the opening (JCK); the Primo Levi line on the wall (4en5mei); the "Piccadilly" party name (JCK).
- The 1962 architect is given as L. Waterman (nl.wiki) or H.H.P. Waterman with S.L. Harzt (4en5mei). I left it out.
- Pool overlaps: abc4cxzk (city cluster "The war in the Plantage"), ktqm7g4r (stumbling threshold at the tram stop, referred to in Look for), awjvl1lz (Café Eik en Linde), ujned0pv (Moederhuis), lisy94en (Van Hulst). abc4cxzk probably needs this place as a member, or this place replaces part of it: coordinator's call.

## C-07 Nationaal Holocaustmuseum
- Sources: JCK (location, the Kweekschool as a place of resistance, the deportation page, the exhibition page); nl.wikipedia (museum and Kindersmokkel); 4en5mei 1515; dagjeweg (misdadenbehang); PolyLester and NH Nieuws 330694 (the ladder).
- Pin: 52.366845, 4.910990, the old Kweekschool front at no. 27 (BAG 52.366883, 4.910953).
- Children saved: JCK says about 600 and joodsamsterdam "at least 600"; Flim's estimate is 500 to 700 (nl.wiki); 4en5mei and Wieberdink give 1,100. I wrote "about six hundred, estimates from five hundred to more than a thousand".
- The ladder monument: nl.wiki names Gabriel Lester. The PolyLester (Lester's studio) page and NH Nieuws describe a ladder to an open hatch, unveiled before the March 2024 opening. Its exact date is unconfirmed, so the draft gives none.
- Dropped: the King and President Herzog at the opening (wiki only, and not needed); the Joodse Raad card index (157,694 cards, UNESCO Memory of the World), because I found no confirmation that it is on display; the architect Winhov and the "Braziliaans" brick bond (wiki plus JCK, but left out for length); the ticket price of €20 (one source, and it will change).
- The caretaker, C.A. van Wijngaarden, is named only by JCK, so the draft leaves him out.
- Photo gap: none. Two photos are listed: the front (CC0) and the courtyard panels (CC BY 4.0).

## C-09 Nieuwe Keizersgracht 58
- Sources: Rijksmonument 2796; Presser, *Ondergang* (DBNL); nl.wikipedia, Joodsche Raad voor Amsterdam; JCK deportation page; joodsamsterdam; en.wikipedia Nieuwe Keizersgracht; tracesofwar 24388.
- Pin: the front, 52.364810, 4.904757.
- The start date conflicts: joodsamsterdam gives 25 October 1941; Presser cites the Joodsche Weekblad of 19 December 1941 announcing the new office; the Schaduwkade plate says February 1941 (the Council's founding, not this house). I wrote "from the end of 1941".
- Dropped, single source (joodsamsterdam): Nunes Henriques buying the plots in 1723-1724; a Jacob de Wit ceiling (the register mentions only a late-18th-century stucco ceiling); Sim Josephus Jitta from 1853; a diamond fund in 1920; crowds and mounted police in July 1942; the current use as psychotherapy practices.
- The plaque on the front is supported by joodsamsterdam (put up by neighbours, 2016) and an hmdb entry I could read only by title. If the plaque turns out not to be on the facade, drop that Look-for line.

## C-16 Geldersekade 105
- Sources: Anne Frank House via Google Arts & Culture (title, date, photographer); Duitsland Instituut, 2 May 2019 (description of the photo, read in the PDF, which shows the image); joodsmonument (Barend Jacobs, Aaltje Jacobs-Drukker); joodsamsterdam.
- **Conflict, check: Facts.** The joodsamsterdam guest essay says the photo shows no. 105 being kicked in at the Jacobs couple's arrest. But the photo is dated 26 May 1943, and both died at Sobibor on 21 May 1943. The draft does not link the photo to their arrest; it says only that they lived there and were murdered that month. Someone should compare the photo with the houses on site.
- Dropped: that Wijnne had a pharmacy opposite at no. 84a (joodsamsterdam only). Duitsland Instituut says only "from his window across the street".
- The guest essay's author is a living private person and is not named. No descendants are named.
- Photo: `Geldersekade 105.jpg` (CC BY-SA 3.0, WLM 2011). It shows a narrow house numbered 105 A-C with a Chinese bookshop; the shop may have changed since 2011.
- Pool neighbours: cd4t9q64 (Geldersekade 78), j5ynez7k.
