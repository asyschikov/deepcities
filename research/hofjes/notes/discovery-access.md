groups, open days, articles on hofjes closing). Per-hofje checks belong to `jordaan` and
`city`; single-hofje items met in passing are at the end as leads. Dates are the source's date;
"fetched" = the page as read on 2026-11-24.

### C. hofjesvannederland.nl / hetphoenixproject.nl (2025-2026 app, 33 Amsterdam hofjes)
Each entry has "Bezoekersinformatie" (Toegang, Openingstijden). **Do not use as evidence**: the
texts read machine-written, are undated, repeat one template ("weekdays c. 10:00-17:00, closed
weekends") and contradict primary sources (Begijnhof "usually 09:00-17:00" vs the Begijnhof's own
10:00-18:00). At most a lead for which hofjes to check. Data:
`https://www.hetphoenixproject.nl/hofjes/get_hofjes.php`.

### D. General statements (dated where possible)
- jordaanweb.nl, `HTML/hofjes.htm` (BMA text, undated): "Hofjes are in principle accessible,
  but more and more often they are locked." Its Jordaan map (`HTML/jordaankaarthofjes.htm`):
  "Not all hofjes are open to visitors. During the coronavirus groups are asked to stay away
  entirely."
- amsterdam-monumentenstad.nl, Hofje page (BMA text, undated): same sentence on locking.
- amsterdamsights.com (page 2026): "Many of them are free to enter ... as long as you don't
  break the peace"; "Some ... closed to the public, and only opened on special days ('open
  monuments days')". Tourist site, lead.
- Vereniging Ons Amsterdam, lecture announcement (16 Oct 2023, Henk Looijesteijn): Amsterdam
  hofjes are "shut away behind gates to stop tourists overrunning them", harder to visit than
  those of Haarlem and Leiden.
  https://verenigingonsamsterdam.nl/product/hofjes-in-amsterdam/
- Stadsherstel, on its Broek in Waterland hofje (14 Apr 2020): old hofjes are usually walled
  and "the homes are not accessible to visitors, certainly after a certain hour".
  https://stadsherstel.nl/nieuws/stadsherstel-en-de-vrienden-gaan-door-aankoop-wederopbouwhofje/
- NH Nieuws, 11 Aug 2026, on Haarlem (not Amsterdam): "people who live in hofjes already
  suffer from tourists peering in". Context only.
  https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/362356/haarlem-het-nieuwe-amsterdam-we-willen-hier-absoluut-geen-tiktok-rijen
- Het Parool, 12 Mar 2025, "8x verborgen hofjes in Amsterdam om stilletjes te genieten"
  (Bente Strijbosch; paywall, not read). Lead: a 2025 list of visitable hofjes.
  https://www.parool.nl/uit-in-amsterdam/8x-verborgen-hofjes-in-amsterdam-om-stilletjes-te-genieten~bb4ea309/
- No 2024-2026 news article found on Amsterdam hofjes closing to tourists (searched Parool,
  AT5, NH Nieuws, wijkkrant Jordaan & Gouden Reael 2005-2026).

## Rules that apply to every hofje in Centrum: guided groups

Gemeente Amsterdam, "Ontheffingsbeleid rondleidingen 2020" (CVDR637379), in force from
1 April 2020 for the whole of stadsdeel Centrum: a guide needs a permit for groups of 5 to 15;
max 15 participants; tours only 08:00-22:00; no standing still at "druktegevoelige plekken" such
as entrances of homes; no amplification; guides must ask participants to respect residents.
Survey (2019): around the Begijnhof over 80% of residents had nuisance from groups once or more
a day; counted 277 guided groups a week at the Begijnhof in 2019.
https://lokaleregelgeving.overheid.nl/CVDR637379/1
(Check the current version: the municipality's "Uitvoeringsprogramma bezoekerseconomie 2025/2026"
continues the policy; the 2025 PDF does not mention hofjes.)

## Single-hofje evidence met in passing (for the area slices)

- **Begijnhof** (C), official site, fetched 2026-11-24: open daily 10:00-18:00 incl. holidays;
  closed to tourists on Koningsdag (27 April); entrance and exit for tourists is the gate in the
  Begijnensteeg / Gedempte Begijnensloot; private ground; no bikes, dogs or wheeled suitcases; no
  eating or drinking, no bins; groups max 12; hosts at busy times; visitors only in the
  non-hatched part of the court. https://hetbegijnhof.nl/info/ and https://hetbegijnhof.nl/
- **Raepenhofje** (J): wijkkrant Jordaan & Gouden Reael, Feb-Mar 2026, walk 1: "Loop op de
  Palmgracht nr. 28-38 het Raepenhofje in" (walk into it). https://www.jordaangoudenreael.nl/documenten/00000221.pdf
- **Concordia Zuid** (J): wijkkrant Dec 2005/Jan 2006, a resident: the hofje has been closed at
  night "for some years" because of drunks from nearby bars. https://www.jordaangoudenreael.nl/documenten/00000092.pdf
- **Karthuizerhof** (J): Facebook video (2025, not an official page, lead only) with a comment
  "I thought it was no longer open to the public?".
- **Bossche Hofje** (J): owner Vereniging Hendrick de Keyser since 15 May 1997 (plaque, via
  hofjesinamsterdam.nl).
- **Hodshon-Dedelhofje** (C): owner Stadsherstel since 1980; amsterdamoudestad.nl says closed.
  https://stadsherstel.nl/monumenten/hodshon-dedelhofje/
- **Deutzenhofje** (C): Stadsherstel owns Kerkstraat 184-196, houses that once belonged to it,
  and has held open-monument walks there (date of event unreadable: page error).
- **Lindenhofje** (J): a children's hospice of the Leger des Heils (wijkkrant 2016, 2020, 2024).
- **Platanenhof** and **Looiershof** (J): used as public concert venues on Sunday afternoons
  (2019-2026), see above.
