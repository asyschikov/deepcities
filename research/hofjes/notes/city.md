## Corrections to our existing texts

- Three almshouses of the Weteringbuurt (mmoem3yv): "All three are homes; look from the street" is
  wrong for Grill's Hofje, which is open daily 07:00-19:00. Founders per Hofjesberaad: Anthonie
  Grill (1664-1727) and Elisabeth Grill (1661-1725), founded 1721, built 1724-1726.
- Van Brienenhofje (ijifjk0o): owner Het Oosten is now Stadgenoot (merger). Hours 2023 still the
  best evidence; the check can stay.
- Zon's Hofje (osr8dtiz): the check "whether the passage is open" can be answered: weekdays,
  about 10:00-17:00 (see unsure).
- Augustanakerk (gxemo5to): clear the "Still there?" check; the diaconate says the silence garden
  is open to visitors and passers-by, chapel 15:00-16:00.
- Nieuwe Suykerhofje (eu76f4la): chapel dated 1755 in our text; hofjesinamsterdam says c. 1777,
  the historian's substack 1772. Flag for a check.
- Liefde is 't Fondament (mrw332h6): RCOAK office here since 1982 (from Keizersgracht 384).
- Remonstrant almshouse (h9bk063j): founded by the 1882 bequest of Everdina de Lanoy to the
  Remonstrant congregation (Everdina de Lanoyhof); sold 2001 to Het Oosten (now Stadgenoot);
  residents students and starters. Our text says it was built by the Remonstrantse Gemeente.
- Hugo de Groothof (d716qm82): our text says the first stone was laid on 18 April 1882; Hofjesberaad
  and HIA give 11 April 1882 (by N.J. van Limmik jr.). Check the stones in the plinth.
- Gijsbert Bakker hofje (k6irx6il): the foundation sold all its property; now ordinary rentals.
- Hilmanhofje (w1d4xchf): now a supported-living project with a house master (2018).
- Looiershofje (e09fcw1w): text says the front and gate are seen from the street; it is in fact
  open by day (hours unconfirmed).
- Swigtershofje (x4qocbeu): De Key was the owner in 2018; parts let to a hotel and a trust.
- INVENTORY.json `wikipedia_missing`: "Claes Reinierszhofje" = mrw332h6, "Corvershof" = uxgxywib;
  both already in the pool.
- In the pool but missing from INVENTORY `in_pool`: Corvershof (+ Amstelrank), Hodshon and Van
  Limmik, Lutherhof, Hugo de Groothof, Vredenburgh, Falckshuis, Tweede Hugo de Grootstraat.
