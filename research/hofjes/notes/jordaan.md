## Concordia: three blocks (correction)

- Our place da5c… ("Concordia on the Westerstraat") is **Concordia Noordwest**: Westerstraat 327-405 and Lijnbaansgracht 63-65, 1862, 72 homes, the Bible-text block. Correct as placed.
- **Concordiahof (Noord)** is a separate, larger complex: Westerstraat 215-295 (RCE 6323) plus Anjeliersstraat 308-394 (city 209002), two inner courts reached by the gate at Westerstraat 221 and passages from the Anjeliersstraat. Sources saying "Westerstraat 221-289" mean this block. New entry and draft.
- Correction to da5c… Look for: it says gates at "221-289 and 327-405 into the inner courts of Concordia Noord". 327-405 is the place itself; Concordia Noord's courts are behind 215-295 / the Anjeliersstraat. It also says "free hofjes concerts on summer Sundays": the Concordiahof was a venue in 2019-2020, not in 2026. Rewrite both lines.
- Concordia Zuid (whv0…) is the third (Lijnbaansgracht/Elandsstraat side), Closed.
