## Merge proposals

### 15. Reimerswaal murals → `b3xm4nosj1qpcwoj3u8gs6ou` "Nieuw Reimerswaal, school and homes (Mecanoo)"

Why a merge: seven painted gables spread over several blocks (Ookmeerweg, Zonzeelstraat, Schoonboomstraat, Willemskerkestraat, Steelvlietplein), and no source gives the address of each one, so there is no single object to pin; the story is short.

EN (add to body, before "Visiting"):
In 2024 the illustrator Gino Bud Hoiting painted murals on the blank end walls of seven renovated blocks in the surrounding Reimerswaalbuurt, for the housing corporation Ymere. Children from two local primary schools wrote poems about how they see their neighbourhood; Hoiting based his designs on them, showed them to a children's art committee from both schools, and worked fragments of the poems into the walls. Each block has its own theme (light, water, birds, plants), the same themes as the drawings he had made earlier for the glass of the entrances.

Look for: the painted end walls of the blocks around the Reimerswaalstraat, with lines from the children's poems in them (one reads "Hier zoem zoem, daar zoem zoem").

NL:
In 2024 beschilderde illustrator Gino Bud Hoiting in opdracht van Ymere de witte kopgevels van zeven gerenoveerde woongebouwen in de Reimerswaalbuurt eromheen. Kinderen van twee basisscholen uit de buurt schreven gedichten over hoe zij hun buurt beleven; daarop baseerde Hoiting zijn ontwerpen, die hij voorlegde aan een kinderkunstcommissie van beide scholen. Stukjes van de gedichten staan nu op de muren. Elk gebouw heeft een eigen thema (licht, water, vogels, planten), net als de tekeningen die hij eerder op het glas van de portiekentrees maakte.

Let op: de beschilderde kopgevels rond de Reimerswaalstraat, met regels uit de kindergedichten (zoals "Hier zoem zoem, daar zoem zoem").

Links: Stadscuratorium https://stadscuratorium.nl/collectie/muurschilderingen-reimerswaal/ ; Ymere https://www.ymere.nl/nieuws/nieuwsberichten/2024-09-26-muurkunst-fleurt-reimerswaalbuurt-op/
Sources: Ymere news 26 Sep 2024 (seven white gables; children's poems from two primary schools; children's art committee; parts of the poems in the murals; themes light/sun, water, fauna/birds, flora; earlier entrance artworks; AFK co-funded); Stadscuratorium advice SCA.22.114 (10 June 2022, kopgevels; streets listed; entrance glass decorations); Stadscuratorium collection photo (2024) showing "HIER ZOEM ZOEM / DAAR ZOEM ZOEM". Note: the 2022 advice approved black-and-white designs, but the finished 2024 murals are in colour (photo and Ymere: "kleurrijke buurt"), so the text does not mention colour schemes.

### 18. 5 Elementen (gone) → `r4jmwk82cf1jvy3p93jbutv8` "Surinameplein housing by Roodenburgh"

EN (one sentence in the body, or a Look for line):
From December 2014 until July 2026 the roundabout held André Volten's huge bronze arches, part of a 1982 group from the Jaarbeursplein in Utrecht, lent for a few years and never sent back; the owner then sold them to the Verbeke Foundation in Kemzeke, Belgium, and the square is bare again.

NL:
Van december 2014 tot juli 2026 stonden op de rotonde de enorme bronzen bogen van André Volten, onderdeel van een groep uit 1982 van het Jaarbeursplein in Utrecht; ze kwamen voor een paar jaar in bruikleen en gingen nooit terug, tot de eigenaar ze verkocht aan de Verbeke Foundation in het Belgische Kemzeke. Het plein is weer kaal.

Link: De Westkrant https://www.dewestkrant.nl/enorme-bronzen-bogen-zijn-weg-van-het-surinameplein/
Sources: De Westkrant 15 Jul 2026 (bogen since Dec 2014, removed on a Sunday and taken to Belgium; made 1982 for the Jaarbeursplein; loan, never returned; gemeente told residents in June that the loan would not be extended; owner sold to Verbeke Foundation, Kemzeke; future of the empty square unclear); nl.wikipedia "Zes cirkelelementen" (unveiled 14 Jan 2015; Parool 14 Jul 2026 "Het Surinameplein blijft kaal achter"). ## Conflicts and uncertainties

- Corned Beef: when the cattle market and abattoir left is disputed. Stadscuratorium and nl.wikipedia say 1988; the live place `ixnb2giip3y4qys3mkb7cj3w` says around 1980 (some sources 1974). The text avoids a year.
- H-Balk: made 1965 (Wikipedia, Commons), placed in the Sloterpark 1968 (Stadscuratorium, Wikipedia). Attribute set to "1965 (here since 1968)". OSM says 1966.
- 178 Bottles: nl.wikipedia says ALF 2015; Parool says made for the ALF in 2014. Text says "made in 2014 for the Amsterdam Light Festival" without the edition year.
- RISE: Stadscuratorium and Wikipedia say 2023, the nl.wikipedia list says 2024. Opening Saturday 30 Sep 2023 (Rodi, 3 Oct 2023). Palm lines: buitenkunst says 101; Rodi says 6 named plus 95 others (also 101).
- 5 Elementen: titles differ (5 Elementen, Vijf elementen, Zes cirkelelementen, Drie monumentale sculpturen) and the year of the Utrecht installation (1980 per Stadscuratorium, 1982 per Westkrant and Wikipedia). The merge sentence uses 1982 (the more recent and detailed sources).
- Reimerswaal: 2022 advice speaks of six gables and black-and-white designs; Ymere and Stadscuratorium (2024) say seven, in colour. Merge uses seven.
- Familie and Corned Beef sit in Oost; the slice heading said West and Nieuw-West.
