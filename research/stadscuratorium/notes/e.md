## Merge proposals

### 1. Ruigoord and the Sint-Gertrudiskerk (`jbyuj41uiemtajl9n2l8ir0h`): Gedenk te overstromen

Add as a paragraph before "Look for:".

EN:

> Since Ruigoord turned fifty in 2023 the village has also been an open-air art route, the Oase van Verwondering (Oasis of Wonder), free and open every day from sunrise to sunset; some of its works are meant to rot back into the ground. One of them, *Gedenk te overstromen* (Remember to flood) by Collectief Walden, commemorates a disaster that has not happened yet: the flooding of Amsterdam, which the group expected within 250 years, by 2275 at the latest. Taking their cue from the "hunger stones" people once left in European riverbeds as warnings, they began building it in 2022 in the Tolhuistuin, on Shell's former grounds, and pitched it there in 2023 as a request to the mayor for a temporary permit: for 250 years. The finished monument was unveiled at the RE_NATURE festival on Ruigoord in 2024. The collective disbanded at the end of that year.

## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **De Muur height**: Wikipedia gives about 4 m, the Stadscuratorium and NRC 5 m. The text gives only the length (15 m, all agree).
- **De Muur remake date**: Het Hamerkwartier says "end of November" 2020; Wikipedia and Het Parool (29 Dec 2020) say December. Text says December.
- **De Muur recent status**: newest photos on Commons are from May 2022; the Stadscuratorium lists it in its 2026 collection and NRC wrote about it in October 2025 without saying it is gone. No sign of removal.
- **Gedenk te overstromen at the Tolhuistuin**: the Tolhuistuin's "art on the site" page still lists it with "Locatie: Buiksloterweg". That page also lists older works and looks out of date; the collective itself says the monument went to Ruigoord. What, if anything, is still at the Tolhuistuin is not confirmed, which is why the merge goes to Ruigoord. Nobody describes the form of the finished Ruigoord monument in text; the only photo (ruigoord.nl, June 2025 upload) shows a performance on a structure of oil drums on wire cages filled with bricks, but whether that is the monument itself is not stated, so the merge text does not describe it.
- **Gedenk te overstromen group make-up**: the Stadscuratorium says philosopher, biologist, dramaturg and scenographer; the collective's own farewell says biologist, philosopher, dramaturg, mime and producer. Left out.
- **BuurtVuur unveiling date**: 30 October 2025 comes only from a search snippet of the artist's Instagram; NRIT (7 Nov 2025) confirms it had opened. Text says "end of October 2025".
