# Scout: Contemporary Architecture, Oost and IJburg

Area: Oost (Oud-Oost, Dapperbuurt, Indische Buurt, Watergraafsmeer, Amstelkwartier), the
Eastern Docklands (Java, KNSM, Borneo-Sporenburg, Cruquius), IJburg, Zeeburgereiland, Science
Park, Diemen. Shortlist only, nothing drafted.

Main sources: Arcam's Architectuurgids map feed for district 82 (Oost), all 200 entries read
one by one (architect and year from each page; a per-year re-query 1995 to 2026 found no
entries beyond the 200, so the sweep is complete), Arcam's AAP 2024 and 2026 nominee pages,
nl.wikipedia's AAP winners table (cross-check), NH Nieuws (BNA Beste Gebouw 2023), UvA and
Benthem Crouwel on LAB42, ArchDaily's Diemen tag. Existing-place check: `pnpm dc places sights
--all` dump plus `--q` per candidate. Pins in brackets are Arcam map pins (lat, lng).

Overlap note: Arcam's Oost district also covers the Oostelijke Handelskade, Oostenburg and the
Plantage edge, which the brief gives to `centrum-ij`. Those are listed under Borderline, not in
the main list, except where noted.

## Shortlist (ordered by strength)

1. **Sluishuis** (2022; BIG with Barcode Architects) (E: "Sluishuis", dueh7jpz2cx3g7v0nk7e6m6z): a block standing in the IJmeer with two corners lifted and two dropped, a public roof route stepping up one side and a marina in its belly. Evidence: Arcam guide, Dezeen, ArchDaily, wide international press. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/sluishuis/ Type: new. Address Haringbuiskade, Steigereiland. Caveat: homes private, roof route and quays public.
2. **Hotel Jakarta** (2018; SeARCH) (N): timber-framed hotel with a subtropical garden atrium on the tip of Java-eiland, where emigrants once left for the Indies. Evidence: AAP 2019 public prize; Arcam "15 jaar AAP" feature. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/hotel-jakarta/ Type: new. Address Javakade 766.
3. **Amsterdam University College** (2012; Mecanoo, Francine Houben) (N): a ring of brick-and-glass student housing and teaching around a garden court at Science Park. Evidence: AAP 2013 winner. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/amsterdam-university-college/ Type: new. Address Science Park 113. Caveat: campus, courtyard semi-public.
4. **Wooncoöperatie De Warren** (2022; Natrufied Architecture) (E: "De Warren, Amsterdam's first housing co-op", r71hlbvs6zeveot5vw9d32o2): timber co-op block built and run by its residents, shared spaces stacked through the building. Evidence: AAP 2023 public prize. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-warren/ Type: new. Centrumeiland, IJburg. Caveat: private homes.
5. **Jonas** (2022; Orange Architects) (N): a cliff-like block on the IJburg waterfront with a carved, wood-lined "canyon" atrium running through it. Evidence: BNA Beste Gebouw van het Jaar 2023 (NH Nieuws); Arcam guide. https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/318865/amsterdams-gebouw-jonas-beste-2023 Type: new. Pin (52.35311, 5.00331), Centrumeiland. Caveat: private; atrium access limited.
6. **The Whale** (2000; de Architekten Cie., Frits van Dongen) (E: "The Whale (Sporenburg)", wljwzs0xspf11fl1hkc5u4cq): a zinc-clad block with its belly and roof tilted up to let light into the court; the landmark of Borneo-Sporenburg. Evidence: Arcam guide, Kemme, every Docklands route. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/the-whale/ Type: new. Address Baron G.A. Tindalplein. Caveat: private homes, court visible.
7. **Python Bridge and the Borneo-Sporenburg bridges** (2001; West 8, Adriaan Geuze) (E: "Python Bridge (Hoge brug)", k27w3j9a0l1f4ynq8z299224): the red wriggling footbridge over the Spoorwegbassin. Evidence: Arcam guide (as "Bruggen Borneo-Sporenburg"), international press. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/bruggen-borneo-sporenburg/ Type: new.
8. **Enneüs Heermabrug** (2001; Grimshaw) (E: "Enneüs Heermabrug", b67273p5w1fai6rv13tdtsuj): the white "Zeeburgerbrug" with its rippling arches, gateway to IJburg. Evidence: Arcam guide, Grimshaw project page. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/enneus-heermabrug/ Type: new.
9. **Nesciobrug** (2006; Wilkinson Eyre with Arup) (E: "Nesciobrug", z6xh4nj8s4it2fhgckawrjxz): the longest bike-and-foot bridge in the Netherlands, a self-anchored suspension span over the Amsterdam-Rijnkanaal toward Diemen. Evidence: Arcam guide, Wilkinson Eyre, structural press. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/nesciobrug/ Type: new. Caveat: on the Amsterdam/Diemen border.
10. **LAB42** (2022; Benthem Crouwel Architects) (N): UvA informatics building at Science Park, energy-neutral and fully demountable, steel frame designed for reuse, public ground floor. Evidence: BLT Built Design Award, National Steel Award 2024, European Steel Design Award 2025, Architectenweb honourable mention. https://www.benthemcrouwel.com/projects/lab42 Type: new. Address Science Park 900. Caveat: not in Arcam's guide (yet).
11. **Waterbuurt West** (2010; Marlies Rohmer with Villanova architecten) (E: "Waterbuurt West, the floating neighbourhood", pe7esuhcoyw6bcpqega5q9p7): about 75 floating houses on jetties off Steigereiland, the Dutch floating-homes prototype. Evidence: Arcam guide, international press. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/waterbuurt-west/ Type: new. Caveat: private homes, jetties public.
12. **Het Funen** (2005 onward; plan de Architekten Cie., buildings by NL Architects, Geurst & Schulze, Dick van Gameren and others) (E: "Het Funen, a car-free quarter on top of the last bastion", yx7lrrl0q75vv0gvv24niz8v): car-free "architectural puzzle" of freestanding blocks in a triangular park. Evidence: Arcam guide, Kemme. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/het-funen/ Type: new. Caveat: live entry sits under borough Centrum.
13. **HAUT** (2022; Team V Architectuur, Arup) (E: "HAUT, the timber tower", bufcczstxwp0954iao2zrskx): 73 m hybrid timber tower on the Amstel, one of the tallest timber residential towers in the world when built. Evidence: Dezeen, ArchDaily, international timber press. https://www.teamv.nl/en/projects/haut Type: new. Address Amstelkwartier. Caveat: not in Arcam's Oost list; private homes.
14. **De Nieuwe Meent** (2025; Office Raumplan, Time to Access with Roel van der Zeeuw) (N): a resident-led housing co-op in the Watergraafsmeer (details to check). Evidence: AAP 2026 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-nieuwe-meent/ Type: new. Pin (52.35296, 4.94694). Caveat: private homes.
15. **Bakstayn** (2025; Time to Access) (N): a cooperative housing block on IJburg built by its own association. Evidence: AAP 2026 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/bakstayn/ Type: new. Pin (52.35296, 5.01354), Centrumeiland/Strandeiland edge. Caveat: private homes.
16. **De Telefoondienst** (2025; Ronald Janssen Architecten, original Albert Boeken 1923) (E: "Telephone exchange, Middenweg (De Telefoondienst)", lh7yqrhjd7kiuuyrh0o4697f): the Amsterdam School telephone exchange turned into homes. Evidence: AAP 2026 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-telefoondienst/ Type: remodel (conversion to housing). Address Middenweg.
17. **De Vrijheid, Bajeskwartier** (2025; Atelier Kempe Thill) (E, part of "Bajeskwartier and Bajesdorp", nrx2dgcndexsweb2agamfjtl): the first big block on the site of the demolished Bijlmerbajes prison. Evidence: AAP 2026 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-vrijheid-bajeskwartier/ Type: new. Pin (52.33702, 4.92496). Caveat: rest of Bajeskwartier still being built; Bajesdorp (Studio Seven, 2023, also in Arcam) belongs in the same entry.
18. **AMST** (2023; VenhoevenCS) (N): a mixed block by the Amstelstation (programme to check). Evidence: AAP 2024 nominee (VenhoevenCS news), Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/amst/ Type: new. Pin (52.34667, 4.91913).
19. **Jakoba Mulderhuis** (2022; de Architekten Cie., Marc Koehler Architects, Powerhouse Company) (N): the HvA's new Amstelcampus building on the Weesperzijde (stacked open study spaces, check details). Evidence: AAP 2023 nominee (Amsterdam UAS news). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/jakoba-mulderhuis/ Type: new. Pin (52.3593, 4.90779). Caveat: university, lobby accessible.
20. **Lloyd Hotel** (2004; MVRDV, original Evert Breman 1921) (E: "Lloyd Hotel and the Lloyd complex", qv0a05zf47i06yulvlmtq4dd): emigrants' hotel, then prison, then artists' squat, remade as a "cultural embassy" with 1- to 5-star rooms. Evidence: Arcam guide, MVRDV, international press. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/lloyd-hotel/ Type: remodel (conversion, new interiors). Address Oostelijke Handelskade 34. Caveat: Oostelijke Handelskade, may also appear in `centrum-ij`.
21. **Smiley** (2016; Studioninedots) (N): a curving wall of student and starter flats on Zeeburgereiland that bends into a grin around its court. Evidence: Arcam guide, ArchDaily. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/smiley/ Type: new. Pin (52.37141, 4.96616). Caveat: private.
22. **Islamitisch Uitvaartpaviljoen** (2012; Atelier PUUUR) (N): the Netherlands' first purpose-built Islamic funeral pavilion, a slatted timber box in De Nieuwe Ooster cemetery. Evidence: Arcam guide, Architectenweb. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/islamitisch-uitvaartpaviljoen/ Type: new. Pin (52.34163, 4.93698). Could hang off E "De Nieuwe Ooster" (bjtlsgawp94nm7bw59v7zc2r).
23. **De Nieuwe Ooster office and funeral centre** (2012; Bierman Henket, landscape Karres en Brands) (N): cemetery's new entrance building plus Karres en Brands' landscaped urn gardens. Evidence: Arcam guide; Karres en Brands' cemetery work widely published. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/kantoor-en-rouwcentrum-de-nieuwe-ooster/ Type: new. Could also hang off E bjtlsgawp94nm7bw59v7zc2r.
24. **Volkshotel** (2014; Bureau Kraaijvanger, Steven Steenbruggen) (N; the street entry "Wibautstraat, the newspaper street" jphe2n6mbe6vwjkkd7h4kqio mentions it): the Volkskrant's newspaper building turned hotel with a rooftop club and hot tubs. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/volkshotel/ Type: remodel (office to hotel). Address Wibautstraat 150.
25. **Studio Thonik** (2001 MVRDV; extension 2020 MMX-Architecten with Thonik) (N): MVRDV's bright orange studio for the graphic designers Thonik, later topped up. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/studio-thonik/ Type: new + remodel (2020 extension). Pin (52.35463, 4.91158).
26. **Ruby Emma Hotel (ex Qo)** (2018; Paul de Ruiter Architects, Mulderblauw) (N): a hotel tower by the Amstel with a "breathing" facade of moving panels and a rooftop greenhouse. Evidence: AAP 2019 nominee (Paul de Ruiter news), Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/qo-hotel/ Type: new. Address Amstelvlietstraat 4.
27. **De Kauwgomballenfabriek** (2012; Jinx Architecten, Mei Architecten) (N): former chewing-gum factory turned creative workspace on the Amstel edge. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-kauwgomballenfabriek/ Type: remodel (factory to studios). Pin (52.33366, 4.92119).
28. **De Gieter en De Slijper** (2023; Space Encounters, LOLA) (N): two timber-hybrid blocks on the old Oostenburg works with a raised collective garden. Evidence: AAP 2024 nominee (Arcam). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-gieter-en-de-slijper/ Type: new. Pin (52.37265, 4.92811). Caveat: Oostenburg sits in Centrum; may be `centrum-ij`'s.
29. **Houtrak** (2025; Marcel Lok_Architect, Workshop Architecten) (N): a timber housing block on Oostenburg. Evidence: AAP 2026 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/houtrak/ Type: new. Pin (52.37183, 4.92908). Caveat: Oostenburg, as above.
30. **Akropolis, De Generaal** (2017; Studioninedots) (N): Zeeburgereiland housing with a temple-like, raised courtyard. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/akropolis-de-generaal/ Type: new. Pin (52.37602, 4.96445).
31. **De Loswal** (2005; UNStudio) (N): the only UNStudio housing in Oost, a sinuous facade on the Cruquius side. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-loswal/ Type: new. Pin (52.36638, 4.94522).
32. **Vrijburcht** (2007; Hein de Haan, CASA) (E: "Vrijburcht and its fish wall", dccz2puust6ktiq2zrh42sw7): early collective self-build complex with a theatre and a fish wall on Steigereiland. Evidence: wide CPO literature; Arcam lists Hein de Haan's Nautilus nearby. Type: new.
33. **De Witte Kaap** (2010; Claus en Kaan) (E: "De Witte Kaap", kxnm07444exidgvz0do638rz): the white block at the tip of Haveneiland. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/witte-kaap/ Type: new.
34. **Telefooncentrale IJburg (Blok 30)** (2001; Atelier Zeinstra van der Pol) (N): IJburg's very first building, a windowless KPN exchange given a sculpted skin. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/telefooncentrale-ijburg-blok-30/ Type: new. Pin (52.3538, 5.00015).
35. **Harbour Club** (2021; LEVS architecten) (N): restaurant-hall in the old harbour landscape of Cruquius. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/harbour-club/ Type: new or remodel (check). Pin (52.36931, 4.94728).
36. **Pillows Grand Boutique Hotel Maurits at the Park** (2022; Office Winhov) (N): a 19th-century building on the Mauritskade by the Oosterpark reworked into a hotel (check the original use). Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/pillows-grand-boutique-hotel-maurits-at-the-park/ Type: remodel. Pin (52.36192, 4.91998).
37. **Langhaus and Hofhaus** (2001; Diener & Diener) (N): Swiss restraint on the KNSM-eiland, a long block and a court block. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/langhaus-hofhaus/ Type: new. Pin (52.37644, 4.93629). Caveat: private.
38. **Hoop, Liefde en Fortuin** (2002; Rudy Uytenhaak) (N): three blocks with Uytenhaak's patterned brick on the Entrepot side. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/hoop-liefde-en-fortuin/ Type: new. Pin (52.37099, 4.93698).
39. **Datacenter AM4** (2017; Benthem Crouwel) (N): Equinix data centre at Science Park in a perforated facade, the internet hub made visible. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/datacenter-am4/ Type: new. Could link to E "CWI and the Science Park internet hub" (z2pkmjflnz68ahb9mpzjde6a).
40. **150 kV Station Science Park** (2008; Schutter ETH) (N): a high-voltage substation as a sculpted box. Evidence: AAP 2009 nominee, Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/150-kv-station-science-park/ Type: new. Caveat: no entry.
41. **Hoofdkantoor Waternet** (2006; Herman Hertzberger) (N): late Hertzberger office by the Amstel. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/hoofdkantoor-waternet/ Type: new. Address Korte Ouderkerkerdijk 7.
42. **Booster Oost and Booster Zuid** (2005 and 2006; Bekkering Adams; Group A) (N): sewage pumping stations dressed as sculpture. Evidence: Arcam guide (two entries). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/booster-oost/ Type: new. Caveat: no entry; works.
43. **Jan Schaeferbrug** (2001; VenhoevenCS) (N): the bridge that runs through a warehouse (Pakhuis Afrika) to Java-eiland. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/jan-schaeferbrug/ Type: new. Caveat: lands on the Oostelijke Handelskade, may overlap `centrum-ij`.
44. **Einde van de Wereld** (2000; Meyer en Van Schooten) (N): the housing at the eastern tip of Java-eiland. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/einde-van-de-wereld/ Type: new. Could fold into E "Java-eiland canals and bridges" (qs3dakpvmuac90i44ltf2pr3).
45. **Homestead Diemen (Woonhoeve)** (2017; Marcel Lok_Architect) (N): twelve flats in a long barn-like volume around a shared yard on a former organic nursery. Evidence: ArchDaily; Arie Keppler Prijs 2018 entry. https://www.archdaily.com/890395/homestead-diemen-marcel-lok-architect Type: new. Caveat: private; the only Diemen building with real architectural press.

- IJburg's self-build plots (S-House VMX, Villa K01 Studioninedots, Larikslaan, Lisdoddelaan, Tamariskhof, Cornelis Zillesenlaan, Pedro Nunesstraat etc., about 25 Arcam entries 2003 to 2013): a well-documented private-house laboratory; better as one group entry than 25 pins.
- IJburg blocks (Blok 4 Maccreanor Lavington, 23 VMX, 11AB, 47, 24, 62, Solid 18 Claus en Kaan, Emmy Andriese, Havenmeester, Vrijmanseind etc.): in Arcam, but ordinary housing by today's bar; maybe one "IJburg housing lab" entry.

- Sporenboog (2004), Cruquiuskwartier (2008), Borneohof (2011), Borneodriehoek (2005), Fusion (2008), Het Luycksterrein (2007), Casa 400 (2010), Tugelawegblokken (2015), De Smaragd (2016), Amstelloft (2016), Kraaipan (2013): Arcam-listed housing, solid but not stand-out.

- Tropenpunt (2002, Erick van Egeraat), Kriterion Studentenpomp (2002), P+R Zeeburg (2005), QFactory (2009), Laterna Magica daycare (2011), Werkplaats Steigereiland (2011), Restaurant De Kas (2001, greenhouse made restaurant), Paviljoen Puur (2012): small or slight.
