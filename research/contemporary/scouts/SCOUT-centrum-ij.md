# Scout: Contemporary Architecture, Centrum and the IJ banks

Area: Centrum (canal belt, Wallen, Nieuwmarkt, Plantage, the eastern islands Kattenburg,
Wittenburg, Oostenburg and the Marineterrein), the IJ banks (Stationseiland, Oosterdokseiland,
Westerdokseiland, IJdok, Oostelijke Handelskade / Piet Heinkade) and the Houthavens.
Shortlist only, nothing drafted.

Main sources: Arcam's Architectuurgids map data (all pins in the area, about 370 entries read
one by one for year, architect, address), Arcam's AAP pages 2008 to 2026 (winners and the ten
nominees per year), nl.wikipedia's AAP winners table (cross-check), nl.wikipedia Stadsschouwburg
article. Existing-place check: full `pnpm dc places sights --all` dump plus `--q` per candidate.
Lead link is the Arcam guide page unless noted. "AAP nom." = nominated for the Amsterdamse
Architectuurprijs that year.

Overlap note: Arcam files Oostelijke Handelskade / Veemkade and the Houthavens under Oost and
West; I kept them here per the area split. Silodam, Pontsteiger and the Houthavens blocks may
also turn up in SCOUT-west-nieuw-west.

## Shortlist (ordered by strength)

1. **Nationaal Holocaust Namenmonument** (2021; Studio Libeskind with Rijnboutt) (N): a maze of brick walls, one brick per murdered Dutch Jew, Roma and Sinti, with steel letters reading "in memory of" in Hebrew from above; fought by neighbours in court for years. Evidence: AAP 2022 jury prize; Dezeen, ArchDaily. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/nationaal-holocaust-namenmonument/ Type: new (memorial building). Address: Weesperstraat 3. Caveats: none; only the nearby Monument van Joodse Erkentelijkheid (nuktl5uy3xu0mvlfdo2uey91) is on the map.
2. **Nationaal Holocaustmuseum** (2024; Office Winhov) (N): the old Reformed teachers' college opposite the Hollandsche Schouwburg, where children were smuggled out, opened up so the surviving fabric tells the story. Evidence: AAP 2025 jury prize. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/nationaal-holocaustmuseum/ Type: remodel (19th-century school turned museum). Address: Plantage Middenlaan 27. Caveats: the area cluster "The war in the Plantage, around the Hollandsche Schouwburg" (abc4cxzkbb2up9bt5ofdd59b) touches it but the museum building has no place.
3. **Universiteitsbibliotheek UvA** (2025; Buro van Stigt with MVSA) (E: "Binnengasthuis, from dreaded hospital to university library", t1krd0r563cdq8veimt97ml7): the old hospital wards made into the university library, behind a screen of 3D-printed bronze-coated letters spelling a line of Nijhoff's "Awater" in 24 languages and 6 scripts. Evidence: AAP 2026 jury prize (with De Nieuwe Meent). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/universiteitsbibliotheek-uva/ Type: remodel (monumental hospital buildings plus new screen and interiors). Address: Vendelstraat 2.
4. **Trippenhuis complex** (2019; Office Winhov) (E: "Trippenhuis, the arms dealers' palace", yjq5jq1ioc8486w9luwqohcl): the Academy of Sciences' 17th-century palace and its back buildings knitted into one, the jury's model of how to redo a monument. Evidence: AAP 2020 jury prize. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/trippenhuiscomplex/ Type: remodel. Address: Kloveniersburgwal 29. Caveats: office interior, visible mainly from the canal and on open days.
5. **Het Scheepvaartmuseum** (2011; Dok architecten, Ney & Partners, Rappange) (E: "Het Scheepvaartmuseum and the ship Amsterdam", rjcpk14s3jr8sj7gl2yjq1db): the Admiralty storehouse's courtyard roofed in glass on a steel grid drawn from old sea charts' compass lines. Evidence: AAP 2012. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/s-lands-zeemagazijn-scheepvaartmuseum/ Type: remodel (1656 storehouse, new courtyard roof). Address: Kattenburgerplein 1.
6. **Fietsparkeergarage Stationsplein** (2023; wUrck) (E: "The bicycle garage under the water at Stationsplein", ald0hvuu778j1ig0zxjhew18): 7,000 bikes parked under the Open Havenfront, entered from the quay like a cellar. Evidence: AAP 2023 jury prize. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/fietsenstalling-stationsplein/ Type: new (infrastructure). Address: Stationsplein.
7. **Rokin metro station** (2018; Benthem Crouwel, Noord/Zuidlijn) (E: "Rokin station: the archaeologists' wall and Wilhelmina on horseback", ami94e2gi27i7sj865fi756u): the deep Noord/Zuidlijn stations, whose whole line took the AAP. Evidence: AAP 2019 jury prize (Noord/Zuidlijn). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/noord-zuidlijn/ Type: new (infrastructure). Address: Rokin. Caveats: the existing place is about the find-wall art; the theme entry would be the station itself.
8. **Vijzelgracht metro station** (2018; Benthem Crouwel) (E: "Ramses Shaffy's face over the longest escalator, Vijzelgracht metro", cbzh9wvssffy2wxvvhmeu29s): the longest escalators in the Benelux under the canal belt. Evidence: AAP 2019 (Noord/Zuidlijn). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/noord-zuidlijn/ Type: new. Caveats: same as Rokin, the place is the artwork; decide whether to list or reframe.
9. **IJhal, Centraal Station** (2017; Wiel Arets Architects) (N): the new passenger hall on the IJ side of the station, linking the trains to the bus station and ferries. Evidence: AAP public prize (Wikipedia table; Arcam lists it in the 2018 nominees and in "15 jaar AAP"). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/ijhal/ Type: new (inside the 1889 station).
10. **Conservatorium van Amsterdam** (2008; de Architekten Cie., Frits van Dongen) (N): a glass box of five concert halls stacked in colour-coded floors, with fish-scale glass fins on the sawtooth front. Evidence: AAP nom. 2009. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/conservatorium/ Type: new. Address: Oosterdokskade 151.
11. **OBA Oosterdok** (2007; Jo Coenen) (E: "OBA Oosterdok, the library with a free view", i49qa98lxns1x90vlsxrpmxf): the country's biggest public library, with a free rooftop view. Evidence: AAP nom. 2008. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/openbare-bibliotheek-amsterdam/ Type: new. Address: Oosterdokskade 143.
12. **Booking.com Campus** (2022; UNStudio, Ben van Berkel) (N): glass headquarters that cantilevers 23 metres over the railway, with 41 flats and a club named after the old Post CS nightclub. Evidence: Arcam guide (Groene Architectuurgids); Dezeen, ArchDaily. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/booking-hq/ Type: new. Address: Oosterdoksstraat 158. Caveats: offices private; ground floor and passages public.
13. **Muziekgebouw aan 't IJ and the Bimhuis** (2005; 3XN) (E: "Muziekgebouw aan 't IJ and the Bimhuis", bl7qb8jvn5x9g1oa2xh1bwh5): glass concert hall on the IJ with the black Bimhuis box cantilevered off it. Evidence: widely published (3XN, ArchDaily). Type: new. Address: Piet Heinkade 1.
14. **Hermitage Amsterdam, now H'ART Museum** (2009; Hans van Heeswijk) (E: "H'ART Museum in the Amstelhof", rdwnvux0lssmnnlu9hihuuzy): the 17th-century old people's home made into a museum with an underground entrance hall. Evidence: AAP nom. 2010. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/hermitage-amsterdam/ Type: remodel. Address: Amstel 51. Caveats: Hermitage name dropped in 2023 after the Russian invasion.
15. **De Nederlandsche Bank** (2024; Mecanoo, on Duintjer's 1968 bank) (E: "De Nederlandsche Bank, the gold vault anyone can walk into", edil67xueaar1irciltvq49k): the closed central bank opened to the public, old gold vault included. Evidence: AAP nom. 2025. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-nederlandsche-bank-2/ Type: remodel. Address: Frederiksplein 61.
16. **Pontsteiger** (2019; Arons & Gelauff) (E: "Pontsteiger", hovzau2tsb28z8w98v918cau): an arch of flats on a peninsula in the IJ, the top floors bridging over a gap you can see the water through. Evidence: AAP nom. 2020. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/pontsteiger/ Type: new. Address: Pontsteiger, Houthavens.
17. **Silodam** (2002; MVRDV) (E: "Silodam (MVRDV)", f7vjybzqezxdn3wnm9jumub9): a stacked container ship of flats on piles in the IJ, each "container" a different housing type and colour. Evidence: ArchDaily, MVRDV, every Dutch guide. Type: new. Address: Silodam, Houthavens. Caveats: also West's territory.
18. **IJdock and the Paleis van Justitie** (2012-2013; Claus en Kaan; Van Gameren Mastenbroek; Zeinstra Van Gelderen) (N): an artificial peninsula west of the station with the Court of Appeal as a white "palace" with porthole windows facing the cruise ships, a 15-metre-wide glass hotel and housing. Evidence: AAP nom. 2013 (Paleis van Justitie). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/paleis-van-justitie/ and https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/ijdock/ Type: new. Address: IJdok 20 (court), IJdok 6.
19. **Mövenpick Hotel** (2006; Claus en Kaan) (N): a hotel tower clad in bands of green granite, white marble and glass on a plinth running from the Muziekgebouw to the cruise terminal; famously closed, no floor visible from outside. Evidence: AAP nom. 2008. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/movenpick-hotel/ Type: new. Address: Piet Heinkade 5.
20. **Lloyd Hotel** (2004; MVRDV with Evert Breman's 1921 building) (E: "Lloyd Hotel and the Lloyd complex", qv0a05zf47i06yulvlmtq4dd): the emigrants' hotel and later youth prison remade as a "1 to 5 star" hotel and cultural embassy. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/lloyd-hotel/ Type: remodel. Address: Oostelijke Handelskade 34.
21. **Passenger Terminal Amsterdam** (2000; HOK, Larry Malcic) (N): a wave-shaped glass-and-steel roof for cruise passengers on the IJ. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/passagiersterminal-amsterdam-pta/ Type: new. Address: Piet Heinkade 27. Caveats: debated, as the city decided to move sea cruises out of the centre; check current use.
22. **Arcam pavilion** (2003; René van Zuuk) (E: "Arcam's folded pavilion and the Museumhaven", ydcx3eia4ko7v4fz7s84x4yl): one folded zinc skin forming walls and roof. Evidence: widely published; Arcam's own home. Type: new. Address: Prins Hendrikkade 600.
23. **Ons' Lieve Heer op Solder, new museum building** (2015; Claus Van Wageningen) (E: "Our Lord in the Attic (Ons' Lieve Heer op Solder)", q459if11mofdctbi93ajiglz): a quiet new house next door with an underground passage, so the attic church no longer has to carry the visitors. Evidence: AAP nom. 2016. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/ons-lieve-heer-op-solder/ Type: new plus remodel. Address: Oudezijds Voorburgwal 38-40.
24. **Rabozaal and the Stadsschouwburg extension** (2009; Jonkman Klinkhamer, Jim Klinkhamer) (N): a black-box theatre built partly over the Melkweg with a foyer that hangs over the Lijnbaansgracht. Evidence: AAP nom. 2010; nl.wikipedia Stadsschouwburg. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/rabozaal-melkweg/ Type: new (extension). Address: Leidseplein 26 / Lijnbaansgracht.
25. **DeLaMar Theater** (2010; Jo Coenen) (E: "Maison Stroucken, the party hall inside DeLaMar", f6wig611lr5t2vh8ebutbcfn): a glass skin wrapped round two theatres and an 1881 party-hall front.
