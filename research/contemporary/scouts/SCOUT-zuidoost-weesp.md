# Scout: Contemporary Architecture, zuidoost-weesp (Zuidoost, Duivendrecht/Ouder-Amstel, Weesp, Amstelland, Schiphol area)

Shortlist only, nothing drafted. Main sweep: Arcam's guide feed for district 85 (Zuidoost, 49
entries, every page read for architect, year and address). Outside Amsterdam: web searches on
prizes (AAP lists, BNA, Arie Keppler, RIBA, European Hotel Design Award), architects' pages,
Detail, Dezeen. Checked against `pnpm dc places sights --all` (2,306 places) by word and by a
bounding box over Zuidoost and Weesp. Years are completion years as the sources give them.

Overall: Zuidoost is rich (the Bijlmer renewal since 1992 produced a whole catalogue of 2000s
housing), but almost none of it won a prize; Kleiburg, the Bijlmer ArenA station and
Kraaiennest are the only real prize winners. Weesp, Duivendrecht, Ouderkerk and Uithoorn are
nearly dry. The Schiphol area gives two solid buildings.

## Shortlist (ordered by strength)

1. **Kleiburg, "De Flat" renovation** (2016; NL Architects with XVW architectuur, on F. Ottenhof's 1971 slab) (E: "Kleiburg (\"De Flat\")", jqh6xpfsjb1ozu87k7738b9k): the last original honeycomb slab, sold for one euro and saved as a "klusflat": shell homes buyers finish themselves. Evidence: EU Mies van der Rohe Award 2017 (Arcam guide states it). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/kleiburg/ Type: remodel (exterior, galleries, lifts, ground floor; interiors left to owners). Caveats: residential, the 450 m gallery is private. Address: Kleiburg (Arcam gives Kleiburg 24C, 1104 EA).
2. **Station Amsterdam Bijlmer ArenA** (2007; Grimshaw with Arcadis, Jan van Belkum and Neven Sidor) (N): a long white roof over eight tracks that turned the railway embankment, the old wall between Arena and Bijlmer, into an open gateway. Evidence: BNA Gebouw van het Jaar 2008 (DutchNews); Arcam guide. https://www.dutchnews.nl/2008/04/bijlmer_station_is_building_of/ Type: new (infrastructure). Caveats: none. Address: Johan Cruijff Boulevard 602.
3. **Metro station Kraaiennest** (2012/2013; Maccreanor Lavington) (N): a brutalist Bijlmer metro stop opened up and made safe with glass, delicate detail and light; now a gateway to the K-buurt. Evidence: RIBA European Award 2014; Dezeen, RIBA Journal, Arcam. https://www.dezeen.com/2014/04/08/kraaiennest-metro-station-amsterdam-maccreanor-lavington/ Type: remodel (station rebuilt on the existing viaduct). Caveats: none. Address: Kraaiennest (Karspeldreef). Note: the Taibah Mosque (fanacxlqejz0bxaddp3u4gwj) stands next to it.
4. **Ziggo Dome** (2012; Benthem Crouwel) (N): a 17,000-seat black box for touring acts, the gap between the Arena and the clubs, designed as a single-purpose hall that hosts tennis, skating and weddings. Evidence: Arcam guide, ArchDaily, Wikipedia. https://www.archdaily.com/350923/ziggo-dome-benthem-crouwel-architects Type: new. Caveats: ticketed inside. Address: De Passage 100.
5. **Living Tomorrow ("de kruimeldief")** (2003; UNStudio) (N): a futuristic pavilion nicknamed the dustbuster, meant to stand five years and still there; later a music events base. Evidence: Arcam guide; UNStudio's only Zuidoost work. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/living-tomorrow/ Type: new. Caveats: current use unclear, check status. Address: De Entree 300.
6. **Egoli-toren / Nieuw Echtenstein** (2023; LEVS architecten, for Rochdale) (N): 80 social-rent homes for a vulnerable group with a public ground floor, in the E-buurt. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2024 nominee (Arcam exhibition page). https://arcam.nl/en/events/exhibition-amsterdam-architecture-prize-2024/ Type: new. Caveats: residential. Address: not found (E-buurt, near 52.3258, 4.9594 per Arcam; confirm).
7. **Bijlmer Parktheater** (2009; Paul de Ruiter Architects) (N): an elliptical pavilion with shifted floors at the edge of the park, home to Circus Elleboog and youth theatre. Evidence: Arcam guide; architect page. https://paulderuiter.nl/en/projects/bijlmer-parktheater-theatre Type: new. Caveats: none. Address: Anton de Komplein 240. (The Rona Batho memorial, j3iukjcjhg6900bp5w1ssgqm, is next door.)
8. **De Hogeweyk dementia village, Weesp** (2009; Molenaar & Bol & VanDillen) (E: "De Hogeweyk dementia village", serc40sjx67ac8v2tqkgdyit): a closed care home rebuilt as a village of streets, squares and a boulevard for 150 people with dementia; copied worldwide. Evidence: Detail; Hedy d'Ancona Prize 2010 nominee (care-architecture prize, per Hogeweyk's own site). https://www.detail.de/de_en/dementia-village-de-hogeweyk-in-weesp-16433 Type: new (replaced a 1970s home). Caveats: people's home, visits only on its terms.
9. **Hilton Amsterdam Airport Schiphol** (2015; Mecanoo) (N): a cubic hotel with diamond-shaped windows round a 42 m glass-roofed atrium. Evidence: European Hotel Design Award 2016 (Newbuild); Domus, ArchDaily. https://mecanoo.nl/News/ID/301/Hilton-Amsterdam-Airport-Schiphol-wins-European-Hotel-Design-Award Type: new. Caveats: lobby public. Address: Schiphol Boulevard 701 (from memory, confirm).
10. **TNT Centre, now FedEx International Centre, Hoofddorp** (2011; Paul de Ruiter Architects) (N): billed as the first CO2-neutral office in the Netherlands, LEED Platinum, powered by its own biomass plant. Evidence: Detail ("Mission Zero"), LEED Platinum, RVO report. https://paulderuiter.nl/en/projects/tnt-centre Type: new. Caveats: office, view from outside. Address: Taurusavenue 111, Hoofddorp.
11. **Decoratelier Nationale Opera & Ballet** (2013; VMX Architects) (N): the scenery workshop, with a fly tower built to the exact size of the Muziektheater's stage so sets can be tested in place. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/decoratelier-nationale-opera-en-ballet/ Type: new. Caveats: workshop, no entry. Address: Kollenbergweg 1.
12. **Rijkswaterstaat office by the A2** (2001; Atelier Z / Zavrel) (N): a triangular office whose sharpest point cuts through the motorway dike, aimed at drivers on the A2. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/kantoorgebouw-rijkswaterstaat/ Type: new. Caveats: office. Address: Burgemeester Stramanweg 100B.
13. **Wi Eegi Kerki** (2012; 70F architecture) (N): a rare new church, paid for by the Moravian (Evangelische Broedergemeente) congregation's own collections. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/wi-eegi-kerki/ Type: new. Caveats: church, open at services. Address: Luthuliplein 11 (shares the plot with De Nieuwe Stad, 1993).
14. **De Kameleon** (2012; NL Architects) (N): shops on one side, homes stacked above and around, in one of the last run-down corners of the Bijlmer by Kraaiennest. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-kameleon/ Type: new. Caveats: none. Address: Karspeldreef.
15. **Wooncomplex F-Side** (2007; ONL, Kas Oosterhuis) (N): housing in the renewed F-buurt by the Dutch pioneer of computer-generated "non-standard" architecture. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/wooncomplex-f-side/ Type: new. Caveats: residential. Address: Claus van Amsbergstraat 71.
16. **De Kandelaar** (2007; Ria Smit architecten) (E: "De Kandelaar", cz3wra0vbel3fdvf00rnyaug): a shared church building with five halls for some fifteen migrant congregations, linked to the Grubbehoeve flat; Arcam calls it unique in the Netherlands. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-kandelaar/ Type: new. Caveats: none. Address: Bijlmerdreef 1239.
17. **Daklozenopvang Anton de Komplein** (2012; Atelier Kempe Thill) (N): sheltered flats for long-term addicts on the main square, by the office later nominated (AAP 2026) for De Vrijheid. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/daklozenopvang-a-de-komplein/ Type: new. Caveats: private. Address: Anton de Komplein 232.
18. **Bijlmer Sportcentrum** (2008; Vera Yanovshtchinsky Architecten) (N): a sports hall made the social anchor of the new Anton de Komplein. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2009 nominee (Bright); Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/bijlmer-sportcentrum/ Type: new. Caveats: none. Address: Anton de Komplein 157.
19. **Foe Ooi Leeuw** (2009; hvdn architecten) (N): housing for mainly Chinese elders, plus Surinamese, Curaçaoan and Indonesian residents; the name means "house to live in harmony". Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/foe-ooi-leeuw/ Type: new. Caveats: residential. Address: 3e Kekerstraat 78.
20. **Mi Akoma Di Color** (2007; Pattynama Ahaus) (N): G-buurt low-rise whose residents, moved out of demolished flats, co-designed their own homes. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/mi-akoma-di-color/ Type: new. Caveats: residential. Address: Goed Geluimd hof 3.
21. **SWITI, Kelbergen** (c. 2025/2026; HOH Architecten) (N): a timber tower of 45 flats plus 24 low-rise homes, all biobased above the foundations, solid CLT core, bamboo cladding; billed as unique in the Netherlands. Evidence: Divisare (published June 2026, photos MWA Hart Nibbrig). https://divisare.com/projects/561732-hoh-architecten-mwa-hart-nibbrig-marcel-van-der-burg-switi Type: new. Caveats: confirm completion and address.
22. **Jinso Paviljoen** (2008; cepezed with Karres en Brands) (N): a glass oval restaurant in the middle of the Johan Cruijff Boulevard. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/jinso-paviljoen/ Type: remodel (1990s pavilion extended into the oval). Caveats: none. Address: Johan Cruijff Boulevard 155.
23. **Villa ArenA** (2001; Benthem Crouwel) (N): a furniture mall staged like a theatre over four levels. Evidence: Arcam guide; Structurae. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/villa-arena-winkelcentrum/ Type: new. Caveats: none. Address: Burgemeester Stramanweg (ArenA Boulevard).
24. **AFAS Live, ex Heineken Music Hall** (2000; de Architekten Cie., Frits van Dongen) (N): a black concert box by the station, twin of the same architect's Pathé ArenA. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/afas-live/ Type: new. Caveats: ticketed. Address: Johan Cruijff Boulevard 590.
25. **Cultureel Educatief Centrum Ganzenhoef** (2004; Hans van Heeswijk) (N): school, culture, services and 23 homes stacked in one compact block; an Arcam "voorbeeldproject" of stacking. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/cultureel-educatief-centrum-ganzenhoef/ Type: new. Caveats: none. Address: Bijlmerdreef (Ganzenhoef).
26. **K-flats renovation** (2008; Bastiaan Jongerius) (N): honeycomb slabs of the K-buurt stripped and renewed instead of demolished. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/k-flats/ Type: remodel. Caveats: residential. Address: Karspeldreef.
27. **Grubbehoeve renovation** (2007; CASA architecten) (N for the building; its Bijlmer Museum is E: "Bijlmer Museum", xhe0sv9qpt5g5394htc10lum): one of the original honeycomb flats kept and renewed. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/grubbehoeve/ Type: remodel. Caveats: residential. Address: Grubbehoeve 41.
28. **ING Cedar, Cumulus Park** (2022; Benthem Crouwel) (N): two five-storey volumes joined by a glass link in the new innovation district. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/ing-cedar/ Type: new. Caveats: office. Address: Bijlmerdreef 106-108.
29. **MediArena, Duivendrecht** (2010; OZ / OeverZaaijer) (N): a media and entertainment complex meant to look playful yet stay neutral and adaptable. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/mediarena/ Type: new. Caveats: office. Address: MediArena 2, Amsterdam-Duivendrecht.
30. **Stadsdeelkantoor Zuidoost** (2006; ADP architecten) (N): district office where the Amsterdamse Poort, the Nelson Mandelapark and the eastern Bijlmer meet. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/stadsdeelkantoor-zuidoost/ Type: new. Caveats: none. Address: Anton de Komplein 150.
31. **Hogeschool voor Economische Studies (HES)** (2004; Hoogstad) (N): a rounded school among the square offices. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/hogeschool-voor-economische-studies-hes/ Type: new. Caveats: school. Address: Fraijlemaborg 133.
32. **Woontorens Burgemeester Stramanweg** (2006; Claus en Kaan, Van Sambeek & Van Veen) (N): homes along the road that runs under the Arena, to give the office zone a residential side. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/woontorens-burgemeester-stramanweg/ Type: new. Address: Anna Blamansingel.
33. **Wooncomplex Blok AB** (2009; Dick van Gameren) (N): first block on the lowered Karspeldreef after the parking garages came down. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/wooncomplex-blok-ab/ Type: new. Address: Karspeldreef 1083.
34. **Wooncomplex Fenice** (2008; Köther en Salman) (N): two blocks that use the four-metre drop from the raised Bijlmerdreef. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/wooncomplex-fenice/ Type: new. Address: F-buurt, 1102 BP.
35. **Karspelhof** (2018; Geurst & Schulze) (N): 150 fully energy-neutral family homes closing the K-buurt. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/karspelhof/ Type: new. Address: Klaroenstraat 62.

## Borderline

- **Johan Cruijff ArenA** (E: "Johan Cruijff ArenA", t70e9cjjigw6f1phx30kryw3): built 1996 (Rob Schuurman, Sjoerd Soeters), so pre-2000; the post-2000 upgrades (roof, open corners) are not documented here as recognised architecture.
- **Het Zandkasteel conversion** (E: "Het Zandkasteel", jgydmqaokja1hittehuuhfz0): 1980s Alberts & Van Huut bank partly turned into homes from c. 2019 (Van Wijnen, phase 2), later D/DOCK; the conversion architect and any prize not found.
- **The Frame, Bijlmerplein 858** (c. 2023; MVSA) (N): first new building of the Amsterdamse Poort masterplan; architect page only.
- **Hoekenrodeplein** (2014/2015; Karres en Brands) (N): 2.1 ha square by the redeveloped Nieuw Amsterdam building; on the Landezine award site and Barcelona Landscape Biennial 2016 list. Landscape, not a building.
- **Fleerde, Ganzenpoort, Gerenstein Gallery, Kavel 206a, Shri Laksmi School, ROC Fraijlemaborg** (2002-2007; Duinker van der Torre, KCAP, Heren 5 / H+N+S, Benthem Crouwel and others, KuiperCompagnons, SP Architecten): Arcam guide entries of the Bijlmer renewal, decent but no prize or press beyond Arcam. (Arcam's pin for ROC sits wrongly in Centrum.)
- **Warmteoverdrachtstation** (1999; cepezed): a fine industrial shed by the motorway, but one year too early.
- **Heesterveld Creative Community** (E: "Heesterveld Creative Community", pxu3s5r4644w8auqm844c71y): painted 1982 block given to artists; art and reuse, not recognised architecture.
- **Three portiekflats, Weesp** (renovation, Arie Keppler Prize 2024 nominee per MooiNH jury report): small, careful refresh of postwar flats; address and architect not found.
- **citizenM Schiphol** (2008): the chain's first prefab hotel; not verified this round.
- **Schiphol Pier A** (AECOM with cepezed): main structure done but opens April 2027; under construction.
