# Scout: Contemporary Architecture, West, Nieuw-West, Sloterdijk, Houthavens west edge, Zaanstad

Area: stadsdeel West (Spaarndammerbuurt, Staatslieden, Westerpark, Bos en Lommer, Oud-West, De Baarsjes),
Nieuw-West (Slotermeer, Geuzenveld, Osdorp, Slotervaart, De Aker, Sloten), Sloterdijk (Haven-Stad),
the Danzigerkade / Minervahaven edge of the Houthavens, and Zaanstad. Shortlist only, nothing drafted.

Main sources: Arcam's Architectuurgids map feed for West (83, 86 entries), Nieuw-West (87, 65),
Westpoort (851, 8) and Haven (88, 3), every guide page read for architect, year, prize field and
address; Arcam's AAP nomination pages 2021 to 2026 and the AAP 2025 list; nl.wikipedia AAP winners
table (cross-check); Arie Keppler Prijs 2014 jury report (Noord-Holland prize, Zaanstad entries);
ArchDaily, Dezeen, architects' pages for Zaanstad and recent Sloterdijk work.
Existing-place check: `pnpm dc places sights --all` dump searched per candidate, spot-checked with `--q`.
Coordinates in brackets are Arcam's map pins (lat, lng). "AAP" = Amsterdamse Architectuurprijs.

## Shortlist (ordered by strength)

1. **Westbeat** (2020; Studioninedots) (E: "Westbeat and the dancers' Super Space (Lelylaan)", ivvg2pcrabt8f8mj7k6bmjvs): 150 flats lifted on 86 brick arches over one public hall at station Lelylaan, meant to stitch West to Nieuw-West across the rail line. Evidence: AAP 2021 jury and public prize (double). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/westbeat/ Type: new. Address Delflandlaan / Rijnlandlaan 3. Caveats: homes private, hall public by programme.
2. **Ru Paré Community** (2017; BETA office for architecture & the city with Elisabeth Boersma planB) (N for the building; its memorial stone is E fmu7d4zq3ktackomktr0jex4): the school once called "the worst in the Netherlands" turned into a neighbourhood living room, designed on site with residents. Evidence: AAP 2018 jury prize; ArchDaily. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/ru-pare-community/ Type: remodel (1950s school into community centre). Address Chris Lebeaustraat 4. Publicly accessible.
3. **Station Wildeman** (2024; BETA) (N; related E "Wildemanbuurt, waiting for the wrecking ball", sa5xqua54m3jd0gzdhz4yvh2, which already uses this address): the old Kraemerschool reworked into a bottom-up community hub for a neighbourhood that chose its own demolition. Evidence: AAP 2025 public prize; Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/station-wildeman/ Type: remodel (school with added volumes, new entrance and public foyer). Address Nieuwe Laan 34. Publicly accessible.
4. **Vier torens Osdorp / Aquamarijn** (2009; Wiel Arets Architects) (N): four white 11-storey slabs lifted at both ends so they "open" to the Slotervaart water and the park. Evidence: AAP 2010 winner; Arcam has two entries (4 torens Osdorp, Aquamarijn). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/aquamarijn/ Type: new. Address Jan van Zutphenstraat 51-60 (52.34960, 4.79553). Caveat: private homes.
5. **Parkrand** (2007; MVRDV) (E: "Parkrand (MVRDV)", y6pfaut9dxywsnbsbs04g72v): a giant black block with three holes punched through, furnished as outdoor living rooms. Evidence: AAP 2008 nominee; Arcam guide; MVRDV. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/parkrand/ Type: new. Address Dr. H. Colijnstraat 250.
6. **Klussen op Klarenstraat** (2014; VanSchagen Architecten) (N): 30 buyers jointly gutted and rebuilt a 1956 Groosman walk-up flat, the first whole-flat collective self-build transformation in the garden cities. Evidence: AAP 2015 jury prize (Arcam also tags a public prize). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/klussen-op-klarenstraat/ Type: remodel. Address U.J. Klarenstraat 13 (52.35086, 4.83123). Caveat: private.
7. **Brede school De Kikker** (2006; Atelier Zeinstra van der Pol, now Dok) (N): two primary schools, crèche and health post in one glittering green-and-purple building in Osdorp. Evidence: AAP 2008 jury prize (the first AAP). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/bredeschool-de-kikker/ Type: new. Address Evertsweertplantsoen 3 (52.35631, 4.79347). Caveat: school, outside only.
8. **Basisschool De Wereldburger** (2021; Moke Architecten, landscape Landlab) (N): a 1960s concrete school re-skinned in metal and timber, with an aula built from reused wood. Evidence: AAP 2022 public prize. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/basisschool-de-wereldburger/ Type: remodel. Address Koos Vorrinkweg 3-5 (52.35262, 4.79926). Caveat: school.
9. **Rhapsody in West** (2019; TANGRAM) (N): rental block on a noisy A10 plot shaped as a "city oasis" against noise and fine dust. Evidence: AAP 2020 public prize. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/rhapsody/ Type: new. Address Vrouwengelukhof 53, Kolenkitbuurt (52.37850, 4.84330). Caveat: private.
10. **Sportplaza Mercator** (2006; VenhoevenCS) (E: "Sportplaza Mercator", x9jffzoxm1lvuu3ud06mgq3n): a pool dressed as an overgrown fortress, 2,000 m2 of planted walls at the head of the Rembrandtpark. Evidence: AAP 2008 nominee; Arcam guide; wide international press. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/sportplaza-mercator/ Type: new. Address Jan van Galenstraat 315. Public.
11. **Inntel Hotel Amsterdam-Zaandam** (2010; WAM architecten, Wilfried van Winden) (N): some 70 traditional green Zaan houses stacked into an 11/12-storey hotel by Zaandam station; the most published building in Zaanstad. Evidence: Dezeen 2010, ArchDaily, Building, architectureguide.nl. https://www.dezeen.com/2010/05/17/inntel-hotel-by-wam-architecten/ Type: new. Address Provincialeweg 102, Zaandam. Caveat: who invented the stacked houses is disputed between Van Winden and Soeters (NH Nieuws).
12. **Stadhuis Zaanstad and the Inverdan centre** (2011; Soeters Van Eldonk architecten; Inverdan masterplan from 2003) (N): a town hall built as eight blown-up Zaan houses over the tracks, crowning a whole new "Zaans" city centre; loved, mocked and called the summit of postmodernism. Evidence: Arie Keppler Prijs 2014 entries (Stadhuis, Inverdan); NH Nieuws on the debate. https://www.welingelichtekringen.nl/cultuur/zaanstad-hoogtepunt-van-postmodernisme Type: new. Address Stadhuisplein, Zaandam (by the station). Caveat: town hall interior public in office hours.
13. **EDGE West** (2022; de Architekten Cie., atrium landscape DS Landschapsarchitecten) (N): the 1970s Sociaal Fonds Bouwnijverheid office, a pioneering open-plan "kantoortuin", stripped to its clover-leaf frame and roofed in glass over a green atrium; BREEAM Outstanding, energy-positive. Designed by the successor firm of the original architects. Evidence: Arcam guide (filed under Westpoort, flagged by the Noord scout). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/edge-west/ Type: remodel. Address Basisweg 10 (52.39275, 4.84182). Caveat: offices, outside only.
14. **Opportuna** (2025; Office Winhov and Office Haratori) (N): a 20-storey social-rent tower for 23 to 28 year olds, with load-bearing prefab facades that echo the 1960s Westelijke Tuinsteden. Evidence: AAP 2026 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/opportuna/ Type: new. Address August Allebéplein 8, Overtoomse Veld (52.36344, 4.83844). Caveat: private.
15. **Cornelis** (2025; FARO architecten with Van Bergen Architectura) (N): a family apartment block built around collective spaces and routes that make children move and neighbours meet. Evidence: AAP 2026 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/cornelis/ Type: new. Address Schielandplein 3 (52.35673, 4.83913). Caveat: private.
16. **MO\*town Track 8** (2024; De Zwarte Hond) (N): three towers tied together by steel bridges between green galleries, the first real living block in the Sloterdijk office district. Evidence: AAP 2025 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/motown-track-8/ Type: new. Address Radarweg 309 (52.38691, 4.83570). Caveat: private.
