# Scout: Contemporary Architecture, Noord and Westpoort

Area: Amsterdam-Noord (Overhoeks, NDSM, Buiksloterham, Hamerkwartier, Banne, Waterlandplein,
Cornelis Douwes) and Westpoort / Haven. Shortlist only, nothing drafted.

Main sources: Arcam's Architectuurgids map data (district filters Noord, Westpoort and Haven,
89 entries read one by one), Arcam's AAP nomination pages 2021 to 2026, nl.wikipedia's AAP
winners table (cross-check), ArchDaily / Divisare / Archello / architects' pages, Dezeen.
Existing-place check: `pnpm dc places sights --all` dump plus `--q` per candidate.
Coordinates in brackets come from Arcam's map pins (lat, lng).

## Shortlist (ordered by strength)

1. **Eye Filmmuseum** (2012; Delugan Meissl Associated Architects) (E: "Eye Filmmuseum", on9jz6pbt28km2f5uje7b8xq): the white crystal that put Noord on the architectural map; a tilted, faceted shell facing the IJ. Evidence: Arcam guide, international press, AAP 2013 shortlist era. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/eye-filmmuseum/ Type: new. Address IJpromenade 1.
2. **A'DAM Toren** (2016; Claus Van Wageningen Architecten, original Arthur Staal 1971) (E: "A'DAM Toren and A'DAM Lookout", jw0u3fn0stggkq9mumx9uyfz): Shell's office tower reworked into hotel, clubs, offices and a lookout with a swing over the edge. Evidence: Arcam guide, wide press. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/adam-toren/ Type: remodel (conversion, new lookout and glazed lobby). Address Overhoeksplein 1.
3. **Noorderparkbad** (2015; de Architekten Cie., Branimir Medic and Pero Puljiz) (E: "Noorderparkbad", g4u6mhmp7639tu3359rn2lpo): a municipal pool with a park roof and a glazed wall on the park; won both the jury and public AAP 2016. Evidence: AAP 2016 double winner, Arcam "15 jaar AAP" feature. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/noorderparkbad/ Type: new. Address Sneeuwbalweg 5.
4. **Metro station Noord** (2018; Benthem Crouwel Architects) (N; the "Flyways" artwork on its platform is E cqnn6fjin80y41zevrrqqefr): the line's terminus in the median of the Nieuwe Leeuwarderweg, bus station and bike park under one long roof. Evidence: Noord/Zuidlijn won AAP 2019 and BNA Beste Gebouw van het Jaar 2019; Dezeen. https://www.dezeen.com/2018/07/26/amsterdam-metro-line-seven-stations-benthem-crouwel-architecture/ Type: new. Address Elzenhagen / Nieuwe Leeuwarderweg (by Buikslotermeerplein).
5. **Noorderpark metro station** (2018; Benthem Crouwel Architects) (E: "Noorderpark station", esb6r8k6aqfusx73n675vkou): the elevated sister station, a glass box on the dike across the park. Evidence: same AAP 2019 / BNA 2019 prize for the line; Arcam noord-zuidlijn entry. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/noord-zuidlijn/ Type: new.
6. **Kraanspoor** (2007; OTH, ontwerpgroep Trude Hooykaas) (E: "Kraanspoor", fvxpqry52soxpns7o35laz3j): a glass office slab lifted onto a 270 m concrete crane track of the NDSM yard. Evidence: Arcam guide, ArchDaily, ULI case study, widely cited reuse icon. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/kraanspoor/ Type: remodel (new building on a 1950s crane track). Address Kraanspoor 12-58.
7. **Alliander Westpoort** (2023; De Zwarte Hond, landscape de Urbanisten) (N): regional grid-company campus by the A5, built around reused halls and a big green court; Westpoort's only AAP winner. Evidence: AAP 2024 jury and public prize (double). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/alliander-westpoort/ Type: new + reuse. Pin (52.39153, 4.81304). Caveats: business campus, view from outside.
8. **Schoonschip** (2019 to 2021; Space&Matter) (E: "Schoonschip floating neighbourhood", e4pg5vcam69f2zcyxqjtzgzv): 46 floating homes on a smart energy grid on the Johan van Hasseltkanaal. Evidence: Arcam guide, international press. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/schoonschip/ Type: new. Caveat: private homes, seen from the jetty and the quay.
9. **De Ceuvel** (2014; Space&Matter, DELVA, Metabolic, Smeelearchitecture, Jeroen Apers, Studio Valkenier) (E: "De Ceuvel", ihp6bionlwz4429w1gxf33uo): recycled houseboats on a polluted shipyard, cleaned by plants. Evidence: Arcam guide, wide press. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-ceuvel/ Type: new (temporary-turned-lasting). Address Korte Papaverweg 4.
10. **Timber towers: Patch22, Top-Up, Stories** (2016, 2020, 2021; FRANTZEN et al. and Olaf Gipser Architects) (E: "Timber towers of Buiksloterham (Patch22, Top-Up, Stories)", pkma8bvlbd7gfxkzaebx9y1s): the timber-high-rise lab of Buiksloterham. Evidence: Top-Up AAP 2021 nominee, Stories AAP 2022 nominee, Arcam guide entries for all three. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/stories/ Type: new. Caveat: private homes.
11. **De Jakoba** (2022; Studioninedots, landscape Buro Sant & Co) (E: "De Jakoba, Overhoeks", xyxfglfgflta5ag71viy8m8d): 135 social rentals in a stepped block with a stacked collective garden. Evidence: AAP 2023 nominee, Divisare. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-jakoba-overhoeks-kavel-l/ Type: new.
12. **Republica** (2023; Marc Koehler Architects) (E: "Republica, Papaverhof", sden16s0vztjz80tfafdpz56): a mixed block with a public route climbing through it. Evidence: AAP 2024 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/republica/ Type: new.
13. **De August** (2024; Team V Architectuur) (N): social housing that holds its own between the luxury towers of Overhoeks, crafted brick, prefab. Evidence: AAP 2025 nominee with honourable mention; BLT Built Design Award 2025. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-august/ Type: new. Pin (52.38817, 4.90505). Caveat: private.
14. **Hyperion Lyceum** (2018; Ector Hoogstad Architecten) (E: "Hyperion Lyceum", lnz0j65x2n9vph5y922sdqeu): a stacked school with a big atrium staircase on the IJ bank. Evidence: Arcam guide, ArchDaily. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/hyperion-lyceum/ Type: new. Address Badhuiskade 361. Caveat: school, outside only.
15. **Pontkade** (2020; de Architekten Cie.) (N): the two NDSM towers that greet the ferry, industrial skin, courtyard on top of the plinth. Evidence: AAP 2021 nominee, Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/pontkade/ Type: new. Pin (52.40158, 4.89029), at the NDSM ferry, MS. van Riemsdijkweg.
16. **De Europa** (2011; Álvaro Siza) (N): the only Siza in Amsterdam, a white marble residential block in the row of IJ giants next to Eye. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-europa/ Type: new. Pin (52.38692, 4.89990). Caveat: private homes.
17. **Gentiaanbuurt Blok 6-10** (2024; Ibelings van Tilburg Architecten) (E: "Gentiaanbuurt, the shipping line's garden village", d4fcvc2o1r0213xz7z83vldv): a protected 1920s garden village rescued after decades of neglect. Evidence: AAP 2025 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/gentiaanbuurt-blok-6-10/ Type: remodel (full renovation of a listed ensemble). Address Gentiaanplein area.
18. **Breehorn IJdoornlaan** (2020; LEVS architecten) (N): three big brick blocks replacing the old Waterlandplein courts, 80% social rent. Evidence: AAP 2021 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/breehorn-ijdoornlaan/ Type: new. Pin (52.39355, 4.95661). Caveat: private.
19. **Xplore Agora** (2025; Studio Ard Hoksbergen with VDNDP) (N): a secondary school built for open "Agora" teaching, one big flexible hall. Evidence: AAP 2026 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/xplore-agora-amsterdam/ Type: new. Pin (52.39907, 4.92565), near Termini. Caveat: school.
20. **Cityplot: Tolhuiskade and C14** (2020s; Civic Architects; Studioninedots) (E: "Cityplot and the Tolhuiskade beacon", t710ga3j0qmsdepjq4zhllx3): the brick "nautical beacon" and the tilted-warehouse C14 on the Tolhuiskanaal. Evidence: Divisare, Metalocus, ArchDaily (C14). https://www.archdaily.com/1026037/cityplot-buiksloterham-c14-building-studioninedots Type: new.
21. **Self-build Buiksloterham, incl. Drie Generatiehuis and Kashuis** (2014 onward; BETA 2018; cc-studio / Dill) (E: "Self-build houses of Buiksloterham", nvjd0uo5qnecfeqnipo93f5x): a street of architect-built plots; BETA's three-generation house is the best known. Evidence: Arcam guide entries drie-generatiehuis and kashuis. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/drie-generatiehuis/ Type: new. Caveat: private homes.
22. **Faralda Crane Hotel** (2014; IAA Architecten, Harry Abels) (E: "NDSM slipways and Kraan 13 (Faralda Crane Hotel)", hg9i3fh18swl7pja9upzkol2): three suites hung in a 1950s shipyard crane. Evidence: Frame, IAA project page. https://iaa-architecten.com/projects/6-faralda-crane-hotel Type: remodel. Address NDSM-plein 78.
23. **NDSM Loods with Kunststad** (2004 to 2007; Dynamo Architecten) (E: "NDSM Loods (Scheepsbouwloods)", seladrj25mmhtixn8g69n02c): an artists' town of user-built studios inside the shipbuilding hall. Evidence: Architectureguide.nl (Dynamo, Atelierstad NDSM-werf), Arcam ndsm-loods entry. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/ndsm-loods/ Type: remodel (studio city in a 1920 hall). Caveat: Dynamo credit rests on one listing.
24. **Klein Amsterdam school** (2019; SeARCH) (N): an all-timber school designed to be extended, taken apart and moved. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/klein-amsterdam/ Type: new. Pin (52.40493, 4.90059). Caveat: school.
25. **Theater Amsterdam** (2014; Dedato) (N): a glass box on the Danzigerkade with a stage the size of a hangar, built for one long-running show. Evidence: Arcam guide (Westpoort). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/theater-amsterdam/ Type: new. Address Danzigerkade 5. Caveat: overlaps the Houthavens west edge (other scouts).
26. **Smederij NDSM** (2011; Group A, Sid Lee Architecture) (E: "Smederij and Timmerwerkplaats, NDSM", tzjl2uzho1zfviatco26b2ui): offices slotted into the old forge hall. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/smederij-ndsm/ Type: remodel.
27. **Brug Overhoeks** (2009; Korth Tielens Architecten) (N): the bridge into Overhoeks, in Arcam's guide as architecture. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/brug-overhoeks/ Type: new. Pin (52.38274, 4.90192).
28. **De Albatros** (2006; hvdn Architecten) (N for the building; its light cabinets are E qyuhkuf3w4fv9z4aoxt8lvop): the big mixed block at the Meeuwenlaan that announced Noord's urbanisation. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-albatros/ Type: new.
29. **Noorderparkbar** (2012; bureau SLA and Overtreders W) (E: "Noorderparkkamer, the Swiss pavilion and the Marktplaats bar", ffuzb80s0rfixsbqlol68t4d): a pavilion built by neighbours from donated, second-hand materials. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/noorderparkbar/ Type: new.
30. **Restaurant Noorderlicht** (2006; Steven Gerritsen) (E: "Noorderlicht", rv8ru6n0a5t6ld6h3qluqw40): a greenhouse café on the NDSM bank. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/restaurant-noorderlicht/ Type: new.
31. **KAAP** (2025; Powerhouse Company, Orange Architects, NEXT architects) (N): five harbour-loft blocks and a circular pavilion at the Kop Grasweg between Overhoeks and NDSM. Evidence: ArchDaily. https://www.archdaily.com/1041661/kaap-powerhouse-company-plus-orange-architects Type: new. Caveat: completion 2025 (ArchDaily) vs 2026 (Architectenweb); check.
32. **The Doors** (2024; Space Encounters) (N): every flat has two front doors, one to live and one to work. Evidence: ArchDaily, Arie Keppler Prijs 2024 entry. https://archdaily.com/1003653/the-doors-housing-complex-space-encounters Type: new. On the Tolhuiskanaal (Cityplot); could fold into item 20.
33. **B'Mine Tower** (2017; Paul de Ruiter Architects) (N): 75 m energy-efficient tower that opened "The Strip" at Overhoeks. Evidence: architect's page, Skyscrapercenter. https://paulderuiter.nl/en/news/nieuwe-amsterdamse-skyscraper-opgeleverd-bmine-toren Type: new. Caveat: private.
34. **De Ark, Banne** (2024; Open Kaart) (N): a church hall converted into a social market (food bank, shops). Evidence: AAP 2025 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/de-ark/ Type: remodel. Pin (52.40786, 4.91261).
35. **Botel** (2015; MMX Architecten) (E: "Botel and its letter rooms", d1d4qd4s9fkru1lj8d2ntozb): giant letters on the deck you can sleep in. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/botel-amsterdam/ Type: remodel.
36. **RWZI West** (2006; Architectuurstudio Herman Hertzberger) (E: "RWZI West, Hertzberger's sewage works", vgjf1ebbycivi4tby8u1qoo9): a sewage plant designed by a star architect. Evidence: Arcam guide (Haven). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/rioolwaterzuiveringsinstallatie-rwzi/ Type: new. Note: the live place has borough Nieuw-West; it sits in the western harbour.
37. **PVH Campus** (2016 to 2019) (E: "PVH Campus, Danzigerkade", dop1jx6jdpe8ppu4l0k0khqx): already on the map; check that it meets the bar (no prize found). Type: new + reuse.
38. **CPO Srefi Bow** (2024; JoustraReid and lab03) (N): a collective self-commissioned block. Evidence: AAP 2025 nominee. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/ (no guide page found) Type: new. Caveat: exact site not confirmed; "Bow Overhoeks" listings suggest Overhoeks. Verify.
39. **Student housing at NDSM: Student Experience (2017, OZ) and NieuwDok (2016, Moke Architecten)** (N): container-scale student blocks that started NDSM's housing boom. Evidence: Arcam guide (Student Experience), ArchDaily (NieuwDok). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/student-experience/ Type: new. Caveat: private.
40. **HISWA havengebouw, Amsterdam Marina** (2013; SeARCH) (N): marina and harbour office on the widest part of the IJ by NDSM. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/amsterdam-marina/ Type: new.
41. **Drijvend Kantoor Waternet** (2010; Attika Architekten) (N): a floating office for the IJ's litter boats. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/drijvend-kantoor-waternet/ Type: new.
42. **De Nieuwe Noorder, office and funeral centre** (2012; Dok Architecten) (N for the building; war memorials there are E d2zbxeyfpjleb1thyaf3x4bo): asymmetric roofs beside the Amsterdam School reception hall. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/kantoor-en-rouwcentrum-de-nieuwe-noorder/ Type: new + remodel. Address Buikslotermeerdijk 83.
43. **Moskee El Mousshine** (2005; Gerard Rijnsdorp) (N): bright colours and odd window shapes at the Meeuwenlaan / Valkenweg. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/moskee-el-mousshine/ Type: new.
44. **Nieuw Waterlandplein** (2013; Atelier PRO, de Architekten Cie., Hans van Heeswijk) (N; the "Zilveren Manen" art there is E otp63ski7awy22cq48klljwa): renewal of the shopping square with new towers. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/nieuw-waterlandplein/ Type: new + remodel.
45. **Voorzieningengebouw and SET-gebouw** (2016; MOPET Architecten) (N): the two technical buildings marking the north end of the metro line. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/voorzieningengebouw-en-set-gebouw/ Type: new. Caveat: not public.
46. **Ronald McDonald Centre** (2009; Fact Architects) (N): transparent sports centre for disabled children. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/ronald-mcdonald-centre/ Type: new.
47. **SO De Heldring** (2014; Berger Barnett) (N): first new public building of the Buiksloterham transformation. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/so-de-heldring/ Type: new. Caveat: school.
48. **Hitachi training centre, Westpoort** (2002; Kajima Design Europe) (N): Japanese-designed factory and demo hall in the harbour. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/bedrijfsgebouw-hitachi/ Type: new. Caveat: business site.
49. **Search office, Westpoort** (2008; Witteveen Architecten) (N): cradle-to-cradle office by the Coentunnel. Evidence: Arcam guide. https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/kantoorgebouw-search/ Type: new.
50. **Cornelis Douwes business park cluster: FriJo Lichtreclame (2007), Hulshoff Archiefbeheer (2010), Amsterdam Museum Collectiecentrum (2010), Union sorting hall (2005), The Set Company (2008)** (N): a business park whose "closed boxes" got designer skins under supervisor Bastiaan Jongerius. Evidence: Arcam guide (five entries). https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/collectiecentrum-amsterdam-museum/ Type: new. Best as one cluster pin, not five.

## Borderline

- Brink Tower, Overhoeks (Mecanoo, 28 floors, about 90 m): sources say 2022 to 2025 but no confirmed completion; check before adding.
- De Teunis, Papaverweg (Studioninedots, Circular Buiksloterham): completion not confirmed.
- De Baanderij, NDSM canteen remodel (2005, Ontwerpers A'dam): Arcam guide, modest.
- De Bongerd (2007, Paul de Ruiter, Uytenhaak): new "garden village" estate; neighbourhood, not a building.
- Kadoelerbreek (2011, EGM) and Meerpad (2009, Heren 5): solid Arcam-guide housing, low curiosity.
- Bredero College extension (2000, Van Gameren Mastenbroek): Arcam guide, school.
- Blackjack (2016, BNB / BO6): competition-winning energy-neutral block; Arcam guide only.
- North Orleans, Hamerkwartier (SeARCH): Arcam guide, no year.
- Kaap-Noord (2005), GSUS (2003), Asterpark (2012): Arcam-guide business buildings, could join the cluster in item 50.
- Noorderpark (West 8, 2010): landscape, not a building; already covered by park places.
- Startblok Elzenhagen (MUST, RoosRos): temporary campus with a planned end date.
- Undercurrent (Attika, 2000): IJburg's floating visitor pavilion, Arcam pins it in Noord; current state unknown.
- Tolhuistuin pavilion (E jy7exiurclv6nusx4f9bu3zk) and A Lab (E vd3hm8pvuxvxgd972g75x1vk): Shell buildings reused since about 2010, remodel architects not found.
- Lasloods / STRAAT (E xn6hd2k80skaxcozyj0vzb6t, opened 2020): reuse, no architect found.
- Pllek (E onzcl74s6kps21bou4msg3mt) and Hotel de Goudfazant (E feyuw76f647qvlyui3qmgfat): container and garage reuse, no recognition found.
- Ceres Paragon terminal (E fpetk5m6ums4jp27izpt3p6i, 2000 to 2001): engineering oddity, not recognised as architecture.
- Amsterdam Heliport (E ni0n6v70uxjuzjikudzx36iy, 2005): no recognition found.
- KiMu (E nfselzkaiz0q4uxen6w4nqk8, 2026): architecture not checked.
- Student Experience Minervahaven (VURB, 2021): Westpoort / Haven-Stad, overlaps the Houthavens west edge.
- EDGE West (de Architekten Cie., 2022 remodel of a 1970s office): Arcam files it under Westpoort but it is at Sloterdijk, so it belongs to west-nieuw-west.
