# Scout: Contemporary Architecture, zuid-zuidas (Zuid, Zuidas, Buitenveldert, Amstelveen)

Shortlist only, nothing drafted. Checked against `pnpm dc places sights --all` (2,299 places;
note: the map has no places at all in the municipality of Amstelveen, so every Amstelveen item
is N). Years are completion years as given by the sources; where sources differ it says so.

## Shortlist (ordered by strength)

1. **Rijksmuseum renovation** (2013; Cruz y Ortiz, Wilmotte interiors) (E: "Rijksmuseum", h6dfmgyc1m6iicoixkub2q5y): ten-year rebuild that dug out the courtyards into glass-roofed atria and kept the cycle passage through the middle. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs (Gouden A.A.P.) 2014. https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdamse_Architectuur_Prijs Type: remodel (courtyards, entrance, Philips wing, new Atelier building on the Hobbemastraat). Caveats: none. Museumstraat 1.
2. **Stedelijk Museum extension, "the Bathtub"** (2012; Benthem Crouwel) (E: "Stedelijk Museum", eqql7e6yjc56w7y65quaozct): a white fibre-composite tub on stilts that turned the entrance to face the Museumplein; loved and mocked in equal measure. Evidence: Dezeen, ArchDaily, every Amsterdam guide since. Type: new wing. Caveats: none. Museumplein 10.
3. **Valley** (2022; MVRDV) (E: "Valley, the monkey rock of the Zuidas", tqidorof611ngiey24td478b): three towers (67, 81, 100 m) "bitten" into a planted stone canyon open to the public via outdoor stairs. Evidence: Amsterdamse Nieuwbouwprijs 2023; Emporis Skyscraper Award 2021 (per NH Nieuws); Dezeen. https://www.dezeen.com/2022/09/20/valley-skyscraper-mvrdv-open-amsterdam/ Type: new. Caveats: upper terraces private. Beethovenstraat 503.
4. **ING House, now Infinity ("the shoe")** (2002; Meyer en Van Schooten) (E: "ING House, the shoe beside the ring road", uay4pu8zk8fskb5mvt7hz1rp): glass hull on slanted legs beside the A10, the Zuidas's first icon; nicknamed shoe, skate, iron, dustbuster. Evidence: nl.wikipedia (Infinity (gebouw)), widely published. Type: new. Caveats: office, view from outside. Amstelveenseweg 500.
5. **nhow Amsterdam RAI** (2020; OMA, Reinier de Graaf) (N): three stacked, twisted triangles, 650 rooms, the largest hotel in the Benelux; won in competition against ten others. Evidence: Dezeen, Domus, designboom, CTBUH. https://www.dezeen.com/2019/12/02/oma-nhow-amsterdam-rai-hotel-amsterdam/amp/ Type: new. Caveats: none (hotel lobby public). Europaboulevard 2b.
6. **Tripolis-Park** (c. 2023; MVRDV with Delta Vormgroep, on Aldo van Eyck's Tripolis of 1994) (E: "Tripolis", y5tv2u65b2eyhslz23l56w7p): Van Eyck's listed towers restored and shielded from the A10 by a 12-storey "landscraper". Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2024 nominee (Arcam); ArchDaily; MVRDV. https://www.archdaily.com/1022500/tripolis-park-mvrdv Type: remodel (restoration plus new office wall). Caveats: Tripolis 100 still to be converted to housing. Burgerweeshuispad 101.
7. **The Edge** (2014/2015; PLP Architecture) (N): Deloitte's office scored BREEAM 98.36%, billed as the world's most sustainable and "smartest" office; a 15-storey atrium faces north to the tracks. Evidence: BRE case study, Bloomberg/press, Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Edge_(Amsterdam) Type: new. Caveats: office, atrium visible only from inside. Gustav Mahlerlaan 2970 (address from memory, confirm).
8. **Rechtbank Amsterdam, new courthouse** (2021; KAAN Architecten) (N; its forecourt statue is E: "Love or Generosity, the giant at the court door", ya228xixcy1ix41lum9sj8en): the country's largest court, 50 courtrooms, a calm stone block with a public hall. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2021 nominee (KAAN news), nl.wikipedia (Zuidas). Type: new. Caveats: security screening to enter. Parnassusweg 280.
9. **Ernst & Young tower ("Cross Towers", ex Drentestaete III)** (2008; Foster + Partners) (N): Foster's first Amsterdam building, twin slabs in a silver steel diagrid. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2009 nominee (Bright); Dezeen; ArchDaily. https://www.dezeen.com/2008/06/14/vivaldi-tower-by-foster-partners/amp/ Type: new. Caveats: office. Antonio Vivaldistraat 150 (number to confirm).
10. **Ito Tower** (2005; Toyo Ito with ZZDP) (N): a Japanese master's only Dutch building, 100 m, white louvred skin, first phase of Mahler4. Evidence: nl.wikipedia (Ito-toren); Mahler4 masterplan by de Architekten Cie. Type: new. Caveats: office. Gustav Mahlerplein (Mahler4, code 1AH).
11. **The Rock** (2009; Erick van Egeraat) (N): 90 m tower that looks top-heavy, a pile of glass, stone and aluminium. Debated: nominated by NRC Next in 2010 for ugliest building in the Netherlands; renovated 2024-2025. Evidence: ArchDaily; nl.wikipedia. https://www.archdaily.com/183612/the-rock-erick-van-egeraat Type: new. Caveats: office. Claude Debussylaan 80.
12. **Crystal Houses** (2016; MVRDV with Gietermans & Van Dijk) (E: "Crystal Houses, the shopfront of glass bricks", izdm8mvjbjp4a1nqk3ibe9sx): a 19th-century shopfront rebuilt in solid glass bricks that melt into brick halfway up. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2017 public prize. https://www.mvrdv.com/news/540/crystal-houses-wins-amsterdam-architecture-prize Type: new (replica facade). Caveats: none. P.C. Hooftstraat 94-96.
13. **Liberal Jewish synagogue** (2010; SeARCH, Bjarne Mastenbroek and Uda Visser) (E: "The Liberal Jewish synagogue on Jacob Soetendorp's island", nrgi6zgog3xjlyaryea20c1r): grey block on its own spit with a menorah-shaped glass cut. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2011 winner (Wikipedia list). Type: new. Caveats: guarded, visits by arrangement. Zuidelijke Wandelweg 41.
14. **Van Gogh Museum entrance hall** (2015; Hans van Heeswijk) (E: "Van Gogh Museum", v84iqfmph1ur1jevk5h68na1): a glass hall on the Museumplein side joining Rietveld's building and the Kurokawa wing. Evidence: architect page, press at opening. Type: remodel (new entrance hall). Caveats: Kurokawa wing itself is 1999 (pre-2000). Museumplein 6.
15. **Station De Pijp, Noord/Zuidlijn** (2018; Benthem Crouwel) (E: "Station De Pijp, the deepest platform in the country", ts0z1rwsxm2ub0aaeflt48wt): stacked platforms 26.5 m below sea level under a narrow street. Evidence: the whole Noord/Zuidlijn won the Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2019. Type: new (infrastructure). Caveats: none. Ferdinand Bolstraat. (Station Europaplein, same line and prize, is also in Zuid and not on the map: N.)
16. **Gerrit Rietveld Academie extensions** (2003/2004 Benthem Crouwel glass tower; 2019 Fedlev, Paulien Bremmer with Hootsmans) (E: "Gerrit Rietveld Academie: the building its architect never saw", tst7bxgkr5md52wu7r71g1od): two additions facing Rietveld's 1966 school, the newest a dark, translucent box linked by a bridge. Evidence: Dezeen 2019, ArchDaily, Detail; MooiNH architecture prize 2020 entry. https://www.dezeen.com/2019/10/12/gerrit-rietveld-academie-paulien-bremmer-amsterdam-netherlands/amp/ Type: new wings. Caveats: school. Fred. Roeskestraat 96.
17. **Stepstone** (2023; LEVS architecten, for Lieven de Key with VORM) (N): first social-housing tower on the Zuidas, 80 m, 216 homes for starters, sports pitch on the roof. Evidence: Zuiderkerkprijs 2024 winner; Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2025 nominee. https://zuidas.nl/en/blog/2024/12/11/stepstone-social-housing-tower-wins-2024-zuiderkerk-prize/ Type: new. Caveats: residential. Address not found (Zuidas, confirm).
18. **Van der Valk Hotel Zuidas** (2020; Wiel Arets Architects) (N): 55 m white hotel on a triangular plot by the A10, the toucan chain gone minimal. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2021 nominee; BNA Gebouw van het Jaar 2021 entry. https://www.wielaretsarchitects.com/en/media/news/van_der_valk_hotel_zuidas_is_nominated_for_the_2021_amsterdam_architecture_prize Type: new. Caveats: none. Tommaso Albinonistraat 200 (one source).
19. **Crossover** (2023; Team V) (N): offices for the market and 120 social flats for young people in one brick block at the motorway exit. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2024 nominee (Arcam); Arie Kepplerprijs 2024 (MooiNH). https://arcam.nl/en/events/exhibition-amsterdam-architecture-prize-2024/ Type: new. Caveats: none. Gelrestraat 30-46 (Kop Zuidas).
20. **Amsteldok, ex Rivierstaete** (2018/2019; MVSA Architects with BDG) (E: "Rivierstaete, the Monkey Rock that lost its tiles", a3htxw8cp92cvg5l94844z5b): Maaskant's 1973 "apenrots" kept as a frame and reglazed. Debated: lost its tiles, its name and its character. Evidence: ArchDaily; Crimson study. https://mvsa-architects.com/en/projects/amsteldok/ Type: remodel. Caveats: office. Amsteldijk 166.
21. **European Medicines Agency** (2019; MVSA Architects with Fokke van Dijk, Rijksvastgoedbedrijf) (N): a Brexit building, put up in record time when the agency left London. Evidence: MVSA, RIBA Journal Q&A, Nationale Staalprijs entry. https://mvsa-architects.com/en/projects/european-medicines-agency-ema/ Type: new. Caveats: EU agency, no entry. Domenico Scarlattilaan 6 (from memory, confirm).
22. **Amsterdam Symphony** (2009; Pi de Bruijn, de Architekten Cie; hotel by AWG) (N): two orange-red towers (105 and 97 m); central in the big Dutch property fraud case. Evidence: nl.wikipedia (Amsterdam Symphony). Type: new. Caveats: residential/office. Gustav Mahlerlaan / Gershwin.
23. **Viñoly tower, Mahler4** (2005; Rafael Viñoly) (N): 91 m office tower, Viñoly's only building in the city. Evidence: nl.wikipedia (Mahler), zuidas.nl. Type: new. Caveats: office. Mahler4.
24. **UNStudio Tower** (2010; UNStudio) (N): last of Mahler4, horizontal aluminium strips designed around daylight. Evidence: zuidas.nl, world-architects. Type: new. Caveats: office. Gustav Mahlerplein (with FOZ).
25. **Summertime** (2016; SeARCH) (N): 197 mid-rent flats in an hourglass plan with zigzag stepped facades and green walls. Evidence: ArchDaily, Divisare. https://www.archdaily.com/893509/summertime-housing-search Type: new. Caveats: residential. Gustav Mahlerlaan 637-841 / George Gershwinlaan 351-537.
26. **ACTA, Academic Centre for Dentistry** (2010; Benthem Crouwel) (N): dental school and clinic in one block; the VU cites a third prize in a 2011 Amsterdam architecture competition. Evidence: ArchDaily. https://www.archdaily.com/146796/academic-centre-for-dentistry-benthem-crouwel-architekten Type: new. Caveats: none. Gustav Mahlerlaan 3004.
27. **Elicium, RAI** (2009; Benthem Crouwel) (N): congress block lifted over the car park between two halls; Benthem Crouwel has been RAI supervisor since 1989. Evidence: ArchDaily, Arup. Type: new. Caveats: event venue. Europaplein.
28. **Hourglass** (2020; Dam & Partners Architecten) (N): 80 m tower pinched at the waist, after Brancusi, on top of station Zuid's edge. Evidence: ArchDaily. https://www.archdaily.com/1024804/hourglass-dam-and-partners-architecten Type: new. Caveats: office/hotel. Parnassusweg corner, by station Zuid.
29. **Stibbe head office** (2016; Jo Coenen) (N): organic, rounded office with a garden in the style of an English park facing the Beatrixpark. Evidence: zuidas.nl. Type: new. Caveats: office. Beethovenplein (number to confirm).
30. **Brittenpassage, station Zuid** (2026; Zuidasdok) (E: "Brittenpassage: the first finished piece of the Zuidasdok", swg0rz8ku7w8q9xy5ac0hv3c): seven concrete decks slid under motorway and tracks. Evidence: zuidas.nl, Parool on costs. Type: new (infrastructure). Caveats: rest of Zuidasdok under construction.
31. **AkzoNobel Center** (2016; GROUP A) (E: "AkzoNobel Art Space", w762ux83yio25twjt62p3x3w): head office with a public art plinth demanded by the city. Evidence: Archello, iF Design. Type: new. Caveats: none. Christian Neefestraat 2.
32. **PI59** (2024; V8 Architects) (N): a 1970s brutalist office (later the international school) reopened with its concrete frame kept and the closed facade swapped for glass. Evidence: ArchDaily. https://www.archdaily.com/1015048/pi59-offices-v8-architects Type: remodel. Caveats: office. Prinses Irenestraat 59.
33. **Telecom tower raised, with White Noise** (2009; AGS Schwencke Rosbach Architects; light art Giny Vos) (E: "White Noise, a night sky on the telecom tower", lpsm8fwgspv3ngvliyomfo9y): the 1963 KPN mast heightened and wrapped as a beacon. Evidence: Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2009 nominee ("KPN Zendmast Zuidas", Bright). Type: remodel. Caveats: none. Barbara Strozzilaan.
34. **NU.VU, New University Building** (2021; Team V) (N): 31,000 m2, seven to twelve floors, glass and open to the campus. Evidence: zuidas.nl, Arcadis. Type: new. Caveats: none. De Boelelaan 1111.
35. **The George** (2020; DOK architecten, Liesbeth van der Pol) (N): a former Rijksbouwmeester's nod to the Amsterdam School, "a summer dress in the breeze". Evidence: zuidas.nl interview, Architonic. Type: new. Caveats: residential. Gershwin.
36. **O|2 lab building, VU** (2016; EGM architecten) (N): shared lab for VU, UvA and VUmc. Evidence: VU, Bouwwereld. Type: new. Caveats: weak on architecture press. De Boelelaan 1108.
37. **VU Research Building** (2024/2025; cepezed) (N): vibration-free lab with a deep basement. Evidence: cepezed, VU news. Type: new. Caveats: confirm opening. De Boelelaan.
38. **Conservatorium Hotel** (2011; Piero Lissoni) (E: "Rijkspostspaarbank (Conservatorium hotel)", i0zr1fckm1jyhx7y8h6496wj): the 1901 bank's courtyard covered in glass as a lobby. Evidence: design press. Type: remodel. Caveats: summary on the map does not mention the conversion. Van Baerlestraat 27.
39. **Stadsdeelwerf / stadsdeelkantoor Zuideramstel** (2008; Claus en Kaan) (N): two L-blocks round a court, offices shielding housing. Evidence: listed as Amsterdamse Architectuur Prijs 2009 winner on nl.wikipedia; ArchDaily. https://www.archdaily.com/36148/stadsdeelkantoor-zuideramstel-claus-en-kaan-architekten Caveats: address not found, and winner status only on Wikipedia; verify.
40. **Crystal Court** (2009; Tangram) (N): Buitenveldert housing block. Evidence: Amsterdamse Nieuwbouwprijs 2010; AAP 2009 nominee. Type: new. Caveats: residential, modest. Willem van Weldammelaan.

Amstelveen (all N):

41. **Culture strip and P60, Stadsplein** (2001; Benthem Crouwel) (N): theatre, P60 pop venue and library in a long strip on the new town square. Evidence: Benthem Crouwel. https://benthemcrouwel.com/projects/culture-strip-amstelveen-p60 Type: new. Caveats: none. Stadsplein.
42. **Esprit head office Benelux** (2010; Bekkering Adams) (N): Amstelveense Architectuurprijs 2013, jury and public prizes. Evidence: TU/e prize record, e-architect. https://www.e-architect.com/amsterdam/esprit-building-amstelveen Type: new. Caveats: office, current use unknown. Krijgsman 75.
43. **VU Sportcentrum Uilenstede** (c. 2016; Slangen+Koenis) (N): Amstelveense Architectuurprijs 2017 jury prize, buildings. Evidence: Amstelveenweb. Type: new. Caveats: none. Uilenstede.
44. **Apartments Jan Lievensweg** (c. 2017; Studioninedots) (N): Amstelveense Architectuurprijs 2017 jury prize, housing. Type: new. Caveats: residential, small.
45. **The Mayor (ex KPMG "De Bovenlanden")** (c. 2022-2023; MVDS) (N): Alberts en Van Huut's organic office turned into about 300 flats; debated: the original architect went to court against the facade changes and lost. Evidence: Accountant.nl 2017; De Nijs. Type: remodel. Caveats: completion date unconfirmed. Burgemeester Rijnderslaan area (confirm).
46. **Wagener Stadium grandstand** (c. 2016; IAA Architecten) (N): timber stand of the national hockey stadium; Amstelveense Architectuurprijs 2017 public prize, 3rd. Type: new. Caveats: modest recognition. Amsterdamse Bos, Nieuwe Kalfjeslaan.
47. **Noorddamcentrum Bovenkerk** (2022; Hoogeveen architecten) (N): Amstelveense Architectuurprijs 2023 jury prize, other buildings. Type: remodel and extension. Caveats: local only.
