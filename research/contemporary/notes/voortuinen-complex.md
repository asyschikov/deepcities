## 1. Project name, planners, phases

- **Name: Westerpark West** (also written Westerpark-West). Used by MVRDV (MVRDV-MP), the city's
  zoning plan (BP-2017), Van Wijnen (VW-2B), Amsterdam Woont (AW-BLEND) and GP+B (GPB).
- Site: the former ING (earlier Rijkspostspaarbank/Postbank) office complex, Haarlemmerweg
  506-520, between the Haarlemmertrekvaart and the Brettenpad/allotment parks
  (BP-2017; STCRT-2018). In the old setup the offices were linked into three building clusters
  (BP-2017).
- Developer: Pinnacle B.V. bought the buildings and land from ING (MVRDV-MP gives "Client:
  Pinnacle B.V."; BP-2017 says "een ontwikkelaar" bought it; BP-PART names Pinnacle). Individual
  plots were developed through plot companies: "HLW 506" (Voortuinen, AW-VT), "HLW 512" (Pepper,
  AW-PEP), "HLW520 B.V." (Kruidentuin, VW-3).
- Masterplan: MVRDV (Nathalie de Vries, Frans de Witte), years "2014–2026", 77,000 m²
  (MVRDV-MP). Earlier MVRDV texts say 70,000 m² (MVRDV-2023, ARCHELLO-2018) **[conflict, minor]**.
- Landscape: Gustafson Porter + Bowman, London, from 2016 (GPB; MVRDV-MP).
- Plan structure: five plots in a strip along the Haarlemmerweg, each with its own design motif
  (MVRDV-MP). The zoning plan names the plots 1A, 1B, 2A, 2B, 3 and X, with working names
  Westertower (1A), Westerhaven (1B), Westerloft (2A), Westerblok (2B), Westercampus (3) and
  Westerpoort (X) (BP-2017).
- Planning: zoning plan Westerpark West adopted by the city council on 29 Nov 2017, amended so
  that Kavel X was taken out; the programme became max. 775 homes (instead of 925), underground
  parking and 1,200 m² of ground-floor uses (STCRT-2018; BP-2017 note). Building permits for
  kavel 3 (7 residential buildings) and kavels 1B+2A (3 residential buildings over a new garage)
  were granted 6 Dec 2017 (STCRT-2018). Voortuinen had its own earlier permit (BP-2017 §3.8.5).
  95 extra homes on kavel 2B (Haarlemmerweg 514) by deviation permit, published 10 Mar 2021
  (STCRT-2021). That is how the total rose above 775.
- Planned housing mix: half for sale, half mid-to-high rent; **no social rent** in the plan
  (BP-2017 §3.7 and §2). Residents regretted the lack of social rent at the information
  markets (BP-PART).
- Phases: construction began in 2019, plot by plot from east to west (MVRDV-2026). Voortuinen
  2021; Blend, Salt and Pepper delivered in August 2023; Basil 2024; Kruidentuin 2026
  (MVRDV-2026; MVRDV-2023).
- Totals: "15 buildings with 921 homes" (MVRDV-MP, MVRDV-2026). The Blend and Salt pages say
  913 homes (MVRDV-BLEND, MVRDV-SALT). The per-building figures below add up to 913, and so do
  the BAG dwelling counts (94+128+78+96+250+267) **[conflict: 913 vs 921]**. Earlier counts of
  "12 buildings" and "750 homes" (MVRDV-TOP, MVRDV-2023) are out of date.
- Concrete reuse: 7,000 tonnes of demolition rubble from the ING buildings were processed into
  aggregate for new concrete with New Horizon Urban Mining (MVRDV-2023, MVRDV-TOP).

## 2. Buildings (east to west)

Pins are computed inside each building's BAG outline as the point farthest from the edges.

### 2.1 De Voortuinen (plot 1A): conversion
- Architect: Elephant (Menno Kooistra, Peter van Gelder and team) (EL-VT; AW-VT). Original
  building: Rijkspostspaarbank offices, Arthur Staal, 1969-1971 (live place; nl.wikipedia, lead only).
- Completed 2021 (EL-VT; MVRDV-2026).
- Use: 94 homes, owner-occupied (AW-VT: "koopwoningen", 94); 2 commercial spaces (KW); BAG has
  94 dwellings plus 2 meeting-function and 2 other units.
- Height: 14 storeys, 12 of them residential (KW) **[1 source]**; 3D BAG roof max about 47 m
  (3DBAG). The live place says "two storeys on top"; NRP/Pieters wording ("three extra floors")
  was seen only in a search snippet, not checked **[conflict, unresolved]**.
- Trees: AW-VT says 100 trees; the live place says "about 120" **[conflict]**.
- Address: **Haarlemmerweg 290-488** (BAG pand 0363100012116511, bouwjaar 1970; AW-VT gives
  the same range). The live place's "Haarlemmerweg 506-520" is the old ING office address
  range and should probably be corrected.
- Pin: 52.385598, 4.854777 (the live pin; inside the BAG outline).
- Client: listed as "Westerpark West" (EL-VT), "HLW 506" (AW-VT); contractor Kondor Wessels
  Amsterdam (AW-VT; KW).
- Recognition: NRP Gulden Feniks 2022, winner in the transformation category (NRP-GF). Already
  on the live place, together with "Amsterdam Architecture Prize 2022 (nominated)" (not
  re-checked). It was a candidate for the Amsterdamse Nieuwbouwprijs 2022 (AW-VT page exists;
  nl.wikipedia says it lost to Spaarndammerhart, lead only).

### 2.3 Pepper (plot 2A, "Westerloft" pair): new build

### 2.5 Basil (plot 2B): new build, 4 buildings
- Architect: Elephant (EL-BAS; MVRDV-2026). Client per Elephant: Van Wijnen Haarlemmermeer;

- Completed 2024 (EL-BAS; MVRDV-2026; BAG bouwjaar 2024).
- Use: 250 rental homes, 155 free sector and **95 mid-rent** (VW-2B). Four separate buildings

  buildings' structures was reused (that is Voortuinen) **[inferred]**.
- Addresses (BAG): Haarlemmerweg 1140-1144 (60 dwellings, pand 0363100012570119), 1146-1150
  (60, pand 0363100012570124), 1152-1166 (130, pand 0363100012570120). BAG has three panden for
  four buildings, so one pand probably covers two linked blocks **[inferred]**.
- Pins: 52.385511, 4.852153 (pand ...570119); 52.385765, 4.852374 (...570124);
  52.385882, 4.851679 (...570120). For one ensemble pin use 52.385882, 4.851679 (largest pand).
- Recognition: none found.

## 3. Public space and landscape

- Landscape design: Gustafson Porter + Bowman, extending their 2003 Westergasfabriek park design
  (MVRDV-2023; MVRDV-MP; GPB).
- A 450 m public promenade and a boardwalk along the Haarlemmer canal (Haarlemmertrekvaart),
  with access to the water; each plot gets its own landscape character; planting inspired by
  Amsterdam's heemparks plus native, biodiverse planting (GPB).
- Green public squares, plazas and courtyard gardens that vary from plot to plot; largely
  car-free; the Brettenpad cycling and walking route passes through (MVRDV-MP; MVRDV-2026).
- Kruidentuin: four green plazas, stepping stones instead of solid paths to keep soil and water
  (MVRDV-KRUID); meeting zones and a sport and play zone (VW-3).
- Basil: shared central courtyard (VW-2B; EL-BAS).
- Kavel X (the parking lot east of Voortuinen, at the Bos en Lommerweg axis): the 2017 plan had a
  44 m "Westerpoort" tower here (BP-2017). The council removed it (STCRT-2018). A new plan,
  "Westerpark West Kavel X", rezones it as "Groen" (green), with parking allowed until 31 Dec
  2021 (STCRT-2019 draft; KX-CHV). That the plan was adopted is **[inferred]** from its VG01 id;
  the adoption decision itself was not read. Whether the lot is now actually green was not checked.

## 4. Still under construction or planned?

- MVRDV announced on 29 Oct 2026 that the masterplan "has completed construction" (MVRDV-2026).
- BAG: all eight residential panden are "Pand in gebruik" (BAG, 2026-11-25).
- Kavel X is zoned green, not for building (see above).
- Nothing else is under construction or planned within the project. BAG shows a few
  permitted or started panden around 4.8456 E, 52.3864 N, about 300 m west of Kruidentuin.
  They lie outside the project's outlines and were not investigated.
