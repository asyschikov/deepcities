## Per place

### Station Wildeman (west-nieuw-west-3)
- Scout said "AAP 2025 public prize": wrong. Arcam lists it as nominated, 2025. Its real
  prizes: honourable mention, BNA Gebouw van het Jaar 2026 (NL Times 5 June 2026, quoting the
  jury on post-65 buildings usually being demolished); Geurt Brinkgreve Bokaal 2025, second
  and third places went to De Tafelberg and Station Wildeman (Mecanoo news page). Mecanoo's
  wording does not say clearly which got which, so I wrote "top three".
- Kraemerschool full name, 2020 lease, name meaning (arrive, meet, travel on), entrance
  towards the Osdorper Ban with a big W: Rodi, 10 July 2023. Rodi is a local paper, the BNA
  entry and BETA page confirm the entrance move and the W.
- 1960s building, entrance moved from between stairwells to the south end, extension between
  stairwells, public foyer, classrooms used for homework classes, physiotherapy, life coaching:
  BETA project page and BNA entry pdf (BETA's own submission).
- Completed: Arcam says 2024; BNA entry says 2020-2025. Used "2024 (conversion)".
- Access: Arcam "openbaar toegankelijk: ja"; stationwildeman.nl rents out the foyer. Set
  Public parts only. No opening hours found.
- Photos: none on Commons.

### Four Towers Osdorp (Aquamarijn) (west-nieuw-west-4)
- Arcam's entry is called Aquamarijn; a caption reads "Aquamarijn, onderdeel van 4 Torens
  Osdorp". The scout's second Arcam entry ("4 torens Osdorp") does not resolve. Whether
  Aquamarijn is the name of one tower or of the whole is unclear: title uses both.
- Completion: Arcam 2009, Wiel Arets Architects 2010 (design 2002-2007). The AAP 2010 (won,
  per Arcam and Arets's awards list) judged work of 2009, so I used 2009 / 2000s. Check.
- Arets's awards list also gives "Amsterdam New Building Prize, Nomination, 2010".
- Facts: Arcam (renewal of the area, 390 flats, rent and sale, central core and mirrored
  plans, façade bands and aluminium panels, garage roof as public park with waterside walk,
  Berghoef comparison) and Arets (lifted ends over waterway and road, islands and bridges,
  glazed lobbies, oversized paving along the water).
- Arcam lists "openbaar toegankelijk: nee"; the park and walk are public per Arcam, so
  Outside only.
- Pin: Jan van Zutphenstraat 60 (PDOK), Arcam's address. Photos: none found on Commons.

### Klussen op de Klarenstraat (west-nieuw-west-6)
- Names vary: Arcam "Klussen op Klarenstraat", VanSchagen and S&A "Klussen op de
  Klarenstraat", DASH "Klussen aan de Klarenstraat". Used the S&A/architect form.
- Groosman 1956, 40 flats of 75 m2 (S&A says 74), plinth of storerooms and garages,
  De Alliantie demolished most other flats: Arcam, VanSchagen. 30 buyers, 40 to 190 m2,
  shell by Slokker delivered spring 2014, Urbannerdam on the business model: VanSchagen, S&A
  (Stedebouw & Architectuur 7, Dec 2014). DASH gives 45 to 200 m2; used the architect's figures.
- "First post-war walk-up in NL renovated under CPO" is VanSchagen's own claim, attributed.
- Scout called it "the first whole-flat collective self-build transformation in the garden
  cities"; Arcam says first in this way in the Staalmanpleinbuurt context ("voor het eerst ...
  een gehele portieketageflat"), the architect says first in the Netherlands. Attributed to
  the architects only.
- AAP 2015: Arcam "winnaar vakjury", VanSchagen "winnaar". Nationale Renovatie Prijs 2015 last
  three: VanSchagen only.
- Photos: the only Commons hit, "U.J. Klarenstraat foto 1.JPG", shows a taller gallery flat,
  not this block. None used.

### Basisschool De Wereldburger (west-nieuw-west-8)
- Facts: Arcam (metal cladding, aula of reused wood, Tripolis grandstand, gym floor, donor
  buildings, 3 m corridors, Landlab playground, wide pavement, AAP 2022 public prize) and
  ArchDaily (opened 1965, architect Gianni Cito, kept concrete frame, added timber frame).
- Arcam calls Tripolis "van Aldo Van Eyck"; it is generally credited to Aldo and Hannie van
  Eyck. Kept Arcam's wording; coordinator may want "Aldo and Hannie van Eyck".
- Still a school: allecijfers (DUO data) 513 pupils 2025-2026, including the dislocated
  Islamitische Basisschool De Kameleon. A 2017 school guide gave Koos Vorrinkweg 5; Arcam
  gives 3. Pin from PDOK on no. 3; the two are next to each other.
- No architect project page found (mokearchitecten.nl did not list it). Photos: none on Commons.

### Stadhuis Zaanstad (west-nieuw-west-12)
- Scout said "built over the tracks": wrong. The town hall stands beside the railway and the
  Provincialeweg (its closed long sides face them, town hall booklet); the bus station is
  underneath it (NH Nieuws 2012).
- In use since November 2011, officially opened January 2012 by Pieter van Vollenhoven, mayor
  Geke Faber's "mooiste stadhuis van Nederland", four floors, 25,000 m2: NH Nieuws, 25 Jan 2012.
- Eight enlarged Zaan houses, false fronts, the quoted architects, Tetris, Japanese tourists,
  wedding rings and makelaar: town hall booklet (pphp.nl pdf, apparently the municipality's
  own booklet; publisher not stated in the text I read) and welingelichtekringen (summarising
  NRC, Bernard Hulsman, 25-10-2012). Venturi/decorated sheds: welingelichtekringen and Lambo.
- Scout's "summit of postmodernism" is the headline of welingelichtekringen (a blog) based on
  an offline NRC piece: left out.
- Scout's Arie Keppler Prijs 2014 entries: not checked, no recognition set.
- Public hall: the booklet describes the publiekshal with its counters; no opening hours read.
- The draft covers the town hall only, not the whole Inverdan centre (Inverdan is described
  inside both Zaandam texts).
- Photos: Zaanstad Rathaus 10 (pyramid gable), 16 (Tetris gable), 01 (town hall with the
  Inntel hotel), all by Zairon, CC BY-SA 4.0, checked visually.
