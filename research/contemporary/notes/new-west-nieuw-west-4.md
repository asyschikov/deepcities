## De Henricus (west-nieuw-west-46)
- **Scout correction:** it is in Slotermeer (Louis Naarstigstraat 1, 1063 EN), not Geuzenveld. It is a
  remodel of the 1953 Catharinacomplex school, not a new school: work = Remodel or extension.
- Catharinacomplex (church and two schools round a courtyard, cloister), Evers and Sarlemijn, 1953 to
  1960, part of the AUP; pupils of Dom Hans van der Laan, plastic number (attributed to Berger Barnett);
  renovated for De Henricusschool and Visio (Visio 2020); Architectenweb Schoolbouw van het Jaar 2018:
  Berger Barnett project page (format.com/8741346, title "Het Catherina Complex - Berger Barnett Architecten").
- Bossche School, heavy lintels, mosaics and reliefs above portals, Romanesque references, municipal
  monument; brief (new teaching concept, more ground-floor room, new entrance, aula); start end 2015;
  glazed cloister; pink doors; stained ceiling visible again; glass entrance linking infant and primary
  school; bay windows giving a view through; playground grid; AAP 2018 nominee: Arcam.
- Address, year 2017/2018, project architect Lard Joordens, services hung under the old ceilings,
  large openings between classrooms: Gebouwd in Amsterdam 1572.
- Award date (8 Nov 2018) also in Pieters Bouwtechniek news via search summary (page redirected for curl).
- Completion: Arcam says 2017, Berger Barnett says Henricusschool 2018, Gebouwd in Amsterdam lists it in
  the 2017/2018 book. I used "2018 (remodel)" per the architect.
- Pin = Nominatim for Louis Naarstigstraat 1 (52.383482, 4.828389).

## Nieuw Reimerswaal (west-nieuw-west-47)
- Programme (school, sports hall, two playgroups, day care, care post; 51 flats, 16 wheelchair; 21 houses),
  six to three storeys, clients Ymere, Bouwfonds, ASKO; residents may use school facilities after hours;
  Makkink & Bey gates, Elspeth Pikaar glass: Mecanoo project page (design 2005 to 2007, built 2007 to 2011).
- Nine blocks under supervision of Architecten Cie; housing dominates exterior; glass panels as a band
  with pupils' texts and drawings; anthracite railings; AAP 2012 nominee: Arcam. Gebouwd in Amsterdam 723
  confirms addresses and programme.
- The school in the building is the Lukasschool (ASKO), per school-guide search results; I did not read a
  primary source for the name, so it is left out.
- Pin = Nominatim Osdorper Ban 134 (Mecanoo's address).

## LommerRijk (west-nieuw-west-48)
- **Scout correction:** not only Korth Tielens. Korth Tielens did the two northern blocks, Wingender
  Hovenier (now Office Winhov; Divisare credits "office winhov") the other four. Stadsdeel West, Kolenkitbuurt.
- Zuidelijk Veld renewal, six blocks, rent and owner-occupied, shared garden and public court, transition
  zone, balcony ceilings with round holes, half-sunken car park, stoops, client VOF Kolenkitbuurt Zuid (Far
  West, Stadgenoot), address Leeuwendalersweg 629, AAP 2012 nominee: Arcam.
- Canal-house relation, acid-treated concrete sills/bands/lintels, "arabesque" lettered railings: Office
  Winhov's text on Divisare (attributed to the architects).
- Brick colour: Arcam "lichtbruin", Winhov "yellow-hued"; written as "pale".
- Not found in Gebouwd in Amsterdam (could not locate the West listing). Number of homes unknown, left out.

## Tuinstede (west-nieuw-west-49)
- Tuinstede = Noordstrook, Masira Blok C (Gebouwd in Amsterdam 604, 110 homes, listed 2009/2010).
  Arcam and ArchDaily give 2011; I used 2011.
- Urban plan by Snitker/Borst 2008, three closed blocks (Van Gameren, ANA, Snitker/Borst), U-shape, two
  gates, black lower two floors and white upper three, clients Far West and De Key, AAP 2012 nominee: Arcam.
- 8.1 m bays, flexible plans, birch garden, half-sunken car park partly under the homes, split-level houses
  with 4.25 m living rooms, three floors of flats: Gebouwd in Amsterdam 604 and Snitker/Borst text on Archello.
- Left out: Arcam's "no formal street front versus informal courtyard front", which Gebouwd in Amsterdam
  gives for ANA's Blok B, not Blok C. Left out the planned second phase closing the U (future, unconfirmed).
- Pin = Nominatim Loosduinenstraat 40.

## Woningen Scheepvaartkwartier (west-nieuw-west-50)
- Khandekar plan; first block; seven parts by four firms, diversity on purpose; Geurst & Schulze parts
  A2, A4, A7, 46 homes; white concrete grid, round columns, red-brown and sand/bronze brick, bronze frames,
  French balconies; tall ground floor with live-work homes; car park fully underground; four passages:
  Gebouwd in Amsterdam 954 (2012/2013).
- South of the shopping centre, (half) columns, bands over corners and balconies, mix of home types,
  courtyard on the car park roof, AAP 2013 nominee: Arcam. Arcam spells it "Scheepsvaartkwartier".
- Arcam and Gebouwd in Amsterdam name different sets of other architects (hvdn, Moriko Kira, Nine Oaks, AWG
  against studioninedots, awg); I only say "four firms".
- Left out the "Nieuwe Osdorpergracht still to be dug" (2012 state, current status unchecked).
- Address from Gebouwd in Amsterdam (Arcam gives Van Suchtelen van de Haarestraat 205P). Pin = Nominatim no. 315.

## Hartenlust (west-nieuw-west-51)
- 23 houses, Domela Nieuwenhuisstraat 5-49, 4.80 m width (Bouwregelgeving minimum), deep plan, cheap
  facade ratio, large openings, parking under raised terraces: Gebouwd in Amsterdam 305 and Kempe Thill.
- "etalagewoningen", kitchen facing front garden, void, bedrooms upstairs, no corridors, sliding door,
  cheap material sprayed one colour, AAP 2009 nominee: Arcam.
- Zuiderkerkprijs 2008 nomination, client DeltaForte: Kempe Thill project page.
- Clients differ: Arcam "Stadgenoot i.s.m. Delta Forte", Gebouwd in Amsterdam "Algemene Woningbouw
  Vereniging, Delta Forte". I wrote Stadgenoot and Delta Forte. Hartenlust as a plan also includes DKV houses
  (separate Gebouwd in Amsterdam entry); this draft covers only the Kempe Thill row.
- Arcam's address (no. 68) is across the street; I used the 5-49 row, pin = Nominatim no. 27.

## Cadiz (west-nieuw-west-52)
- 69 homes, mostly for sale, Osdorp Midden-Noord, Mediterranean air, nine blocks perpendicular to the
  canal, three standalone, six paired by car park, rooflight, head houses with big windows, shutters for
  privacy, AAP 2009 nominee, client Stadgenoot: Arcam.
- Former duplex homes, car-free, openness to the water, white render, aluminium frames, railings designed
  for the project, reference to modernism and residents' backgrounds, Karres en Brands and Botteskerkpark,
  client Delta Forte for Stadgenoot: Gebouwd in Amsterdam 491.
- Pin = Nominatim Wolbrantskerkweg 69 (middle of 1-137; Arcam gives no. 1).

## Averroes (west-nieuw-west-53)
- Arcam's text is only context (Confuciusplein renewal: greener, safer, livelier; square with four
  neighbourhoods kept), plus credits (Erna van Sambeek, VOF De Stadstuinen and Far West, 2009, AAP 2010 nominee,
  Confuciusplein 33).
- Architecture: Gebouwd in Amsterdam 487 "Confuciusplein - Averroes, Avicenna, Noordblok" (Van Sambeek
  Architects, 2008/2009): two buildings with shops, inner garden and roof garden, square of trees, five to two
  storeys, 1950s horizontal brick lines, large frames, folded loggias. The 101 homes figure covers the whole
  project, so no number is given.
- Name after the philosopher: Geheugen van West (local site; also says Far West built 47 flats there, not
  used as single-source).
- Uncertain: which of the two buildings is "Averroes" exactly; I describe the pair and say Averroes is one.

## Kalverpolder lookout tower (west-nieuw-west-60)
- In Zaanstad (Zaandam), so `municipality: Zaanstad`.
- DaF-architecten project page: client Stichting Zaanse Schans, completed 2013, 10 m, 80 m², engineer
  Tentij; all oak; start of a struinpad through the Kalverpolder; trilveen and broad ditches; Zaan industrial
  mills; "boerse logica", much timber sawn by Het Jonge Schaap; 1:20 oak model; millwright Bart Nieuwenhuijs
  prefabricated and assembled on site; pen-and-hole joints, toognagels, forged nails, bolts; view changes on
  the way up. Architizer (DaF's English text) agrees.
- Daily free access, location Schansend 7: DagjeWeg (checked by them Feb 2025). Pin = OSM "Uitkijktoren"
  viewpoint node (52.474330, 4.822702).
- **Scout correction:** the Arie Keppler Prijs 2014 jury report lists it only among the entries (not among
  the 14 nominations), so no recognition is given. Category set to `green` (lookout at the start of a nature
  path); work = Landscape or public space.

## Verification

Checked against the sources again (Arcam pages and architects' pages by curl; Gebouwd in Amsterdam 305, 487, 491, 604, 605, 606, 723, 954, 1572 read in BrowserOS, own tabs, closed). All nine buildings are still standing: BAG lists each pand as "Pand in gebruik". The Dutch has no 8-word run shared with any source fetched (shingle check, including GiA 487). validate.py: 0 errors. Photos were not searched again (a separate pass follows).

Changes:
- **Averroes, which building:** the site plan in the city's GiA drawings (projectdocumentatie_tekeningen/487.pdf, p. 1) labels "Averroes" as the freestanding block on the square. Across the Socratesstraat is a second block with "Avicenna" and the "Confucius Noordblok". Arcam's address (Confuciusplein 33) falls inside that block's BAG pand (0363100012112771, 48 units). Designer Van Sambeek & van Veen / Erna van Sambeek is given by both Arcam and GiA, so the place stays. Text rewritten: it now describes one block, not "two blocks", and the wrong "next to Avicenna" is gone. The plan-level facts (inner and roof garden, five down to two storeys, commercial space) are now tied to the plan, not to Averroes alone. "Shops below" was cut for Averroes: BAG gives health care and sport uses there, not retail. Pin unchanged (the BAG address point of no. 33, inside the pand). The GiA drawings PDF was added to the sources.
- **Hartenlust, client:** cut Stadgenoot. Only Delta Forte is named by more than one source (Kempe Thill "DeltaForte BV", GiA "Delta Forte", Arcam "Stadgenoot i.s.m. Delta Forte"). GiA's other client, AWV, and Arcam's Stadgenoot do not agree, so EN and NL now say only "for the client Delta Forte". The "developer" label was dropped too. **Pin:** BAG shows the 23 single-house panden of the row (bouwjaar 2007) between 52.350344 and 52.351343. The existing pin (52.350825, 4.799519, address point of no. 27) lies inside pand 0363100012071028, the middle house, so it is kept.
- Kalverpolder: "freely open, every day" / "elke dag vrij toegankelijk" narrowed to "open to the public" / "openbaar toegankelijk". Daily and free access came only from DagjeWeg. Public access is also supported by zaanseschans.com, where the walk starts at the tower behind the Zaans Museum. Pin confirmed: it is the OSM node 3954924488 "Uitkijktoren".
- Cadiz: "the architects sought a Mediterranean air" is now attributed to Arcam (EN and NL summary). "A neighbourhood people would recognise as their own" was narrowed to "a recognisable character" (Arcam: "goed herkenbare buurt"). "Patterned railings" became "decorative railings" (NL "sierhekken").
- LommerRijk: "pale brick" became "light-brown brick" (Arcam "lichtbruin", the only source covering all six blocks). "A place to sit out" became "a place to linger" (Arcam "verblijfsruimte").

- De Henricus: NL "Na ruim zestig jaar" became "In de loop der jaren" (an inference, not in any source). The Schoolgebouw van het Jaar 2018 award is confirmed by Berger Barnett and the Pieters Bouwtechniek news item (that page now redirects, so it was confirmed through its search listing).
