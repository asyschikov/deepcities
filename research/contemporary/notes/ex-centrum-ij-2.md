## centrum-ij-20 Lloyd Hotel (qv0a05zf47i06yulvlmtq4dd)
- MVRDV 2004: facade, leaded windows and tower kept; floors removed in the middle for voids, sky visible from some spots; cells and bars removed; extra floors and stairs in side wings; rooms by MVRDV, Bureau Lakenvelder, Joep van Lieshout; work by Seyferth, Ineke Hans, Jurgen Bey, Richard Hutten: https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/lloyd-hotel/
- The Hoxton refit: Kentie Architecten, 16 rooms added, one in the tower; Nicemakers did entrance, foyer, restaurant, bar: https://kentie-architecten.nl/projecten/the-hoxton-lloyd-amsterdam/
- Extra written: the live text only says "sketched out by MVRDV".
- Access "Public parts only" (hotel lobby, restaurant, bar). Arcam says "not public", which reflects hotel rooms.
- Uncertain: live text dates the competition 1996, Arcam says 1999; I left the year out. Whether all of MVRDV's 2004 interiors survived the Hoxton refit is not known; the extra describes them in the past tense and only the central void in "Look for". The scout's "1 to 5 star" was not sourced (MVRDV's project URL now shows another project) and is not used.
