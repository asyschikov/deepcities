## Jakoba Mulderhuis (oost-ijburg-19)
- Sources: Marc Koehler Architects story page (opening 1 Sept 2022, 6,000 to 7,000 students,
  Faculty of Technology, Koehler and Nanne de Ru as former HvA students taught in the old
  Leeuwenburg building, de Architekten Cie. joined in 2014, tower 54 m/13 storeys and low block
  33 m/6 storeys, Jura limestone matching the Theo Thijssenhuis, window pattern rising left to
  right, Benno Premselahuis as listed counterpart, working name Conradhuis, Jakoba Mulder and the
  Amsterdamse Bos, high- and low-rise can function separately); Powerhouse Company (each
  department its own floor with a showcase terrace, atrium terraces); Arcam (AAP 2023
  nomination, "Openbaar toegankelijk: Ja", terraces meet in the atrium); ArchDaily.
- Dropped: Arcam's "only the top floor has traditional classrooms", because the Koehler page
  says the high-rise wing largely consists of classrooms. BREEAM score and BIM tender left out
  (architect's page only, and not needed).
- Address: Arcam says Mauritskade; the Koehler page says "on Rhijnspoorplein". PDOK's
  Rhijnspoorplein 2 lies at the far west end of the campus, so the address is given as
  Rhijnspoorplein, corner of Wibautstraat and Mauritskade, and the pin is on the Wibautstraat
  corner of BAG pand 0363100012241500 (where the tower stands). Worth a check on the ground.
- Access: Public parts only, attributed to Arcam in the text.
- Leeuwenburg (c5h6lbhnx9cthv71wk9qx99n) and the Jakoba Mulder memorial
  (j3808eim27rd4v9ndfrkw65b) are on the map; the text points to Leeuwenburg as "its own place".

  hollowed centre with stepped terraces, window widths and pier thickness "the building smiles",
  curved plan following the road); Arcam (Piet Heintunnel, curve, terraces on the south side,

- Pin: BAG pand 0363100012245757 (built 2016, 365 units, in use), centroid; addresses on Kees
  Broekmanstraat per PDOK reverse geocoding. No single house number used.
- An NH Nieuws/AT5 item about DUWO tiling over residents' gardens at "a student complex on the
  Kees Broekmanstraat" may be this building, but it doesn't name it: not used.

## De Gieter and De Slijper (oost-ijburg-28)
- Scout line corrected: not "timber-hybrid". De Gieter is green-grey concrete, De Slijper white
  tiles; the "raised" element is a narrow mesh-wrapped circulation volume between them; the
  green spaces are an inner courtyard and a roof garden.
- Sources: Arcam (Space Encounters, LOLA, client VORM, 2023, AAP 2024 nomination, not publicly
  accessible, facade descriptions, roof garden); ArchDaily (2023, client Vorm, collaboration with
  OZ, Workshop Architects and BETA on the Werkspoorhal block); Metalocus (architects' full text:
  names after casting and grinding of metal for locomotives, three bays echoing canal houses,
  shed-roof roofline, white tiles with blue joints contrasting with the Werkspoorhal, mesh
  stair volume, green patio, atelier dwellings, brothers Gijs and Joost Baks); Marcel Lok's
  Oostenburg page for the Werkspoor history.
- Pin: OSM footprint "De Slijper en De Gieter" (way 892437013), matching BAG pand
  0363100012253353 (2023, in use); nearest addresses on Oostenburgermiddenstraat. The
  Werkspoorhal itself is not on the map.
