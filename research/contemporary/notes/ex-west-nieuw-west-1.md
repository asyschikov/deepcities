## west-nieuw-west-26 Westergasfabriek (k63k760wpsjf7b8y2ah9ajm5, see "Wrong ids" above)
- Competition won 1997 by Kathryn Gustafson with Francine Houben (Mecanoo); soil displaced within the site into a new relief; straight axis and "Path of Dreams"; events field with slope; water and planting against the railway; Mecanoo supervisor of the architecture: https://mecanoo.nl/Projects/project/91/Westergasfabriek-Terrain
- "Changement"; Neil Porter; awards list (Gouden Piramide 2004, RIBA Urban Space by Design 2005 second place, European Prize for Urban Public Space 2006 finalist, LI Award 2007, Europa Nostra 2010): https://gp-b.com/cultuurpark-westergasfabriek (the landscape architects' own page; the Gouden Piramide is a state prize for commissioning, so it went to the client side)
- Reopened 2003; 1,024 solar panels on the Gashouder roof in summer 2018: https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/westergasfabriek/
- Mecanoo's 2026 note on 25 years of the park: https://www.mecanoo.nl/News/ID/737/Westerpark-turns-25-a-park-shaped-by-people-evolving-with-the-city
- Dates: Mecanoo gives realisation 2000 to 2001, GP+B 2006 (later phases), Arcam and most sources 2003 for the opening. Completed set to 2003.
- Work "Landscape or public space". Extra written: the live text is about the gasworks and only says the ground was "capped and landscaped".
