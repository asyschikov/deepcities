# ex-centrum-ij-1: notes

Overrides: `research/contemporary/overrides/ex-centrum-ij-1.json` (14 entries, all 14 keys).
Extra descriptions written for 9 places, left out for 5. No wrong matches, nothing dropped.
`validate.py` checks Kind A YAML only, so it does not apply to an overrides file. I checked the
select values against `scripts/data/amsterdam-contemporary-fields.json` instead (all valid). I ran
an 8-word overlap check of the EN and NL extras against every fetched source: the only hit is the
quoted Nijhoff line. `nlcheck.py` is not at the path the brief gives, so it was not run.

How the prizes were checked: Arcam's AAP year pages (arcam.nl/architectuur-prijs/<year>/) and
jury reports (arcam.nl/architectuurprijs/vakjuryrapport-amsterdamse-architectuurprijs-<year>/).
Arcam's own guide pages are not always up to date: the guide still says only "Genomineerd" for
the Holocaust Museum (2025), the UB (2026) and the bike garage (2023), but the jury reports name
all three as winners.

- Vijzelgracht: cut "the long run of escalators here is that rule applied at depth" (NL "De lange roltrappen hier zijn die regel op grote diepte"): no source links the escalators to the design rule.
- Nationaal Holocaustmuseum, NL: "prees vooral" narrowed to "prees onder meer"; the BNA jury praised several things (NL Times, 6 June 2025).
- De Nederlandsche Bank: the cash hall became "a meeting centre with a 120-metre reading room" (Mecanoo), not the reading room itself; NL "kassierszaal" changed to "kashal" to match. Dropped "built it from 2020 to 2025" from the extra (EN and NL) because the live text already says Mecanoo rebuilt it from 2020 to 2025.

- Sources added to these notes: Muziekgebouw 3XN and 2005 confirmed by ArchDaily (https://www.archdaily.com/38816/muziekgebouw-3xn), not only the live text; bike garage completion (delivered Q1 2023) and AAP 2023 on Arcam's guide page https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/fietsenstalling-stationsplein/.
