## Solid 11 (west-nieuw-west-37)
- Tony Fretton, 2011, client Stadgenoot, team Laszlo Csutoras (Arcam); Fretton names Tony Fretton, Jim Mckinney, David Owen; 8,000 m2, EUR 10m (Fretton PDF).
- Solid concept (no fixed use, live/work/both, change over time), inspiration partly from 1980s/90s squatting culture, m2 and rent set by online auction, Stadgenoot keeps the shell and maintains exterior and common parts, tenants fit out: Arcam.
- Auction 7 May 2011, ~7,000 m2 rentable; 120 bidders, 39 winners, ~60% businesses incl. shops and a hotel, 40% housing (25% free sector, 15% social): Binnenlands Bestuur, 29 April and 13 May 2011 (read via WebFetch summaries; the site refuses full reproduction).
- 200-year lifespan: Fretton project page ("A 200 year lifespan") and Building Design standfirst. I did not say the client demanded it (that wording came only from search summaries).
- Near Vondelpark, one of three new buildings masterplanned by Jo Crepain on the site of a former hospital; self-supporting brick on a porphyry base; south-facing balconies; metal tree forms supporting a glass acoustic screen towards the street: Fretton page and PDF.
- RIBA Award 2012, listed among "the 9 RIBA Award buildings in the European Union": Dezeen 21 June 2012. AAP 2012 nominated: Arcam.
- **Uncertain:** which hospital. Funda listings on the same street call the area "WG-terrein" (Wilhelmina Gasthuis), but I found no authoritative source, so the text says "a former hospital". Current tenants not confirmed (OSM shows hotels at nos. 10 and 26 on the same street but I could not tie them to Solid 11), so the text only gives the auction result of 2011.
- Pin: Nominatim for Eerste Constantijn Huygensstraat 30 (Arcam gives 30D).
- No photo: nothing on Commons identifiable as Solid 11 (Commons API was also rate-limiting).
- Category: housing (Arcam typology "Commercieel, Woningbouw"); could equally be civic.

## Van Beuningenplein (west-nieuw-west-38)
- Garage for 200 cars financed from years of saved parking money; play square as meeting place; Concrete commissioned with Dijk&Co and Carve; "huiskamer van de buurt"; pavilion on Van Hallstraat as garage entrance, bus stop and teen centre; five pivot doors in expanded-metal panels to skate area and blue pitch; central café pavilion with terrace dividing pitch and playground (older/younger children); H-beams with timber joists as imaginary ceilings; old poplars kept; undulating play surface; credits; 2011: Arcam.
- Two-layer underground parking; Concrete chosen for the pavilions after a small closed competition: ArchDaily.
- Square's former function as play and sports field brought back; two climbing towers with slides, sand, swings, hammock forest; two pitches, one sunken with concrete edges for skaters, water play in summer and can be flooded for skating in winter; Richard Krajicek Foundation funding (cut for length): Carve.
- Café now: Grand Café Het Paviljoen, Van Beuningenplein 21, with roof terrace (its own site).
- **Date conflict:** Arcam says the stadsdeel decided on renewal in 2004, Carve says the city decided in 2007; Carve dates its part 2010, Arcam and ArchDaily 2011. I left the decision year out and used 2011.
- Photos: two Commons photos by A. Bakker, June 2011, showing the Van Hallstraat pavilion with the H-beam frame (I looked at both). "Van Beuningenplein.jpg" shows the De Bazel houses, not used.
- Category green, as other slices used for public spaces; it is mostly paved, so civic would also do.

## Medisch Centrum voor Dieren (west-nieuw-west-39)
- Dedato (Dick Venneman, Migiel van der Palen, Jurgen Stoye), client Minerva Development, 2011; next to metro Isolatorweg; specialist pet hospital with 24-hour emergency desk; small budget; free-standing on Sloterdijk business park; slightly raised, seems to float; grey synthetic roofing skin with expanded-metal screens, no visible fixings, deliberately referring to a dog cage; glass curtain wall at the head with entrance and waiting room; white plastered core with operating rooms and ICU, timber corridor walls, exposed timber frame and services: Arcam.
- Coarse expanded metal all round, friendly at night through half-transparency, AAP 2012 nomination: Dedato project page; the metal by MetaDecor: Dedato second page.
- Still at Isolatorweg 45 next to the metro station; emergency and specialist clinic: mcvoordieren.nl (home and route page). Part of AniCura since 1 November 2016: AniCura careers page.
- Access "Outside only": only clients with an animal go in (Arcam: not publicly accessible).
- No photo found on Commons.

## Rembrandtpark Building / Leonardo Hotel and Hotelschool The Hague (west-nieuw-west-40)
- **Scout correction:** not a new building. It is a conversion (2012) of an early-1970s office block by Zanstra, Gmelig Meyling & De Clercq Zubli; set as Conversion with `original_use`. The hotel is no longer a Ramada: it is now the Leonardo Hotel Amsterdam Rembrandtpark (Leonardo site; Arcam photo captions; Commons description "anno 2024"). Title uses the building's name (RVO "Rembrandtpark Building", Commons "Rembrandtparkgebouw").
- Tower of 19 floors, low wing of 3, at right angles on a half-sunken car park with a parking deck roof; orange mirror glass; AMRO bank first user, then GAK, Pink Roccade, Getronics, vacancy; ZZDP as successor of the original firm; reopened 2012 as four-star hotel plus Hotelschool The Hague; 1.85 m grid suits rooms of 2 or 3 bays; 446 rooms, 8 conference rooms, prefab bathrooms hoisted in; glass front round the double-height base, new entrance on the street; pebble concrete cleaned, mirror glass replaced by clear glass; school with kitchens, boarding with 128 double rooms, public training hotel and restaurant, auditorium in a former courtyard; entrance moved to the Jan Evertsenstraat side; walkway removed; client Pronam; AAP 2013 nominated: Arcam (the original architects' name is spelled "de Clerq Zubli" there; I used the usual spelling De Clercq Zubli).
- 1973 office; Pronam (Vastint since 2014) owner and initiator; 30-year lease made the conversion feasible; Apollo Hotels was the first operator; low wing with two restaurants for outside guests, media library, 128 student rooms: RVO.
- Leonardo: Staalmeesterslaan 410, 446 rooms, Skybar on the 18th floor with roof terrace (RVO says sky bar on the 17th floor, so the text says only "at the top of the tower"). The hotel's claim that its 85 m terrace is the city's tallest is not used.
- Hotelschool Amsterdam campus still here, Jan Evertsenstraat 171; Les Saveurs welcomes guests from inside and outside the school: hotelschool.nl.
- Photos: four from Commons Category:Leonardo Hotel Amsterdam Rembrandtpark and Category:Hotelschool The Hague, Amsterdam (2015 to 2021).

## Buurtfabriek Ruimzicht (west-nieuw-west-41)
- **Scout correction:** not "housing over a former factory". The factory was replaced: Arcam says the new building stands where a factory used to be. Set as New building.
- Bellamybuurt village character, 19th-century farm plot pattern; triangle between Bellamystraat, Wenslauerstraat and the quay; built in close consultation with neighbours; five strips of different height and length, lower in the middle so homes behind see the water; stepped plots leave a meeting zone along the quay; plinth with workshops, studios, youth space and grand café on ground and first floor; client Stadgenoot with Delta Forte; 2009; AAP 2010 nominated: Arcam.
- 26 homes; home types; full-width folding windows; white concrete planks, big roof hoods, steel escape stair; everything in one colour; factory look as reference to the canal's industry: Rohmer.
- **Address conflict:** Arcam gives Bellamystraat 141-H; Rohmer gives "Kostverlorenkade 69 e.o." and calls it De Baarsjes (it is in Oud-West). Nominatim puts Tweede Kostverlorenkade 69 at 52.36634, 4.86211 and Bellamystraat 141 at 52.36587, 4.86182; the pin is between the two.
- Current café not confirmed (OSM shows a bar called Barricade at Tweede Kostverlorenkade 70; its site is JavaScript-only), so the text says the café was "planned". Access "Outside only", although Arcam marks it publicly accessible (the quay zone).
- No photo found on Commons.

## Noordkop Spaarndammerstraat (west-nieuw-west-42)
- Crossing of Spaarndammerdijk, Tasmanstraat and Spaarndammerstraat; renovation of existing buildings plus a new block; supermarket and coffeeshop at ground level; white half-round concrete entrance pieces; brick "coulisses" as noise screen for the galleries; gold-coloured wall and railings; five four-room homes per floor; kitchens on the gallery side, living rooms and balconies on the garden; garden at the foot of the dike half a level below the street; Marlies Rohmer and Floris Hund; client De Key/De Principaal; 2007; AAP 2008 nominated: Arcam.
- Hinge between old Spaarndammerbuurt and new Houthaven islands; mostly social rent, hence a simple access; one lift for all homes, garage and garden; open "galerette" on the north-east, the noisy side; tripartite façade fitting the 19th-century neighbours; 20 homes (6 sale, 14 social rent), supermarket, coffeeshop, studios, half-mechanical sunken car park: Rohmer.
- Albert Heijn still at Spaarndammerdijk 3: ah.nl store page. The coffeeshop's current status is not verified and the text does not claim it is still there.
- Address: Arcam Spaarndammerdijk 1, Rohmer Spaarndammerdijk 7; pin = Nominatim for no. 1.
- Photo: Commons has "Spaarndammerdijk - bebouwing (1)/(2).jpg" (2024) without coordinates; I could not confirm they show this block, so none used.

## De Binderij (west-nieuw-west-43)
- **Scout note:** not purely new; the Stokkink bookbindery was kept and converted, with new-build parts. Set as Conversion with `original_use`.
- Stokkink bookbindery: hall of 1906 and prefab halls of the 1970s; concrete skeleton with mushroom columns kept, visible in garage and homes; old uninsulated brick façade replaced, top floor demolished, openings enlarged with folding windows, glass top storey; ten boat houses on the former loading quay with ground dug away for an indoor mooring, boats up to 7 m, warehouse-like fronts; square between street and water used as site during building; neighbours lost roof gardens, got a large roof garden; 115 parking places; Bureau Kroner (Mark Graafland, Jaap Mittelhaeuser); client De Key/De Principaal with PFC2 Envelopment; 2010; AAP 2011 nominated: Arcam.
- Basement and ground floor of the old building as parking; canal-side rooms as live-work units, studio homes and lofts; new homes on Gillis van Ledenberchstraat by a new square; ten waterside villas; 71 homes; quote from Het Parool (not used, I did not read the Parool piece): Bureau Kroner.
- **Count conflict:** Arcam lists 23 flats, 10 boat homes, 16 studios, 9 live-work homes and 4 business units (58 homes); Kroner says 71 homes. The text gives no total.
- Pin: Nominatim for Gillis van Ledenberchstraat 46 (car park "De Binderij 1" per a Funda listing, used only for the address). The complex runs along the street to the canal, so the pin is approximate.
- No photo found on Commons.

## De Titaan (west-nieuw-west-44)
- Two symmetrical blocks along the metro at the edge of the Kolenkitbuurt; Sjoerd Soeters urban plan; Eigen Haard renewing all 500 homes; rehousing plan let many residents move straight into new build; 57 social rent four-room homes with closed kitchen for large families, 56 mid-rent three-room homes with open kitchen; inspired by nearby "Nieuwe Akbar" by post-war architects Evers and Sarlemijn in detailing and colour; north block orange with yellow frames, south the reverse; Jeroen Geurst and Rens Schulze; client Eigen Haard; 2015; AAP 2016 nominated; Berceusestraat 38: Arcam.
- Architect's name for it "Ringspoorzone Kolenkit, blok 5": 12 ground-level homes and 101 flats with car park; parallel to the ring railway that bounds the neighbourhood on the west; middle part between two projecting end bays; family homes in the two-storey base grip over the car park or storerooms; galleries switch sides above, explained by A10 noise; concrete lintel and sill with differently coloured brick between: Geurst & Schulze project page. The architect's page is muddled on compass directions (railway on the west, A10 noise "in the east"), so the text says only that the galleries change sides because of the A10.
- Delivered by Eigen Haard in 2015 and nominated for the AAP 2016: Eigen Haard magazine June 2016.
- The scout's architect (Geurst & Schulze) is confirmed by Arcam and the firm's own page.
- No photo found on Commons.

## Brede School Het Meervoud (west-nieuw-west-45)
- August Allebéplein in Slotervaart; primary school plus day nursery, after-school care, GG&GD and children's dentist; ground-floor entrance with stepped stair; playrooms joined to the central hall by a transparent industrial curtain to form an aula; raised gym in the middle gives the front its height and marks the entrance; rosettes refer to Arab culture and the multicultural society, inspired by Jean Nouvel's Institut du Monde Arabe; glass and grey-painted concrete below, dark grey windows above; benches and planters in the façade; Marlies Rohmer; client De Key; 2013; AAP 2014 nominated: Arcam.
- "Institut du Monde Arabe, but in brick"; Westelijke Tuinsteden transformation; children 0 to 14, families and surroundings; full list of functions; 22 m wide building with central heart; double-height gym with its own access for evening use; ends the sightline from the Marius Bauerstraat; roof for school gardens; address August Allebéplein 5-21: Rohmer.
- Current use: OSM still has the building "Het Meervoud" (2013) here and it was a polling station under that name (allecijfers.nl, search summary). Which primary school occupies it now is not confirmed, so the text does not name one.
- Pin: OSM building centroid (52.363033, 4.839038), matching Nominatim for August Allebéplein 5.
- No photo: the only nearby Commons photo (August Allebeplein (N), 2022) looks north at the Albert Heijn and the sculpture, not the school.
