## Station Amsterdam Bijlmer ArenA (zuidoost-weesp-2)
- 1970s station too small after the Arena (1996) and track doubling (2006); dark narrow underpass replaced by a wide promenade linking Amsterdamse Poort and the Arena side; no traditional hall; tracks about 10 m up; ~200 m roof clad in glass and wood; voids for daylight; BNA Gebouw van het Jaar 2008 and RIBA European Award: Arcam (architects listed as ARCADIS Architecten, Grimshaw & Partners; AAP "Genomineerd, 2008").
- 70 m wide diagonal boulevard; eight tracks; raking A-frame columns; 18 m cantilevers at the ends; voids for safety and daylight; work started 2001; opened by Princess Máxima 17 Nov 2007; Stirling Prize 2008 shortlist: Dezeen 2008 (Grimshaw text). Stirling shortlist also stated in Dezeen 2014 (Kraaiennest article).
- Eight tracks, two of them metro: nl Wikipedia (six train tracks + two metro tracks). Arcam's "vier treinsporen en twee metrosporen ... in totaal acht" is internally inconsistent; I used "eight, two for the metro".
- Architects Jan van Belkum (Arcadis) and Neven Sidor (Grimshaw), BNA "modern classic": DutchNews 2008.
- Roof vault count left out: Arcam says five V-shaped trusses, Dezeen four identical segments.
- Grimshaw's own project page did not load by curl (generic page). Pin: Wikipedia coordinates. Address is approximate (Arcam gives Johan Cruijff Boulevard 602, which BAG lists as a small office unit in the station).
- Photos: two 2019 Flickr imports (CC BY 2.0) and a 2011 exterior.

## Metro station Kraaiennest (zuidoost-weesp-3)
- Part of the Bijlmer renewal; K-buurt; transformation around and over the existing platform and track; brief: functional, manageable, socially safe; canopy, hall, emergency stair; technical rooms in a closed volume clad in stainless steel with plant motifs; lowest 4 m perforated against graffiti and fly-posting; platform 11 m up; massive-looking canopy on slender columns with a bright red underside; ready 2012; AAP nomination 2013: Arcam.
- Laser-cut stainless steel; old station 1970s with stairs only, new escalators; started on site 2010; lantern quote; RIBA: Dezeen 2014 (and the architect's press text quoted there).
- Reuse of existing platform; entrance on the Karspeldreef; market square; completed 2012; "RIBA Awards 2014": Maccreanor Lavington project page.
- **RIBA award wording uncertain.** DutchNews 2014 (citing Parool) says "RIBA European Award"; Archinect's 2014 RIBA National Award winners gallery lists it under "European Union"; the architects say only "RIBA Awards 2014". I wrote "RIBA Award 2014 (European Union)" and in the text "a RIBA award ... listed among the European Union winners". Scout's "RIBA European Award 2014" may be right but I could not read RIBA's own page.
- Reopened September 2013 and cost ~€13 million (nl Wikipedia), €14 million (architects' press text): left out; completed year follows Arcam and the architects (2012). Froukje Taconis as designer of the screen (nl Wikipedia only): left out.
- Pin: Wikipedia coordinates. Photo: only one post-2012 Commons file found (the steel screen, 2013); the other Kraaiennest files are 2005 and 2011 (old station).

## Ziggo Dome (zuidoost-weesp-4)
- 17,000; gap between Arena/GelreDome and HMH/Ahoy; multi-usable (tennis, ice rink, Olympic pool); shell as a bell jar to keep sound in; LED façade as video screen; facilities level with the hall floor, wide corridors; architects Jan Benthem, Job Schön [sic]; client Black Box Real Estate; 2012; De Passage 100; AAP nomination 2013: Arcam.
- "Single purpose - multi use"; delivered 2012; 80,000 kg of gravel for tennis in 2014; pop-up restaurant in the pandemic; former colleague Job Schroën, one of its architects, married there: Benthem Crouwel news. I used BC's spelling Schroën.
- Left out (Wikipedia only or conflicting): 90 x 90 x 30 m; LED count (Arcam 120,000, nl Wikipedia 840,000); first concert 24 June 2012; seat breakdown. Scout's "weddings" (plural) narrowed to the one sourced wedding.
- Pin = PDOK geocode of De Passage 100. Photos: 2020 and 2013 Commons exteriors.

## Living Tomorrow, the "kruimeldief" (zuidoost-weesp-5)
- UNStudio; nickname kruimeldief and "Huis van de Toekomst"; temporary, five years; two rounded volumes (vertical and horizontal) in profiled aluminium; domotics (mirror with news and traffic, fridge ordering groceries); Living Tomorrow left 2008, empty, city bought it for nearly €7 million to keep a grip on the area; ID&T based there for some years; not publicly accessible; De Entree 300: Arcam.
- 4,000 m², 32 m tower: UNStudio project page.
- Third Living Tomorrow project after two in Belgium; official opening 11 December (2003), public from 3 January 2004; five-year expo; designed by Living Tomorrow's architects with UN Studio: Architectenweb news id=1932.
- Opening 11 December 2003 and the continuous curve of the façade: Architectuur.org (10-12-2003).
- ID&T to rent from the city as new head office; empty since June 2008; city paid about €7 million: Architectenweb news id=21102, dated Wednesday 18 November (2009 by the weekday and the 2008 vacancy).
- **Current use unconfirmed.** BAG (via PDOK) lists De Entree 300 as built 2003, "Pand in gebruik" (registered use winkelfunctie). Google Maps still labels it "Gemeente Amsterdam Huis Van De Toekomst". A Gemeenteblad 2026 notice records a withdrawn permit for temporary Big Brother (Endemol Shine) buildings "thv De Entree 300" (decision 4 Nov 2025), not about the pavilion itself. ID&T later moved to the A'DAM Toren (search summary only). nl Wikipedia's "used by a Dutch bank" is unsourced. The text says only that it still stands and is closed to the public. Worth a site check.
- Scout's "later a music events base" is right (ID&T). No Commons photo found.

## Nieuw Echtenstein (Egoli-toren) (zuidoost-weesp-6)
- 80 social-rent homes plus community ground floor, E-buurt, Rochdale, LEVS, safe home for a vulnerable group, remnant of honeycomb flat Echtenstein, low-rise estate, Gooiseweg roads; neighbourhood garden; balconies and columns leading greenery up; flats 40 to 85 m², all with outdoor space; checkerboard façade of leftover bricks; realised 2023; AAP nomination 2024; "Openbaar toegankelijk: Nee": Arcam guide (name "Egoli-toren/ Nieuw Echtenstein") and Arcam AAP 2024 exhibition page.
- Western tip of the E-buurt (LEVS NL page and Archello; LEVS EN page says "eastern end", a translation slip); sheltered housing ("beschermd wonen"); ~100 t CO2 saved by leftover bricks (architects' estimate, attributed); passive cooling by balcony overhangs: LEVS project pages.
- Nine storeys, steel cables for climbers on the first six storeys, prefab, six months without scaffolding: Archello (architect-supplied text; attributed to the architects in the body).
- Left out: Archello's claim that 60 of the 80 flats went to people from the nearby "G&G blocks" (architect text, unclear which blocks).
- **Address found via BAG**, not given by any source: pand 0363100012254989, Egoli 11 to 33 (1103 AC), bouwjaar 2023, exactly 80 dwellings plus 6 assembly and 4 other units, flats 11 to 85 m². This matches the project; pin = the pand's centroid. "Egoli" is the street name (BAG spells it "EGoli").
- No Commons photo found (search API rate-limited).

## Bijlmer Parktheater (zuidoost-weesp-7)
- Commission won spring 2004; four users (Circus Elleboog, Krater Theater, Jeugdtheaterschool, Theaterwerkplaats); site between Bijlmerpark and the new Anton de Komplein; ellipse with offset floors giving a covered entrance; wooden slats and corrugated sheets; LED colour; daylight via a glazed band round the main hall on the first floor so parents can watch: Paul de Ruiter project page.
- Grandstand steps to the water; corrugated aluminium with timber ribs; circus ring with 7 m clear height combined with a theatre hall; black outer wall for chalk messages; opened 9 October 2009; AAP nomination 2010: Arcam. PdR says steel sheets, Arcam aluminium: the text says "corrugated metal".
- 2026: city builds a new, larger Bijlmer Parktheater on the Anton de Komplein (€25 million), current building to get another cultural function; first theatre hall in Zuidoost: Architectenweb id=60449. Hall of 160 seats, current building stays as space for makers and presentations, €5 million for renovation in 2024, location "in het Nelson Mandelapark": Nieuw Amsterdams Peil, 4 Feb 2026.
- Current operator: the theatre's own site (read in BrowserOS, curl got a redirect) describes it as a city theatre and one of four Amsterdam Cultuurhuizen. I did not confirm whether Circus Elleboog is still a tenant, so the text names the four original users only as the brief.
- Photo: "2020 Bijlmer Parktheater.jpg" (CC BY-SA 4.0).

## Hilton Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (zuidoost-weesp-9)
- Mecanoo; developed and owned by Schiphol Real Estate; opened December 2015; 433 rooms; 42 m glazed atrium roof; covered walkway from the terminals; European Hotel Design Award 2016 (Newbuild), jury quote: Mecanoo news.
- Cube rotated 45 degrees on a plinth, rounded corners, visible from the motorway; three kinds of prefab composite panel (straight, curved, arched); atrium preconditions outside air; white balustrades, timber: Mecanoo project page.
- Address and year: BAG via PDOK (Schiphol Boulevard 701, bouwjaar 2015, logiesfunctie, in use). Municipality Haarlemmermeer.

## TNT Centre, Hoofddorp (zuidoost-weesp-10)
- Paul de Ruiter; U-shape round an atrium; LEED Platinum; 2011; six floors, 600 workstations; wooden central stair, hidden lifts; north-south orientation; sunshades; Green Machine bio-CHP on recycled organic waste, surplus heat to neighbours; client OVG / Triodos-OVG Green Offices with TNT Express: Paul de Ruiter project page.
- First of a planned series of TNT Green Offices; four targets (LEED Platinum, GreenCalc+ >1000, Groenverklaring, CO2-emission-free); handed over 9 February 2011 instead of end 2010; LEED Platinum NC v2.2, GreenCalc+ label A 1005 points, energy label A+++; WKO, Bio-WKK on biological residual waste, green roof with solar panels: RVO NESK final report (government agency PDF).
- **Scout correction:** "billed as the first CO2-neutral office in the Netherlands" is not stated in anything I read. The RVO brochure pages inside the report say it would be the first Dutch office with a GreenCalc+ score of at least 1000 (a forward-looking claim); I used neither "first" claim.
- **Current occupant, weak sourcing:** BAG lists Taurusavenue 111 (bouwjaar 2010, kantoorfunctie, in use). tnt.com now redirects and its footer names FedEx as owner. 2026 P2000 fire-alarm logs call it "FedEx Head Office Taurusavenue"; business directories list "FedEx International Center". The text says only that TNT Express is now part of FedEx, which works from the building. The FedEx takeover itself I did not read in an authoritative source this session.
- Recognition field left empty: LEED and GreenCalc+ are certifications, not prizes; they are in the text.
- Detail.de "Mission Zero" article is gone (page removed). No Commons photo found. Municipality Haarlemmermeer.
