## Decoratelier Nationale Opera & Ballet (zuidoost-weesp-11)
- VMX Architects, project architect Don Murphy, client De Nederlandse Opera, contractors ABN-Belbouw and Hegeman, 15,000 m² GFA, 2012/2013; renovation and extension; fly tower about 20 m, functionally an exact copy of the Muziektheater's, so sets can be fully built and tested; whole north facade glazed like a showroom; red colour and tower make it a landmark: Gebouwd in Amsterdam 1006.
- Stands on the still-visible base of the original atelier of 1962; more workshops, storage, truck dock; inside insulation; north light and views; showrooms around; red outside; realised 2013; AAP nominated 2014; not public; Kollenbergweg 1: Arcam.
- In use since 1985, the only NOB department outside the centre, 40 staff: NOB's own page (read in BrowserOS; curl got nothing). So the company moved into an existing 1962 building in 1985; who built it in 1962 is not known to me, and the text does not say.
- **Scout correction:** this is a renovation and extension, not a new building (work = Remodel or extension, completed "2013 (remodel)"). The scout's hook (fly tower at the Muziektheater's size) is right.
- VMX's own site lists no project page for it that curl or the site index shows.
- Pin = PDOK BAG for Kollenbergweg 1. Photo: Commons "Decoratelier nationale opera en ballet.jpg" (2016, red facade with the name), checked visually.

## Rijkswaterstaat office by the A2 (zuidoost-weesp-12)
- Arcam: offices of the managers of the road network around Amsterdam; on the A2 for a striking presentation to motorway users; triangular plan with the sharpest point through the dike; glass screen folded round a ceramic inner facade, 90 cm naturally ventilated cavity for energy and noise; glass roof over the central hall; client Rijkswaterstaat directie Noord-Holland; 2001; not public; Burgemeester Stramanweg 100B.
- Designers Zdenek Zavrel and Marcel Baake, new build, contractor BAM, 2001: akar.nl reference list (supplier). Atelier Z = "Atelier Z. Zavrel Arch. B.V." with Zavrel and Baake as project architects also appears in Gebouwd in Amsterdam (project 2369, an unrelated housing project), which confirms the office name.
- Still in use: rijkswaterstaat.nl lists Burgemeester Stramanweg 100B as the West-Nederland Noord Amsterdam location, responsible for the national roads Schiphol, Amsterdam, 't Gooi.
- Not in Gebouwd in Amsterdam (no Zuidoost entries for 2001). Only Arcam describes the design; akar confirms architects and year. I left out Zavrel's biography (Czech exile, later dean in Prague) because I only saw search snippets.
- Pin = PDOK BAG for Burgemeester Stramanweg 100B (52.306932, 4.930745). I did not verify on imagery that this point sits on the triangle's tip. No photo found on Commons.

## Wi Eegi Kerki (zuidoost-weesp-13)
- **Scout corrections:** opened 2013, not 2012 (owner's PDF: "in 2013 in gebruik genomen"; Gebouwd in Amsterdam 2013/2014; Arcam says 2012). The address is Krommehoekstraat 136, 1104 KV (owner's PDF); Arcam's "Luthuliplein 11" is wrong (that address geocodes 500 m away in another part of the Bijlmer) and Gebouwd in Amsterdam gives Kortvoort 73. OSM's building "Wi Eegi Kerki" sits on Krommehoekstraat 136; pin = OSM way 171037880 centroid. The scout's "paid for by collections": Arcam says the members raised the budget themselves; I wrote that.
- Arcam (text Fieke de Groot): church reuse context; EBG approached 70F in 2006; members raised the budget, unusually modest for the building; white modern church with light tower; light and white central in the liturgy; ceiling rises to 9 m; 13 m white rear wall lit through a full-width light slot; tower a beacon; 550 white chairs; monolithic; mottled yellow-white brick in wild bond; raised beds with stone bands as seating; lit glass above the entrance as welcome; name = Surinamese for "our own church"; owner and user is the EBG; AAP nominated 2013.
- Gebouwd in Amsterdam 1108: 70F, Bas ten Brinke and Carina Nilsson, Reimert Bouw, 1,350 m²; entrance and kitchen at the centre; walk under the balcony; 9 m hall; light slot in the 13 m tower; you have to turn round to see the light's source; built on a terp (mound).
- BauNetz Wissen: visible bell in the tower; metal letters "wi eegi kerki" left of the entrance; chairs for 550, all white.
- EBG site: Sunday services at 11:00 (livestreamed); owner PDF: Protestant congregation "met Surinaamse signatuur", halls for hire, main hall 300 to 500 seats. The chair count disagrees (550 vs max 500), so the text says only that the chairs are white.
- Access "At set times" (Sunday service). Photo: Commons "Wi Eegi church.jpg" (undated, own work), checked visually.

## De Kameleon (zuidoost-weesp-14)
- **Scout correction:** not "homes stacked above and around shops on one side": shops on the ground floor, two parking decks, then 164 flats round a raised garden plus a 10-storey slab of 58 flats (222 total).
- Arcam: one of the last run-down parts of the Bijlmer; mixed functions vs. the separated original Bijlmer; shops on a street; parking on two levels above; 222 homes; tower added to make the centre visible, seen from the metro; big window in the head; crumpled facades over 150 m long; garden at level three with a lightning-bolt water feature; clients De Principaal, De Key; 2012; AAP nominated 2013.
- Gebouwd in Amsterdam 898: superblock by metro Kraaiennest; one public parking layer, one residents' layer; four housing floors; garden at exactly the height of the elevated metro; ten-storey tower as a signboard; four-storey cut-out opens the court to the station, residents can wave, the departing metro is heard deep in the block; 222 homes; Kruitberghof and Karspeldreef 1169-1383.
- EUmies Awards page (prize year EUmies Awards 2013, nominee; the site carries spam ads but the record is EUmies' own): 164 apartments in four storeys, 10-storey slab with 58 apartments as a billboard to the subway, Karspeldreef side continuous against noise, other side punctured, 8 m zigzag bays.
- Shops still trade (it is the Kraaiennest shopping centre); I did not check individual tenants, so the text says only "shops". No Commons photo I could confirm shows this building.
- Pin = PDOK for Karspeldreef 1169 (west end of the long block). The building runs about 150 m east to the metro; the coordinator may prefer a point nearer the tower.

## Wooncomplex F-Side (zuidoost-weesp-15)
- Arcam: Bijlmer renewal, gallery flats and garages partly demolished for mostly low-rise; "Bijlmer nieuwe stijl"; social rent, two storeys, flat roofs; inner area with caretaker's room, bike store, gardens; glasshouse in each garden with frosted back as storage; aluminium front facades with a drawing after a sketch by Ilona Lénárd, folded and incised, continued on the roof for neighbouring flats; no front gardens; bike stands with number, bell, letterbox and light; client Delta Forte/Rochdale; 2007; AAP nominated 2008; Claus van Amsbergstraat 71.
- Gebouwd in Amsterdam 176: ONL [Oosterhuis_Lénárd], project architect Gijs Joosen, BAM, 53 social-rent homes, two closed blocks, all facades equal; corner houses with large windows; each panel deformed individually with Centraal Staal's shipbuilding technique; aluminium untreated; street list.
- oosterhuis.nl (housing projects): 6 mm cold-formed aluminium plates, each unique, made by a shipbuilder with a CNC procedure; Lénárd's sketch parametrised to drive manufacture; wraps the whole exterior including the roof. Hyperbody "who" page and oosterhuis.nl header: professor at TU Delft from 2000 (2000-2016), leading Hyperbody, the knowledge centre for non-standard and interactive architecture. I did not call him a "pioneer": no authoritative source in hand says so.
- Still standing: not separately confirmed by a recent source beyond the register; nothing suggests demolition. Pin = PDOK Claus van Amsbergstraat 71. No Commons photo found.

## Daklozenopvang Anton de Komplein (zuidoost-weesp-17)
- **Scout correction:** for drug users (HVO-Querido), not homeless people in general; the line about the office being "later nominated (AAP 2026) for De Vrijheid" is not verified and left out (Gebouwd in Amsterdam confirms Kempe Thill designed De Vrijheid, 2024/2025, but I found no AAP 2026 source).
- Arcam (text Fieke de Groot): flats for drug users, HVO-Querido; commission 2007 between the square and the Nelson Mandelapark; must fit in, no nuisance; four stacked rings; atrium with lowered floor; 25 m² per person; skylight; roof courtyard on the third floor; ten-year lease, unclear after 2022, hence a neutral frame; client Rochdale; 2012; not public; Anton de Komplein 232.
- Gebouwd in Amsterdam 936 ("Junkie Hotel"): Kempe, Thill, David van Eck; 2,128 m²; 2011/2012; overpressure stair.
- Architect's text (arqa.com and EUmies page): such shelters usually in containers on industrial or railway land; ensemble with the police station; 25 x 25 m plot; ring plan; 9 m cube common room over three floors; fixed glazing of 2.6, 4 and 6 m in slim black-anodised profiles; green glass mosaic balustrades matching the police station. It gives 45 m² per person against Arcam's and the register's 25; I left the figure out.
- Kempe Thill project page and EUmies record: nominated EU Mies van der Rohe Award 2013.
- Current use (2026): HVO-Querido location page: since 2012 a shelter for 46 people, 9 short-stay rooms and 36 long-stay studios, hard-drug use allowed inside. HVO blog (ten-year anniversary): replaced a nuisance night shelter with a user room and the Elsrijkdreef shelter.
- Title uses the architect's name for it ("Drug Addicts Hotel"; the register says "Junkie Hotel", used in the Dutch title). Category housing; the coordinator may prefer civic.
- Pin = PDOK Anton de Komplein 232. No Commons photo found.

## Bijlmer Sportcentrum (zuidoost-weesp-18)
- Arcam: 2008; part of the renewal of the Bijlmer centre and F-buurt; social function; on the market square linking Amsterdamse Poort, Nelson Mandelapark and Bijlmer-Oost; closed on two sides for planned housing (built from 2022); polygonal plan, zigzag glass facade; "zien en gezien worden" inspired by non-Western cultures of strolling; views from the hall to sports hall, pools, café; pools and gym visible from outside; slats close for privacy or sun; "feest" theme with green walls, blue pool mosaic, gold-orange seating; 80 cm above the square with a gentle slope; 8 m concrete columns with big timber beams; client Stadsdeel Zuidoost; AAP "Genomineerd, 2009".
- Gebouwd in Amsterdam 545: VYA with Bothof, Pronk, Verryn Stuart, Beerends, Yanovshtchinsky, Lubbers; clients Ontwikkelingsbedrijf Gemeente Amsterdam and Stadsdeel Zuidoost; 8,500 m²; 2008/2009; Van den Belt.
- VYA project page: shortlisted in the top six nominees for the "Gouden A.A.P. 2008", with a jury quote. **Year conflict:** VYA says 2008, Arcam 2009 (the scout also said 2009). Recognition is written without a year; please settle if the AAP archive is to hand.
- Operator: Optisport (pool, health club, sports hall; read in BrowserOS). New homes around it under construction (VPRC, an adviser on the project: "Smile" and "View" in aanbouw, 440 homes). Kept vague in the text ("those homes are now under construction"), supported by Arcam's plan statement.
- Pin = OSM/BAG node for Anton de Komplein 157. No Commons photo of the building found.
- The scout's "Bright" tag (AAP 2009 nominee "Bright") is not explained by any source I read; not used.
