## Xplore Agora (noord-19)
- Agora concept (challenges, self-chosen subjects, groups of 12 to 17 of mixed age and level); 35 classrooms, six maker spaces, grand café; client Stichting ZAAM; opened August 2025; corner of Elzenhagen; café open to parents and neighbours; movable partitions; dark-red entrance, large light aula, two wings of open classrooms; façade of concrete with red slanted bricks in a rhythmic pattern; AAP nominated 2026; Elzenhagenpad 2; not publicly accessible: Arcam guide.
- Won the architect selection summer 2021 with Studio Ard Hoksbergen; 600 pupils; "first building in the Netherlands designed specifically for this concept" (attributed to the architects in the text): VDNDP project page. Hoksbergen's page also says "eerste gebouw ... specifiek ontworpen voor het Agora onderwijsconcept".
- Half a year of weekly design sessions with pupils, coaches and craftspeople; nest boxes in the façade, façade and quay planting: Studio Ard Hoksbergen.
- Low hall into a high aula with views of water and athletics track; building and concept fused; could later serve as a regular secondary school; aula open to parents and neighbours by day: AAP 2026 vakjury report. Nomination list: Arcam "Tien nominaties ... 2026". Did not win (UvA library and De Nieuwe Meent won).
- Access "Outside only" because Arcam lists it as not publicly accessible, although the café is meant for parents and neighbours.
- Pin: Nominatim, Elzenhagenpad 2 is tagged "Xplore agora".
- No Commons photo found.

## Klein Amsterdam school (noord-24)
- **Status checked:** still standing and in use. The school's own site (kleinamsterdam.school) says it is in "een prachtig houten gebouw op de Tuinderijlaan 2, een tijdelijke locatie in de Bongerd". DUO data via allecijfers.nl: 145 pupils in 2025-2026. The TU Delft Circular Design Atlas says the school "had to be permanently closed" in October 2023; this is contradicted by the DUO figures and the school's site (the school was in serious trouble, with a minister's intervention in 2024, per rijksoverheid.nl search summaries), so I did not use it.
- **Outlook:** NH Nieuws, 17 November 2026: the city will refurbish the school building on the Schoenerstraat so Klein Amsterdam can move in. The text says so and that the fate of the modules is unknown. The coordinator may want `outlook: Leaving or closing` on it; I did not set it because the brief limits attributes.
- **Scout correction:** Arcam said in its text that the school was expected to move to a permanent site at the NDSM yard around 2024; that did not happen, and the NDSM plan is not mentioned in any current source. Left out.
- SeARCH commissioned in 2017 by Stichting Leren in de Tussenruimte; fully timber, can be extended, dismantled, moved; delivered 2019; prefab modules stacked into a layout; modules stand without foundations; atrium and plug-ins; Hannah Arendt's "tussenruimte"; growth and shrinkage: Arcam. Module size 8.7 x 3.3 m fits a standard truck; finish per module (classroom, office, sanitary): SeARCH and Arcam. Winner of the municipal school competition: the school's own site.
- Module counts disagree (Arcam: 24 modules at delivery; SeARCH: 34 modules, 1,250 m², initially). Left out. Completion: Arcam 2019, Circular Design Atlas 2020, a search summary says SeARCH lists 2018; DUO shows 61 pupils already in 2018-2019 (possibly elsewhere or in a first phase). Used 2019 (Arcam).
- Recognition: Arcam guide label "Genomineerd, 2020" (AAP). Not double-checked against a 2020 nominee list.
- Address: Arcam gives Tuinderijlaan 1; the school and DUO give Tuinderijlaan 2. Used 2; pin = Nominatim node "Klein Amsterdam" at Tuinderijlaan 2.
- Title: the scout's "Klein Amsterdam school"; the NL title is "Basisschool Klein Amsterdam".
- No Commons photo found.

## Theater Amsterdam (noord-25)
- Dedato, client Imagine Nation, 2014, Danzigerkade 5, Westpoort/Houthaven; built for ANNE; designers chose a restrained building because the production had "zeggingskracht genoeg"; publicly accessible: Arcam.
- Dedato's own Danzigerkade 4 page calls the theatre "het naastgelegen zwarte theater" and says the gold-anodised cladding of no. 4 was chosen as a contrast. Dedato has no theatre project page that I could find (checked its portfolio pages and WP search).
- Opened May 2014 with ANNE; 1,100-seat auditorium with sets revolving around it; curved screens; built "in a record time of 9 months" (attributed to the producers in the text): Imagine Nation. Built and financed privately by Kees Abrahams and Robin de Levita; play by Leon de Winter and Jessica Durlacher; turntable decor as for Soldaat van Oranje: NL Times (Oct 2013). Premiere 8 May 2014: nl Wikipedia and the Commons description; text says only "May 2014".
- Anne Frank House criticism, Ronald Leopold quote "Anne Frank should not be a nice evening out": JTA, 18 March 2014 (the quote is marked as a quote in EN; paraphrased in NL).
- **Scout correction / status:** ANNE no longer runs (no current listing; heren2.nl search summary says the theatre became an event centre after the show stopped). Today: comedy, cabaret, live podcasts, concerts on a stand of 926 seats expandable to 1,100, plus events and congresses: theateramsterdam.nl home and "theatershow" page. I found no source for when ANNE closed, so the text gives no date. The scout's "stage the size of a hangar" is not sourced; left out. "Glass box" is from the Commons photos (2014, 2015) and the overall look, not a sourced phrase.
- Left out: Arcam's note on the planned IJ bridge landing here (only a plan); SceneAround name (Wikipedia only).
- Access "At set times" (shows and events).
- Pin: Nominatim, Danzigerkade 5 is tagged Theater Amsterdam.
- Photos: Commons "Theater Amsterdam, Danzigerkade.jpg" (2015) and "Theater Amsterdam exterior with 'ANNE' poster.jpg" (2014).

## De Ark (noord-34)
- Former church hall turned into a meeting and shopping place, the Sociale Markt; food bank, chemist, clothes shop in a wooden tribune; no Sunday services since 2022; Protestant Church Amsterdam and the Diaconie with Open Kaart; free supermarket with guidance; workshops, haircuts, coffee; film nights, funerals, concerts; stone floor, green-painted wooden ceiling, brick walls; clear-lacquered wood; doors recognisable only by the keyhole; homes on the roof being studied; realised 2024; AAP nominated 2025; not publicly accessible; Aakstraat 60: Arcam.
- Three opening days a week; "Van buitenaf ziet het gebouw er nog gesloten uit" (Dirk van den Hoven, director of the Protestantse Diaconie Amsterdam); VKB-MercerMarsh Award 2025: Kerkbeheer 4/2025 interview (PDF).
- AAP 2025 vakjury: wood grain running across the panels, the fitted changing cubicles: jury report. Nominee list: Arcam 2025 nominations page. Did not win.
- VKB-MercerMarsh Award 2025 also on gereformeerdekerken.info, which adds that the market opened in 2023 after the interior was rebuilt and names the architect as Pieter Graaff of OpenKaart. Arcam gives realisation 2024. Since the hobby site is the only source for 2023 and for Graaff, the text gives no opening year and names only Open Kaart; `completed` is "2024 (conversion)" after Arcam.
- Original church: gereformeerdekerken.info says De Ark dates from 1967 and was a Reformed (gereformeerde) church. Single hobby-site source, so left out of the text; `original_use` is just "Protestant church".
- Category `religious` (still a church building that hosts services and funerals); the coordinator may prefer `civic`.
- Access "At set times": the market is open three days a week for its visitors, and the hall hosts events; Arcam lists it as not publicly accessible.
- Open Kaart's own website could not be found (openkaart.nl is a publisher).
- Pin: Nominatim, Aakstraat 60 tagged De Ark.
- Photos: Commons "Amsterdam-Noord - De Ark kerk.JPG" and "Amsterdam-Noord - Banne Zuid de Ark.JPG" (both April 2012, exterior before the conversion; the conversion was internal).

## CPO Srefi Bow (noord-38)
- Centrumeiland, Amsterdam Oost; CPO; 8 homes (2 maisonettes, 5 apartments, 1 penthouse); rainproof, circular, gas-free; load-bearing timber-frame (HSB) façades; circular façade cladding of dry-stacked bricks; variety of colour and texture; dark frames; metal grid for climbers on the balconies; rear balconies of different depths; column-beam structure; square plans with a continuous front balcony; casco-plus delivery; client Bouwgroep CPO Srefi Bow; realised 2024; AAP nominated 2025; Srefidensiplein 70E: Arcam.
- Almost entirely timber except basement and lift shaft; HSB façades chosen over CLT walls to optimise material; column-beam structure; CLT floors, beams and columns left visible; balcony across the whole front, CLT, unsupported at the building line; step in the façade at first floor carried by a timber beam (not used); collaboration with JoustraReid as Maathuis Projecten: lab03.
- Shell delivered November 2023, residents finishing their own homes; rainproof, circular, gas-free: JoustraReid.
- Residents decided on layout, materials and appearance from the first sketches; rear clad in timber with two profile sizes: AAP 2025 vakjury report. Public jury less taken with it.
- Completed: Arcam 2024 (shell November 2023). Used "2024".
- Pin: Nominatim, Srefidensiplein 70B (70A to 70G all within a metre).
- No Commons photo found.
