## EDGE West (west-nieuw-west-13)
- 1970s office for Stichting Sociaal Fonds Bouwnijverheid; open-plan office garden for equality, new in NL; original architect A.N. Oyevaar of Oyevaar, Stolle en Van Gool; de Architekten Cie. is the successor firm; 7 floors of about 7,000 m², 8 clovers round a green heart; octagon as base: Arcam, Archello (architect's text).
- First pile 5 November 1970 by minister Roolvink: Nationaal Archief / Anefo photo on Commons (CC0). BAG gives build year 1974.
- Remodel 2022: new façade form and materials, glass roof over the core, atrium by DS Landschapsarchitecten, four staircases replacing lift shafts, first-floor terraces, 6,000 m² solar, WKO, gas-free, energy-positive, BREEAM Outstanding, WELL Platinum: Arcam. EDGE's own page confirms most of these.
- Dome span 76 m: EDGE only, so attributed to EDGE. EDGE's "second-largest glass dome in Europe" left out (owner claim). SIDstudio as co-designer of the dome came only from a search summary of an engineering case study: left out.
- Tenants incl. Alliander: EDGE page; OSM/Nominatim also tag Basisweg 10 as Liander/Alliander.
- Recognition left empty: EDGE says it was nominated for Architectenweb Awards 2022 (Office Building of the Year) and the Herengracht Industrie Prijs 2024, but I found no independent confirmation.
- Access Outside only (Arcam: not publicly accessible). Arcam files it under Westpoort; it is in the Amsterdam municipality.
- Photos: three 2024 Commons photos titled "2024 Basisweg 10, Asd-Edge-West (A/B/C)".

## Opportuna (west-nieuw-west-14)
- 20 storeys, 174 social-rent homes of 32 to 45 m² for 23 to 28 year olds, priority for young people from Nieuw-West, wide and shallow plans, prefab concrete façade elements over two or four storeys, load-bearing façade for future flexibility, lounge and study space, roof terrace, community builders, name from the former youth centre: Arcam, Lieven de Key, AAP 2026 jury reports.
- About 65 m, open since February 2025, contracts of at most five years, youth centre opened September 1998 and run by local young people, roof terrace on the 19th floor: Lieven de Key (which credits only Office Winhov; Arcam, ArchDaily and both jury reports credit Winhov with Office Haratori).
- Jury points (polished balcony undersides, small windows from the load-bearing façade, benches toward the future park on the railway side; public jury split over temporary contracts): AAP 2026 professional and public jury reports. Nominated 2026; winners were UB UvA (jury) and De Nieuwe Meent (public).
- **Address correction:** Arcam gives August Allebéplein 8, but BAG puts a 1961 building there. The 2025 building with 175 units (174 homes plus one) is on the Postjesweg beside the square, matching Lieven de Key ("bij het August Allebéplein, dichtbij metrostation Postjesweg") and the jury ("bij de Postjesweg"). Pin is that BAG footprint's centre (52.363618, 4.835078).
- Winhov page (winhov.nl/en/projects/august-allebeplein) returned 500 to curl and the browser; it is linked because the search index shows it is the project page, but I did not read it. A pre-selection for the Zuiderkerkprijs 2025 appears only in a search summary of Winhov's news: left out.
- No Commons photo found.

## Cornelis (west-nieuw-west-15)
- FARO with Van Bergen Architectura for BuildingforLife, Prosper Vitae and Heutink; 2025; 49 for sale and 36 free-sector rent (85); double-height plinth with horeca, studios, day nursery, after-school care; collective spaces, slides; hanging azobé galleries resting on the concrete frame without ground supports; oak, bamboo, parquet; Dijk&co landscape: Arcam.
- Winning tender plan "Tikkertje Trap" (FARO news, 19 July 2021); 60 to 170 m², south-facing outdoor spaces; 3 m height difference; wooden slides beside the stairs; Van Eesteren and post-war gallery blocks as inspiration; grey-green brick developed for the building; stair garden by Dijk & Co; glass artworks by Marjet Wessels Boer: FARO project page and the BNA submission PDF.
- FARO dates Van Eesteren's AUP to 1943; it is usually dated 1934/35, so I left the year out.
- AAP 2026: public jury had it in all three jurors' top three and dropped it because it has no social housing; quote "echt een mensenarchitect"; door windows at a child's eye height; professional jury found the link to the square behind cold and closed: AAP 2026 jury reports.
- The BNA PDF is a submission for BNA Beste Gebouw van het Jaar 2026; I found no nomination, so it is not under recognition.
- Café and nursery are in the plinth as built (Arcam, FARO); current tenants not checked, so access is Outside only.
- Photos: three 2025 Commons files titled "2025 Cornelis, Schielanplein/Lelylaan, Asd". BAG: Schielandplein 3, build year 2025.

## MO*town Track 8 (west-nieuw-west-16)
- De Zwarte Hond for BPD and ERA Contour; 2024; part of Haven-Stad; co-commissioning by future residents; valley with shared gardens; steel footbridges between three volumes; dark brick; PV in glass balustrades; bikes and 30 shared cars: Arcam and De Zwarte Hond.
- 260 m³ rain basin, glossy dark façade, "one of the first pioneers": De Zwarte Hond.
- **Unit count left out:** Arcam says 82 homes, De Zwarte Hond 94.
- Scout's "first real living block in the Sloterdijk office district" not used; the architect only says "one of the first".
- AAP 2025 professional jury ("wow-effect", planting a year before completion, inner side "unheimisch") and public jury (video game, shady lower parts): AAP 2025 reports. Nominated 2025; winners were the National Holocaust Museum (jury) and Station Wildeman (public).
- Pin: BAG Radarweg 309 (build year 2024). No Commons photo found.

## De Houten Leeuw (west-nieuw-west-17)
- Arcam: first social-sector apartment building in Amsterdam built entirely of timber (attributed to Arcam); 53 studios of about 35 m² for 18 to 28 year olds; CLT; central pod with kitchen and bathroom; rendered façade; playful rear stair; benches with storage boxes; Stadgenoot; 2024.
- Completed mid-November 2024 behind the Kolenkitkerk; Heren 5 as architect; De Nijs as delegated developer; Hamlet for the timber structure; about 760 m³ of Austrian timber; frame up in 9 weeks (project manager's quote); timber and render façade: Stedenbouw.
- Five residential floors; CLT stairwell; azobé gallery leaning against the building, made stable by a right-angled corner of 16 m CLT panels, so no inner stair or lift core: De Nijs project page.
- **Architect name:** De Nijs credits "Some Architecten". Never Too Small (interview with the partners) says SOME architects is the new name of heren 5. The text says "Heren 5 architecten (now called SOME architects)".
- AAP 2025: built within a year; façade gave the city's quality committee headaches, render a sensible compromise; mostly closed curtains; public jury liked the timber back and found the front closed: AAP 2025 jury reports.
- Pin: BAG De Leeuw van Vlaanderenstraat 41 (build year 2024). No usable Commons photo; the 2023 street photos predate completion.

## De Hood (west-nieuw-west-28)
- 15 x 8.5 m pavilion in a green strip; glazed ground floor as the answer to neighbours' fear of insecurity; drop-in room, kitchen, office, toilets; closed upper floor for the young people with a movable partition, insulated, rooflights; 5 m panes and glued glass doors used much of the budget; polyurethane spray façade as the cheapest finish in NL; André Kempe, Olivier (sic) Thill, David van Eck; client VOF De Stadstuinen (Ymere, Bouwfonds); 2011; AAP nominated 2012; publicly accessible: Arcam.
- Invited competition, first prize; "public living room"; tree canopy part of the interior; client Ymere: Atelier Kempe Thill. Sand-lime brick under the spray: search summaries of Designboom and Baunetz, consistent with the architect's text, not read in full.
- **Address correction:** Arcam gives Reimerswaalstraat 5A, but BAG puts a 436 m² health-care unit there. BAG Reimerswaalstraat 101 is a 200 m² meeting-function building from 2011 in a 15 x 8.5 m footprint, the address the architect listings give. Pin is that footprint.
- **Still a youth centre:** Rodi (4 March 2026) lists an activity at "Jongerencentrum de Hood aan de Osdorperban 132"; ONE Jongerenwerk's official Instagram lists De Hood among its locations for 10 to 23 year olds (seen as a search snippet, not opened). The municipal activity calendar (activiteiten.amsterdam.nl) also lists it with ONE Jongerenwerk but returned 403. BAG has Osdorper Ban 132 as a house next to it, so the mail address and the building differ; I kept 101 for the pin.
- Access set to Outside only (it is a centre for local young people), though Arcam says publicly accessible.
- Opening date 5 April 2011 is in search summaries only; text says 2011.
- No Commons photo found.

## Zaans Medisch Centrum (west-nieuw-west-31)
- First lean hospital in NL (Mecanoo's claim, attributed); first Mecanoo hospital, architecture, urban plan, landscape and interior; developed by ZMC and Vitaal ZorgVast, built by BAM; SILO illustrations; stacking of 2 clinic floors, 2 ward floors, flexible layer between; standardised departments; multi-storey lobby with semicircular voids, rooflights, wood, colours; Zaan industrial heritage illustrations; spiral slide; health boulevard alongside; Frame Awards 2018 People's Vote, Healthcare Centre of the Year: Mecanoo project page and 2017 news; ArchDaily (architects' text).
- Move on Saturday 28 January 2017, 130 patients in beds through a heated tunnel, new building beside the old: NH Nieuws (dated 28 January 2017).
- **Scout correction:** "designed around daylight and a garden": no source for a garden; the text sticks to daylight and the lobby. Interiors "with SILO": SILO did illustrations and graphics, not the interior (Mecanoo designed the interior).
- Completion 2016 (Mecanoo news: construction completed September 2016, per search summary; the project page gives design 2012 to 2014, realisation 2014 to 2016 in search summaries). In use from January 2017.
- Zaanstad: `municipality: Zaanstad`. Pin: Nominatim "Zaans Medisch Centrum, Koningin Julianaplein 58".
- No usable Commons photo found (searches gave only logos).

## Pondok (west-nieuw-west-36)
- CPO, residents jointly client; delivered 2018 in the Delflandpleinbuurt (Slotervaart); site chosen by the city between Zuid and the Westlandgracht to close the inside/outside-the-ring gap; Tom Jonker's vision for a self-build group from the squatting scene; nearly ten years; plinth for neighbourhood uses via crowdfunding; dance school and work-experience business as tenants; solar, collective heat network, shower-water reuse, vegetable gardens, orchard; four floors, upper three live-work of 180 or 90 m², each resident designed their own unit; rear galleries; Stichting Pondok aan de Westlandgracht; AAP nominated 2019: Arcam.
- **Scout correction:** the scout's "housing with care for elderly Indo-Dutch residents, built around a shared pendopo" is not supported by any source I found; Arcam describes a squatter-scene self-build CPO. Text follows Arcam.
- **Thin sourcing:** Arcam is the only source I could read. CRUQ's site did not respond; no press found. BAG confirms Poeldijkstraat 391 as a 2018 building with shop and meeting functions; OSM tags it Beatzone (a dance school, consistent with Arcam's tenant). The coordinator may want to hold this one until a second source turns up.
- Weekly meditation and an annual Pondok Festival (Arcam) left out: not checked as current.
- No Commons photo found.
