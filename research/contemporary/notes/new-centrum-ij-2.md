## Het Duivenhuis, Artis (centrum-ij-33)
- Sources: Arcam (ATELIERFRONT, veranda half roof half balcony bringing back balusters, stairs,
  carved bargeboards; south front restored, symmetry; insulated, off gas; poffertjes, meeting
  room; AAP 2026 nominated); Artis news (team incl. Cor Bouwstra, Hank van Tilborg; uses
  over time, first manatee 1928-1943; green roof); RCE register 518287 and RCE advice
  1412970 (earlier plan too dominant with two stairs on the east side, cut to one on the west).
- Dating conflict: Arcam/Artis "about 1730" (garden pavilion of Costerus); register "largely
  before 1850", part of the Plantage pleasure gardens, added to Artis as pigeon house 1863;
  Arcam says converted to pigeon house 1872-1875. Text says older than the zoo, "Artis dates it
  to about 1730", and "later in the nineteenth century" for the pigeon use.
- Artis headline calls something the "smallest national monument" but it is unclear which of the
  two buildings: left out.
- Access At set times (zoo ticket). Coordinates from OSM (building "Duivenhuis").

## Spider monkey house, Artis (centrum-ij-34)
- Scout line was wrong: it is the black spider monkey house (slingerapen), not a gibbon
  enclosure. Artis has a separate gibbon pavilion (by ATELIERFRONT per Archello, not checked
  further). Title corrected.
- Sources: Arcam (June 2019, Enzo Valerio, timber, stilts in 't Veentje, glass, willow-mat
  island with dredged mud, first fossil-free heated enclosure at Artis, heat/cold storage in the
  timber structure, monumental bank line visible again, AAP 2020 nominated); Valerio's CV
  (abebonnemaprijs.nl: "Nomination Gouden A.A.P (Spidermonkey house)", confirms attribution);
  Artis species page (monkeys live at 't Veentje, 1911, modelled on the Naardermeer) and ponds
  page (2019 pavilion; island "ended 't Veentje as symbol of Dutch nature"; bridges restored 2019).
- "First fossil-free" is attributed to Arcam in the text. Access At set times (zoo ticket).
  Coordinates from OSM ("Zwarte slingeraap" attraction point).

## Centraal Station metro station (centrum-ij-45)
- Sources: Arcam ("Kathedraal", A-shaped columns hiding services, greys, 2.5 x 23.5 m slot meant
  for a media screen now Claerbout's work, A.M. Schreuders prize, commission 1996, opened July
  2018); Benthem Crouwel project page (22 years, seven stations, shortest route, daylight,
  selection committee 1995) and BNA news (BNA Best Building 2019, plaque at the Damrak entrance);

  unanimous expert jury); De Ingenieur March 2010 (1889 station given new foundation; 136 m
  tunnel to be floated in from the IJ); Tunnels & Tunnelling 15 July 2011 (floated in and lowered
  on 8 July).
- Scout said "AAP 2019 (Noord/Zuidlijn)": confirmed; recognition says it was for the line.
  A.M. Schreuders prize year not found.
- Access Public parts only (hall public, platforms with a ticket).
