# A-list scout: the famous sights, city-wide (2026-11-24)

Shortlist only. Nothing written to the live site, nothing committed. Status per item:

- **(a) new**: not on the map (some are already parked in `research/zuid/A-LIST.md`, `research/centrum/A-LIST.md` or `FOLLOW-UPS.md`; that is noted).
- **(b) on the map**: exact live title and rank; with a call on relabelling it A-list (rank dropped).
- **(c) covered**: not a place of its own, but inside a live place (named).

Evidence keys (details and links under "Method"):

- **V**: visitor numbers. V24 = national day-attraction top 50, 2024; V-wiki = nl Wikipedia "Lijst van best bezochte musea in Nederland" (mostly 2017 to 2019 figures).
- **M**: Michelin Green Guide stars as shown on ViaMichelin (2026 edition): M3 worth the trip, M2 worth a detour, M1 interesting.
- **W**: en Wikipedia "List of tourist attractions in Amsterdam" (a broad list, so weak on its own).
- **LP**: Lonely Planet "17 best things to do in Amsterdam" (LP17) or "12 best museums in Amsterdam" (LP-mus).
- **RG**: Rough Guides Amsterdam page ("three world-famous sights: the Anne Frank Huis, the Van Gogh Museum and the Rijksmuseum", plus the Royal Palace, the Resistance Museum and the canals by name).
- **WV**: Wikivoyage Amsterdam, "See" section.

Hooks are leads only, each from the source linked. Several of them point at things that are already a place or a draft; that is said where it applies.

## Centrum

1. **Canal ring (grachtengordel) as a whole**: (c), a concept, not a place; covered by many canal places (e.g. "The Golden Bend's double houses: the mayor's residence and a gable that stayed", "Herengracht and Singel offices", "Reguliersgracht and the Amstelveld, seven bridges and a wooden church"). Evidence: UNESCO 2010; M3 ("The Canals belt", "Herengracht", "The Southern Canals"), M2 Keizersgracht, Prinsengracht, Singel; W ("the city's most famous sight"); RG; LP17 #1 (canal cruise). Hook: the World Heritage site is formally the canal ring "binnen de Singelgracht", so the outer moat and its edge are inside it, not only the three main canals. [nl.wikipedia Grachtengordel](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grachtengordel_(Amsterdam))
2. **Dam square**: (c) in "The Dam's corners: the shooting of 7 May 1945 and the buildings round it" (Okay). Evidence: M1 (Dam); W (squares); WV. Hook: the square is the dam itself, the 13th-century barrier in the Amstel between Damrak and Rokin that gave the city its name. [nl.wikipedia Dam](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dam_(Amsterdam))
3. **Royal Palace (Paleis op de Dam)**: (a). Evidence: V24 312,236; M1; W; RG. Hook: the former town hall stands on 13,659 wooden piles. (Its six lanterns are a Deep Centrum draft and get a mention in "The Dam's corners".) [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Amsterdam)
4. **National Monument on the Dam**: (a). Evidence: W (Dam entry); M1 (Dam); every Dam description. Hook: the curved wall behind the pylon has 14 niches for urns: 11 for the provinces of 1956 and 3 for the Dutch East Indies and the other colonies, filled with soil from execution grounds and war cemeteries. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationaal_Monument_op_de_Dam)
5. **Nieuwe Kerk**: (a). Evidence: M2; W; WV. Hook: Admiral Michiel de Ruyter lies under his monument here, and the Catholic poet Vondel is buried in this Protestant church. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nieuwe_Kerk,_Amsterdam)
6. **Anne Frank House**: (a). Evidence: V24 1,229,080; M2; RG (one of "three world-famous sights"); LP17 #7; LP-mus. Hook: the museum exists because a foundation was set up to stop developers demolishing the block; it opened on 3 May 1960. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Frank_House)
7. **Westerkerk and Westertoren**: (a). Evidence: M1; W; RG (via its own Westerkerk page). Hook: Rembrandt was buried here in 1669 but nobody knows where; the memorial on the north aisle dates from 1906. The tower carries the crown of Maximilian I. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerkerk_(Amsterdam))
8. **Begijnhof**: (a). Evidence: M3; W; WV. Hook: Het Houten Huys (no. 34) is one of only two wooden houses left in the city; the other is Zeedijk 1, already in "The head of the Zeedijk: a wooden house, a sailors' chapel, Chet Baker and 't Mandje". [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Begijnhof,_Amsterdam)
9. **Oude Kerk**: (a). Evidence: M1; W; WV. Hook: every 9 March (8 March in leap years) at 8:39 the early sun lights Saskia van Uylenburgh's tomb for a moment. (Belle and the red window outside are already in "The red-light district's own monuments: Belle, the red window and the Majoor".) [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oude_Kerk,_Amsterdam)
10. **Ons' Lieve Heer op Solder (Our Lord in the Attic)**: (a). Evidence: M1; W; WV ("well worth the visit"). Hook: the pulpit is normally hidden inside the altar and is pulled out for the sermon. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_Ons%27_Lieve_Heer_op_Solder)
11. **Red-light district (De Wallen) as such**: (c), a concept; the district's own details are in "The red-light district's own monuments: Belle, the red window and the Majoor" (Nice). Evidence: M3 ("De Walletjes"); W. Hook: the trade runs from about 300 one-room cabins. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Wallen)
12. **Centraal Station (the building)**: (c) in "Arriving at Centraal Station" (Okay) and "Centraal Station's two plaques: the cold welcome and the sergeant" (Okay). Evidence: W; WV (Cuypers, 1889). Hook: of the two towers only the east one has a clock; the west one shows the wind direction. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Station_Amsterdam_Centraal)
13. **Rembrandthuis**: (a). Evidence: V-wiki 265,000 (2017); M2; LP-mus; W. Hook: the rooms were refurnished from the inventory drawn up at Rembrandt's bankruptcy in 1656. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rembrandt_House_Museum)
14. **Waterlooplein market**: (c) in "Waterlooplein's poor relief: the peat stores, the back gate and the Moses and Aaron church" (Okay) and "The Stopera, the NAP under it and the Violist" (Nice). Evidence: W (markets, squares); M1 (Jodenbuurt). Hook: under the Stopera next to the market, the zero of Dutch sea level as a bronze bolt (already in the Stopera place). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterlooplein)
15. **Jewish Museum (Joods Museum)**: (a). Evidence: V-wiki 370,000 for the Jewish Cultural Quarter (2017); M2; LP-mus; W. Hook: the entrance is the covered space between four synagogues, restored to their 18th-century state; a mikveh (ritual bath) turned up in a 2006 excavation. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joods_Museum_(Nederland))
16. **Portuguese Synagogue (Esnoga)**: (a). Evidence: M1; W (twice); WV. Hook: the main hall has no electric system; services are still lit by candles in the original brass chandeliers. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Synagogue_(Amsterdam))
17. **National Holocaust Museum and Hollandsche Schouwburg**: (c) in "The war in the Plantage, around the Hollandsche Schouwburg" (Amazing). That place is effectively built on this A-list pair; I'd keep it as it is (it is pitched on the crèche, the tram stop and the register) but the user may want to rule. Evidence: V-wiki (Jewish Cultural Quarter, 370,000); centrum/A-LIST. Hook: the museum is in the former teachers' college (Kweekschool) opposite the theatre. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationaal_Holocaustmuseum)
18. **Verzetsmuseum (Resistance Museum)**: (c) in the same "The war in the Plantage" place; the building is a Deep Centrum draft ("Plancius, the choir's clubhouse"). Evidence: M1; W; RG (named). Hook: the Plancius building with its Star of David was built in 1876 for a Jewish singing society. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verzetsmuseum)
19. **Artis**: (a); its free corner is in "Plantage Middenlaan's villas and the free corner of Artis" (Okay). Evidence: V24 1,272,515; M1; W. Hook: opened 1838, the oldest zoo in the country and the fifth-oldest still working in the world. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artis_(zoo))
20. **Hortus Botanicus**: (a); its back is in "Behind the Hortus, a phone for the dead and elms planted lying down" (Nice). Evidence: about 245,000 visitors (en wiki infobox); W (parks); M1 (Plantage). Weakest of the Centrum museums. Hook: one coffee plant from this garden was the parent of the coffee crops of Central and South America. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hortus_Botanicus_(Amsterdam))
21. **H'ART Museum (ex Hermitage), Amstelhof**: (a); the court behind it is a Deep Centrum draft ("Corvershof and the couples' homes behind H'ART") and the corner is in "Almshouses of the Amstelhof corner, six ways to house the old" (Okay). Evidence: V-wiki 503,000 (2017), V24 196,000 (2024); M1; WV. Hook: the museum began in 2004 in a side building on the Nieuwe Herengracht and only moved into the Amstelhof old people's home in 2009. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%27ART_Museum)
22. **Amsterdam Museum (Kalverstraat)**: (a). Evidence: V-wiki 475,000 (2017); M2; LP-mus; W. Hook: the city orphanage used the building until 1960. Note: the Kalverstraat site is closed for renovation and the museum is temporarily in the Amstelhof, with H'ART. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_Museum)
23. **Het Scheepvaartmuseum** (with the replica ship Amsterdam): (a). Evidence: V-wiki 348,000 (2017); M2; LP-mus; W. Hook: the building is the Admiralty's storehouse of 1656 by Daniël Stalpaert; the "VOC ship" moored outside was built in 1985 to 1990. (The Marineterrein next door is on the map.) [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Het_Scheepvaartmuseum)
24. **NEMO Science Museum**: (a). Evidence: V24 639,196; M1; LP-mus; W. Hook: Renzo Piano's green hull stands on the foundations of the IJ tunnel entrance. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/NEMO_Science_Museum)
25. **Magere Brug**: (a). Evidence: M1; W ("probably the most famous drawbridge"); WV. Hook: the two thrifty "Mager sisters" are invented; the bridge is "thin" because after the 1672 crash the city built a cheap narrow wooden bridge instead of the planned stone one. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magere_Brug)
26. **Bloemenmarkt**: (a). Evidence: M1; W ("the only floating flower market in the world"). Hook: the stalls float in the Singel itself, which is the whole claim to "the only floating flower market"; worth checking what holds them up today. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemenmarkt)
27. **Munttoren**: (c) in "Rokin's west bank: from the watchmaker's house to the Munt" (Okay); its guardhouse is a Deep Centrum draft. Evidence: W; centrum/A-LIST. Hook: the tower is named after the guardhouse beside it, where coins were struck in the disaster year 1672. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munttoren)
28. **Rembrandtplein**: (c) in "The Rembrandtplein beyond the neon: Schiller, Thorbecke and the bank's thrifty animals" (Okay). Evidence: W (squares); centrum/A-LIST. Hook: it was the butter market (Botermarkt) from 1669, with the old Regulierspoort as its weigh house. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rembrandtplein_(Amsterdam))
29. **Leidseplein**: (c) in "Leidseplein east, from the police station to the prison yard" and "Leidseplein west, party halls, a milk factory and Kramer's bridge" (both Okay). Evidence: W (squares); centrum/A-LIST. Hook: under the Kleine-Gartmanplantsoen, a bicycle cellar designed by Hans van Houwelingen (2021). [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leidseplein_(Amsterdam))
30. **Tuschinski**: (a). Evidence: M1; Time Out named it the most beautiful cinema in the world (2021, via en wiki); centrum/A-LIST. Hook: Abraham Tuschinski and his brother-in-law Gerschtanowitz were murdered in Auschwitz and Hermann Ehrlich in Sobibor, all in 1942. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuschinski_Theatre)
31. **Waag (Nieuwmarkt)**: (c) in "The Nieuwmarkt beyond the Waag: plane trees, a paving-slab bird and a sluice of 1454" (Okay). Evidence: M1; W. Hook: the surgeons' guild had its anatomy theatre upstairs and commissioned Rembrandt's Anatomy Lesson of Dr Tulp for it. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waag,_Amsterdam)
32. **Nine Streets (De 9 Straatjes)**: (c) in "Prinsengracht by the Nine Streets" (Okay). Evidence: M1; LP17 #16. Hook: the outer six street names (Reestraat, Berenstraat, Wolvenstraat, Huidenstraat, Runstraat...) recall the hide and leather trades once based here. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_9_Straatjes)
33. **Jordaan as a district**: (c), a concept; covered by many places (e.g. "Hofjes of the central Jordaan", "Theo Thijssen's Jordaan", "Jordaan legends at the Elandsgracht"). Evidence: M2; WV; RG (section). Hook: its canals carry flower names, and two were filled in: the Goudsbloemgracht (1857, now Willemsstraat) and the Anjeliersgracht (now Westerstraat). [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordaan_(Amsterdam))
34. **Beurs van Berlage**: (a); parked in centrum/A-LIST. Evidence: M1; W (twice). Hook: Albert Verwey's lines on the tower, "Beidt uw tyd" and "Duur uw uur", answer the old proverb on the cornice. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beurs_van_Berlage)
35. **Brouwerij 't IJ at De Gooyer windmill**: (c) in "The eastern bastions, from the first power station to De Gooyer and the Funen" (Okay); the mill is a Deep Centrum draft. Evidence: LP17 #3; W (breweries); WV ("the best one to visit"). Hook: since 1985 the brewery has been in the former Funen public bathhouse beside the mill. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brouwerij_%27t_IJ)
36. **Sex Museum (Venustempel)**: (a), commercial. Evidence: V-wiki 780,210 (2017, more than the Stedelijk). Hook: the museum, opened 1985, sits in a national monument (no. 857) at Damrak 18. [nl.wikipedia](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexmuseum_Amsterdam)
37. **Madame Tussauds**: (a), commercial. Evidence: named among the national top-50 attractions that did not report 2024 figures (V24 footnote); W-level fame; centrum/A-LIST. Hook: opened 1970; it fills the upper floors of the Peek & Cloppenburg building on the Dam, which is a Deep Centrum draft ("Peek & Cloppenburg: from the Nieuwendijk to the Dam"). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madame_Tussauds_Amsterdam)
38. **Red Light Secrets (Museum of Prostitution)**: (a), commercial. Evidence: V-wiki 398,000 (2017); V24 footnote. Hook: none found worth leading with; it is a reconstructed window house on the Oudezijds Achterburgwal. [nl.wikipedia list](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lijst_van_best_bezochte_musea_in_Nederland)
39. **Body Worlds**: (a), commercial. Evidence: V-wiki 381,000 (2017); V24 footnote. Hook: none found; it is a travelling show with a permanent Amsterdam branch. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_Worlds)

## Method

Candidates came from the A-lists already written (`research/zuid/A-LIST.md`, `research/centrum/A-LIST.md`, the "Parked (A-list)" sections of the Noord, Oost, West and Nieuw-West scouts) and were then tested against independent sources, all fetched with curl (`User-Agent: deep-cities-research/0.1`; no iamsterdam):

- visitor numbers from the national [day-attraction top 50 for 2024](https://www.limburg.nl/publish/pages/1653/factsheet_dagattractie_top_50_2024.pdf) and nl Wikipedia's [best-visited museums list](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lijst_van_best_bezochte_musea_in_Nederland);
- Michelin Green Guide stars from the structured data of [ViaMichelin's Amsterdam tourist-sites page](https://www.viamichelin.com/maps/tourist-attractions/poi/amsterdam-_-a2baffc48e30) (2026), pages 1 and 2. Oddly it gives the Royal Palace and the Oude Kerk only one star, and lists 108 sites but returns stars for about 60;
- en Wikipedia's [List of tourist attractions in Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tourist_attractions_in_Amsterdam);
- Lonely Planet's [17 best things to do](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/top-things-to-do-in-amsterdam) and [12 best museums](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/best-museums-in-amsterdam) (its old "attractions" ranking now redirects away);
- the [Rough Guides Amsterdam page](https://www.roughguides.com/netherlands/amsterdam/) and [Wikivoyage Amsterdam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Amsterdam).

An item is A-list when at least two of these put it in the top tier (M2 or M3, more than about 300,000 visitors, LP or RG by name), or when it is the thing a whole district is visited for (the Dam, the canals). M1 plus W alone counted as borderline. Each candidate was then checked against the live map (`pnpm dc places sights`, 1,556 places, read-only), against `research/centrum/places/*.yaml` (716 Deep Centrum drafts) and the other districts' research files. Hooks are taken from the Wikipedia article linked on each line and are leads for a drafting agent to verify, not checked facts.
