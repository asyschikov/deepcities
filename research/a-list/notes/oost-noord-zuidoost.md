## Verification

Second pass, 2026-11-24: every claim in the EN and NL summary, body, Look for / Let op and the
attributes was checked against the listed sources, fetched again (curl; nl/en Wikipedia through the
API; muziekgebouw.nl through BrowserOS because it blocks curl, own tab, closed after). The pins were
checked against PDOK and OSM: Muziekgebouw is 14 m from the Piet Heinkade 1 address point, A'DAM
matches IJpromenade 4, Eye is on the OSM Eye node (IJpromenade 1) and the ArenA is on the stadium's
OSM centroid. All four have `a_list: A-list` and no rank. All summaries are at most 280 characters
(longest: NL A'DAM, 277). No em dashes or spaced en dashes. 8-word overlap check of EN and NL against
every source text: 0 hits in the Dutch (the one EN hit is the name "Huil van de Wolff" with the time).
Validator: 0 errors.

Fixes:

- Muziekgebouw, NL: "leeg meubelpand" became "oude meubelzaak". No source says the building was
  empty (bimhuis.nl says "oude meubelzaak", nl.wikipedia "voormalige meubelshowroom").
- A'DAM, summary EN/NL: "an experimental method" / "een nieuwe methode" became "the Prepakt
  method" / "de Prepakt-methode". Cement (1970) presents it as a known method with tests at the
  Staatsmijnen, not as experimental or new.
- A'DAM, summary EN/NL: "a swing at 100 metres" became "a lookout at 100 metres and a swing over
  the edge". adamlookout.com gives 100 metres for the observation deck, not for the swing.
- A'DAM, attribute built: "1966–1971" became "1971". The sources disagree on the start (nl.wikipedia:
  piling from March 1966; Stelling 2011: first pile March 1965). Only the 1971 completion is agreed.
- Eye, summary EN: "Amsterdam only fought to keep it once it was almost gone" became "only then did
  Amsterdam fight to keep it". "Almost gone" was an interpretation; Eye's history says only that the
  city "woke up" ("wakker geschrokken") and worked to keep it. The NL already said this.
- ArenA, body EN/NL: "Since 2008 grow lights stand on the pitch all week after a match" became
  "Grow lights arrived in 2008; now they stand on the pitch all week after every match". Fieldmanager
  gives October 2008 for the lights, and nl.wikipedia ("tegenwoordig") says they stand there all week.
  No source says they have been used that way since 2008.
- ArenA, visiting EN/NL: "booked online" became "book online to be sure of a place, or buy at the
  stadium". The stadium-tours FAQ says booking ahead is not required and tickets are sold at the
  stadium. The classic tour price (EUR 27.50 adults) confirms tours are paid but is left out so the
  text does not date. "Walking round the outside is free" (no source) became "the outside and the
  road beneath it need no ticket". The road under the stadium is the public Burgemeester Stramanweg
  (nl.wikipedia).
- ArenA, sources: dropped the Q-Park page. It no longer mentions a garage under the ArenA, and the
  car park claim rests on nl.wikipedia.

Checked and left as they were: the In Fis details (all five parts, from muziekgebouw.nl/in-fis), Huil
van de Wolff "under the large canopy at the front" on the 22nd at 20:00 (muziekgebouw.nl), the Dudok
café on the ground floor open to local residents (it has replaced the 4'33 café that nl.wikipedia
names), the Cement 1970 Prepakt story (1.80 m slab, gravel nests, "zwarte bladzijde"), the F3 ferry
taking two minutes (adamtoren.nl; adamlookout.com says four, and the text follows adamtoren.nl), the
Eye Rotterdam episode, the 12,000 nitrate titles, the visiting details (until 19 December 2026 the
exhibitions are closed on Mondays and the restaurant opens at 18:00 on Mondays, so "open daily" still
holds), and the ArenA's Ouder-Amstel, 8.40 m, 18-minute roof, six relayings, more than 50 pitches by
2010 and one a year now.
