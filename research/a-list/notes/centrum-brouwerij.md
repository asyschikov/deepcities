## Relation to De Gooyer

The mill is told in "De Gooyer, the windmill that lost its wind to Napoleon" (oxn7yak6vdowiqk2dmvnx754).
This draft only names the mill and points to its place; none of its history is retold.

Conflict to flag: the De Gooyer text (EN and NL) says the brewery "is not related to it, except that the mill
is on its labels" (from nl wiki De Gooyer). De CaféKrant (25 Oct 2023) says the brewery had its malt ground
in De Gooyer in the early years, until the quality proved too uneven and it bought its own mill. Not used in
this draft; a moderator may want to soften "not related" in the De Gooyer text to "has no function for the
brewery now", citing CaféKrant. Single source, so not stated as fact here.

## Uncertain

- Zeeburgerpad rebuild status: the sources say work started October 2025 (installers from 1 Jan 2026) and
  should run less than a year. No source found that says it reopened by 24 Nov 2026. The draft says "has been
  under full rebuilding since October 2025": recheck before publishing.
- The 2019 full takeover rests on Quote (2023) and CaféKrant (citing Quote). No Duvel or brewery statement found.
- Founding year: nl wiki says founded 1983 (home brewing); the brewery dates its brewing in the bathhouse
  from 1985. The draft uses only 1985 (lease, first brew) and avoids 1983.
- Hours: AT5 2015 and the 2019 blog say 14:00 to 20:00; the tasting-room page (read 24 Nov 2026) says
  Mon to Fri 14:00 to 22:00, Sat and Sun 12:00 to 22:00. The draft says it now closes at ten.
- Monument status of the bathhouse: not found in the municipal or national monument data. No heritage_status set.
- The "board with the old bathing hours" is from the 2019 blog ("goed zoeken"); not verified on site.
