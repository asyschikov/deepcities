## Important for another slice

The Amsterdam Museum's temporary main location at Amstel 51 has been **closed since 1 December 2025** (amsterdammuseum.nl/tickets-bezoek). The museum now runs "Amsterdam in Motion" at the Westergas and Huis Willet-Holthuysen, and the Kalverstraat building is being renovated. The SCOUT note that it is at H'ART is out of date, and the centrum-dam-west Amsterdam Museum entry may need to say so.

## Claims left out, and why

- **NEMO:** the nl wiki says the foundation piles under the IJ tunnel and the museum are 95 m long. This is implausible and has no other source.
- **Magere Brug bulb count:** the sources disagree (1,200 in the en wiki, 1,800 elsewhere).
- **Magere Brug, Diamonds Are Forever and the 4 May concert:** these are true but trivia, and the text had no room for them.
- **Magere Brug date of the first bridge:** the RCE says about 1670, while the nl wiki says 1691 by Hans Jansz van Petersom. The text avoids a year ("when building there finally picked up").
- **Magere Brug listing year:** 2001 in the register, 2002 in the nl wiki. No `listed` value is given.
- **Artis pond bridges:** the RCE says two identical cast-iron bridges of 1866 by J. Zimmer en Zn. The nl wiki says the canal became three ponds with bridges by architect Salm. The text follows the RCE for what stands now.
- **Artis and the quagga building:** the nl Artis wiki says the quagga died near the Eenhoevigenhuis, but the register dates the zebra stable (518292) to 1920, after 1883. The quagga is not tied to a building.
- **Artis "one of the five oldest zoos in the world":** this comes from Artis itself, and the en wiki says "fifth oldest still operating". It is left out as a superlative that the text doesn't need. "The country's oldest zoo" stays, sourced from the en wiki and Artis ("eerste publieke dierentuin van Nederland").
- **Artis, Jewish members barred in September 1941 (nl wiki):** true, but the place already had enough material. It could be a future hidden-history item.
- **Replica ship dates:** the museum says it was built from 1985 and has been at the jetty since 1989. The nl wiki says 1982 to 1990, with 400 SABO volunteers, "sold" for 1 guilder. The text says "begun in the 1980s".
- **Ship Amsterdam wreck date:** the summary of earlier research had 26 January 1749. The text says "January 1749" (nl wiki: "begin 1749").
- **Tuschinski architect:** the RCE credits H.L. de Jong (designed 1918). The nl wiki says Willem Kromhout took over De Jong's tasks after a quarrel. Both are named.
- **Tuschinski listing year:** 1967 in the en wiki, 1974 in the nl wiki. No `listed` value is given.
- **Tuschinski, other details:** the Wurlitzer organ, the 2019 to 2021 restoration of the Den Besten murals, the royal predicate of 2021, and the Time Out ranking. These are left out for length, and the ranking is a superlative.
- **Tuschinski's 1936 sale:** the earlier notes said "bankrupt", but the nl wiki says he was "financieel aan de grond" and had to sell to NV Tubem. The text says money trouble forced the sale.
- **Esnoga fittings:** the RCE says the whole interior dates from the building, ark included. The en wiki says the Torah ark dates from 1744 and the benches came from a 1639 synagogue. The text says only that the bimah, benches and chandeliers "have stayed as they were in the 17th century" (JCK: "alles is nog precies zoals het in de 17e eeuw was"). The ark is described only as jacaranda wood.
- **Esnoga architect:** the RCE names Stalpaert and Bouman, and the nl wiki names Bouman. Both are in `architect`.
- **Esnoga sand floor, "only one surviving outside the Caribbean" (en wiki):** left out as a superlative.
- **Esnoga 350th anniversary (2025, with the King):** left out as news.
- **Grote Synagoge architect:** the register names Stalpaert, and other sources name Bouman. No architect is given.
- **Hortus "eerste koffieplanten":** kept, attributed to the Hortus itself ("what the garden calls"). The "parent of the coffee culture in Central and South America" claim is from the nl and en Wikipedia.
- **H'ART "no collection of its own":** the en wiki states it in the context of 2021. Kept.
- **H'ART plaque unveiled by Putin and Beatrix in 2013:** from a gallery caption only, so not used.
- **H'ART opening date:** officially opened 19 June 2009, public 20 June (nl wiki). The text says "June 2009".
- **H'ART adult price:** €29.50 plus surcharges for Chez Matisse (3 Oct 2026 to 14 Feb 2027). The text says "paid", because the price changes per exhibition.
- **Rembrandthuis adult price:** not captured. The text says "paid".
- **Rembrandthuis, Saskia:** this is from the museum's own story page ("pas getrouwd met Saskia").
