### Stedelijk Museum
Hook: the empty medallions (register: Weissman wanted to fill them later with relief portraits of contemporary artists). Sources: register 505840; nl/en wiki; stedelijk.nl/en/visit (paid).
Conflict: en wiki says Sandberg had the walls covered in white jute (1938); nl wiki says he had them painted white; the register says the interior was "in 1938 al voornamelijk wit". EN text says white jute, NL says "wit liet maken" (neutral). Same fact, no contradiction.
Opening year: register gives building 1892–1894 (used for `built`); en wiki gives opening 14 Sep 1895 (not in text).
Left out: Serra's "Sight Point" and the façade lettering (A-LIST.md parks them as part of the museum); the 2006 stone through the Sandberg wing window (nl wiki; fun but not visible).

### Concertgebouw
Hook: built in a Nieuwer-Amstel pasture on 2,186 piles, opening delayed by the neighbouring municipality, piles replaced after 1983; lyre is a 1993 copper copy of the zinc original. Sources: register 288; nl/en wiki; concertgebouw.nl lunchtime concert page ("every week", 12:30, free ticket online).
Neighbours pointed at: none in text ("Concertgebouwplein 3–13 and 20", "Berlage's drawing school" exist; not repeated).
Left out: the dust layer kept for acoustics (en wiki, unsourced there); the 2025 Chanukah concert dispute (current controversy, not a visitor detail); Leipzig Gewandhaus model (cut for length, both languages). Summer break of the lunch concerts known only from iamsterdam (not used), so the text says "every week" as the hall's own page does.

### Museumplein
Hook: ice rink of 1887 to 1940 with the first official world speed skating championships (1893, Jaap Eden); wartime bunkers; the "shortest motorway in the Netherlands" (1953–1997); the ezelsoor. Sources: nl wiki Museumplein (Amsterdam); en wiki; NH Nieuws 10 Jul 2026 (water back in the rebuilt pond).
Correction: the scout's hook said "before 1903 it was the skating club's ground"; nl wiki says the rink started in 1887 and the IJsclub stayed until 1940, the name Museumplein dates from 1903.
Not said: who built the bunkers (nl wiki does not say; a search snippet says 1943). Text says "bunkers were built".
Neighbours pointed at: "Two memorials on the Museumplein". Not repeated: villas on the Museumplein street side, Former ANWB office.
Left out: 1981 anti-nuclear demonstration (420,000), I amsterdam letters (2005–2018), Legacy Art Court (parked in A-LIST.md).

### Heineken Experience
Hook: the register's verdict on the brewing hall; four riveted kettles of 1911–1913; Zijl's haystack relief for De Hooiberg. Sources: registers 527809, 527810, 527811; nl wiki Heineken Brouwerij (Amsterdam), Heineken Experience; en wiki; heinekenexperience.com (online tickets, drinks included).
Conflict: en and nl wiki say the museum building was "built in 1867"; the register dates the listed buildings 1911–1934. Text: brewery on the site from 1867, listed buildings later.
Brewhouse architect: not in the register; `architect` names only Ouëndag for silo and cold store.
Neighbours pointed at: "Marie Heinekenplein: Mandela, Marie and a star nobody sees", "6,716,341 litres, the works-kitchen plate on the brewery wall".
Left out: KLM Delft blue house no. 95 (2014) as trivia; ticket prices (change often).

### Moco Museum (Villa Alsberg)
Hook: the villa itself (Eduard Cuypers, 1904–1906, English country-house style per the register). Sources: register 1992; en/nl wiki; mocomuseum.com (read in BrowserOS; the about page has no history of the villa).

### Albert Cuyp market
Hook: the street lies on the filled-in Zaagmolensloot, a sawmill ditch, hence its width. Sources: nl wiki Albert Cuypmarkt, Albert Cuypstraat, De Pijp; en wiki Albert Cuyp Market. Days (Monday to Saturday) from nl wiki De Pijp.
Neighbours pointed at: "André Hazes on the Albert Cuyp", "Pawn bank turned bicycle garage, Gerard Doustraat 156" (which also mentions the Zaagmolensloot in one clause), "Buiten-Amstelkerk, the church on the Albert Cuyp".
Left out: "largest day market in Europe" (a claim the market makes; nl wiki states it flatly, en wiki says "claims"); six million visitors a year (one 2011 quote); the artwork "Alles wat er op de markt te koop is" (2016, named in nl wiki, no artist or location found).
