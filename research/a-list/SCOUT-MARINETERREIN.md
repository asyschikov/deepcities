# Marineterrein scout shortlist (Deep Amsterdam)

Scouted 2026-11-24. Shortlist only, no drafts. Fetched with curl (UA deep-cities-research/0.1); the
marineterrein.nl history timeline (/verhalen/) loads its text by script and was read in a BrowserOS tab
(closed after). No iamsterdam.

Already on the map (not proposed again): the ensemble "Marineterrein and the Dijksgracht"
(kxtuc6i8msgw5cycla8tfkit, Amazing) with children "Marineterrein, the navy yard behind the wall"
(mqhz13s7x8994o1wovg0en78, Amazing), Mariniersbrug, Sluisdeurenloods, The Schietbaan
(eca42qma36vf0m6a538q08jw, Nice) and The Shaffyboom. The Marineterrein place already tells: Admiralty yard
1655, Hollandia and Van Speijk's gunboat, closure 1915, Van Gogh 1877 to 1878, IJ tunnel clearance 1962,
the commander's house with bunker, the four municipal listings of 2025, the wall (2023), opening 1 April 2015,
swimming since April 2025, and Look for lines for the gate, the wall, the commander's house and boardwalk,
and the Kweekschool memorial.

Rank is a proposal. "Own place" means a new child of the ensemble; "Look for" means one line added to the
existing Marineterrein place (or the named sibling).

## Shortlist

### 1. The swimming harbour that is also a water lab
- Hook: the old navy harbour is Centrum's first official swimming spot, and the water around you is full of
  experiments: two oloid mixers called Kriek and Maria, a reef of 30 modules, floating gardens and a bubble screen.
- Why it fits: swimming in a former navy dock, among working prototypes, is unlike anything else in the city.
  The existing place mentions swimming in one sentence and none of the experiments.
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/news/binnenhaven-wordt-eerste-officiele-zwemlocatie-in-amsterdam-centrum/ ;
  second: https://marineterrein.nl/experimenten/de-oloide-beweging/ (oloids launched 2 Apr 2026, design
  Maria Blaisse), https://marineterrein.nl/experimenten/een-schuilplek-voor-onderwaterleven/ (MOSES reef,
  30 modules, 2020), https://marineterrein.nl/experimenten/dobbernatuur/ (2024),
  https://marineterrein.nl/experimenten/the-great-bubble-barrier/ ; local voice: Buurtorganisatie 1018,
  14 Oct 2026, the stadsdeel asks for more swimming spots like this one.
- Rank: Amazing.
- Form: own place (the inner harbour and boardwalk), with the experiments as Look for lines.
- Caveats: no lifeguards; the floating jetty closes for 72 hours after bad water readings; swimming season and
  rules may change with the new site manager (Voets+TEPP since Sep 2025). Experiments are temporary by nature:
  recheck each one is still in the water before publishing.

### 2. The 1960s navy campus: officers' tower, a signal-flag classroom on the roof, schools on stilts
- Hook: after the IJ tunnel cleared the west of the yard, F.C. de Weger rebuilt it as a modernist campus: an
  officers' building with a glass tower (now a hotel), a signals school with a ship's-bridge classroom on the
  roof, and two teaching blocks on Le Corbusier-style pilotis.
- Why it fits: a complete post-war military ensemble in the middle of the old city that few people read as
  architecture; three of its buildings were made municipal monuments in July 2025 ("beetje brutalistische
  architectuur", AT5).
- Lead: 2018 survey PDF (above), sections on 006 Officiersgebouw ("opmerkelijk gaaf", longroom),
  028 Verbindingsschool (rooftop signal-flag classroom shaped like a bridge), 027 TOKM (two blocks on columns);
  second: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/233547/vier-gemeentelijke-monumenten-marineterrein ;
  https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/gebouw-027e-marineterrein/ (pilotis after Le Corbusier; SLA's 2016 slats
  shaped as EU flags for the Dutch EU presidency).
- Rank: Nice.
- Form: own place ("De Weger's navy campus"), pin on 006. Could absorb item 5's EU-flag slats.
- Caveats: the listed buildings stand on the west edge where the city park is planned (AT5); the purchase is
  unsettled (item 13). 006 is open as Pension Homeland (hotel and restaurant, per the site map); the others are
  offices, seen from outside. 028's rooftop classroom: check it is visible from public ground.

### 3. The Moluccan marines' monument (2026)
- Hook: on 29 April 2026, 75 years to the day after the first ship with Moluccan KNIL soldiers moored at the
  Javakade, a work by Moluccan artist Yopi Abraham was unveiled on the Voorwerf for the fourteen marines
  stationed at kazerne Kattenburg in 1951; their names were read aloud one by one.
- Why it fits: new, little known, and it ties the yard to a chapter of Amsterdam history most visitors never hear.
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/news/onthulling-moluks-monument/ ; second:
  https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/359370/eerste-molukkers-kwamen-75-jaar-geleden-aan-mis-mijn-vader-nog-elke-dag
  (fourteen marines, Kattenburg barracks).
- Rank: Nice.
- Form: own place (or Look for in the Marineterrein place if the ensemble gets crowded).
- Caveats: form and material of the work not yet described in any source read; need a photo or 4en5mei entry.
  Not to be confused with the national Moluccan monument Ulu Kora in Rotterdam (21 Jun 2026).

### 4. The gate: Stalpaert's facade, a 1982 hole in the wall and a motto in the windows
- Hook: the yard's 17th-century front has a carriage gate under the Admiralty's arms and carved shipwrights'
  tools; in 1982 Benthem Crouwel sawed a 12 m opening in the old wall next to it for a gatehouse whose masts
  recalled ships.
- Why it fits: two eras of "keep out" side by side, now the way in. The current text has the gate as one Look for line.
- Lead: city monuments API 2206 ('s Lands Werf, rijksmonument 1970, Stalpaert; pilasters, carved frieze and
  pediment); 2018 survey on 003 Voorwerf/poortgebouw and 007 Portiersgebouw (Benthem Crouwel 1982, 12 m hole,
  masts later removed); second: https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/poort-open/ (tympanum with Admiralty arms and
  shipbuilding tools; opened to the public in 2015), https://marineterrein.nl/10-jaar-marineterrein/ (2015:
  "Sumus et fore speramus" projected through the gatehouse windows; green gate),
  https://marineterrein.nl/experimenten/de-oasepoort-experimenteel-binnenkomen/ (rolling planters at the gate).
- Rank: Nice.
- Form: Look for, expanded (two lines in the existing place), not its own place. Promote only if item 2 is not added.
- Caveats: the motto projection was a 2015 event, not permanent; Oasepoort planters are an experiment, check.

### 5. The marechaussee's black box
- Hook: a black-tiled cube with 1,750 identical windows, built to exactly the size of the Scheepvaartmuseum
  next door, designed so staff can look out but nobody can see in.
- Why it fits: a striking 2008 building the public walks past without knowing whose it is.
- Lead: https://arcam.nl/architectuur-gids/marinekazerne-amsterdam/ (Wansleben, 2008; 1,750 windows; same
  dimensions as the Scheepvaartmuseum); second: 2018 survey (014 Marechaussee, 2005 design, Wansleben).
- Rank: Nice.
- Form: own place, seen from the Kattenburgerstraat or the Jan Schaeferbrug.
- Caveats: Defence/marechaussee, no entry. Date: Arcam 2008, survey gives 2005 (design vs completion, check).
  Defence's 2026 plan puts a conference building next to it (item 13).

### 6. Twenty-one guns from the Marineterrein
- Hook: when a head of state comes on a state visit, Defence fires the salute from here: 21 blanks, five
  seconds apart; 105 for the birth of an heir.
- Why it fits: an old ritual with a fixed spot that most Amsterdammers have heard and never placed.
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/news/saluutschoten-voor-de-president-van-finland/ (Dec 2025; previous one for
  Oman in April 2025; 21, 35, 105 shots); second: https://marineterrein.nl/news/saluutschoten-staatbezoek-japanse-keizer/ (same site; an
  independent Defence or news source is still needed).
- Rank: Nice (as a line), not its own place.
- Form: Look for or text line in the Marineterrein place.
- Caveats: only on state visits, a few times a year.

### 7. Behind the wall: the Cold War shelters
- Hook: under the commander's house is a communications bunker; under barracks 029 lies a nuclear shelter
  for 350 servicemen, put forward for listing in 2025.
- Why it fits: the Cuba crisis built bunkers in the middle of the old town (timeline: "enorme bunkers", in use
  into the 1980s).
- Lead: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/233547/vier-gemeentelijke-monumenten-marineterrein ; second:
  https://marineterrein.nl/news/meer-monumenten-op-het-marineterrein/ ; https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/koude-oorlog/ .
- Rank: Nice.
- Form: text in the Marineterrein place (the commander's bunker is already there); add the 350-man shelter and
  its pending listing. Not its own place: nothing to see.
- Caveats: not accessible; listing decision for the shelter was due "later in 2025", outcome not found.

### 8. The ship carpenters' workshop and the scheepskameel
- Hook: one of only two buildings left from the old yard, now restaurant Scheepskameel, named after the 1690
  invention of Meeuwis Meindertsz. Bakker for lifting deep ships over the IJ shallows.
- Why it fits: you can sit inside the yard's oldest working building; the name carries a good story.
- Lead: monuments API 230005 (Gebouw 024, ship carpenters' workshop of 1878, listed 2025); second: 2018 survey
  (024, "around 1860"); https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/de-scheepskameel/ ; site map (Scheepskameel in 024 A).
- Rank: Nice.
- Form: own place, or Look for if the ensemble is full.
- Caveats: build date conflict (1878 register vs about 1860 survey). Restaurant still open? Recheck before
  publishing given the RVB's wish for less catering (item 13). Arcam says "only two original buildings left":
  name the other one before writing.

### 9. The wall that is a row of vanished back walls, now a forest
- Hook: the long wall on the Kattenburgerstraat is mostly the back walls of buildings that are gone, full of
  traces; a "Muurbos" now grows on it, with steel letters that double as nest spots.
- Why it fits: an everyday wall that reads like an archive, plus a living one.
- Lead: monuments API 200977 (wall, municipal 2023); Krabbe appendix in the 2018 survey (oldest parts possibly
  17th century); second: https://marineterrein.nl/news/muurbos-wint-prijs-voor-meest-weerproof-geveltuin-van-amsterdam/ ;
  https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2023/4/24/muur-marineterrein-wordt-gemeentelijk-monument .
- Rank: Okay (as a line).
- Form: Look for (extend the existing wall line with the Muurbos).
- Caveats: Muurbos location along the wall to be pinned.

### 10. The provost's house with two cells
- Hook: the military police post kept two prison cells on the quay side; the crane beside it recalls the supply ships.
- Why it fits: small, odd, and on the public part.
- Lead: 2018 survey, 015 Provoosthuis.
- Rank: Okay.
- Form: Look for.
- Caveats: single source (survey 2018); second source and current use needed; cells probably not visible.

### 11. Buildings with numbers, not addresses
- Hook: there are no street names on the terrain; since 2019 buildings carry big number boards from 001 to 039.
- Why it fits: a navy habit that visitors meet straight away and that explains every name on this list.
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/news/navigeren-op-nummer/ ; second: https://marineterrein.nl/10-jaar-marineterrein/ (2019).
- Rank: Okay.
- Form: text line in the Marineterrein place.
- Caveats: none.

### 12. "Geheim": the yard that was blurred
- Hook: until the gate opened, what went on behind the wall was largely secret: crown witnesses and threatened
  politicians were sheltered here, and the site was blurred on satellite photos.
- Why it fits: good local lore with an official source; Ayaan Hirsi Ali was housed here in 2005 (nl wiki).
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/geheim/ ; second: nl.wikipedia Marineterrein (Amsterdam) (Hirsi Ali 2005;
  signals intelligence centre until 2005).
- Rank: Okay.
- Form: text line in the Marineterrein place.
- Caveats: avoid any claim about current secret use.

### 13. What happens next: purchase in doubt, a new barracks, closures (2025 to 2026)
- Hook: the city adopted plans for about 800 homes and a park in 2023 and took a right of first refusal in
  June 2025, but in 2026 the purchase is "very uncertain" with geopolitics; Defence sketches a compact new
  barracks in two strips with a heliveld, and the state agency is winding down catering.
- Why it fits: the existing place should carry an outlook; this is the fight residents follow closely.
- Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2026/8/27/efensie-deelt-eerste-gedachten-over-bouw-nieuwe-kazerne-op-het-marine-etablissement ;
  https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2026/2/26/de-samenwerking-op-het-marineterrein-krijgt-vorm ;
  https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2025/6/30/gemeente-vestigt-voorkeursrecht-marineterrein ;
  https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2026/11/1/twee-horecagelegenheden-sluiten-op-het-marineterrein ;
  second: https://marineterrein.nl/over-het-terrein/straks/ (projectnota 9 Nov 2023, about 800 homes, city park).
  Local voice: residents' working group (WOM) and the tenants' collective presented a manifest, 23 Sep 2025.
- Rank: n/a (attributes).
- Form: outlook on the existing place: "Major change ahead", outlook_note with the plan and the doubt.
- Caveats: the barracks plan is "first thoughts"; the city page's "ready in 2032" was seen only in a search
  snippet, unverified.

### 14. Gone: Kanteen 025 and the Buurtboerderij
- Hook: in autumn 2026 café-restaurant Kanteen 025 and the Regenbooggroep's Buurtboerderij (here since late 2024,
  after its Westerpark home burned) left, because the state agency wants fewer catering leases before handover.
- Why it fits: status correction; residents call them two key meeting places.
- Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2026/11/1/twee-horecagelegenheden-sluiten-op-het-marineterrein ;
  https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2026/6/28/buurt-wil-dat-de-buurtboerderij-marineterrein-voorlopig-mag-blijven .
- Rank: n/a.
- Form: check the map for any place or route stop naming Kanteen 025 or the Buurtboerderij; mark Gone or remove.
- Caveats: single local source; second source needed (Regenbooggroep or marineterrein.nl).

### 15. Van Gogh's room: gone or in building 003?
- Hook: the existing text says Van Gogh lived with his uncle "in a house that is gone"; a marineterrein.nl
  letting ad of 17 Jul 2026 says the office in gebouw 003 is in the part of the building where he stayed.
- Why it fits: correction check on an Amazing place.
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/news/nu-te-huur-monumentale-kantoorruimte-in-innovatiedistrict-marineterrein/ .
- Rank: n/a.
- Form: check: Facts on the Marineterrein place. Needs a second source (Van Gogh Museum letters, Stadsarchief).
- Caveats: a letting ad is a weak source; do not change the text on it alone.

### 16. History lines for the Marineterrein text (no own place, nothing left to see)
- De Olifant (1868): a floating steam crane lifting 80 tons, nicknamed "the Elephant". https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/de-olifant/
- Het Paleis: a brick complex with towers on the dock's south dike, called "the Palace"; gone. https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/het-paleis/
- Bijltjesdag, 30 May 1787: shipwrights of the Admiralty and VOC yards (the "Bijltjes") against the patriots at the Kattenburg bridge. https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/bijltjesdag-op-kattenburg/
- Fire of 20 June 1883: an iron shed, three slipway roofs and a steamship lost. https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/brand-op-werf/
- Aardappeloproer 1917: crowds raided the army's potato barges; 9 dead. https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/aardappeloproer/
- Last ships: the tanker Artemis (1914) and Canopus and Bellatrix (1915). https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/het-laatste-schip/
- 1945: about 1,200 Germans marched out of the gate after the liberation. https://marineterrein.nl/verhalen/de-tweede-wereldoorlog/
- Rank: Okay. Form: pick one or two for the text. Caveat: the timeline is the site's own popular history;
  each needs a second source (Stadsarchief, Delpher) before use.

### 17. The Kweekschool memorial: update the count
- Hook: already a Look for line. 4en5mei says 138 names at unveiling (14 May 1949), four added to 1995, 18 more in
  2019 and more in 2023: 186 now.
- Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3257/amsterdam-monument-op-het-marineterrein
- Form: no change needed (existing text says 186); keep for the fact check.

### 18. The Voorwerf fountain and the sea cadets' "follie"
- Hook: a 1990s fountain by A. Spronken in the middle of the Voorwerf, and a small brick sea-cadet building the
  survey calls worth keeping "as a kind of folly".
- Lead: 2018 survey (fountain; 031 Zeekadetcorps). Earlier scout: "Fontein met boegbeelden, Marineterrein: no story".
- Rank: Okay. Form: one Look for line at most.
- Caveats: single source; artist's full name not found.

### 19. De Heg and Kunst in de Heg
- Hook: 20 parking spaces given back in 2023 became a hedge garden by Naomi Jansen, with monthly art in it
  March to August 2026.
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/experimenten/parkxpark/ ; https://marineterrein.nl/news/kunst-in-de-heg-april/.
- Rank: Okay. Form: Look for. Caveats: seasonal, experiment.

### 20. Trees: the 1945 poplar and the three that were saved
- Hook: a Canada poplar planted in 1945 sheds "populierensneeuw"; in 2015 residents saved an ash and two elms from felling.
- Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2015/6/30/kap-van-drie-bomen-marineterrein-gaat-niet-door ;
  poplar from the earlier GR scout in research/centrum/scouts (tree register N5569).
- Rank: Okay. Form: Look for. Caveats: check the trees still stand (2015 is old evidence).

### 21. "Nasi Goreng": the canteen's morse-code artwork
- Hook: Else van der Burgt's 2004 work in the navy canteen refers to the navy's fixed Wednesday dish since 1949,
  in lit letters that refer to morse code; the canteen also has a brick relief by Jo Klingers.
- Lead: 2018 survey (030 Bedrijfskantine).
- Rank: Okay. Form: notes only.
- Caveats: in the Defence part, not accessible. Single source. Do not map.

### 22. Innovatie Paviljoen and the experiments field
- Hook: a biobased pavilion with tours every third Thursday; Roboat and the slim roof (Smart Roof 2.0 on 002) were
  tested here.
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/experimenten/innovatie-paviljoen/ ; https://marineterrein.nl/experimenten/smart-roof-2-0/ ;
  https://marineterrein.nl/experimenten/roboat-verlicht-verkeersdruk-in-een-volle-stad/
- Rank: Okay. Form: one text line on the "test site" character. Caveats: temporary, project pages may be stale.

### 23. Art on the buildings: "Vallend licht" and Abri Maximaal
- Hook: Titia Ex's light work "Vallend licht" on building 003 (2022); the Abri Maximaal light work built here in Nov 2025.
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/10-jaar-marineterrein/ (2022);
  https://marineterrein.nl/news/abri-maximaal-lichtkunstwerk-wordt-opgebouwd-op-het-marineterrein/
- Rank: Okay. Form: Look for (evening) if still there. Caveats: status unknown for both; Abri Maximaal may be a festival piece.

### 24. Where the BurgerReserve began
- Hook: the civilian crisis reserve started about five years ago among entrepreneurs on the terrain; the Tweede
  Kamer backed its expansion in Oct 2026, possibly with training here.
- Lead: https://marineterrein.nl/news/kamer-steunt-uitbreiding-burgerreserve-mogelijk-ook-trainingen-op-marineterrein/
- Rank: Okay. Form: notes, possible text line later. Caveats: second source needed; not a sight.
