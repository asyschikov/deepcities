## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Trompenburgstraat place vs Rivierstaete (309).** The live "Trompenburgstraat, the workers' street older than Plan Zuid" says the Spyker factory buildings "were demolished in 1993". nl.wikipedia (Amsteldok) puts Rivierstaete, built 1967 to 1973, on the Spyker site after a paper factory that was liquidated in 1968. One of the two is off; the new place only says the works "stood here". Worth a check of the 1993 date in `rijnbuurt.yaml`.
- **"Largest office building in Europe" (309)** comes from De Tijd of 3 April 1973 as cited by nl.wikipedia; the text attributes it to "a newspaper of the day".
- **Crystal Houses (301).** Left out: the cracks in the glue a year after delivery (nl.wikipedia only, no reference). The bricks were cast by Poesia / Vetreria Resanese in Resana, near Venice, not in Venice: the text says "in Italy". Hermès: nl.wikipedia says April 2019, Hermès itself 18 May 2019: the text says 2019. Latest evidence of the tenant: a Hermès window listed for summer 2025 on MVRDV's project page.
- **Kinderboerderij (303).** Left out: the slogan "Sol-do-mi-terop" (not found in any source opened) and the Okura weather light (one 2006 source, current state unknown). Free entry is the scout's statement; the farm's site lists hours but no price.
- **Marie Heinekenplein (305)** rests on nl.wikipedia, which cites de Volkskrant (1993) and Het Parool (1994, 2023); the papers themselves were not opened. Latest photos: 2018.
- **Synagogue (307).** Left out: stones of the 1966 synagogue built into the front and the Hebrew greeting in matze-shaped letters (nl.wikipedia only; Arcam's page now redirects to its home page). The official notice calls the site a "schiereilandje" (small peninsula) named Jacob Soetendorpeiland; the text says both. That the building is guarded is not stated; the text says it is not open to passers-by.
- **De Mirandabad (308).** De Miranda's death: 3 November 1942 (nl.wikipedia, citing the civil register) against 23 October (Ons Amsterdam); the text says "that autumn". Left out: "largest glass dome in Europe" (one Wikipedia article), the Carasso plaque of 1948 and the surviving 1932 building at President Kennedylaan 7 (one blog each, current state unknown). Whether the palms of 1979 are still there was not checked. The city's page (read 24 Nov 2026) carries a banner that the 50 m outdoor pool is closed for works from 19 October: not in the text, as it will pass.
- **Giele Skull (317).** The skull is in section De Peere (2.4), not in Paradiso: the Trust refused Paradiso. Pin estimated from the cemetery's 2020 map by fitting seven labels to OSM points (residuals up to 26 m). No photo or report of the skull after 2006 found, only the Sleutelwerken entry (a visitor comment of May 2023) and the 2020 map: hence `check: Location` with the rest in the note. The scout's grave number N-III-1129 was not confirmed and is not used. Willem Endstra also lies in Paradiso (2020 map) but is left out of the text.
- **Vossiusstraat 16 (302).** Purchase year (2018) and price are from en.wikipedia only and left out. Volozh's name is spelled as in English-language press; the Dutch text uses "Volozj".
