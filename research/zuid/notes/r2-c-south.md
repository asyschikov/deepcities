## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **313, are Valley's stairs open.** zuidas.nl says the inner garden on the lower levels "is open to
  everyone" and that stairs link its levels; MVRDV said the same at the opening in 2022. Nothing from
  2025 or 2026 confirms or denies it, and the building's own site gives no hours. The body says
  "by design" and the place carries `check: Facts`. If the stairs turn out to be closed, the rank
  should drop to Okay.
- **314, the Viñoly stair.** zuidas.nl says the stair was to lead the public to a roof terrace and was
  closed for safety before the building opened. The architect's own page describes the same stair as
  an outdoor route and gathering space for the people who work there. One source against another, so
  the story is left out. The 91 m (zuidas.nl) against 95 m (nl.wikipedia) is not used either.
- **321, how the garden was saved.** The scout's version (the university said in 2010 that it could
  stay and the hospital redrew its buildings around it) is on en.wikipedia only. The two news
  sources it cites say something else: Het Parool (7 April 2009) reports the threat and the email
  campaign, RTV NH (11 August 2011) reports that the hospital's building was postponed by the crisis
  and the garden could stay 8 to 10 years while the hospital still wanted the plot. The text follows
  the news sources and the 2014 covenant.
- **321, number of species.** "About 8,000 species and hybrids" (the garden's site, 2026) against
  "more than 6,000 species in 2016" (nl.wikipedia). The garden's figure is used. "More than 30,000
  plants" for the cactus collection is on nl.wikipedia only and left out.
- **321, rank.** Amazing, as the scout proposed: free, large, with a story and a collection found
  nowhere else in the country. It is the only Amazing of the nine.
- **310, when ING left.** The scout had 2012; nl.wikipedia and NOS (10 April 2014) say the first half
  of 2014. Het Parool's headline of 29 December 2023 says the building had not been the bank's for
  13 years, which refers to ownership. The article itself is behind a paywall and was not opened.
- **312, costs.** 1.385 billion euros (2012 agreement, price level 2011) and "about 5.4 billion"
  (NH Nieuws, 16 October 2026). The scout's remark that the entrances are openly temporary could not
  be confirmed and is left out. The Zuidasdok works beside the twelve fists are not mentioned: no
  source says the sculpture is affected.
- **312, pin.** On the northern end of the passage as drawn in OpenStreetMap (24 November 2026). The
  entrance buildings were three weeks old; a few metres' error is possible. No check flag.
- **315, the neon letters.** Dutch Birding describes pink neon letters above the entrance in 1989 to
  1991. Photos of 2019 show the griffin emblem and no letters, so the text says "that then hung".
  The scout's occupations (100 hours in February 1972; 18 May 2026, 22 arrests) are left out: the
  1972 page no longer resolves, and the 2026 one is confirmed (vu.nl, NH Nieuws) but adds nothing a
  visitor can see.
- **315, access.** The text says "a working university building" and gives the entrance. Whether
  visitors may walk the ground floor is not stated on any page read.
- **318, admission.** Zone2Source's pages give hours but no price, so the text does not say "free".
  The founding year of the platform was not found and is left out. This is the thinnest of the nine
  places; it could be folded back into the existing Look for line in "Amstelpark".
- **319, trains in 1972.** Four on nl.wikipedia, two on the operator's site. No number is given.
  The season in the text is the operator's 2026 timetable (Thursday to Sunday), not nl.wikipedia's
  (Wednesday to Sunday).
- **322, hours.** zuidas.nl (2019) gave 10:00 to 17:00; the foundation's own contact page, read in a
  browser on 24 November 2026, says "We are open", Monday to Friday 9:00 to 18:00, free entrance.
  The foundation's hours are used. The current exhibition could not be read from the site.
- **Dutch texts.** All written fresh from Dutch sources except the AkzoNobel place, where the only
  source with substance is an interview on zuidas.nl read in English.
