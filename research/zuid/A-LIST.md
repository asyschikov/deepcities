# Zuid: A-list sights, parked

Deep Amsterdam is the city off the A-list. Zuid holds most of Amsterdam's A-list, so the rule
is written down here. The user's decision (2026-11-24): list them, don't prepare them for
import.

## The rule

- An **A-list sight** is one that any general guidebook's top list of Amsterdam carries and
  that visitors come to Zuid for. It gets no place on the map, and its monument records are
  parked here.
- A **separately listed or separately standing thing beside it** that most visitors walk past
  may be a place, if it has a hook of its own and is pitched on that (a director's villa, a
  garden of building fragments, a drawing school, a statue with a story).
- Well-known but second-tier sights (the Olympic Stadium, the Amsterdamse Bos edge, the
  Sarphatipark, De Dageraad, the Zuidas) are **not** A-list: they go on the map, pitched on
  what the guidebook leaves out.
- A-list sights may be named in other places' texts as landmarks.
