# Zuidoost: Bijlmermeer overview notes

1 place: **Bijlmermeer** (hidden-history, rank Amazing). Not a register monument. Not gone.

## Pin

52.320030, 4.971750: the lawn inside the hexagonal court of Gooioord, on the public paths of the Gulden Kruispad (checked on OpenStreetMap). The wings of Gooioord close round the court on three sides, so a visitor stands inside one cell of the honeycomb. Gooioord is one of the six flats in the protected Bijlmermuseum area. Nearest existing places: Tayouken Piss 157 m, Groeiend Monument 201 m; the Bijlmer Museum and Kleiburg are over 400 m away.

Hakfort and Huigenbos were suggested, but they are in the H-buurt by Bullewijk (about 52.306, 4.959), not in the Bijlmermuseum area, and close to Perfumed with Mint and Heesterveld. So I used Gooioord instead.

## Attributes checked

- built 1966–1975: first pile 13 Dec 1966 (Van Hall), first residents in Hoogoord 25 Nov 1968, the honeycomb high-rise "verrezen 1968-1975" (nl.wikipedia).
- architect: a team of the afdeling Stadsontwikkeling of the Dienst der Publieke Werken led by Siegfried Nassuth (nl.wikipedia, Ons Amsterdam).

## Source conflicts and corrections

- **Demolition share.** The request said "about a quarter". That was only the first round: in 1992 the city decided to demolish a quarter of the flats, and around 2002 (Finale plan van aanpak) another quarter followed. Ons Amsterdam (2009) and the Canon van Amsterdam (venster 43) both say half the high-rise went (about 6,500 of 13,000 flats; Sociale Vraagstukken says 7,000). The text gives both rounds and "about half".
- **Storeys.** nl.wikipedia: nine storeys on a two-storey base with indoor street and storage. Other sources say ten or eleven. The English says "ten-storey"; the Dutch keeps the wiki wording.
- **1975.** The Canon dates the exclusion rules against Surinamese and Antilleans to 1972 and the Gliphoeve squat to before independence (nl.wikipedia: 1974). The text says "around independence in 1975" and dates the rules to 1972.
- **Crash toll.** 43 dead (4 on board, 39 on the ground), per the Canon venster 46. The Canon also notes that the number of undocumented victims has never been settled; left out for length.
- The Van Hall quote ("het prettigste woonoord dat zich laat denken") is from Ons Amsterdam (2009).
- "Bims" (Smibanese for the Bijlmer) is from the Canon venster 43.
