## Merges

- Scout 6 and 7 are one place. The Pa Sembrug (brug 1145, Kunstwacht 3618) and De boom die alles zag (Kunstwacht 2100) are both parts of the Groeiend Monument (Kunstwacht 2101). nl.wikipedia lists the bridge as one of the monument's parts.

## Decisions for the coordinator

- **Time capsule rank**: the scout suggested Amazing. I gave it Nice because what you see on site is a granite slab with gold lettering, and the 500-year story is the draw. Raise it if the map wants two Amazings here.
- **Anton de Kom**: the statue is still in place. The debate about it is live: Akwasi dressed it in June 2025, and in January 2026 the artist wrote an open letter in Parool saying she would now make a different statue. I found no decision to replace it. Check again before going live.
- **Rona Batho**: one of the seven faces was stolen on the night of 1–2 March 2023. I found no report that it was recovered or replaced, and the text says so. The Commons photos are from September 2021, so they still show all seven faces.

## Corrections to SCOUT.md

- **Rona Batho date**: the work was chosen in July 2019 but unveiled on 5 September 2021, by Halsema (nl.wikipedia, Stadscuratorium, NH Nieuws). I used 2021.
- **"Silence at 18:30"**: not found in any source. NH Nieuws (October 2026) says only "een minuut stilte", so the text says a minute's silence. The crash time was 18:35 (en.wikipedia).
- **Vogelparade**: the 2026 NH Nieuws piece on Ringmaster Amsterdam does not say where the contests are held now, so I didn't repeat the scout's claim that they take place near the Amsterdamse Poort. nl.wikipedia says the contests used to be held in the Bijlmerpark.
- **Iron Lady**: the scout's line about rainwater keeping escaped people alive is Kensmil's own account, and the text attributes it to her.

## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Death toll**: the official Dutch figure is 43, which NH Nieuws repeated in 2026 and the English and Dutch texts use. en.wikipedia now says at least 47 (all 4 on board plus 43 on the ground). Both texts say the true toll may be higher because undocumented residents were not counted, a point en.wikipedia also makes.
- **Flats hit**: NH Nieuws and en.wikipedia say Groeneveen and Klein-Kruitberg. Kunstwacht says "Groeneveen en Kruitberg". I used Klein-Kruitberg.
- **Achtergelaten bagage year**: Kunstwacht gives 1978, Stadscuratorium gives 1977. I used 1978 for `built`, and the English says "1977 or 1978".
- **Tayouken Piss year**: nl.wikipedia's infobox says 2008, while Kunstwacht and the 2009 Straat van Sculpturen opening say 2009. I used 2009. Whether the fountains run at the moment is unknown: they are often dry (pumps, frost, energy cost), and the text says so without claiming either state.
- **Time capsule**: Kunstwacht says the idea was bought in 1971. nl.wikipedia says the idea came up in 1970, when the city paid 750 guilders. The text gives "1970 or 1971". The "breath of the artist" detail comes only from nl.wikipedia and is marked "reportedly".
- **Iron Lady unveiling**: nl.wikipedia (citing the artist) gives 6 July 2024. The municipal text on Stadscuratorium says "June 6". I wrote "the summer of 2024".
- **Pa Sembrug**: Kunstwacht dates the bridge 2007, but nl.wikipedia (citing Korrel and the Gemeenteblad) says it was built in 1999 and named in 2017. I used 1999 and 2017.

## Route idea

**Memory walk through the Bijlmer (about 6 km, from Bijlmer ArenA to Ganzenhoef):**

1. Vogelparade
2. Anton de Kom monument
3. Rona Batho (Nelson Mandela memorial)
4. Tayouken Piss
5. Groeiend Monument and De Boom die Alles Zag
6. Monument voor de stad Amsterdam (time capsule)

Theme: how the Bijlmer remembers. It covers a resistance hero, Mandela's ubuntu, the 1992 disaster and a message to 2477. Extensions: the Iron Lady in the Brasapark to the south, and Achtergelaten bagage at the Gaasperplas terminus by metro.
