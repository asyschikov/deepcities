# Dutch pass, Oost batch 04

## Hooks

The NL text was written fresh for all 29 places, and most open on a new hook (the most surprising sourced fact) rather than following the EN order. Examples: Badhuis Javaplein opens on the 3% of homes with a shower; Katsenarie on the artist wanting his own work demolished; Leeuwen van Zeeburg on two pairs of lions becoming four identical ones; Lloyd Hotel on delousing before check-in; the Boerhaaveplein bathhouse on the alderman's equal-baths boast; the sewage pumping station on seventy years of untreated sewage into the IJ; Scheepstimmermanstraat on plots drawn by lot. The English was not reordered anywhere; it was only changed to fix claims.
