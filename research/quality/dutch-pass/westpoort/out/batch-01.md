# Dutch pass: Westpoort, batch 01

16 places done. Every place has a new Dutch summary and body, written fresh rather than translated. Where the English has a Look for list, the Dutch ends with "Let op:" and the same items. Heliport and Marbon have no Look for list, so they get no "Let op:".

- Every Dutch summary is 280 characters or less (the longest is 274).
- The copy checker finds 0 copied runs in all 16 places.
- There are no em dashes and no spaced en dashes in the Dutch.

REM-eiland had no draft, so its claims were checked against its live links. I also fetched en.wikipedia (REM Island, ADM, Amsterdam Heliport) through the API. The REM-eiland sources include rem.amsterdam/rooftop.
