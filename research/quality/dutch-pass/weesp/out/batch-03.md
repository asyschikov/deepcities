## For the user

- **Source conflicts:**
  - Binnenveer 2-6 is dated 1904 by the register and 1905 by DBNL. The text keeps 1904.
  - The Grote Kerk choir screen is c. 1525 in DBNL and c. 1530 in the register.
  - For the synagogue, the plaque says 29 April 1942. The JCK says all Jews had been expelled from Weesp by the end of 1941. The text quotes the plaque.
- **IVN schans leaflet errors:** It says the fort had 250 men, a 1922 restoration (after a 1976 purchase) and that Weesp was a fortress until 1947. hmdb gives 1959 for the end of the fortress. None of these claims was taken from the leaflet.
- **Theetuin opening hours:** IVN (2022) gives April-October, 10-17. theetuin.nl now says all year, 9-18, closed on Sundays and public holidays. The text follows theetuin.nl.
- **Binnenveer 11:** The Westerbork date was sourced in the end. The live text can keep it.
- **Live Moppenbakker text:** It had a 2025 "750" story with no source. If it is true, it needs a source before it goes back in.
- **hmdb.org:** curl cannot fetch it (Cloudflare). Read it in a browser.
