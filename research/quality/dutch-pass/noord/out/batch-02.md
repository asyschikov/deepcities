## For the user

- **Kadoelen stolp farms**: the second stolp (Kadoelenweg 306) was said to be 'also of 1908'; the city monuments API gives 1890. Body now says the register dates it to 1890 and the summary drops the shared year. Worth a look if another source says 1908.
- **Ransdorp bandstand / Durgerdam bandstand**: the art-register version ('the bands built the three themselves around 1922 under Cornelis Schreuder, with Amsterdam timber') merged two separate pages. Narrowed per bandstand: Ransdorp built by fanfare Wilhelmina under Schreuder with Amsterdam timber (no year); Durgerdam built early 1920s by Hou en Trou. Durgerdam's 'listed' attribute says 2004; the art register says it was recognised as a municipal monument in 2003 (attribute not changed).
- **Afbramerij, NDSM**: several claims had no source (position between the two original yards, route of the steel, blue doors, creative businesses). Narrowed to the register; current use sourced to the Hund Falk architects' page (high-tech on/offshore training centre). The 'files and chisels' definition rests on a search snippet about a different Afbramerij (Ulft).
- **De Wegwijzer school, Ransdorp**: 'two schools within a hundred metres, next to the public school of 1868' cut: no source found.
- **Noorder IJplas**: 'tens of millions' claim sourced only via a Parool search snippet (page behind a consent wall); 'not an official swimming spot' cut.
- **Durgerdam**, **Kadoelen**, **Buiksloterdijk**, **Sint-Augustinuskerk**, **Ransdorp tower**: the Dutch body runs 300 to 400 words because the English is long and the brief asks for the same facts; trimming would need the English shortened too.
