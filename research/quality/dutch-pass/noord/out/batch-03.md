# Noord batch 03: Dutch pass

23 places. Every claim in the EN and NL summary, body and Look for was checked against the place's links and draft sources. Sources used: RCE register pages, data.amsterdam.nl, nl.wikipedia via its API, DBNL, Amsterdam op de kaart, NH Nieuws, AT5, STRAAT, Schoonschip, Beeldenbos Noord and Staatsbosbeheer. The amsmon cache has empty text for rijksmonumenten, so I read those on the RCE pages instead.

Claim changes: 3 sourced (added to sources_add), 44 narrowed, 10 cut, 1 reordered. Copy checker: 0 copied runs in 23/23 places.

## Sourced (sources_add)
- Witte Kerkje: en.wikipedia Holysloot backs the chapel by 1342, the parish by 1520, the smallest graveyard in North Holland and burials inside the church until 1841.
- Krijtmolen d'Admiraal: nl.wikipedia Aart_Staartjes gives Nieuwendam as his birthplace.
- Schoonschip: Schoonschip's own news post backs the European Solar Award 2019.

  - Cut "last trass mill in the world still worked by wind": the sources say only last of its type, or last in the Netherlands.
  - Cut the line about mortar that sets under water, and the National Mill Days.
  - Narrowed: "brick-and-timber shed" is now "shed"; "volunteer millers" is now "a volunteer miller".
- **Villa De Volewijck**:
  - Cut "the first of them": DBNL dates nos. 21 and 25 to 1912-13.
  - Cut Rutgers' career and the "Amsterdam School motifs".

  - Cut the post-war boom and riveting-to-welding story, which no source has.
  - Narrowed: the columns are "welded and bolted" per the register, not riveted.
  - Narrowed: "the last ships of the yard were welded here" is now "Aarding was the last shipbuilder here".
  - Narrowed: STRAAT's date and the yearly changes of the murals are gone.
- **'t Tolhuis**, two factual errors fixed:
  - The trekvaart went to Broek in Waterland, Monnickendam and Edam, not Purmerend.
  - The concerts began around 1870, after the Vondelpark (1865), not before it. The summary was fixed to match.
  - Narrowed: 1919 was only a plan for a volkskoffiehuis.
- **Doctor's house**:
  - Narrowed: "the only villas" is now "villa-like houses".
  - Narrowed: 1922 is now 1924-1925.
  - Narrowed: the "teachers' houses" label is now hedged, as the register does.
- **Vliegenbos**:
  - Narrowed: elm richness is no longer linked to regrowth from the stumps, and the regrowth is "partly", not "most".
  - Narrowed: the Raad van State struck down one zoning plan, not two. Its pages were blocked, so the second ruling is unconfirmed.
  - Narrowed: Bregman "was to unveil" the gateway; only the announcement was found.
  - Narrowed: Zosenerdam is no longer "drowned medieval".
- **Monnikendammerplantsoen**: Look for item fixed. The wall and railing are behind Monnikendammerplantsoen 21-28, on the Volendammerweg, not at Volendammerweg 26-40.
- **Kobra**:

## Verification

All 23 places were checked. Each claim in the new Dutch was compared with the new English and the original texts, and the facts were compared between the two languages. No change has kind "sourced". The three sources_add entries were fetched and confirmed:
- Witte Kerkje: en.wikipedia Holysloot (API) says the chapel existed by 1342 with services by the pastor of Ransdorp, the parish dates from 1520, the graveyard is "the smallest graveyard in North Holland" and burials took place within the church walls until 1841.
- Krijtmolen: nl.wikipedia Aart_Staartjes (API) says "Aart Staartjes (Nieuwendam, 1 maart 1938 ...)".
- Schoonschip: schoonschipamsterdam.org, "Posted 24 november 2019": "Schoonschip heeft een European Solar Award gewonnen!"

The copy checker still reports 0 copied runs in 23/23 places after the fixes. There are no em dashes and no spaced en dashes. Every Look for list matches its Let op list. The Police post and Villa De Volewijck have neither. Every NL and EN summary is at most 280 characters.
