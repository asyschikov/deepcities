# Dutch pass: West, batch 01

27 places done. Every place has a new Dutch summary and body, written fresh rather than translated. Each body opens with its most surprising sourced fact and, where the English has Look for items, ends with "Let op:" and the same items. Every Dutch summary is under 280 characters. The copy checker finds 0 copied runs in all 27 places (8-word window, against all fetched sources). There are no em dashes and no spaced en dashes in the Dutch.

- nl.wikipedia: Machineslootbrug (railings broken vs continuous), GAK-gebouw ("Het aquarium", Elling and Bodon, 2012 to 2020) and Gemeenschappelijk Administratiekantoor
