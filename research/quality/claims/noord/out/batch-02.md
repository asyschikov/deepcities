## For the user

- Botel: ArchDaily body text does not load without a browser; the 2011 / 6.5 m / funnels / Histoire d'O claims are sourced from e-architect.com instead (added to sources). e-architect spells the O designer "Ronald Hoofd (&Prast&Hooft)"; live text keeps "Ronald Hooft".
- Hollandia-Kattenburg: 4en5mei.nl gives Cephas Stauthamer as 1899-1983, yet the column was chosen and unveiled in 1985 (herdenking site: "Het sobere ontwerp van Cevas Stauthamer werd uitgekozen"). Possibly the 1946 plaques are his and the column a later setting, or the dates are wrong. Left as is; worth a look.
- Zilveren Manen: attribute built changed from 2013 (no source; only a 2013 Commons photo) to 2000–2013, the rebuild period; the Tjep sculpture is 2011 per the Stadscuratorium advice, not 2013. Removal of the sculpture is sourced only as planned for March 2026; no source confirms it was carried out.
- Iepenarboretum: the quote "the Italian poplar among elms" belonged to the three transplanted 'Sarniensis' elms, not 'Columella'; moved.
- Gijs Frieling cabinets: the tenure mix (owner-occupied, social rent, business space) came from the IJside entry in the same NUL20 archive inventory, not De Albatros; cut.
- Johan van Hasseltkanaal: the Schoonschip / De Ceuvel sentence was cut only for lack of a listed source; a source would restore it.
- Finse school: nl.wikipedia says the schools were built in NL from Finnish timber (designed by the Laurentius brothers, built by Panagro), about 115 in all, not prefab schools shipped from Finland; text reworded.
- Kitchen houses: listed 1997 confirmed by the city API (datumAanwijzing 1997-10-21; RCE register entry 1998).
