## For the user
- **Liberty Tree, Bos en Lommerweg: the stone is in Dutch, not English.** 4en5mei.nl (monument 1496) and Buitenkunst both give "1940-1945 VRIJHEIDSBOOM TER HERINNERING 5 MEI GEPLANT DOOR R. SAUNDERS 1E LTN. R.A.F. OP 27 JUNI 1945. DE BUURTBEWONERS". The live English text (from TracesOfWar, now behind a bot wall) looks like a translation. The place's hook ("English-language stone", "mix of Dutch and English") is gone; the text now uses the Dutch inscription with a translation. 4en5mei also says the stone was unveiled in August 1973 and the tree is a lime; Buitenkunst gives 1957 for the stone. Both are left out. Worth a look on site.
- **Polder houses, Jan Hanzenstraat:** several details (derelict by 2000, the cultural-history survey, 30 cm higher, four flats round a patio) rest on the Parool article, which now sits behind a DPG consent wall and could not be re-read. They are kept, not verified. The 2003 steel-frame plan is now sourced to Rowin Petersma's site.
- **Hotel Not Hotel:** the live link to nl.wikipedia "Piri_Re%C3%AFsplein" is a 404; the article is at https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piri_Reisplein. Fix the link.
- **Jan Evertsenstraat:** the live text put the "two blocks joined into one long volume" gateway over the Hudsonstraat. In the register that is block C (gateway to the John Franklinstraat, north wall); the Hudsonstraat gateway is block F (south wall). Corrected.
- **Cabotstraat:** the middle school was called a "middle school"; the register says K.O. (nursery). original_use changed to "Primary and nursery schools".
- **Former post office:** built changed from "c. 1915" to "1915–1920" (city API; the register text itself gives no date).
- **Troost:** brouwerijtroost.nl returns 403 to the research user agent; it was read once with a browser user agent via curl (hours, Saturday tour, "grootste onafhankelijke brouwerij" as the brewery's own claim).
- **Banksy Museum:** the museum site refuses scripted access, so closing times and timed tickets are not verified; hours narrowed to "from 10:00". AmsterdamTips says many pieces are certified by Pest Control, so "most of them reconstructions" was narrowed.
- **Streetart Frankey:** Zorro, Rocky and one Meneer de Uil page return 403; Zorro's status comes from the "still in place nearby" lists on the Guard Frankey and Bingo pages. Years 2024 for Zorro and Guard Frankey were unsourced and cut.
- Copied Dutch was also rewritten where it matched register or survey texts the checker cannot see (RCE texts via SPARQL for Cabotstraat, Jan Evertsenstraat and Vespuccistraat; the DANS building survey for Highlights of Holland).
