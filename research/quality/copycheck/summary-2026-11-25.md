# Copied Dutch text on Deep Amsterdam (`sights`)

Measured 2026-11-24 on all 1347 published places (every borough on the map). Read-only: nothing was written to the site or the repo. Per-place detail is in `results.json` (same folder).

## Method

- NL text = `translations.nl.summary` + `translations.nl.body` (markdown stripped, list items counted as sentences, sentences under 4 words ignored).
- Sources per place: every URL in `links`; for every number in `attributes.monument_number` the Amsterdam monumenten API (all text fields) plus the RCE register page when the number is not a municipal one; plus `sources:`/`links` from the research YAML/JSON (`research/*/places/*.yaml`, `slices`, `rewrite`, from both the documents and current worktrees), matched by title (1346 of 1347 matched; only "REM-eiland" had no research entry). Wikidata ids were mapped to their nl.wikipedia article. en/de Wikipedia, Commons and image links skipped (not Dutch).
- nl.wikipedia: the extracts API was rate-limited (HTTP 429) for this User-Agent, so articles were fetched as wikitext via `prop=revisions` in batches of 50 and converted to plain text (templates, refs, tables removed).
- buitenkunstamsterdam.nl and amsterdam.kunstwacht.nl are JavaScript apps (curl gets an empty shell), so their texts were read from the site's own public JSON API (`api-v3.kunstwacht.nl`, with the token the page embeds).
- A sentence counts as copied if it shares a run of 8+ consecutive words (lowercased, punctuation stripped) with any source, and the run has at least 3 lowercase non-numeric words other than name particles (van, de, der, het...), so pure names, addresses and dates do not count.
- Bands are exclusive: `>50%` copied share; else `3+ or >25%`; else `1-2`; else `0`.

## Sources that could not be fetched

- No source text at all: 1 place, **Cabotstraat school island** (West). Its monument numbers 527139-527142 are unknown to both the RCE register (404) and the Amsterdam API (empty); the same holds for 527130-527133 (Vespuccistraat housing by Roodenburgh) and 527144-527149 (Jan Evertsenstraat housing by J.F. Staal). These numbers may be wrong or not yet registered.
- 252 places had at least one failed source (406 failures, listed per place in `sources_failed`). Main causes: RCE 404 on numbers that are not rijksmonumenten (82), monument numbers absent from the Amsterdam API, mostly Weesp (74), parool.nl paywall/blocked (42), gebouwdin.amsterdam.nl (36), tracesofwar (24), amsterdam.nl (15), hmdb.org (8), 5 nl.wikipedia titles that do not exist.
- Places where half or more of the sources failed, so the score may be understated: Bakemabuurt, Self-build row Overtoomse Veld (Nieuw-West); Adcock station, Kraanspoor (Noord); Anna's Tuin & Ruigte, Flowers by Karel Appel (Oost); Banksy Museum Amsterdam, Liberty Tree Bos en Lommerweg, Silodam, Zeeslagenkaart (West); Marie Heinekenplein, Modern girl with a pearl, The pooping cat (Zuid); Bank swallow wall Hemweg (Westpoort). Most Weesp entries in this situation only lack an Amsterdam API record; their RCE text was fetched.

## Validation

- **Gerardus Majellakerk and its Catholic enclave (NedPhO-Koepel)**: 13 of 21 sentences copied (62%), 10 from nl.wikipedia (runs of 12 to 33 words, e.g. the 1992 closure and Stadsherstel sentence, the 1930 roofer murder) and 3 from RCE 523303. Read by hand: the other 8 sentences are reworded or new. Matches expectation.
- **Grote man, kleine man (Stephan Balkenhol)**: 0 of 19. Rechecked at 4-word granularity: the longest overlap with any source is 6 words (a date-and-name phrase). Clean.
- **Blauwe Moskee, Henri Dunantstraat**: 0 of 16. At 4-word granularity the longest overlap is 5 words. Clean.
- Random sample of 1-2 band hits read by hand: genuine shared phrases or quoted inscriptions, no boilerplate false positives.

Limits: a translated or closely paraphrased sentence with no identical 8-word run is not caught, so these numbers are a floor. Wikipedia was compared as converted wikitext, which can split a few runs at link or template boundaries.
