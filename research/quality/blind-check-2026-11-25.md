# Blind check 2026-11-25: fair re-run

Why: the 2026-11-23 check had West best (23.7), the 2026-11-24 check had West worst (17.5).
The second sampled all of West against only the newest rounds elsewhere, used 8 per group,
and its reviewer scored copied Dutch far more harshly than the first.

Method: 10 random places from each whole district (any round, seed 20261125) plus 6 West
anchors from the two earlier checks, shuffled and anonymised; two independent reviewers;
anchored rubric (`rubric.md`), Dutch measured as sentences sharing 8+ consecutive words with
a source. Reports: `blind-check-2026-11-25-A.md`, `-B.md`.

Reviewers agree: correlation 0.92 on totals (0.94 on Dutch), mean difference 0.7 points.
Copied sentences in the NL (reviewer B): Oost 99, West 75, Noord 58, Nieuw-West 55 per 10
places. Anchors scored 20 to 29, against 17.5 for some of the same places on 2026-11-24.

Conclusions: (1) a single reviewer without anchors is not reliable; the 2026-11-24 result
was an outlier. (2) No district is clearly behind: West and Oost about 2 points below Noord and
Nieuw-West. (3) The one shared weakness is Dutch copied from sources, in every district and
every round.
