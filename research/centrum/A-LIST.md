# Centrum: A-list sights, parked

Deep Amsterdam is the city off the A-list, and Centrum is where the A-list lives. Same rule
as Zuid (`research/zuid/A-LIST.md`, the user's decision of 2026-11-24): list them, don't
prepare them for import.

## The rule

- An **A-list sight** is one that any general guidebook's top list of Amsterdam carries and
  that visitors come to Centrum for. It gets no place on the map; its monument records are
  parked in the ledger.
- A **separately standing or separately listed thing beside it** that most visitors walk
  past may be a place, if it has a hook of its own and is pitched on that (a side door, a
  stone in the pavement, a neighbour, a detail on the back).
- The **canal belt, the Jordaan, the Red Light District** as wholes are A-list as
  *concepts*: no place called "Herengracht" or "De Wallen". Individual houses, alleys,
  hofjes and stories inside them are exactly what this map is for.
- Second-tier sights that guidebooks mention in passing (most hofjes, the smaller museums,
  the Western Islands, the Plantage, most churches) are **not** A-list: they go on the map,
  pitched on what the guidebook leaves out.
- A-list sights may be named in other places' texts as landmarks.
- In doubt: put it under "Candidates" below with a reason, not on the map.
