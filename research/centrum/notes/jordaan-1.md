## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Fort van Sjako**: the city register text (213059) repeats that Jaco hid in the "fort" here
  and was beheaded on the Nieuwmarkt on 6 August 1718. nl.wikipedia (citing Spierenburg's
  review of Frans Thuijs, 2007) calls the fort a myth created by Justus van Maurik in 1901,
  and has Muller broken on the wheel. The draft follows Wikipedia/Thuijs and leaves out the
  manner of execution.
- **Papeneiland name**: the Commons description of `Prinsengracht2.jpg` says the name refers to
  a Catholic hidden church in this block until 1860. Single, weak source, left out. A
  stronger source would add the obvious hook (worth a check).
- **Sint Andrieshofje dates**: hofjesinamsterdam.nl gives both "founded 1613" and "will of 1614";
  nl.wikipedia (Jordaan) says 1614. Draft uses the will of 1614 and opening in 1617.
- **Bloemstraat 191 listing year**: nl.wikipedia says 11 December 2011; slice data says 2001
  (518xxx numbers belong to the 2001 round). Draft uses 2001. Current use of no. 191 not
  found; text says only that it can be seen from the street.
- **Rijckerbrug sculpture**: nl.wikipedia (Rijckerbrug) describes Frans Werner's relief as a
  fire-breathing dragon with a fireman; the Commons caption of `Brug167-3a.jpg` says
  "Joris en de draak". Draft follows Wikipedia. `check: Facts` set for the Oranje Loper
  works and the rail stub.
- **Bloemgrachtkerk**: outlook "Under threat" rests on nl.wikipedia citing Quote (January 2026);
  current state not checked on site.
- **Hoppe**: the register calls Hoppe a wine merchant "later known mainly as distillery and
  liqueur maker". No link to Café Hoppe on the Spui is claimed.
- **Hofje access**: jordaanweb says not all Jordaan hofjes admit visitors; no opening times
  were found for Sint Andrieshofje, Claes Claesz. Hofje or Venetiae, so none are given.
- **De Voorzienigheid**: current use of the three buildings not found; no photo verified (the
  Commons hit `Voorgevel - Amsterdam - 20016721 - RCE.jpg` is captioned only "voorgevel").
- Van Houten group spans two clusters: the Laurier/Rozendwarsstraat houses (pin) and two
  outliers (Anjeliersstraat 23, Egelantiersgracht 191–195) named in the Look for list.
  Coordinator may prefer to split the outliers off.

  at the Prinsengracht (nl.wikipedia Elandsgracht).
- Stencil art of dancing women, Elandsgracht (Commons photo by Fons Heijnsbroek, 2014): check if still there.
- Oranje Loper emergency-bridge artworks: Constructief bloemmotief (Gerda Kruimer, bridge 117),
  Kreukel (Karin Colen, bridge 167), Grachtenvlinder (Sam Hersbach, bridge 63); probably gone
  with the temporary bridges.
- Monument Jordaanoproer (Sofie Hupkens, 1987) at the Noorderkerk.

  national monument (city register 200534).
- Concordia Noord court, Westerstraat 221–289 (with Anjeliersstraat 364–394 behind it): a
  philanthropic court by P.J. Hamer with pumps, gas lamps and bleaching fields (hofjesinamsterdam.nl).
- Bloemgracht 24 plaque: night shelter for women and children 1904–1945 (nl.wikipedia Bloemgracht).

   bridges → Bridge 102 → Rozengracht bridges and the tram to Zandvoort → Bullebak → Papeneiland

   house → Hoppe's wine house → Papeneiland.
