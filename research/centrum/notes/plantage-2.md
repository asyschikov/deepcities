## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Hugo de Vries**: the city register calls him a "Nobelprijswinnaar". He never won the Nobel
  Prize; left out. nl.wikipedia says the laboratory was given to him when he might leave for
  Columbia University; single source, left out. The register's claim that "many Jewish
  children" rescued from the Hollandsche Schouwburg hid in Plantage Parklaan 9 rests on the
  register alone; kept, as the register is the official text, but worth a second source
  (joodsamsterdam.nl could not be searched this run). The gable stone with Hebrew letters was
  hidden behind a firm's sign in 1996; whether it is visible now is unknown, so it is not a
  Look-for line.
- **Desmet**: the register gives the 1926–1927 front to J. Boterenbrood; nl.wikipedia says
  J.F. Staal led the rebuild and the theatre reopened in September 1930. The text follows the
  register for the front and gives no reopening date. The register calls Prot a former scene
  builder of the old Frascati, Wikipedia a singer who came with the French opera; the text says
  "a theatre man from the old Frascati".
- **Hubertushuis**: the national register (listed 5 June 2026) calls it a "former" home; current
  use unconfirmed, flagged `check: Facts`. Kriterion's article gives about a thousand children
  rescued from the crèche; other sources (plantage-1) say about six hundred. No number used.
- **Joods Lyceum**: the city register text for no. 2 (200628) says the old building had been
  demolished for new building; nl.wikipedia (Voormalige Stadstimmertuin 2) shows the 1901
  building stands, converted to flats in 2017. The register passage evidently describes the
  Maimonides move, not this building. Wikipedia gives the Lyceum at no. 1 as 1941–1942 in one
  article and to September 1943 in the other; the text says it stopped in 1943 (register: 1941–1943).
- **Joodsche Invalide**: the register dates Elte's first building to 1915, nl.wikipedia to
  1924–1925. The text gives no date for Elte's wing. Plaque location: register "next to the
  entrance on the Weesperplein", Wikipedia "in the front of Weesperplein 3"; text says "by the
  entrance on the Weesperplein". The register calls it a GG&GD building since 1979; current
  tenant not re-checked.
- **Kweekschool voor Machinisten**: the register says the boarding house ended in 1895 and the
  school went into the MTS; Wikipedia says the MTS took it over in 1910. Neither date used. The
  register text names the second stone both 1886 and 1887; the text says "the year of this building".
- **Sint Jacob**: the surviving chapel wing stands inside the block; flagged `check: Location`
  for whether the Barbara statue can be seen from the street.
- **Weesperflat**: the El Salvador mural is from nl.wikipedia without a date; flagged
  `check: Still there?`. Left out the residents' café De Doos (no current source).
- **Amstel Hotel**: dropped a terrace Look-for line that had no source. Celebrity guests and the
  drug baron left out (padding).
- **Dr. Sarphatihuis**: "nursing home" rests on nl.wikipedia (present tense); the Amsta site
  blocked curl. If Amsta has plans to close or rebuild it, an `outlook` is needed.
- **Hollandsche Schouwburg** (532241) parked as A-list; Eik en Linde and the Hubertushuis pitch
  their own hooks beside it.

## Leads

- **Panorama building** (1880–1935, Gosschalk), on the site of the Plantage Westermanlaan
  gardens; gone, but the plantsoen holds Carel Kneulman's **Monument voor het
  kunstenaarsverzet 1940–1945** (1973). nl.wikipedia "Plantage Middenlaan".
- **Stolperstein for Henriëtte Pimentel**, Plantage Middenlaan 31 (head of the crèche).
  Same article.
- **Stadsdrukkerij**, Voormalige Stadstimmertuin 4–6 (c. 1915), old harbour bollards reused as
  stair newels; now a flex office with a public ground floor. nl.wikipedia "Voormalige
  Stadstimmertuin".
- **Centrale Schoolkinderbad**, Voormalige Stadstimmertuin 9 (1908), bath house for the poorest
  schoolchildren, tile panel on the front. Same article.
- **Peel Plaza** by Gabriel Lester (2022), Weesperplein side of the Diamantbeurs.
  nl.wikipedia "Diamantbeurs (Amsterdam)".
- **Muiderpoort outer gate** rebuilt at the Flevopark entrance (Oost), 1938.
- **Mural El Salvador** on the Weesperflat (street-art scouts): nl.wikipedia "Muurschildering
  El Salvador".
- **Plaque for the first Eik en Linde** on the wolves' house inside Artis (eikenlinde.nl).
