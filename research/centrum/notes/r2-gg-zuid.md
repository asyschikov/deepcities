## Conflicts and uncertainties

- Vijzelflat collapse date: VVAB Binnenstad 178 says 23 March 1955, the theobakker source used in gg-zuid-3 says 3 March 1955. Not used in my text; gg-zuid-3 says 3 March and may need checking.
- Poentunnel bricking-up: nl.wikipedia says the north side was bricked up after 1999; onh.nl (2024, quoting the architecture historian) says 2007, on the De Bazel side, against unwanted visitors to the Stadsarchief. I used onh.nl. No opening after Open Monumentendag 2024 found.
- Poentunnel name: "possibly" after the Coentunnel (nl.wikipedia); written as "perhaps".
- Reguliersgracht 34 number of hiders: 4en5mei says twelve permanent hiders; Böhl says the Kahns plus 7 to 9 others. The relief has twelve faces; I used twelve.
- The Unica pun in the motto: the motto contains the word "unica", the society's name; I say it "carries the society's name", not that it was meant as a pun.
- Krullen count: city open data (Nov 2026) has 39, nl.wikipedia says 37 in 2024, Gerritsen 37 (2024). I used the city's 39 with "the city's own count".
- Krul plasrecht case: nl.wikipedia dates it 2017; scout CE said 2015. Used 2017.
- Kerkstraat 404 is not a listed monument (402, 406, 408 are).
- Herengracht 524 "snijraamhek": the register's fanlight grille, not a railing gate as first drafted; fixed.
- Stichting 1940-1945: the betrayer (the foundation's legal adviser, named in 4en5mei) is not named in the text.
- Hans Brinker: no Dutch second source; the campaign facts rest on Arcam plus KesselsKramer's own portfolio title and an English travel blog (2025) for current status.
- Ranks were not added to `scripts/data/amsterdam-c-list-ranks.json` (outside this slice's allowed files).

## Route ideas

- "Borrowed tops": Prinsengracht 755, then Van Houten's recycled gables (Keizersgracht 826), Amstelstraat 49 (the tritons' first home, top from the Haarlemmer Houttuinen since 2003), on to the Jordaan for Bloemgracht 19 (top no. 1 of the monuments office, from Kalverstraat 222, placed 1980), Bloemgracht 73 (1976 rebuild with a top from Waterlooplein 94) and the Rozenstraat row.
- "Krul walk" through Grachtengordel-Zuid: Nieuwe Spiegelgracht, Utrechtsestraat, Amstelveld, Lijnbaansgracht 282; or a Centrum-wide one ending at the Oude Kerk krul with the two figures.
- "Hidden in the canal belt, 1943-1945": Reguliersgracht 34, Keizersgracht 567, Keizersgrachtkerk and its Kerkstraat stone, the Lippmann Rosenthal bank, the Carlton crash site.
- "Money under the canals": Poentunnel, De Bazel, the Vijzelbank over the Kerkstraat, the Carlton/Spaarbank site.
