## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **De Gecroonde Raep**: the national register swaps the two records relative to the addresses (6042 is the canal front, 84 the back house). The back house extension is dated 1635 in the register and 1633 on nl.wikipedia (date stones 1615 and 1633). The text says "the 1630s" and does not quote the stone dates.
- **Krasnapolsky power station**: the register calls the 1883 plant "the first in Amsterdam". Industrial-heritage sources treat Electra's 1892 central station as the first large-scale public one. The text attributes the claim to the register.
- **Nuns of the Warmoesstraat**: the city register says the buildings were joined into a convent in 1983. nl.wikipedia (after Lucas, Blaauwlakenblok) and the Amsterdam Museum have the sisters there from 1952 until 1 February 2010. The text gives both: shelter from 1952, buildings rebuilt as convent in 1983. Current use (supermarket below, flats d'Augustijn above) rests on a search result and a 2015 Parool piece about the supermarket.
- **'t Aepgen**: the sailors-paid-with-monkeys story is told as unproven; nl.wikipedia finds no evidence the house was ever an inn.
- **Schreierstoren**: the weeping-wives origin is presented as a legend; the name comes from schray (sharp corner).
- **Vredenburgh**: the 2011 niche figure by Joris Baudoin is not confirmed as still in place: `check: Still there?`.
- **Agnietenkapel**: heritage status set to "National + municipal monument" because 214042 is municipal; current status was checked on uva.nl.
- **Rokin 85**: the city register notes that a supposed Beckmann inscription was not found, so the text mentions none.
- **Left out**: a Chet Baker link for Prins Hendrikkade 49-51 (the plaque is at no. 53, outside the slice); OZV 18 builder-copy story mentioned only as a look-alike in the Gecroonde Raep text.

## Route ideas

- **Rokin quiet side**: De Roos (Rokin 9-15), Marine Insurance (69), Beckmann (85), Rokin 93 and 101 tobacco offices, Rokin 99, Krop at 115; back along the Nes past Nes 104 and Nes 67 to the Brakke Grond gate and the Vleeshal.
- **North tip of the old town**: Schreierstoren, Gebouw Batavia, Vettewinkel façade on the Oudezijds Kolk, Nicolaasbasiliek, 't Aepgen, Sint-Olofskapel, Vredenburgh, Wapen van Riga, Gecroonde Raep (both fronts).
- **Warmoesstraat**: Krasnapolsky power station, the nuns' convent, dike shopfront at 101, infant school at 71; out via the Oude Hoogstraat skull gate to the Walloon Church.
- **University quarter**: Spinhuis gate, Agnietenkapel, Huis aan de Drie Grachten, Prinsenhof and the Krop urinal, OZV 131 stone, Damstraat monkey.
