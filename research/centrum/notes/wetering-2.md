## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Search budget**: the shared WebSearch budget ran out early in this slice. Current-status checks therefore rest on nl.wikipedia (with 2023-2026 references), the places' own sites (Stadsherstel, deduif.net, parking listings) and the registers. Records not drafted were checked against the register texts and, where an article exists, nl.wikipedia; several "no story found" entries had no extra web search.
- **Hotel American**: is it A-list? Not in A-LIST.md; drafted, pitched on Kromhout's tower, the 1928 wing and Mata Hari. Move to Candidates if the user thinks it is guidebook top-list. Mata Hari's wedding night (1895) was in the first hotel, not the present building; nl.wikipedia's claim that the room is still let as the "Mata Hari suite" is contradictory and left out. The reading table as "oldest in Amsterdam" rests on nl.wikipedia only; text says "what is called". The 1990 murder of Roy Adkins in the hotel is left out.
- **Paleis van Justitie architect**: the national register credits I. Warnsinck and J.G.J. van Gendt (1804-1825); nl.wikipedia (from the former bMA text) credits city architect Jan de Greef (1825-1829). Text follows nl.wikipedia; the register is in the sources. Founding date of the orphanage: register says 1633-1665, wiki 1663-1665; text uses 1663-1665 for the building.
- **Stadsbestedelingen (202043)**: the register calls the 1902 part "the orphanage". The English text says "home for children in its care"; exact function of the Inrichting voor Stadsbestedelingen not researched further.
- **Maison Stroucken**: the city register (2004) gives "vermoedelijk" Ed. Cuypers and says the ground floor was used by the neighbouring cinema. nl.wikipedia (DeLaMar, citing Norbruis 2025) says the 1881 Cuypers front was reconstructed within the glass front of the 2010 theatre. Whether the winged dog came back is unconfirmed: `check: Facts`. Numbers of hostages shot on 6 January 1945 are unclear in the nl.wikipedia article ("vijf willekeurige politieke gevangenen en 20 ambtenaren"); left out. The memorial itself is not in this slice (lead).
- **Kaiser Wilhelm Schule**: the only source is the city register (2006), which also names the Eerste Montessorischool as user. The 1e Montessorischool De Wielewaal is in the Corellistraat (Joods Monument), so the current school here is unconfirmed: `check: Still there?`.
- **Foresters**: TracesOfWar has a "Forestershuis Amsterdam" built 1936-1937 for the AOF, used by the Luftgaukommando Holland in WWII, damaged, restored to 1952. The page refused automated reading (403), so address and wartime use are unconfirmed and left out. Current use (clinic per 2004 register) unconfirmed: `check: Still there?`. "Friendly society of British origin" is general knowledge, not from a read source.
- **Rath & Doodeheefver**: register gives 1911 and six storeys for the first phase; nl.wikipedia says 1912 and seven storeys. Text uses 1911 and "six-storey" in the summary. Current use of the complex not found; text only says it is seen from the street.
- **Nicolaas Witsenkade 38**: nl.wikipedia says the gasworks stood here "from the mid-18th century", which cannot be right for a coal-gas works; no start date used. Its later use by "Sociëteit Eldorado and club Elegance" (1983-2008) left out.
- **Looiershofje**: no founder or name source found beyond the national register; Urban Oases (TU Delft) lists it as 1828. Text gives only register facts. Whether the courtyard can be entered is unknown; text says seen from the street.
- **Hilmanhofje**: register (2006) says "begeleid wonen" with 14 homes; current use not rechecked. "Jan Goddome" glossed only as "after a curse".
- **Prinsengracht 526-528 (Hendrix)**: contractor, co-founder and chairman (1903-1919) of the Nederlandse Aannemersbond, councillor and later senator, built this for himself; first stone probably with his son's name. Filed as no story; could become an Okay place if the user wants contractor-politicians.
- **Amsterdamse Binnenstad, onsamsterdam**: not used (fetches failed or robots).
