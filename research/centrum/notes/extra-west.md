## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Gebouw Helios dates.** Register: built 1895–1897, small extension 1899. Amsterdam op de kaart (newspaper timeline): rebuilding of 15-17 tendered 1895, demolition of 15-19 and new building tendered June 1899, "new building" announced September 1900; nl.wikipedia (Büttinghausen) also has demolition and new build in 1900. Text avoids a build year in the body; `built` is 1895–1900.
- **Drie Fleschjes age.** Amsterdam op de kaart and nl.wikipedia say the tavern opened in 1650; the tasting room's own site says "anno 1619". Text gives no year. The hotel at no. 14 was a Best Western in 2019; its current name is not given.
- **Telegraaf building dates.** Register 1928–1930, nl.wikipedia (Nieuwezijds) "verrees in 1928", De Telegraaf article: building started 1926, in use 1930. Text: 1928–1930. Current user Guerrilla Games (since 2021) from Amsterdam op de kaart, last edited April 2026.
- **Kas-Vereeniging moving date.** Register and aodk 1914, nl.wikipedia (KAS BANK) 1915. Text: 1914.
- **Kas-Associatie interior.** The elephant clock by Lion Cachet, the Mercury statue and the De Distel tile panel are from the 2001 register text; not confirmed in the hall since it became The Duchess. Hence `check: Facts`. The Duchess was taking bookings in November 2026.
- **'t Binnenhuis.** Register: built for and financed by De Utrecht, shop 1907–1923; nl.wikipedia: built for N.V. 't Binnenhuis, financed by Carel Henny, left in 1922. Text follows the register for the client (De Utrecht) and wikipedia for 1922 (a piano dealer came in 1923 per aodk). Current fashion shop per nl.wikipedia, undated.
- **Raadhuisstraat gallery.** Register: completed 1898 per the stones; aodk 1896–1899; nl.wikipedia 1899 and "designed by Van Gendt's sons, not by himself". Text: stones date it to 1898, architect "the Van Gendt firm".
- **Hana homes.** Register 1852–1853, aodk 1853–1854; text 1853.
- **Koning Willemshuis.** The register names the preacher "Adama van Scholten"; aodk has Adama van Scheltema, the well-known temperance preacher of the Westerkerk. Text uses Van Scheltema.
- **Breitner's studio.** Register: first purpose-built studio-home in Amsterdam and probably in the Netherlands; aodk disputes it (Stadhouderskade, 1871). Text gives both. Current use (homes?) not confirmed; text says private.
- **Kettner.** Current tenant unknown and whether the name in the ridge railing survives: `check: Facts`.
- **Stähle bakery.** aodk (2023) still has bakery Egstorf; egstorf.nl did not answer curl. `check: Still there?`.
- **Singel 340.** Empty since about 2020, plans for offices (aodk, June 2025): `outlook: Major change ahead`, no date known.
- **Assurantiehuis.** aodk says the Arion medallion is "probably older" and by Rien Hack; one weak source, so the maker is left out. The fraud bank's name is the directors' surnames; left out (private persons). Whether "HET ASSURANTIEHUIS" is still on the frieze is unclear (aodk past tense), so it is not in Look for.
- **Lloyd building.** Hotel since 2013 (aodk); hotel name not checked. The stained-glass window in the hall is in the register but left out (not confirmed visible to the public).
- **VU.** aodk: main building until the move to Buitenveldert in 1966; register: sold in 1968. Text: "in the 1960s".
- Damrak 37-38 had a fair detail (an exchange office whose beer cellar was armoured so it could become a vault; monkeys at the Oudebrugsteeg gate) but not enough for a place; left as no story.

## Leads

- Proeflokaal De Drie Fleschjes, Gravenstraat 18: oldest tasting room, drinks organ of 50 casks, mayors' bottles; open Mon–Sat 14.00–20.30 (https://www.dedriefleschjes.nl/). A food-drink place in its own right for the scouts; referred to in the distillery text.
- De Telegraaf printed its first issue in 1893 in the Gravenstraat (no. 10), per aodk; Sint Nicolaasstraat 37 was a later address.
- Statue "Argus" (Bommel strip journalist) by Saske van der Eerden, placed June 2022 on the Nieuwezijds in memory of the newspaper street (nl.wikipedia Nieuwezijds Voorburgwal); exact spot not checked.
- Hildo Krop's original newsboys from the Telegraaf spire and the Telegraaf gate are at the Basisweg (outside Centrum).
- Krop's entrance from the Herengracht side of the telephone exchange was moved to the PTT building, Kamerlingh Onneslaan 1 (outside Centrum).
- Memorial stone of 1946 by the residents for Louise Went ("Gedenksteen uit waardering voor mevrouw L. van der Pek-Went"), Bouwonderneming Jordaan blocks; location on the blocks not found. Worth a look on site.
- Herengracht 326 and 344, Keizersgracht 374 garden houses: candidates for the Open Tuinen Dagen line in the gg-west-4 garden place.

## Route ideas

- **Newspaper street and money street (Nieuwe Zijde):** Telegraaf building, Nieuwezijds Voorburgwal 225 → Kas-Associatie hall (The Duchess), Spuistraat 172 → Associatie Cassa, Herengracht 179 → Raadhuisstraat gallery and 't Binnenhuis → Assurantiehuis, Keizersgracht 213. The KAS BANK thread ties the first three together.
- **Spui and Heiligeweg loop:** Helios, Spui 15 → Kettner, Heiligeweg 21-25 → Stähle bakery, Spuistraat 274 (buy something) → Telegraaf.
- **Housing experiments of the 19th century:** Hana's model homes (1853, Passeerdersdwarsstraat) → Bouwonderneming Jordaan (1896, Lindengracht) → Planciusblok (1856, haarlemmer-2) → Breitner's studio on the Prinseneiland on the way. Koning Willemshuis fits as the "civilising" side of the same story.
- **Behind the Nieuwe Kerk:** De Drie Fleschjes distillery and tasting room, then the Lloyd building at the Martelaarsgracht end of the Nieuwendijk.
