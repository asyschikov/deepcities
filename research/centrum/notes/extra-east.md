# extra-east: notes

Slice: second screen, records that passed only on a long national register text (why = rce), spread over the Oude Zijde, Nieuwmarkt/Lastage, Weesperbuurt/Plantage and Oostelijke Eilanden/Kadijken. 56 records.

Outcome: 15 places (9 own, 6 group) covering 37 records; 12 records fit places already drafted in other slice files (no new draft); 7 no story found; 0 A-list.

Ranks: 1 Amazing (Kweekschool voor de Zeevaart), 8 Nice, 6 Okay.

WebSearch was not available (budget used up). All research by curl: national register texts (in the slice), nl.wikipedia raw wikitext and its search API, Amsterdam op de kaart item pages, Stadsherstel, and the places' own sites (Rho Hotel, The Bulldog hotel, Stadgenoot, Protestantse Kerk Amsterdam).

## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Kweekschool**: the register dates the fund to 1785; Amsterdam op de kaart says the school was on this spot from 1783. nl.wikipedia (with sources) gives 1785 for both, so 1785 is used. Merger: Amsterdam op de kaart 1970/1971, nl.wikipedia 1971 (and 1972 in its intro); 1971 used. nl.wikipedia's "first maritime boarding school on the continent" and the HZA article's "first in Europe" are left out (unsourced, contradictory). The Kaatje III concrete training ship (now in Enkhuizen) left out: unclear which closure the article means.
- **Oud Nico**: register gives 1899-1890 (typo), Amsterdam op de kaart 1889, Stadsherstel 1890: written 1889–1890. Current tenants per Stadsherstel's monument page: Levi Strauss showroom, artists' studios and homes; the body says only "a showroom, studios and homes".
- **Diamond works**: 92 mills (register) against 99 (Amsterdam op de kaart): written "more than ninety". The client S.J. Boas is not linked to the Boas brothers of the big Uilenburg factory (nieuwmarkt-2): no source connects them.
- **Drijfhout**: the 1974 photos on Amsterdam op de kaart (old silver guilders melted down on the spot) have truncated captions, so the episode is left out. Register says 1912–1913; the hotel is Rho Hotel (own site, Nes 5-23).
- **Raamgracht 4**: rename to Zorg en Bijstand 1944 (Amsterdam op de kaart) vs 1946 (register, nl.wikipedia): written "in the 1940s". Vrij Nederland's arrival: 1949 (Amsterdam op de kaart) vs "the 1970s" (Louis Blankenberg article); Igor Cornelissen's memoir covers the 1960s there, so "after the war" is used. Departure 2015 rests on Amsterdam op de kaart only.
- **'t Schipblok**: the wording of the warning against drink is not given in any source read, so it is not quoted; check on site. Current ground-floor tenants (burger restaurant after coffeeshop Extase left in 2014; a cannabis gallery at the canal office in 2020) not stated as they may be out of date.
- **Feldmann**: tenant history rests on Amsterdam op de kaart only (a careful source with dates); hotel confirmed by The Bulldog hotel's own site.
- **Sibbelwoningen**: the origin of the name Sibbel is not explained in any source read; left out. The rotten-fish story comes from the title of a Parool article of 28 Jan 2015 as cited by nl.wikipedia (article itself not read). Mural De brouwers (May 2023) assumed still there.
- **Militair Hospitaal**: AvL moved out in 1973 (hospital) with the lab six years later (nl.wikipedia AvL), register 1976, Amsterdam op de kaart "end of the 1970s": written "in the 1970s". Architect "probably Colonel D.G. Andreae" (Amsterdam op de kaart), register "under direction of" Andreae.
- **Physisch Laboratorium**: built 1880 (register) vs in use from the 1882 academic year (Handelsblad via Amsterdam op de kaart): written 1880–1882. That Zeeman worked in this building rests on nl.wikipedia Zeemanlaboratorium ("ended the lack of space in the earlier lab at Plantage Muidergracht 6") and Pieter Zeeman ("old laboratory").
- **Huis met de Vazen**: register gives listing 2001; Amsterdam op de kaart says municipal monument 1988 and rijksmonument 2004: `listed` left out. Coach house 1907–08 (register) vs 1909 (Amsterdam op de kaart).
- **Hodshon**: the register spells Hodson, Amsterdam op de kaart Hodshon; Hodshon used. Nurses: 1970 (Amsterdam op de kaart) vs 1975–1995 (register): written "later". Van Limmik's current use not stated: Amsterdam op de kaart (2017) named the Protestant Church office and a gallery, but that office is now at Nieuwe Herengracht 18 (its own contact page).
- **Plantage Middenlaan 60**: current use unknown, not stated. The AOM horse stable of 1875 in the block (damaged by fire in 2005) is not visible from the street and left out of the Look-for list.
- **Jan de Liefde school**: register spells De Boomspijker, Amsterdam op de kaart De Boomsspijker (used). Its website could not be reached; status rests on Amsterdam op de kaart (edited May 2026).

## Route ideas

- **IJ front east** (about 1 km): Oud Nico, De Ruijterkade's last shipping offices, then over to the Prins Hendrikkade: Kweekschool voor de Zeevaart, Scheepvaarthuis, D'Oude Werf.
- **Oude Zijde trades** (about 1 km): Drijfhout on the Nes, the tobacco offices on the Rokin and Nes, Feldmann on the Oudezijds Voorburgwal, 't Schipblok on the Oude Hoogstraat, Raamgracht 4.
- **Plantage science and charity**: Salerno, Hodshon and Van Limmik, Wittenberg, Physisch Laboratorium and the Zeeman lab next door, Huis met de Vazen, Plantage Middenlaan 60, then out along the Sarphatistraat: Militair Hospitaal, the depots, the cavalry barracks (Rijksakademie) and the Muiderpoort.
- **Kadijken**: Sibbelwoningen, Werf 't Kromhout, Entrepotdok.
