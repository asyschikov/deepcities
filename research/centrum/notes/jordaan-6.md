## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Willemsstraat naming and date.** Registers and nl.wikipedia give the filling as 1857 and the
  opening as 24 August 1857; hofjesinamsterdam.nl says 1875 and names Willem III. The street was
  named on the birthday of Willem I (wiki, amsterdamhv), while Willem III reigned and was the
  association's patron: I left the namesake out. nl.wikipedia marks the "at the urging of the
  Vereeniging" claim with Bron?, but three city register texts state it, so it stays.
- **Willemsstraat 132 passage.** amsterdamhv.nl tells that an owner refused to sell, forcing a
  passage; the register says the gate led to homes on the inner yard. One weak source: left out.
- **200403 status.** Other register texts call Willemsstraat 160-176 a rijksmonument; the slice
  and its own text make it a municipal monument (listed 1996). Kept as municipal. They also give
  the corner as Palmdwarsstraat 44-46 or 46-48.
- **Constantia dates and names.** Register 209049 dates nos. 143-147 to 1870 and 1872; wiki and
  amsterdamhv say 1868 (145-147) and about 1873 (143). Body says "around 1870". Founder spelled
  Josua (hofjesinamsterdam) or Joshua (wiki); RCE has J. van Eik. Used Josua. Wiki says
  rijksmonument since 2002; slice says listed 2001; used 2001.
- **Westerstraat 187 date.** Register 1867 (citing Bouwkundige Bijdragen 1873); wiki 1868. Used
  1867. Marcel Wanders's studio rests on the wiki line about the February 2007 purchase; the
  municipal-monument list says "business centre" in the 21st century. `check: Facts` set.
- **Café Nol** (Westerstraat 109): confirmed only by the 2006 register text and the wiki list
  ("al jaren in gebruik bij Café Nol"). cafenol.nl did not resolve. `check: Still there?` set on
  the Westerstraat group. The register's "typical music culture of the Jordaan" is all I said
  about it; I did not add the levenslied or sing-alongs.
- **Westerstraat 109 client/year.** Slice says client H. Reijnhoudt, year 1934; text says
  C.J. Reijnhoudt, built 1935. Body only says "members of the Reijnhoudt family".
- **Moor's head stone, Westerstraat 184.** Register: a head in profile, possibly from an older
  house. Wiki: recorded since a 1740 deed as a Moriaanshoofd, but whether it shows a Black man is
  disputed (only a slave collar suggests it). Body says it was "recorded here since 1740 as a
  Moor's head" and no more.
- **Concordia Noordwest register** gives 1962 (typo for 1862). Client: register says
  Bouwmaatschappij Concordia; wiki says Van Lennep's initiative. Concordia was formed in 1864 from
  his and Van Eeghen's holdings, which squares both; the body says so.
- **Pianola rolls.** Wiki says 35,000+, the museum site "more than 40,000". Used the site.
- Bewaarschool: the famous wartime "Heil" story (Stroethoff and the Hitler salute) happened at the
  association's building on the Oudezijds Achterburgwal, not here, so it is not used.

  Arnhem in 2002 (nl.wikipedia Westerstraat): gone, but a strong story for a Look for or a note.

## Route ideas

- **Philanthropy against the slums** (about 1.5 km): Willemsstraat (alley tiles, first block 1860,
  Constantiahofje, bewaarschool) to the Lijnbaansgracht corner, then the Westerstraat (Concordia
  Noordwest and Noord), Marnixstraat De Greef blocks (jordaan-3) and on to Concordia Zuid on the
  Elandsstraat (jordaan-3).
- **Westerstraat end to end** on a Monday market morning: Noorderkerk (jordaan-3), Pianola Museum,
  the 1930s rebuilds and Café Nol, the Burgerschool, Concordia Noordwest.
