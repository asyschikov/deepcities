# Visual checks, slice 2 (25 Nov 2026)

Main source: Amsterdam city panoramas (api.data.amsterdam.nl/panorama, capture dates 2024-2025). I rendered views toward each pin. Pano ids are given so each view can be re-rendered.

- **Huis met de Neuzen** (vd98gucf): Commons photo taken 24 Jun 2024 ("Amsterdam, Singel 110-124 RM5294tm8 IMG 3433 2024-06-24 13.28.jpg") shows all three heads: one in the crown at the top of the gable and one at each top corner of the neck. The Look-for line is now precise (EN and NL).

- **Weesperstraat embroidery** (hkap0iw5): the 19 Jun 2025 pano (recording_2025-06-19_03-28-47_03096) shows it on the wall at the corner of the Nieuwe Keizersgracht. No text change.
- **Boomstraat phone box** (rfxd4avj): the 3 Jul 2025 pano (recording_2025-07-03_07-31-54_00111) shows the box full of books beside no. 67, so the pin is right. No text change.
- **Keizersgracht 516** (xewjba75): the 16 Jun 2025 pano (recording_2025-06-16_06-21-55_00175) shows an exhibition in the hall. This matches the body. No text change.

## Still open, note updated
- **Cineac**: the 19 Jun 2025 pano shows the canopy and booth window intact and a **Merkur Casino** trading under the canopy. Web listings name "Merkur Casino Cineac" at nos. 31-33. One unattributed snippet says the Cineac is "tijdelijk weer open" with vintage sellers and concerts, which may match the Google Maps "Cineac Amsterdam" association. The body says nothing about the casino. Revisit once the current use is confirmed.
- **Door wind bewogen beweging**: comparing the panos of 16 Oct 2024 and 17 Jun 2025 shows the vanes in different positions, so it turns. Whether it swings freely cannot be seen.
- **Reestraat 6**: in the 16 Jun 2025 pano there is no plaque for De Kom on the front. A small two-line plate under the window box is unreadable.
- **Swigtershofje**: in the 19 Jun 2025 pano the gate is shut, with an intercom, and has boarding and a scaffold tower above it.
- **Walenweeshuis**: the 16 Jun 2025 pano shows site fencing and no scaffolding. This imagery is older than the Dec 2025 permit.
- **De Swarte Mvele**: the stone is not visible in the Jan and Jun 2025 panos, and the corner building was in scaffolding in June 2025. The pin is unchanged.
- **Elizabeth Wolffschool**: in the 16 Jun 2025 pano there is no school sign, and the bell plates read 927, 927a and 927b.
- **First Tooneelschool**: in the 3 Jul 2025 pano the building is intact, with no sign on it.
- **Trompettersteeg**: in the 16 Jun 2025 pano the entrance is covered with tags and a poster. The 3D arms and the sailors cannot be made out.
- **Van Hall / Frederiksplein**: the 14 Jul 2025 pano shows the bronze tree in place. The painted acacia has not been found in dated imagery.
