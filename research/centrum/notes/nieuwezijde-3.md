## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **5593 (Spuistraat 129)**: national register text is only "two houses behind a step gable, c.1600/XVIIa but dated 1662, gable stone". Wikidata links it to Hotel Die Port van Cleve, which is drafted in nieuwezijde-2 on 5988 only. If the hotel really includes this back pair, add 5593 to that place's `monument_number`; I did not edit another slice's file.
- **Dominicuskerk tower**: nl.wikipedia says 85 m and the city refused the permit; Amsterdam op de kaart says 88 m and the church lacked money. Text says "about as high as the Westertoren" and that accounts differ on the reason. Hidden church founded 1621 (wiki) or 1624 (Spuistraat article): written "from the 1620s". Building years 1884-1893 (register) vs 1884-1886 (AOK); used consecration 1893. Independence "since 1990" from AOK; wiki only says the break followed the 1960s-70s experiments.
- **De Gouden Bril**: "oldest optician still trading in the Netherlands" is the register's claim (2001), repeated by AOK; written as "the register calls it". Firm now trades as Schmidt Akkerman; Rokin 72 store confirmed on their site (Nov 2026). Register gives client J.C. Schmidt, AOK names founder Johan Michaël Schmidt (1866); used the founder for the history.
- **Gerzon**: wartime deaths: nl.wikipedia "325 staff", joodsamsterdam "some 300 Jewish employees"; used "about 300". Present hotel from joodsamsterdam (undated, "het huidige NH-hotel"); NH site refused curl, so `check: Facts`. The register's name "De Kaatsheuvel van 1713" is mentioned as the register's name only; whether it is on the front is unknown. Left out the "first big fashion show in the Netherlands" (one source).
- **Zoutsteeg 9-11**: built 1891 (register) vs 1899/1900 (AOK drawing date); written "1890s". Register says the shop text is under white paint, AOK reads it on the frieze; `check: Facts`. Café name (Kadinsky 2014) not rechecked, so not named.
- **Tabakspanden**: Wijdesteeg 3-7 dated 1932 (AOK) vs 1935-1940 (register): written "1930s". Not claimed that Tabak built the garage (AOK: designed for NV Mac, Tabak's after the war). Post-2015 conversion to flats from AOK (2017). The gloss of "Lang gewagt" was left out.
- **Spuistraat 34**: register attributes the top storey to a 1920 alteration by Eijrond; AOK (citing Den Boer 2024) says Sanders added the arched-window floor before moving in. Text avoids dating the top; attribute says "top altered later".
- **Justus van Maurik**: register says the firm was founded in 1794 by Justus van Maurik Senior, but nl.wikipedia gives the grandfather's birth year as 1781; founding year left out. Lettering on the front in 1896 is from a newspaper quote, not visible today, so it is in the story, not the Look for list.
- **P.C. Hoofthuis**: nl.wikipedia says the UvA wants to sell it (undated, written when the Bungehuis was also for sale); `check: Facts`. Visiting access not stated.
- **Pakketpostgebouw**: current use unknown (register 2004: NS); `check: Facts`.
- **Keuken van 1870**: AOK (2017) says a "normal" restaurant since end 2012; name not found; `check: Still there?`.
- **Magna Plaza**: The Food Department food hall (opened 2019) not rechecked, left out. Hours from magnaplaza.nl footer, Nov 2026.
- **Noord-Zuid-Hollands Koffiehuis**: Loetje hours confirmed on loetje.nl (Nov 2026).
- Tabak's conviction is stated without the sentence details; his wartime collaboration trial (nl.wikipedia) left out as not needed.
