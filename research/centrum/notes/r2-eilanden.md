## Conflicts and uncertainties
- Het Funen: Arcam says 16 blocks by 9 architects; Wikipedia says each block has a different designer. The text says "each designed differently" and avoids a count of architects. Arcam also files it under stadsdeel Oost; the point-check says Centrum.
- Floating gardens: the 2026 1018 article dates the launch to 2021. Other 1018 articles date the towing and handover to June and July 2022. Used 2022.
- Woning-Maatschappij: 1018 Magazine 65 gives 1871 for the complex; round 1 (city register) gives first handover 1 May 1874. Round 1 kept; the conflict is noted here.
- Werkspoor hall: 1018 Magazine 65 (Feb 2025) reports a temporary permit for padel in "the Werkspoorhal", owned by Nedstede, against a 2016 agreement that it stay public. Magazine 69 (Nov 2025) says the padel plan is off and the gym BigGym is coming, with the hall freely open during opening hours for seven years. Magazine 71 (Feb 2026) expects it to open before the end of 2026. It is unclear which of round 1's halls "the Werkspoorhal" is (probably Stelplaats 3), so it was not added. Decide before using it.
- Kattenburgerplein: the sources do not say which house numbers form Rouwhorst's reconstructed row. The pin is at nos. 56-59 (the stone De Groenne Oven); check flag set.
- Hoogte Kadijk: the gable stones' numbers come from the scout's gevelstenen.net/VVAG lead (Arion at 78, 1990; Koorn Aaren 1777; Udinck at Tussen Kadijken 13). Check flag set. The scout's storm-barrier/sluice claim was weak (NH Nieuws 2023 has no link to the Kadijk) and is omitted.
- Mbulu Ngulu: moved in 2019; Wikipedia's coordinates are approximate. Check flag set.
- Tkramrepus: the latest dated evidence is July 2024 (crowdfunding). Check "Still there?" set.
- Kogelbron: last dated photo October 2018. Check "Still there?" set.
- Plukbos: the initiator is a private person and is not named.
- Shaffyboom: the 2008 naming rests on zwartekat.nl (scout lead, not re-read) plus the CBH 2024 advice and AT5 2009. The tree stands on ProRail land.
- Schietbaan: the marineterrein.nl page says "from 1 March" the building is available for art and culture, with no year; left out.
- S-221 fence: the 300 m fence artwork (Jan 2023, Oostenburg-Noord, until at least 2028) was not added; it needs a second source.
