## Enrich edits

- **nieuwmarkt-1, "De Ruijterkade's last shipping offices"**: Look for / Let op line for the 1933 stone on no. 142, TIME TO RE-TIRE GET A FISK (VVAG list). The figure on the stone is not described: no photo checked.
- **nieuwmarkt-1, "Goslerhuisje, the last house of the Joden Houttuinen"**: Look for / Let op line for the theatre school front at Jodenbreestraat 25-27 where the Jewish cinema Tip Top stood until 1953, small hall named Tip Top (nl.wiki Tip_Top_Theater).
- **nieuwmarkt-1, "Christ Church, the English church in a cloth hall"**: Look for / Let op line: the entrance is where Hendrick de Keyser had his workshop and home, next to the city stonemasons' yard 1605 to 1626 (Stadsherstel page).
- **nieuwmarkt-2, "The bridge yard and the last of the Jewish nursery school"**: one sentence in both languages on the VVAB objection (monument, Jewish heritage, already threatened in 2003) and residents' health fears; outlook changed from Major change ahead to Under threat, outlook_note rewritten in both languages; VVAB news 431 and 452 added to sources.
- **nieuwmarkt-3, "Zuiderkerk, the first church built for Protestants"**: sentence on the Salvator bell (1511, Willem and Jaspar Moer, from the Oude Kerk, still rung); the tombstone Look for now says it is seen through a glass plate in the floor; ONH source added.
- **nieuwmarkt-3, "Mozes en Aäronkerk, the church named after two houses"**: corrected the hunger strike from 1978 to 7 to 15 November 1975 (about a hundred Moroccan workers), added the 1978 second strike in De Duif and the permits for all 182 "kerkmarokkanen"; summary changed in both languages ("asylum seekers ... 1978" became "Moroccan workers ... 1975"). ONH source added.
- **nieuwmarkt-3, "Huis De Pinto, the house that stopped a motorway"**: sentence on the residents' campaign "De Pinto Bieb Moet Blijven!" against closing the library branch (OpNieuw 2006 to 2008).

## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Mozes en Aäronkerk hunger strike**: nl.wiki says 1978 and 182 Moroccans; ONH (Ons Amsterdam, Nov 2011) says the Mozes en Aäron strike was 7 to 15 Nov 1975 (about 100), the 1978 strike was in De Duif, and 182 got permits on 17 Oct 1978. Followed ONH.
- **Alex Evers (Valkenburgerstraat)**: 4en5mei gives the evening of 11 April, the plaque and OpNieuw 2012-1 say 12 April; the body says "an April evening".
- **Flesseman ceremony**: OpNieuw 2022-1 says the yearly ceremony lapsed, 2023-1 says it still happens; body says "for years pupils held a ceremony".
- **Nieuwmarkt shelter**: 6,000 people per Arcam (delivered 1979), 3,000 in the 1960s plan per ONH; body follows Arcam for what was built.
- **Plane trees on the Nieuwmarkt**: OpNieuw 1995-3/4 announces planting on 11 to 15 Dec 1995; OpNieuw 2008-2 says 1996. Body says "the winter of 1995 to 1996".
- **Geldersche Gaai**: all three accounts (OpNieuw 2025-2, 2025-4, d'Oude Binnenstad 2015-1) are by the same neighbour, who also cleans it; the 2015 piece says the refit was 2006, the 2025 piece says 2010. Kept only as a Look for line, using 2010 (the year the city relaid it). Not a single independent second source; your call whether to keep it.
- **Fort van Sjakoo opening**: OpNieuw 2001-3 says October 1977 at a street party, OpNieuw 2015-3 says November 1977. Used October (the earlier, closer account).
- **Huis Serne**: Hendrick de Keyser Monumenten says the Serne family owned the house from 1883; OpNieuw 2016-3 says the firm was on the Groenburgwal from 1876 (founded 1866). Both kept, as different facts. The ceiling attribution to Hermanus Noordkerk is the Vereniging's own "possibly"; written as possibly.
- **Monument Joods Verzet pin**: OSM and Kunstwacht differ by about 64 m; Megadlé place pinned on the Megadlé building, the column is a Look for.
- **Vlooienburg plaque**: exact spot on the Amstel 7 front not confirmed; place pinned on the Spinoza statue.
- **Spinoza inscription**: "Het doel van de staat is de vrijheid" (sources) vs OSM's inscription field "vrijheid"; followed the sources.
- **Stopera**: the Provo/PSP slogan and the architects' succession story were cut for length.
- **Megadlé**: named carers left unnamed (private persons).
- **Leprozenpoort pin**: on the PDOK point of Sint Antoniesluis 22; the gate is on the front of nos. 21-24 (check attribute set). Grenspaal date 1986 from SCOUT-AC only.
- **Marionettentheater**: OpNieuw 2025-1 says the Aktiegroep Nieuwmarkt met in the Smederij; older OpNieuw issues say the Bewonersraad did. Wrote "Bewonersraad". OpNieuw 1993-4 says Komedie van Hout used the Smederij then, so the puppet theatre may not have been there continuously since 1987; body says "now the home of", and "already playing Mozart in the Smederij in 1987".
- **Rokende schipper**: allowed only "vooralsnog" (Dec 2025); outlook Under threat.
- **Living by Numbers**: "first HIV/AIDS monument in Europe" is the makers' and Kunstwacht's claim, written as such.
- **Overlap with running slices**: Ruijtergaard and the 's-Gravenhekje park sit on the same Shaffy's Tuin walk as Shaffy's Tuin itself (S-218, r2-eilanden, still running); no overlap in places, but r2-eilanden may want to link the walk.
