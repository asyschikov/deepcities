# Photo pass (Wikimedia Commons), Deep Centrum drafts

Central serial pass over places that had no `photos`. Method: Wikidata P18 for the monument numbers,
notes suggestions, Commons geosearch (40 m), Commons search on the address, direct title probes
(`<address>.JPG`, `Amsterdam - <address>.JPG`, ...), address categories, then targeted searches
(names, `"rijksmonument number N"`) for places still empty. Every chosen file was checked by eye
on its thumbnail. One request at a time, 1.5 s apart (3 s for thumbnails), honouring 429/retry-after.

Result: 185 places got photos, 83 still without.
