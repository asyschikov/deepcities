## Conflicts and uncertainties

- Arsenaal (268, 266) vs nieuwmarkt-3: the Oudezijds Huiszittenhuis (6306) next door is in slice nieuwmarkt-3. The three belong to one poor-relief complex; whoever writes 6306 should either merge it into this Arsenaal place or link to it. Decision needed.
- Boas listing year: the register says 1995, nl.wikipedia says 1996. Left out of the place.
- Boas: some sources say largest diamond factory in the world, others Europe. Used Europe.
- Rosenthal school demolition: city register and JCK/joodsamsterdam say 1956, nl.wikipedia says around 1960. Used 1956.
- Rosenthal school house outlook: Liander's renewal of substation Uilenburg (Nieuwe Uilenburgerstraat 27) is in design, planning "nog te bepalen". The house-moving variant comes from the May 2024 Bewonersraad 1011 report; check before publishing if a newer plan exists.
- A claim that Piet Kramer drew his bridges at Werf Uilenburg rests on one weak source; left out.
- Koningsstraat 5 (200191): a nl.wikipedia street list says Ed van der Elsken lived there; not confirmed in any biography consulted. Not used.
- Splendor: the bath master is named in AoK, the couple he sheltered are private people and are not named. Bath master left unnamed in the text too.
- Korte Koningsstraat 14: the five-year-old is named on the public stone; the text says only "his youngest child".
- Sophia Adriana Stichting: current use (still a hofje or not) not confirmed; text keeps to the front.
- Premsela & Hamburger: whether the firm still exists is unconfirmed (premsela.com showed only an error page). Text ends the story at the 1952 move to the Rokin. Whether no. 27-29 still has a shop on the ground floor was not checked, so the text only claims it for the corner building.
- Schottenburch current uses (homes, gallery, wine shop) come from nl.wikipedia, not freshly checked; text says only that it became homes.
- The WebSearch budget was used up during this slice, so the last checks (Premsela status, Schottenburch and Sophia Adriana current use) were done only with curl against known sites.

## Route ideas

- Uilenburg loop (about 1.5 km): Peperbrug, the Handwerkers Vriendenkring block, Werf Uilenburg and the Rosenthal house, the Uilenburgersjoel, Splendor, Boas (tour), then over the Amstel side to the Arsenaal, Nieuwe Herengracht 43 and 143, and back north via the Krom Boomssloot (Surp Hoki, Schottenburch, Korte Koningsstraat 14) to the Nieuwe Hoogstraat (Premsela & Hamburger).
- Jewish Uilenburg theme: synagogue, bathhouse, Rosenthal school, Jewish school no. 2, Handwerkers block, Boas; pairs with the A-list Jewish Cultural Quarter.
