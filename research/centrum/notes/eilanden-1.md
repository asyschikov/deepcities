## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Web search budget ran out** before the minimum one search could be done for the seven "no story found" records (six schools and Hoogte Kadijk 9). They rest on the register texts only. Worth one quick search each in a later round.
- **De Engel**: the register dates the sculpture 1953; Wikipedia and an Algemeen Handelsblad report give its unveiling as 11 October 1952. The text says 1952.
- **Mariniersbrug designer**: the register and the bridge foundation name Piet Kramer; nl.wikipedia (after Smit) names Leupen; other bridge books name no one. Set `check: Facts`, and the text avoids a firm attribution.
- **Werf 't Kromhout**: "oldest covered shipyard" is the museum's own claim; worded as such. Trimmed the 1911 sale and the Prins Bernhard Fonds detail for length.
- **Volkskoffiehuis**: current use unclear (a 1986 squat claim could not be confirmed; the fadingad page could not be fetched with curl). Removed and set `check: Still there?`. A "Koffiehuis van de Volksbond" social café now runs at Haarlemmerstraat 146-H, not here.
- **Zeemanshuis**: the original towers are gone; the text says so. Current user (YWAM) from a 2026 search summary.
- **Czaar Peterbuurt**: the Kalff plan is dated 1875 in one source, 1876 in another; the text avoids the year.
- **Van Gendthallen**: the register says the firm made Suez Canal lock gates in 1851; implausible for that date and not confirmed elsewhere, left out. Drift Museum and restaurant opening dates have slipped (April 2026 still under renovation; July 2026 summary expects after summer 2026), so `outlook: Major change ahead` with no `outlook_when`.
- **Ropewalks**: Peter the Great's stay is "possibly" (the 1947 plaque); worded as "may have stayed". The Werkspoor office date is stylistic (1880s or 90s), given as "late nineteenth century".
- **Oosterkerk**: earlier talk of selling the church; a 2021 letter says sale is no longer the aim. Sale not mentioned.
- **Sluisdeurenloods**: present use (2014/2015 onwards) rests on search summaries.
- **Lifeboat shed**: interior details come from sale brochures and are not used; `check: Still there?` for the painted lettering. Dropped "on the market in 2026" for the Marineterrein (weak source).
- **Hulpbank van Lening**: conversion to flats rests on a 2022 permit; completion unknown, so the text only says it no longer is a pawnshop. Architect "W. Hamer" is the register's attribution.
- **Entrepotdok**: bridge 80 is halfway along the dock, not at the east end (corrected).
- **De Gooyer**: "date stone" corrected to "gable stone".
- **Centrale Oost**: dropped "part of the old Entrepotdok was demolished for it" for length; an unread Liander link removed.

## Route ideas

- **Kadijken loop** (about 2 km): Volkskoffiehuis and Zeemanshuis on the Kadijksplein, the Entrepotdok gate and along the dock, Werf 't Kromhout, Centrale Oost, De Gooyer, the Oranje-Nassau Kazerne.
- **The islands loop** (about 2 km): Marineterrein gate, Mariniersbrug, Sluisdeurenloods, Old Kattenburg's last corner, the Annakerk gate, the Oosterkerk, the lifeboat shed on the Windroosplein.
- **Oostenburg and the Czaar Peterbuurt** (about 2.5 km, "industry and its workers"): the VOC and Admiralty ropewalks, the Woning-Maatschappij houses, the Werkspoor halls and Stelplaats 3 seen across the Wittenburgervaart, then into the Czaar Peterbuurt for the pawnshop branch, the workers' blocks, the horse-tram depot and De Engel.
