## Conflicts and uncertainties

- Montelbaanstoren: wiki gives 1516 for the tower, the moat dated 1515-1518; I used 1516. Current tenant (wiki: private boat tour firm since 2014) not verified, so the text just says "an office".
- Huis De Pinto: RCE dates the sandstone front "second half 17th century"; wiki credits David de Pinto after 1686, probably Elias Bouman. Text says "later 17th century", no architect.
- Oudezijds Huiszittenhuis: RCE names Staets, wiki Willem de Keyser; architect left out.
- Pentagon: city register names Theo Bosch (Aldo van Eyck's office), wiki says designed with J. van Berge in 1975; register year 1983. Text: "designed from 1975, finished around 1983, with Aldo van Eyck's office".
- Zwanenburgwal 218-230: register names Paul de Ley and Fenna Oorthuys, but per wiki Oorthuys partnered De Ley only from 2003. Text names De Ley only.
- Girls' orphanage: Collegie founded 1734 (joodsamsterdam) or 1761 (wiki); left out. Clearance 10 February 1943 (wiki) vs "1942 to Westerbork" in the war-memorial list; text says February 1943.
- Atelierwoningen: register 1958, wiki 1961; used 1958.
- Bewaarschool: city register address 103, wiki 105; text says "number 105 (registered as 103)".
- Ets Haim: register says college came from the Amstelstraat (room above the Dritt Sjoel); fine. NIW left in 2004 (joodsamsterdam); current use "offices" from RCE, not re-verified.
- Uylenburgh: arrival "about 1625" per wiki only.
- Van Kollem tile tableaux in the basement: register says they "reportedly" show the old houses; text attributes it to the register.
- Raamgracht 6-8: AVSV use from 1962; current use unverified (avsv.nl is a parked domain), text says "private".
- D'Oude Werf: current use unknown; text says not open.
- WebSearch budget was exhausted early; current-use checks relied on direct site fetches (huisdepinto.nl, droog.com, cafededruif.nl, zuiderkerkamsterdam.nl, academievanbouwkunst.nl).
