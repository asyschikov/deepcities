## Extra: De Gooyer (oxn7yak6vdowiqk2dmvnx754), not flagged

Second source found, so the text is changed. The databank of the Stichting Erfgoed Nederlandse Biercultuur (entry Brouwerij 't IJ) says the malt was ground in De Gooyer in the brewery's early years. The quality proved uneven, so the brewery bought a "horsefriend" (an oat crusher) to use as a grist mill. This matches De CaféKrant 2023.

Summary and body (EN and NL) now say the brewery opened in 1985 and has the mill on its labels, and that in its first years it had its malt ground there. The roods.io app story says the same but does not count.
