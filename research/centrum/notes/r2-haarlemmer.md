## Conflicts and uncertainties

- Zandhoek "Oranjeboom" (1898 lime): only the Wijkkrant 2018 says it; the city's protected-tree list has a lime on the Zandhoek of about 1950. Dropped.
- Vreelant stone: the dentist's town is Moordrecht (Wijkkrant) or Reeuwijk (VVAG); written "near Gouda".
- Van Lennep's first water: Wijkkrant says 1851 (first spade), Wikipedia and the speech 1853. 1853 used.
- Ples mosaic: Kunstwacht says Jacob Ples, Wijkkrant 2016 (photo credit Saskia Ples) says Henk Ples. Henk used.
- Posthoorn statie move: Theo Bakker gives around 1687 in one text and 1678 in another; "late 17th century".
- Roode Bioscoop: outcome of the 2024 court case with the owner not found; programming continues in 2026. Owner not named.
- Aardige Amsterdammer: reported damage of May 2024 is a post title only; check Still there?.
- Meursing sculpture: vanes nearly stuck in 2022; whether repaired is unknown (check Facts).
- Mandela mural on Prinseneiland: maker unknown; last seen 2021 and 2022.
- Noordkop garden: the 2006 rescue of the farm and the outcome of the 2015 tree-felling plan could not be verified; left out. Gardeners not named.
- Prinseneiland winch: exact spot by the Sloterdijkerbrug to confirm on site.
- De Kruiwagen: café name (Kobalt) and its opening year rest on the scout; the body says only "a café today".
- Bickersgracht 14 and 22: monument numbers not found, heritage fields omitted.
