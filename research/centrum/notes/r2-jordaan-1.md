## Conflicts and uncertainties

- Bevrijdingslinde (S-010): sources disagree on whose names are in the urn. The Wijkkrant says the children who died; 4en5mei says the pupils who raised the money. The draft gives both, with check: Facts.
- Lindengracht (S-007): the date and death toll of the Palingoproer differ by source (26, 27 or 28 dead). Where exactly the Zaterdagse brug crossed is not known (check: Location).
- Telegraafrellen date: 13 June (Wikipedia) or 14 June (WJGR). The draft avoids giving the day.
- Tante Leen's year: 1993 in WJGR, 1994 on Wikipedia (Johnny Jordaanplein).
- Johnny Jordaanplein: the keeper was Hannie Pastor, a man (1932-2015), not a woman as the scout wrote.
- Kees de jongen: Wikipedia names one painter "Es Simons", WJGR has "Ed Simons"; I used Ed. The mural was unveiled on Friday 12 September 2025.
- Noordermarkt: Ai! Amsterdam campaigned against the city's "vertrutting" (blandification); the scout wrongly gave this as the residents' word for the square.
- Eerste Marnixplantsoen: the bastion is called Bolwerk Haarlem by Wikipedia and the OSM plaque, and De Palm by WJGR. Weggelaar died of a heart attack; whether the police caused it was never settled.
- Vaste klant: the seat belongs to the café, not a public bench. The latest dated evidence is NRC (May 2020) and the 2024 events of Festina Lente.
- Harmonica player: the latest dated mention is 2017 (check: Still there?).
- Nassaubioscoop: the conversion that started in 2020 may have changed the front (check: Still there?).
- Papeneiland: Theo Bakker says the "first" arch held the ratelwacht's post, while Wikipedia says the "western" arch was used for storage. The text says "a bricked-up arch" so as not to equate them.
- Weespad: how the 2009-2010 fight against the building behind Rozenstraat 183 ended was not found. The text says only that residents fought it.
- Concordia Noord: the latest concert evidence is 2019 (Jordaanweb). Day access to the courts is not confirmed, so the text does not claim it.
- Rozenhofje (Jordaanweb, undated) and Suyckerhoff-hofje (2023 walk): the access statements are not recent.
- Noorderspeeltuin: Jan Klaassen and Katrijn are "vermoedelijk" buried on the old graveyard (WJGR 2017). This is left out.

## Route ideas

- North Jordaan, about 2 km: Noordermarkt (post, plaque, outline), Lindengracht (eel riot), Karthuizerhof and Noorderspeeltuin, Perlee and the Pianola Museum on the Westerstraat, Kees de jongen on the Tuinstraat, the harmonica player on the Anjeliersstraat, Bevrijdingslinde, Eerste Marnixplantsoen, Nassaubioscoop on the Lijnbaansgracht, and the Papeneiland bridge to finish.
- South Jordaan, about 1.5 km: the Bloemgracht stones, Marnixstraat 291–293, Energie en tijd, Theo Thijssen Museum (via the Leliedwarsstraat), Johnny Jordaanplein, Vaste klant, the Fort van Sjako stones, then the fruit market blocks (Marnixstraat 200–220) and the Turnzaal.
- A themed walk on the Jordaan's own monuments, the ones residents made or fought for: Johnny Jordaanplein, Vaste klant, Kees de jongen, the harmonica player, the Gangenproject tiles, and the fruit market blocks' "Gallisch dorp".
