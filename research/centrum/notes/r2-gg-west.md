## Conflicts and uncertainties

- Westerkerk graveyard: Zeven 114 says it closed after the plague of 1655 and went into the market; the city register for the meat hall says it had "just closed" before 1814. Both kept in sources; body unchanged.
- The graveyard gate rests on Zeven 114 alone; check on site that the putti gate stands left of the tower on the Prinsengracht.
- Coymanshuis plaque: Zeven 149 prints the date as 14 March 1635 in the text but the inscription reads MDCXXV (1625); used 1625. Whether the plaque is visible from the street is unclear.
- Molenpad plane: CBH 2024 says oriental plane, 5.75 m, about 1860; LRMB 1686161 says common plane, 550 cm, 1830 to 1840.
- Gaper: Wikipedia says the replica lasted "less than four years"; Zeven shows June 2018 to July 2020. Drugstore closed spring 2024; gaper photographed June 2024 (check flag set).
- Singel 390 globe: removed 28 March 2003 to be toned down; no evidence found that it returned (check flag set).
- Waterline: the date "late 2025" rests on a Press TV/Reuters headline (7 November 2025) whose page would not load; Zeven 152 confirms it in September 2026. Pin approximate (corner Leliegracht-Herengracht).
- Radio de Vrije Keyser no longer broadcasts (vrijekeyser.nl); not claimed as live.
- Knobbeliep pin is from the tree register; on site, confirm which Westermarkt elm.
- Bergstraat statue pin is at Bergstraat 6; place the statue exactly.
- Descartes tile: confirm it lies in front of number 6.
- Zuid-Afrikahuis: confirm the double-headed eagle is visible on the front.
- Groote Keijser: monument numbers 2544 to 2549; number 252 is not wholly listed.

## Route ideas

- Squatters' West: De Groote Keijser (Keizersgracht 242-252), Vogelstruys site (Herengracht 329 / Singel 370), Zuid-Afrikahuis (the 1984 action), ending at the Westermarkt.
- Gable stones and shop signs: Brouwersgracht 94 to 101, Herenstraat 7 gaper, Bergstraat Koorenmaat, Wolvenstraat 32, Herengracht 394, Langestraat 78-80.
- Westermarkt circle: Descartes at no. 6, the Knobbeliep, the meat hall and graveyard gate, Reestraat 6, then the Keizersgracht Knobbeliep sisters opposite 208, 224, 234.
- Herenstraat and Leliegracht: O Johnny, the Turfkruier stones, Waterline on the quay, the Poezenboot on the Singel.
