## Conflicts and uncertainties

- Eenhoornsluis: the city register calls the Hudde stone the only one left; nl.wikipedia reports a second stone found in 2013 when the Nieuwe Brug (Damrak) was demolished. The text says "the only one still in its wall".
- Hammerfest (211001): the city register says the firm A. Smit & Zoon designed the warehouses itself; nl.wikipedia on the Brouwersgracht gives other details (architect, names). No architect attribute is given.
- Francisca (211004): the register spells the name "Francesca"; nl.wikipedia (and the stone, per its text) say "Francisca". Used Francisca.
- The Movies: it claims to be Amsterdam's oldest cinema still in use; De Uitkijk claims the same. Written as a claim, not a fact. nl.wikipedia says the art deco interior was kept in 1971; the register says the entrance hall got art deco-like decor in the 1971 rebuild. Both reflected.
- Hk. Jonker factory: the register says converted to homes in 1982; mforamsterdam says 2018. Used the register date.
- Haarlemmerplein 48: called "former fire station" in a planning notice; current use not confirmed, so not stated.
- Haarlemmerpoort: today's café-restaurant name ("De Willem") only from a listing site; left out of the text.
- Willem Drees: nl.wikipedia says he was born here "or spent his first days" here; the text reflects that hedge.
- Droogbak (518416): the register says the keystone heads are "probably" railway staff; written as such. Current tenant not verified, so the text says only "offices".
- Blaka Watra: opening hours change; the text says only that it is a walk-in centre for its own visitors.
- Bickerseiland housing: dates differ by source (register 1972-1982, mforamsterdam 1975-1985). Used the register.
- Commons was rate-limited (HTTP 429 on every Wikimedia host) during the run; photo file names come from Wikidata and the nl.wikipedia infoboxes of each building and were not all re-checked against Commons. See Missing photos.

## Route ideas

- Haarlemmerstraat and Haarlemmerdijk, east to west (about 2 km): Haarlemmerstraat 2 angel, Droogbak and the Harm Smeenge school, Francisca and Petrus, West-Indisch Huis, the ghost signs, Brouwersgracht 132 and Hammerfest (short detour), Posthoorn church, Eenhoornsluis and Haarlemmerstraat 150, Roeraade, The Movies, Drees, Haarlemmerpoort, Korte Marnixkade 4, Willemsbrug. Havengebouw is a 5-minute detour from the Droogbak.
- Western Islands loop from the Haarlemmer Houttuinen: under the railway to the Jonker factory, De IJzerstaven, Lalk's gate, the renewal housing on Grote Bickersstraat and Bickersgracht, then to the Sloterdijkerbrug and back over Prinseneiland. Link with the De Reus lead.
