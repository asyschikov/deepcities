## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Web search budget ran out** early in the slice (session-wide limit). Current-status checks therefore rest on Amsterdam op de kaart (curl), nl.wikipedia and the registers; where those are old I set `check` rather than guess. Commons was not called (coordinator's rule).
- **Trouw (Prinsengracht 493):** nl.wikipedia's Trouw article says both that the first number (Oranje-Bode) was printed in Meppel and that the first editions were printed at Prinsengracht 493; the Prinsengracht 493 article says the first numbers were printed here. Text says "early issues". The 23 men shot at Vught: year left out (not stated in the source read).
- **Theatre fire of 1772 (bakery, 4348):** nl.wikipedia Schouwburg van Van Campen gives 18 dead, gg-west-3's Schouwburgpoort place 16; text says "more than fifteen". The bakery lies inside the block down the alley beside no. 495; `check: Location` set because visibility of the time verse from the street is unconfirmed. The time verse's wording is not known to me; not quoted. Wikidata's photo "Prinsengracht 501 across.JPG" not used (may not show 497).
- **Van Brienenhofje:** founder data from the hmdb.org marker via a search snippet (hmdb itself refuses curl, 403) plus cicerize.me for the brewery; nl.wikipedia has no article. A popular legend (founder locked in his own strongroom) was not found in any source and is left out. Courtyard access not confirmed: `check: Facts`. Whether this Aernout Jan van Brienen is the same man as gg-west-2's Arnout Jan van Brienen of Herengracht 182 (1772) is likely (same title, heer van de Groote Lindt) but not confirmed, so no link made.
- **Zon's Hofje:** the church's name De Kleine Zon rests on scout DW-36 (Buurtkrant Zeven); nl.wikipedia only says a Mennonite church. Access hours unconfirmed: `check: Facts`. Scout DW-36 drafted the same hofje as a lead: merge, this is the monument record.
- **Meat hall (200822):** the register says a restaurant from about 2000; the current business was not confirmed (`check: Facts`). Removed my own unsourced reason for the Waag's demolition.
- **Singel 250:** post office dates differ (AOK: main post office after 1988, closed 2011; nl.wikipedia: 1990–2015). Text says "from around 1990" and leaves the end out.
- **Old central library (587):** OBA article says converted to a hotel after 2007; the hotel's name and present use not confirmed (`check: Facts`).
- **Huis met de Neuzen (5295):** register only; no second source reached. Kept at Okay with `check: Facts` on the heads.
- **De Nederlander (579):** antiques dealer per AOK, not recently confirmed (`check: Still there?`). **Cable box (200699):** listed 2013, no recent sighting (`check: Still there?`).
- **Garden houses:** a group of 14 records chosen over 14 "no story found" because several have real hooks (De Bazel, Jan Wils's bike shed, the Huiszittenhuis gate). It is mostly invisible; the way in is the Open Tuinen Dagen (Museum Van Loon, June), but which of these gardens ever take part was not checked. Decision for the user: keep as an Okay group, or move all 14 to "no story found".
- **Raadhuisstraat group:** kept to the four north-side fronts; the story is the 1895 breakthrough. Raadhuisstraat 8 (4749) is only the rear of Singel 240, which is not in this slice.
- **Singel 94 (210057):** J.J. Boldoot's house; belongs to gg-west-3's "Boldoot's coach house (Langestraat 47)". Not drafted; suggest adding 210057 to that place's monument numbers and one line on the house.
- **Necofa (203048):** the 18th-century back front on the Roomolenstraat is national monument 5269, not in this slice; linked, not counted.
- Removed during writing as unsourced: "one of the larger hofjes", a hidden-church claim for De Zon, the Westerkerk-view reason for the Waag's demolition, "one of the few listed street furniture pieces", a generic claim about neck-gable decoration, the Olympic Stadium line for Jan Wils, "the passage is closed off" at the Suykerhofje, "not open to visitors" at Te Huis, and the Huize Labor name on the front.

## Route ideas

- Brouwersgracht to Westermarkt along the Prinsengracht: Van Brienenhofje, Zon's Hofje, the meat hall, Homomonument, Huize Labor (Anne Frank House and Westerkerk as landmarks), then down the Raadhuisstraat to Singel 250.
- Westermarkt to Leidsegracht: Nieuwe Suykerhofje, Te Huis, Trouw at 493, the poor-relief bakery, the frog stone, Runstraat 14-18, De Nederlander, the old library.
- Singel west side: Necofa, Langestraat cable box, Huis met de Neuzen, Centraal Beheer, De Dolphijn, Singel 250.
- Theme thread "war on the Prinsengracht": Anne Frank House (landmark), Trouw at 493, the frog stone at 535, Homomonument.
- Theme thread "heritage fights": Huize Labor (Hendrick de Keyser), Runstraat 14-18 (Van Houten), the old library (small-scale infill), Raadhuisstraat breakthrough.
