## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Mixed heritage in groups.** The Koningsplein group mixes municipal records with national 518359 (Kosmos); the Keizersgracht church group mixes national 518347 with municipal 200422 and 208059; Huis Geloof is all national. `heritage_status` follows the pinned building. If the map wants "National + municipal monument" for mixed groups, change those two.
- **Herbermann (202023):** the slice's architect field says A.N. Godefroy; the register text says Jacot designed Herbermann, Broekmans, Meijer and Nieuw Engeland. The text follows the register's narrative and does not name the architect of Herbermann on its own.
- **Keizersgrachtkerk style:** RCE says neo-Venetian, nl.wiki says neo-Gothic. The text says "pointed-arch" only.
- **WWII memorial stone, Kerkstraat 109-111:** the register names a WWII memorial stone in the school plinth; nl.wiki says stones for Van der Baan and Ferwerda are in the walls on both the Kerkstraat and canal sides. Probably the same stone, but not confirmed, so the text does not link them. Both victims are named because their names are on public memorials.
- **Keizersgracht 453:** nl.wiki says Dunselman became commissioner of Opekta in 1935 "when Jews could no longer run companies". That chronology looks wrong (such rules came in 1941), so the text only says he was Otto Frank's lawyer and had his office or flat here in 1932. Vincent's commission is described as nl.wiki has it ("townscapes of The Hague"); other sources call them drawings.
- **Keizersgracht 516:** current tenant after BorzoGallery moved (2021) not found: `check: Facts`.
- **Leidsestraat 54:** register (2004) describes three statues (bronze, with a question mark); not confirmed recently: `check: Still there?`. De Gruyter's line of trade isn't stated in the sources I read, so the text doesn't call it a grocer.
- **Nieuwe Spiegelstraat 64:** Frankey's scaffold (2021) not confirmed since: `check: Still there?`.
- **Senembah (205024):** the register names only "De Senembah Maatschappij". "Grew tobacco in Deli, Sumatra" rests on search snippets (Leiden thesis, German Deli tobacco source); a reviewer may want a firmer source or to cut that clause.
- **Kerkstraat 342-344:** nl.wiki gives Russian Orthodox use 1995-2005, register says they moved in 2006; text says "until the mid-2000s". Current use unclear (an undated fundainbusiness listing offers it for rent or sale).
- **Kerkstraat 312-314:** register says it "appears to be" a club building; current use not stated.
- **Nieuwe Waalse Kerk:** Museum of Illusions opening date (July 2025) is from two sources (amsterdamnow, NH Nieuws), not a single explicit statement.
- **Foam:** foam.org mentions "sustainability upgrades 2026" with no closure dates; I didn't set `outlook`, but the body says to check before going. Set `Temporarily closed` if a closure is announced.
- **Museum Van Loon:** "history runs back to the East India Company" rests on the museum's VOC exhibitions and the Willem van Loon portrait in nl.wiki. I left out that Ferdinand Bol was Rembrandt's pupil (well known, but not in the sources I read).
- **Left out:** the Pride church service's start year ("for ten years", undated); Keizersgracht 554's dentist (Rogmans versus Boerma, unresolved); Nieuwe Spiegelstraat 8-18's current tenants (the register's "gallery, restaurant, office" is undated).
