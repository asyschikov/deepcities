## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Petit Versailles is no longer green.** The scout had it "saved" (Feb 2021). 1018 (17 Feb 2022) reports the UvA reversed course: a temporary teaching building (three storeys, 11 m, five to ten years) on that lawn; UvA permit May 2022 (contractor De Meeuw), REC V delivery listed in the UvA's 2023 plan. Told as "saved and lost". Not confirmed on site that REC V stands exactly on the whole lawn.
- **Grier children's death dates**: the stones (and wiki list) give 19-11-1943 for Manfred and Martin; the 1018 dossier gives 31 March 1944 for both. Left out of the text.
- **Jacob Barend van Thijn** (1018: "recently learned he died around 1944") has no stone in the list; left out.
- **Fernandes Nuneshuis founding**: hofjesinamsterdam says founded 1786, Joods Amsterdam says by will around 1788 (Fernandes Nunes 1721–1788). Text says "under the will of Joseph Fernandes Nunes (died 1788)".
- **Russensjoel status**: only a 2004 NIK listing (Shabbat and festival mornings) found; Ons Amsterdam ("Lief en leed in de Jodenkerkstraat", per SCOUT-G: restored, in use again since 1987) not read (robots; browser not used). Hence `check: Still there?`.
- **Weesperstaete dates**: design 1966, completed and let 1971 (wiki lead says "uit 1969"); `built: 1971 (design 1966)`. Current tenant not stated (wiki's "Ontwikkelingsbedrijf" rests on a 2008 link); text says "offices again" (Arcam).
- **Tulpplein naming**: wiki says the square was named in 1875; Stadsherstel says "Professor" was added to the street (Tulpstraat) only in 1946. Text keeps the two apart.
- **Jonge Rembrandt**: wiki says the 1968 original was never finished; text says only "made in 1968", the mould went to Schiphol.
- **Wisteria**: only city sources (CBH advice 2024 and the tree register); the 2018 green facade prize is cited by both but its organiser's page was not found.
- **Urban Jungle**: the title comes from 1018 only; Folia and Street Art Cities do not use it. Rodermans' earlier corona mural at Sarphatistraat/Weesperplein and the Centrum Begroot proposal to replace it with "Groene Urban Jungle" (June 2024) left out: outcome unknown.
