## Settled

- **Bevrijdingslinde van Jordaankinderen**: no text change. The two sources only disagree about which children's names are in the urn, and the text just says "an urn with children's names". Source: 4en5mei.nl monument 1610, read 25 Nov 2026.
- **Canvas van Amsterdam**: Hedy Tjin's *Dit is de koudste zomer van de rest van je leven* has hung since August 2026 and stays until April 2027. Sources: Het Parool, 14 Sep 2026; Mokumse Muurtjes Instagram (the project's initiator); nl.wikipedia *Canvas van Amsterdam*. Body updated in EN and NL.
- **De Poezenboot**: no text change. The hours in the text (Tue, Fri and Sat 13:00 to 15:00, six visitors at most, no booking since late 2025) match the boat's visitor page on 25 Nov 2026. The holiday closure from 25 Dec 2026 to 2 Jan 2027 is confirmed there but is not in the text.
- **Metz & Co**: since May 2024 the building has been NIO House, a showroom for an electric car brand, with a café. Rietveld's glass room had been shut for years and was open free on 17 to 19 Sep 2026 for the GLUE design route. Sources: nl.wikipedia *Metzgebouw*; nio.com; ELLE NL, 14 Sep 2026. Note: Wikipedia says the roof room "reopened in May 2024", while ELLE says it "was shut for years". The text therefore says it opens "only now and then". "Look from the street" was replaced by "you can walk in".
- **Torensluis**: Stichting De Torensluis runs the vaults as a neighbourhood house for residents' own activities. The city handed over management in 2017 and the rooms in 2022. It is not a café or event venue. Sources: detorensluis.nl (undated pages, read 25 Nov 2026); Het Parool, 11 Mar 2017. The text now says the vaults are not open as a sight.
- **Vijzelstraat**: no. 66-80 was renovated in 2014, has been called Prins & Keizer since, and is let as offices (Spaces coworking at 68-78). Sources: nl.wikipedia *Vijzelbank*; Google Maps business listings; Funda in business. One sentence added.
- **Café Theo Ruiter**: still a café, now Bar Theo, named after him. It is a music and DJ bar and was an ADE venue in Oct 2026. The business changed hands on 1 Nov 2026 (Adhoc horecamakelaars, 3 Nov 2026), and the name may change later. Ons Amsterdam gives an earlier name, Struik. One sentence added.
- **Electric Ladyland**: still running. Its online booking calendar on 25 Nov 2026 shows tours on Thu 3, Fri 4 and Sat 5 Dec 2026 at 14:00 to 17:00, some already partly booked. The days changed from "Wednesday to Saturday" to "Thursday to Saturday".
- **Keuken van 1870**: the restaurant at Spuistraat 4 is now DAY'S StoneGrill 1870 (own site, TheFork, Tripadvisor). Name added.
- **Singel 458**: still a hotel, Supper Boutique Suites, run by Supper, whose club is in the Odeon at no. 460. Sources: supper.nl hotel-suites page, Tripadvisor and Booking listings (2026), amsterdamopdekaart (2023). Supper's own pages do not print the 458 address. Judgement call: three independent listings agree.
- **Tkramrepus**: open, now also named De Omgekeerde Supermarkt. The shop's own site gives Wed and Thu 10:00 to 14:00, about 150 customers a week, and Czaar Peterstraat 233. Its 2025 annual report came out in April 2026. It was shut on 25 and 26 Nov 2026 because of a leak. The summary no longer gives "300 households a week" as a current figure.

## Still open (updated note)

- **Geldersekade 105**: the Joods Monument caption (M. Polak, 7 Jun 2024) dates the raid to 25 or 26 May 1943 and marks no. 105 as the lowest house on the right. The couple died at Sobibor on 21 May, so the photo cannot show their arrest. The text already avoids saying it does. The house still needs matching on site.
- **Pakketpostgebouw**: now called Gebouw De Oost and still owned by NS. In 2024 NS had TAK architecten research the front and restore its original look (TAK news, 27 Nov 2024). What the inside is used for was not found.
- **Swigtershofje**: ownership was traced only as far as 1978 (Stichting Leefklimaat) and 1991 (De Key), from hofjesinamsterdam (2018). No current source. Whether the gate is ever open is still unknown.
- **Zuid-Afrikahuis**: the hours are confirmed. The eagle was put over the door around 1620, but the front was rebuilt in 1877 (Amsterdam Monumentenstad). Whether the eagle is still visible, and which floor the reading room is on, needs a visit. Commons photos from 2018 show the entrance up the stoop.
- **Walk of Pride**: the city (video, July 2026) counts 52 tiles; the foundation says "ruim 50". The pin still has to go on Bruggeman's tile.
- **Volkskoffiehuis**: Google Maps lists a "Pizza Giuseppe" at no. 4 with no reviews and a website that does not resolve, so it is not confirmed. nl.wikipedia *Kadijksplein 4* gives 1896 and Publieke Werken (as a waiting and pay-out house for dock workers, run by the R.K. Volksbond), against the text's 1902 Volksbond story. This conflicts with the text and should be checked against the city register before anyone relies on it. The text is unchanged.
- **O Johnny**: a hobby blog post of 1 Sep 2026 shows the gaper. That is not enough on its own.
