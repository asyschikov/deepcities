## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Rembrandt statue (518357)** parked as part of the A-list Rembrandtplein. It is the oldest surviving public statue in Amsterdam and the first statue of an artist in the Netherlands, so it could stand as its own place pitched on that; needs a decision.
- **Garden houses**: gg-west-4 has a parallel group "Garden houses behind the western canals" (different numbers, 280101-280803 range). Mine is titled "Garden houses behind the southern canals" to match. They could be merged into one canal-belt place later. None is visible from the street, so I ranked it Okay; the text points to the Open Tuinen Dagen (Museum Van Loon, June) without claiming these particular gardens take part.
- **Knegt / Breman 1891**: the city registers disagree whether no. 919 or no. 921 was built for Knegt's widow; the text avoids the house number.
- **Schwartze-huis (4481)**: the name suggests the painter Thérèse Schwartze, but her studio was at Prinsengracht 1021; Van Eeghen wrote on "Het Schwartze Huis" and its restoration (Amstelodamum 1974) but I could not read it, so the link is unproven and the record is filed as no story found. Worth a look by someone with Amstelodamum access.
- **Deutzenhofje**: the spendthrift-son motive is told as "the story goes" (nl.wiki only says "schijnt").
- **Prinsengracht 703-705**: slice `year_from` 1875 versus register 1897/1905; I used the register.
- **De Krijtberg**: nl.wiki gives a 31 m vault height flagged "Bron?"; left out.
- **Thorbecke**: Haaxman's 1918 claim that a Scheveningen coachman posed for the statue rests on one source; left out. Van Houwelingen's Spinoza swap proposal also left out to keep the length.
- **Odeon**: wiki gives the cinema start as 1895 or 1912; the text says only "then it was a cinema". Tenants confirmed only to 2017-2018, so `check: Facts`.
- **Schiller**: Café Schiller's site is live (Nov 2026); nh-hotels.com refused curl, so the hotel's 2026 status is a `check`.
- **Cineac, Rembrandtplein 44, Vijzelstraat 66-80, Singel 458, De Bazel**: current use not confirmable by curl (amsterdam.nl rate-limited me; Parool behind a consent wall); each carries a `check_note`.
- **Muntstaete**: the TQ startup hub (since 2016) is unconfirmed for 2026, so the text does not mention the current tenant.
- **Content fixes**: removed an unsourced "grandest hofje gate" claim; on the Knegt front the figures are called "Black" rather than "African", as the register gives no origin.

## Route ideas

- **Rembrandtplein to the Pijp**: Thorbecke statue, Schiller, Rembrandtplein 44, Cineac (opposite Tuschinski), the Carlton and Muntstaete with the garage across the Reguliersdwarsstraat, De Bazel, down the widened Vijzelstraat to the Vijzelbank, then east along the Prinsengracht: Knegt's office, Elizabeth Wolffschool, Deutzenhofje, the Mennonite orphanage, Prinsengrachtziekenhuis, ending at Reguliersgracht 57-59. About 2.5 km.
- **Singel bend**: De Krijtberg, Singel 458, Odeon, Singel 428, then round to the Carlton crash site at Singel 542-548. Short, under 1 km, pairs with the Flower Market.
- **Open Tuinen Dagen** (June): the garden houses behind the Herengracht and Keizersgracht together with Museum Van Loon.
