## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Weteringschans villas and Mathilde Willink**: nl.wikipedia (Weteringschans) says no. 22 was lived in by Mathilde Willink and by the couturier Frank Govers. No reference is given, and neither person's nl.wikipedia article mentions the address. Left out as a single weak claim; worth checking (e.g. Stadsarchief, Delpher). If confirmed it would lift the villas place (Willink was found dead at her home in 1977).
- **Villas: how many were demolished** for Peper en Zout is not stated (nl.wikipedia: "gesloopte villa's"); the text says "older villas of the row".
- **Singel 512 dates**: the register says the vaudeville theatre was commissioned in 1856 and calls Gosschalk's office front his first work, but gives no year for it (Gosschalk was born in 1838). The text gives no year for Gosschalk's front. Mrs Mesdag-van Calcar is named as in the register; no first name was confirmed. The Coba Ritsema link rests on the register's mention plus nl.wikipedia saying she had a studio on the Singel; the text only says the building is linked to the Joffers through her. "By the flower market" rests on the Wikidata image name for this monument ('The Flower Market.jpg'); the pin is the register point.
- **Keizersgracht 461 and Fahrenheit**: the register says he taught statics and optics in the 1675 corner house; nl.wikipedia says he lectured on chemistry in Amsterdam from 1718. Both are in the text. No plaque found; none is claimed.
- **Metz & Co**: current tenant of Keizersgracht 455 after Abercrombie & Fitch (left end 2022) unknown, and whether Rietveld's roof room is open: `check: Facts`. Kerkstraat 45-49 is named "Liberty" in Wikidata; the register's wording is unclear, so the name is not used.
- **Bensdorp**: nl.wikipedia says the Eden Hotel is in the former Amsterdam factory by Joling; the Eden Hotel's booking site gives Amstel 144 and says it is in a former sugar refinery and chocolate factory. The text says the old Amsterdam complex now houses the Eden Hotel.
- **Javasche Bank**: current use of 666-668 not found; the text claims nothing about it. Escompto 575: nl.wikipedia says "thans de Garantibank", undated; left out.
- **AMI milk factory**: register only; no other source on the AMI was found (title guesses failed).
- **Barlaeus**: nl.wikipedia lists Multatuli among old pupils, but that was the Latin school before this building, so he is not mentioned. W.F. Hermans is in the alumni list and published his first piece in the school paper.

## Leads

- Weteringschans 26-28, "Peper en Zout" offices by Frans van Gool, municipal monument since December 2022 against the owner's will (https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weteringschans_26-28): a modern-heritage fight, not in this slice.
- Commiezenhuisje, Weteringschans 30 by the Weteringpoortbrug: 1848 toll house, now a pumping station (nl.wikipedia Weteringschans).
- The Barlaeus has its own memorial inside to pupils and teachers killed in the war, and has adopted the monument in the H.M. van Randwijkplantsoen (nl.wikipedia Barlaeus Gymnasium).
- Keizersgracht 508, cigar shop by A.C. Bleys with a bust of P.C. Hooft, named in register text 518498.
- Leidsestraat 86-88, Art Deco ornament with Christiaan Huygens; Leidsestraat 103, home of the actor Louis Bouwmeester (nl.wikipedia Leidsestraat).
