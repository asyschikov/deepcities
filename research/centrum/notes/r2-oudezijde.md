## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Tree height:** 36 m (city register, CBH 2024, LRMB) against 35 m (Bomenstichting walk 2021). I used 36 m.
  - Girth: 472 cm (city) against 482 cm (LRMB). I used 4.72 m.
  - The courtyard is only open on weekdays, during UvA building hours.
- **Chet Baker:** wiki gives the hotel as Prins Hendrikkade 55 or 52-58, while RCE 4131 is no. 55. I left the house number out of the address.
  - Date: wiki says the night of 12 to 13 May 1988, d'Oude Binnenstad says 12 May. I wrote "night of 12 to 13 May".
  - "Room 210" is from d'Oude Binnenstad only.
- **GOD ROEPT U:**
  - The James Irwin lighting (1981), the Karsten and De Wereld hotel names and the Salvation Army 1905-1971 all come from one resident's account in d'Oude Binnenstad 2021. They are attributed as such in the text.
  - The city register text confirms only that it was a late 19th-century hotel.
- **Bijleveld sluice:**
  - "40 km away" comes from the slice hook and the Ons Amsterdam article, which I did not open (browser-only).
  - The wiki confirms the water board of Bijleveld and the 1492 handover. I did not add the scout's 1413 detail.
- **Mata Hari:** the wiki says gambling went on "deep into the 21st century", while d'Oude Binnenstad 2009 says the casino closed in the early 1990s. The text says only that it went on long after Adriaanse's death (1996). The owner is not named in the body.
- **Blaauwlakenblok** (from earlier in this slice): the register says "H.G. Blom" and Stadsherstel says "Bom". The body avoids the name.
- **Ranks:** S-113 and S-102 came in as Amazing and I set both to Nice, which keeps Amazing at 3 of 21. Bet van Beeren could go back to Amazing if the curator prefers.
- **Body length:**
  - Blaauwlakenblok (294) and Veilinghuisje (270) run over the 250 target, Look for lines included.
  - Several Okay bodies are 170 to 235 words rather than 80 to 150.
- **Pins:**
  - The Pijlsteeg pin (52.37232, 4.8953) and the sluice pin (52.3731, 4.90045) have fewer than 6 decimals; both are taken from the slice.
  - The Mata Hari pin is the slice pin on the Korte Niezel.
- **Bureau Warmoesstraat:** the plaque date is from NH Nieuws 353682, published "today". I wrote October 2025, which matches the scout's dating; check it against the article date if needed.
