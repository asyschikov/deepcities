## Enrich edits

- **Theo Thijssen's school** (plantage-1.yaml): body sentence on the 2007 plaque and the 2010 monument made exact in both languages (three teachers and sixteen deaf pupils named on the plaque; monument by Truus Menger-Oversteegen, unveiled by Anna Vos-van Dam, the only Dutch deaf survivor of Auschwitz). Two Look for / Let op lines: the sign drawings "wereld, doof, blijven" (the world stayed deaf); the 1948 Van Det and Davidsschool plaque just behind the front door of no. 1. Sources 4en5mei 3545, 2943, 4577 added.
- **Bridges with wartime names** (plantage-1.yaml): Look for / Let op line for the Walter Süskindbrug plaque (named 1972). Source 4en5mei 2947 added.
- **Glass Palace of the Joodsche Invalide** (plantage-2.yaml): Look for / Let op line for the August 1947 G.G. en G.D. staff plaque for fifteen colleagues. Source 4en5mei 1608 added.
- **The city workhouse (Dr. Sarphatihuis)** (plantage-2.yaml): Look for / Let op line for the Ramses Shaffy portrait with "Leef!" on the Nieuwe Kerkstraat wall, restored for 29 August 2023. Second source found: stadscuratorium.nl (the scout had only 1018). Sources added.
- **Weesperflat** (plantage-2.yaml): one sentence in both languages on the 50th-birthday festival for past and present residents, September 2016, about a thousand expected. Source 1018 added.

## Conflicts and uncertainties

- **Shelter capacity, Weesperplein**: 12,000 (nl.wikipedia, Arcam) against 3,000 (ONH, the 1960s plan). Written as "plans of the 1960s spoke of 3,000; the finished shelter is usually given as 12,000".
- **Wingnut planting year**: 1860 (1018) against about 1900 (nl.wikipedia, city register). Written "around 1900". Girth also differs (almost 4 m in 1018, 433 cm in 2011 per wiki, 507 cm in the register): left out.
- **Struikeldrempel date**: the 1018 post of 31 January 2025 says "eind februari", which cannot be; IamExpat says Holocaust Memorial Day 2025. Written "January 2025". Exact platform not confirmed, hence `check: Location`.
- **Gerrit van der Veen's execution place**: Overveen (nl.wikipedia, 4en5mei 1518) against Wassenaar (4en5mei 1602). Left out.
- **Shaffy mural date**: 2010 (Ramses Shaffy Fonds via 1018) against "shortly after his death in 2009" (Stadscuratorium). Written "soon after his death".
- **Anne Frank seedling**: March 2022 (CBH) against April 2022 (register). Written "spring 2022".
- **Brilleslijper arrest place**: Naarden (Anne Frank research) against 't Hooge Nest in Huizen (nl.wikipedia). Written "a villa in the Gooi". The relation of the Tak family to the Brilleslijpers is not known and is not claimed.
- **Holl pavilion**: no completion year found (stevenholl.com refused, ArchDaily has none); Het Oosten moved in 1998 per amsterdamopdekaart (in the extra-east draft). Year left out. Scout's "one of the few buildings by a foreign star architect" had no source: left out.
- **Artisplein spoonbills**: "largest collection in the world" is Arcam's claim only; attributed to Arcam in the text.
- **Telefoon van de Wind**: installation date not found (OSM node created May 2024). Left out.
- **Krop's burghers**: the wiki statue list puts Poorter and Poortersvrouw (1940–1942) on the Weesperstraat and OSM puts two Krop statues at the Weesperplein end; which building is not confirmed, so the Look for line says only "at the Weesperplein end".
- **Not from my slice, noticed in passing**: the round 1 draft "Bridges with wartime names" calls the man of the Halverstadbrug "Felix Halverstad" in the body while its own source is the nl.wikipedia article "Raphaël Halverstad". Worth a check by whoever owns plantage-1.
- **Left out, unverified**: Ko van Geemert's poem plaque in the Wertheimpark (2012); the 1975 metro demonstration's details beyond the wiki photo caption; the 2024 boulders on the Plantage Middenlaan.
