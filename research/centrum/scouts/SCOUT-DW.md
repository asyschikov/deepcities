# Centrum scout DW: theme D (local papers and blogs, Dutch and English), zone West

Scouted 2026-11-24. Zone West of stadsdeel Centrum: Jordaan, Haarlemmerbuurt (with the Western Islands), Grachtengordel-West. Nothing is written to the map: the user picks.

Raw material, helper notes and downloads: `$S/centrum-scout-DW/` (helper-A.md, helper-B.md, helper-C.md for the Wijkkrant; hD/notes-*.md for Buurtkrant Zeven and the Binnenkrant). Every pin below was point-checked with `areas.locate` and is in borough Centrum and the wijk named. Citation form for the papers: Wijkkrant Jordaan & Gouden Reael = "WJGR <year>, <id>" (PDF `https://jordaangoudenreael.nl/documenten/<id>.pdf`); Buurtkrant Zeven = "Zeven <nr>" (PDF `https://oudestadt.nl/wp-content/uploads/Zeven<nr>.pdf`); Binnenkrant = "BK <nr>".

**55 candidates** (DW-01 to DW-55): Jordaan 22 (1 Amazing, 15 Nice, 6 Okay), Haarlemmerbuurt with the Western Islands 16 (1 Amazing, 10 Nice, 5 Okay), Grachtengordel-West 17 (2 Amazing, 9 Nice, 6 Okay). Two are merge candidates with monument records (DW-33, DW-40); the rest are new places. Items still marked "Still there?" or "Location" need a site visit before writing.

## Shortlist

### Public art, walls, war and memory (sweeps 2 and 3)

**Jordaan**

**DW-01 Het Gangenproject: the vanished alleys of the Willemsstraat in the pavement.** Pin 52.381436, 4.885284, information panel on the side wall of Willemsstraat 22; the tiles run between numbers 22 and 110. The filled-in Goudsbloemgracht had some 57 alleys (gangen) to slum courts behind the fronts; a handful survive. The Jordaanmuseum (Mieke Krijger, with architect Yuri Werner) proposed in 2006 to mark them in the pavement, and it took nine years: unveiled 5 March 2015 with the re-laid street, a panel of old maps and photos, and a monthly alley walk. Local voice: Krijger calls it "een Lieu de Memoire, èn een eerbetoon aan de stratenmakers"; her quiz on the walk: "Wie weet het verschil tussen een gang en een steeg?". The slum landlord Jacobus Mens (1805 to 1869) was "De Bokkebek", "de burgemeester van de Willemsstraat". Lead: WJGR 2006, 00000083; 2015, 00000015 p1 and p3; 2017, 00000112 p4; 2021, 00000191 p9. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willemsstraat_(Amsterdam). Status: panel there 2017; WJGR 2023 (00000202) "24 uur per dag te zien". Caveats: counts differ (56 in 1778 in the paper, 57 in 1775 on Wikipedia); who made the tiles is credited to the Jordaanmuseum, design Jaap Elzas. Rank **Amazing**. New place.

**DW-02 The anonymous sculptor of the Anjeliersstraat.** Pin 52.377028, 4.880194, the harmonica player on the front of Anjeliersstraat 175. Small iron figures appear on Jordaan walls with no maker: the "(trek)harmonicaspeler" placed "in het holst van de nacht" in 1994, street-organ figures at Anjeliersstraat 82 (2010/2011), a figure on the street-lamp cable at the Eerste Anjeliersdwarsstraat, and one on café Het Bruine Paard that went missing in 2015. Wikipedia credits them to an Amsterdam doctor who wants to stay anonymous, the same hand as the Boomzagertje and the Violist; the girl on skates "Madeliefje" (2007) on the Madelievenplein was wrecked three times and stolen in 2010. Local voice: the Wijkkrant calls them "geveltoeristen" and ran "Opsporing verzocht: Geveltoerist vermist" (2016), adding "Geruchten gaan dat koningin Beatrix ze heeft vervaardigd"; What's up with Amsterdam repeats the Beatrix rumour. Lead: WJGR 2016, 00000002; WJGR 2007, 00000073/74/76 (Madeliefje). Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Lijst_van_beelden_in_Amsterdam-Centrum (rows 1994, 2007, 2010/2011); https://whatsupwithamsterdam.com/the-mystery-of-the-sculptures/. Status: harmonica man photographed on Wikipedia (undated); Wijkkrant 2016. Caveats: how many figures remain needs a walk; the 2007 Wijkkrant "Monument voor de Onbekende Hond" on the Madelievenplein was an April Fools' joke and never existed. Rank **Nice**. New place (group the street's figures under one pin).

**DW-03 Kees de jongen on the "Tuinpleintje".** Pin 52.377240, 4.883540, side wall of Tuinstraat 61. Theo Thijssen's Jordaan schoolboy, after Dick Matena's 2012 comic, painted by Ellen Bouman and Ed Simons in September 2025, paid from Centrum Begroot after a Theo Thijssen Museum volunteer saw Matena's Kees on a comics wall in Antwerp; a first wall fell through because one neighbour opposite was against it; pupils of the Theo Thijssenschool sang the Kees de jongen song at the unveiling. Local voice: "de jongen die na ruim honderd jaar 'thuis' kwam"; locals call the spot "het Tuinpleintje". Lead: WJGR 2025, 00000219 and 00000220 p3; 2026, 00000226 p1. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muurschildering_Kees_de_jongen. Status: there November 2026. Caveat: day of unveiling (Friday 12 September 2025 per the announcement). Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DW-04 Bevrijdingslinde van Jordaankinderen.** Pin 52.377630, 4.879982 (pin approximate: the plantsoen at the corner of Westerstraat and Marnixstraat). A lime planted on 4 May 1946, paid by the pupils of thirteen Jordaan schools, for the liberation and for the Jordaan children who did not survive the war; an urn with their names lies under it; the stone came in 1949. In 1984 a substation was to replace it; after three years of protest the substation went next to it (1987) and the stone onto a column. In 2021 residents fought its use as a dog toilet and urinal. Local voice: headline "Bewoners verzetten zich tegen oorlogsmonument als pispaaltje"; "Ook de sokkel is soms letterlijk 'het pispaaltje'". Lead: WJGR 2021, 00000191 p1 and p3. Second: https://tracesofwar.com/sights/28322/Bevrijdingslinde-Jordaankinderen-Amsterdam.htm (lime on the Marnixplein, inscription 4 May 1946); a 1950 photo "Defilé langs bevrijdingslinde" on Wikimedia Commons. Status: tree and stone there April 2021. Rank **Nice** (Amazing if the urn checks out). New place.

**DW-05 Noordermarkt: what the locals point at.** Pin 52.379750, 4.886185 (pin approximate, the square by the Noorderkerk). Not the market: (1) the bronze plaque on the Noorderkerk for the protest meeting of February 1941 that sent out the strike call, found by a dog walker in late 2010 leaning against a house in the Boomstraat after bronze thieves dropped it; (2) "een raadselachtig paaltje" by the Woutertje Pieterse statue, remnant of the second tap of clean dune water (a cent a bucket; the first was at the Haarlemmerpoort), with the basin still in the ground; (3) the old churchyard outline laid in the paving; (4) the corner of the Prinsengracht where the wall was hacked away to fit the street sign; (5) the Christmas tree that "kerstboomvrouw" Mireille of the Lindengracht fought back from the cuts in 2025 and 2026 (children hang the baubles, café Hegeraad lends the ladder). Local voice: "zijn er in de Jordaan bronsdieven actief?" (2010); "bij de gemeente als een leeuwin gevochten" (2026). Lead: WJGR 2010, 00000057 p8; 2016, 00000006; 2026, 00000221 pp8 to 9; 2026, 00000226 p1. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Lijst_van_beelden_in_Amsterdam-Centrum (Monument Jordaanoproer and the Noorderkerk plaque rows). Status: February and November 2026. Caveats: whether the 2010 plaque was put back is not in the paper (check on site); the tree is seasonal and its future is "ongewis" with halved budgets. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DW-06 Jaco's gable stone: the folk hero who never was.** Pin 52.369217, 4.880005, Elandsgracht 73 (stone on 71 to 77, listed). The stone recalls Jacob Frederik Muller, "Jaco", executed 1718, in Jordaan legend a Robin Hood with a fort full of secret passages on this spot. In 2007 a thesis by Frans Thuijs took it apart, and the Wijkkrant printed: "Jaco was geen Amsterdammer. Het fort van Jaco heeft nooit bestaan. En Jaco was helemaal geen Robin Hood". Local voice: a reader from the Prinsengracht, "De Jordaan is dus een mythe kwijt." Lead: WJGR 2007, 00000074 p3 and 00000078 p3; 2008, 00000068 p7. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_van_Sjako (the fort probably an invention of Justus van Maurik, 1901). Status: stone there (Wikipedia photo). Caveat: the squat Fort van Sjakoo (Jodenbreestraat) borrows the name: not this. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DW-07 Johnny Jordaanplein: the people's statue garden.** Pin 52.370150, 4.882267, head of the Elandsgracht at the Prinsengracht. Five busts of Jordaan singers (1991 to 2010) on a square built as a witkar station, a name the council never formally gave. In 2005 canal-belt residents planned a fountain square with the statues swept into a heap; the self-appointed keeper collected 500 signatures in two days and Manke Nelis was unveiled by mayor Cohen on 30 October 2005; in 2010 René Froger paid for his parents' statue himself, to the borough's surprise. Local voice: the keeper on the planners, "ik noem ze altijd de hondenclub"; the paper calls her "de burgemeester van het Johnny Jordaanplein"; she nailed up the name sign herself. Lead: WJGR 2005, 00000086 p5 and 00000091 p1; 2010, 00000057; 2015, 00000014 p4. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Jordaanplein. Status: Wikipedia photo 2024. Caveat: well known to Amsterdammers; pitch the self-management and fights. Rank **Nice**. New place (keep the 1880 krul on the square as a Look for).

**DW-08 Gedenksteen Bloemgracht 82.** Pin 52.374874, 4.880850, Bloemgracht 82. The upper floors were a base and arms store of the Raad van Verzet; Ko Stevense, Jan Keune and Durk Wolters died in the house shortly before the liberation. A new stone went on the front on 4 May 2014; the third-floor resident has held an "Open Verzetshuis" with relatives' stories. Lead: WJGR 2013, 00000039 p5; 2014, 00000024; 2023, 00000201. Second: none found. Status: 2023 (plaque text quoted). Caveat: private house, plaque only. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**Haarlemmerbuurt and the Western Islands**

**DW-09 De Reus van Bickerseiland.** Pin 52.384881, 4.890289, corner Touwslagerstraat and Hollandse Tuin. A 1.70 m wooden Roman soldier, a 1951 replica (by old ship's carpenter D. Rijnsdorp) of the figure that stood on Jan Bicker's house and later on the gate of machine works Jonker & Zoon. When Jonker left in 1972 the director took it to Alicante; from 2000 islanders searched for almost ten years, found it with a grandson in Canada in 2006, it nearly went down in a storm off Cape Verde, came back in 2009 and was unveiled by mayor Van der Laan on a plinth by island artist Egon Schrama with a line by a neighbour cut in the steel. Local voice: "de houten nachtwacht en tot voor kort verdwaalde mascotte van Bickerseiland"; "maar een meter zeventig hoog, niet echt een reus dus". Lead: WJGR 2009, 00000058 p1; 2011, 00000047 p6; 2019, 00000160. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reus_van_Bickerseiland. Status: Wikipedia photos July 2021. Caveat: the paper gives 29 June 2010 and 2011 for the unveiling; Wikipedia "vanaf 2011": use 2011. Rank **Amazing**. New place.

**DW-10 "De walvis" and the poem on the quay, Hollandse Tuin.** Pin 52.383269, 4.890133, waterside of the Hollandse Tuin. "Omgevallen boom" (1983) by Pieter Engels and Shlomo Koren, a slab of black granite with a half-round lid sawn out and tilted up, was meant as a fountain spraying a tree; the islanders call it "De walvis". The city later put three lamps in the basin without asking the artists. Fifty metres on, neighbour Carla Bogaards's lines from "Walk on water" are cut in orange letters in the low quay wall (2006), after she defended them before the council committee. Local voice: Engels, "Met een slokje op zou ik er misschien ook wel een walvis in zien", and on the lamps, "Dat mag eigenlijk niet"; the poet, "Sommige mensen voelen aan de letters." Lead: WJGR 2007, 00000072 p3; 2011, 00000047 and 00000048 p3. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omgevallen_boom ; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollandse_Tuin_(Amsterdam). Status: Wikipedia photos August 2020. Rank **Nice**. New place (the poem as its Look for).

**DW-11 Tuin Bickersgracht Noordkop, the bird board and the yellow submarine.** Pin 52.385765, 4.888761 (pin approximate, north head of Bickerseiland by children's farm De Dierencapel, Bickersgracht 207). A neighbour-kept "tuinreservaat" of about 200 m2 since 2000: pond, fruiting shrubs, floating bird islands with a chick gangplank, and a board of 50 bird species drawn by two neighbourhood designers (2014); in 2015 the group fought a plan to fell the trees. The children's farm next door was saved by a 2006 campaign and has its own Sint Maarten animal lanterns. Amsterdamian's writer calls it her lunch spot "where the yellow submarine is anchored". Local voice: "een van de mooiste plekjes op aarde"; "als je naar boven kijkt waan je je bijna in een bos". Lead: WJGR 2014, 00000027 p7; 2015, 00000010 p9; 2019, 00000161 p4. Second: https://amsterdamian.com (Amsterdam Gardens post, 2025 or later). Status: garden 2019, farm in every 2026 issue. Caveat: the submarine is a private boat; outcome of the tree plan not found. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DW-12 The Oranjeboom on the Zandhoek.** Pin 52.387918, 4.889912 (pin approximate: the fenced tree in the quay row by Zandhoek 10). A royal lime planted in September 1898 for Wilhelmina's inauguration, photographed by Jacob Olie, born at Zandhoek 10. Local voice (columnist): "Destijds was het een kleine boom met een vrij groot hek ... nu een grote boom met een vrij klein hek", and "lekker handig om je fiets aan vast te maken". Lead: WJGR 2018, 00000146; 2020, 00000186 p4. Second: none found (nl.wikipedia Zandhoek silent). Status: March 2018. Caveats: which tree needs a look on site (the fence is the clue); where Olie lived in 1898 is unclear. Rank **Nice**. New place (with the Stolpersteine at Zandhoek 12 and the 2013 resistance stone at Zandhoek 10 as Look for).

**DW-13 Kunstroute Nieuwe Houttuinen.** Pin 52.384283, 4.885467 (Jonas en de walvis). Behind the Haarlemmerdijk, the street the paper calls "een weg van niks naar nergens" became a small art route because one resident minded a bronze stuck in an underpass: Nic Jonk's "Jonas en de walvis" (1968, moved from the Beursplein, neighbours held a "doopfeestje" on 8 July 2016), Eja Siepman van den Berg's "Grote Staande" moved into a flower bed, Henk Ples's mosaic put up in 2016 thirteen years after his death, stripes on Hertzberger's porch pillars, the eye-twisting "Evergreen" foil (1996) in the underpasses against urinating and dumping, and the gable stone "Het walvisbeen is nu steen". Lead: WJGR 2016, 00000028. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonas_en_de_walvis_(Amsterdam) ; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evergreen_(Amsterdam). Status: Jonas photo August 2022, Evergreen August 2022. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DW-14 Jacob van Lennep and the first water tap.** Pin 52.385039, 4.882875, behind the Haarlemmerpoort. Lia van Vugt won a commission in 2004 for a bronze on a fountain that was never built; twelve years later she made a steel-and-glass statue for this spot (2016), on a plinth so no bikes can be chained to it. In 1851 the first dune water was tapped here, a cent a bucket for those without a house connection (the second tap was on the Noordermarkt, see DW-05). Local voice: "Sommige mensen zullen denken: 'Wie is dat nou weer?' Anderen vinden dat dat wel eens tijd werd"; the verse plaques fell off soon after the unveiling. Lead: WJGR 2016, 00000006 and 00000028 p8. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standbeeld_voor_Jacob_van_Lennep. Status: photo May 2021. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DW-15 Door wind bewogen beweging (Wicher Meursing).** Pin 52.388139, 4.891889, Westerdoksdijk between Westerdok and IJ. A tall wind-driven sculpture built after Meursing's death in May 2015 by friends from his design model and paid by them; unveiled 28 August 2015. The paper says it should have stood on Bickerseiland, where his great-grandfather had a yard. Local voice: "Daar krijgt het de kans om de wonderlijkste bewegingen te maken, en om soms doodstil te staan". Lead: WJGR 2015, 00000012. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Door_wind_bewogen_beweging. Status: photo August 2021. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DW-16 't Bronzen vingertje, Eenhoornsluis.** Pin 52.381219, 4.889363 (pin approximate, western abutment, south side). A tiny bronze finger for Nel de Jager (1953 to 2019), street manager of the Haarlemmerbuurt; prised out months after the 2021 unveiling, replaced on 29 July 2023 with a heavier anchor after fifty people crowdfunded. Local voice: "Voor de lezers met kwade bedoelingen: afblijven!". Lead: WJGR 2023, 00000202. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eenhoornsluis. Status: July 2023. Rank **Okay**. New place, or a Look for line at the bridge (the bridge also has one of the first Amsterdams Peil marks on the east quay wall, WJGR 2011, 00000049).

**Grachtengordel-West**

**DW-17 The Blauwburgwal bomb, 11 May 1940, and the stone with 44 names.** Pin 52.376621, 4.890038, plaque in the Blauwburgwal front of the 1952 corner block (Blauwburgwal 17 to 19 / Herengracht 107). At "vijf voor elf" on Saturday 11 May 1940 a German bomber dropped four bombs here: seven houses and Jan Pelle's brown café wiped out, 44 dead and 79 wounded, then largely forgotten. Retired chemical engineer and neighbour Fred Geukes Foppen, volunteering on a cause-of-death study, found the "bominslag" certificates, named all 44 (aged 6 to 72) and traced relatives; residents raised the money through Centrum Begroot and the stone was cut into the existing hardstone base of the corner building; Job Cohen unveiled it at 10.54 on 11 May 2020. Flowers are laid every year at 10.55; since 2022 the 4 en 5 mei comité runs it. One bomb did not explode and still lies in the mud of the Herengracht. Local voice: front-page cartoon "Zeg, die Geukes Foppen… liet de bom niet in een doofpot stoppen"; slogan "HET GEDENKTEKEN MOET ER KOMEN!". Lead: Zeven 118 p4, 121 p2, 124 pp2 to 3, 127 pp2 to 3, 131 p7, 135 p7. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardement_op_Amsterdam_(1940) (with the unexploded bomb, after NRC 7 December 2019); https://nos.nl/artikel/2170971-slachtoffers-onbekend-amsterdams-bombardement-krijgen-een-naam ; AT5 11 May 2022 (https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/215111). Status: plaque photographed March 2022; flowers May 2023 (Zeven 139). Rank **Amazing**. New place.

**DW-18 The gaper of Het Heertje, now "O Johnny".** Pin 52.376994, 4.889168, Herenstraat 7. A drugstore gaper by Joop Mackenbach (1980s) was stolen in November 2016; the "kale haak" stayed in the wall while the neighbourhood paper demanded its return. A replica by Rein Veldboer hung from 2018 until July 2020, when a mocking social-media remark set off a row about its black face and the shop took it down. Sculptor Adriaan Rees, who became a sculptor because of gapers, rang the bell with an offer, and in 2021 his "O Johnny" went up: a two-faced figure with a Medusa head of green-yellow tongues and drugstore tubes, only the pill on the tongue recalling the old gaper. Local voice: "de kale haak in de muur van Herenstraat 7 bleef ons al die tijd herinneren aan dit gevoelige verlies"; the neighbours' reactions to "deze opzienbarende nieuwkomer lopen nogal" apart (Zeven 133). Lead: Zeven 114, 119 p3, 128 p7, 133 pp1 and 9. Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaper_Oh_Johnny (photo June 2024); NH Nieuws 2020: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/269910 ; DutchNews 2020: https://www.dutchnews.nl/2020/07/gap-where-gaper-was-after-amsterdam-drugstore-image-accused-of-being-racist-caricature/. Status: June 2024. Caveat: sensitive; tell it factually. Rank **Amazing**. New place.

**DW-19 Bergstraat: De Naaister and the corn measurer who came home.** Pin 52.375490, 4.889598, Bergstraat 6 (the gate beside it). The residents' own facade bronze "De Naaister" by Caroline Kampfraath (2017), modelled in foam and cast with the foam texture, marks the street's change from window brothels and sewing workshops; and in autumn 2018 the gable stone of corn measurer Jan Stevens (bought here 1608: scoop, measure and strike-stick, "aan de strijkstok blijven hangen") returned after the house was demolished in 1955 and a neighbour spotted the stone in 2013 in the hall of the Korenmetershuis. Local voice: "Voortkomend uit een breed gedragen bewonersinitiatief heeft het beeld immers ook vele trotse 'eigenaren'." Lead: Zeven 116 p7, 117 p7, 124 p1. Second: none found yet (the statue list has a different "Naaister"). Status: 2018 to 2019; 2025 advert (Zeven 147) still names "het beeld De Naaister in de Bergstraat". Caveats: house number of the statue not printed (pin approximate); unveiling date unclear. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DW-20 Descartes at Westermarkt 6.** Pin 52.374880, 4.884448, Westermarkt 6. A plaque with his line on Dutch freedom ("Quel autre pays où l'on puisse jouir d'une liberté si entière?") marks the house of 1624 where Descartes lived in the summer of 1634 and, by his own note, fathered a daughter with the housekeeper Helena Jans; since 2019 a pavement tile in front reads "Ik denk, dus ik ben" (Humanistisch Fonds tiles project). Local voice: "Van Descartes rest de gevelsteen aan het huis van zijn grensoverschrijdend gedrag". Lead: Zeven 122 p5, 128 p4, 129 pp1 to 2. Second: to find (nl.wikipedia Westermarkt). Status: 2020. Caveat: beside the A-list Westerkerk: pitch on itself. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DW-21 Herenstraat 21: Meijer and Regina Turfkruier.** Pin 52.377154, 4.888725, Herenstraat 21. Two Stolpersteine laid at 11.00 on 28 January 2026 for the cigar maker and his wife, murdered in Auschwitz, at the request of the shopkeepers who opened here in 2023 and researched the house; inside, a memorial with Regina's only surviving objects, a ring and a silver purse. Zeven 129 notes only one other stumbling stone in Buurt 7 (Langestraat 84). Lead: Zeven 150 p3. Second: to find (stolpersteine-amsterdam). Status: January 2026. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**DW-22 Waterline, the red climber on the Leliegracht quay.** Pin 52.375300, 4.888800 (pin approximate, quay wall at the corner Leliegracht and Herengracht). ### Hidden history and behind the gate (sweeps 4 and 5)

**Jordaan**

**DW-23 Lekkeresluis, "de brug van tante Mie".** Pin 52.380459, 4.887954 (pin approximate, the bridge at Prinsengracht and Brouwersgracht). - **Lijnbaansgracht, Berta de Vriesbrug** (brug 128): named 2022 after the socialist of the 1917 potato riot and the 1934 Jordaanoproer, at her grandson's request (WJGR 2022, 00000199 p2).

- **Marnixstraat at the Brouwersgracht, "Bullebak"**: brick piece of the old bridge on corten steel by houseboat dweller Martie van der Loo, who asked for the bricks herself (2023) (nl.wikipedia Bullebak (beeld)).
