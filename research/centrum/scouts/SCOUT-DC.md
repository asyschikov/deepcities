# Centrum scout DC: theme D (local papers and blogs, Dutch and English), zone Core

Scouted 2026-11-24 (continued after a cut-off; the first scout's notes were checked and folded in).
Zone Core: Burgwallen-Nieuwe Zijde (NZ), Burgwallen-Oude Zijde (OZ), Nieuwmarkt/Lastage (NL).
Nothing is written to the map. Items are numbered DC-01 onward. Every pin was point-checked with
`research/centrum/scripts/areas.py` (borough Centrum, wijk as given). Pins come from OSM objects or
PDOK address points; "approx" means the object itself still needs fixing on site.

**42 candidates**: Burgwallen-Oude Zijde 21 (1 Amazing, 12 Nice, 8 Okay), Nieuwmarkt/Lastage 15
(1 Amazing, 6 Nice, 8 Okay), Burgwallen-Nieuwe Zijde 6 (4 Nice, 2 Okay). 21 dropped, 16 Look for
lines. Several items rest on one local paper only: each says "second source needed" where so.

## Shortlist

### Sweep 2: public art and walls

**Burgwallen-Oude Zijde**

**DC-01. "GOD ROEPT U / JESUS LOVES YOU", the neon over the station square.** Pin 52.376544, 4.899285;
Prins Hendrikkade 49-51. Blue neon letters on the corner house facing Centraal Station, the first
thing many arrivals read. The building, once Hotel Karsten, houses an evangelical community, a
bookshop and café Dwaze Zaken; d'Oude Binnenstad says the letters were switched on by the American
astronaut James Irwin. Local voice: the neighbourhood calls a coffee at Dwaze Zaken "een bakkie doen
bij Jezus" (d'Oude Binnenstad 2021-4); the paper also profiled the bookshop "onder het opschrift
'God roept u'" in December 2007. Lead: d'Oude Binnenstad 2021-4 and 2007-4 (oudebinnenstad.nl/kranten).
Second source: none independent yet; the Irwin story must be confirmed or attributed. Status: lit
per the 2020 and 2021 issues. Caveats: religious organisation, still in use; pin on the facade.
Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-02. The squatter in the gable of Vredenburgh.** Pin approx 52.375136, 4.900496; corner Oudezijds
Voorburgwal and Vredenburgersteeg. A stone squatter with a mohawk swinging a hammer, by Joris
Baudoin, unveiled 18 September 2011 in an empty niche of the top gable; the brief was to show
something from the last thirty years of the house, which was squatted in 1979 and since then holds
three living groups with dance, theatre and music studios; owner Ymere co-funded it. Local voice:
the buurtkrant reported it as the house's own history put on its front. Lead: d'Oude Binnenstad
2011-3 (October 2011). Second source needed (Kunstwacht, Stadscuratorium). Status: no later evidence
seen. Caveat: house number to fix on site (check: Location). Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-03. Belle and the bronze breast, Oudekerksplein.** Pin 52.374179, 4.897406 (Belle; the breast is
at 52.374024, 4.897906). Belle (Els Rijerse, 2007) was billed as the first statue of a prostitute,
inscribed "Respect sex workers all over the world"; a few steps away a bronze hand groping a breast is
set into the paving, left in 1993 by an anonymous maker ("De Onbekende Beeldhouwer"). Local voice:
What's up with Amsterdam and RLD guides tell the anonymous-sculptor story; the Wallen residents' paper
followed the window closures around the square. Lead: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/26034 (31 March
2007); OSM artwork tags; What's up with Amsterdam. Caveat: beside A-list Oude Kerk, pitched on itself;
the 1993 date and anonymity rest on blogs and OSM: check. Rank **Nice**. New place (group of two).

**DC-04. Trompettersteeg, the narrowest alley, and its hidden coat of arms.** Pin 52.373741, 4.897706.
About 90 cm wide (d'Oude Binnenstad; others say 1 m). In 2021 the street artist Dopie painted the
city arms so that they only come right from one spot (anamorphic); it was blacked out in anger and
restored in July 2021. Local voice: residents fought tourists queuing in the alley, and the paper
covered the mural as a reclaim. Lead: d'Oude Binnenstad 2021-1; https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/289333
(23 July 2021). Status: restored 2021; no later check. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-05. "Wandelend naar de Nieuwmarkt", the brick walkers on the Varkenssluis.** Pin 52.371837,
4.895850 (bridge over the Oudezijds Voorburgwal between Dam and Chinatown). Brick figures with granite
arms by Martie van der Loo (1986), walking toward the Nieuwmarkt; vandalised, remade and set back on 12
October 2011 as four Chinese walkers, with a lion dance; a fifth once sat on the Paulusbroedersluis.
Local voice: d'Oude Binnenstad called the saga "de hoop of sloop-soap". Lead: d'Oude Binnenstad 2011-4
and 2007-2. Second source needed (Kunstwacht). Status: back 2011, no later check. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**DC-06. Majoor Bosshardt on her bench.** Pin 52.375548, 4.900026; Oudezijds Voorburgwal by the
Oudezijds Armsteeg. A bronze of the Salvation Army officer who worked among prostitutes and addicts
on the Wallen sits on a bench; the bridge 211 over the Achterburgwal was named after her on 7 June
2013. Local voice: AT5 (2014) "Dronken toeristen onteren beeld Majoor Bosshardt"; d'Oude Binnenstad
wrote that the bench "gaf gedoe" and a cloth goes over her at night; in 2018 the Bulldog put up its own
tribute ("In gedachte bent u bij ons", AT5). Lead: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/104696 ; nl.wikipedia
Majoor Bosshardtbrug. Look for: the Majoor Bosshardthuis (Salvation Army museum) next door. Rank
**Nice**. New place.

**Burgwallen-Nieuwe Zijde**

**DC-07. The stamp market, Argus and the gnome, Nieuwezijds Voorburgwal.** Pin 52.371145, 4.890439
(Postzegelmarkt, by no. 280). A stamp and coin market has gathered here on Wednesday and Saturday
afternoons for about a century; in June 2022 the statue Argus (Saske van der Eerden) went up for the
vanished newspaper quarter of this canal, near the Kabouter (Ruchama Noorda) with its apple tree, a nod
to the Kabouters of 1970. Local voice: VVAB Binnenstad followed the square's redesign. Lead: VVAB
Binnenstad; nl.wikipedia Argus (beeld) and Kabouterstandbeeld. Status: market and statues present per
OSM 2026. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-08. Wijdesteeg, the mosaic alley.** Pin 52.371575, 4.890035. From 2014 the artist Otto Kruijssen
turned a dark alley used as a toilet into a mosaic street, first chased by enforcement four or five
times a day, then tolerated. Local voice: "Vroeger vonden ze dit maar een smerig straatje" (AT5, 7 Nov
2014). Lead: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/136873 . Status unknown after 2014 (check: Still there?).
Rank **Okay**. New place.

### Sweep 3: war and memory

**Nieuwmarkt/Lastage**

**DC-09. The plaque for the Nieuwmarkt children, De Flesseman.** Pin 52.371934, 4.900814; Nieuwmarkt 77.
Four boys of the neighbourhood were shot by German soldiers in 1944-45, among them Izy and Bari Baars
at the corner of the Recht Boomssloot, and Alex Evers on 12 April 1945, two minutes after curfew.
Former residents met in 1994, the plaque went up on 23 April 1995, and the two neighbourhood schools
(Sint Antoniusschool, De Witte Olifant) adopted it: every year children lay wreaths and release
balloons. Local voice: OpNieuw reports the ceremony nearly every year. Lead: OpNieuw 2006-2, 2007-1,
2012-1; d'Oude Binnenstad 2015-1. Check the 4en5mei.nl record before drafting. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-10. Vlooienburg, the plaque that took thirty years.** Pin approx 52.367646, 4.900165 (Stopera,
Amstel 1; fix on the plaque). The island quarter under today's Stopera was the heart of Jewish
Amsterdam; it was emptied by deportation, decayed and was cleared for the city hall and opera. A
memorial plaque for its murdered residents was unveiled in November 2022 after almost thirty years of
asking; since June 2026 a maquette and finds are on show inside the Stopera. Local voice: initiator
Anita Mizsahi, "het kan toch niet dat hier zomaar die Stopera op gezet i[s]" (NH). Lead:
https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/311053 ; https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/360894 ; nl.wikipedia
Vlooienburg; OpNieuw 2022-3 (invitation), 2026-3. Caveat: an OpNieuw letter (2023-1) dates the
monument 15 November 2023, which must be a slip for 2022 (the issue is of March 2023). Rank **Nice**.
New place.

**DC-11. Spinoza at the Stopera.** Pin 52.367940, 4.899201; Zwanenburgwal. Nicolas Dings' statue of
the philosopher, who was born on Vlooienburg, unveiled 24 November 2008, quoting "Het doel van de staat
is de vrijheid". Local voice: OpNieuw 2008-4 and its 2018 series "Spinoza heel actueel" with a
neighbourhood Spinoza expert. Lead: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/11341 ; OpNieuw 2008-4. Rank **Nice**.
New place (could merge with DC-10 if the user wants one Vlooienburg pin).

**DC-12. The Armenian genocide stone, Krom Boomssloot.** Pin 52.371530, 4.902078; Krom Boomssloot 22.
A carved cross with Armenian and Dutch text, unveiled 21 April 2018 on the front of the Armenian
Apostolic church and blessed by the archbishop; about 250 people came, and it is now the place of the
yearly 24 April commemoration. Local voice: "Het maakt me blij en verdrietig tegelijk" (a parishioner,
OpNieuw 2018-2). The bridge in front is the Armeensebrug. Lead: OpNieuw 2018-2. Second source needed
(4en5mei / church site). Rank **Okay**. New place.

**DC-13. Uilenburg: the Jewish bathhouse and the teacher's house.** Pin approx 52.370682, 4.905758;
Nieuwe Uilenburgerstraat 29-31. The former Jewish bathhouse held an emotional 4 May commemoration in
2019 ("Hier heeft mijn moeder gelopen", AT5); next to it the teacher's house of the Sophie Rosenthal
nursery school (1887), a municipal monument, is wanted by grid operator Liander for a substation.
Local voice: VVAB news 431 (2023) and 452 (2024) against the plan. Lead: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/193782
; VVAB news 431, 452. Outlook: Under threat. Caveat: pins and house numbers to fix. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**Burgwallen-Oude Zijde**

**DC-14. Bureau Warmoesstraat, the police station of Baantjer.** Pin 52.375511, 4.898238; Warmoesstraat 48.
From 1903 to 2000 the station of the Wallen, notorious for corruption stories and the model for the TV
series Baantjer (Appie Baantjer worked here); it closed after a homeless man died after being put out
on the pavement. A small plaque went up on 26 October 2025 on the sandwich shop now in the building.
Local voice: crime reporter Cees Koring, "Je vraagt ... wat hier zat en dan worden de schouders
opgehaald" (NH). Lead: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/353682 ; nl.wikipedia Warmoesstraat (Amsterdam).
Rank **Okay**. New place.

**Burgwallen-Nieuwe Zijde**

**DC-15. The Dam shooting of 7 May 1945.** Pin 52.373393, 4.892800. On 7 May 1945, while the Dam celebrated liberation,
German soldiers fired from the balcony of De Groote Club on the corner of the Kalverstraat: at least
32 dead. On 7 May 2016 31 name stones were unveiled in the square, a 32nd followed a year later; the
older plaque is in the wall (52.372620, 4.891944).
Lead: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/184667 ; nl.wikipedia Schietpartij op de Dam op 7 mei 1945. Caveat:
the Dam is A-list; this is pitched on itself. Rank **Nice**. New place.

### Sweep 4: hidden history

**Burgwallen-Oude Zijde**

**DC-16. Bet van Beeren, 't Mandje and Hartjesdag on the Zeedijk.** Pin 52.374815, 4.900983; Zeedijk 63.
't Mandje, Bet van Beeren's bar, is counted the oldest gay and lesbian bar of the city and reopened
after 25 years shut (opening year and closure dates to confirm); in 2017 the Stormsteeg bridge (210) was named after her,
and every third Monday of August the Zeedijk holds Hartjesdag, a drag and dressing-up street party
revived here in 1997. Local voice: AT5 "Koningin van de Zeedijk krijgt eigen brug"; landlord Martin
Vermaak on Hartjesdag, "Het is vooral een hoop lol" (AT5 2023, after five rainbow flags were wrecked
on the street). Lead: nl.wikipedia Bet van Beerenbrug and Hartjesdag; https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/222025 ;
https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/306298 (2022). Caveat: a café; see "Food and drink, borderline".
Rank **Amazing**. New place.

**DC-17. Chet Baker's window, Hotel Prins Hendrik.** Pin 52.376525, 4.899666; Prins Hendrikkade 53-55.
The trumpeter fell from a window of the hotel on 13 May 1988 and died on the stone post below. Fans
first put up an illegal pole with a trumpet; the bronze plaque followed on 13 May 1999, and every
night of 12 to 13 May a wake is held. Local voice: d'Oude Binnenstad 2013-1. Lead: nl.wikipedia Chet
Baker (gives no. 55); d'Oude Binnenstad 2013-1; OSM plaque. Rank **Nice**. New place (neighbour of DC-01).

**DC-18. Koestraat, "Ons Dorp", the street with its own mayor.** Pin 52.372125, 4.898268; Koestraat.
The residents of this short street hold a street party in the back garden where Sweelinck lived from
1590 to 1621 and install a street mayor with a chain of office ("bijzonder detail is de aan-uitknop").
At no. 5 a memorial stone of 1912 (restored 2004) marks Jan van der Heyden, inventor of the fire hose
and street lighting. Local voice: d'Oude Binnenstad 2017-3 ("Koestraat, Ons Dorp"); 2007-2. Lead:
d'Oude Binnenstad; VVAB Binnenstad 210. Caveat: the mayor tradition is from one 2017 report; check
whether it continues. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-19. Walenpleintje, the square behind the gate.** Pin 52.371230, 4.897710 (the Walenpoortje).
A hidden court behind a gate off the Oudezijds Achterburgwal, by the Walloon church; it only got its
name in 1978. Local voice: d'Oude Binnenstad 2016-4. Lead: d'Oude Binnenstad; Wanderlustingk;
Amsterdam Sights. Visiting: open by day. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**DC-20. Oost-Indisch Huis and the dismantled VOC room.** Pin 52.370898, 4.898007; Oude Hoogstraat 24 /
Kloveniersburgwal 48. The VOC's headquarters, now the UvA; the courtyard is open by day. The
university took apart the VOC room (Bewindhebberszaal) without a permit and the VVAB filed an
enforcement request; Folia explains why the neighbour is called the Bushuis. Local voice: VVAB news
707 (2026). Lead: nl.wikipedia Oost-Indisch Huis (Amsterdam); VVAB news 707. Caveat: second-tier sight,
pitch on the contest. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-21. Oudemanhuispoort, the booksellers' arcade.** Pin 52.369364, 4.895584 (Oudezijds Achterburgwal
entrance). Built 1601 as an old people's home from a lottery; the shops in the passage came with a
rebuild in the 18th century, sold jewels and silver first, lost trade when it became a cholera house in
the 1830s, and have held booksellers for more than a century. In the garden the UvA unveiled (30
September, year to confirm from the UvA page) a memorial with 254 students and 28 staff who died in the war. Local voice: Folia
"UvA-geheimen" (2020) and November 2026 on the booksellers' future. Lead: Folia; nl.wikipedia
Oudemanhuispoort; UvA news. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-22. Two smallest houses: Oude Hoogstraat 22 and the Trips' coachman.** Pin 52.371220, 4.897664
(Oude Hoogstraat 22; Kloveniersburgwal 26 at 52.371633, 4.898905). A house about two metres wide on
the Oude Hoogstraat, and the narrow house opposite the Trippenhuis that legend says the coachman of
the Trips asked for, "as wide as your front door". Local voice: d'Oude Binnenstad 2014-2; OpNieuw
2019-3 ("Het Trippenhuis"); That Dam Guide. Caveat: the coachman story is legend; listed houses.
Rank **Nice**. New place (group of two).

**DC-23. Stoofbrug and the 3D-printed bridge.** Pin 52.372371, 4.897816; Oudezijds Achterburgwal at the
Stoofsteeg. MX3D's printed steel bridge lay here from July 2021 to October 2023; the Vereniging filed
an enforcement request because "temporary" threatened to become permanent; the old 19th-century bridge
had gone missing, parts were found, and it came back with a lantern on 12 April 2024. Local voice: "Ik
ben blij dat er weer een brug komt" (AT5, 16 Feb 2024). Lead: nl.wikipedia Stoofbrug; AT5 225085; VVAB
news 450. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**DC-24. Grimburgwal, the oldest lampposts in the canal.** Pin approx 52.369350, 4.895300. The only
row of the city's oldest lamppost model (1847, foundry Enthoven) and 12 cast-iron balusters fell in
when the quay collapsed on 1 September 2020 and were fished out at the Vereniging's request. Lead:
VVAB Binnenstad 299; UvA campus page on the quay replacement (2026 onward). Status: unknown whether
the posts are back. Outlook: Major change ahead (quay replacement). Rank **Okay**. New place, or a
Look for line for a Huis aan de Drie Grachten place.

**DC-25. Heintje Hoekssteeg, the alley that got its gate.** Pin 52.375203, 4.899122. Named after
Heyntgen Hoec, who lived here 1382 to 1410, and the source of the old nickname "Het Haantje" of the
attic church on its corner; residents asked ten years for a gate against dealers and drunks and got
it in March 2026 (shut 20:00 to 08:00). Local voice: "Was openbaar toilet" (AT5, 4 March 2026).
Lead: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/237136 ; nl.wikipedia Heintje Hoekssteeg. Caveat: beside A-list
Ons' Lieve Heer op Solder. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**DC-26. The head of the Geldersekade: fish market, water gate and Bredero.** Pin 52.373188, 4.900783.
From 1840 to the 1930s the fish hall stood here behind the Waag; the water gate under the Nieuwmarkt
and two wooden landings remain. Piet Esser's statue shows Bredero's Jerolimo trying to steal a kiss
from Trijntje Snaps; city papers keep calling the spot "Brederoplein". Local voice: columnist Janne,
"Waar is het Brederodeplein?" (OpNieuw 2022-4). Lead: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/356648 (1 Feb
2026); OpNieuw 2022-4. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**Nieuwmarkt/Lastage**

**DC-27. Blauw Jan (gone), the menagerie inn.** Pin 52.369314, 4.897597; Kloveniersburgwal 87. In the
17th century an inn here showed animals from far countries; the site now carries the letters of the
craft school "Maatschappij voor den werkenden stand" (1854). Lead: nl.wikipedia Blauw Jan;
https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/293902 . Gone, with a building and letters to stand by. Rank **Okay**,
status Gone. New place.

**DC-28. Montelbaanstoren, "Malle Jaap".** Pin 52.372039, 4.905658; Oudeschans 2. The tower's
nickname comes from bells that rang at the wrong times; OpNieuw ran "De klokken van Malle Jaap"
(2018-2) and reported the tower's restoration in 2007. Lead: OpNieuw 2018-2, 2007-1; Amsterdam Sights;
Wanderlustingk. Caveat: second-tier sight; nickname origin needs a firm source. Rank **Nice**. New place.

### Sweep 5: behind the gate

**Nieuwmarkt/Lastage**

**DC-29. Speeltuin De Waag, a playground since 1932.** Pin 52.372236, 4.904336; Oudeschans 10d.
Opened in 1932 for the tenth birthday of buurtvereniging De Waag (1922) and still on the same spot;
the association keeps its banners and asks old players for memories. Lead: OpNieuw 2021-4;
speeltuindewaag.nl. Visiting: public playground, opening hours. Rank **Okay**. New place.

### Sweep 6: modern and contested

**Nieuwmarkt/Lastage**

**DC-30. Nieuwmarkt metro station, the riot remembered underground.** Pin 52.371318, 4.900879.
The station (opened 11 October 1980) was dug where houses were pulled down for the metro, the cause of
the Nieuwmarkt riots of 1975; inside are works on that history, among them a wrecking ball hitting the
wall and the photo collage "Groeten uit de Nieuwmarkt". In 2012 residents founded "Red de ondergrondse
metrokunst" when the renovation threatened the art; 1,670 signed and the art was kept. Local voice:
"Het is een symbool voor de vernieling van de oude Nieuwmarktbuurt" (AT5 2023); OpNieuw 2022-2
"Ondergrondse metrokunst gered" (the photo collage itself was lost to graffiti long ago, only frames
remain). Lead: nl.wikipedia Nieuwmarkt (metrostation); https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/76163 ;
https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/221229 . Rank **Amazing**. New place.

**DC-31. Auke Bijlsmabrug and Theo Boschbrug, bridges for the neighbourhood's fighters.** Pin
52.373028, 4.901956 (Auke Bijlsmabrug, Recht Boomssloot). On 31 May 2018 bridge 294 was named after Auke
Bijlsma, whose lobbying helped the council reject a four-lane road through the neighbourhood by 22 votes
to 21 on 5 January 1972, and bridge 230 over the Raamgracht by the Zwanenburgwal after the architect of
the Pentagon, Theo Bosch. Local voice: OpNieuw headline "Eindelijk gerechtigheid!"; the neighbourhood's
yearly front-garden prize carries Bijlsma's name. Lead: OpNieuw 2018-3; city decision of 5 Dec 2017
(gmb-2017-217097). Look for: Theo Boschbrug at the Zwanenburgwal end of the Raamgracht. Rank **Okay**.
New place.

**DC-32. Huis De Pinto, saved by the neighbourhood.** Pin 52.370096, 4.900857; Sint Antoniesbreestraat 69.
The city bought the house in 1968 to demolish it for a planned road and the metro; squatted by the
VVAB in the early 1970s and defended by Geurt Brinkgreve and the Stichting De Pinto (1971), it was
restored in 1975 and housed a library branch from 1975; residents fought the branch's move in 2007.
Today a cultural centre (OSM). In September 2025 a memorial for fifty years of Huis De Pinto took the last line
of the poem city poet F. Starik wrote for it: "Blijf. En durf te zijn. Ga niet kwijt." Local voice:
OpNieuw used the line as its motto (2025-4). Lead: OpNieuw 2025-4, 2015-4; nl.wikipedia Huis De Pinto (read 24 Nov 2026).
Visiting: opening hours of the centre. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-33. The Marionettentheater in the old smithy.** Pin 52.373443, 4.902377; Nieuwe Jonkerstraat 8.
A seventy-seat puppet theatre in the former smithy where the Aktiegroep Nieuwmarkt used to meet; in
March 2025 residents unveiled a squatters' map with over 200 addresses of the 1970s there. Local voice:
OpNieuw 2024-4 and 2025-1. Lead: OpNieuw; second source needed for the smithy-meeting claim. Rank
**Okay**. New place.

**DC-34. Fort van Sjakoo, the bookshop from the squatters' years.** Pin 52.369111, 4.901893;
Jodenbreestraat 24. Founded 1977, one of the firms OpNieuw counts among those "uit het verzet van de
jaren 70 geboren". Lead: OpNieuw 2015-3; Amsterdamian. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**Burgwallen-Nieuwe Zijde**

**DC-35. The Handelsblad complex and the Keizerrijk.** Pin 52.372200, 4.890367; Nieuwezijds Voorburgwal
234-240. The gold letters "Algemeen Handelsblad" still mark the paper's old office and printing works,
squatted in 1978 and now social rented housing, "één groot complex met allemaal kruip door, sluip door
routes"; a lesbian squat group lived in the street in the 1980s. Lead: https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/237135
(3 March 2026); nl.wikipedia Algemeen Handelsblad. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-36. Vrankrijk.** Pin 52.371691, 4.889222; Spuistraat 216. A squat bar and political venue of the
squatters' movement that has outlasted most of the Spuistraat squats. Lead: What's up with Amsterdam;
nl.wikipedia Vrankrijk (to read). Status: open per OSM. Rank **Okay**. New place.

**DC-37. Het Lieverdje.** Pin 52.368777, 4.889027; Spui. Carel Kneulman's statue was the stage of
Provo's happenings in 1965-67; restored and back on 27 May 2024 ("Hij heeft al heel wat ellende
gehad", AT5). Look for: the Friday book market on the Spui, squeezed by the street's renovation (NH,
11 Sep 2026). Lead: nl.wikipedia Het Lieverdje; https://www.at5.nl/artikelen/226839 . Rank **Nice**. New place.

### Sweep 7: curiosities, green and odd

**Nieuwmarkt/Lastage**

**DC-38. "De Gelderse Gaai", a bird carved from a broken paving slab.** Pin approx 52.372550, 4.900750;
east side of the Nieuwmarkt, by the bench opposite café Bern. When the square was redone in 2010 a
broken slab fell off a lorry outside the Waalsteeg; sculptor Klaartje Verhoeven carved it by hand into
a bird and the city let her lay it back in the square in November 2010. After the last refit it came
back without its bench, neck broken, and was relaid in January 2025 (now spelt "Geldersche Gaai");
the city's art department declined to take it on. Local voice: a resident and her granddaughter clean
it every Monday; "Ik lig hier weer, ga niet meer weg" (the bird speaking, OpNieuw 2025-4). Lead:
d'Oude Binnenstad 2015-1; OpNieuw 2025-2, 2025-4 (same author, Greetje van der Krieke). Second source
independent of her needed. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-39. Aprilfeesten on the Nieuwmarkt.** Pin 52.372553, 4.900089 (the square). A neighbourhood fair
every April on the square, run by residents ("Heb mijn vakantie er voor omgeboekt", AT5 21 April 2026).
Lead: nl.wikipedia Aprilfeesten; OpNieuw 2017-1, 2024-2. Caveat: a yearly event, not a thing to see;
could be a Look for line in DC-38. Rank **Okay**. New place or Look for.

**Burgwallen-Oude Zijde**

**DC-40. The plaskrul with the two watchers, Oudekerksplein.** Pin approx 52.374300, 4.897600 (by the
Oude Kerk; fix on site). One of about 37 remaining cast-iron "krullen" (the city's own design, first
placed about 1880); the one at the Oude Kerk has two small figures on top, and another on the
Oudezijds Voorburgwal is in Amsterdam School style. The city is letting them die out. Local voice:
photographer Renzo Gerritsen, "Ik vind het wel ballen hebben dat hij hier nog staat" (NH, 30 May 2024).
Lead: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/336685 ; nl.wikipedia Plaskrul. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-41. He Hua temple and the Chinese Zeedijk.** Pin 52.373726, 4.900075; Zeedijk 106-118. A
palace-style Buddhist temple opened by Queen Beatrix in September 2000; around it bilingual
street signs, Dun Yong (the first Chinese toko on the Zeedijk) and the Vesak procession. Local voice:
VVAB Binnenstad 188, "opvallende vreemdeling op de dijk". Lead: VVAB Binnenstad 188; NH 356249 (Dun
Yong, Jan 2026). Rank **Nice**. New place.

**DC-42. Jacob Hooy & Co., the drugstore of 1743.** Pin 52.371956, 4.899184; Kloveniersburgwal 10-12.
A herb and liquorice shop with its old drawers and jars; a rent rise in 2017 threatened "het einde voor
eeuwenoude drogisterij" (AT5). Lead: AT5 166258; d'Oude Binnenstad ads. Status: open per OSM; check.
Caveat: a shop; borderline under the shops rule. Rank **Okay**. New place.
