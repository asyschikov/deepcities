# Centrum scout DE: theme D (local papers and blogs), zone East-South

Scouted 2026-11-24 (two sessions; the first was cut off, the second checked and extended its notes). Wijken: Grachtengordel-Zuid, De Weteringschans, Weesperbuurt/Plantage, Oostelijke Eilanden/Kadijken. Nothing is written to the map: the user picks.

**44 shortlisted items (DE-01 to DE-44)**, all new places (nothing in Centrum is on the map yet). Every pin was point-checked with `research/centrum/scripts/areas.py`: all are in borough Centrum and in one of the four wijken. Checked against the draft titles in `research/centrum/places/plantage-1.yaml` (De Burcht, the Wertheim fountain, the Henri Polaklaan houses and others are there already: not proposed again).

By wijk: Oostelijke Eilanden/Kadijken 21, Weesperbuurt/Plantage 13, De Weteringschans 7, Grachtengordel-Zuid 3. By rank: Amazing 3 (DE-01, DE-22, DE-35), Nice 21, Okay 20.

Local voice is where theme D is strongest: almost every item comes with what residents wrote, fought about or named. The East-South zone is lopsided in its sources: the Eilanden and the Plantage have the richest neighbourhood press of Centrum (Buurtorganisatie 1018 has posted almost daily since 2014), while the canal-belt south has only De Binnenkrant and a VijzelCourant that is not online.

## Shortlist

### Oostelijke Eilanden/Kadijken

**DE-01. The floating gardens of Robert Jasper Grootveld, Wittenburgervaart.** Pin 52.370100, 4.922300 (the rafts in the Wittenburgervaart at the foot of the Ezelsbrug; approximate, on the water). Three planted rafts named Grootveld, Jasper and Robert, built with the knotting technique of the provo "anti-smoke magician" Robert Jasper Grootveld (1932-2009) and moored opposite his own tyre boat, so that the spot is, in the maker's words, one Gesamtkunstwerk by Grootveld. The city banned them in June 2021 under a 50,000 euro penalty (Waternet said the materials were unsafe), relented in October 2021, and they were handed to the neighbourhood on 19 July 2022, Grootveld's 90th birthday. Local voice: paid for by residents' vote (Centrum Begroot, 50,000 euro); the film "Drijfland" was shown in De Witte Boei on 30 Sept 2026. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2022/7/26/drijvende-tuinen-overgedragen-aan-de-buurt ; second: https://stadscuratorium.nl/en/collectie/floating-gardens/ and https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2021/6/24/gemeente-verbiedt-drijvende-tuinen . Status: in place (Aug 2026). Caveats: 1018 Magazine says the permit ends Feb 2027 (Outlook: Under threat); Grootveld's own boat needs checking on site. **Amazing**; new place.

**DE-02. Tug of war on the Kippebrug, Kattenburg against Wittenburg.** Pin 52.371694, 4.919498 (Kippebrug, by the Wittenburgerhoofd). A small Amsterdam School footbridge by Piet Kramer (1923), once part of a round bridgehead with a bathhouse, is the yearly battlefield of a "traditional" tug of war between the two islands, with prizes and a shared meal in De Witte Boei afterwards (2017 during the Aardappeloproer festival, 8 June 2019). Local voice: island rivalry ("eens een Eilander, altijd een Eilander", buurtboeken.nl). Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2019/6/7/zaterdag-8-juni-1330-u-touwtrekken-wittenburg-tegen-kattenburg-op-de-kippebrug ; second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kippebrug_(Amsterdam) (bridge facts). Status: bridge standing; latest tug of war found June 2019. Caveats: whether the contest is still yearly is not confirmed after 2019 (check: Still there?); the animal bridge names of 1018 (Kippebrug, Ezelsbrug, Pelikaanbrug, Nijlpaardenbrug, Oliphantsbrug) can be a Look for line here. **Nice**; new place.

**DE-03. Shaffy's Tuin, Dijksgracht-West.** Pin 52.376411, 4.912446 (the garden, OSM). A neighbourhood garden on the Dijksgracht-West, named after Ramses Shaffy, who lived on a houseboat here in the 1970s; ten years old on 6 Sept 2025, when its gardeners published a 5 km "green discovery walk" past twelve hidden gardens of Centrum-Oost. Local voice: a garden made and kept by residents on a forgotten strip between the railway and the water. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2025/9/18/groene-wandeling-vanaf-shaffys-tuin ; second: https://shaffystuin.nl/buiten . Status: open (Sept 2025). Caveats: the houseboat claim comes from 1018 only (verify); the new bike path on the Dijksgracht-Oost (2026) is a fight next door. **Nice**; new place.

**DE-04. The Sibbelwoningen, Hoogte Kadijk 112-150.** Pin 52.368758, 4.916806 (Hoogte Kadijk 130). A row of 19th-century workers' houses on the old sea dike, squatted in 1976 to stop demolition; the Kadijkencomité fought for them, a renovation followed that put some residents out of their homes for five years, and after it damp and mould forced a rent cut (2015). Workers of the brewery De Gekroonde Valk lived here. Local voice: "de parel wil maar niet glanzen" (1018, 2015). Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2015/1/26/tentjes-sibbelwoningen-hoogte-kadijk ; second: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2015/1/29/sibbelwoningen-in-het-parool and the 1018 Magazine renewal series (no. 58 to 62). Status: inhabited (2024). Caveats: private homes, front only; squat year from 1018 Magazine, needs a second source. **Nice**; new place; DE-05 can be its Look for line.

**DE-05. The crowned falcon on its pillar, Hoogte Kadijk.** Pin 52.368650, 4.917400 (approximate, at the brewery site; check: Location). The statue of the falcon of brewery De Gekroonde Valk, put back on its pillar in 1993; in 2017 a great-great-grandson of brewer Willem Hovy (known for his social policy, director from 1858) stopped to look at it and was invited into a Sibbelwoning to hear the story. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2017/1/9/ontmoeting-met-nazaat-van-brouwer-de-gekroonde-valk ; second: nl.wikipedia "De Gekroonde Valk" (fetched; brewery history). Status: standing (2017). **Okay**; group with DE-04.

**DE-06. Koning William yard, Hoogte Kadijk 145B (the lost fight).** Pin 52.368568, 4.918878. One of the last shipyards on the old dike; residents ("Wakkere Kadijkers") sought monument status, which the stadsdeel refused in 2018 (the committee granted it rarity value but not enough architecture), lost their appeal, and the Raad van State allowed housing in Feb 2023; the yard closed in Dec 2017 and a corten tower now stands. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2018/11/19/aanvraag-monumentenstatus-werfterrein-koning-william-hoogte-kadijk-afgewezen ; second: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2023/2/1/raad-van-state-staat-nieuwbouw-koning-william-toe . Status: new building (2026). Caveats: gone yard, high bar: keep only as part of a Hoogte Kadijk item. **Okay**; group with DE-04 or DE-07.

**DE-07. Hoogte Kadijk, the sea dike that still works.** Pin 52.368400, 4.917800 (on the dike road; approximate). The street is a dike of the Zuiderzee era; on 2 Nov 2023, during storm Ciarán, the city shut 14 sluices for the first time in 25 years, and the dike streets (Hoogte Kadijk, Zeeburgerdijk, Zeedijk) were again the city's water barrier. Local voice: 1018 Magazine's piece on how well the inner city is protected. Lead: 1018 Magazine 64 (Dec 2024), https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/s/1018-Magazine-64-dec-2024.pdf ; second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoogte_Kadijk . Status: in use. Caveats: the 14 sluices and "25 years" rest on 1018 Magazine; a drafter should find the Waternet source. Energetica (coal power station until 1950) is a Look for line. **Nice**; new place (would carry DE-05 and DE-06 if the user wants fewer pins).

**DE-08. The Marineterrein wall, Kattenburgerstraat.** Pin 52.371804, 4.916275 (Kattenburgerstraat 5, at the wall). For centuries the navy yard was a closed world behind this wall; much of it is the back walls of vanished buildings; it became a municipal monument in April 2023. Behind it since 2015: the former shooting range rebuilt with a roof garden (2023), the Binnenhaven as the first official swimming spot of the centre (from April 2025), and the Buurtboerderij Ons Genoegen, which residents fought to keep (2026). Local voice: a blogger took apart Defence's case for keeping the site (2019); the swimming that used to draw fines. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2023/4/24/muur-marineterrein-wordt-gemeentelijk-monument ; second: https://nltimes.nl/2025/02/21/amsterdam-promises-new-official-swimming-hole-city-center . Status: open grounds (Sept 2026). Caveats: the farm's permit ran out (left mid-Sept 2026 per 1018 Magazine 74); theme B or C scouts may propose the Marineterrein as a whole: merge there. **Nice**; new place.

**DE-09. Oosterkerk, the church the neighbours saved.** Pin 52.369988, 4.919306. Closed in 1963 as unsafe, bought by the city in 1969, which planned to demolish it in the renewal of the islands until residents stopped it; now a free concert hall and the islands' meeting place, scene of the 2018 memorial for Mohamed Bouchikhi (see DE-10) and of a fight over the elm in front of it (57 objections, 2015). Local voice: Maria Bervoets's photo book "Oosterkerk, hart van de buurt" (2021). Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oosterkerk_(Amsterdam) ; second: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2015/11/8/strijd-om-boom-voor-oosterkerk . Status: in use (2026). Caveats: listed (rijksmonument); a monument scout may take it: merge the local story in. **Nice**; new place.

**DE-10. Memorial stone for Mohamed Bouchikhi, Wittenburg playground.** Pin 52.371348, 4.921531 (speeltuin by De Witte Boei; check: Location). Unveiled by the family and mayor Halsema on 26 Jan 2019, a year after his violent death, after a full Oosterkerk and a silent march. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2019/1/26/indrukwekkende-herdenking-mohamed-bouchikhi-in-de-oosterkerk-en-onthulling-gedenksteen . Status: 2019, no later evidence. Caveats: recent death of a private person, sensitive; only one source; better as a Look for line in DE-09. **Okay**; group with DE-09.

**DE-11. Aat Veldhoen's house with the palette, Wittenburgergracht.** Pin 52.369706, 4.920070 (Wittenburgergracht 241; check: Location). The painter and printmaker (1934-2018), arrested in the 1960s for spreading "indecent" prints of lovers and nudes, lived on the Wittenburgergracht in a house marked by a big painter's palette and kept a studio in the Grote Wittenburgerstraat; after a stroke in 2004 he taught himself to paint left-handed. Wikipedia shows his gable stone at Wittenburgergracht 241 (1992). Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2018/12/9/aat-veldhoen-overleden ; second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aat_Veldhoen . Status: palette and stone not checked since 2018. Caveats: private house; whether the palette house is no. 241 is not confirmed (check: Location). **Okay**; new place.

**DE-12. Nijlpaardenbrug, roses, swimmers and the oboe maker's ship.** Pin 52.368788, 4.913981. The hippo bridge between Artis and the Entrepotdok is the islands' informal swimming spot and a stage for small rituals (65 red roses hung on it for a birthday boat, 1 Sept 2018); iamsterdam sends people here for the view of the warehouses. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2018/9/1/65-rode-rozen-aan-de-nijlpaardenbrug ; second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nijlpaardenbrug and https://www.iamsterdam.com/ontdek/buurten/de-plantage/verborgen-parels . Status: standing. Caveats: thin as a place on its own; stronger as the pin for "the animal bridges of 1018" (Kippebrug, Ezelsbrug, Pelikaanbrug, Oliphantsbrug, named 2014 with a wink to Artis). **Okay**; new place.

**DE-13. Entrepotdok chestnut and quay.** Pin 52.369680, 4.911334 (approximate, Entrepotdok west end; check: Location). In 2019 the sagging quay wall was to be rebuilt and the giant monumental chestnut felled; residents organised; the quay collapsed under a houseboat in Dec 2017. Also the "Dokoproer" of 10 Dec 2022 against selling social housing on the dock, which made the corporation back down. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2019/7/10/kapdreiging-monumentale-kastanjeboom-entrepotdok-kom-donderdag-11-juli-1900-u-naar-t-kromhoutar ; second: 1018 Magazine 54 (April 2023). Status: whether the chestnut survived is not found (check: Still there?). **Okay**; new place, or Look for lines in an Entrepotdok place by another scout.

**DE-14. Werkspoorhal, the hall residents want open.** Pin 52.372682, 4.927932. The 1929 Werkspoor hall on Oostenburg, one of two kept (decision of Dec 2015); the 2024-25 plan for padel courts met protest from neighbours (fine with sport, not with padel noise), then BigGym, with public access promised. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2015/12/17/besluit-werkspoorhallen-op-oostenburg-n-van-de-twee-blijft-behouden ; second: 1018 Magazine 65 to 69 (2025). Status: in use (2025). Caveats: theme C overlap; the Van Gendthallen next door are nearly done (May 2026). **Nice**; new place.

**DE-15. Tkramrepus, the supermarket in reverse, Czaar Peterstraat 233.** Pin 52.371584, 4.931617. "Supermarkt" spelled backwards: you bring groceries instead of taking them, and people with a food bank pass or Stadspas shop free; opened Dec 2022, saved by crowdfunding in 2024 (30,000 euro goal). Local voice: the Czaar Peterstraat's own social institution. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2024/7/1/crowdfunding-moet-sluiting-supermarkt-tkramrepus-voorkomen ; second: OSM node "Tkramrepus" (amenity social_facility); a news source to find. Status: open 2024; not checked for 2026 (check: Still there?). **Nice**; new place.

**DE-16. Hondenliefhebbersbankje, the dog lovers' bench by the railway.** Pin 52.372000, 4.931500 (approximate, dog field by the railway along the Czaar Peterstraat; the field itself may lie across the borough line in Oost: an earlier guess at 52.372500, 4.933000 point-checked as Oost; check: Location). A home-made bench with "hondenliefhebbersbankje" carved in the seat and a back of three old café chair backs carved with the names of the dogs that come here, plus commands ("af", "zit", "pootje"); a tree-trunk bench opposite has the same treatment. The field went from brambles to plantsoen to fenced dog run. Lead: 1018 Magazine 49 (2022), https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/s/Buurtmagazine-1018-nr-49.pdf . Status: 2022, no later evidence; only one source (check: Still there?). **Okay**; new place.

**DE-17. Nieuwe Oostenburgerstraat, "het schattigste straatje".** Pin 52.370250, 4.923574 (Nieuwe Oostenburgerstraat 10). Workers' houses of 1871 with postage-stamp front gardens and benches under the kitchen windows, backing onto the Wittenburgervaart; a 1018 Magazine portrait calls it "met stip het schattigste straatje op de Oostelijke Eilanden". Lead: 1018 Magazine 2025 (portrait of a resident film-maker); second: monument register to check. Status: inhabited. Caveats: one local source for the nickname; check listing status. **Okay**; new place.

**DE-18. Touwbaanpark and the 400-year fence, Oostenburg.** Pin 52.369830, 4.922950 (Touwbaanpark). The park on the line of the VOC's old rope walk; first container gardens of Centrum here (2020); along the railway a 300 m building fence by Josje-Marie Vrolijk tells 400 years of Oostenburg (2023, to stay until at least 2028). Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2023/2/25/400-jaar-geschiedenis-van-oostenburg-op-schutting-langs-het-spoor ; second: OSM Touwbaanpark; nl.wikipedia Oostenburg (to read). Status: fence until 2028 (Outlook: Leaving or closing, "2028 or later"). **Nice**; new place.

**DE-19. Tonia Stieltjesplein, Oostenburg.** Pin 52.371606, 4.930568. The new square was named in 2025 by residents' choice (twenty suggestions) after Tonia Stieltjes (1881-1932), a Black trade-union woman, portrayed by Iris Kensmil (2023). Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2025/5/30/buurtplein-oostenburg-wordt-genoemd-naar-tonia-stieltjes ; second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonia_Stieltjes . Status: named 2025. Caveats: little to see beyond the sign; Look for: the "groen intiem asfaltpleintje" by the Zonnewendebrug that residents redesigned (May 2026). **Okay**; new place.

**DE-20. Museum Werf 't Kromhout, 50 years.** Pin 52.368120, 4.920180. A working 19th-century shipyard turned museum of the Kromhout engine (ships, then trucks, buses, pumping stations); 50 years old in 2025; it hosts the neighbourhood meetings (the chestnut fight of 2019 was argued here). Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2025/4/28/museum-t-kromhout-viert-50-jarig-bestaan ; second: OSM (tourism=museum); Stadsherstel. Status: open (2025). Caveats: listed; theme C overlap. **Nice**; new place.

**DE-21. The railway tunnel under the Dijksgracht, a graffiti free zone in the making.** Pin 52.376500, 4.913500 (approximate, pedestrian tunnel between Dijksgracht and Piet Heinkade; check: Location). A tunnel known for rough sleepers and bike wrecks is to become "the Hall of Fame", a legal graffiti wall by Amsterdam Street Art with the Ambassade hotel (project "Rafel", announced Aug 2026). Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2026/8/19/kunstvrijplaats-en-horeca-in-plaats-van-daklozen-onder-de-spoorbrug-bij-de-dijksgracht . Status: plan (Outlook: Major change ahead). Caveats: one source, not yet built. **Okay**; new place, or hold until it opens.

### Weesperbuurt/Plantage

**DE-22. Schaduwkade, the quay that remembers.** Pin 52.365070, 4.904029. Since 2013 the quay of the Nieuwe Keizersgracht carries, opposite their houses, the names of about 200 Jewish residents taken away during the war: a residents' initiative. Because passengers of moored party boats showed no respect, the stadsdeel strung a yellow ball line around Koningsdag 2024, and from mid-April 2025 a permanent red ball line keeps boats off most of the quay. Local voice: residents across the water "enthousiast" about the ball line. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2025/4/18/ballenlint-beschermt-schaduwkade ; second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schaduwkade and https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=113425 . Status: in place (April 2025). Caveats: theme A overlap; the three Kok plaques are Look for lines. **Amazing**; new place.

**DE-23. Monument van Joodse Erkentelijkheid, the monument nobody wanted next door.** Pin 52.361922, 4.907939 (Wikipedia coordinates, Weesperplein). The 1950 thank-you to Amsterdammers who helped Jews in the war; moved for the metro to the Weesperstraat, then again for the Holocaust Namenmonument; in 2017 residents of the Overloopplantsoen fiercely refused it in their park; stored in 2020, it returned to the Weesperplein green strip in Feb 2021. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2017/11/19/overloopplantsoen-bewoners-fel-tegen-komst-monument-van-joodse-erkentelijkheid ; second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_van_Joodse_Erkentelijkheid . Status: in place (2021). Caveats: theme A overlap; check whether the Weesperplein pin is in Weesperbuurt/Plantage (it is, point-checked). **Nice**; new place.

**DE-24. The Stolperschwelle at the tram stop, Plantage Middenlaan.** Pin 52.366300, 4.909600 (tram stop opposite the Hollandsche Schouwburg; check: Location). Gunter Demnig's wide stumbling threshold: "In 1942 and 1943 tens of thousands of people were deported from here by municipal tram because they were Jewish". A threshold, not a stone: for the many, not the one. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2025/1/31/struikeldrempel-geplaatst-op-de-tramhalte-bij-de-hollandsche-schouwburg ; second needed (NH Nieuws or Stolpersteine Amsterdam). Status: placed Jan 2025 (the 1018 text says "eind februari" in a post of 31 Jan: date to settle, check: Facts). Caveats: beside the A-list Hollandsche Schouwburg, pitched on itself; the boulders laid on the pavements for museum security in Feb 2024 are a Look for line. **Nice**; new place.

**DE-25. The Caucasian wingnut "IJzeren Hein", Wertheimpark.** Pin 52.367865, 4.908654 (tree beside the Auschwitz monument; check: Location). No. 9 in the experts' top ten of the city's monumental trees (2022): nearly 4 m round, so far gone in the 1970s that it was held together with iron anchors, hence the nickname. Local voice: the nickname; Ko van Geemert's poem plaque in the park (2012) is a Look for line. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2022/10/28/vleugelnoot-uit-wertheimpark-in-bomen-top-tien ; second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wertheimpark (tree 3750). Caveats: planting year disputed (1018 says 1860, Wikipedia about 1900: check: Facts); the Wertheim fountain is already drafted in plantage-1.yaml: could be merged there. **Nice**; new place or merge into "Wertheim fountain (Wertheimpark)".

**DE-26. Mahatma Gandhi Plantsoen, the houseboat dweller's park.** Pin 52.368228, 4.913690 (OSM garden). At the end of the Plantage Kerklaan, a pocket park laid out by houseboat dweller Roy, who put up the name board himself; a worm hotel opened here on 21 April 2018. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2018/4/20/wormenhotel-in-mahatma-gandhiplantsoen ; second: OSM (leisure=garden, name). Status: 2018, nothing later (check: Still there?). Caveats: name unofficial; the resident is a private person (no surname given, keep it so). **Okay**; new place.

**DE-27. Ramses Shaffy on the Sarphatihuis, Roetersstraat.** Pin 52.364697, 4.910550 (Roetersstraat 2; check: Location of the wall). A portrait of the singer on the outer wall of the care home where he lived for years, almost unreadable under graffiti until it was restored and "re-unveiled" by mayor Halsema on 29 Aug 2023, his 90th birthday. The Sarphatihuis began as a workhouse where work was compulsory until 1957 (1018 Magazine; staff podcast 2025). Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2023/8/30/ramses-shaffy-straalt-weer ; second: OSM "Ramses Shaffy Huis" is a different home on Oostenburg: a drafter should find a second source for the mural. **DE-28. "De buurt borduurt", the embroidered wall, Weesperstraat 100.** Pin 52.364920, 4.905512. A 4 by 8 m embroidery hung on a bare wall at the corner of the Nieuwe Keizersgracht on 29 June 2024, started by Gerda Leeffers of textile studio Studio Koekoek against the grey of the Weesperstraat and stitched by 277 neighbours; an XL follow-up started in 2026. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2024/7/1/borduurwerk-weesperstraat-onthuld ; second: 1018 Magazine 61 (June 2024). Status: 2024 (check: Still there? textile outdoors). **Nice**; new place.

**DE-29. Johnnyspot, the freerunners' square behind Carré.** Pin 52.363227, 4.907712 (Lepelpleintje by the Lepelkruisstraat; check: Location). The low walls and level changes of the Lepelpleintje make it a top spot of Amsterdam freerunning, known as "Johnnyspot" after its mural; the scene's best gave a demonstration in Sept 2023; neighbours complain of noise. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2023/9/21/freerunners-geven-demonstratie-in-weesperbuurt ; second needed (a freerun site). Status: 2023. **Okay**; new place.

**DE-30. Urban Jungle, Sarphatistraat 163.** Pin 52.362489, 4.913452. Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2024/6/25/urban-jungle-in-de-sarphatistraat ; second: Street Art Cities or the city's wall-art register (theme A scout). Status: 2024. **Okay**; new place, or theme A's list.

**DE-31. Petit Versailles, the courtyard park that stayed green.** Pin 52.363300, 4.912300 (approximate, behind the Sarphatistraat on the Roeterseiland campus; check: Location). Lead: https://www.buurtorganisatie1018.nl/nieuws-uit-1018/2021/2/3/petit-versailles-blijft-groen ; second: Folia (to find). Status: green (2021). Caveats: access hours of the campus grounds to check. **Okay**; new place.

**DE-32. Hubertushuis, Aldo van Eyck's colour house for single parents.** Pin 52.366780, 4.911324 (Plantage Middenlaan 33). Built 1973-80 by Van Eyck with Theo Bosch for the Hubertus association, a refuge for single parents and children ("het Moederhuis"), a small town of passages and roof terraces in every colour, joined to two 19th-century houses; named a rijksmonument in Sept 2025. ### De Weteringschans

**DE-35. Speeltuin UJ Klaren, the first public playground in the country.** Pin 52.358777, 4.892067 (OSM playground, Tweede Weteringplantsoen). On 8 May 1880 the first public children's playground of the Netherlands opened here, free for all, on the park laid out about 1845 on the old bastion De Wetering; local papers call it "de oudste speeltuin van Amsterdam". In 2025-26 the plantsoen was redone: the 1975 pancake house demolished, the playground moved, the Van Randwijk monument shifted, and since May 2026 it reopens as part of "Het Weteringpark", a name residents are still getting used to. Krop's Moeder Aarde (a replica) stands here. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weteringplantsoen ; second: De Binnenkrant 93 (https://oudestadt.nl/wp-content/uploads/Binnenkrant93.pdf) and De nieuwe VijzelCourant editie 2, 2026 (https://denieuwevijzelcourant.nl/editie-2/). Status: reopened end May 2026. Caveats: the 1880 "first" rests on Wikipedia: a drafter must find the archive source. **Amazing**; new place (Van Randwijk monument and Moeder Aarde as Look for lines).

**DE-36. Kruidenpluktuin, Eerste Weteringplantsoen.** Pin 52.360183, 4.888242 (OSM garden, opposite Carl Denig). In 2014 neighbours turned a dog-fouling lawn into a public herb garden anyone may pick from; work afternoons every Friday, autumn markets and a cocktail party with herbal snacks. Same plantsoen: the execution of 30 political prisoners on 12 March 1945 (monument Fusillade) and the 4 May gathering at the Gevallen Hoornblazer. Lead: De Binnenkrant 70 (2015) and 99 (2022), https://oudestadt.nl/wp-content/uploads/Binnenkrant99.pdf ; second: OSM "Kruidenpluktuin Weteringbuurt"; Wetering Groen. Status: active (Binnenkrant 101, 2023). **Nice**; new place.

**DE-37. Blauw Jan, forty bronze lizards, Kleine-Gartmanplantsoen.** Pin 52.363900, 4.883900 (the lawn; approximate). Since 1994 forty life-size monitor lizards, agamas and iguanas by Hans van Houwelingen crawl about the little fenced lawn beside the Leidseplein; they vanished and were put back in 2021 ("Foetsie"); in 2025 residents noted the lizards' lawn had shrunk and a row of trees was gone. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleine-Gartmanplantsoen ; second: https://www.priveekollektie.art/work/blauw-jan/ and De Binnenkrant 94 and 107. Status: in place (Binnenkrant 107, 2025). Caveats: beside the A-list Leidseplein, pitched on itself; theme A overlap; De Kring (DE-38) is next door. **Nice**; new place.

**DE-38. Sociëteit De Kring, Kleine-Gartmanplantsoen 7.** Pin 52.363615, 4.883504. The artists' club, 100 years old in 2022, now also a venue for the neighbourhood (buurtborrels, Binnenkrant 2026). Lead: De Binnenkrant 99 (2022) ; second: dekring.nl. Status: open. Caveats: a members' club; check what non-members can see or attend. **Okay**; group with DE-37.

**DE-39. Leidse Dwarstuin, Lange Leidsedwarsstraat.** Pin 52.364023, 4.884380 (approximate; check: Location). A walled neighbourhood garden and play space in the tourist strip, with a big mural by Gerti Bierenbroodspot (2019), redone in 2024-25. Local voice: "een oase van rust" (Binnenkrant 106). Lead: https://oudestadt.nl/wp-content/uploads/Binnenkrant106.pdf ; second needed. Status: 2025. **Okay**; new place.

**DE-40. Monument Kinderverzet, Falckstraat.** Pin 52.360249, 4.897356 (Falckstraat 10, the plantsoentje; check: Location). The play equipment of a small plantsoen turned into an interactive monument for the children's resistance and friendship of the former Catharina Wilhelminaschool in the war, opened to great interest (2024). Lead: https://oudestadt.nl/wp-content/uploads/Binnenkrant106.pdf ; second: kinderverzet.nl. Status: 2024. Caveats: theme A overlap. **Nice**; new place.

**DE-44. De Nederlandsche Bank's garden, open since 2025, Frederiksplein.** Pin 52.359364, 4.900123 (Frederiksplein 61; point-checked as De Weteringschans, not Grachtengordel-Zuid). After its rebuild (Duintjer's tower and Mecanoo's addition), the bank opened a jetty by the water and its inner garden to the public from 4 March 2025. Local voice: the VijzelCourant welcomed the bank "back in our neighbourhood". Lead: https://denieuwevijzelcourant.nl/archief-2/ (No. 1, 2025) ; second: dnb.nl. Status: open 2025 (check hours). **Okay**; new place.

### Grachtengordel-Zuid

**DE-41. Amstelveld, the square of the wooden church.** Pin 52.362321, 4.896482 (Amstelveld 10). The square by the mostly wooden Amstelkerk (1668-70): Monday flower market, brocante days, "Klassiek op het Amstelveld", the start of the Pride Walk (26 July 2025), a statue of the market crier Kokadorus. De Dogger (1865), "the oldest houseboat in Amsterdam", left the Prinsengracht here in 2022. Local voice: the Amstelveldbuurt association meets about it four times a year; Pride Walk. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstelveld ; second: https://oudestadt.nl/wp-content/uploads/Binnenkrant99.pdf (De Dogger) and https://amstelveldbuurt.wordpress.com/ . Status: in use (2026). Caveats: Amstelkerk is listed; the place should be pitched on the square's life. **Nice**; new place.

**DE-42. Keizersgrachtkerk and its plaque, Keizersgracht 566.** Pin 52.364659, 4.888117. A plaque on the church recalls shootings in November 1944; the church now hosts neighbourhood fairs. Lead: De Binnenkrant (kwic hit, issue to cite); second: 4en5mei.nl (theme A). Status: in use (Nov 2026). Caveats: thin; better a theme A item; listed. **Okay**; new place or theme A.

**DE-43. Halvemaansteeg 4-6, the illegal demolition.** Pin 52.366731, 4.895714. In 2014 the owner of Halvemaansteeg 6 and the linked Amstel 46 demolished almost all of both, houses with medieval structures, without a permit; the Vrienden van de Amsterdamse Binnenstad found out; in 2025 the site was still "waiting for its renovation". Lead: https://oudestadt.nl/wp-content/uploads/Binnenkrant81.pdf (Binnenkrant 81) ; second: Binnenkrant 105 and the VVAB (not readable today). Status: unfinished (2025). Caveats: gone thing (high bar): there is a gap to stand at; one source family. **Okay**; new place.
