### Source 2: Theo Bakker's Amsterdam (theobakker.net), PDFs for the zone
Downloaded: kattenburg, plantage, schans, amstelkerk, reguliersplein, vijzelstraat, weesperstraat, deutzenhofje (Van Eeghen, Amstelodamum 1960), casino, wagenpleinen, verhelinghe, trapjesschans, synagogen, scheepsbouw, raadsels, hirsch, poorten, vierde_uitleg, straattypen, biblehotel.
Leads: Amstelkerk as a "noodkerk" of 1668-70 placed off-centre so a stone church could be built beside it (never built: hence the Amstelveld); Stadsherstel bought it for one guilder (1986); bell fell askew during a service 1982. Kerkstraat as the service lane of the canal houses, with garden houses and coach gates on it. Deutzenhofje: Agneta Deutz left her only son the legal minimum and the rest to the hofje (a "tragedy" of her last 15 years, Van Eeghen); regentenkamer with her portraits and silver. Weesperplein 2-4 diamond exchange of 1911 (Beursvereniging voor den Diamanthandel, "de Club"); Joodsche Invalide 1925 (Nieuwe Achtergracht 100). Amstelsluizen and the horse-driven water mill by the Stadstimmertuin (1675-87), an underground conduit under the Weesperplein.

### Source 3: ONH (onh.nl) stories, the 43 already saved by scout BC, searched for the zone
Leads: the Poentunnel under the Keizersgracht between De Bazel and the Vijzelbank (ABN, 1975; 50 m, the only tunnel under the canals; open on Open Monumentendag); the Weesperplein metro station as a Cold War nuclear shelter for 3,000 (steel doors, tables hung upside down from the ceiling, decontamination showers; shelters kept until 1999, from 2008 partly AMS-IX server rooms); Paleis voor Volksvlijt (Frederiksplein, burnt 1929); Panoramagebouw (Plantage Middenlaan, gone).

### Source 4: Arcam, "Fietsroute langs de Openbare Schuilplaatsen van Amsterdam" (Kees van Leeuwen, 2022 PDF), read in full
Zone: Weesperplein metro shelter (Project 102, 1979, "12,000 people, the largest shelter of Amsterdam"; sluice and sliding doors visible before the gates at the Rhijnspoorplein/Sarphatistraat entrance; the tunnel gate also a flood barrier, still working); Hortusbrug shelter (bridge 239, Project 11, 1962, 100 people, stairs on both sides at Nieuwe Herengracht/Plantage Middenlaan); Torontobrug (Weesperzijde side: Oost). ONH gives 3,000 for Weesperplein: numbers disagree.
Poentunnel second source: NH Nieuws 15 Sept 2024 (50 m by 3.5 m, the only tunnel under a canal; about 1,500 visitors on day one of Open Monumentendag 2024, queues over 3.5 hours).
