## Shortlist

### Sweep 4: hidden history

#### Grachtengordel-Zuid

**BE-01 Poentunnel: the bankers' tunnel under the Keizersgracht.** Pin 52.363869, 4.892125 (the tunnel line under the Keizersgracht between De Bazel and the Vijzelbank), Vijzelstraat 32 / Keizersgracht. The only tunnel under an Amsterdam canal: 50 m long, 3.5 m wide, dug in 1974-75 together with a new main sewer so the ABN directors could walk dry from one building to the other. It was never meant for money, but the nickname "Poentunnel" (poen: dough, rhyming on the Coentunnel) stuck. After 1999 the north side was bricked up; on Open Monumentendag 2024 it opened for two days and people queued more than three and a half hours, and the city told visitors to stop coming. Local voice: the "Keizerssnede", the cut through the Keizersgracht. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poentunnel ; second https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/340608/extreme-belangstelling-bezoekers-wachten-ruim-35-uur-voor-amsterdamse-poentunnel and https://onh.nl/verhaal/de-amsterdamse-poentunnel-ging-voor-heel-even-open . Status: closed, opened only on special days (latest: Open Monumentendag 14-15 Sept 2024). Caveats: nothing to see from the street; the pin is the tunnel line, not an entrance. Rank: **Amazing**. New place.

**BE-02 The Vijzelbank and its gate over the Kerkstraat.** Pin 52.363039, 4.891865, Vijzelstraat 66 (where the building bridges the Kerkstraat). On 23 March 1955 the Vijzelflat that Van der Mey had built over the Kerkstraat (1926-28) collapsed; Duintjer's bank of 1969-73 that replaced it set off "a storm of unprecedented ferocity" in the council, which broke party discipline and changed how the city thought about its centre. It still spans the Kerkstraat like a gate. Local voice: VVAB Binnenstad chose its "dull" facade as a cover to tell that story. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerkstraat_(Amsterdam) ; second Binnenstad 178, "De Vijzelbank" (Geurt Brinkgreve), https://web.archive.org/web/2021/https://amsterdamsebinnenstad.nl/binnenstad/178/vijzelbank.php . Status: standing (wiki, read Nov 2026); current use not checked. Caveats: the building is plain; the story is the point. Rank: Okay. Group with BE-01.

**BE-03 Amstelkerk: the "temporary" church that stayed.** Pin 52.362321, 4.896482, Amstelveld 10. Built in wood in 1668-70 by Stalpaert as a stopgap, placed off-centre so a stone church could rise beside it; the stone church never came, and that empty half became the Amstelveld. Because it was temporary there were no burials. In 1982 the bell fell askew during a service; in 1986 Stadsherstel bought the church for one guilder. Van Gogh sat here in 1877-78 listening to his uncle Stricker. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstelkerk ; second https://stadsherstel.nl/monumenten/de-amstelkerk/ and theobakker.net amstelkerk.pdf. Status: in use, services again since 2006 (wiki, read Nov 2026). Caveats: listed; DE-41 covers the Amstelveld square. Rank: Nice. Group with DE-41.

**BE-04 Het Vrededuifje to De Duif: a clandestine church that was squatted by its own parish.** Pin 52.361450, 4.896697, Prinsengracht 756 (Kerkstraat 169 was the hidden church). The Catholic congregation first met in the clandestine "Het Vrededuifje" in the Kerkstraat; in 1796 it moved to the Prinsengracht, and the present church by Molkenboer followed in 1857. When the bishop closed it in 1974 the parishioners occupied their own church and kept it open as an ecumenical community. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Duif_(Amsterdam) ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerkstraat_(Amsterdam) . Status: in use (Stadsherstel restoration 2006). Caveats: locate puts the Prinsengracht church in De Weteringschans (outer side of the canal); listed. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-05 Reguliersgracht: the canal that started Amsterdam's conservation movement.** Pin 52.364456, 4.895592, Reguliersgracht 6. In 1900-01 the city wanted to fill it for an electric tram; the brochure "Stedenschennis" (talks by D.C. Meijer and Jan Veth, 1901) and the young Amstelodamum society saved it, the start of systematic care for the cityscape. Even earlier, in 1866, the Chamber of Commerce had drawn a railway line from a station south of town straight over this canal. Number 6 also keeps rare "linksdekkende" (left-lapping) roof tiles. Local voice: Meijer mocked the hurry for electric trams: would it be such a disaster to keep the horses on the Utrechtsestraat line? Lead Binnenstad 202 "De Reguliersgracht: boter, kaas en eieren", https://web.archive.org/web/2021/https://amsterdamsebinnenstad.nl/binnenstad/202/reguliersgracht.html ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reguliersgracht . Status: canal open; checked Nov 2026. Caveats: the canal itself is well known (the seven bridges view); the hook is the near-filling. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-06 Huis met de Kolommen: the mayor's house given on one condition.** Pin 52.364861, 4.893444, Herengracht 502. Built 1672 for a West India Company director; in 1927 banker Van Aalst gave it to the city on condition that it become the mayor's official residence. The wartime NSB mayor Voûte lived here; Samkalden made the top floor a private flat; the first reception was 2 Sept 1927. The money and portraits of the last Bors van Waveren daughter went to the Deutzenhofje (BE-27). Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huis_met_de_Kolommen ; second slice gg-zuid-1 (Ambtswoning van de burgemeester). Status: in use as official residence (wiki, Nov 2026). Caveats: closed; seen from the street only; on the monument slice. Rank: Okay. New place.

**BE-07 Herengracht 524: Jurriaan Andriessen's painted landscape room.** Pin 52.364634, 4.895091, Herengracht 524. One of two identical merchants' houses of 1697-98 in the Golden Bend; behind the neck gable hides a room painted in 1771 by Jurriaan Andriessen, the leading Dutch wall painter of his day, with a wide landscape of trees, water and figures. Its coach house stood on the Keizersgracht behind. Lead https://www.hendrickdekeyser.nl/de-huizen/herengracht-524 . Status: owned by Hendrick de Keyser since 1995, let; no regular opening found (Nov 2026). Caveats: private; mention only if a visiting day exists. Rank: Okay. New place.

#### De Weteringschans

**BE-08 Max Euweplein: the prison where Anne Frank was held.** Pin 52.362283, 4.883161, Max Euweplein. The square stands on the site of the Huis van Bewaring (1850, on bastion De Schinkel); after the arrest of 4 August 1944 Anne Frank and the others were held here before Westerbork. During the war "any day could be your last" there. The prison closed in 1979; the 1991 square kept a round shape against the city's wish for a rectangle, and the colonnade carries "Homo sapiens non urinat in ventum". Memorial stone 1992, "Tranen voor het verzet" (1993). Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Euweplein ; second https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/348203/in-het-amsterdamse-huis-van-bewaring-kon-tijdens-de-oorlog-iedere-dag-de-laatste-zijn (4 May 2025) and https://research.annefrank.org/en/locaties/51209a6b-cf00-4fe8-8533-828555d56401/ . Status: public square (2025). Caveats: the chess board and casino are what people know; the prison is the hidden layer. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-09 Walenweeshuis: Dortsman's orphanage waiting behind closed doors.** Pin 52.362063, 4.890745, Vijzelgracht 2A. The Walloon orphanage of 1669-71 by Adriaan Dortsman, with wings of 1679, 1683 and 1728 and a school of 1866 in the courtyard; from 1971 to 2016 the French consulate and Maison Descartes, empty since. In 2025 new owners announced a restoration and new use. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walenweeshuis ; second https://www.rodi.nl/amsterdam-nieuw-west/nieuws/436615/walenweeshuis-aan-vijzelgracht-krijgt-nieuwe-bestemming (16 Mar 2025) and https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/341809/zorgen-om-walenweeshuis-vijzelgracht-amsterdam . Status: empty, restoration planned (March 2025). Outlook: major change ahead. Caveats: listed; courtyard not accessible now. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-10 Noortsche Bos: Vingboons' weavers' houses.** Pin 52.361762, 4.887327, Spiegelgracht 27 (corner Tweede Weteringdwarsstraat). In 1670-71 Philips Vingboons, architect of grand canal houses, also designed 211 weavers' homes in the Weteringbuurt, an early piece of social housing; many still stand. The corner house is the first monument you meet coming from the Rijksmuseum. The neighbourhood was weavers' and dyers' country: Prinsengracht 997-999 began as two dye works (1671). Local voice: VVAB counted on the corner house "13 awnings, 7 flags and a man-sized cook" in 2002. Lead Binnenstad 193, https://web.archive.org/web/2021/https://amsterdamsebinnenstad.nl/binnenstad/193/spiegelgr27.php ; second https://www.hendrickdekeyser.nl/de-huizen/prinsengracht-997 . Status: standing; signage not re-checked (2002 evidence). Caveats: hook is the ensemble, the corner house is only the entry point. Rank: Okay. New place.

**BE-11 Frederiksplein 10: an admiralty captain's house that lost its canal.** Pin 52.360982, 4.898787, Frederiksplein 10. Rebuilt around 1730, probably for admiralty captain Adam van Ingen: his arms crown the sandstone facade, with Neptune and Mercury, ship's cannon and navigation instruments. It stood on the Achtergracht, filled in 1870 to make the Frederiksplein. Hendrick de Keyser was offered it in 1918 but only bought it in 1975 because of the price. Lead https://www.hendrickdekeyser.nl/de-huizen/frederiksplein-10 . Status: HdK house (Nov 2026). Caveats: interior private. Rank: Okay. Look-for type; group with DE-44 (same square).

**BE-12 Paleis voor Volksvlijt: the glass palace under the Nederlandsche Bank.** Pin 52.360600, 4.899300 (approximate, axis of the Sarphatistraat on the Frederiksplein). Amsterdam's Crystal Palace (Outshoorn, 1859-64) burnt on the night of 17-18 April 1929; the surviving gallery wings stood until 1961, demolished for the Nederlandsche Bank. Rudy Kousbroek called that "the most extreme act of vandalism in Amsterdam after the war"; Wim T. Schippers proposed knocking down the bank and rebuilding the palace. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleis_voor_Volksvlijt ; second Binnenstad 194 (Geurt Brinkgreve), https://web.archive.org/web/2021/https://amsterdamsebinnenstad.nl/binnenstad/194/volksvlijt.php . Status: gone. Caveats: gone thing, high bar; pin approximate. Rank: Okay. Group with DE-44.

**BE-13 Amstelsluizen: Hudde's sluices that flush the canals.** Pin 52.362231, 4.903047, Amstel by the Sarphatistraat. Built 1673 under Johannes Hudde, the sluices are still closed to let fresh water push through the canals; a sluice keeper's house and two small customs houses stand on them. In 1675-87 a horse-driven mill by the city timber yard pumped water, with an underground conduit under the Weesperplein. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstelsluizen ; second theobakker.net raadsels.pdf. Status: in use (wiki, Nov 2026). Caveats: listed (rijksmonument 518510). Rank: Okay. Group with Hogesluis (drafted).

#### Weesperbuurt/Plantage

**BE-14 Weesperplein station: the Cold War shelter for 12,000.** Pin 52.361111, 4.907778, Weesperplein metro station. Built 1979 as a public nuclear shelter (Project 102), the largest in Amsterdam: the ceiling panels fold down to be tables, sliding steel doors and a decontamination sluice are still visible before the gates at the Rhijnspoorplein/Sarphatistraat entrance, and the tunnel gates double as flood barriers, still tested. Under the station lies an unused tunnel stub for an east-west line that never came. Maintenance stopped in 1999. Lead https://arcam.nl/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Fietsroute-langs-de-Openbare-Schuilplaatsen-van-Amsterdam-1.2.pdf ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weesperplein_(metrostation) and https://onh.nl/verhaal/bijna-vergeten-veertig-verborgen-bunkers-in-amsterdam . Status: station in use (2022-26). Caveats: sources disagree on capacity (12,000 Arcam and wiki, 3,000 ONH); the shelter rooms are closed. Rank: **Amazing**. New place.

**BE-15 Hortusbrug shelter: room for 100 under a bridge.** Pin 52.364167, 4.908333 (approximate, Hortusbrug, Nieuwe Herengracht / Plantage Middenlaan). The 1960 bridge by Sterenberg hides a public shelter (Project 11, 1962) for 100 people; the stairs down are on both banks. Lead https://arcam.nl/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Fietsroute-langs-de-Openbare-Schuilplaatsen-van-Amsterdam-1.2.pdf ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hortusbrug . Status: closed shelter (2022). Caveats: pin approximate; the stair hatches are the only thing to see. Rank: Nice. Group with BE-14.

**BE-16 Diamantbeurs: the exchange that never lost a stone.** Pin 52.361933, 4.906969, Weesperplein 4. Built 1911 (Van Arkel) for the Beursvereniging voor den Diamanthandel, which left the Casino on the Waterlooplein (where a rival club was nicknamed the "kinnesinnebeursie"); housed Belgian refugees in WWI; burnt on 12 Feb 1956 without a single diamond lost thanks to the vaults; its first woman member was admitted in 1952. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamantbeurs_(Amsterdam) ; second theobakker.net casino.pdf. Status: now Capital C (creative hub; wiki). Caveats: listed. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-17 Muiderpoort: the gate that sank.** Pin 52.363726, 4.919355, Sarphatistraat 500 / Alexanderplein. The only surviving gate of the 17th and 18th-century walls is the second one: on the night of 29-30 January 1769 the first gate slid off its foundation. Rauws' replacement went up in 1770; on 9 October 1811 Napoleon rode through it in a coach drawn by eight white horses. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muiderpoort_(Amsterdam) ; second theobakker.net poorten.pdf. Status: standing (office use). Caveats: listed, well visible; the hook is the sinking. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-18 Nieuwe Kerkstraat hidden synagogues and hofjes.** Pin 52.363781, 4.903895, Nieuwe Kerkstraat 16. The Fernandes Nuneshuis at no. 16 was a hofje for poor Portuguese-Jewish women; further east, no. 149 housed the "Russensjoel", the prayer house of Russian Jews; the street also keeps the 1782 wall remnant of the Sarphatihuis workhouse. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nieuwe_Kerkstraat_(Amsterdam) ; second theobakker.net synagogen.pdf. Status: buildings standing (wiki read Nov 2026). Caveats: the street article is thin and the Russensjoel and Fernandes Nunes pages do not exist; needs a gable check before drafting. Rank: Okay. New place.

**BE-19 Joodsche Invalide: the home that became the health service.** Pin 52.362525, 4.909003, Nieuwe Achtergracht 100. Built 1925 for the Jewish home for invalids, now the GGD headquarters. Lead theobakker.net weesperstraat.pdf ; second slice plantage-2 (De Joodsche Invalide). Status: GGD offices. Caveats: thin from my sources; theme A scout may hold the war story. Rank: Okay. New place.

**BE-20 Plantage Muidergracht 1-23: a row restored by its own residents.** Pin 52.366149, 4.909390, Plantage Muidergracht 19. After the war the row was shabby and threatened by university plans to clear the Plantage, Hortus included. Eleven owners restored their houses one by one from 1993; the last square metres of no. 19 were painted on Easter Monday 2003. Local voice: Ine Vermaas, "stadsherstel in eigen beheer", with neighbours pushing each other over shared party walls. Lead Binnenstad 201, https://web.archive.org/web/2021/https://amsterdamsebinnenstad.nl/binnenstad/201/plantage.html . Status: standing (2003 evidence). Caveats: ordinary to look at. Rank: Okay. New place.

#### Oostelijke Eilanden/Kadijken

**BE-21 Oranje-Nassau Kazerne: Napoleon's barracks, saved by the neighbours.** Pin 52.364749, 4.921370, Sarphatistraat 600. Built 1810-13 as Napoleon's Quartier Saint-Charles, 278 m long, paid for by a 5% levy on rents or by having soldiers billeted; his arms were later swapped for Orange. In 1839-40 Artis kept its elephants and tigers here; from 1892 the army's carrier pigeons. Slated for demolition after 1987, it was saved by residents' protest and became 150 social homes (1990). Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oranje-Nassau_Kazerne ; second slice eilanden-1. Status: housing (wiki, Nov 2026). Caveats: listed; courtyard private. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-22 Marineterrein commandant's house with its Cold War bunker.** Pin 52.372419, 4.915313, Marineterrein, Kattenburgerstraat 5-7. Under the commandant's house is a communications bunker from the Cold War; in 2025 the house and three other navy buildings became municipal monuments, and a second protected shelter under building 029 is in procedure. The 17th-century gatehouse (Stalpaert) at Kattenburgerstraat 7 is the way in. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marineterrein_(Amsterdam) ; second slice eilanden-1 (Gebouw 001, Beschermd Onderkomen Kazerne V.B.S.). Status: listed 2025; bunker not open. Caveats: navy site partly public; bunker access unknown. Rank: Nice. Group with DE-08 (the wall).

**BE-23 Kattenburg's fake old facades.** Pin 52.370900, 4.917200, Kattenburgerplein. The whole island was demolished in 1962-68; the gabled row on the Kattenburgerplein is a reconstruction by Theo Rouwhorst of the city's monuments office, inspired by what had stood there, criticised by historians as a falsification. The Kattenburgers were Orangists: on 29 May 1787 they raised the double drawbridge and plundered patriots' houses ("the battle of the Kattenburgerbrug"). Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kattenburg ; second theobakker.net kattenburg.pdf. Status: standing. Caveats: pin approximate (the row); Scheepvaartmuseum next door is A-list, this is not. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-24 Marie Altelaar and the rent strike of Kattenburg.** Pin 52.372346, 4.919470 (approximate, Kattenburgerkruisstraat). When the new homes turned out dearer than promised, residents led by Marie Altelaar held back part of the rent every month for more than fifteen years; a court ruled for them on 19 Sept 1986. A square on Wittenburg carries her name. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kattenburg ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wittenburg_(Amsterdam) . Status: history; the Marie Altelaarplein exists. Caveats: pin approximate; a gate arch on the Kattenburgerkruisstraat is mentioned but not confirmed. Rank: Okay. Group with BE-23.

**BE-25 The VOC and Admiralty ropewalks.** Pin 52.368043, 4.924566, Oostenburgergracht 77 (ropewalk front buildings, nos. 75-81). In 1661 the VOC opened a 500 m ropewalk and a vast sawmill on fresh Oostenburg, the start of what became the largest industrial firm in the world. The front building of the VOC ropewalk (1660s) and the Admiraliteitslijnbaan are among the last 17th-century buildings on the island; Tsar Peter the Great trained four months at the VOC yard here in 1697. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oostenburg_(Amsterdam) ; second https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiraliteitslijnbaan,_Amsterdam . Status: standing (wiki 2026). Caveats: house numbers differ between sources (75-77 VOC, 79-81 Admiralty); DE-18 covers the Touwbaanpark and 400-year fence, nearby. Rank: Nice. Group with DE-18.

**BE-26 Kadijksplein: dockers' coffee house and sailors' home.** Pin 52.370033, 4.911917, Kadijksplein 4. The Koffiehuis van den Volksbond (no. 4) sits at an angle facing the gate of the former Entrepotdok (no. 1); the Zeemanshuis for sailors is at nos. 17-18. Lead slice eilanden-1 (Koffiehuis van den Volksbond, Zeemanshuis, both municipal monuments 2004) ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadijksplein . Status: buildings standing; Zeemanshuis used by YWAM (2026). Caveats: the coffee house is no longer one. Rank: Okay. New place.

### Sweep 5: behind the gate

#### Grachtengordel-Zuid

**BE-27 Deutzenhofje: a mother's will in brick.** Pin 52.362653, 4.889931, Prinsengracht 855-899. Agneta Deutz (d. 1692) left her only son Jan Meerman just the legal third and gave the rest to a hofje for poor women, built 1694-95; the regents' room still has her portraits and silver, and the gable stone carries a verse. Van Eeghen called her last fifteen years a tragedy. Extended in 1964 with houses on the Kerkstraat; older women still live here. Lead theobakker.net deutzenhofje.pdf (I.H. van Eeghen, Amstelodamum 1960) ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutzenhofje . Status: inhabited, normally closed; sometimes open on Open Tuinen Dagen. Caveats: listed; private courtyard, respect residents. Rank: **Amazing**. New place.

**BE-28 Collectie Six: Rembrandt's friend still at home.** Pin 52.365053, 4.900680, Amstel 218. The Six family has lived here since 1915 with Rembrandt's portrait of Jan Six; since 1957 the state helps on condition of limited access by introduction, so visits are by request. Guestbook since 1809 (25 volumes); the collection includes a planetarium of 1754. Lead https://www.collectiesix.nl/bezoek ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collectie_Six . Status: visits by request (site live, Nov 2026). Caveats: private home; no walk-in. Rank: **Amazing**. New place.

**BE-29 Coach house of Keizersgracht 524 on the Kerkstraat.** Pin 52.365064, 4.886189, Kerkstraat 61. The Kerkstraat was the canal belt's service lane, lined with coach houses (about 45 were counted); this one, painted by Hendrik Keun in 1772 (Rijksmuseum), has Hercules and a goddess in its niches and a garden house with Hope, Faith and Charity. Lead Binnenstad 187 "Open Tuinen" (Juliet Oldenburger), https://web.archive.org/web/2021/https://amsterdamsebinnenstad.nl/binnenstad/187/opentuinen.php ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerkstraat_(Amsterdam) . Status: private garden, opened on Open Tuinen Dagen (2001 evidence). Caveats: needs a current opening check. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-30 Museum Van Loon's coach house and its fake windows.** Pin 52.362865, 4.893158, Kerkstraat 259 (garden side of Keizersgracht 672). The garden front of the coach house (1775) carries Apollo, Flora and Silenus, but its first-floor windows are fake: the family did not want servants looking into the garden. The car replaced the horses in 1920; the museum bought it in 2009 and reopened it as a coach house in 2011. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_Van_Loon ; second slice gg-zuid-2 (Koetshuis Museum Van Loon). Status: open with the museum (2026). Caveats: inside a paid museum; the Kerkstraat side is visible free. Rank: Nice. New place.

**BE-31 Kerkstraat craftsmen's houses: 1673 shop with a date ribbon.** Pin 52.362389, 4.895527, Kerkstraat 324. Built as a "winkelhuis" for maltster Gillis Moyaert, with a date ribbon reading 1673 in the facade; split from the start into rented upper and lower homes. Nos. 288-292 were built together around 1691 by master carpenter Adriaen Sessincks, 288 with its 17th-century layout intact. Lead https://www.hendrickdekeyser.nl/de-huizen/kerkstraat-324 ; second https://www.hendrickdekeyser.nl/de-huizen/kerkstraat-288 . Status: HdK rental houses (Nov 2026). Caveats: private; seen from the street. Rank: Okay. Look-for type.

**BE-32 Herengracht 412: perhaps the oldest garden house in Amsterdam.** Pin 52.367060, 4.887194, Herengracht 412. Philips Vingboons published the plan of this 1667 house with its garden, and it is the only garden house plan of his known: a room, a chicken coop ("Hoenderhock") and a wash house. In 1734 Paulus Loot van Schooten crowned it with a sandstone dome-like top and his alliance arms, but brickwork and layout suggest the core is still 17th-century. The garden is now a lawn. Local voice: Juliet Oldenburger imagined the prosaic chicken coop turned into a "speelhuys". Lead Binnenstad 186 "Het oudste tuinhuis van Amsterdam?", https://web.archive.org/web/2021/https://amsterdamsebinnenstad.nl/binnenstad/186/tuinhuis.php . Status: garden house standing (article undated, around 2000); access only on Open Tuinen Dagen if at all, not checked. Caveats: private; the dating is the author's hypothesis. Rank: Nice. New place.

#### De Weteringschans

**BE-33 Commiezenhuisje: the toll house that became a pumping station.** Pin 52.360282, 4.888122, Weteringschans 30 (by the Weteringpoortbrug). A small toll collectors' house of 1848 at the old Weteringpoort; today it serves as a pumping station. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weteringschans ; second slice wetering-3 (Commiezenhuisje). Status: standing (wiki 2026). Caveats: tiny; one-fact item. Rank: Okay. Look-for type.

**BE-34 Lange Leidsedwarsstraat 144: next door to the inn "Het Land der Belofte".** Pin 52.363353, 4.885401, Lange Leidsedwarsstraat 144. Part of a row of six intact 18th-century houses (144-146 around 1725, the rest 1770) in a quarter laid out for working people. Next to no. 144 Gijsbert van Dorpen ran the inn "Het Land der Belofte" (the Promised Land) with a kolf course; his daughter and her husband took it over in 1734 and lived in no. 144. Lead https://www.hendrickdekeyser.nl/de-huizen/lange-leidsedwarsstraat-144 . Status: HdK rental house (Nov 2026). Caveats: the inn is gone; private. Rank: Okay. New place.
