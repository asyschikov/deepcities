## Shortlist

### Sweep 6: modern and contested

#### Grachtengordel-West

**CW-01 De Groote Keijser: the squat that became a fortress.** Pin 52.372148, 4.884450, Keizersgracht 242 to 252 (listed canal houses). The empty offices of an electrical wholesaler were squatted on 1 November 1978 and the squatters turned six canal houses into a fortress: barricades, a roof watch, stocks of fireworks and firebombs. A court ordered eviction in October 1979, but the mayor refused to send in the police; squatters occupied the council chamber in December 1979 and from January 1980 ran a pirate station, Radio de Groote Keijser (later Radio de Vrije Keyser, which still exists). The city bought the block in November 1980 for 1.8 million guilders, and the purchase split the movement into those who negotiated and those who wanted to fight on. Now social housing for groups; number 246 is "Het Suijkervat", a sugar bakery of 1666. Local voice: the radio name lives on in the Vrije Keyser, still broadcasting as the movement's own station. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groote_Keijser . Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krakersrellen and https://vrijekeyser.nl . Status: housing, facade unchanged (Wikipedia, checked 2026-11-24). Caveats: private housing, seen from the quay; no plaque known. Rank **Amazing**. New place.

**CW-02 De Vogelstruys: three evictions and a riot.** Pin 52.369679, 4.887194, Herengracht 329 (through to Singel 370). The squat in this canal-belt block was evicted three times; the third eviction on 8 September 1980 ended in some of the heaviest street fighting of that year. It was squatted again in October 1992. Lead: IISG Staatsarchief dossier SAVRZ015 (read via Wayback, https://web.archive.org/web/2020/https://staatsarchief.nl/ ). Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krakersrellen (list of riots). Status: unknown use 2026, not checked on site. Caveats: private building; dates rest on the IISG dossier, a second source for the 1992 squat is needed; Huidenstraat 19 appears in the same dossier. Rank **Okay**. New place, or a Look for line in CW-01 ("the other big canal squat").

#### Haarlemmerbuurt

**CW-03 Haarlemmer Houttuinen: the motorway that stopped at a bridge.** Pin 52.381855, 4.890108, brug 95 over the Korte Prinsengracht. A 1931 plan for a four-lane through road from the Haarlemmerweg to the Weesperstraat and Gooiseweg cost the old workers' quarter here its houses from 1969; the road opened in 1973 but was never continued, because the Nieuwmarkt fight killed the rest of the route. Brug 95 (1970 to 1972) is the high car bridge built for it and is due to be replaced by a low bridge (plan of 2018, works "in the first half of the 2020s"). Tram rails laid in 1985 were never used and wore out unridden. Hertzberger's housing (1970s) fills the gap between the road and the Haarlemmerdijk. A 1980 photo shows the fence here painted "Geen woning, geen kroning". Local voice: the Wijkkrant calls the street behind it "een weg van niks naar nergens" (see DW-13). Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haarlemmer_Houttuinen . Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brug_95 . Status: bridge still standing (AW-13 mural 2023 on it, active 2026); replacement date unknown, the city project page is gone (404, 2026-11-24). Caveats: check the replacement plan before writing. Rank **Nice**. Group with AW-13 (same underpass): the road fight is the place's story, the mural its look-for; DW-13 covers the Nieuwe Houttuinen side.

### Sweep 7: curiosities, green and odd museums

#### Grachtengordel-West

**CW-04 The double krul at the Westermarkt, and the zone's last krullen.** Pin 52.374496, 4.884908, Westermarkt by the Prinsengracht, next to the Homomonument. The green cast-iron curl urinal is an Amsterdam invention of about 1870; the standard model (1916) was see-through on purpose so a policeman could tell whether more than one man stood inside, which made krullen cruising spots (about forty were used that way around 1980). When the city wanted them gone in the 1960s the gay movement fought for them; on 24 January 1970 Dolle Mina tied krullen shut with pink ribbons to demand "plasrecht" for women; in 2017 the "Zeikwijf" protest followed a woman's fine for peeing in an alley. The city now has 39, all restored in 2008, and lets them die out: a broken krul is not replaced. The Noordermarkt krul (over a hundred years old) went to the Openluchtmuseum in Arnhem in February 2022. In this zone the city data lists seven: Westermarkt (double), Brouwersgracht by the Singel lock 52.378983, 4.894051, Herenmarkt 52.379413, 4.891667, Haarlemmerplein 52.384333, 4.883677, Lindengracht 52.379636, 4.880664, Marnixplantsoen 52.381291, 4.880182, and Marnixstraat by the Elandsgracht opposite police headquarters 52.368764, 4.877074. Local voice: in August 2026 the city tried women's urinals ("een soort krul met 2 toiletten") in the Elandsstraat and on the Elandsgracht during Pride (oudestadt.nl, 2026-08-12). Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaskrul . Second: city toilets data https://maps.amsterdam.nl/open_geodata/geojson_lnglat.php?KAARTLAAG=OPENBARE_TOILETTEN&THEMA=openbare_toiletten (2026). Status: all seven in the city data, November 2026. Caveats: in use, smells; the Johnny Jordaanplein krul named in DW is not in the city list (probably gone). Rank **Nice**. New place (one pin, the others as a Look for list).

**CW-05 De Poezenboot.** Pin 52.378144, 4.893079, Singel 38G (houseboat in front of Singel 40). In 1966 a woman living on the Herengracht took in a cat and her kittens found under a tree opposite her house; by 1968 the cats had a barge, by 1971 a second, and in 1979 a purpose-built ark. It became a foundation in 1987 and is still a working shelter for strays you can visit. The stadsdeel forced the second boat away on 20 March 2006 after an eighteen-month fight; a new hull came in 2023 through crowdfunding. Local voice: the boat's own history quotes the official line in the fight as "katten zijn niet water gebonden, weg ermee!". Lead: https://www.depoezenboot.nl (history page). Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Poezenboot_(Amsterdam) . Status: open Tuesday, Friday and Saturday 13:00 to 15:00 without booking; closed 25 December 2026 to 2 January 2027 (site, read 2026-11-24). Caveats: on Atlas Obscura and in some guides, so second tier; donation asked. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**CW-06 The Fault in Our Stars bench, Leidsegracht.** Pin 52.367454, 4.886210, Leidsegracht by number 4, at the Herengracht. A plain city bench by the water became a pilgrimage spot after the film of John Green's novel (2014) put its lovers on it; young readers come to sit and cry ("I cried a week", overheard by the Binnenkrant). The bench was stolen, and the replacement draws the same visitors. Local voice: Buurtkrant Zeven, in a piece written as a neighbourhood bench, calls it "wereldberoemd" and the only famous one, "ik ben alleen in de buurt geliefd" (Zeven 151, 2024). Lead: Binnenkrant 67 (De Binnenkrant archive). Second: Zeven 151 (2024), citing Het Parool of 30 July 2024. Status: replacement bench, OSM 2026; not seen on site. Caveats: tourist-fan spot, thin on history; the theft date needs Parool. Rank **Okay**. New place.

#### Jordaan

**CW-07 Theo Thijssen Museum.** Pin 52.375538, 4.882737, Eerste Leliedwarsstraat 16. The birthplace of the teacher, writer, union man and politician Theo Thijssen (1879 to 1943, "Kees de jongen") was due to be pulled down for new housing; after a fuss the house was rebuilt in its old form (1991 to 1995) with a small museum inside, run by a foundation since 1987. Exhibitions on school and the Jordaan of about 1890, and walks. Local voice: the Wijkkrant of 2026 reports it closed from 20 July to 2 September for its first big refit in thirty years, back with new shows, a youth trail and an audio tour of the Jordaan, plus a public programme every last Sunday of the month. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_Thijssen_Museum . Second: Wijkkrant Jordaan & Gouden Reael 2026 (00000224/00000226, wcjordaan.nl). Status: open after refit, autumn 2026. Caveats: small, limited hours. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**CW-08 Noordermarkt: the markets and the fight against "vertrutting".** Pin 52.379368, 4.886774, Noordermarkt. A market square since 1616 (the Prinsenmarkt); the Monday morning market and, since 1987, the Saturday organic farmers' market with second-hand stalls. The Noordermarktfeest (1977 to 1987) began as a protest against gentrification; in 2009 regulars of café Finch ran the "Ai! Amsterdam" protest against the square's "vertrutting", and Finch, Proust and Bordewijk all closed in 2018. The square's century-old krul went to the Openluchtmuseum in Arnhem in 2022 (CW-04). The Westerstraat Monday "lapjesmarkt" (150 stalls of fabric and clothes, 52.378517, 4.884078) belongs to the same Monday. Local voice: "vertrutting" is the residents' word for what happened to the square. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noordermarkt . Second: Amsterdamian, "Noordermarkt: Amsterdam's Beloved Organic Market" (2026-01-23). Status: both markets running 2026. Caveats: the Noorderkerk draft already names the markets as a Look for line. Rank **Nice**. Group with DW-05 (Noordermarkt small things) or new place on the market.

**CW-09 Lindengracht: the filled canal, its market and the eel riot.** Pin 52.380038, 4.883459, Lindengracht (Saturday Lindenmarkt). The canal was filled in 1895 and became a market street in 1910; plans to dig canals open again have met resistance. Here on 25 July 1886 police broke up the "paling trekken", a banned game of pulling a live eel strung over the canal; the Palingoproer that followed left 26 dead, and the first death of the Jordaanoproer of July 1934 also fell on the Lindengracht. Today the Saturday Lindenmarkt fills the street. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palingoproer . Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindengracht . Status: market every Saturday, 2026. Caveats: exact spot of the eel rope (by the Zaterdagse brug, gone with the canal) is approximate; no draft has the riot. Rank **Nice**. New place (check with BW: sweep 4 may want the riot).

#### Haarlemmerbuurt

**CW-10 Westerdokseiland: the rail yard's wild flowers and the lost IJ view.** Pin 52.383948, 4.892674, Westerdoksdijk 51. An island made in 1832, a rail yard from the 1920s where over 150 wild plant species grew, seeded from the colonial trade sheds. The housing built in the 2000s ignored the old sightlines, and the Prinsengracht lost its view of the IJ; the later IJDock was built with notches to keep views open. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerdokseiland . Second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerdok . Status: built-up 2026. Caveats: little to see of the flora story; the Goedkoop draft (Westerdoksdijk 40) already cites the article. Rank **Okay**. New place, or a Look for line in the Goedkoop draft.

## Look for lines

- **Vinkenstraat 81**, Haarlemmerbuurt (52.382519, 4.886286): squatted in 1978 by the residents' committee fighting speculators and made into a neighbourhood centre, De Vink, with people living above; the committee went to the city hall every Thursday "met z'n allen op de stoep" (WJGR nr 7, 00000092). For a Haarlemmerbuurt renewal place.
- **Haarlemmerpoort**: empty for years and squatted in 1978; the squatters and the city made it housing (restored 1986, architect André van Stigt, 17 homes); Ymere moved the residents out for renovation in 2017 (WJGR 00000044, 00000156). For the Haarlemmerpoort monument draft.
- **Open Atelierroute Westelijke Eilanden**: begun in 1985 by artists in squatted workshops threatened with demolition, "de oudste kunstroute van Nederland", every Whitsun (WJGR nr 2, 00000024). For the Seepaert or Prinseneiland drafts.
- **Passeerdersgracht 23-25** (HES complex), Jordaan: squatted on 9 January 2010 by the art collective Schijnheilig (WJGR 00000052, 00000056). For a Jordaan squat or renewal place.
- **Westermarkt trees**: in 2024 residents and the wijkcentrum fought a permit to fell four trees (planted 1950, 1980, 1990) for the square's redesign (oudestadt.nl, 2024-03-14). For CW-04 or the Homomonument.
- **Women's urinal trial**, Elandsstraat and Elandsgracht, Pride 2026 (oudestadt.nl, 2026-08-12). For CW-04.
- **Singel 114**: squat evictions with riots on 23 October 1984 and 7 April 1987 (Krakersrellen list). Wijk to check; for CW-01.
- **Woonbootmuseum**, Prinsengracht 296K: in 2005 the museum complained that the krul at the head of the Elandsgracht spoiled the visit for its 25,000 visitors a year (WJGR december 2005, 00000092). For CW-04 if the museum is ever proposed.

## Food and drink, borderline

- **Saarein**, Elandsstraat 119, Jordaan (52.370279, 4.880271). For: the oldest women's café still open, opened 19 August 1978 by a collective including Lesbian Nation, paid for by its future guests, women only at first, which stirred the street; open to all since a new owner in 1999; its long-time host received the city's Andreaspenning on 3 November 2018; in summer 2024 regulars bought it into a foundation by crowdfunding. The Wijkkrant calls it "als buurtcafé ongeëvenaard, een safe haven pur sang", and the queer neighbourhood group still meets there. Against: a café; its story is queer history (theme A could take it). Sources: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarein ; WJGR 00000035 and 00000185. Status open 2026. My view: passes, as queer history rather than as a café.
- **Woonbootmuseum**, Prinsengracht 296K: not food, but the same trade question. DW dropped it as tourist trade; I agree.
