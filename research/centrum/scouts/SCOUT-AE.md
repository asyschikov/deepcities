## Shortlist

### Sweep 3: war and memory

#### Weesperbuurt/Plantage

**AE-01. The population register attack, Plantage Kerklaan 36.** Pin 52.366743, 4.912312 (OSM memorial node, on the building), Plantage Kerklaan 36. On 27 March 1943 a resistance group of artists and students, Gerrit van der Veen's among them, set fire to the city's population register here, to make the forged identity papers of people in hiding hard to check. The plaque, designed by Willem Sandberg and unveiled on 4 August 1945, names the twelve executed on 1 July 1943: Arondeus, Bloemgarten, Groeger, Hartoch, Van Musschenbroek, Reitsma, Halberstadt, Brouwer, Limperg, Bakker, Barentsen, Roos ("zij streden en vielen voor de vrijheid"). Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1602/amsterdam-monument-aan-de-plantage-kerklaan ; second: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_voor_het_kunstenaarsverzet_1940-1945 (why the Kunstenaarsverzet monument stands nearby). Status: on the 4en5mei register and OSM, 2026. Caveat: the building belongs to the Artis complex (A-list); the plaque faces the street. Rank: **Amazing**. New place (pair with AE-02 in text; the Stolpersteine of the Prins family at Plantage Kerklaan 63, seven stones, are a Look for line here).

**AE-02. Monument for the artists' resistance, Westermanplantsoen.** Pin 52.365543, 4.914189, Plantage Middenlaan by the Westermanlaan. Carel Kneulman's monument stands on the strip left when the Panorama building was pulled down, chosen because Gerrit van der Veen led the register attack round the corner; plans for a memorial to him dated from the day he was shot, 10 June 1944, and took until the 1970s. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_voor_het_kunstenaarsverzet_1940-1945 ; second: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1518 . Status: on Kunstwacht and 4en5mei, 2026. Caveat: unveiling year from my earlier note (1973), check the article before writing. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-03. "Nooit meer Auschwitz", the broken-mirror monument, Wertheimpark.** Pin 52.367754, 4.909258, Wertheimpark. Jan Wolkers' mirrors lie broken so that the sky they reflect is never whole again ("De hemel blijft voorgoed geschonden"); beneath lies an urn of ashes collected at Auschwitz-Birkenau in January 1952. First at the Oosterbegraafplaats (1977), moved here larger in 1993; the national Holocaust remembrance is held here every last Sunday of January. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auschwitzmonument_(Amsterdam) ; second: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1501 . Status: in use for the January ceremony. Caveat: fairly well known. Rank: Nice. New place (or group with "Wertheim fountain (Wertheimpark)").

**AE-04. The school plaques and the deaf memorial, Hortusplantsoen.** Pin 52.366428, 4.906203 (the deaf memorial), Hortusplantsoen 1-2. Three memorials in a row: Truus Menger-Oversteegen's monument for Jewish deaf war victims (17 October 2010, with sign-language drawings by Bart Koolen); a plaque for the Ammanschool for deaf children (1928-1976) at no. 2, whose Jewish pupils had to flee and hide; and plaques for the Van Det and Davidsschool, Jewish trade schools for girls and boys, from which "bijna alle bestuursleden, vele leerkrachten en de meeste leerlingen" were taken. Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3545 ; second: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/2943 and /4577 . Status: on the register 2026. Caveat: the Van Det plaque of 1948 hung inside the school hall; check what is visible outside. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-05. The Kok family plaques, Nieuwe Keizersgracht 24.** Pin 52.365564, 4.906254, Nieuwe Keizersgracht 24. Two plaques of 2009: on the left Max, Julie, Samuel and Jacques Kok, taken from this house and killed in Auschwitz and Mauthausen; on the right Betty Kok, who at 17 went into hiding from here in 1942 through David and Jansje Gomes de Mesquita-Prins, the only one of the family to survive. Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3642/amsterdam-monument-aan-de-nieuwe-keizersgracht ; second: Joods Monument (not read). Status: unveiled 13 December 2009, on register 2026. Caveat: private house front. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-06. The Brilleslijper house, Nieuwe Achtergracht 14.** Pin 52.361800, 4.905296, Nieuwe Achtergracht 14. Stolpersteine for Joseph, Fijtje and Jacob Brilleslijper and Gerrit Tak, all murdered in Auschwitz in 1944. Jannie and Lien Brilleslijper, resistance workers who were deported on the same train as the Frank family and survived, had lived at this address in 1939. Lead: https://joodsmonument.nl/en/page/89342/nieuwe-achtergracht-14-ii-amsterdam ; second: https://research.annefrank.org/en/personen/6bc77428-56e8-4712-a115-751a1d080c09/ and https://tracesofwar.com/sights/144118/Stolpersteine-Nieuwe-Achtergracht-14.htm . Status: stones on the wiki list 2026. Caveat: the wiki list counts six stones here, TracesOfWar three names; count on site. No stones for the sisters (they survived). Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-07. The Stolpersteine of the Nieuwe Prinsengracht.** Pin 52.363059, 4.905532, Nieuwe Prinsengracht 18. Twelve stones in front of no. 18 and eight at no. 50, the densest clusters in the zone: whole households of the old Jewish Weesperbuurt in one doorstep. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lijst_van_Stolpersteine_in_Amsterdam-Centrum ; second: Joods Monument per name (not read). Status: on the list 2026. Caveat: stories per family not yet read. Rank: Okay. New place (one pin for the street).

**AE-08. The Stolpersteine of the Plantage Badlaan.** Pin 52.364236, 4.916589, Plantage Badlaan 15. Eleven stones at nos. 13, 15 and 24 of a short street of 1900s blocks. Lead: the wiki Stolpersteine list (above); second: Joods Monument (not read). Status: list 2026. Caveat: stories unread. Rank: Okay. New place.

**AE-09. LIRO, the robbery bank, Sarphatistraat 47-55.** Pin 52.360951, 4.906645, Sarphatistraat 47-55. From 1941 this building held the "Lippmann, Rosenthal & Co." that the occupiers set up for "de stelselmatige beroving van de Joodse bevolking van al haar aardse bezittingen" (plaque text). The 2003 plaque ends with Isaiah: "tot beroving en er was niemand die zei: geef terug". Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/2969/amsterdam-plaquette-op-het-voormalige-liro-bankgebouw ; second: the gg-zuid draft "Lippmann, Rosenthal & Co., the bank whose name was stolen" (the real bank in the Nieuwe Spiegelstraat). Status: unveiled 26 May 2003, register 2026. Caveat: office building. Rank: Nice. New place (cross-reference the Lippmann Rosenthal draft).

**AE-10. Walter Süskind's bridge plaque, Nieuwe Herengracht.** Pin 52.365831, 4.901969, Walter Süskindbrug. Süskind, born in Lüdenscheid in 1906 and dead after the Auschwitz evacuation in 1945, worked at the Hollandsche Schouwburg; the plaque (30 August 1972) says he "onttrok vele joodse medeburgers aan deportatie". Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/2947 ; second: OSM. Status: register 2026. Rank: Okay. Group with "Bridges with wartime names".

**AE-11. The health service plaque, Nieuwe Achtergracht 100.** Pin 52.362549, 4.908730, Nieuwe Achtergracht 100. Placed by the staff of the city health service (G.G. en G.D.) on 1 August 1947 for fifteen colleagues killed in the war. Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1608 ; second: OSM. Status: register 2026. Rank: Okay. New place.

**AE-12. Telefoon van de Wind, Hoftuin.** Pin 52.366064, 4.904488, Hoftuin by the Weesperstraat. A disconnected phone in a garden for calling the dead, run by Humanitas Amsterdam, after the Japanese wind phone of Otsuchi. Lead: OSM node 11944513513 (website humanitas.nl/afdeling/amsterdam_en_diemen); second: Humanitas page. Status: on OSM 2026. Caveat: check opening hours of the Hoftuin. Rank: Nice. New place.

#### De Weteringschans

**AE-13. The wall in the middle of the school, Weteringschans 263.** Pin 52.359379, 4.897263, Weteringschans 263. During the war a wall suddenly went up in the middle of the Wilhelmina Catharinaschool: the Jewish half of the pupils was moved to the back. The 2005 plaque by Frits Nolte begins "Voor alle kinderen die van school naar huis gingen en daarna nooit meer werden gezien". Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3385 ; second: OSM. Status: unveiled 25 February 2005, register 2026. Rank: **Amazing**. New place.

**AE-14. Walraven van Hall's snapped tree, Frederiksplein.** Pin 52.359946, 4.899631, Frederiksplein. Fernando Sánchez Castillo's bronze tree, broken over a low wall, for the "banker of the resistance" shot in 1945; the fallen branches are a bench. A retired GP, Hans Weijers, started it after a 2005 documentary and raised €250,000 in the crisis years. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_Walraven_van_Hall ; second: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3543 . Status: unveiled 3 September 2010. Rank: Nice. New place (listed as a lead in notes/wetering-1.md).

**AE-15. The Navalny memorial, Frederiksplein.** Pin 52.359961, 4.899699, beside AE-14. A plaque for Aleksej Navalny ("Geef niet op en geloof in het beste") that moved here after the city would not let it stay at its first site; a Groningen student, Vladimir, travels down about once a month to clean it. Lead: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/357218/vladimir-onderhoudt-al-twee-jaar-monument-ter-nagedachtenis-aan-aleksej-navalny ; second: OSM node 12994026750. Status: NH Nieuws, two years of upkeep (2026). Caveat: informal memorial; status may change. Rank: Nice. Group with AE-14.

**AE-16. Krop's monument for the Spieghelschool fire, Marnixstraat 404.** Pin 52.363956, 4.881643, by the DeLaMar theatre. On 5 January 1945 the resistance set fire to the Arbeitseinsatz office in the Spieghelschool; the occupiers shot five political prisoners and twenty officials in reprisal. Hildo Krop's bronze of 1947 was torn off its base and taken in September 1967 and recast. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oorlogsmonument_Marnixstraat ; second: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1611 . Status: register 2026. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-17. The WSBZ staff stone, Nieuwe Looiersdwarsstraat 15-17.** Pin 52.360728, 4.893644. A 1947 stone by Hans Krieks, placed by colleagues for twelve staff of the W.S.B.Z. "gevallen als slachtoffers van de Duitse terreur", most of them Jewish names (Pereira, Rodrigues, Nunes Vas). Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1609 ; second: OSM. Status: unveiled 2 January 1947. Rank: Okay. New place.

**AE-18. "Tegen alle stromen in", Peter R. de Vries, Leidseplein.** Pin 52.363838, 4.882760, Leidseplein by the Lange Leidsedwarsstraat. Rini Hurkmans' two bronze hands gripping each other, with De Vries's own life rules in 41 languages, a few metres from where he was shot on 6 July 2021. Lead: https://nos.nl/l/2528991 ; second: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/338489/monument-peter-r-de-vries-trekt-veel-bekijks-groot-maar-hij-verdient-het-echt . Status: unveiled 15 July 2024. Caveat: on an A-list square, but the monument is its own place. Rank: Nice. New place (lead in notes/wetering-1.md).

**AE-19. Zeelandbank, Weteringcircuit.** Pin 52.359220, 4.891929. Nico Onkenhout's bench, Schouwen-Duiveland's thank-you for Amsterdam's help after the 1953 flood: a lion on waves, a fish tail, eighteen Zeeland town arms in the backrest. A plaster stand-in was put here in 1957; the real one was unveiled in July 1958 with four Zeelanders in costume. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeelandbank ; second: Kunstwacht record 532. Status: register 2026. Rank: Nice. New place (named in notes/wetering-3.md).

#### Grachtengordel-Zuid

**AE-20. Saint George and the dragon, Reguliersgracht 34.** Pin 52.363774, 4.895659, Reguliersgracht 34. Ben Guntenaar's white relief of 21 November 1945: the twelve faces are the twelve Jews hidden in this house by Ivo and Lydia Schöffer. The motto is a pun: "Submergo unica ut emergam", I dive under to come up. Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1514/amsterdam-sint-joris-en-de-draak ; second: OSM. Status: register 2026. Caveat: private house; the forging story (Lydia Schöffer and false IDs) needs a second source before writing. Rank: **Amazing**. New place.

**AE-21. Where Stichting 1940-1945 was founded, Keizersgracht 567.** Pin 52.364372, 4.890469. On 13 October 1944 representatives of the illegal groups met in deepest secrecy in this building and founded the Stichting 1940-1944, renamed 1940-1945 after the liberation, to carry the resistance's "verantwoordelijkheidsgevoel... voor elkanders moeilijkheden en noden". Plaque of 13 October 1994. Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/2944 ; second: OSM. Status: register 2026. Rank: Okay. New place.

#### Oostelijke Eilanden/Kadijken

**AE-22. The hunger winter relief, Oosterkerk.** Pin 52.369827, 4.919263, Wittenburgergracht front of the Oosterkerk. An interchurch committee had food bought from Groningen and Frisian farmers and shipped secretly to the islands in 1944-45; the neighbourhood's thanks is Hildo Krop's relief (a man with a sack on a ship, a woman with children before ruined houses) and a verse: "Dit monument vertoont de dank van heel de buurt". The weathered original is inside; a cast copy is on the street side. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_hongerwinter ; second: https://www.buitenbeeldinbeeld.nl/Amsterdam_C/Hongerwinter.htm . Status: OSM 2026. Rank: Nice. Group with "Oosterkerk, the islands' church the city nearly demolished".

**AE-23. De Waarheid's stone, Windroosplein.** Pin 52.373079, 4.924318. For the staff of the communist underground paper De Waarheid "gevallen in de strijd tegen het fascisme"; by Rob Schotman and Wim Tap, 1983, moved here from the Hoogte Kadijk in 1993. Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1495 ; second: Kunstwacht 586. Rank: Okay. New place.

**AE-24. Werkspoor's war memorial, Oostenburgergracht 77.** Pin 52.368022, 4.924538. A long stone of names, "Vrijheid, gerechtigheid, vrede", for the staff of the engineering works killed in the resistance, in bombings and in camps. Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/4707 ; second: Werkspoormuseum (photo 2025). Rank: Okay. Group with "Werkspoor's halls on Oostenburg".

**AE-25. The nautical college memorial, Marineterrein.** Pin 52.372376, 4.917126. Pieter Starreveld's plate for the former pupils of the Kweekschool voor de Zeevaart killed 1939-1945; 138 names at first, more added later. It stood in the school's courtyard on the Prins Hendrikkade until 2000. Lead: https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/3257 ; second: OSM. Caveat: Marineterrein access hours. Rank: Okay. Group with "Marineterrein, the navy yard behind the wall".

### Sweep 2: public art and walls

#### Oostelijke Eilanden/Kadijken

**AE-26. "Er ligt een roofstaat", the ship of the islands, Grote Wittenburgerstraat.** Pin 52.371708, 4.921704. A blue-stone ship on high waves (1983, Wim Tap, Marius van Beek, Hank Beelenkamp), commissioned to recall the islands' shipyard past, with a stout figurehead and frightened crew at the stern. The makers cut in Multatuli's line from Max Havelaar, "Er ligt een roofstaat tussen Oost-Friesland en de Westerschelde": the VOC yards' dark side, set in their own neighbourhood. Local voice: De Eilander, no. 7, 2008 (via Kunstwacht). Lead: https://www.buitenkunstamsterdam.nl (record 527, Plastisch teken) ; second: OSM. Status: register 2026. Rank: **Amazing**. New place.

**AE-27. Kattenburger Triomfboog.** Pin 52.372737, 4.919126, Kattenburgerstraat underpass. David Smithson and Kristina Leko asked Kattenburgers to write their own history; 25 residents, mostly in their sixties, did: the fight to return after the 1960s demolition, the low-rent movement, the union men. Their handwriting is fired onto tiles on the six columns of the main underpass. Lead: Kunstwacht record 556 ; second: Kunstwacht cites the project; artists' site not read. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-28. Mbulu Ngulu on the advertising column, Czaar Peterstraat.** Pin 52.367421, 4.925193, corner of the Oostenburgergracht. Since 1991 a West African reliquary guardian (orange head, red-and-white horns, purple rods) by Alexander Schabracq sits on top of a peperbus, diagonally across from De Gooyer. Lead: Kunstwacht record 504 ; second: OSM. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-29. Jakoba Mulder's lamp, Mulderplein.** Pin 52.366008, 4.924396. Gijs Bakker's 1997 memorial to one of the first women architects and planners: a tilted granito disc, a six-metre street lamp with leaf-shaped cuts, nine chairs, and 38 steel blocks spelling "Droombeeld. Visie. Werk. Inspanning. Verwachting. Wording." Lead: Kunstwacht 496 ; second: OSM. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-30. Tussentijd, the shards of the Funenpark.** Pin 52.368708, 4.929850. Gabriel Lester's five giant "shards" with relief maps of the old bastion, after pottery and the 1663 wall found when the park was dug. Lead: Kunstwacht 557 ; second: gabriellester.com. Rank: Okay. New place.

**AE-31. Simon Erb's sgraffito school front, Tweede Leeghwaterstraat 7.** Pin 52.370064, 4.931299. A 1960 sgraffito on the De Ruiterschool, rated "Top" in the city's wall-art register. Lead: maps.amsterdam.nl WANDKUNST ; second: OSM not found. Caveat: not seen. Rank: Okay. New place.

#### Weesperbuurt/Plantage

**AE-32. The art of Weesperplein metro station.** Pin 52.361451, 4.907986. Three works from a 1976 open call with 198 entries: Charles Bergmans' Verplaatsing, ten blue-stone blocks polished by forty years of sitters; Matthijs van Dam's Luchtspiegelingen, the Weesperstraat and Sarphatistraat seen from below; Pieter Engels' Signatuur van de anonieme arbeider, three bronze beams forming an A for Amsterdam, aannemer, arbeider. Lead: Kunstwacht 1182-1184 ; second: OSM. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-33. Krop's burghers, Weesperstraat.** Pin 52.360785, 4.908626. Hildo Krop's Poorter and Poortersvrouw (1940) on the Weesperstraat blocks, with Piet Kramer's year stones and locomotive relief. Lead: wiki statue list ; second: OSM nodes 4370446624 and 7084303792. Rank: Okay. New place.

**AE-34. The sphinxes of the Parktuin gate, Plantage Middenlaan.** Pin 52.367426, 4.909140. The empire gate is all that remains of the Parktuin and its concert hall; its lantern-bearing sphinxes vanished during or after the war. Hans 't Mannetje, once Krop's apprentice, carved marble copies from old prints, unveiled by mayor Polak on 16 January 1982 for the Plantage's 300 years. Lead: Kunstwacht 508 ; second: OSM 8272072528. Rank: Nice. New place.

#### De Weteringschans

**AE-35. Bureaucracy in Diversity, Kleine-Gartmanplantsoen.** Pin 52.363235, 4.883981. Judith de Leeuw's 150 m² mural (2021) of a Surinamese ballerina dancing through a whirlwind of official forms, made after her own permit and funding trials and the childcare-benefits scandal; the paint is meant to absorb pollution. Lead: Kunstwacht 607 ; second: SAC. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-36. La demeure humaine, back at De Nederlandsche Bank.** Pin 52.359052, 4.899237, Frederiksplein 61. Zadkine's last big work, unveiled two days after his death in 1967, has hung on a console, stood on the Westeinde and gone to ArtZuid; in spring 2025 it returned to the green strip by the bank's new entrance. Jan Wolkers called it a pile of broken chocolate bars. Lead: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_demeure_humaine ; second: Kunstwacht 545. Status: placed spring 2025. Rank: Nice. New place (group with DE-44 if the DNB garden goes in).

**AE-37. Ramses Shaffy, Levenslijnen, Vijzelgracht metro.** Pin 52.359696, 4.891689. Marjan Laaper's 24 by 13 metre LED portrait above the longest escalator in the Benelux. Lead: Kunstwacht 1168 ; second: OSM. Rank: Nice. New place (DE-27 is a different Shaffy portrait).

**AE-38. Monument for the tree that fell too young, Frederiksplein.** Pin 52.360361, 4.899557. In 1971 the Frisian sculptor André van der Linden got permission to turn a dead acacia into a brightly coloured sculpture, an early environmental happening. Lead: Kunstwacht 469 ; second: wetering-1 notes mention a 1970 "Knakenpaal" on the square: check if same. Caveat: survival after 55 years unconfirmed. Rank: Okay. New place.

#### Grachtengordel-Zuid

**AE-39. Karel Appel's glass, Singel 548.** Pin 52.366593, 4.892794. A 1964 glass appliqué on the former Spaarbank voor de Stad Amsterdam, rated "Top" in the city register. Lead: maps.amsterdam.nl WANDKUNST ; second: none yet. Caveat: check visibility from the street. Rank: Nice. New place.

#### Gable stones and tribute stones (sweep 2, by street)

**AE-40. Four views of Den Helder, Kerkstraat 402-408.** Pin 52.363229, 4.900950, Kerkstraat 404. Four stones of 1672: Huisduinen, Den Helder, Het Nieuwe Diep and the island of Wieringen, made for a carpenter from that coast (VVAG). Restored 2017. Lead: https://www.gevelstenen.net (Amsterdam, 1017JC) ; second: VVAG page. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-41. CURACAO and PORTORICO, Nieuwe Keizersgracht 55-57.** Pin 52.365744, 4.907162. A twin neck-gable pair of 1728/29 by Pieter and Hendrik Pantel with house-name stones for two Caribbean islands. Lead: https://www.amsterdam-monumentenstad.nl/database/grachtenboek_objecten.php?id=4436 ; second: gevelstenen.net and https://www.monumenten.nl/monument/2773 . Caveat: why the names is unknown; an article in the Nieuwe West-Indische Gids (Brill, "Merkwaardige gevelsteenen te Amsterdam") may hold it. Do not imply slave trade without a source. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-42. Stones of the Hoogte Kadijk.** Pin 52.369460, 4.914713, Hoogte Kadijk 78 corner of Tussen Kadijken. Arion and the dolphin (1990); De 3 Bloeyende Koorn Aaren (1777, no. 109-117); D Heerlyckheyt Udinck (18th c., Tussen Kadijken 13); and De Key's "Van de dijk af" (2011, no. 150), a man falling off a chair. Lead: gevelstenen.net (1018B) ; second: VVAG. Rank: Nice. New place.

**AE-43. Stadsherstel's thank-you stones.** Pin 52.361423, 4.896536, Prinsengracht 754 (WS). A cyclist on a bridge, "Onvermoeibaar bruggen slaend", for Jaap Vermeulen (2001); at Reguliersgracht 13 (GGZ) the sun and moon for Hengeveld; at Spiegelgracht 20a (WS) golden wind feathers for jeweller Anneke Schat, 1960-2020 (2021). Lead: gevelstenen.net ; second: VVAG. Rank: Okay. New place (one pin; the walk spans two wijken).
