## Shortlist

### Sweep 6: modern and contested

#### Burgwallen-Nieuwe Zijde

**CC-01. The bicycle garage under the water at Stationsplein.** Pin 52.379300, 4.901000
(approximate, the entrance on the Stationsplein, reverse-geocodes to Stationsplein 35M). Under
the Open Havenfront, nine metres below the water, about 7,000 bikes park in a garage built from
2018 and opened around 2023; its sister garage at the IJboulevard (4,000 places) opened on
22 February 2023. Hidden inside: two sgraffito wall pieces of 1965 by Lex Horn, each about
3 by 7 metres, saved from the demolished Jan Swammerdam Instituut and moved here in one piece.
The pair replaced the Fietsflat of 2001. Local voice: none found yet. Lead
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fietsenstalling_Stationsplein_(Amsterdam) ; second source needed
(gemeente or Wurck project page). Status: in use (wiki, read 24 Nov 2026). Caveats: exact pin of
the Horn panels inside not known; check that they are visible to the public. Rank **Nice**.
New place.

#### Burgwallen-Oude Zijde

**CC-02. Blaauwlakenblok: the block the Bijenkorf wanted, the squatters held and the
residents rebuilt (with W139 and the Condomerie).** Pin 52.373215, 4.896842 (gate of the
Blaauwlakensteeg; W139 at Warmoesstraat 139, 52.373751, 4.895988; Condomerie at Warmoesstraat
141, 52.373679, 4.895872). The diamond-shaped block between Warmoesstraat, Sint Annenstraat, OZ
Voorburgwal and Sint Jansstraat was earmarked in the 1960s for large-scale "city" building:
De Bijenkorf owned much of it for offices and a garage (the garage went to the Beursplein
instead). Squatters took it in the late 1970s, the city bought the houses in 1983, and after
years of residents' participation about sixty monumental houses were restored with over a
hundred homes and dozens of workspaces (finished early this century). Four gated alleys cut
through it; the Zwartlakensteeg leaves by the Oliphantsgang, 70 cm wide. W139, squatted in 1979
by young artists in a former cinema used as Bijenkorf storage, was a home of the Nieuwe Wilden
(Rob Birza, Peter Klashorst) and still runs free shows; the Condomerie next door opened on
1 May 1987 as probably the world's first condom shop. Local voice: on W139's reopening after
the renovation (19 January 2007) the board said the building should be "as public as a post
office, only with longer opening hours" (d'Oude Binnenstad, April 2007); in 2009 the district
showed residents a Project 1012 film while discussing the renovated block (OpNieuw 2009-2).
Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaauwlakenblok ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/W139
and d'Oude Binnenstad 2007-1 (DC's `ob/OB2007.1.6.w.one`). Status: W139 and Condomerie open
(Condomerie advert in d'Oude Binnenstad April 2020; W139 in OpNieuw 2021-4 as partner of
"Ongekend Zeedijk"). Caveats: alleys gated, residents and visitors only, so the pitch is the
gates, the open front of W139 and the shop; BC lists "Torpedo Theater, W139" as a theme C lead.
Rank **Amazing**. New place.

**CC-03. Oudezijds Armsteeg "street gallery".** Pin to set on site (Oudezijds Armsteeg, OZ;
not point-checked as no exact spot is known). In August 2013 residents turned a dumping corner of
the steeg into an open-air gallery of small street vitrines, after geveltuinen had already
reclaimed it (d'Oude Binnenstad 2013-3). Local voice: it is a residents' own project against
litter on the Wallen edge. Lead: d'Oude Binnenstad 2013-3 (DC's `ob/`); second source needed.
Status: unverified since 2013. Caveats: may be gone; check on site before anything. Rank
**Okay** (conditional). New place, or a Look for for AC-09 (White Cube, Nieuwezijds Armsteeg is
the other Armsteeg, so do not merge by name).

#### Nieuwmarkt/Lastage

**CC-04. The Stopera and the NAP under it.** Pin 52.367726, 4.902017 (the NAP display in the
passage between Amstel and Waterlooplein). Town hall and opera house in one, by Wilhelm
Holzbauer and Cees Dam (opera opened 23 September 1986, town hall in use from 1988), chosen from
803 competition entries in 1968. The fight: "Het stadhuis staat op de Dam" campaigners, the
name read as "Stop de opera", and on the first pile day (5 July 1982) protesters wrecked and set
fire to machines, after which the site was fenced. It ran more than 100 million guilders over
budget. In the passage: the NAP monument by Louis van Gasteren and Kees van der Veer (1988),
with a bolt at 0 m NAP and water columns (what each column shows: check on site). In autumn 2026 Stichting NAP opened an info point here and runs a
"Boutfunding" campaign until 31 December 2026 for a new NAP museum (target 100,000 euros). Local
voice: nickname "Het Kunstgebit" (the dentures, seen from the Blauwbrug); OpNieuw calls it "deze
kille kolos" whose sight still gives "painful stabs in the warm Amsterdam heart" (Haije Bouwman,
OpNieuw 2003-4), "nog steeds een steen des aanstoots" (OpNieuw 2007-4) and "dat spuuglelijke
gebouw dat Stopera heet" (market traders' piece, OpNieuw 2017-1). Lead
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stopera ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normaal_Amsterdams_Peil
and https://www.architectuur.org/bouwwerk/197/Stadhuis_Muziektheater.html ; NAP campaign:
rodi.nl (15 Oct 2026); nickname and Schippers detail from feitjes.amsterdam and
architectuur.org (read earlier today; recheck the exact page). Status: open; info point open since 16 Oct 2026. Caveats: AC lists the
NAP display as theme C and AC-15 (Violist) sits in the same building: group AC-15 and the
wedding room by Wim T. Schippers under this item. The Stopera is not on A-LIST.md. Rank
**Amazing**. New place.

**CC-05. Where the Maupoleum stood: the "Colosseum" and the green glass box.** Pin 52.369605,
4.901658 (Theaterschool, Jodenbreestraat 3); second pin 52.368389, 4.905577 (Filmacademie,
Markenplein 1). The Maupoleum (1971 to 1994), Maup Caransa's brutalist block, was long called
the ugliest building in Amsterdam. Its replacement was nicknamed before it was even finished:
"Het wordt nu al in de volksmond het Colosseum genoemd" (OpNieuw 1996-1); it houses the
Academie voor Theater en Dans and a city office. Across the square the Filmacademie (Koen van
Velzen, 1999, a tight front of green glass) promptly inherited the "ugliest building" title.
Local voice: OpNieuw ran a street poll in 1998 ("Een ouderwetse zwarte doos", Haije Bouwman):
"Vreselijk, vreselijk", "op een weiland waar niemand het ziet, zo lelijk vind ik het", and "it
goes well with that ugly Stopera"; the client replied in OpNieuw 1999-1. Lead
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Visserplein ; second https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maupoleum
and OpNieuw 1996-1, 1998-3 (DC's `on/txt/`). Status: both in use (wiki, 24 Nov 2026). Caveats:
the Goslerhuisje draft already mentions the Maupoleum; pitch this on the successors and the
nicknames. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**CC-06. The tunnel under the Mr. Visserplein: a motorway that never came.** Pin 52.367950,
4.903100 (approximate, on the square; reverse-geocodes to Waterlooplein 207). The square was
laid out in 1967-68 for a broad road that was to cut through the Nieuwmarktbuurt to the Central
Station; the Nieuwmarkt fight stopped the road, but the car tunnel under the square was built.
Its pedestrian tunnel was later bricked up; from 2003 to 2020 the tunnel held the children's
play hall TunFun (bankrupt in the pandemic), and since then the VROG foundation uses it for
freerunning, trampoline and urban dance. Local voice: none found yet. Lead
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Visserplein ; second source needed (VROG). Status: VROG use per
wiki (read 24 Nov 2026). Caveats: entrance pin to be fixed; only open for classes. Rank
**Okay**. Group with CC-05 if preferred.

**CC-07. Arcam: the folded aluminium pavilion on the Oosterdok.** Pin 52.371509, 4.912474
(Prins Hendrikkade 600). Amsterdam's architecture centre sits in René van Zuuk's compact
sculpture of coated aluminium folded over the roof to the ground, glass to the water. The brief
was to swallow Renzo Piano's small lookout pavilion for his New Metropolis (now NEMO) and to
look as modest as possible from the Scheepvaartmuseum, so it is lower on the water side. Free
entry to the exhibition floor; since 2020 a "Tijdlijn Amsterdam 2000-2030" downstairs. Local
voice: none found yet. Lead https://arcam.nl/over-arcam/over-het-gebouw/ (read 24 Nov 2026);
second source needed (nl.wiki Arcam was rate-limited). Status: open (site, 24 Nov 2026).
Caveats: the 2003 date is from memory; verify. NEMO itself is A-list; this is beside it, pitched
on itself. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**CC-08. OBA Oosterdok: the free view from the library roof.** Pin 52.375824, 4.908375
(Oosterdokskade 143). The central library by Jo Coenen opened on 7 July 2007 (7/7/7) on the
Oosterdokseiland, where the PTT post sorting tower (Merkelbach and Elling, 1968; Stedelijk's
temporary home until 2008; demolished 2009 despite Heemschut) stood. Its top floor has a café and
a terrace with one of the few free high views of the old city; it also houses IHLIA, the LGBTI
heritage archive. Local voice: none found yet. Lead
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Openbare_Bibliotheek_Amsterdam ; second
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oosterdokseiland . Status: open (wiki). Caveats: popular, but not
on A-LIST.md; check whether the terrace is open year-round. Rank **Okay**. New place.

### Sweep 7: curiosities, green and odd museums

#### Burgwallen-Nieuwe Zijde

**CC-09. Beurspassage, the "Amsterdam Oersoep".** Pin 52.374714, 4.894597 (passage between
Damrak and Nieuwendijk). A dark passage left after the 1963 C&A fire was turned in 2016, when
Damrak 70-79 was rebuilt, into an underwater canal: mosaic and glass ceiling by Arno Coenen and
Iris Roskam full of Amsterdam things (herring, chips, tulips, bikes), chandeliers by Hans van
Bentem made of bicycle parts, and a public drinking tap with a bronze fish head. Local voice:
none found yet. Lead https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_Oersoep ; second
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beurspassage . Status: open public passage (Atlas Obscura list,
24 Nov 2026). Caveats: AC and BC mention the ceiling only as a lead; it is on no shortlist.
Rank **Nice**. New place.

#### Nieuwmarkt/Lastage

**CC-10. The plane trees of the Nieuwmarkt: a square that fought over trees.** Pin 52.372600,
4.900400 (east side of the Nieuwmarkt; reverse-geocodes to Nieuwmarkt 4). In 1991 a residents'
foundation raised about 20,000 guilders to plant plane trees on the bare square; others wrote in
to stop it. In 1993 the council asked for a tree plan, residents cut down the planners' version
(they objected to "packing in" the Waag with ten pleached planes), and in mid-December 1995 the
planes went in with a small party. Today the city's tree consultant names them as the square's
shade trees among the elms of the Wallen. Local voice: "laat het leukste, spannendste en mooiste
open plein van Amsterdam niet vertrutten tot de brink van Dwingeloo" (letter, OpNieuw 1991-4);
the alternative from one Flesseman resident: grow ivy on the lamp posts. Lead OpNieuw 1991-4,
1993-4, 1995-3/4 (DC's `on/txt/`); second: d'Oude Binnenstad 2021-3 (Hans Kaljee walk). Status:
trees standing (Kaljee, October 2021). Caveats: OpNieuw 2008-2 dates the planting to 1996; the
1995 issue announces it for 11 to 15 December 1995. Rank **Nice**. New place.

**CC-11. Museumhaven Oosterdok: twenty working boats that are not a museum ship.** Pin
52.372991, 4.912696 (Oosterdok, along the Prins Hendrikkade by Arcam). The Vereniging
Museumhaven Amsterdam (40 years in 2025) moors over twenty historic working vessels here, lived
in and sailed, and holds the yearly Y-zeilen (32nd edition 5 Sept 2026) and a market for ship
parts. Local voice: none found yet. Lead https://www.museumhavenamsterdam.nl/ (read 24 Nov 2026);
second source needed (nl.wiki rate-limited). Status: active (site news March 2026). Caveats:
boats are private homes, seen from the quay. Rank **Okay**. Group with CC-07 if preferred.

## Look for lines

- **CC-04 Stopera**: AC-15 Violist; the wedding room by Wim T. Schippers; the real NAP bolt is
  under the Dam (1953, on a 22 m pile, under a bluestone lid about 90 cm below the paving), not
  at the Stopera.
- **DC-40 plaskrul (Oude Kerk)**: the Urilift by the Royal Palace (OSM 52.372745, 4.890941), an
  oval sinking urinal of 2016 with a women's urinal beside two men's, rising at night; invented
  by Marco Schimmel (nl.wiki Verzinkbare plaszuil).
- **BC-33 Heiligewegbad**: the Kalvertoren (Pi de Bruijn, 1997) stands on that site; glass lift
  and restaurant on top; the "Beetle Sphere" in the Kalverpassage (52.367750, 4.891042).
- **Amsterdam Museum / Kalverstraat 102**: register lime (Tilia europaea, "1920", monumental) at
  52.370268, 4.891024; OSM names it the Wilhelminalinde and says planted 1898. Dates conflict
  and the register height is "not applicable": check it stands before using.
- **Prins Hendrikkade 168A**: a black mulberry registered as a monumental tree, planting year
  given as 1795, privately managed (52.371631, 4.909313). Check whether it can be seen.
- **Dokwerker / Jonas Daniël Meijerplein**: two monumental plane trees of about 1900, over 24 m
  (52.366911, 4.905588 and 52.367034, 4.905578).
- **Zuiderkerk draft**: the half circle of twelve pleached planes on the Zuiderkerkhof
  (OpNieuw 2012-3).
- **DC-18 Koestraat "Ons Dorp"**: the garden "Van Twee Walletjes" (residents since 1995, gated,
  entrance Koestraat 15, key only; d'Oude Binnenstad 2018-2).
- **Prinsenhof / The Grand draft**: its inner garden, reached from the OZ Voorburgwal
  (d'Oude Binnenstad 2008-1, 2009-4).
- **AC-10 / Oude Kerk**: the contested 2015 roof installation by Tatzu Nishi (d'Oude Binnenstad
  2015-2, 2021-2).
- **Keizersstraat (nieuwmarkt-2 draft)**: the small green at Keizersstraat 2B (52.371992,
  4.901190) and the yearly Geveltuinendag held since 2007 at the Siebbeleshof (d'Oude
  Binnenstad 2007-1), named after Jan Siebbeles (1903 to 1985), Nieuwmarkt market-stall builder
  (OpNieuw 1985-5). Not confirmed that the 2B green is the Siebbeleshof.
- **Pentagon draft**: Paul de Ley's Zwanenburgwal 218-230 and Zeinstra's Oudeschans 3 are
  already in it; add the city's listing dates (2025 and 2023) if missing.
- **Rokin station (AC)**: the "Rode Loper" street redesign of Damrak and Rokin was meant to be
  red and ended up anthracite because that tested most "naaldhak-proof" (stiletto-proof), 2012
  (nl.wiki Rode Loper Amsterdam).
- **OZ**: a face made of bicycle wheels on the corner of Bethaniënstraat and Kloveniersburgwal
  (d'Oude Binnenstad 2017-2); status unknown.
